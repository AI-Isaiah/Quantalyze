<objective>
Finish WIZFORM-01 as a **class**: every rule this form can evaluate refuses at its own field, and a
submit-with-errors lands the user's cursor on the first thing that is wrong — even when that
control is hidden inside the collapsed "More details (optional)" disclosure.

Purpose: D-13's bar is behavioural. The founder's failure was not an ugly error, it was an error
that named no field, so they edited the wrong one. Fixing only the description would leave AUM,
max capacity and category able to reproduce the identical failure through the identical path —
the instance fix this repo has already paid for once.

Output: client mirrors for category, AUM and max capacity (AUM and capacity reuse the SERVER'S OWN
`isValidDollar`, not a re-typed rule); the first-invalid-control focus orchestration that opens a
collapsed `<details>` before focusing into it; and a visible form-level summary line that
`LiveRegion` re-states rather than authors.

⛔ Prerequisite: 153.2-01 must be complete — this plan generalises the shape it established and
extends the `handleSubmit` predicate it widened. ⛔ It does not touch the supported-exchanges
chips (153.2-04) or the budget / tombstone / `resilient-fetch.ts` (153.4).
</objective>

From `src/lib/dollar-validation.ts` — **the server's own predicate**, imported by
`finalize-wizard/route.ts:9` and applied at its `aum` (`:421`) and `max_capacity` (`:432`) arms:
  `isValidDollar(value: unknown): boolean`
The route calls it as `!isOmitted(x) && !isValidDollar(x)` — a blank value is OMITTED and therefore
valid. Mirror BOTH halves or a blank AUM will be refused client-side and accepted server-side.

⚠️ **The `Input` and `Select` primitives do NOT wire `aria-invalid`.**
`src/components/ui/Input.tsx` and `src/components/ui/Select.tsx` both colour their border with a
JS `error && "border-negative"` and render their own `<label>`. Their `error` prop is the FLAG-1
defect shape. Do not use it. The house pattern for an aria-derived field is `Field` wrapping a
BARE control — `MetadataStep.tsx:325-336` (`<textarea>`) and
`AllocateDialog.tsx:336-358` (`<input type="number">`) are the two live examples.

From `src/components/ui/LiveRegion.tsx`:
  `LiveRegion({ message: string | null, assertive?: boolean })` — `sr-only`;
  `assertive` ⇒ `role="alert"`, else `role="status" aria-live="polite"`.
  Its docblock (`:45-51`) states the contract: pass the SAME sentence the surface already renders
  visually. It is **never** a place to author new copy.

Field-level codes minted by 153.1-04 (Table A): `METADATA_NAME_INVALID`,
`METADATA_DESCRIPTION_TOO_SHORT`, `METADATA_DESCRIPTION_TOO_LONG`, `METADATA_CATEGORY_REQUIRED`,
`METADATA_AUM_INVALID`, `METADATA_CAPACITY_INVALID`, `METADATA_CAPITAL_OWNERSHIP_INVALID`.
Re-derive the exact member names from `src/lib/wizardErrors.ts` before use (D-10).
