<objective>
Make MT5 **declarable and submittable in the same ship** (D-14). Widening the chip set without the
probe skip leaves MT5 declarable and still unsubmittable — a HARD BLOCKER, and the state the
founder hit five Retry clicks against. Skipping the probe without the chip leaves the venue
undeclarable. Both halves, one plan.

Purpose:
- **MT5-14** — MT5 becomes a member of the set the metadata chip group offers, preselected from the
  key already connected, rendered as a FACT (a `<span>`) rather than a choice (D-15). It ships as
  **Option B** (D-20): a narrow wizard-declarable set plus a POSITIVE pin assertion, so the public
  marketing count at `(marketing)/page.tsx:115` does not move and MT5 is not claimed as an
  "exchange" in public copy.
- **WIZFORM-04** — `finalize-wizard` stops demanding a ccxt scope probe from a venue that has no
  scope surface (D-06: zero live MT5 calls on submit), and the catch-all that maps every probe
  failure to `KEY_NETWORK_TIMEOUT` stops reporting permanent conditions as temporary blips (D-14b).
  ⛔ **No retry loop is added** (D-07) — the fix removes calls, it never multiplies them.

Output: `WIZARD_EXCHANGE_CODES` / `WIZARD_EXCHANGES` in `closed-sets.ts` with the
`closed-sets.mt5-flag` pin deliberately re-cut in the SAME COMMIT; the pinned-fact chip, its
provenance eyebrow and the payload guarantee in `MetadataStep`; and the capability-gated probe plus
the split catch-all in `finalize-wizard`.

`venueSupportsScopeProbe` (153.1-02) and reorders this route's emitters to code-first (153.1-05).
