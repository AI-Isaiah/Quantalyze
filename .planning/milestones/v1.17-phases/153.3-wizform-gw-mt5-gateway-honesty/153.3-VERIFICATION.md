### May WIZFORM-05 be ticked? **No — and here is the reasoning, not an inherited claim.**

WIZFORM-05 (`.planning/REQUIREMENTS.md:713`) is worded as an END-TO-END property: *"an MT5 key
validation's honest verdict **always arrives inside the budget the client grants the request**."*
I measured both halves at `c707e13a`:

- **Client budget:** `src/lib/resilient-fetch.ts:538` — `SEAM_ROUTE_BUDGETS["validate-key"].timeoutMs`
  is still `30_000`.
- **Server worst case:** 75 s (end-to-end deadline) + 10 s (release, deliberately outside it) + 20 s
  (bounded lease wait) = **105 s**, as the file's own arithmetic block states
  (`routers/exchange.py:70-83`).

105 s > 30 s. A slow-but-honest MT5 verdict still lands after the client has abandoned the request.
What 153.3 removed is the *structural* censorship — the 60 000 ms `initialize()` inside a 30 s rpyc
bound that made a verdict impossible *at any client budget* — which is precisely why D-24 sequenced
this sub-phase BEFORE the budget work. The remaining arithmetic gap is D-26's 120 000 ms client
ceiling plus `BREAKER_LOCK_TOMBSTONE_S` 60 → 90, and `src/lib/seam-budgets.invariant.test.ts` (D-04)
is the gate that proves it. **Both are Phase 153.4's, in TypeScript, which this sub-phase does not
own and cannot edit.** Tick WIZFORM-05 when 153.4 lands, not now.

One thing worth saying plainly to the founder: 153.3 did **not** reduce the server worst case. It was
~105 s before (3 × 35 s) and it is 105 s now. That is deliberate — D-27/D-32 say measure before
tuning — but it means the *client-visible* symptom (a 30 s abandon) is unchanged until 153.4 ships.
Nobody should read "153.3 complete" as "MT5 validation works for a user."
