## The load-bearing constraints (full reasoning in the parent)

- ⭐ **A two-minute wait must stay legible.** The budget is `120_000` ms (D-26). The escalation
  ladder is driven by the **fraction of the configured budget elapsed**, never by hand-typed
  durations — if the budget changes, the copy must follow automatically.
  ⚠️ `resilient-fetch.ts` cannot be imported by a client component (`next/server`, `@upstash/redis`),
  so the client reads a **duplicated client-safe budget module** pinned to the server constant by a
  hand-typed-both-sides equality assertion. That duplication is the repo's own documented
  convention (`resilient-fetch.ts:110`), not an accident.
- ⛔ **`Stop waiting` needs NO confirmation.** `validate-key` is strictly pre-encrypt / pre-RPC —
  nothing is persisted server-side, so there is nothing to lose by aborting. Do not add a
  "are you sure?" step to a control whose whole purpose is escaping a stall.
- ⚠️ **`prefers-reduced-motion` must suppress the animation.** `globals.css:182` already handles
  `.animate-pulse` — keep that utility rather than writing a bespoke animation, and assert the
  stylesheet rule, not merely the class name.
- ⚠️ **D-21: ASCII `Validating...`**, never `Validating…` — and the same spelling in **all** new
  card copy (e.g. `Signing in to your broker...`), or the card mixes both forms.
- ⚠️ **`Button.tsx:35` hard-codes `ring-accent/50`** and `cn` is a plain join, not tailwind-merge —
  so a caller cannot override the focus ring. Fixing `Button.tsx` is out of scope; use a plain
  `<button>` carrying the verbatim focus contract, and the conflict stays logged in `TODOS.md`
  (Rule 7: surfaced, not blended).
- ⚠️ **Per panel, not global.** In `MultiKeyConnectStep` the wait state belongs to the panel whose
  key is validating — a global spinner would freeze panels that are not waiting on anything.
