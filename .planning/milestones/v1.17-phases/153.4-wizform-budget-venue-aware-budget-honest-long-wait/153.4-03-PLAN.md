<objective>
Give the browser an honest, client-safe copy of the budget it was granted, and build the long-wait
card that spends it: a status label, an escalation ladder keyed to fractions of the budget, and a
`Stop waiting` affordance (D-05, UI-SPEC Surface 1).

Purpose: a ~2-minute silent spinner is a worse product than a fast wrong answer (D-05). The card
must state a duration, and UI-SPEC forbidden item #8 requires that duration to be READ from the
configured budget — but `resilient-fetch.ts` imports `next/server` and `@upstash/redis` and can
never be imported into a client component. The answer is the repo's own convention for exactly
this: a deliberate hand-typed duplicate with an equality pin, the same shape
`resilient-fetch.ts`'s dependency vocabulary already uses.

Output: `src/lib/wizard/validate-budget.ts`, `ValidateWaitCard.tsx`, their tests, and the pin that
makes the duplication safe. No consumer is wired here — plans 04 and 05 do that.
</objective>
