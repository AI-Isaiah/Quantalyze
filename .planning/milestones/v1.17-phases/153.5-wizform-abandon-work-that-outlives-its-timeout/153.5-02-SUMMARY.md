## Decisions Made

- **Entry check outside the `_timed` bracket**, per the plan's step 1 rather than RESEARCH §Q4's worked shape which puts it at the top of the closure. Both fence the same path; the plan's placement additionally keeps a refusal out of the D-32 `duration_ms` population, which is the reason the file already gives for every other instrumentation choice.
- **The second check is an inline comparison, not an `_assert_live` call.** `_assert_live` raises immediately; the fenced path must dispose two sockets and restore a latch first. Documented at the site so a future reader does not "tidy" it into the helper.
- **`occupancy_epoch` is named distinctly from `self._epoch`** and the comment says why (RESEARCH Pitfall 2). They are different variables with different lifetimes — the token's epoch is the spawning lease's, `self._epoch` is the first touch's and stays `None` at construction.
- **No `mt5.stage` event is emitted for a construction refusal** (RESEARCH §Q-4 supersedes PATTERNS §Artifact 2 trap 1). The fenced *restart*, by contrast, does still emit its `mt5.stage restart` event with `ok=False` — because on that path a real round-trip (the WR-01 reconnect) genuinely happened, so the duration is a true measurement rather than a zero-duration non-call.

**T-153.5-08 remains ACCEPTED, not mitigated** — D-43: a client-side fence cannot un-send an rpyc call already dispatched on the wire, because `sync_request_timeout` is a purely client-side TTL and an in-flight call completes server-side. If the zombie had already dispatched `stale.shutdown()` when the lease released, the pipe still dies for the next holder. The fence closes the case where the zombie *arrives at* the shutdown after the release; `_assert_expected_login`'s detection bracket therefore stays. Stated in the restart docblock as well as here.

**A second honest limit, same class, recorded here rather than left to be discovered:** a construction that COMPLETES inside the window between the `wait_for` firing and the lease's bump passes both the pre- and the post-connect check and still leaks one socket, because nobody is left to receive the client. The construction fence narrows the leak to that window; it does not close it to zero. The window is bounded by the caller's unwind, and no sink-side mechanism can do better without the caller-side holder RESEARCH §Open Q-1 already ruled out.
