## HIGH-1: the multi_asset blend drops BTC's move across every US-market holiday

- **Where:** `src/lib/factsheet/allocator.ts:137-160` (`alignBlend`: `blendDates` is a weekday
  filter, and `blend()` nulls a date when any leg is null). It surfaces in
  `src/lib/factsheet/build-payload.ts` (the `multi_asset` entry of `allocatorPortfolios`), which
  feeds `BatchDPanels` `AllocatorSection` on every api-arm factsheet and on the allocator Overview.
- **Failing scenario:** a holiday such as MLK Monday, 2024-01-15, is a weekday, so it is in
  `blendDates`. SPX, GLD and IEF have no close that day, so `alignCoveredReturns` nulls it and
  `blend()` nulls the blend. BTC has a close that day, so BTC's Friday-to-Monday move sits at the
  holiday index and is discarded with it. On Tuesday, BTC's interval is based on Monday's close.
  Its return is Monday-to-Tuesday only, while the weekday legs read Friday-to-Tuesday. The move is
  lost for good: it never enters `own`, so `cum_ret`, `ann_vol` and `max_dd` never see it.
- **Measured** (2024-01-02 to 2025-12-31 on the bundled fixtures): 20 holiday intervals lose
  BTC's move. Those moves compound to **-10.1%**, including 2024-07-04 at -5.25%. At the 25% BTC
  weight that is about 2.5 pp of the 2-year cumulative return, and more on the vol and drawdown
  paths.
- **What the user sees:** "Cum. Return", "Portfolio Vol", "Max Drawdown" and the sleeve scan on the
  "Multi-Asset Risk Parity" tab are silently wrong. No caption says so, because `through` is
  unaffected. D-70 names this exact defect class as the reason to reject plain compaction ("drops
  BTC's weekend moves from the multi-asset blend (a new wrong number)"). Here it comes back on
  holidays instead of weekends. The 60/40 blend is not affected, because none of its legs trade on
  a holiday.
- **Recommendation:** build a weekday blend's dates from the dates every leg's calendar carries
  and every weekday leg actually has a close for. Then a holiday is not a blend date at all, and
  Tuesday's interval (the previous blend date to Tuesday) gives BTC its Friday-to-Tuesday move
  through the same `alignCoveredReturns` call. Pin it with a test that goes red under the current
  code: a Monday holiday with a non-zero BTC Friday-to-Monday move, where the blend's compounded
  return must include that move. **Known cost of this fix:** on the day after a holiday, a
  weekday leg gets `count = 1` (one bridged return) where `expected = 2` on the 5-day calendar, so
  `paired` is false there. The blend value is correct and enters `own`, but the correlation and
  the sleeve scan lose those roughly 10 days a year. That is not a wrong number. The fixer should
  expect it rather than meet it as a red test.

## HIGH-2: tail windows are silently discarded, then the caption claims there were none

- **Where:** `src/lib/factsheet/allocator.ts:312-326` (a 21-date window holding any null is
  `continue`d, with no count kept) and `:329-352` (with `tailMm` empty, `tail_mm_mean`,
  `tail_mm_median` and `tail_mm_pos` return a literal `0`).
  `src/app/factsheet/[id]/v2/BatchDPanels.tsx:367-379` renders them.
- **Failing scenario:** holiday nulls (HIGH-1) fall inside most 21-weekday windows.
  **Measured** on the 60/40 blend, 2024-01-01 to 2025-12-31, well inside the fixtures' coverage:
  **317 of 502 windows (63%) are dropped**, leaving `tail_count` 3. Every window past the
  fixtures' last close (2026-05-08) is dropped as well. So on a current strategy almost every
  window is discarded, and `tail_count` is small or 0.
- **What the user sees:** with `tail_count === 0`, the panel states *"No stress windows in the
  observed sample — portfolio never drew ≥ 5% in any 21-day window."* That is an affirmative claim
  about windows that were never examined. Beside it, "MultiMarket mean return +0.00%", "median
  +0.00%" and "Windows MM was positive 0%" are fabricated zeros, not em-dashes. That breaks D-70(4)'s
  own rule that a measured figure with no basis is null. With `tail_count > 0` but most windows
  discarded, "During the 3 stress windows…" reads as a complete census when it is not.
- **Recommendation:** HIGH-1's fix removes the holiday nulls, which clears most of the discards.
  Independently of that: count the discarded windows and say so in the caption ("N of M 21-day
  windows had every leg priced"). Return `null` for `tail_mm_mean`, `tail_mm_median` and
  `tail_mm_pos` when `tailMm.length === 0`. Word the zero-window caption as "no fully priced
  window reached the threshold", not "never drew".

## MEDIUM-1: signature headers count benchmark traces, which now diverge from the capital panels

- **Where:** `src/lib/factsheet/event-signatures.ts:169-170` (`winCount: winBench.length`,
  `lossCount: lossBench.length`) and `:44-46` (an empty population aggregates to all-zero
  arrays). They render in `SignaturePanels.tsx:58,64` and `CrossSignaturePanels.tsx:91,97`.
- **Failing scenario:** before this phase, BTC gaps were 0-filled, so no benchmark trace was ever
  dropped for a gap, and the bench and equity populations were the same size. Now, under
  D-70(1), a BTC null drops the benchmark trace only. `buildEquityTrace` checks the event series,
  not BTC. When BTC is unavailable (a read failure under D-54, or "no covered interval"),
  `btcAligned` is all null, and in `eventSignatures` every benchmark trace is dropped while every
  equity trace is kept.
- **What the user sees:** "Returns Signatures for 7 Days Horizon (0 wins · 0 losses)" above two
  populated "of Accumulated Capital" panels built from many events. Beside them are two "of BTC"
  panels drawing a flat 0% mean and zero-width bands, which looks like a real finding that BTC
  does not move around events. In the cross panels, "BTC events (0W · 0L)" also draws a flat 0
  line. The section stays mounted because `hasComparator` is true through the SPX, GLD, IEF and ETH
  fixtures. A stale BTC feed produces the same count mismatch, just smaller.
- **Recommendation:** carry separate counts for the benchmark and equity populations and render
  each panel with its own N. When a population is empty, return a sentinel (`null` or a
  `count: 0` flag) and have the panel render its empty state instead of aggregating to zeros. The
  CSV gate at `FactsheetView.tsx:451-456` names this fabrication already, but only for CSV.

## MEDIUM-2: a failed BTC read is captioned as "too few priced days"

- **Where:** `src/app/factsheet/[id]/v2/BatchDPanels.tsx:289-298`, fed by `btcLeg` in
  `build-payload.ts`, which turns `{ unavailable: true }` into a leg with no prices, so
  `alignBlend` returns `through: null`.
- **Failing scenario:** the BTC read fails. That is logged once server-side under D-54, with no
  Sentry capture.
- **What the user sees:** on the Multi-Asset and Crypto Book tabs, *"Prices unavailable for this
  portfolio — too few days with every leg priced to measure it."* The cause given, sparse
  overlap, is not the real cause, an outage. The strategy looks too short or too gappy to measure.
  This repo requires empty-state copy to name the true cause (#509: distinct bodies per cause).
- **Recommendation:** carry an explicit `unavailable` flag from `btcLeg` through `alignBlend`
  and the portfolio entry, and render a distinct line such as "BTC prices are unavailable right
  now". Keep "too few days" for a genuine `own.length < 2` on priced legs.

## MEDIUM-3: the scenario-share page's BTC fetch fails with no trace at all

- **Where:** `src/app/scenario-share/[token]/page.tsx:85-101` (`fetchBtcCloses`: `if (!res.ok)
  return null`, a parse returning null, and a bare `catch { return null }`).
- **Failing scenario:** there are four ways in: `NEXT_PUBLIC_APP_URL` unset or wrong in an
  environment (the fallback is `http://localhost:3000`), a 2.5 s timeout on a cold unbounded read,
  a route contract drift that `parseBtcCloses` now refuses as a whole body, or a 429 from
  `publicIpLimiter`. The server fetch runs from the function's IP, so all anonymous share traffic
  may share one limiter bucket. That last one is unmeasured: it depends on what `getClientIp`
  resolves for a self-fetch.
- **What the user sees:** "Benchmark comparison unavailable", with no overlay, on every shared
  scenario, permanently. Nothing is logged server-side, so nobody can tell an outage from a
  misconfiguration or a rate-limit wall. The client twin in `ScenarioComposer.tsx:2208-2238` was
  given `console.warn` on each of these branches (F-08) for exactly this reason. This rewrite
  kept the server path silent.
- **Recommendation:** log each branch with a stable message and its discriminator: status code,
  "unexpected body shape", AbortError versus other errors. Rate-gate a Sentry capture as the
  closes route does (`shouldCaptureNow` + `after()`). The page stays honest-degrade. Only its
  observability changes.

## MEDIUM-4: the alpha/beta widget lost its minimum-sample guard and hides its N

- **Where:** `src/app/(dashboard)/allocations/widgets/attribution/AlphaBetaDecomposition.tsx:86-124`.
  At `f1ca32b56` the widget returned null for fewer than 10 observations. Now `book.length < 2`
  is the only floor, and `pairedCount` is computed but never rendered.
- **Failing scenario:** a newly connected allocator whose book is `"ready"` with 3 days of
  history. That is a common first-week state. It pairs 2 or 3 BTC intervals, `jointMetrics`
  returns finite values, and the `ok` branch renders.
- **What the user sees:** an annualized alpha such as "+812.3% annualized" in bold, sign-coloured,
  with a decomposition chart. Nothing marks it as a 2-day estimate. The Scenario's "vs BTC"
  section gates the same statistics on a 30-day floor (`BENCHMARK_FLOOR`) and names its N.
- **Recommendation:** D-69 requires this widget and the Overview comparator to show one number,
  so gate both together. Either apply the Scenario's `evaluateSampleFloor` to the paired count here
  and in the comparator block, or at minimum render "over N paired days" next to the alpha. The
  first keeps parity, and the second makes the thin basis visible.
