### WR-01: The Scenario BTC overlay is not anchored at the scenario's first date

**Files modified:** `src/app/(dashboard)/allocations/lib/scenario-benchmark.ts`,
`src/app/(dashboard)/allocations/components/ScenarioComposer.tsx`,
`src/app/scenario-share/[token]/page.tsx`, plus tests in `scenario-benchmark.pairing.test.ts`,
`ScenarioComposer.test.tsx`, `src/app/scenario-share/[token]/page.test.tsx`
**Commit:** `7eb4ef24d`
**Status:** fixed

**Applied fix:** `btcLevelsFromCloses(prices, fromDate?)` bases the level on the last close dated on
or before the scenario's first date and starts the curve there, so BTC reads 1.0 on the portfolio's
first day. It falls back to the first close when no close precedes that date. The composer passes
`portfolioDaily[0]?.date`. Its memo moved below `portfolioDaily` so it can read that date. The share
page passes its own `portfolioDaily[0]?.date`. `pairScenarioWithBtc` does not read the overlay, so the
vs-BTC statistics do not change.

**Neuter record (re-run by this fixer):** a byte backup of `scenario-benchmark.ts` was taken with `cp`.
The `fromDate` branch was made inert (`if (fromDate !== undefined && false)`) and the three test files
were run. The result was **4 failed / 441 passed**:
- `reads 1.0 on the scenario's first date and has no point before it`
- `bases on the last close BEFORE the first date when that date has no close`
- the composer case `the overlay is based at the scenario's first date (2023-01-01) ...`
- the share-page case `... not at the served series' first close years earlier`

The file was restored with `cp` and checked with `cmp`. A re-run was **445/445 green**.

wants alerting, it should add the capture.

### SFH-M4: the alpha/beta widget lost its minimum-sample guard

**Files modified:** `src/lib/factsheet/joint.ts`, `src/lib/factsheet/comparator-block.ts`,
`src/app/(dashboard)/allocations/widgets/attribution/AlphaBetaDecomposition.tsx`,
`src/app/(dashboard)/allocations/widgets/attribution/AlphaBetaDecomposition.book-btc.test.tsx`,
`src/lib/factsheet/build-payload.benchmark-opt.test.ts`
**Commit:** `08b20aa69`
**Status:** fixed: requires human verification (a logic gate; the floor value is a judgment call)

**Applied fix:** D-69 requires the widget and the Overview to show one number, so both gate on one
constant, `MIN_PAIRED_OBSERVATIONS = 10`, in `src/lib/factsheet/joint.ts`. 10 is the minimum the
widget carried at `f1ca32b56`, before 169.4.
- **The widget:** `computeBookAlphaBeta` returns `{ kind: "below_floor", pairedCount, floor }`
  under the floor. The widget then renders "—" with no sign colour and no chart, plus one muted line:
  "Alpha and beta need at least 10 days paired with BTC; this book has N."
- **The Overview:** `buildComparatorBlock` builds no joint under the floor. A null joint is the
  factsheet's existing absence form. The KPI strip drops its α and IR cells (9 cells become 7) and
  section IV is not rendered.

**Deliberate consequences:**
1. **The Overview's form is "absent", not a "—" cell with a reason.** Giving the Overview a dash and a
   reason would need a new field on `ComparatorBlock` in `types.ts`, which is fixer B's file. Below
   the floor, neither surface shows a figure. At or above it, both show the same figure. That is the
   parity D-69 asks for.
2. **The comparator-block floor applies to every factsheet, not only the allocator Overview.** It
   covers the BTC and SPX blocks on strategy and scenario factsheets and the OG, tearsheet and PDF
   routes, because they all build through `buildComparatorBlock`. A record with fewer than 10 paired
   days now shows no α/IR cells and no section IV. This was the minimal path: there is no
   allocator-only hook without editing `build-payload.ts` (fixer B's file). The review's own
   recommendation was to gate the comparator block. The leverage re-derive (`basis-context.tsx`, at
   L≠1) goes through `deriveSeriesBundle` → `buildComparatorBlock`, so the floor holds there too.
   `jointMetrics` has no other caller in `src/` apart from the widget.
3. **The zero-pairs case changed.** A ready book with zero paired intervals used to be
   `undefined_beta` and is now `below_floor` with `pairedCount: 0` ("this book has 0"). No test
   asserted the old kind.

**Factsheet figures that moved: none in any fixture or snapshot.**
- One test moved. The D-58 pairing test in `build-payload.benchmark-opt.test.ts` used a 10-date window
  with 8 paired, so its block now has no joint. The window was widened to 14 dates (12 paired). The
  test's intent is unchanged: the joint still excludes k and k+1, checked against `jointMetrics` over
  the same paired indices.
- The `build-payload.test.ts` snapshot fixture (62 dates) passed byte-unchanged.

**Tests added** (`AlphaBetaDecomposition.book-btc.test.tsx`, describe "the shared paired-observation
floor"):
- The floor is 10.
- A 3-day book gives the widget `{ kind: "below_floor", pairedCount: 3, floor: 10 }` and renders "—"
  with the reason line and no "annualized". The Overview payload's `comparators.btc.joint` is null.
  The rendered Overview (`FactsheetBody` mounted as `AllocationDashboardV2` mounts it) has no
  "α vs BTC" cell and no "Alpha (ann)" row.
- 9 paired gives no figure on either surface.
- At 10 and at 30 paired, the widget's alpha and beta equal the Overview's joint exactly. The rendered
