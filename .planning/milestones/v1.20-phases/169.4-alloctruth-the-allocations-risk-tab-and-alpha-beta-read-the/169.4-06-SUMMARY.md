# Phase 169.4 Plan 06: Allocator blends on their legs' common calendar Summary

Each demo allocator portfolio is now built by `alignBlend` on the strategy dates every leg's calendar carries, with every leg and the book aligned through `alignCoveredReturns`. Its figures skip every null. Each blend annualizes on its own calendar: 60/40 on 252, crypto_book on 365, and multi_asset on 252 under the founder's 2026-09-30 exception to #597 BLEND-02. A blend with too few priced days renders as the em-dash with a dated caption. The null-as-0 helper is deleted, and the factsheet cache key moved v9 -> v10 (169.5 D-65; 169.4 D-70(2)-(5), D-71).
