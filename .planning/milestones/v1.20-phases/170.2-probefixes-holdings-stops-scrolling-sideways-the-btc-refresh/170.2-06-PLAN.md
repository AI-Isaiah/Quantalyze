SC-1: at 390, 640 and 735 CSS px the Holdings tab's `#main-content` overflow is 0, and each wide table
scrolls inside its own scroller, proven by a seeded e2e case with a wide positions table that fails on
the old code.

that sizes to the widest table's min-content, so the `ResponsiveTable` scrollers grow with their tables
and `#main-content` scrolls instead (885 vs 735 on PROD, evidence (1)). RESEARCH measured that the
root-only fix leaves the nested grids blowing out; all four need `grid-cols-1`. CI missed it because
the seeded book has one narrow row.

Output: a wide seed option, the seeded Holdings describe, the four-grid fix, the Exposure drill-down in
a named `ResponsiveTable`, and class guards.
</objective>
