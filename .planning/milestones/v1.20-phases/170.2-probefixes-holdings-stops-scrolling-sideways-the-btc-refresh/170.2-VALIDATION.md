## Sampling Rate

- **After every task commit:** the task's own `<automated>` command (the quick run for the touched layer).
- **After every plan wave:** the full vitest suite, the full pytest suite and `make typecheck`; after waves containing plans 06, 07 or 08, the touched seeded describes on the local lane.
- **Before `/gsd-verify-work`:** plan 09 Task 1: every full suite green, all three seeded describes green with no skip, `node scripts/verify-plan-anchors.mjs --pending` exits 0.
- **Max feedback latency:** 120 s for unit and pytest tasks; seeded describes up to about 300 s including the lane.
