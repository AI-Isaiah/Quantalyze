# Phase 170.2 Plan 02: Full-history benchmark read (SC-5) Summary

The BTC benchmark read is now driven by the strategy's first return date (`since`) instead of a fixed trailing 1000 days, and the cache read pages past PostgREST's 1000-row `max_rows` with a keyset cursor, so a CSV strategy older than 1000 days keeps its alpha, beta, Treynor and R-squared history on recompute.

## What changed

- **`services/benchmark.py`.** `BTC_FIRST_DAY = date(2017, 8, 17)`; `_BENCHMARK_PAGE_SIZE = 1000`; `_BENCHMARK_MAX_PAGES`. `get_benchmark_returns` gains the `since` keyword and computes `window_start = max(min(yesterday - (days - 1), since - 1 day), BTC_FIRST_DAY)`; the fetch asks for `(today - window_start).days + 1` days (equal to `days + 1` when `since` is None). The single `.limit(days + 1)` read became a keyset loop: page 1 keeps the exact `select("*").eq("symbol", ...).order("date", desc=True).limit(...)` chain, later pages add `.lt("date", cursor)` after `.eq(...)`. `_cache_miss_reason` gains the optional `window_start` keyword (the existing `days=`/`yesterday=` call and its "gaps between" message are unchanged). The plan-01 merge (cache wins, only new dates upserted) is untouched and is what makes the older-history backfill a one-time write.
- **`services/analytics_runner.py`.** `run_csv_strategy_analytics` passes `since=returns.index.min().date()` (None for an empty or non-datetime index) inside the existing `try`. Nothing else at that site changed.
- **Tests.** `_FakeTable` in `test_benchmark_refresh.py` gained `max_rows` (applied after the limit slice, like the server) and a `requests` log. New `tests/test_benchmark_since.py` (8 tests) pins the clock to 2026-10-09 00:30 UTC through `benchmark_mod._utc_now` (the file's own 2020-01-02 `_NOW` would let the `BTC_FIRST_DAY` floor clamp any window over ~867 days).
