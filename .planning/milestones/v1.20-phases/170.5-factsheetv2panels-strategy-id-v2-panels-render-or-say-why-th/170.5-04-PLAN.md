<objective>
D-07 (replaces D-02): v2's benchmark greeks are the SAME as the factsheet's: alpha, beta, IR,
Treynor and correlation, from the factsheet's live TypeScript computation, so both pages always
match exactly. This plan builds the computation as a server-safe module, proven equal to the
factsheet builder by a parity oracle, plus the two adapters the wiring plan (06) needs:

- the **light path** (`readV2BenchmarkJoint`), one admin read through `withPublishedOnly` of
  `asset_class` and the analytics series columns, then the factsheet's own steps (PATTERNS "D-07,
  Recommended path"); it answers `needs_builder` for a composite, which the light path cannot read;
- the **builder path mapping** (`v2JointFromBuildResult`), which turns a
  `fetchAndBuildPayloadWithReason` result into the same closed status, for composites (UI-SPEC
  "No composite rung"; called from the page in plan 06).

Known difference from the full builder, stated rather than hidden (PATTERNS gap table): v2 shows
the factsheet's default view (cash basis, leverage 1x), and the light path's BTC read window is the
cash axis only (`buildOpts` undefined), which does not change a cash-date alignment.

Purpose: one number on both pages, by construction and by test.
Output: `src/lib/factsheet/v2-joint.ts` and two test files. No caller yet (plan 06 wires it).
</objective>
