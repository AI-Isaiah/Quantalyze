## Phase Boundary

On `/strategy/[id]/v2`, every panel either renders or says why it can't. The ROADMAP heading names five defects; the goal itself is a placeholder, so the criteria are set here:

1. **Rolling Alpha and Beta:** the panel is an empty heading in the 3M and 6M windows.
2. **Benchmark greeks:** they read all em-dash with "Benchmark correlation unavailable", while the factsheet shows alpha, beta, IR and Treynor.
3. **Net and Gross Exposure:** the axis reads 5000000%.
4. **Turnover:** the panel is empty.
5. **Footer:** it calls an API-verified strategy self-reported.

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

- **D-01 (short rolling window):** keep the 3M/6M toggle. When a window has too little history, the panel says why, along the lines of "Not enough history for a 3-month rolling window (needs N days, has M)", and the layout stays stable. Founder choice (2026-10-09, Recommended), preferred over disabling the toggle.
- **D-02 (benchmark greeks):** v2 reads the same stored source the factsheet uses for alpha, beta, IR and Treynor, so the two pages agree. A greek that cannot be computed reads "—" with the reason. It never contradicts the factsheet.
- **D-03 (exposure axis):** the Net and Gross Exposure axis shows the correct unit and scale. Research must establish whether exposure is stored as a ratio or as a percent. A test with a known exposure pins the axis label.
- **D-04 (turnover):** the Turnover panel renders from stored data, or says why ("Turnover isn't available for this strategy's data source"). It is never empty.
- **D-05 (footer):** the footer's verification label follows the strategy's real trust tier, the same rule as the factsheet's `TrustTierLabel`. An API-verified strategy never reads "self-reported".
- **D-06:** each of the five items gets a test that fails on today's page. Never invent data: an uncomputable metric is null and renders "—".

### Founder decision and corrections after research (2026-10-09)
- **D-07 (replaces D-02):** v2's benchmark greeks are the SAME as the factsheet's: alpha, beta, IR, Treynor and correlation. They come from the factsheet's live TypeScript computation (`comparator-block.ts` / `joint.ts`), so both pages always match exactly. The stored Python values are not used for them. Founder choice, over "stored values, made honest". Research sized this as possibly its own phase. The planner sizes it inside 170.5 first. If it truly does not fit, split it with `/gsd-phase --insert` and keep it next in order. It is never dropped.
- **Correction to canonical refs:** `/strategy/[id]/v2` is `src/components/strategy-v2/*`, fed by `getStrategyDetailV2` in `src/lib/queries.ts`. `src/app/factsheet/[id]/v2/` is the comparison target, not the edit target.
- Research resolutions adopted:
  - The rolling-window rule is N = 90 paired intervals, and a series renders with at least 2 points.
  - Exposure is USD notional: currency ticks and a "through {date}" caption.
  - Turnover is gated on a `{date, value}` contract, and legacy rows say why.
  - The footer reads `readPublicVerificationSignals`.
  - `Disclaimer` keeps its unknown-tier default (Q5).
  - Q7 (the 3M/6M label windows) and the "Verified" text in v2's `generateMetadata` go to 170.1 COPY.
  - Land 170.5 before 170.6.
