# Phase 164.5.1 Plan 05: Worker Retry Seam Summary

**`_claim_priority` and `_reset` in `main_worker.py` now consume the plan-02 `db_read_with_retry` seam instead of the fail-fast `db_execute`, with the 42883 permanent-latch contract proven byte-identical via three anti-vacuity levers including a dynamic-dispatch mutant on the shared predicate.**

- **Tasks:** 2 completed

## Accomplishments

- `analytics-service/main_worker.py`: `dispatch_tick`'s priority claim call and `watchdog_tick`'s reset call both swapped from `db_execute` to `db_read_with_retry` — a transient Supabase gateway 504 on either read is now retried (up to `DB_READ_MAX_RETRIES=3`, ~3s worst-case backoff) instead of costing the worker a whole tick and filing a Sentry event.
- Both legacy-claim fallback call sites (the latched-and-fresh early branch, and the except arm's last-resort fallback) deliberately stay on `db_execute` — verified by a source-scan acceptance criterion asserting exactly 2 `db_read_with_retry(` call sites exist in the file.
- The except arm's classification logic (`_is_undefined_function`, `_is_undefined_function_structured`, the `_FALLBACK_CLAIM_RPC` latch, the re-probe interval) is untouched apart from two explanatory comments — no branch added, removed, or reordered.
- 5 new tests (`TestWorkerGatewayTimeoutRetry`, selectable via `pytest -k gateway_timeout`) prove: retry-then-succeed at both call sites with the latch staying False; exhaustion at both call sites preserving the original exception's IDENTITY (not just type); and the 42883 case making exactly ONE attempt (not gateway-shaped) while still latching and still running the legacy fallback.
- Three-lever anti-vacuity procedure: two levers neuter each call site's retry back to `db_execute` (each reddens exactly its own two tests), and a third neuters the shared `_is_gateway_timeout` predicate itself to a dynamic-dispatch mutant (always-True) rather than deleting it, proving the 42883-narrowness assertion detects real behavior change, not name presence.
