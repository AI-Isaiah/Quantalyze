## Things the orchestrator and founder should weigh before acting on the gate line

1. **The reading was taken at a quiet moment for the options book.** The one open option row on the options-holding account has zero USD value, `options_value` is 0 on every currency, and the options session UPL is about 1.1e-5 of equity. The identity is exercised in the plan's sense and in the stronger measured sense (54.4 tolerance units of discrimination), but it was not tested against a materially valued option book. If that matters for SC-1's anchor choice, wait for a slot with a valued book; the runbook makes that a single re-read.
2. **Every account is portfolio margin with cross collateral on its main coins.** RESEARCH A2 and OQ-1 assumed standard margin and said a PM or cross account needs a separate argument if the identity fails. It did not fail, so the plan's rule yields the gate. The founder may still want the PM/cross fact recorded as the reason SC-1's guard (option (a) in Task 3 step 3) deserves a test, even though it is not needed to pass this gate.

This reading shows the cap does not strand a key at one instant; it does not show the cap catches the earlier measured offset.
4. **The sibling derive spread on the options account (about 218 differing days) is unchanged by PR-1**, as intended: PR-1 is capture only.

## Deviations from Plan

**1. [Addition] Two forms beyond the ten.** `deribit-keys` is a shared fragment (the ranked key list every per-key read expands) and `cron-run` reads the derive cron's run history, which the precondition needs and no listed form reads. Both are read-only. All ten named forms exist and render. (The first commit's message says thirteen; the file holds twelve.)

**2. [Addition] `identity_ratio_if_osu_added` column in `capture-read`.** The plan defines "exercised" by the options session UPL flag. A nonzero flag on a 1e-5-of-equity value could be too small to discriminate, so the column measures it. The flag-based rule still decides the verdict; the column is evidence beside it.

**3. [Shape] `dust_rel_*` are stored as ratios, not booleans.** The runbook selects `dust_rel_*_le_1e4` booleans against the 1e-4 relative allowance, which is what the plan wanted to read.

**4. [Process] The reading waited about 50 minutes for the 05:30 UTC queue to drain.** The backfill worker runs the fan-out's 8 jobs serially. The plan's wording ("wait for the next slot") was written for a key that did not derive; here every key was queued and simply not yet run, so the wait was for the same slot. Nothing was enqueued by hand.
