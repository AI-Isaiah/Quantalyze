## Unregistered flags (all non-blocking, none counted in threats_open)

- F1 (informational): `key_capture:<api_key_id>` rows are a new row family, owner-SELECT-able under the same RLS as `key_inputs` (flagged in 03-SUMMARY). Same exposure, no secret. Note: they are not removed when a single key is deleted (cascade is by `allocator_id` only), so a stale capture can linger until the allocator is deleted. Not a disclosure beyond the owner.
- F2 (watch item, carried from REVIEW WR-02, confidence 5): the 5 USD cap now gates every section 5 caller, including the `_reconstruct_deribit` composite path under cash and MTM. The OQ-3 clearance was read from key-mode cash diagnostics only. A later `stitch_composite` could flip to `failed`. Not measured. Action before the Wave 5 composite recompute: record a `composite-cap-verdict:` from one read-only scratch measurement. Do not loosen the cap.
- F3 (hardening): the runbook write scan names INSERT/UPDATE/DELETE, cron and enqueue calls (what T-18 declared) but not DDL or `set_config`. Every form was audited as SELECT-only and parameters are quoted and character-restricted, so there is no live gap. Widening the regex would make the guard match its own claim.
- F4 (ship-time check): T-31 is verified on commit messages. The PR body does not exist yet; count the skip tokens in the draft before opening the PR. Pre-existing and out of scope, noted for the record: the empty-ledger refusal message (`job_worker.py` EmptyLedger branch, moved not authored by this phase) prints a rounded equity amount, and the compose-enqueue warning prints allocator/api_key ids; both exist on `origin/main`.
