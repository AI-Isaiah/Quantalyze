## Deviations from Plan

**1. [Plan inconsistency] The trigger-call anchor cannot be carried verbatim.**
- **Found during:** Task 2.
- **Issue:** the plan says every `20261009120000` anchor is carried verbatim except the single-strategy `v_strategy_id::text` bridge-lock regex. But the other bridge anchor, `PERFORM sync_strategy_analytics_status(v_strategy_id, p_job_id);`, describes the same single-strategy call the multi-strategy loop replaces, so it cannot hold either.
- **Fix:** re-expressed it inside `v_failed_bridge_anchored` together with the lock regex (loop calls `(r_b.strategy_id, r_b.job_id)`; the own `(v_strategy_id AS strategy_id, p_job_id AS job_id)` pair anchored as the UNION ALL's first branch; the sorted `ORDER BY hashtext(d.strategy_id::text), d.strategy_id`; the `unnest(v_casc_strats, v_casc_jobs)` source). Recorded as re-expressed in the carry table.
- **Files modified:** the migration. **Commit:** `6217b91e4`.

**2. [Rule 2 - determinism] DISTINCT ON tie-break.** RESEARCH's skeleton orders the de-duplication by `u.strategy_id` only, which leaves the job id chosen per strategy arbitrary. Added an `ord` column (own pair 0, cascaded 1) and `u.job_id` to the DISTINCT ON order, so the strategy's own pair wins and otherwise the lowest failed job id. Outer sort unchanged. **Commit:** `6217b91e4`.

**3. [Rule 2 - robustness] Position pins.** The cascade and lock anchors also check position (as `v_done_lock_anchored` does), beyond the plan's bare presence regexes. **Commit:** `52210c054`.

**4. [Procedure] The plan's `<automated>` verify blocks use `set -- $P`, which does not word-split under the Bash tool's zsh.** Ran them from `bash` scripts (scratchpad), identical commands otherwise (same as plan 04's deviation 3).

Total deviations: 4 (1 plan inconsistency, 2 Rule 2 hardening, 1 procedure). No behaviour differs from the plan's intent.
