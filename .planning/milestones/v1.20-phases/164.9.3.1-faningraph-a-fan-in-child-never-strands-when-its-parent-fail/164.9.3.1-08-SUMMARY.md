## Deviations from Plan

### Deviations from the suggested twins (all inside the plan's allowance: "the executor measures every needle and may choose another production edit")

1. **FC8 narrows the REVOKE, it does not delete it.** The suggestion replaced the REVOKE with a comment. The migration's self-verify calls `_assert_no_public_execute` right after the helper anchor, and that call is not an `IF NOT v_<name>` line, so with the REVOKE gone PUBLIC keeps EXECUTE and the apply aborts. `FROM PUBLIC` only keeps PUBLIC revoked and leaves the lane's default-privilege grant to anon and authenticated, which is the observable FC8 reads.
2. **FC6 drops the filter in two places.** The suggestion named the level SELECT only. The level UPDATE re-checks `done_pending_children`, so a pending child in `v_kids` is still skipped; the twin has to drop both. Two production edits, no stand-down (no anchor reads that text).
3. **OD4 neuters the INSERT branch's assignment (`NULL;`) instead of removing the branch.** Removing the branch makes an INSERT reach `ELSIF OLD.match_decision_id`, and `OLD` is unassigned on INSERT, so every seed insert in every earlier arm would raise and no arm could be first.
4. **No stand-down where the suggestion expected one for OD3 and OD4**, because the anchors (`v_index_key_anchored`, `v_sync_owned_anchored`) read only the column name and the `TG_OP`/OLD tokens; the migration applied unmodified by the stand-down. Measured by the runner: both arms RED with own identity.
5. **Annotation placement.** The twin sits immediately above the IF guard of the arm's behaviour assertion. For OD2, OD5 and OD6 (whose first raise of the arm identity is the column-exists guard) it sits above the behaviour assertion instead (`IF v_ok <> 2 THEN`, `IF v_former IS NOT NULL THEN`). The grammar keys on the `arm` field, not on placement.
