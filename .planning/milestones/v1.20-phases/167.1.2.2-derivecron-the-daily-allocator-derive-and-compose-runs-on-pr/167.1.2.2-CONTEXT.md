### Founder decisions after the D-08 rehearsal (2026-10-07, AskUserQuestion; rehearsal UNCLEAN, plan 02 SUMMARY)
- **D-11:** the bybit key-mode derive failed because the key's IP allow-list does not include the backfill service's outbound address (the interactive service's is on it). The founder adds the backfill service's outbound address to that key's allow-list at the venue. The address is read live at the time and is NOT written into tracked text. The re-check is one key-mode derive for that key, which must read `done`.
- **D-12:** the `derive_allocator_equity` compose refusal (forward/backward self-check diverged at day-index 1024 of 1199, 1 of 8 composes) is root-caused BEFORE the cron is registered. If it is a code defect, it is fixed before plan 06. Plans 04/05/06 do not start until D-11's re-check and D-12's verdict are in.

- **D-13 (founder 2026-10-08, AskUserQuestion; debug .planning/debug/derivecron-compose-divergence.md follow-up):** opening flows are judged by whether the account started from zero, not by their position. A consecutive run of pre-window flows ending the day before the first return day, on a venue whose history reaches the account's start, is the normal opening shape when the capital the replay implies existed before the first flow (level on day 1 minus that day's flow) is within the D-14 band of zero, relative to the level on the window's first day. Such flows do NOT block. Outside the band, the curve blocks under an honest reason naming an unreconciled start (e.g. `inception_unreconciled`), not `OUT_OF_WINDOW_FLOW`. Pre-window flows not adjacent to the window, and flows after it, keep `OUT_OF_WINDOW_FLOW`. Today `OUT_OF_WINDOW_FLOW` fires on 14 of 14 PROD keys with flows.
- **D-14 (founder 2026-10-08):** the D-13 band is **0.01%** (1e-4) of the window's first-day level. Founder's own pick, tighter than the 1% recommended. Consequence to measure, not assume: the clean keys measured up to about 0.4%, so some may stay blocked under the honest reason at this band. The implementation reports per key which side of the band it lands on.

- **D-15 (founder 2026-10-08, AskUserQuestion; root cause in .planning/debug/derivecron-compose-divergence.md Follow-up 2):** D-13's check assumed a day with no stored return had zero P&L. Days the TWR writer drops (the funding day under `negative_nav_guard`, and flow-dominated days) still carry real P&L in NAV, so the check measured that P&L as "missing start capital" (account A: -0.355%, one ordinary day's P&L; its native §5 gate had already proven a zero start), and the replay's forced r = 0 on those days mis-states every earlier level by that P&L. Decision: the derive stores each dropped day's actual P&L (plus the §5 verdict where one exists) in `key_inputs`, and the compose (1) uses it on dropped days instead of r = 0, and (2) checks the real invariant `level(d0) - pnl(d0) - F(d0)` within D-14's 1e-4 band. D-13 and D-14 stand; this corrects D-13's formula, not its rule.
- **D-16 (founder 2026-10-08, AskUserQuestion after review round 2, "Fix both before the cron"): DEVIATION from the worst-MEDIUM-means-no-fixer rule.** Round 2 left two MEDIUMs: R2-WR-01 (confidence 6: a compose that reads a sibling key between its returns write and its `key_inputs` write marks the book untrustworthy with `KEY_INPUTS_MISMATCH`, and the sibling's corrective compose is dropped by the one-in-flight index, so the book stays untrustworthy until the next day) and R2-WR-02 (confidence 7: OKX key-mode levels before the anchor day carry the open-uPnL shift because the ccxt path stores no realized terminal). Both are fixed before the cron is registered, because R2-WR-01 threatens SC-3 directly and a PROD reading of it costs a full day per attempt.
- **D-17 (founder 2026-10-08, AskUserQuestion after the plan 05 re-rehearsal read UNCLEAN):** (a) the Bybit 'Unmatched IP' is answered by turning on Railway Static Outbound IPs for `quantalyze-analytics-backfill` (and `quantalyze-analytics`) and adding that address to the key's allow-list (founder action); Railway's live config showed no static egress on either service, so the egress address could change. The address stays out of tracked text. A single Bybit derive is re-run after, and must read `done`. (b) The Deribit+MT5 allocator's refused compose ('non-positive reconstructed equity … a flow dominates prior capital') is root-caused BEFORE the cron is registered ('Debug first'): debug note `.planning/debug/derivecron-compose-refusal.md`. Plan 06 (cron registration) waits on both.
- **D-18 (founder 2026-10-08, AskUserQuestion after the compose-refusal debug, `.planning/debug/derivecron-compose-refusal.md` on branch debug/derivecron-compose-refusal):** the refusal is a WRITER defect, not a compose one. Under cash_settlement the Deribit terminal wedge subtracts `options_session_upl` twice (Phase 131 REVIEW 'Residual #2', frozen), leaving a constant phantom BTC balance that becomes the whole level when the account is emptied; `native_nav`'s `is_dust` allowance let it pass inception. (a) The writer fix, a capped dust allowance and the re-baseline of affected Deribit returns go to a NEW phase in Wave 2 (not folded into DERIVECRON); its recompute joins Wave 5. (b) An emptied stretch (level within a small band of zero) composes as zero capital with no return, and the book continues on re-deposit; it is not refused and not degraded. That compose rule ships in the same new phase. (c) The daily cron is registered NOW (plan 06) without waiting: 4 of 7 owners read trustworthy v2 books; the Bybit owner reads INCOMPLETE until D-17(a) lands and the Deribit+MT5 owner reads REFUSED until the new phase ships. This overrides the runbook's UNCLEAN verdict for registration; the plan 05 verdict stays UNCLEAN as recorded.
