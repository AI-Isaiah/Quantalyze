<domain>
## Phase Boundary

Revoke TRIGGER and MAINTAIN from `anon` and `authenticated` on every `public` relation and in the `postgres` default privileges on `public`. A client role holding TRIGGER can attach code that a BYPASSRLS writer then runs (RLS audit HIGH, 164.9.7 review). PG17 MAINTAIN lets a role LOCK and VACUUM FULL a table, which is an outage. No user-facing behaviour changes.

</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

Carried from 164.9.7 D-01..D-04 by the booking's "same shape" (founder D-06, 164.9.7 CONTEXT):
- **D-01 (scope):** revoke TRIGGER and MAINTAIN from `anon` and `authenticated` on EVERY relation in `public` (tables, views, materialized views, partitioned tables, whatever the catalogue holds that grants either privilege), and `ALTER DEFAULT PRIVILEGES` for `postgres` on `public` so relations created later never grant either. `service_role` and `postgres` keep them.
- **D-02 (proof):** the migration carries a self-check that fails the apply if `anon` or `authenticated` still holds TRIGGER or MAINTAIN on any `public` relation afterwards. Catalogue only, never table data (TEST is schema-only, `[164.8-DATA-DEPENDENT-MIGRATION-ESCAPE]`).
- **D-03 (gate):** a SQL gate in `supabase/tests` asserts no `public` relation grants TRIGGER or MAINTAIN to `anon` or `authenticated`, and no `postgres` default-ACL row on `public` does. Shown RED once against a re-grant, and every census the new file moves is updated (CLAUDE.md: mutation floors, lint, censuses).
- **D-04 (measure):** before and after PROD readings (marker query first) of the per-role TRIGGER and MAINTAIN holder counts, counts only, recorded in the phase. The booking says to re-measure at plan time.

### Accepted residuals (carried, not re-decided)
- TRUNCATE on `storage.*` and `net.*` stays an accepted platform residual (164.9.7 D-06).
- If a `supabase_admin` default-ACL row on `public` grants TRIGGER or MAINTAIN, it is recorded the same way 164.9.7 recorded the TRUNCATE one (`postgres` cannot alter it). The read-only detector booked under 164.9.7 D-05 (`TRUNCREVOKE-ADMIN-DETECTOR-01`) is where it would be watched. Measure; do not assume either way.

### Founder decisions after research (AskUserQuestion, 2026-10-08)
- **D-07 (MAINTAIN proof):** the `sql-mutation` pg-lane is PG16, which has no MAINTAIN, so no gate arm there can bite for MAINTAIN. Accepted: TRIGGER gets real RED arms with twins; MAINTAIN is covered by compensating controls: (1) the migration's catalogue self-check checks MAINTAIN on every PG17 apply (TEST, PROD, the local-stack lane), behind `server_version_num >= 170000`; (2) one recorded manual RED on PG17; (3) a static vitest pin on `LANE_PG_VERSION` and on the MAINTAIN entry in the verb list; (4) a TODOS booking to move the pg-lane to PG17. This supersedes D-03's "each privilege has its own RED arm" for MAINTAIN only.
- **D-08 (lock premise):** revoking MAINTAIN closes VACUUM/CLUSTER/REINDEX/REFRESH MATERIALIZED VIEW but NOT a table-wide lock, which PG17 also allows to UPDATE/DELETE/TRUNCATE holders (measured). No artifact (migration header, ROADMAP goal, CHANGELOG) may claim the lock outage is closed; the ROADMAP goal sentence is corrected; the client lock-hold is booked as its own TODOS item.
- **D-09 (platform residual):** TRIGGER and MAINTAIN on `storage.*` and `net.*` are an accepted platform residual, as TRUNCATE was (164.9.7 D-06); the `supabase_admin` default-ACL row granting TRIGGER/MAINTAIN is recorded like the TRUNCATE one, and `TRUNCREVOKE-ADMIN-DETECTOR-01` is widened in TODOS to name all three verbs (the detector is not built here).
