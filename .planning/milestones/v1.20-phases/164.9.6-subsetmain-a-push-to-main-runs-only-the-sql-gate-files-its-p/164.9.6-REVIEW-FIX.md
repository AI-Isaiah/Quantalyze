**Status:** fixed: requires human verification (it changes gating logic)
**Applied fix:**
- **The gate.** `judgePush` returns `none` or `subset` only when the before-SHA's
  `predecessorVerdict` is ok. That function is imported from `classify-changed-paths.mjs`, not

  `judge` flags `predecessorOwed`, then `derivePush` reads the predecessor through its own explicit
  `readPredecessor` seam and judges again. `judge` stays pure.
- **The token.** In `ci.yml`, the `sql_gate_subset` step gets the job's existing `checks: read`
  `GH_TOKEN` through `env:` only. The contract test now pins `GH_TOKEN` to exactly two steps,
  `classify` and `sql_gate_subset`.

**Tests:**

  - "WR-03: a push narrows (subset or none) when predecessorVerdict proves the before-SHA green";
  - "WR-03: a red, pending, absent, failed, truncated or unread predecessor turns a would-be subset

- **End-to-end self-test row:** "WR-03 END TO END: gh reporting the before-SHA RED writes full…".

- **No API call on a FULL push:** an added assertion checks that a push already owed FULL never

## Time budget (flagged, not changed)

The `changed-paths` job has `timeout-minutes: 5`. On the last three `main` pushes it took 17 s, 19 s
and 17 s (CI runs `37119205242`, `37116307820` and `37115117611`). The self-tests take about 2.8 s
(subset) and 4.2 s (classify) locally.

Each predecessor reader makes up to two `gh api` calls, each with a 60 s timeout. On a docs-only
push, both `classify` and `sql_gate_subset` read the same SHA. The worst case is therefore about
240 s of lookups plus about 20 s of baseline, which is under 300 s but tight. A job timeout would
red `changed-paths` and skip every dependent job.

Having `classify` share its verdict through a step output would remove the double read. That
changes `classify`, which is outside the founder's scope, so it is not done here.
