## The new jobSlice regex

```ts
new RegExp(`^ {2}${job}:[ \\t]*(?:#[^\\n]*)?\\n([\\s\\S]*?)(?=\\n {2}[A-Za-z0-9_]|$(?![\\s\\S]))`, "m")
```

The not-found message is `` `${label}: ${job} job not found` ``, and `label` defaults to `"ci.yml"`. The existing two-argument call sites (`test-db-drift`, `frontend`) are unchanged.
