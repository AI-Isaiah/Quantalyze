# Phase 167.1.1.1 Plan 01: Spot mark-price rule in both runtimes Summary

**The spot mark-price rule now exists as a pure Python module and as exported TypeScript functions, and one 21-case hand-typed fixture is replayed whole by pytest and vitest.**

## Accomplishments

- `analytics-service/services/holding_marks.py` holds `MIN_PRICED_DAYS`, `PRICE_RULE_WITHHELD_REASONS`, `HoldingMarkContractError`, `DailyMark`, `HoldingMarks`, `daily_mark_prices`, `adjacent_daily_returns`, `spot_withheld_reason` and `classify_spot_marks`. It is pure (no I/O, no logging of row values) and passes `mypy --strict`.
- `holding-compare-adapter.ts` exports `classifySpotMarks`, `adjacentDailyReturns`, `HOLDING_MARK_WITHHELD_REASONS` and the types `HoldingMarkWithheldReason` and `HoldingMarks`. `analyzePriceSeries` now calls `adjacentDailyReturns`, so the adjacent-day loop exists once. `fetchHoldingCompareItem` routes its spot path through `classifySpotMarks`; the derivative "rebuilding" branch is untouched (plan 07).
- The fixture pins: latest `updated_at` wins (winner in the middle of the input), tie to the smallest lowercase key (`Key-B` vs `key-a`, same instant written two ways), a later zero mark never eclipsing a valid one, a negative mark as a gap, a missing day breaking the chain, the 29 / 30 priced-day boundary, every withheld reason, the purchase-at-constant-mark invariant, five read-contract errors and an unusable `updated_at`.
- The venue census (`crypto_venues`) pins `CRYPTO_VENUES` (Python) and `CRYPTO_EXCHANGES` (TypeScript) to one hand-typed list.
- Three independent pytest invariants: constant mark gives all-zero returns whatever the dollar path, gap-free returns telescope to last / first, returns are keyed by the later day.
