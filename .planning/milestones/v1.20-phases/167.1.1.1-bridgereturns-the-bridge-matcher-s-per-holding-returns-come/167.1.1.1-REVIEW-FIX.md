## Two weight notions, named

- **Scoring weights** (`ctx["portfolio_weights"]`): what `match_engine` blends the joined series with. Strategy allocations plus the priced holdings only; they sum to 1 over the joined series.
- **AUM share** (`holding_flags[*].weight`, local `aum_share` in `compute_holding_flags`): `aum_contribution_usd` over the full matcher AUM (withheld spot and derivative P&L included, D-01, D-16). It feeds the max-weight test.
