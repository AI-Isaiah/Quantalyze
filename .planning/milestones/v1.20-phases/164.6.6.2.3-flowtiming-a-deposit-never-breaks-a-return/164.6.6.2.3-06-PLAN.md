writer-mode roll applies. Research falsified D-14's premise for MT5 (RESEARCH Pitfall 1), so this
reading is RESEARCH Open Question 1, marked PENDING FOUNDER: the orchestrator puts it to the
founder, the answer is recorded in CONTEXT.md (and the ROADMAP) as a `**FLOWTIMING-Q1 (founder
YYYY-MM-DD):**` line, and every task below carries an automated precondition probe that exits

plans 05 and 07 change MT5 returns, so the phase does not ship with this plan skipped (the legacy
roll would mis-state MT5 key levels silently, Pitfall 1). PROD at planning time (2026-10-11, marker verified, counts only): 8 MT5 `key_inputs`
rows, 0 with the writer basis, 1 native-unit, 0 with unpriced days.
Output: MT5 basis stored for USD and hole-free native keys; a loud refusal path for the one shape
the compose cannot replay; pins on both readers of `WRITER_BASIS_VENUES`.
