# Phase 164.6.6.2.3 Plan 07: Day-0 inception for Deribit and full-history ccxt accounts Summary

A Deribit account's day 0, and the day 0 of every ccxt account whose P&L history starts inside the venue's flow coverage, is now its inception day: no return, no dust or negative flag. The capped-venue test is the existing DQ-02 coverage terminus read on the account's P&L history, applied at both ccxt sites through one helper.

## What changed

**Deribit (`native_nav.reconstruct_native_nav_and_twr`)**
- `chain_linked_twr(..., full_history=ledger.full_history)`, still no `timing`. The inception predicate (`t == 0 and full_history and flow_0 > 0 and abs(prev) <= floors.residual_abs_tol`) accepts the supplied `prev0_usd`, which is zero within tolerance on a full-history ledger by the section-5 gate. A later Deribit flow day stays at day level and sets `flow_timing_day_level`.
- The D-10 measurement is recorded beside `DayTiming` in `nav_twr.py` ("164.6.6.2.3 D-10 venue timing measurement"): MT5 both instants (intraday); Deribit day level on the FLOWTIMING-Q2 answer with the booked follow-up; OKX, Bybit, Binance, sFOX day level, each with its one-line reason; CSV no flows.

**The shared primitives**
- `nav_twr.ccxt_history_reaches_inception(venue, first_return_day, now_utc)` returns `flow_coverage_terminus_day(...) is None`. No second retention definition. The docstring states why the input is the P&L history (the flow fetch starts at the floor, so a flow-based verdict is True for every account) and that a history on or after the floor is full-history only together with the core predicate.
- `full_history: bool = False` on `trades_to_daily_returns_with_status` (forwarded at both core calls: the daily_pnl branch and the individual-fills branch) and on `combine_realized_and_funding`. Byte-identical by default.
- `combine_sfox_balance_history`: a comment at its core call states why it passes no `full_history`. Its day-0 base is the first observed balance and its day-0 flow is dropped (WR-01), so the zero-base condition cannot arise. CSV uploads carry no flows and the broker-to-CSV bridge re-runs no guard (D-10 comment).

**The two ccxt sites (`job_worker.py`)**
- `_ccxt_full_history_verdict(venue, realized, funding, now_utc)`: takes the earliest timestamp over `realized + funding_rows_to_daily_pnl_records(funding)` (the same combined input the combine receives, parsed as `pd.to_datetime(..., format="ISO8601", utc=True)` and keyed by its UTC date), returns False when there is no record, and otherwise `ccxt_history_reaches_inception(venue, <that day>, now_utc.replace(tzinfo=None))`.
- Single-key derive: `combine_realized_and_funding(..., full_history=_ccxt_full_history_verdict(venue, realized, funding, _now_utc))`, inside the existing `NavReconstructionError` try, with the derive's own `_now_utc`.
- `_reconstruct_ccxt_member`: the same keyword on its `asyncio.to_thread(combine_realized_and_funding, ...)` call, from the member's own `realized`, `funding` and `_now_utc`. The docstring names the shared verdict.
- Neither DQ-02 terminus call nor its evidence gate was touched.

## The capped-venue rule and its tests

Rule: a ccxt account is full-history exactly when `flow_coverage_terminus_day(venue, first_return_day=<first P&L day>, now_utc=<the site's own now>)` is None. Boundary tests (`test_nav_twr.py`) pin OKX day exactly at the floor (True) and one day before it (False), Bybit 365 (True) and 366 (False), plus 30/120 (OKX) and 300/400 (Bybit), Binance any age, an unknown venue, and in every case that the helper equals `flow_coverage_terminus_day(...) is None`.

Derive tests (`TestCcxtFullHistoryVerdictThroughTheDerive`, real combine behind a spy):
- Binance, first deposit on day 0, reconstructed zero base: verdict True, day 0 NaN, no dust/negative flag.
- OKX, realized P&L from 120 days ago (bills archive) and the only fetched flow 30 days ago: verdict False, day 0 measured. The test asserts every fetched flow is on or after the floor, so it fails under a flow-based verdict.
- OKX, P&L and deposit both 30 days ago: inception, no flag.
- Bybit, older unfetched deposit with a later top-up on day 0: verdict True by the rule (its P&L fetch bound is the same 365 days as its deposit floor, so True for every Bybit account) and day 0 is still measured because the base is real capital above `residual_abs_tol`. This is stated in the helper's docstring.
- Every fixture also asserts `returns.index[0]` equals the first P&L day the verdict used.

Stitched member tests (`test_stitch_composite_job.py`): a Binance member with a day-0 deposit on a zero base has no day-0 dust/negative guard in its member series, its meta, or the merged flags, and its verdict equals the single-key derive's on the same inputs; an OKX member with a 120-day P&L history keeps today's day 0 (verdict False). The two hand-composed byte-consistency references now pass the same verdict (`_jw._ccxt_full_history_verdict`), since they mirror the member's exact sequence.
