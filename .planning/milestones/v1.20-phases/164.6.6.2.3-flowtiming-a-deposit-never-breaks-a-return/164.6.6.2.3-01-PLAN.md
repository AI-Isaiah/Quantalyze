<objective>
Make the one arithmetic core measure a deposit day against the capital each position actually
traded on, recognise a full-history account's day 0 as its inception, and stop treating a large
flow as a reason to drop a return.

Purpose: D-02/D-03/D-04/D-09 (cohort sub-period return), D-05 (flow clause deleted), D-06 (pnl
and negative/dust guards kept), D-01/D-12 (inception rule, opt-in through `full_history`), D-07
(day-level fallback named as an annotation). This plan builds the mechanism; venues opt in later
(plan 05 MT5, plan 07 Deribit/ccxt). The retired key stays in `NAV_TWR_GUARD_KEYS` until plan 04
moves it to a carry-only legacy tuple together with its readers, so this wave never drops a stored
flag (D-14).

Output: `nav_twr.py` with `DayTiming`, `_timed_day_return`, the inception rule, two annotations;
an independent Fraction oracle and per-cohort D-06 tests. The pins in OTHER test files that
asserted the old flow-dominance emission move in plans 09 and 10 (wave 2, test-only), split out of
this plan for context budget; until they land those files are knowingly red, and this plan's
SUMMARY lists them.
</objective>
