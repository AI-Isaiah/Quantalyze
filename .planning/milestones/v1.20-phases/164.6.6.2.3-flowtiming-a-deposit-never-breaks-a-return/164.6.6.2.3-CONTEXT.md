# Phase 164.6.6.2.3: FLOWTIMING - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-10-11
**Status:** Ready for planning

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

Returns are measured against the capital that was in the account when each position was opened,
using the hour-and-minute timestamps of deposits, withdrawals and position opens. A deposit or
withdrawal never makes a day's return "uninterpretable", and a full-history account's first day is
its inception, not a data-quality defect. Today two paths in `analytics-service/services/nav_twr.py`
turn both into warnings and drop the day's return: `_guard_denominator` (`flow_dominated_guard`, and
the day-0 near-zero base labelled `dust_nav_guard` / `negative_nav_guard`) and the end-of-day flow
formula in `chain_linked_twr` (`(cur - prev - flow) / prev`).

Not in scope: `pnl_dominated_guard` and the negative-NAV guard on a real broken base (D-06); the
actual PROD recompute, which rides the Wave 5 consolidated recompute (D-11); allocator books (D-12).

</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Founder decisions booked at phase creation (2026-10-08, copied from ROADMAP)
- **D-01 (day 0):** "Fix it, own phase." On a full-history account (MT5 and ledger-complete venues),
  day 0 is the inception day. Its near-zero start is not a warning, whatever the noise's sign.
  Retention-windowed venues, where day 0's base is real capital, keep today's behaviour unless
  research shows otherwise.
- **D-02 (deposits and positions):** "The Return is based on the Capital that was in the Account
  When the Trade opened. The Strategy Looses at the Capital in the Account and then opened the
  Position. Then deposit. That is Idee, as it has notjing to do with the Open Position. When Position
  closed relative Performance is relative to what was in the sccount at Time of opening the
  Position." And: "Deposits and Trade/position opens have timestamps with Hour and Minutes. Use it."
  So a position's P&L is measured against the account capital at the position's open time. A
  deposit or withdrawal changes the base only for positions opened after it. A large flow is never
  by itself a reason to drop a return or warn. The current `flow_dominated_guard` rule is replaced,
  not tuned.

### How D-02 becomes a daily return (founder 2026-10-11, "Accept all")
- **D-03:** a day with a deposit or withdrawal is split at each flow's timestamp. A position counts
  against the capital in the account when it was opened: start-of-day NAV plus the flows
  timestamped before the open. The day's return links those sub-periods (a true time-weighted
  return), never an end-of-day flow assumption where timestamps exist.
- **D-04:** positions carried overnight belong to the start-of-day base; their open-time capital is
  already carried by the earlier days' compounding. The series stays an account return, not a
  trade-by-trade return.
- **D-05:** `flow_dominated_guard` is deleted. A flow, however large, never drops a day's return and
  never warns.
- **D-06:** `pnl_dominated_guard` (P&L at least 10x the base) and the negative-NAV guard on a real
  broken base are unchanged.

### Where a venue has no time-of-day (founder 2026-10-11, "Accept all")
- **D-07:** a flow or a position open without an hour and minute uses today's day-level rule (the
  flow counts at the end of the day), named per strategy as "flow timing at day level", an info
  note, never a warning. No time is ever invented.
- **D-08:** every timestamp is converted to UTC, and days are UTC days (the existing dailies
  convention).
- **D-09:** a flow and a position open with the exact same timestamp: the flow counts first (a
  deposit funds the trade). Order by the full precision the venue gives.
- **D-10:** research measures, per venue (MT5, Deribit, OKX, Bybit, Binance, sFOX), whether flows
  and position opens each carry intraday timestamps. A venue uses intraday timing only where both
  exist; otherwise D-07.

### Day 0 and rollout (founder 2026-10-11, "Accept all")
- **D-11:** an account is full-history (D-01) when its first funding day falls inside the venue's
  flow coverage: every MT5 and CSV account, a no-cap venue, or a capped venue whose first deposit is
  newer than the cap, through the existing `flow_retention_floor`.
- **D-12:** day 0 is measured under D-02: positions opened after the first deposit use that deposit
  as their base, so day 0 gets a real return. With no timestamps, day 0 is the inception day: no
  return and no warning.
- **D-13:** the phase measures before and after for every affected PROD strategy with a dry run (no
  writes), marker first. The real PROD recompute rides the Wave 5 consolidated recompute, consistent
  with 166.5's "Force a recompute (in Wave 5)".
- **D-14:** the allocator compose inherits the change through the per-key daily returns; no separate
  compose change. The TS readers stop rendering the retired flag's warning copy. Stored rows keep
  their old flags until the Wave 5 recompute.

**D-15 (founder 2026-10-12, AskUserQuestion, review F1): "Fix it in this phase (Recommended)".** A day-level flow day whose flow is large against the prior NAV (the retired flow-dominance condition) gets a counted flag that drives the existing "returns can be extreme" factsheet caveat, so an inflated end-of-day return (the dry run measured +529% on Deribit) is visibly marked. ⚠️ DEVIATION from the review rule "worst MEDIUM ⇒ no fixer": the round-1 worst finding was MEDIUM; the founder chose the fix. Recorded here and in the ROADMAP.

**D-16 (founder 2026-10-12, AskUserQuestion, review WR-02): "D-02 literal (Recommended)".** A cohort's base is the capital in the account when the position opened, INCLUDING P&L realized earlier the same day, not D-03's start-of-day NAV plus earlier flows. Required invariants: a zero-amount flow is a no-op, and the day return is continuous as the flow tends to 0. Supersedes D-03's wording where the two differ.

**D-17 (founder 2026-10-13, AskUserQuestion, review round 2 R2-02): "Telescoping base (Recommended)". Amends D-16.** D-16's two required invariants conflict with its literal base whenever an earlier cohort is still open at a later open (D-04's overnight carry is the common case): a $0.000001 flow moved a measured day from 3.0000% to 3.0200%. The base becomes `B_k = B_{k-1} + P_{k-1} + F_k`, where `P_{k-1}` is the earlier cohort's full day P&L, realized or not. It telescopes to `(prev + sum P)/prev` as every flow tends to 0, in every shape, with no mark-to-market input; the earlier docstring reason ("needs mark-to-market") was wrong. Cost, accepted: an earlier position's later-realized P&L counts as capital at the later open (an approximation of equity). Measured difference from the literal base: 11.000% vs 10.991% on a 10x deposit. Required: a convention-free property test, `r(F = eps)` against the no-flow `sum P / prev` over shapes that include overlap, RED on the literal base.

**D-18 (founder 2026-10-13, AskUserQuestion, review round 3 R3-01): "Accept and record (Recommended)". Records a limit of D-17.** The D-06 guards judge each cohort on its telescoped base, which includes an earlier carried position's loss. When that loss approaches the account (about 90% or more), a vanishingly small flow creates a later cohort whose base falls under the dust, P&L-dominance or negative guard, and the day turns NaN where the no-flow day was measured. Measured at prev 10,000: an overnight position at -9,950 plus a +1 trade reads -0.9949 without a flow and NaN (`dust_nav_guard`) with a 1e-6 flow; -9,000 plus +10,500 reads +0.15 and NaN (`pnl_dominated_guard`); -10,200 plus -50 reads -1.025 and NaN (`negative_nav_guard`). The broken chain drops earlier history (`twr_chain_broken`). The guards are kept on the telescoped base, because judging them on deposits-only capital would let a near-zero base publish an extreme return. The break is flagged, never silent.
