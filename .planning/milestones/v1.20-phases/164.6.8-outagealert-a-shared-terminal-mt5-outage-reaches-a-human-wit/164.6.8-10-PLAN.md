CONTEXT D-16, quoted: "**Under D-15, a RECOVERED notice counts as delivered once its Sentry capture succeeds.** The
verifier found that `ops_alert.py` credits a recovered notice only on a 2xx from the founder email (WR-R3-01). With
email unconfigured, a recovered notice could never be credited and would retry on every healthy reading: a Sentry
warning, an ERROR log and a failing email POST each time, until Resend exists. Founder call: \"Credit recovery via
Sentry\". Email still sends whenever Resend is configured. This ships as a gap-closure plan in this phase before it
closes."

CONTEXT D-15, quoted: "**Sentry is the only live paging channel for now.**"

Measured at planning (head `bd29ae80b`): `page()` in `analytics-service/services/ops_alert.py` sets `sentry_counts`
from `sentry_ok` AND the event being `outage`, so a recovered capture with an event id is not credited, and
`_settle_window` releases the window when the email also fails. The six affected suites pass at 251 tests.

Purpose: under D-15 the founder email cannot deliver, so today an MT5 recovery leaves its `ops_alert_open` record
open for ever and re-sends on every healthy reading. After this plan, a recovery is credited once Sentry has it, the
record closes, and the email is still attempted (and still logged loud when it fails), once per window.

Output: a one-line credit fix in `page()`, corrected docstrings in `ops_alert.py` and `ops_alert_open.py`, the three
WR-R3-01 tests rewritten to the D-16 direction, new caller-level tests for both callers, a Sentry-failure guard, and
the RED / GREEN / neutered / restored record in the SUMMARY.

⚠️ KNOWN LIMIT, recorded and not fixed here (founder decision D-16 credits it anyway). The live Sentry rule
"Ops alert: outage (164.6.8)" filters `ops_alert_event=outage`. A recovered capture is a warning-level event on its
own issue, so until that rule is widened (or a second rule added) a credited recovered notice NOTIFIES NOBODY: it is
visible in Sentry, not pushed. Changing the rule is out of scope for this plan. It belongs with the email channel,
`164.6.8-OPS-ALERT-EMAIL-UNCONFIGURED` (owner: the founder): either the email is configured, or the rule is widened,
before a recovered notice reaches a human by push. That entry was written by plan 09 on branch
`bk/164.6.8-golive` and is NOT on this branch's `TODOS.md` (measured: 0 hits at `bd29ae80b`), so this plan edits no
`TODOS.md`; the routing sentence is the orchestrator's bookkeeping when that branch lands. The SUMMARY carries the
limit verbatim under "Known limits".

Out of scope (do not touch): the Sentry alert rule, Resend configuration, `164.6.8-WR-R4-01-SENTRY-CREDIT-INSIDE-RULE-INTERVAL`
and `164.6.8-R4-SFH-F1-DUPLICATE-RECOVERY` (both booked in `TODOS.md`), any SQL or migration, any TypeScript (the
