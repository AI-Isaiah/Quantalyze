## Known limits

The live Sentry rule "Ops alert: outage (164.6.8)" filters `ops_alert_event=outage`. A recovered capture is a warning-level event on its own issue, so until that rule is widened (or a second rule added) a credited recovered notice NOTIFIES NOBODY: it is visible in Sentry, not pushed. Changing the rule is out of scope for this plan. It belongs with the email channel, `164.6.8-OPS-ALERT-EMAIL-UNCONFIGURED` (owner: the founder): either the email is configured, or the rule is widened, before a recovered notice reaches a human by push. That entry was written by plan 09 on branch `bk/164.6.8-golive` and is NOT on this branch's `TODOS.md` (measured: 0 hits at `bd29ae80b`), so this plan edits no `TODOS.md`; the routing sentence is the orchestrator's bookkeeping when that branch lands.

Observation, not a fix: D-16 decides a Sentry-credited recovery inline, so the held recovery wait that `164.6.8-R4-SFH-F1-DUPLICATE-RECOVERY` describes now arises only when the Sentry capture fails. That entry stays open.
