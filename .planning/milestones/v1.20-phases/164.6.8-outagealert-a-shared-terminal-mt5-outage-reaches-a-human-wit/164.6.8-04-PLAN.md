<objective>
Make a shared jobs-terminal outage page the founder, per D-04, D-05, D-06 and D-13. Today the monitor's blind run
rises only from INFO to WARNING (below the Sentry event level) and nothing outside its module reads it; a measured
`dark` reading (code -6) resets that counter, so a logged-out terminal never escalates at all. This plan adds a
separate outage-run tracker, keyed by terminal role, that counts every reading which is not a measured
`authorized`, pages at the EXISTING `blind_run_escalation_threshold(poll_interval_s)` (no new number, D-04), sends
one recovered notice on the first measured healthy reading (D-06), and attaches the pre-recycle wedge evidence to
the page (D-13).

Purpose: SC-1, "a shared-terminal outage reaches a human without one clicking a button".
Output: `services/mt5_outage_alert.py`, its hook in `record_mt5_session_reading`, the evidence stash in the
ipc-fault escalation, and tests. Plan 06 reuses the tracker for the validation terminal.

Sizing note: the estimate (120k) is above the 100k target. It is kept as one plan because the three tasks share one
new module and one state model (outage run, recovery, evidence stash), and splitting it would put the tracker's
state contract across two executors with a hand-off between them. Most of the cost is reading `mt5_relogin.py` and
`mt5_session_episodes.py`, which a split would pay twice.
</objective>
