### CR-01 = SFH-01 (HIGH): a chain-broken row's stored CAGR bypassed the card's 0.95-year gate

**Files modified:** `src/lib/factsheet/og-metrics.ts`, `src/lib/factsheet/og-metrics.test.ts`,
`src/app/api/og/factsheet/[id]/route.tsx`, `src/app/api/og/factsheet/[id]/route.test.tsx`
**Commit:** 134f353dd
**Status:** fixed: requires human verification (a display-policy rule, which the syntax gates cannot judge)

**Applied fix:**
- The route's embed select now projects `data_quality_flags`. `AnalyticsEmbed` carries it, and the
  route forwards it to `computeOgHeadline`.
- `OgPersistedScalars` gains `data_quality_flags?: unknown`. When the stored CAGR is the one shown and
  the flags carry `twr_chain_broken === true` or `insufficient_window === true`, the card renders CAGR
  as NaN ("—"). The flags are narrowed the same way `composite-read-path.ts` reads them (`=== true`
  per flag).
- Sharpe and max drawdown are untouched. A computed CAGR (stored key absent) covers the full series,
  so the flags do not apply to it.
- The docblock states why the card hides here while the factsheet shows the figure with its
  "headline covers from" caveat: the card has no room for the caveat (SC4, "says why").

**Tests:**
- `og-metrics.test.ts`: `twr_chain_broken` and `insufficient_window` cases over a 400-day series with a
  stored `cagr: 0.6` expect CAGR NaN, and the stored Sharpe 1.5 and max drawdown -0.1 still shown.
  Negative controls (flags absent, null, empty, both false, a truthy non-boolean `"true"`) expect the
  stored `0.6`, so a neuter that hid CAGR unconditionally would go RED.
- `route.test.tsx` O1e: the select carries `data_quality_flags`, the persisted argument carries the
  flag, and the card shows "2.34" and "-8.9%" with exactly one "—" (no "+56.7%").

**Neuters:**
- Both flag clauses in `og-metrics.ts` set to `=== "neutered"`: the two flag cases went RED (2 failed,
  23 passed). Restored, 25/25 green.
- The route's `data_quality_flags: analytics?.data_quality_flags` pass-through deleted: O1e went RED.
  Restored, green.

### SFH-02 (HIGH, pre-existing): a failed strategy read rendered a generic card and cached it for a day

**Files modified:** `src/app/api/og/factsheet/[id]/route.tsx`, `src/app/api/og/factsheet/[id]/route.test.tsx`
**Commit:** 7b03563d2
**Status:** fixed

**Applied fix:**
- The route checks `res.error` after `maybeSingle()` (supabase-js does not throw on a query error).
- A small `reportOgFailure(stage, id, err)` logs with `console.error` unconditionally, then reports to
  Sentry through `after(() => captureToSentry(...))`, with tags `route: "api/og/factsheet"` and
  `stage: "read" | "compute"` and `extra.strategy_id`. It uses `shouldCaptureNow` per stage so an
  incident does not burn the Sentry quota. This follows the BTC benchmark route's pattern. Scheduling is
  wrapped in try/catch so the route still cannot 500.
- Three failure paths set `failed`: a PostgREST error, a thrown read, and a thrown headline compute.
  The card they produce is sent `Cache-Control: no-store` (the BTC route's `ERROR_CACHE_CONTROL`
  precedent). A genuine not-found (no row, no error) and a healthy card keep the long
  `public, max-age=3600, s-maxage=86400, stale-while-revalidate=604800`.

**Tests (`route.test.tsx`, new `describe` block):**
- F1: a PostgREST error (`57014`) is logged, captured with `stage: "read"` and the strategy id, and
  sent `no-store`.
- F2 (control): no row and no error keep the long cache and capture nothing. This is the case a
  naive "short whenever data is null" fix would fail.
- F3 (control): a healthy card keeps the long cache.
- F4: a thrown read is captured with `stage: "read"` and sent `no-store`.
- F5: a thrown compute is captured with `stage: "compute"`, renders three "—", and is sent `no-store`.
- The file now mocks `next/server`'s `after` (runs the callback at once) and `captureToSentry`, and
  resets the capture throttle in `beforeEach`, as `src/app/api/benchmark/btc/route.test.ts` does.

**Neuters:**
- Tests written first and run against the unfixed route: F1, F4, F5 RED; F2, F3 green.
- `if (res.error)` changed to `if (res.error && false)`: F1 RED. Restored.
- Cache ternary forced to the long value: F1, F4, F5 RED. Restored, all green.
