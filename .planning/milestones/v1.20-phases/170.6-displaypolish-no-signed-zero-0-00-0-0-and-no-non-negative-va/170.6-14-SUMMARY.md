# Phase 170.6 Plan 14: strategy-v2 headline and trade panels on the one sign rule Summary

The two /strategy/[id]/v2 panels that formatted and coloured on their own now delegate to display-sign: a figure that rounds to zero prints unsigned and neutral, tones follow the rounded sign, and Max DD is red only when it rounds below zero and never green.

## What changed

- `HeadlineMetricsPanel.tsx`: `fmtPct` is `formatUnsignedPercent(v, 2)`, `fmtRatio` is `formatRatio(v, 2)`, `signColor` maps `signTone(v, 2, 100)`. Max DD, previously unconditionally `text-negative`, takes `drawdownColor` = `drawdownTone(v, 2, 100)`. A non-finite value now reads the dash (before: `NaN%`).
- `TradeAndPositionPanel.tsx`: `fmtNum` and `fmtPct` build text with `formatFixedSigned` at their current dp (2 and 1); `null` for missing is kept. The four `negative` flags use `isNegativeRatio` over `signTone(v, 2)`.
- No private sign or rounding rule remains in either panel.
