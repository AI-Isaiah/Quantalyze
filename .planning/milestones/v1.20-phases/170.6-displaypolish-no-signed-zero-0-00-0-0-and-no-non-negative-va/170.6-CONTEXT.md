## Phase Boundary

The numbers the factsheet, its OG card and the comparator panel print. Three defects:

1. **Signed zero.** A value that rounds to zero prints as `-0.00` or `-0.0%`.
2. **Wrong tone.** A non-negative value is drawn red.
3. **Short comparator overlap.** An overlap too short to annualize still prints annualized figures (SPX annualized over 5 days gave a Sharpe of 10.24). The paired-floor sentence also does not say when the comparator's prices end.

## Implementation Decisions

- **D-01 (founder 2026-10-13):** 170.6 is split in three. This phase is NUMBERS only (see Phase Boundary).
- **D-02 (founder):** A value that rounds to zero at its display precision prints unsigned and in a neutral tone. `-0.00`, `-0.0%` and `+0.0%` never appear.
- **D-03 (founder):** Red and green follow the sign of the ROUNDED value, never the raw one. A raw `-0.0004` shown as `0.00` is neutral.
- **D-04 (founder):** Max drawdown is never green.
- **D-05 (founder):** There is one rule, in one place. `src/lib/factsheet/display-sign.ts` owns it. `src/app/factsheet/[id]/v2/format.ts`, `src/components/strategy-v2/format.ts` and `src/lib/utils.ts` delegate to it and do not re-implement it.
- **D-06 (founder):** Charts and text labels follow the same rule: axis ticks, tooltips, the OG card and KPI tiles. Heatmap cell FILLS are unchanged. They encode magnitude, not a printed sign.
- **D-07 (founder):** The comparator annualization floor is a **90-day** overlap.
  - Below the floor, the annualized set reads "—". That set is Sharpe, Sortino, Calmar, CAGR and annualized volatility.
  - Cumulative return and max drawdown stay.
  - A test must fail on today's behaviour (5-day SPX gives Sharpe 10.24).
- **D-08 (founder):** When the comparator ends before the strategy's window, the paired-floor sentence adds "{CN} prices end Mon D.". CN is the comparator's display name and the date is the comparator's last price day.

### Claude's Discretion
- Where the 90-day constant lives. It needs one definition and must be imported, not restated.
- Test shape. Each rule needs a test that fails on today's output. Expected values must be typed by hand, never computed by the code under test.
### Orchestrator calls on the planner's judgment points (2026-10-13)
- **O-1:** `utils.ts` delegation changes every page (`formatPercent` has 114 call sites). This is accepted, because D-02 says a signed zero "never appear[s]", not "never appears on factsheets". Tests that pinned "+0.00%" move with a D-02 note and are never deleted.
- **O-2:** `metricColor` takes a required unit. A rounded zero is neutral and NaN/null is muted (today NaN reads red). Accepted.
- **O-3:** A TimeSeriesChart daily-return bar whose value rounds to zero is drawn muted. D-06 exempts only heatmap/correlation FILLS; a bar is a chart mark that prints a value, so D-03 applies.
- **O-4:** The BatchDPanels own-book "Max DD (blend vs book)" DELTA keeps its favourability colouring on the rounded sign. D-04 governs a drawdown VALUE. A delta says which side is shallower, and green there means "better", not "a positive drawdown".
- **O-5:** The 90-day overlap counts calendar days from the comparator's first to its last covered date, not observations. A selected range shorter than 90 days also shows "—" for the comparator's annualized figures, with no reason line (copy belongs to 170.6.2).
