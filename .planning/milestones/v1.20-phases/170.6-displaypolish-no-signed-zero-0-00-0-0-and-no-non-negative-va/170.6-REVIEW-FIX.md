## R3-SFH-3: `ternaryTail` truncated silently and ended on a comma or apostrophe in JSX text

**Files:** `display-sign-single-source.test.ts`
`ternaryTail` is replaced by `readTail(code, from, cap)`, which returns `{ text, capped }`.
- The end of a read is structural, by bracket depth: a `;` or `,` at depth 0, or a closer with no opener in the tail. Strings and template strings are skipped. JSX is understood: inside an element's children the text is opaque (a comma in `<p>Up, up</p>` and an apostrophe in `<p>Don't</p>` mean nothing), only `{` and `<` do; attribute `{...}` expressions are skipped whole so a `=>` does not end a tag; a `<` is a tag only when the previous significant character cannot end an operand, so `a < b` stays a comparison.
- A read that reaches its cap is a finding in R1, R2 and the R7 uses (`... the read reached its cap and was not read whole`), as the R7 right side already was. The `if (...) return` form no longer has its own silent 200-character cut either: it goes through `readTail`.
