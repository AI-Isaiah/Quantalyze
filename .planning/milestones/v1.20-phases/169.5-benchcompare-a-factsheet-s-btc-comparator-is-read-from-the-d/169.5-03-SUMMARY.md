# Phase 169.5 Plan 03: Comparator coverage caption and fill-free method wording Summary

The comparator picker now dates a comparator whose prices end before the strategy ("BTC prices through Jul 25, 2024"), says "BTC prices unavailable" on a failed read, and every v2 factsheet site that claimed a benchmark was carried forward now describes the paired-interval rule instead.

## What was built

**Task 1 (tracer): the dated caption.** `ComparatorPicker` renders a caption under the chips for the active comparator, read from `payload.comparators[key].through` against the last entry of `payload.dates`. It uses the ISO-string comparison `buildComparatorBlock` already uses for its `pastThrough` windows. The three states:

- `through` earlier than the strategy's last date: "<BTC|SPX> prices through <Mon D, YYYY>".
- `through` null (the unavailable form, 169 D-52): "<label> prices unavailable".
- `through` absent (a hand-built block, 169 D-21), full coverage, or no comparator active: nothing.

The caption uses the existing micro mono muted style (the "Compare to" span's classes). The picker root became a `flex flex-col` wrapper, and `role="group"` stays on the chip row.

**Task 2: the Mandate panels.**
- Strategy Thesis now reads: "Each comparator's return is taken over the strategy's own intervals from the comparator's own closes, and is not measured past its last close."
- The Terms panel's Bench frequency now reads: "Daily close, over each strategy interval".
- `grep -c 'forward-filled' MandatePanels.tsx` = 0.
- The record-length parenthesis and the Sample size Term are unchanged. The new test pins them with the literal rendered today, "0.45 years, 166 daily observations".

The wording stays inside what D-64 allows. It does not claim a zero first day, a fill across a missing date, or that every interval enters the benchmark-relative metrics.

**v9 lineage line.** The 169.5-02 clause named only the carried BTC prices and their `through` and `dropped`. 169.5-02-SUMMARY also records comparator `cumulative` / `cumVsBench` / `volMatched` going null past coverage, plus rolling statistics on the comparator's own basis. A stale v8 entry lacks those too, so the clause now names them. Only the comment changed:
- `grep -c factsheet-v2-payload-v9` = 1
- the `git diff -U0 ... | grep -c 'unstable_cache\|computedAt\],'` check = 0
