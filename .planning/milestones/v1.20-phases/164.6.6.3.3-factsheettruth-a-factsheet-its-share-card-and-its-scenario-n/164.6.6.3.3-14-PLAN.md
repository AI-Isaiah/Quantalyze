<objective>
Close the one verification gap of Phase 164.6.6.3.3 recorded in `164.6.6.3.3-VERIFICATION.md` `gaps:` (D-15 extended, founder 2026-10-15, AskUserQuestion answer "Rail follows the strip"): on a Withheld row at full history with no what-if, the rail's section IV "Alpha (ann)" row reads "—" with no accent, through the same `isHeadlineWithheld` predicate the KPI strip uses; a zoom window keeps its own slice joint (D-78).

Purpose: plan 13 made the KPI strip's alpha and IR cells read "—" on a Withheld row (D-15), but `BenchmarkMetricsBody` in `MetricsColumn.tsx` still prints the Alpha (ann) row from the whole-record joint (`v(j => pctSigned(j.alpha, 2))`, `accent={joint != null}`) without consulting the headline verdict. So the same page shows "—" for alpha in the strip and a numeric, accented, whole-record alpha in the rail. The whole-record joint compounds across the very break the Withheld note names.

Output: a rail gate in `MetricsColumn` and an `alphaWithheld` prop on `BenchmarkMetricsBody`, plus render tests seen RED on today's code, GREEN after, and RED again under a neuter restored from a byte backup.

Decisions taken in this plan (stated so the executor does not re-decide them):
- How the rail knows the scope (cited by symbol): `MetricsColumn` already has `selected` (`scope.kind === "selected"` from `useWindowedView(payload)`) and `railBasis` (`useBasisOrCash()`). It has no leverage read today. The strip's `leverageApplied` is `leverageApplies(payload, basis, useAppliedLeverage())`, both exported from `basis-context.tsx`; the rail derives its own the same way with `railBasis`. `useAppliedLeverage` reads `LeverageContext` without throwing and degrades to L=1 outside a provider, so a rail mounted alone (the test's `renderColumn`) is safe.
- The leverage term is kept for literal parity with the strip even though it is provably false on a Withheld row: `leverageEligibleFor` in `basis-context.tsx` returns false whenever `dataQuality.twrChainBroken === true`, and `isHeadlineWithheld` requires that flag. Mirroring it means a future change to leverage eligibility moves the strip and the rail together.
- Which arm carries the prop: only the `jointCmp.joint ? (...)` arm of section IV, the only arm that can print a numeric alpha. The native-unit arm and the paired-floor arm already pass `joint={null}` (every row "—"). The flat-leg case is the same arm; its alpha is already NaN and reads "—", and its reason line is left as is (scope is the Alpha row only).
- Scope: the Alpha (ann) row only. Beta, Correlation, R², Information Ratio, Treynor, Tracking Error and both captures keep the whole-record figure. The rail's Information Ratio row therefore keeps its whole-record figure on a Withheld row while the strip's IR cell reads "—"; D-15 extended names only Alpha (ann), so this plan does not change it and the orchestrator surfaces it to the founder.
- Copy: no new sentence. The rail's Withheld note (`headlineCoverageNote` "main" slot) is unchanged.
</objective>
