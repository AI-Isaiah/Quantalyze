## Peer Percentile max_dd leak and its fix

`computePeerPercentile` guarded a non-finite Sharpe and Sortino (D7) but not max_dd. On a Withheld api row the read path nulls `max_drawdown` (`BASIS_KPI_MAP`), `overlayBasisScalars` makes it NaN, and `percentileRank(NaN, ...)` counted no peer as `<= NaN` and returned 0, which `PercentileBar` printed as "0th" (worst in cohort). Fixed in `peer-cohort.ts`: `max_dd: Number.isFinite(stratMaxDd) ? percentileRank(...) : NaN`, with a comment citing D7 and this plan. Pinned by a unit test (with a finite contrast) and by a build-level test through `buildFactsheetPayload` with the seven redacted scalars.
