## What changed

- `buildComparatorBlock` takes an optional trailing `spanStart` (default null: every existing call is byte-identical, and `build-payload.test.ts.snap` did not move). When set it computes `summarySince` with `compute()` over `coveredReturns`/`coveredDates` from the first covered date `>=` the start, on the same `benchPeriodsPerYear`, through the same `summaryOf` mapper as `summary` (so the same past-coverage nulling of the five window fields; `summary` itself now goes through that mapper, key order unchanged).
- `deriveSeriesBundle` gained the optional `headlineCoversFrom` arg and passes it to both comparator blocks (BTC and SPX; the unavailable BTC form and `none` are untouched). `buildFromBuildableSeries` computes `suffixStart` once (`twrChainBroken === true` and a string `headlineCoversFrom`) and hands it to `withSuffixWindows` (as before) and to the CASH bundle only. The MTM and smoothed bundles and `rederiveArgs` (leverage, selected range) never receive it.
- `MetricsColumn`: `headlineBenchSource(dataQuality, basis, selected, block)` returns the Withheld `null`, a Dated block's `summarySince`, or `block.summary`. Its gates are the note helper's (cash basis, chain-broken, full history). Cumulative Return, CAGR, Ann. Volatility, Sharpe, Sortino, Calmar, the month/YTD/3M/6M/1Y rows and Max Drawdown read `hb`; Win Rate, Profit Factor and Longest DD still read `b`.
- Task 2: `benchShown = cmpKey !== "none"` is passed at the S2, S3 and S4 call sites. S1 and S5 never carry the clause.

## Deviations from Plan

**1. [Interpretation] Dated row, `summarySince` absent.** The plan said "on a Dated row, `jointCmp.summarySince`". A Dated payload viewed through a re-derived bundle (leverage) has no such member, because its strategy figures are whole-record there too. `headlineBenchSource` falls back to `summary` then (`summarySince !== undefined ? summarySince : summary`), so such a view still compares two whole-record figures. A cash block with a span start but no covered comparator return on or after it carries `summarySince: null` and prints dashes. Pinned in the compute tests ("key present and null") and by the N3 neuter.

**2. [Scope] Payload cache key not bumped again.** Plan 02 already bumped `factsheet-v2-payload-v12` to `v13` in this phase's unmerged range, so no cached payload predating the phase can render under the new notes; a new optional member on a v13 payload needs no further bump within the same release. Verified: `grep` of the key shows `v13` in `page.tsx` and its two pinning tests.

**3. [Process] Pre-commit allow-list.** The `agent-*` branch-namespace assertion was not applied because the orchestrator designated branch `wt/164.6.6.3.3-p10` (same note as plans 01, 04, 08 and 09); the protected-default-branch check passes.

No other deviation.

## Known limits (recorded, not fixed)

- A comparator whose prices are unavailable (BTC with no stored closes) is `cmpKey !== "none"`, so the notes say "in both columns" while every benchmark cell is already a dash. That is the plan's exact condition; the dashes are not a wrong figure.
- A Dated row viewed at a non-default leverage re-derives both legs on the whole record, while the strip-level note still speaks of a suffix; this is plan 04's pre-existing behaviour and unchanged here (the benchmark column is at least consistent with the strategy column in that view).
- `comparator-block.ts` slices the comparator's COVERED returns by the same `>=` date predicate `withSuffixWindows` applies to the strategy, so a malformed non-date `headlineCoversFrom` string yields `summarySince: null` (dashes) exactly as it yields null strategy windows; the note helper already prints nothing for it.
