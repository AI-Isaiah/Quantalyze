## Deviations from Plan

1. **[Rule 3 - blocking, scope] `display-sign.ts` and `display-sign.test.ts` modified (not in `files_modified`).** The plan says the IR cell takes "the ratio formatter at 2dp and the tone at 2dp", but plan 06's only tone function, `percentTone`, scales by 100 and prints percent precision. Passing an IR through it would round at the wrong place. I added `ratioTone(v, dp = 2)` (tone from `Number(v.toFixed(dp))`, matching `formatRatio`) with its unit tests, rather than parsing `formatRatio`'s string in the view. Additive; nothing existing changed.
2. **Branch namespace.** The worktree branch is `wt/164.6.6.3.3-p09`, outside the executor's `agent-*` allow-list (same as plans 06 and 08). HEAD was not protected, not detached, and the toplevel was the assigned worktree; committed there.
3. **Section IV test id for the reason slot.** The native-unit and paired-floor arms keep `joint-floor-reason` (their existing contract); only the flat-leg arm uses `joint-flat-leg-reason`. The UI-SPEC's "`joint-*-reason`" family is thus three-way across arms, one per arm.
