## What the rules read (and how)

- Step splitter: `^ {6}- ` starts a step; a non-comment line shallower than 6 ends it; job tracked from `^  key:` under `jobs:`. The step limit is read from either `^ {8}timeout-minutes:` or the first-line form `^ {6}- timeout-minutes:` (the nightly demo-pdf step uses the latter).
- R1 command position = line start, `&&`, `||`, `;`, `|`, `then/else/do`, `( { !`; prefixes `sudo`, `env`, `nohup`, `exec`, `command`, flags (including `-u user`), bare `VAR=x`; apt options including `-o Key=Val`; backslash continuations joined; comment lines dropped.
- R6 default budget is parsed from `DEFAULT_BUDGET=` in `scripts/ci-apt.sh` (400), not restated. Real tree: psql 400+60=460 <= 480; pg_cron 360+60=420 <= 420 (exact boundary).

## Known limits (stated, not hidden)

- R1 reads workflow `run:` text only; a `timeout N apt-get` form is not special-cased on purpose (bypassing the wrapper is the violation).
- `echo "...; apt-get install"` inside a quoted string is a false positive by design of the command-position match (no such line exists today, and R1-neg pins the plain echo case).
- The self-test check kills the spawned bash with SIGKILL on timeout; a stub `sleep 300` child can outlive it on a machine where the wrapper is neutered. Not an issue on the real tree (the self-test finishes in about 11 s).
