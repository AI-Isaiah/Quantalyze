# Phase 167.1.2.2.2: TRADESYNC - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-10-09
**Status:** Ready for planning

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

Every live strategy key on a ccxt venue (today: 3 OKX, 1 Bybit on PROD) ingests its trades and daily returns every day again. The days each key missed are recovered as far as the venue's own records allow. The "Synced" badge tells the truth about when trades were last fetched.

The scope is ROADMAP `### Phase 167.1.2.2.2` success criteria 1-6:
- a `cron_sync_tick()` shipped by migration and registered by runbook;
- cursor seeding before the first tick;
- the `last_sync_at` overwrite or resume fallback fixed;
- the badge reading the last trade fetch;
- the allocator-key and revoked-key effects measured;
- the pg_net timeout fitting a full tick;
- ADR-0008 corrected and a staleness alarm.

Founder decision D-01 below adds one capability inside that boundary: recovering missed days from account bills where the trade API no longer reaches.

</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Days the trade API can no longer return
- **D-01:** Where the venue's trade (fills) history no longer reaches a missed day, rebuild that day's P&L from the account's **bills / balance / funding records**, if those reach further back. OKX fills cover roughly 3 months, and the two oldest OKX gaps are 138 and 156 days.
  - Founder choice (AskUserQuestion 2026-10-09: "Rebuild from account bills"), chosen over a named gap and over restarting history at the backfill.
  - Research must establish how far each venue's records really reach for these keys: OKX `bills-archive` and the async `bills-history-archive` download, and Bybit's transaction log. It must also establish how a bills-derived daily return reconciles with trade-derived returns on days where both exist.
  - **Reversibility:** costly. Rebuilt days enter `daily_returns` and every metric computed from them. Undoing it means deleting the rebuilt rows and recomputing every affected strategy.
- **D-02 (orchestrator default, from the never-invent-data rule):** a day that neither trades nor bills can supply stays MISSING. It is never filled with zero or interpolated. The factsheet names that gap. Metrics are computed over the days that exist.
- **D-03 (orchestrator default):** a bills-rebuilt day is recorded as reconstructed, distinguishable in storage from a trade-derived day, so a later reader or audit can tell them apart. The planner picks the mechanism, for example a flag or source column, or an existing data-quality field.
- **D-04 (orchestrator default):** where both trades and bills exist for the same day, the trade-derived figure wins. The two are compared on the overlap as a reconciliation check, and a material disagreement fails loud rather than silently picking one.

### Staleness
- **D-05:** a key reads **stale when its last successful trade fetch is more than 2 days old**. The tick runs daily, so this is one missed tick plus slack. Founder choice (Recommended).
- **D-06:** the stale state is shown to **everyone**, including public viewers of a published factsheet ("Last trades fetched N days ago" with stale styling). It is not owner-only. Founder choice (Recommended), consistent with the factsheet-honesty rule.
- **D-07 (from ROADMAP SC-4, restated):** "Synced Nd ago" reads the last successful trade fetch, never `strategy_analytics.computed_at`.


### Founder decisions after research (2026-10-09)
- **D-08:** Before the first tick, each OKX strategy's existing stored points (25-73 legacy points ending May) are **carried forward** into the daily-returns table, marked as legacy, so the re-derive extends the published history instead of replacing it with the ~3 months OKX still serves (founder, Recommended; alternatives "hold" and "accept shorter" rejected). A test fails if a re-derive would shorten a stored series.
- **D-09:** D-01 (rebuild from account bills) is **split out** to Phase 167.1.2.2.2.1 OKXHISTORY, booked 2026-10-09 (founder, Recommended). TRADESYNC names the unrecovered gap (D-02) and records each strategy's pre-tick high-water and post-tick earliest row as OKXHISTORY's work list; it fills nothing. The D-01 decision itself stands, its home moved. Bybit needs nothing (2-year reach).
- **D-10:** A published strategy whose key is disconnected (excluded from the tick) shows the stale line "Last trades fetched N days ago" (founder, Recommended), consistent with D-06.
- **D-11 (amends ROADMAP SC-2, recorded there):** cursor-row seeding is replaced by a data-derived resume high-water (newest stored daily-P&L row floored to UTC midnight, unclamped); the runbook step becomes a read-only pre-tick proof. Seeded markers would be clamped to 30 days by `MAX_MARKER_LOOKBACK_MS`.
- **D-12 (research, SC-3):** the poll keeps writing `last_sync_at`; the resume fallback stops reading it (four other readers depend on the poll's write).
- **D-13 (research, SC-5):** the tick selects only keys with an eligible strategy and not disconnected (4 today); the others are excluded with the reason recorded.

### Founder decisions after the UI-SPEC check (2026-10-09)
- **D-14 (amends ROADMAP SC-4's wording):** the badge says what it measures: "Trades fetched {age}", and "Trades fetched {age} · stale" when stale. The word "Synced" is retired on the strategy freshness badge. Founder choice (Recommended).
- **D-15:** strategy-owner key cards (`ApiKeyManager` "Last synced", `AllocatorSyncStatus` on `/strategies`) are relabelled "Key checked {age}", because the 04:00 poll writes that field (D-12). Allocator holdings cards are unchanged; there the label is accurate. Founder choice (Recommended).
- **D-16 (founder 2026-10-10, D-02 follow-through):** a day with no return (a guarded day, or the gap between carried-forward legacy rows and a re-derived window) is also removed from the persisted chart lists (`returns_series`, `drawdown_series`) at the runner's persist boundary, and the sparklines are re-derived from the filtered lists. The Phase 105 SC-4 oracle (`test_broker_guard_day`) is amended to expect the trimmed lists; its scalar byte-identity is unchanged. Chosen over making the `metrics.py` chart paths NaN-aware (wider blast radius) and over narrowing F1's wording. Founder choice (Recommended).

### Must-carry items for the planner (UI checker, 2026-10-09)
- **Zero-fill vs D-02 (must be a plan task with a failing test):** `combine_realized_and_funding` → `gap_fill_daily_returns` (`analytics-service/services/broker_dailies.py`) reindexes every day in [first, last] with 0.0. A named gap must never become 0.0 returns. Measure how D-08's carried-forward points and the re-derive merge, and assert no 0.0 rows across a recorded gap span.
- **F1:** the gap line states only what is true at ship time, e.g. "No trade data {from} – {to} ({days} days). These days are not recovered; they are left out of the record, not counted as zero." Never "the exchange no longer serves these days" (OKX's archive does; OKXHISTORY recovers them). The second clause ships only once the zero-fill task passes.
- **F2:** a strategy with historical trades but a null `trades_fetched_at` never reads "Trades not yet fetched". Withhold the claim or say "No trade fetch recorded yet".
- **F3:** `tradeFetchVenue` comes only from the api-key's exchange, never `supported_exchanges` (a CSV strategy declaring okx is not-applicable).
- **F4:** the reason for red is (a) the existing red stale token on the same badge and (b) two or more failed daily ticks. Not "does not heal itself".
- **F5:** D-10's stale line for the disconnected published strategy needs a runbook step seeding `trades_fetched_at`, with a before/after read.
- PATTERNS corrections 1-10 (lint blind spot, cache key v12→v13, `_strategy_resume_point` two call sites, data_quality_flags wiped by the derive, mock chains) bind the plans.

### Claude's Discretion
- The tick's time of day. Place it relative to the existing 04:00 poll and the 05:30 derive so that the derive sees the day's trades.
- Whether the tick is synchronous or asynchronous (SC-5, pg_net timeout). Research measures a full tick first.
- Whether to stop `poll_allocator_positions` moving a strategy key's `last_sync_at`, or stop the resume fallback reading it (SC-3). This is the research pick per the ROADMAP.
- The treatment of allocator keys and revoked-but-`is_active` keys by the tick (SC-5). Each is measured, then intended or excluded with a recorded reason.
- Whether the staleness alarm is built here or booked with OUTAGEALERT (SC-6).

<code_context>
## Existing Code Insights

### Reusable Assets
- The vault plus `system_settings` `cron_tick` pattern: a new `cron_sync_tick()` copies it, including the URL from `system_settings`, the key from the vault, and `pg_net`.
- `strategy_sync_cursors`: the resume table. It has 0 rows on PROD, and seeding it is SC-2.
- The derivecron and ledger-refresh go-live runbooks: the template for the registration runbook, with marker first, before/after counts and a manifest re-capture.

### Established Patterns
- PROD crons are registered by runbook only. Migrations ship the function, never `cron.schedule`.
- A `pg_net` "success" only means the request was queued. Tick verification reads the downstream effect (trades and daily returns rows), never the net status.
- Freshness is `computed_at` for analytics. This phase introduces trade-fetch freshness as a separate signal.

### Integration Points
- The tick feeds the 05:30 UTC allocator derive (167.1.2.2), so ordering relative to it matters.
- Recovered days flow into `daily_returns` and from there into every strategy metric. The Wave 5 consolidated recompute (166.3) must include these 4 strategies, so no row is recomputed twice.
