<objective>
Make the founder's 2026-09-03 deferral something the GATE prints, not something a ledger asserts.

Plan 00 measured that `test_reconcile_dropped_enqueue_sweep.sql` (39 sections) and
`test_retention_orphaned_running.sql` (25) open Part 1 with `IF NOT EXISTS (SELECT 1 FROM
pg_extension WHERE extname = 'pg_cron') THEN RAISE EXCEPTION 'TEST FAILED (1/JOB-04|1/JOB-05) …'`
(`:268-269`, `:212-213`) — their pg-lane baseline can never be GREEN, and `run.mjs` never judges an
arm in a red-baseline file. Planner re-measured at HEAD 007708dd: `test_strategy_analytics_
stuck_computing_reaper.sql` (29) baselines GREEN but `RAISE NOTICE 'SKIP …'; RETURN;` on the same
probe at `:282`, `:326-327`, `:483-484`, withholding Part 1b and Parts 2-3 (identities `1/JOB-02`,
`1/JOB-03`, `2…`, `3…`); and `test_derive_allocator_keys_fanout.sql` (7) does NOT raise on the
probe — `:159 IF EXISTS (… 'pg_cron') THEN` guards ASSERTION 6 (`TEST FAILED (6)`) and `:169`
prints `pg_cron not present — skipping cron assertion` otherwise. ⚠️ That corrects Plan 00's record
and the `[REDUNDER-PGCRON]` TODO, which list derive among the files that "RAISE"; it does not, but
its section `6` is un-falsifiable on the lane for the same reason, and CONTEXT's batch rule is
"each plan lands its files FULLY proven — no file left half-annotated". All four are therefore
deferred together; the founder decided 2026-09-03 NOT to install pg_cron on the lane.

Criterion 4 is the home for this: an arm that cannot be given a falsifying mutation is RECORDED
with its reason, never silently skipped. Criterion 1 requires the exclusion be NAMED by the runner
every run. Today these four sit inside `  pending: 43 idiom file(s) without RED-UNDER — …`, which
names them but states no reason and, at the phase's end, would read as unfinished work. Plan 01
built the `unreachable:` line for the 27 non-idiom files from a DERIVED corpus class; this plan adds
one more derived class, `lane-blocked`, with the same three-part contract (print in both modes, CI
MEASURE_FAIL on absence + count/name cross-check, exact-set unit pin), and corrects the ledgers.

⚠️ A printed reason that can never expire is the control-that-cannot-fail this phase exists to
remove. The classification is derived from the corpus, but "the pg-lane cannot host pg_cron" is a
fact about the LANE, and nothing in the classification measures the lane. If [REDUNDER-PGCRON] is
ever fixed, a derived-only class keeps printing four files as blocked and nothing reddens — 100
sections parked forever behind a true-looking line. So Task 1 also PROBES the lane once per
lane-spawning run and MEASURE_FAILs when pg_cron is available while the class is non-empty.

⚠️ The ledger the verifier reads still carries the pre-deferral numbers. Criterion 4 makes the
deferral compliant (an arm that cannot be given a falsifying mutation is RECORDED with its reason),
but criterion 1 and the goal line carry COUNTS — 44 files, ~365 twins — that this phase will not
deliver, and `/gsd-verify-work` evaluates sentences, not intentions. Amending them to what is
reachable IS the recording criterion 4 requires (a scope amendment touching one file is incomplete:
the refused claim survives in the files nobody edited). Task 3 writes SCOPE AMENDMENT #2 into every
site and greps for the old phrasing so it cannot be skipped silently.

Purpose: the deferral is emitted by the gate AND measured against the lane, so no ledger can drift
from what is measured and the deferral cannot outlive its cause; the end-state numerator (40 of 71)
is explained on every run and stated in the ledger.
Output: `lane-blocked:` line + `lane-probe:` line + `lane-blocked-stale` defect + self-test scenario
+ tests + CI assertions + red fixtures; TODOS/CLAUDE.md/ROADMAP/STATE currency.
</objective>
