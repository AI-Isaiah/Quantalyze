<objective>
Fix the `last_sync_at` cursor-advance defect D-03 names: when every strategy on a key is filtered
out of `strategy_ids`, today's code advances the cursor as if the tick were idle, permanently
skipping any real trades the venue held in that window. This is a defect today, independent of
whatever Plan 01 decides about `ALLOWED_STRATEGY_STATUSES` — CONTEXT.md D-03 is explicit that the two
must not share one closure, because they have different causes.

**Purpose.** `should_advance_cursor` currently collapses two different "nothing to store" cases into
one boolean: a genuinely idle tick (no trades fetched — advancing is correct, per the C-0198 comment's
own intent) and a tick where trades WERE fetched but no strategy was eligible to receive them
(advancing is wrong — those trades are gone on the next tick, because `parse_since_ms(last_sync_at)`
now points past them). The fix distinguishes the two using the same `synced_count > 0` disjunct the
C-0198 fix already established for the sibling case (an RPC that was attempted and failed) — this is a
third member of that class, not a new gate shape.

**Output.** A calibrated RED->GREEN pytest addition to `analytics-service/tests/test_cron_router.py`,
and the corresponding two-line formula fix plus an accurate warning-log reason in
`analytics-service/routers/cron.py`.
</objective>
