## Decisions Made

- AR-1 stands as written (SECURITY DEFINER). The A2 risk (definer write to a FORCE-RLS table) did not materialise on the service-role path.
- The function's NULL-explicit guards use `IS NULL` / `IS DISTINCT FROM` and are nested where `jsonb_array_length` would raise on a non-array, because plpgsql does not promise short-circuit evaluation.
- The verify block deliberately omits the COALESCE projection, the boundary IF condition, the DELETE predicate and the D-02 IF, as the plan requires, so plan 03's mutated copies still apply.
