# Phase 167.2.1.1.2 ACCTREBUILD - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-10-22
**Status:** Ready for planning

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

A Bybit-backed allocator whose user holds several api_keys rows gets its past allocation history (`allocator_equity_snapshots`, one aggregate row per allocator and day) rebuilt with open-position profit included: the whole account (all its keys) is rebuilt together and replaced in one atomic step, or the rebuild refuses with a named reason and writes nothing. Accounts that also hold OKX keys are rebuilt over the last 90 days only. Split from 167.2.1.1 UPNLVENUES D-04/D-05; UPNLVENUES shipped the code fix (every new day counts open-position profit) and a sole-key forced rebuild that refuses `multi_key`. Storing marked NAV at each ingest is 167.2.1.1.1 NAVMTM; one mark-price rule is 167.2.1.1.1.1 MARKSOURCE. Neither is in scope.

</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Carried from UPNLVENUES (founder 2026-10-22)
- UPNLVENUES D-04, "Rebuild whole account (Recommended)": a forced rebuild of an account with several keys rebuilds all its keys together and replaces that account's history in one atomic step, or refuses and writes nothing.
- UPNLVENUES D-05, "Add an OKX 90-day path" and "Split Phases if Necessary": accounts that also hold OKX keys get a 90-day rebuild; this phase owns it.

### D-01 Older stored days: keep as they are
Founder 2026-10-22, AskUserQuestion: "Keep them as they are (Recommended)". Only the rebuilt window is replaced. Stored days older than the window (90 days for accounts holding OKX keys, 730 days otherwise) keep their current values, and the boundary date is recorded.

### D-02 Days the rebuild cannot reproduce: refuse that account
Founder 2026-10-22: "Refuse that account (Recommended)". If a stored day inside the window is not reproduced by the rebuild (for example a day where one account has no data), that account's rebuild writes nothing and records why, counts only. The founder decides per account afterwards.

### D-03 The seam: allow the step, mark the date
Founder 2026-10-22: "Allow the step, mark the date (Recommended)". Where the rebuilt window meets the older unchanged days, the curve may jump by the open-position profit at that date. Leave the jump, store the boundary date so charts and returns can name it, and invent no values.

### Claude's Discretion
- Where the boundary date is stored (an existing metadata column or audit, or a new column; a migration needs migration-reviewer and rls-policy-auditor clean before merge, and PROD migrations auto-apply after the TEST apply on merge).
- Whether the window-scoped replace fits the existing `replace_allocator_equity_snapshots` RPC or needs a scoped variant, and whether the stored-days check moves inside that transaction (the UPNLVENUES accepted race).
- How the design inputs (UPNLVENUES plans 07-11, below) change for a window-scoped replace instead of a whole-history replace.

</decisions>
