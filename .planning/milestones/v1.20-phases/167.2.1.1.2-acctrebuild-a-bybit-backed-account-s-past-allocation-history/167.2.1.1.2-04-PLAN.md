## Decision recorded by this plan

  - 1)` days, that is `end_date - 89 days`, for EVERY venue's crawl (one window per account); `window_days` is 90 (90
  calendar days inclusive of today). Otherwise the existing `end_date - BACKFILL_CAP_DAYS` days, `window_days` 730.
  "Holding OKX keys" (D-01) and "an okx key is selected" agree on every account that is not refused: an OKX exchange
  account with no working key refuses, and every other OKX exchange account is selected. Residual edge (RESEARCH Q1):
  a job crossing 00:00 UTC mid-crawl would refuse `anchor_skipped_okx_terminus`; the post-deploy handoff avoids
  enqueueing near midnight UTC, and a re-run clears it.
