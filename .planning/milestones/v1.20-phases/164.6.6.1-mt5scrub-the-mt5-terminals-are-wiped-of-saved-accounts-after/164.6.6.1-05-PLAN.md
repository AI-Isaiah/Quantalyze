<objective>
Build the validation terminal's scrub as its own service module: a supervised background task that
runs after a validation's verdict, in its own lease, and scrubs and house-relaunches the terminal.
Add the scrub-owed bookkeeping the next validation consumes, the W-1 gate on the probe thread's own
event, the alert, and the durable outcome row.

Purpose: criterion 1 on the validation terminal, under the founder's D-08 ("after the verdict, in its
own lease, with a scrub owed marker"). A cold relaunch cannot fit the wizard's 75 s deadline, so the
scrub must not run inside the request.

Decision 6 (stated; the sites wire it in plan 06): the wizard cannot consume an owed scrub inside its
budget. At the ceilings, 5 crossings at its 55 s rpyc bound is far beyond the 75 s / 60 s chain and the
120 s client ceiling. So an owed scrub REFUSES the wizard validation with the existing transient 424
and schedules the scrub. The worker, which has no client budget and holds its lease unbounded,
consumes the owed scrub inline before its probe, through `run_owed_validation_scrub_in_lease`.

Budget (stated): the scrub builds its own `Mt5Client` with the WORKER chain (`MT5_REQUEST_TIMEOUT_S`
30 s rpyc, `MT5_INITIALIZE_TIMEOUT_MS` 20000). That is exactly the `timeout=20000` S-09 and S-10
measured with. `_VALIDATION_SCRUB_BUDGET_S` = `SCRUB_RELAUNCH_RESERVE_CROSSINGS` x `MT5_REQUEST_TIMEOUT_S`
plus a 10 s connect slack. It sits outside every request chain, because the scrub runs after the
response.

Spec-less probe: no requirement IDs, so no edge probe ran. The edges come from criterion 1, D-08 and W-1.
