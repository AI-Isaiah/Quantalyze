# Phase 164.6.6.1 Plan 03: jobs-terminal scrub and credentialed house relaunch Summary

When the heal escalates an `ipc_fault` (`-10005`), it now ends the jobs terminal, deletes its `accounts.dat` (`delete_trades=0`), and relaunches it with the HOUSE credentials, all inside the one heal lease and the one budget. The 2026-10-04 L7 outage (a stale saved house password) heals to `authorized` as house with no human, and the relaunch debt is cleared only by a house-verified snapshot.

## What was built

- **`scrub_and_relaunch_as_house(client, *, house, deadline, delete_trades)`** runs four steps in order:
  1. The scrub verb.
  2. Whatever the verb returned or raised (except `Mt5SessionAbandoned`), a credentialed relaunch `_relaunch_as_house`, if one credentialed reading is still affordable. One `initialize_with_credentials` call is followed by budget-gated credentialed polls, using `_watch_relaunch`'s loop shape. Polling stops on authorized, on `-6`, when the settle window passes, or when the budget runs out.
  3. `_read_post_relaunch_state`.
  4. `clear_mt5_relaunch_debt` only when that state is `verified`.

  It never raises `Exception`, and it never calls `assert_session_authorized`. It returns `ScrubRelaunchResult`, which carries counts, codes, class names and `relaunch_elapsed_s`.
- **`_escalate_ipc_fault`** gained `env_password`, which `_heal_blocking` passes from its own parameters.
  - Debounce, cap, evidence capture and the budget gate are unchanged. The gate now reads `SCRUB_RELAUNCH_RESERVE_CROSSINGS`.
  - With no house login or password it returns `ipc_fault_recycle_no_house_credentials` at ERROR, before the claim.
  - Classification goes through `_classify_scrub_relaunch`.
  - The `terminal_scrub` fence stage gives the claim back.
  - The log line carries the phrase `escalated to a terminal-process recycle with a saved-account scrub and a credentialed house relaunch`, plus `refused`, `accounts_deleted`, `accounts_missing`, `trades_deleted`, `profile_accounts_found`, `relaunch_polls` and `relaunch_elapsed_s` (whole seconds).
  - A scrub that ended the terminal but refused the delete adds `accounts_dat_kept=<reason>` and logs at ERROR.
- **Budget.** The crossing table has a dated 164.6.6.1 block:
  - scrub 3 plus the credentialed reading 2, so the total is unchanged at 8;
  - evidence from S-09 (2.8 s) and S-11 (6.0 s kill-to-authorized);
  - the W-2 slow-launch arithmetic at the ceilings and at the measured costs;
  - the D-11 proxy note.

  `_MT5_RELOGIN_BUDGET_CEILING_S` and `_RELAUNCH_SETTLE_S` are unchanged.
- **`_JOB_TERMINAL_DELETE_TRADES: Final[int] = 0`.** Its comment cites Phase 164.6.6.3, Finding C, D-03 and D-04, and names `TODOS.md` `MT5-JOB-TERMINAL-TRADES-SCRUB-01` as the owner of any later flip.
- **Deleted:** `_watch_relaunch` and `_classify_recycle_verdict`. `grep` over `services/`, `routers/` and `main.py` found no production caller. `_RECYCLE_CROSSINGS` was also removed, because the scrub constant replaces it.

## Deviations from Plan

1. **[Decision, resolved by the plan text] Severity after a scrub.** The plan's action says "keep the severity ladder", but its truths say "a relaunch that did not verify logs at ERROR", and threat T-164.6.6.1-09 says "ERROR on every non-verified outcome". Plan 08 gates "unverified and pending … log at ERROR". So DEGRADED, UNVERIFIED and RELAUNCH_PENDING are all ERROR after a scrub. The rest of the ladder's structure is kept: INFO for a verified recovery, and WARNING for the qualified IN-02 `matched=0` case.

   PENDING was first left at WARNING, following 164.6.5 WR-02's "not yet known". Fix commit `a9be01e18` raised it. WR-02's argument assumed a terminal that kept its saved account and would come back unaided, and a scrubbed terminal does not.
2. **[Decision] `authorized is None` is PENDING.** When the budget could not even cover the relaunch, `_classify_scrub_relaunch` returns RELAUNCH_PENDING rather than STILL_FAULTED. Nothing was shown to fail.
3. **[Double] Fallback chain for a credentialed call on the account-less terminal.** The plan says `relaunch_credentialed` → `initialize_after_recycle` → True. Taken literally, every plain `_WEDGED` test (five-consecutive, WR08, SFH03 hourly, the WR02 settle window) would flip from "the wedge persists" to "authorized". The chain inserts two links before the final True:
   - True when the scenario declared a stale saved login. That stale copy was the wedge, and the scrub deleted it, so L7 and plan 08's rotation test heal without a knob.
   - `initialize`, which keeps the pre-plan default for a persisting wedge.

   Pre-plan behaviour is unchanged (379 green before the production edit).
4. **[Process] Task 1's commit left the migrated gates red until Task 2's commit,** as plan 01 recorded for its own split. `b2d83bb0e` alone is red on 33 escalation tests; `0a8bce02c` is green.
5. **[Process] Task 3 changed no file.** Its commands all passed first time, so it has no commit.
