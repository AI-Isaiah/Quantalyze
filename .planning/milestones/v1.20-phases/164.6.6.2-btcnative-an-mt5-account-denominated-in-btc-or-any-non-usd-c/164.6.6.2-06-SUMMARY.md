## Carry-forward decision from plan 05

**Surfaces:** the End-of-Year Returns bars chart (`DistributionPanels.tsx`, title `End-of-Year Returns`) and the `CAGR` row of the `Cumulative Return Metrics` panel (`MetricsColumn.tsx`).

**Decision: the unit rule does not cover them. Both are left bare.** Not touched, not labelled.

**Basis:**
- CONTEXT D-09: "The unit is shown as a 'Returns in BTC' chip beside the strategy name, and as 'in BTC' on the equity chart and on the return KPI labels." Neither surface is the equity chart, and neither is a KPI label (the KPI strip and the Main Metrics rows are the KPI labels).
- UI-SPEC A13 enumerates by name: "MetricsColumn section I rows `Cumulative Return`, `CAGR` (Main Metrics)". The `Cumulative Return Metrics` panel is a different panel. A20 names the `EOY Returns` table panel and gives only its title (`EOY Returns in BTC`); the bars chart has a different title string (`End-of-Year Returns`) and A20 prescribes no label for it. The Copywriting Contract's chart-title list (`Cumulative Returns in BTC`, `Equity Curve in BTC`, `Equity in BTC`, `EOY Returns in BTC`) does not include it.
- UI-SPEC A23: every other panel "Unchanged. These are the BTC series alone, and the masthead chip covers them." with scope `D-09 scope`; A4 applies the same reasoning to the ratio cells.

**Counter-argument recorded:** the Cumulative Return Metrics `CAGR` is the same quantity as A3 and A13, so a reader sees `CAGR in BTC` in two places and bare `CAGR` in a third. That is a legitimate objection, but fixing it widens the spec, which the plan forbids ("Do not widen scope beyond those two surfaces", and only if the rule covers them). If the founder wants it, it is two `withUnit` calls plus one test; plan 12's real-browser pass is the natural place to see whether it reads as a gap.

**Pinned:** `A23 surfaces the unit rule does not name keep their labels` in `FactsheetView.returns-unit-comparisons.test.tsx` asserts both surfaces keep their bare labels beside Main Metrics' `CAGR in BTC`, so reversing the decision is a deliberate test edit.

## Judgement calls (not rule deviations)

- **The Cross Signatures legend** ("strategy events" and "BTC events" swatches) is hidden when the section is withheld, since it labels lines that are not drawn. Not in UI-SPEC; a one-line condition.
- **`BaseLeverageNote`** ("shown at base 1x leverage") is omitted on the withheld Signatures and Cross Signatures sections: nothing is shown, so there is no magnitude for it to qualify.
- **Strip subtitle punctuation:** the existing subtitle has no final period, so the unit case reads `...diversification benefit. BTC row not measurable: returns are in BTC.`
- **The benchmark name `BTC`** in `Benchmark - vs BTC`, `BTC not available:` and `Strategy x BTC` is the comparator's name, not the strategy's unit, and is a literal by design; the unit, wherever it appears, is composed from `payload.returnsUnit`. The ETH arms in the tests prove the unit side (`...returns are in ETH. A BTC benchmark measured in ETH is a flat line.`).
