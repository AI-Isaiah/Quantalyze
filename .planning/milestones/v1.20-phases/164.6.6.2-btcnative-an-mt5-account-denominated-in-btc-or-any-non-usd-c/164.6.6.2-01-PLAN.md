<objective>
Give the analytics service ONE notion of an account's unit, and make the shared NAV/TWR engine take
its thresholds from it, so a BTC-sized NAV is judged against BTC floors instead of the USD $1000
dust floor that flattens MM-2x today.

Purpose: D-07 asks for one per-currency table read by every USD-sized threshold; D-20 sets the BTC
dust floor at 0.001 BTC (superseding D-05's 0.01); D-01/D-04/D-06 fix how a currency string is
classified. The TWR arithmetic does not depend on the unit and is not duplicated (CONTEXT
Claude's Discretion): only the floors move, through a default-preserving keyword, so every other
venue is byte-identical. This plan also lands the Wave-0 fixture commit plan 03 needs.

Output: `services/account_unit.py` (new leaf), floors threaded through `nav_twr.py` and the two MT5
combiners in `broker_dailies.py`, the classifier/table tests, BTC twins of the dust oracles, and the
MT5 fixture-currency commit.
</objective>
