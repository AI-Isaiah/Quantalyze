## What was built

- `convertNativeReturnsToUsd(series, unit, btc)`: `unit == null` returns the same array reference; `btc === null` returns `[]`; otherwise loops from day 1 and emits `(1 + r) * (1 + b) - 1` only where the BTC interval is paired and both inputs are finite. The single call to `alignCoveredReturns(` is the only interval logic.
- The oracle fixture (`closes_source: "benchmark_prices only"`, `tolerance: 1e-12`): `0.21` (1.1 x 66000 / 60000 - 1), `0.21` then `-0.01` (three-day), `0.375` (account +25%, BTC +10%, so the two moves differ and a swapped or additive variant also fails), `0.4641` (a Friday-to-Monday weekday series over three BTC days), then the honesty cases (missing close, dropped close, after the last close, null unit, null closes, empty, one point, non-finite input, missing Sunday close) and the pre-first-stored-close case.
- `readBtcCloses(client)`: `readBenchmarkPrices(client, "BTC")`, returned as `{ prices, dropped, through }`; a read error is `null` plus one `console.error`; no usable close is `null`.
