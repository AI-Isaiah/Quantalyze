<objective>
Read the MT5 account's currency on every derive, decide its unit once, and carry that unit through
the derive: the floors, the D-03 change check, the persisted `account_currency` / native balance, and
the `native_unit` flag the factsheet will read. Make the two other MT5 reads (the history-settle wait
and the sync balance arm) unit-aware, and let the positions poll parse the currency through the same
classifier while keeping its USD-only valuation.

dollars. Key-mode handling, the analytics runner and the backfill are plan 04.

Output: the derive MT5 branch, `mt5_read.py`, the sync arm, the positions poll, and their tests.
</objective>
