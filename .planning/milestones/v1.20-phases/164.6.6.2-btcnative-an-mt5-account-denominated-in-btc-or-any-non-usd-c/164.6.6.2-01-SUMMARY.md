## BTC floor row and derivation

`BTC = UnitFloors(dust_nav=0.001, material_equity=0.0001, residual_abs_tol=1e-6)`, written once in `_NATIVE_FLOORS`. `dust_nav` is D-20. The other two follow one rule, the USD row (`1000.0 / 100.0 / 1.00`) times D-20's ratio 0.001/1000 = 1e-6: material_equity 100 -> 0.0001 BTC, residual_abs_tol 1.00 -> 1e-6 BTC. The USD row is the only place `1000.0 / 100.0 / 1.00` are written inside this plan's files; `nav_twr.DUST_NAV_FLOOR` is `USD_FLOORS.dust_nav`.
