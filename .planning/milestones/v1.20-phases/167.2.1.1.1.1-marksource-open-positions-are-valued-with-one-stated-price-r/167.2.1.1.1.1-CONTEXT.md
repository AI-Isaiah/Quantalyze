## Implementation Decisions

### Carried (not re-asked)
- NAVMTM 167.2.1.1.1 D-01..D-24. In particular D-03/D-11 (a mark is the venue's own equity at read time, labelled the UTC day that just closed), D-04, D-05, D-06, D-07 (native-unit MT5 priced at the stored daily close of the mark day; a missing close refuses), D-09, D-17..D-21 (per-venue marks).
- NAVMTM already closed the realized-basis last-day step for marked keys (the compose anchors on the stored mark). Left for this phase: the step when today's mark is refused or absent (`replay_key_equity`), and REVIEW-3 IN-02 (a moved MT5 BTC flow priced at close(D+1) beside a mark at close(D)).
- The display does not read the legacy curve (since 167.1.2 plan 11).

### D-01 The two curves: keep both, align them
Founder 2026-10-27, AskUserQuestion: "Keep both, align them (Recommended)". The legacy holdings poll and daily refresh move next to the ~00:10Z mark read (a hand-run pg_cron change from a runbook, like NAVMTM's chain move), legacy rows are labelled by the UTC day that closed, and a cross-curve test proves agreement within max($1, 0.5% of level) on marked non-Deribit days. Pre-start days (realized, NAVMTM L1/D-09) and Deribit (absent from the legacy curve) are stated exclusions. Retiring the legacy curve is booked as its own phase.

### D-02 Spot rule: UTC-day close, gaps named
Founder 2026-10-27: "UTC-day close, gaps named (Recommended)". Every path values spot at the venue's 1d close for the day; the CoinGecko fallback is corrected to close semantics; a live read at the label boundary uses `last`; a missing price is a named gap, never $0 (matching the BRIDGERETURNS `holding_marks` rule); a gap longer than a stated N days is named and skipped, not carried. Derivatives keep the venue mark; Deribit inverse keeps the index. One documented rule, one test per path.

### D-03 Last-bar spike (Claude's call, analyzer recommendation)
The spike is dormant in production (no caller supplies `mark_prices`). The first task proves no screen reads long-fetch `results.twr`/`results.sharpe`; then the dead return-based `EquityCurveBuilder` scalars are deleted and nulled in `long_fetch`. If a reader exists, keep the scalars and stop booking open profit on the last bar instead.

### Split
Part A (rule + SC-3, Python only, no migration or cron) goes first; Part B (curve agreement, cron move, cross-curve test) depends on A's rule. The planner may split them into separate plans or waves.

### D-04 Carry length and gap effect (founder 2026-10-27, plan-check A1)
Founder 2026-10-27, AskUserQuestion: "7 days, gap holds the day (Recommended)". A spot close is carried at most N = 7 days; past that the price is a named gap, and a legacy daily row whose holdings carry a gap is held (not written) for that day.

### D-05 OKX open profit counted once, history rewritten (founder 2026-10-27, plan-check R-1)
Founder 2026-10-27, AskUserQuestion: "Also rewrite past rows" (chosen over the recommended fix-forward). OKX spot is read from `cashBal` so the Holdings USDT row and AUM stop counting open profit twice from now on, AND the stored past rows that carried the double count are corrected. The correction goes through the product's own recompute path where one exists, is measured read-only on PROD first (rows and accounts affected, size of the change), keeps a before/after census, and writes nothing a later run cannot reproduce. It is an operator plan with its own PROD step.

2026-10-31 (plan 14): option-b; follow-up phase 178 measured the gate and closed record-only (178 D-09); a1, a2, a5 recorded, not recomputed (D-06); rows kept and named (F1)

### D-06 Dust floor for the gap hold (founder 2026-10-27, plan-check W3)
Founder 2026-10-27, AskUserQuestion: "Measure PROD, then set a floor (Recommended)". A read-only PROD count of unpriced balances by size comes first; then a balance below a small share of the account (default 0.1% of the account's USD level, to be confirmed by the measurement) is left out of the day with a named note instead of holding it.

### D-07 Price-gap display (founder 2026-10-27, plan-check W2)
Founder 2026-10-27, AskUserQuestion: "Show 'no price' (Recommended)". The Holdings tab labels a price-gap row as unpriced instead of $0, and AUM states that it excludes that row. User-facing: DESIGN.md applies.

### D-08 Named legacy exclusions (founder 2026-10-27, plan-check W1)
Founder 2026-10-27, AskUserQuestion: "Accept, named in runbook (Recommended)". A BTC-unit MT5 account with a missing close and sFOX non-stable balances are left out of the legacy row as named exclusions: written in the runbook, a TODOS entry each, and asserted by name in the cross-curve test.

### D-09 D-05 reach and D-07 wording (founder 2026-10-27, after the plan-10 census)
Founder 2026-10-27, AskUserQuestion: "Measure gate first, then rewrite (Recommended)". The stored-row rewrite of D-05 waits on the OKX ordering gate (`167.2.1.1.2.1-OKX-ORDER-UNMEASURED`): this phase ships the forward fix and records the rewrite as a deferral; a booked follow-up phase measures the gate with a founder-approved worker probe and then rebuilds the 90-day window. Rows older than the window and `allocator_holdings` history are kept and named. D-07 wording "Approve as written (Recommended)": cell "No price"; composer "{N} holding(s) without a price left out."; Holdings tab "{N} holding(s) has/have no price, so portfolio AUM leaves it/them out." Dust floor (D-06): "0.1%, unsizable left out (Recommended)".
