# Phase 167.2.1.1.1.1 Plan 01: the price rule (history, CoinGecko, anchor) Summary

**One documented price rule (`services/price_rule.py`, UTC-day close, 7-day named carry, gaps never $0) now governs the history replay, the CoinGecko fallback with a close-tagged updating cache, the ccxt flow valuer and the live anchor, with a named test on every path.**

## Accomplishments

- `services/price_rule.py`: a leaf module (stdlib and `services.closed_sets` only) whose docstring is the rule: spot history at the UTC close of the day, ticker `last` for a live read, derivatives keep the venue mark, Deribit inverse keeps the index, stable-value assets 1.0, an unusable price is a named gap never $0, a history gap is carried at most `PRICE_GAP_MAX_DAYS = 7` days and named, then named and skipped. It states the history-perp exemption, why BRIDGERETURNS uses N = 0, and the two D-08 legacy exclusions. It leaves room for the D-06 dust paragraph plan 12 adds.
- History replay: `_compute_daily_equity._price_on` returns `(price, source, age)` through `close_on_or_before` in both the OHLCV and the CoinGecko branch; unusable closes never enter the lookup; a 1-7 day carry is used and added to the new `carried_symbols` out-set, an older or absent close is skipped and named in `skipped_symbols`. `carried_symbols` flows through `_fetch_and_price_window` telemetry, `_merge_account_telemetry` (sorted union) and the reconstruct audit (`.get`, capped at 50).
- CoinGecko: `_fetch_coingecko_daily_closes` returns the first point stamped D+1 as the close of D; the cache writes source `coingecko_close` by an updating upsert and the read filters on that tag, so the old open-labelled rows are never read again (no migration, no database command). The ccxt flow valuer inherits this through `_resolve_ccxt_flow_price_index` with no change of its own.
- Anchor: `_price_wallet_totals` treats a ticker that answers without a usable `last` (missing, None, NaN, inf, zero, negative, bool, string, non-dict) as a partial-set hole exactly like a raised ticker, so the reconstruct anchor is skipped by name and `_read_bybit_uta_mark` refuses `wallet_unpriced`.

## Decisions Made

- The CoinGecko close is the FIRST point stamped on D+1, and the usability check runs after that choice: an unusable 00:00 point leaves the day a gap instead of letting a later hourly point of the same date stand in as a "close" taken hours into the next day. The plan's wording allowed either order; this is the one that keeps the label honest.
- `_fetch_coingecko_daily_closes` drops the close labelled before the requested start day (the stamp at the window's first midnight is the close of the day before it, which nobody asked for). The plan's expected output for stamps 03-01 / 03-02 / 03-03 (two entries, not three) requires this.
- `usable_price` rejects a numeric string. CoinGecko returns JSON numbers, so nothing real is lost, and a string price is exactly the shape a hostile or broken body would take.
