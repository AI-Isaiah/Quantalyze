# Phase 167.2.1.1.1.1 Plan 03: a refused mark and a moved BTC flow stop leaking a price Summary

**When a day's mark is refused the key's anchor is the last stored mark (so `replay_key_equity` never writes a live equity onto an older mark day), and a BTC deposit made just before a mark's read is valued at that mark's own close, with the amount that leaves the read day reported separately.**

## Accomplishments

- **Task 1 (tracer), the residual.** In `_run_key_mode_compose_epilogue`, after the existing null-reason ladder: when no mark exists for this derive's label day, the ladder is silent (`_anchor_null_reason is None`), the persisted series ends on a stored mark strictly older than the label day, and that mark's `nav_usd` is finite, positive and above `DUST_NAV_FLOOR`, the stored mark becomes `_anchor_mark` and `_anchor_basis`. `anchor_usd` is then its `nav_usd` and `anchor_asof` its `marked_at`. The label-day branch and the ladder are untouched, so `test_stale_last_mark_does_not_mask_a_live_zero` passes unchanged. Through the real compose, the 03-08 level is the stored 1071.0 and no level is dated after it (the old code drew 1215.0 there, a 144.0 step no day earned).
- **Task 2, the moved flow.** `FlowInstant` gained the defaulted trailing field `amount_prior_day`. The MT5 BTC block now sets `amount` to native x own-day close (0.0 when that close is not stored yet) and `amount_prior_day` to native x close(day - 1), dropping an instant only when neither close exists. `apply_marks` rule 10 counts `amount_prior_day` (else `amount`) in the mark day and removes the stored `amount` from the read day; `MarkedSeries.flow_moves_out` reports the second figure. Both stored-flow consumers in `job_worker.py` apply `+in` on the mark day and `-out` on the next day (the plain branch emits that second `ExternalFlow` only when it is non-zero).
