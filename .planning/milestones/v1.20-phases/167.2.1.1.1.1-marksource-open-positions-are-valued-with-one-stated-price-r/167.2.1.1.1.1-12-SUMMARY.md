# Phase 167.2.1.1.1.1 Plan 12: the legacy refresh holds a day on a sizable price gap Summary

**The legacy refresh no longer writes a day short because a price is missing: a gap sized at or above `price_rule.DUST_FLOOR_SHARE` (0.001) of the account's USD level holds the day by name (`price_gap`, Sentry `held_price_gap`, leader only), a dust gap and an unsizable one (the recorded choice) are left out and counted with their symbols collected, and a dead account's gap is left out with a count instead of freezing the store.**

### Boundary arithmetic (typed in `test_gap_disposition_boundaries`)

Level 10000.0 x floor 0.001 = 10.00. `gap_disposition(9.99, 10000.0)` is `dust` (strictly below), `gap_disposition(10.00, 10000.0)` is `hold` (at the floor). With the floor drilled to 0.01 the boundary is 100.00, so the 10.00 assertion goes RED (RESEARCH's own D-N13 example, 5.00 on 10000, was dust at both floors and would have proved nothing).

## Decisions Made

- The sizing window is anchored on the account's own read day (`latest`), not the run day, so a carried account is sized against marks near its own rows. A priced row exactly 7 days before is inside the window (`gte`), 8 is outside (`test_refresh_unsizable_gap_follows_the_recorded_decision`, the `(7, False, True)` and `(8, ...)` cases).
- The account level is the sum of `holding_equity_contribution` over every row of the account's read day, as the plan words it. That includes a Deribit venue row's derivative P&L, although the refresh total itself skips Deribit; it only matters on a mixed account and only moves the dust boundary by that P&L. Left as written rather than inventing a second level definition.
- A gap on a held, non-working account is not extended into the rows at all (`continue`), which matches the over-age failing split; a hold on a working account still extends its rows because the caller returns before summing.
