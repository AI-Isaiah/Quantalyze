## What was built

- **Route.** `SOURCE_SCHEMA` reads `source` first (absent means `request_call`; an unknown value is a 400 under `fieldErrors.source`; a JSON array body still lands under `_form`). `CALL_SCHEMA` keeps every request-a-call rule and message; `CONTACT_SCHEMA` takes `topic` (CONTACT_TOPICS), `name`, `email`, optional `firm` (stored `''`, D-13), optional `reference` (`Reference is too long` above 200), `message` (`Enter a message.` / `Message is too long`, stored in `notes`). Name and email share fields so both flows say `Enter your name.` / `Enter your email address.`. Responses: honeypot and success tail `{ ok: true, status: "stored" }`; `{ ok: true, status: "duplicate" }` only from the request-a-call 23505 branch. The 429 (both), 503 and 500 strings name no address. Founder notify subject is `Contact form (<topic label>): <name>` for contact and unchanged for a call; every interpolated field goes through `escapeHtml`.
- **Types.** `for_quants_leads` Row/Insert/Update hand-patched with `source`, `topic`, `reference`; tripwire comment and `notify_*` columns untouched (critical-regressions 184/184 green), plus a dated note that these three are hand-added until PROD applies the migration.
- **Modal.** Outcome by status per the UI-SPEC table; `RENDERED_FIELD_KEYS` implements the Rendered-key rule; thrown fetch shows the fixed network string. `Prefer email?`, `MAILTO_HREF` and the `source: "mailto"` click event are deleted. The duplicate view reuses the success layout.
- **CRM.** `LEAD_SELECT` and `ForQuantsLeadRow` carry the three columns; each row has a `Contact form` / `Request a call` label, `Topic: <label>`, and `Reference:` in `.font-metric` (`—` when empty); an empty firm renders `—`. No badge built (RESEARCH Finding 8).
