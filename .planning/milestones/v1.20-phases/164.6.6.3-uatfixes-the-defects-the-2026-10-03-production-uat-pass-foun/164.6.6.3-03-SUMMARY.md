# Phase 164.6.6.3 Plan 03: the jobs terminal's scrub now deletes its deal caches Summary

Every `ipc_fault` recycle of the jobs terminal now passes `delete_trades=1`, so each account is new to the terminal afterwards. That is safe because both callers of the deal-history read wait for a fresh login's history to settle (plans 01 and 02). A trades delete that errored or came back unreadable is logged at ERROR with `trades_kept=delete_errored`, never as a clean recycle.
