## Decisions Made

- **Zero-rows-on-write answers `DASHBOARD_WRITE_INDETERMINATE` (500), not `DASHBOARD_ROW_STALE` (404).** The plan's action text pointed at `strategies/[id]/name/route.ts`'s "update-vs-read split" without naming the exact literal for this route's TOCTOU case (ownership already proven at the pre-read, unlike that route's single-statement WHERE-clause 404). Resolved by reusing `DASHBOARD_WRITE_INDETERMINATE` byte-for-byte (same code, same sentence, same status) for both the zero-rows TOCTOU and the genuine `updateErr` transport fault — both are "an UPDATE was sent and this arm cannot verify whether it landed," which is exactly that code's stated meaning. Recorded here since the plan's phrasing was interpretable more than one way.
- **`retries: SEAM_RETRIES` (the shared constant, currently `0`), not a hand-typed literal `0`.** The plan's action text says `validate-key-serialized` "sets `retries: 0` explicitly" — at HEAD it actually reads `retries: SEAM_RETRIES`. Mirrored the actual sibling row's pattern (Rule 11 — match the codebase, not a paraphrase of it); the effective value is identical either way and `EXPECTED_RETRIES["keys-rotate-secret"]` still pins `0`.
