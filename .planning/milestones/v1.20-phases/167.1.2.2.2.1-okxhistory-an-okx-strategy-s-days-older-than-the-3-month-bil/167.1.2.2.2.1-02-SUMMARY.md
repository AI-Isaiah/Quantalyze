# Phase 167.1.2.2.2.1 Plan 02: Archive parse, classify and reconcile Summary

**Three pure modules that read an OKX quarterly archive CSV with `Decimal` against a pinned header and quarter range, classify bills by a committed `subType` table, and reconcile per-day legs against live legs at a hard-coded one-cent tolerance, each misreading stopping with a named class and no amounts.**

## Performance

- **Duration:** ~40 min (approximate, start time not captured)
- **Completed:** 2026-10-15T16:59Z
- **Tasks:** 3 (1 tracer, 2 TDD)
- **Files created:** 7 (3 modules, 2 test files, 2 fixtures); 123 tests

## Accomplishments

- `okx_archive_files.parse_archive_csv` turns bytes into `ArchiveFile` (frozen, `Decimal` money, 92 or 91 covered days) or raises `ArchiveParseError` with one of ten stop classes (`undecodable_file`, `malformed_csv`, `header_drift`, `malformed_row`, `unparseable_ts`, `empty_file`, `quarter_range_mismatch`, `missing_bill_id`, `duplicate_bill_conflict`, `unparseable_amount`). Details hold dates, labels, counts and line numbers only; tests assert that no fixture amount appears in any detail.
- `okx_archive_classify.aggregate_archive` classifies by `subType`, applies the live instType filter to REALIZED only, keeps funding as its own stream with no instType filter, and reports OPTION/EVENTS/blank realized rows per month as an excluded class. It raises `subtype_map_drift`, `unknown_subtype_nonzero`, `non_usd_ccy` (with a per-ccy census of row counts and months) and `duplicate_bill_conflict`. It also returns the `census`, `unknown_zero_effect` and the per-ccy `bal_continuity` information reading.
- `okx_archive_reconcile` carries `TOLERANCE_USD` (one literal, no parameter, no environment read), `overlap_window`, and `reconcile_leg`, which compares day by day and on the window total and treats a missing day as zero on its side.
- The synthetic fixtures (20 and 14 rows) carry hand-computed oracles written as `Decimal` literals in the tests; none is computed by the modules.
