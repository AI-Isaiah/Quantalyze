# Phase 167.1.2.2.2.1 Plan 20: unchainable_row diagnostic and zero-effect blank-ccy skip Summary

**An `unchainable_row` stop now says how many bills stopped it, why (blank ccy with an effect, or a non-digit billId), in which quarter and between which UTC days, and a blank-ccy bill with balChg, pnl and fee all 0 is left out of the bal chain and counted in `blank_ccy_skipped`.**

## Accomplishments

- `verify_bal_chain` tallies the bills it cannot chain per file (`rows`, `blank_ccy`, `nondigit_bill_id`, `first_day`, `last_day` beside `ts_min_day` / `ts_max_day`), and raises the totals plus `files[]`. `rows` counts each stopping bill once.
- `last_error` continues after `cause=unchainable_row` with `rows= blank_ccy= nondigit_bill_id= first_day= last_day= rows_by_quarter=<YYYY-Qn>:<n>,...`. `_unchainable_tokens` renders anything that is not a clean count or ISO day as `unreadable` and never raises. The other `archive_incomplete` texts are byte-equal.
- `info.bal_chain` carries the totals and the per-file counts and days. Every reading and stop detail carries `blank_ccy_skipped` (0 when none).
- The skip is inside the chaining loop (first statement), so no `""` ccy entry exists and `previous` is never set from a skipped bill. `aggregate_archive` runs first and is unchanged.
- The runbook names `blank_ccy_skipped`, the `unchainable_row` readings and tokens, the `(blank)` census count, and subtracts skipped bills in the `total_pairs` arithmetic.
