The days two OKX strategies lost beyond the 3-month `bills-archive` reach are rebuilt from OKX's async
quarterly `bills-history-archive`, recorded as reconstructed, and reconciled to the cent against the
days the live derive already covers. ROADMAP `### Phase 167.1.2.2.2.1` success criteria 1-5.

The work list is TRADESYNC plan 17's (`167.1.2.2.2-17-SUMMARY.md`, "OKXHISTORY work list"), re-measured
on PROD 2026-10-15 (counts and dates only; ordinals below are this phase's, ordered by status then id
among the 4 non-archived OKX strategies):

| ordinal | status | key | `legacy_ranges` | `unrecovered_gaps` | in scope |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | pending_review | connected | 2026-02-23..05-23 | 2026-05-24..08-01 (70 d) | REBUILD (D-01) |
| 2 | published | connected | 2026-02-23..05-06 | 2026-05-07..08-01 (87 d) | REBUILD (D-01) |
| 3 | published | disconnected | 2026-02-23..05-15 | none | EXCLUDED (D-02) |

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Backfill scope (founder: "Gaps only, check the 0.0s (Recommended)")
- **D-01:** Rebuild ONLY the two named `unrecovered_gaps` (strategies 1 and 2; 157 days in all, inside
  2026 Q2 and Q3). The carried-forward `legacy_ranges` (February-May) are NOT replaced, and strategy 4's
  stored January-April daily P&L is NOT extended into its series.
- **D-02:** The strategy on the disconnected key is EXCLUDED. Reading a disconnected user's exchange
  account is what TRADESYNC refused; its history stays legacy.
- **D-03:** Strategy 4's stored 0.0-return days in June (24 of 30) and July (31 of 31) are checked
  READ-ONLY against the Q2/Q3 archive. If the archive shows realized P&L on any day stored as 0.0, the
  check fails loud and reports the dates and both figures. Nothing is rewritten: any fix is a separate
  founder decision.
- **D-04:** The capability ships as a reusable durable job, triggered by an operator runbook for today's
  work list. Automatic triggering on new OKX key onboarding is out of scope (deferred).

### What a rebuilt day is to a reader (founder: "Count it, one public note (Recommended)")
- **D-05 (storage, SC-2, TRADESYNC D-03):** `strategy_analytics.series_provenance.reconstructed_ranges`
  = `[{from, to, source: 'okx_bills_history_archive', recorded_at}]`, dates `YYYY-MM-DD` inclusive. This
  is the key the TRADESYNC migration `20261010130000` reserved. NO per-row column on `csv_daily_returns`.
  When a range is reconstructed, the matching `unrecovered_gaps` entry stops being an unrecovered gap
  (the planner picks how: remove or narrow it in the same write).
- **D-06 (public text):** one plain line on the factsheet and on the printed tear sheet, shown to
  everyone (consistent with TRADESYNC D-06): "{from} – {to}: rebuilt from OKX's quarterly account
  statement, not the live feed." The chart is NOT marked or shaded.
- **D-07:** Rebuilt days COUNT in headline metrics (Sharpe, CAGR, drawdown, ...). Live OKX days are built
  from the same bills (realized `pnl + fee`, funding excluded), so the method is identical; only the
  delivery endpoint differs.

### Flows (founder: "Transfers = external flows (Recommended)")
- **D-08:** Beyond OKX's 90-day deposit retention, transfer bill types (type 1 and the related transfer
  types, enumerated by research from OKX's bill-type table) are EXTERNAL FLOWS, never P&L. A bill type the
  code does not classify FAILS LOUD (no silent bucket). Funding (type 8) stays its own stream, as today.

### Reconciliation (founder: "To the cent, abort on miss (Recommended)")
- **D-09 (what is compared):** archive file vs live-derived data on the days both cover (Q3 overlaps the
  live derive from 2026-08-02 to 2026-09-30). Both are the same OKX ledger by different endpoints, so the
  check catches parse, aggregation and truncation errors.
- **D-10 (tolerance):** $0.01 per day AND the same bound on the window total, MEASURED FIRST on the real
  overlap. ⛔ If calibration shows any difference above 1 cent, STOP and return to the founder. Do NOT
  widen the tolerance.
- **D-11 (on a miss):** a material miss aborts that strategy's WHOLE rebuild, writes nothing, and fails
  loud with the date range and both totals.
- **D-12 (precedence, TRADESYNC D-04):** live-derived days always win; the archive never overwrites a
  live-derived day.
- **D-13 (seam readings, founder 2026-10-15, "Decide after calibrate"):** `info.realized_balance_vs_rolled_nav.diff`
  and `info.flow_seam.diff` stay READINGS, not gates, for now. No limit is set in code. The founder sees both
  values in plan 14's calibrate result and sets any limit at plan 15's checkpoint. D-10's one-cent
  `TOLERANCE_USD` is unaffected.
- **D-14 (calibrate stop, founder 2026-10-22, "Ship diagnostic, re-run (Recommended)"):** plan 14's first
  calibrate (enqueued 2026-10-22 06:36Z) stopped `failed_final` on `quarter_range_mismatch quarter=2026-Q2`:
  some of the Q2 file's `ts` fall outside the calendar-UTC quarter, and `_parse_stop`
  (`analytics-service/services/okx_archive_job.py`) dropped the parser's observed dates. No PROD row
  changed. A new plan makes that stop carry the observed min/max UTC dates, the rows outside and the
  requested range (dates and counts only), ships it, and then calibrate is re-run ONCE to read the real
  dates. The fix for the mismatch itself (a UTC+8 quarter cut or a shifted quarter label) is a later
  founder decision taken on those dates. Plan 15 waits for a passing calibrate.
- **D-15 (quarter bounds, founder 2026-10-22, "HK bounds, edge days whole-or-missing (Recommended)"):** the D-14
  re-run (enqueued 2026-10-22 11:04Z, `failed_final` at 11:09Z) read `range_start=2026-04-01
  range_end_exclusive=2026-07-01 observed_min_day=2026-03-31 observed_max_day=2026-06-07 rows_outside=1`: OKX
  cuts quarters on UTC+8 (Hong Kong) time, not a shifted label. Each file is accepted on UTC+8 quarter bounds
  ([start 00:00 UTC+8, end 00:00 UTC+8), i.e. 16:00Z the day before), and daily totals stay on UTC days, as the
  live derive uses. A quarter-edge UTC day (03-31, 06-30, 09-30, 12-31) counts only when both neighbouring files
  were read; otherwise it stays missing and named (TRADESYNC D-02), never a partial day. D-10 still compares UTC
  days. A row outside the UTC+8 bounds still stops `quarter_range_mismatch`.
- **D-16 (unchainable rows, founder 2026-10-22, "Diagnose + skip safe rows (Recommended)"):** calibrate run 3
  (enqueued 13:20Z, `failed_final` about 13:26Z, on the D-15 code) stopped `archive_incomplete
  cause=unchainable_row`: `verify_bal_chain` (`okx_archive_classify.py`) found at least one row with a blank
  `ccy` or a non-digit `billId`, and the stop dropped which and how many. One fix: the stop records the cause
  split (`blank_ccy`, `nondigit_bill_id`), the row count per file and the first/last UTC day (counts and dates
  only); a blank-ccy row with zero balance effect is skipped from the chain and counted, since it cannot move
  the balance; a non-digit `billId` still stops, with those details. Then calibrate is re-run once.
- **D-17 (billId export format, orchestrator decision 2026-10-22, within founder D-16 "Diagnose + skip safe
  rows (Recommended)"):** calibrate run 4 (2026-10-22 15:30Z, on the plan 20 code) stopped `archive_incomplete
  cause=unchainable_row rows=1606 blank_ccy=0 nondigit_bill_id=1606`, `rows_by_quarter=2026-Q2:596,2026-Q3:1010`:
  every row of both real files carries a `billId` that fails `isascii() and isdigit()`, so the cause is a
  systematic export format, not bad rows. One fix: `parse_archive_csv` (`okx_archive_files.py`) strips surrounding
  whitespace, tab, apostrophe and double-quote characters from `billId` before any check, which leaves the digits
  unchanged. A `billId` still not ASCII digits after that stops `nondigit_bill_id` as before, and the stop also
  names a value-free shape of the first offending id: digits mapped to `9`, ASCII letters to `A`, other characters
  kept as their Unicode category or a fixed escape name for tab, space and quote, plus the length. Never the id
  itself. Then calibrate is re-run once.

### Carried from TRADESYNC (binding)
- TRADESYNC D-02: a day neither source covers stays MISSING, never 0.0, never interpolated; the factsheet
  names it. A rebuilt range that leaves days uncovered keeps those days named.
- `[167.1.2.2.2-OKX-PERSISTENT-TRUNCATION]` (TODOS, owner this phase): research measures whether the live
  reach starting 2026-08-02 for strategies 1 and 2 (rather than ~07-14) is quiet trading or the page cap;
  the planner decides whether the fix lands here.
- Never invent data: an uncomputable value is null.

### Must-settle in research (founder instruction, 2026-10-15)
- **Currency:** `fetch_daily_pnl` (`analytics-service/services/exchange.py`) sums `pnl + fee` across every
  `ccy` with no conversion. Research settles whether the archive rows for the in-scope account are all
  USDT. If they are not, that is a PLANNING INPUT (stated in RESEARCH.md and handled by a plan), never a
  silent assumption. The live-path defect itself is booked in `TODOS.md` as
  `[167.1.2.2.2.1-OKX-DAILY-PNL-MIXED-CCY]`.
- **Quarter labels:** OKX's doc example labels "2024 Q2" as [2024-07-01, 2024-10-01). Research (or the
  first real file) verifies the label-to-date mapping from the file's `ts` values; the code asserts the
  range of every file it ingests.
- **Unified account / Read permission:** the archive is "only applicable to the data from the unified
  account". Not yet measured for the in-scope account.

### Founder steps (STOP and return the exact step when reached)

- The real archive apply-and-download for 2026 Q2 and Q3, which also calibrates D-10.

  register: SSRF / zip bomb).
- How the rebuilt days enter `csv_daily_returns` without being clobbered by the next live derive, and how
  the recompute hands over to the Wave 5 consolidated recompute (SC-5).
