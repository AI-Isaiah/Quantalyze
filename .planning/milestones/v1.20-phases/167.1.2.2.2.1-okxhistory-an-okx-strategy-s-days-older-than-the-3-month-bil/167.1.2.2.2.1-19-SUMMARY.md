### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug in the plan's re-pin] `Q3_CENSUS` is also the base of the classification-stop census literals**
- **Found during:** Task 1 (re-pins in `test_okx_archive_job.py`)
- **Issue:** `CENSUS_ON_STOP` was built as `{**Q3_CENSUS, ...}`. The plan has `Q3_CENSUS` gain `edge_days`, but `_stop_census` deliberately keeps its four count keys (plan `<context>`, and the runbook text this plan adds). Adding the key to `Q3_CENSUS` alone would have turned the two `TestIngestStops` stop-census cases (`non_usd_ccy`, `unknown_subtype_nonzero`) red, outside the twelve the plan names.
- **Fix:** split the four count keys into `Q3_CENSUS_COUNTS`; `Q3_CENSUS = {**Q3_CENSUS_COUNTS, "edge_days": ...}`; `CENSUS_ON_STOP` now builds from `Q3_CENSUS_COUNTS`, so those literals are byte-for-byte what they were. Also added a top-level `import re` to the job test file for the `YYYY-MM-DD` check in `TestResultShape`.
- **Files modified:** `analytics-service/tests/test_okx_archive_job.py`
- **Verification:** seven OKX files `620 passed` after, then `621`; `TestIngestStops` green unchanged
- **Commit:** `4e01fff8c`

**Total deviations:** 1 auto-fixed (1 test-literal base split). **Impact:** none on behaviour; the stop-census contract (four keys) is now explicitly pinned by a separate constant.
