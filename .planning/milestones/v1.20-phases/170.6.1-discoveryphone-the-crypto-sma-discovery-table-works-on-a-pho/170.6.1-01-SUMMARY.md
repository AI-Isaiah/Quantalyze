## Deviations from Plan

1. **[Coordinator constraint] PROD ledger tip not re-measured.** The plan's Task 1 step 1 calls for a read-only PROD marker + `max(version)` query from the main checkout. The run brief forbade any PROD, TEST or `--linked` command, so that reading was skipped and the planning addendum's reading was used, cross-checked against origin/main and every branch/worktree. Risk is low (the chosen stamp is above origin/main's newest, and merges to main auto-apply to PROD, so PROD's tip cannot exceed main's) but the plan's literal marker-first reading was not taken. Re-measure before merge if wanted.
2. **[Rule 2 - additive] Two arms beyond the plan's seven.** TV-secdef was split into TV-secdef and TV-searchpath, and TV-unpublished-composite was added. A single arm with one twin leaves the other half unproven by mutation (a mutation that only flips SECDEF cannot show the search_path pin bites; a published-gate mutation on the single-key arm cannot show the strategy_keys arm's gate bites). Total 10 arms, above the plan's minimum 8.
3. **[Scope] No mixed-case lowercase arm.** The plan's example `lower` -> `upper` twin would redden TV-1 first (TV-1 expects `okx`), and a mixed-case fixture cannot be seeded on the real schema because `api_keys_exchange_check` forbids upper case. TV-distinct-lower's twin is UNION -> UNION ALL; the `lower()` call stays as belt-and-braces over the CHECK.
4. **[Commit guard] Branch namespace.** The per-task commit guard's positive allow-list expects `agent-*` branches; this worktree is the coordinator-assigned per-plan branch `wt/170.6.1-p01`. The protected-branch and worktree-identity checks passed, and commits stayed on that branch.
5. **Real-schema gate run not performed locally.** The local Supabase stack shared by other checkouts was already running (same project id); booting this worktree's lane would have collided with it, so the gate was proven on the pg-lane (stand-in schema) only. The seed statements copy the already-CI-proven `test_strategy_keys_publish_integrity.sql` shapes; `sql-tests` in CI is the first real-schema execution.
6. **Flaky unrelated test.** `src/__tests__/drift-check-scripts.test.ts` "R2-W02" (machine-wide `mktemp` leak detector) failed once while the sibling executor was running scripts, and passed on isolated re-run (6 passed). Unrelated to this plan's files; not fixed.
