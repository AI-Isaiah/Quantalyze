### WR-02: removing the collapse changes laptop and tablet widths too, and the Strategy name is not pinned there

**Files modified:** `src/components/strategy/StrategyTable.tsx`, `src/components/strategy/StrategyTable.test.tsx`, `src/components/strategy/StrategyTable.default-mounts.test.tsx`, `src/components/strategy/__fixtures__/strategy-table-default-mounts.json`, `e2e/helpers/geometry.ts`, `e2e/layout-narrow.spec.ts`
**Commit:** `0995a4f36`
**Status:** fixed (design call, so a human should confirm the cap below)

**Applied fix (the code, decided from the code and from measurement):**
- The Strategy header, ranked-row cell and placeholder cell take a bare `left-0` below `@3xl` (the `@3xl:left-(--pin-strategy-left)` offset is unchanged at and above the step) and an UNGATED hairline (`border-r border-border`, was `@3xl:`-gated).
- **Rank and star do not pin below `@3xl`.** The name needs no offset from them: pinned at `left-0` it sticks once rank and star have scrolled past, and they scroll out from under it (the name is later in the DOM at the same z-index, so it paints over them in the header and in the body). Pinning them would spend about 100 px of a 326 px region on an ordinal and a star. Measured: the Discovery name column is 118.7 px at V360/V375/V390/V768 and 167.6 px at V1080, leaving 207 px of a 326 px region for the scrolling columns.
- **A width cap was needed and is the part to confirm.** First measurement, with the plain pin: `/strategies` (manager rows) at 360/375/390 failed, because the Strategy column there measured 473 px, wider than the region. A pinned cell wider than the region is clamped by its table (the header sat 132 to 147 px off the left edge at the scroll end), so it cannot stay at the edge and it covers every column under it. The ranked-row cell now carries `max-w-[60cqw]` with an `@3xl:max-w-none` lift, and the badge-and-sync row beneath the name gained `@max-3xl:flex-wrap` so the cap can take effect. 60cqw leaves 40% of the region for the columns that scroll under the name. Desktop at and above `@3xl` is unchanged apart from the new classes (the fixture pins that).
- D-02 still holds at 360/375/390 and at V768/V1080: every column reachable by scrolling, no `More` disclosure, hint absent at the scroll end, Simulate Impact inside the region, the element at its own centre point, and not under the fade (the pinned name sits on the left, Simulate Impact on the right).

**Applied fix (tests):**
- `StrategyTable.test.tsx`: the 170.2 D-05 census (`every sticky horizontal offset ... behind @3xl:`) is rewritten to the new split: exactly three bare `left-0` tokens, each on a line that also carries `@3xl:left-(--pin-strategy-left)`; rank and star stay `@3xl:`-gated (3 + 3 + 3 + 3 = 12 tokens). The header, data-cell and placeholder class pins are updated; the data cell pin names the cap.
- `strategy-table-default-mounts.json`: edited by hand, hunks caused by this change only. 3 header strings, 11 ranked-row cell strings, 1 placeholder cell string, 11 badge-row strings. `default-mounts` stays green (3 passed); a dated paragraph in its docblock lists the hunk classes (as plan 02 did).
- `e2e/helpers/geometry.ts`: `assertColumnUnpinnedBelow3xl` (asserted the opposite relation, used only by `layout-narrow.spec.ts`) is replaced by `assertColumnPinnedBelow3xl`: after scrolling to the end the header and every body cell are within 1 px of the region's left edge, are the element at their own centre point, started to the right of that edge at scrollLeft 0 (not vacuous), and the column is at most 70% of the region wide.
- `e2e/layout-narrow.spec.ts`: the `SC6-DISCOVERY` block asserts the pin at every viewport (V360, V375, V390, V768, V1080) with `requireBelow3xl: true` so a non-narrow container fails rather than passes; the D-01/D-02 checks (every column rendered, hint present at the start, hint absent at the end, Simulate Impact clear) are no longer gated on `width <= 390`, so they run at every viewport in the block; both `MYSTRATTABLE` describes call the pinned helper (they previously asserted unpinned, 170.7 D-04, which D-09 reverses for this shared table). Describe titles are kept (cited by name in older phases); test titles say "the Strategy name is pinned".

**RED evidence, each new check run on the pre-fix source** (backup, `cp` of `git show HEAD:`, `cmp` after restore):
- vitest `StrategyTable.test.tsx` + `default-mounts`: 9 failed / 46 passed (3 mounts of the fixture, the header, star and data-cell pins, and the three census tests).
