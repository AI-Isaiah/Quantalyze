## Summary

No MEDIUM, HIGH or CRITICAL finding. What was checked, and how:

- **Re-base.** The bridge body in `20261023120000` was diffed, comments stripped, against
  `20261009120000`. That is the newest earlier definition, and `origin/main` at the merge-base
  defines no later one. The only differences are the ones D-03, R-03, R-06 and D-08 call for:
  - the date assignment is removed from branches (a), (a)-membership, (b) and (c);
  - `v_side_failed_only` and `v_side_trigger_not_done` become one flag, `v_side_trigger`;
  - the D-09 release subquery is deleted.

  The early return, the branch (c) status hold, the row guard and the marker CASEs are re-keyed
  on the new flag. Signature, SECURITY DEFINER, search_path, lock and ACL are unchanged. The
  schema snapshot is identical to the migration body.
- **Rollback.** The rollback's function body is byte-identical to the `20261009120000` body.
  It has a catalogue-only precondition, so it refuses a database without the change. Its
  postcondition checks the flags, overloads, COMMENT and ACL. The comment restores are the exact
  inverse of the migration's guarded replacements.
- **Every writer of `computed_at` (Python, TS, SQL), and every success exit.**
  - Python: the only success writers are `_mark_complete` (the only `return {"status": "complete"}`
    in `run_csv_strategy_analytics`) and `headline_payload` (the only status-bearing success write
    in `run_stitch_composite_job`). Both send `computation_status` ∈ {complete,
    complete_with_warnings} in the same upsert, so 166.3.1's hold trigger does not discard the stamp.
  - Python, paths that compute nothing: the protected-refresh restore and the
    `_guard_failure_payload_inplace` paths carry no `computed_at`, which is correct.
  - TS: no writer of the column.
  - SQL: apart from the bridge, nothing writes it; the remaining SQL writes are to
    `strategy_analytics_series.computed_at`.
  - Chain topology: `JOB_CHAIN_FOLLOW_ON` means a strategy-mode sync always ends in the CSV runner
    or a failure, so a real finish always stamps.
- **Every branch of the new bridge, traced** with side-only, mixed and counting-only in-flight
  sets, on complete, cww, failed with a sentence, failed with no sentence, and absent rows. No
  branch writes the date. Statuses, sentences and markers match D-03, D-08, R-05 and R-06.
- **Race handling.** The `.neq("status","done")` guards and the TS zero-row re-read are correct.
  The re-read throws on a lost row or a re-read error and returns quietly only on `done`. Both the
  Python and the TS test suites contain a test that fails without the guard (ran: pytest 60
  passed, vitest 24 passed).
- **Gate arms.**
  - N1 to N6, N8 and N9 mutate the shipped migration, and each also has to stand down the
    `v_no_date_write_ok` apply-time anchor, which gives two layers.
  - N7 and N10 mutate the test's own runner-shaped write. They are controls, not bridge detectors.
  - Any bridge date write is refused at apply time by `v_no_date_write_ok`.

The four items below are LOW. They are listed because the brief asked for a judgement on the
display comments. Per the repo's review rule they do not call for a fixer round.

### IN-01: Stale "bridge re-stamps computed_at" comments survive in five display files and one catalogue comment, and 07-SUMMARY says every reader comment was corrected

**Files:**
- `src/components/strategy/SyncBadge.tsx:18`, `:173`
- `src/components/strategy/SyncProgress.tsx:418`, `:436-439`
- `src/components/strategy/StrategyTable.tsx:1530`

- `.planning/phases/166.5-*/166.5-07-SUMMARY.md:55` ("every reader comment tells the new truth")

**Issue:** Each comment states the premise this phase removed: the status bridge re-stamps
`computed_at` on every job transition or failure. Behaviour does not depend on any of them. Each
component's logic is still correct under the new rule:
- the badge reads `trades_fetched_at`;
- the failed-row gates are founder-scoped under `[166.5-STALE01-PREMISE]`;
- SyncProgress's KCS-18 job-state check is still needed, for a different reason: the runner now
  stamps while its own job is still `running`, so a stamped row can be read before
  `mark_compute_job_done`.

Severity: LOW. It is not user-facing and not data-integrity. The risk is a future editor acting on
a false premise. SyncProgress `:436` is the one most likely to mislead, because it gives the wrong
reason for a check that has to stay. The SUMMARY claim is inaccurate as written.

**Fix:** Rewrite each to the new truth in the style of `queries.ts`: "when this was written the
bridge re-stamped …; since 166.5 the runner stamps on its success write only". In SyncProgress,
restate why KCS-18 stays: the runner's stamp lands before its own job is marked done. The view
column comment needs a guarded `COMMENT ON COLUMN` in a later migration, or should be booked.
Optional this round.

### IN-02: R-05's premise "the Eclipse shape has NO sentence" is contradicted by the PROD record; S1 to S4 seed a shape the bridge cannot produce

**Files:**
- `supabase/migrations/20261023120000_sync_status_runner_stamps_computed_at.sql:46-52`
- `supabase/tests/test_sync_status_analytics_scope.sql:188` (and the S2 to S4 seeds at `:253`, `:313`, `:373`)

**Issue:** `computation_error_copy` is total (`20260826120000`, ELSE arm; the HONEST-01 check
raises on NULL). So every row branch (b) ever failed carries a non-NULL sentence. The real Eclipse
row carried "a retries-exhausted sentence" before it healed
(`164.6.6.3.4-04-SUMMARY.md:68`). Under the shipped predicate (`failed AND computation_error IS
NOT NULL`), a realistic false-failed side-only row is therefore HELD `failed` under every side
trigger. It clears only on a real recompute or a NULL-trigger re-sync. The header's "still
resolves to complete under a side trigger" applies only to the NULL-sentence fixture.

No live victim is known:
- since 164.6.6.3.4, side failures no longer fail a row;
- Eclipse was healed on 2026-10-08;
- D-05's forced recompute heals any remaining row.

LOW.

**Fix:** Correct the R-05 sentence in the header and CHANGELOG ("resolves only when the row
carries no sentence, a shape the bridge itself does not produce"). Optionally, add one S-arm
variant seeded with the generic `computation_error_copy` sentence so the gate pins the realistic
case, which is held.

### IN-03: Python retraction logs "refresh_marker_retracted" even when the done-guard matched no row

**File:** `analytics-service/services/ingestion/long_fetch.py:137-144`

**Issue:** When the `.neq("status", "done")` update matches zero rows, the WARNING still says
the marker was retracted. That happens when the job finished between the read and the write,
which is exactly the D-07 race. The TS twin distinguishes the two cases. This is log-only and the
operator channel, so LOW.

**Fix:** Read `.data` from the update response; it returns rows by default in supabase-py. Log
`refresh_marker_not_retracted_job_done` when it is empty.

### IN-04: "Deploy skew: harmless to correctness" understates the window where a real finish does not move the date

**File:** `.planning/phases/166.5-*/166.5-08-SUMMARY.md:182` (the release notes carry the same wording)

**Issue:** Once PROD applies the migration, a real recompute leaves `computed_at` unmoved until
the Railway worker deploys. This is the "stops moving on a real finish" direction. The worker
deploys only after main CI is green, and the migration header itself expects
`baseline-content-drift` to be red until the post-apply re-dump. So the window can last hours,
not minutes. The error is in the safe direction (an older date than the truth) and Wave 5
precondition 2 covers it. LOW.

**Fix:** Reword the claim as "the date can lag a real recompute until the worker deploys". Ship
the baseline re-dump promptly so that main goes green.
