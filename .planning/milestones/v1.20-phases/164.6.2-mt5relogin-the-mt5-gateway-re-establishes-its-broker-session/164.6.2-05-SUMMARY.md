## What closed it, and why the attribution is not a guess

`what closed it:` **the MT5 terminal's OWN saved-session re-authorization when the gateway container
restarted.** Four measured facts, the last three of them absences:

1. The container really restarted, 9 s after the stamp — `X connection to :1 broken` at
   `10:46:44Z`, then `server started on [0.0.0.0]:8001` at `10:46:55Z`. ⭐ The rpyc worker-thread
   counter RESET from `Thread-342` to `Thread-1`, so this is a new process, not a reconnect.
2. **No analytics deployment on 2026-09-15 at all** — the current one still dates from
   `2026-09-14T14:08:11Z`. The operator's analytics restart (the plan's active phase) never ran.
3. **No new `mt5 boot heal:` line.** The heal fires at analytics startup only (D-08).
4. **Nothing logged in.** Between the bridge coming up and the first authorized reading the gateway
   accepted exactly ONE client — a loopback connection served by `Thread-1`, the read-only
   diagnostic's own probe, which calls `initialize()` and `terminal_info()` and never `login()`.

## ⛔ Three limits on the closure, recorded so nobody reads more into it

1. **The delta is an UPPER BOUND.** `railway restart` held the executor's shell for its first 180 s,
   so no probe could be issued across the event; the true recovery could be ≈23 s. The gap in the
   poll log is stated, not smoothed over.
2. **The boot heal NEVER RAN** — deployed, armed and idle. This phase's own mechanism is STILL
   UNVALIDATED in production, and RESEARCH assumption **A3 remains untested**.
3. **A restart does not always recover a session.** This one recovered a **LAPSED** session from a
   still-usable saved credential. That is a different state from the **UNUSABLE**-credential case
   the mt5 arm's shipped remedy addresses, and the two are not distinguishable from outside the
   terminal — which is precisely why that remedy sends a human to the Journal tab.

## ⭐ The finding that inverts the expected answer

RESEARCH expected `UNBOUNDED-WITHOUT-ANALYTICS-RESTART`. The measurement says `BOUNDED`:
**recovery is fast; nothing TRIGGERS it.** Supplementary reading from the hourly prober's own
archives — the session was authorized at `2026-09-15T02:34:53Z` and dark at `10:40:13Z`, so it was
lost inside a **≈8 h 05 m** span having survived ≈11 h 39 m, with nothing in the system able to
notice or act. **The exposure is the dark window, not the recovery**, and that is what Phase 164.6.4
must size against.

⛔ The `08:12Z` scheduled run is recorded as **NOT-READ**, never as a `-6`. A scheduled run carries
no `NARROWED DIAGNOSTIC dispatch of arm mt5:` execution anchor, and its log is polluted by the
self-test step, which prints `mt5-not-authorized` by name, prints whole remedy strings, and even
prints `No defects in the narrowed scope.` for its own fixtures. An unreadable reading is not a
verdict.
