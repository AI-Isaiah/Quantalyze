<objective>
SC-1: settle mechanism A (intraday capital) against mechanism B (cash-basis options) on the PROD
evidence, before any code is written, with a read-only re-crawl of the big days of keys A and C.

Purpose: D-02 and D-03 make this re-crawl the phase's first step and its gate. Every code plan
(185-02 .. 185-07) assumes A. If the verdict is B, the phase replans. A mixed verdict goes to the
founder as open question 5. The re-crawl also produces the evidence for planner decision P-1 (how a
day's P&L rows are placed between flow instants). Settlement and delivery lumps can sit at their
booking instant or in the start-of-day base, and RESEARCH Pitfall 2 shows the choice can move a
withdrawal day from 0.1 to 1.0.

Output: readings and verdict lines in this plan's SUMMARY. No tracked file changes. The orchestrator
runs this plan itself. It is never dispatched to an executor (CONTEXT, Deferred: "SC-1 and SC-4 are
run by the orchestrator only").
</objective>
