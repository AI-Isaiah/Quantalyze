<objective>
The ingest seam for D-04: a Deribit key's options-basis ledger, assembled from the same crawl as its
cash ledger, with no second transaction-log crawl.

Purpose: RESEARCH Q1 and Q4 make "one crawl" a hard requirement (a second full crawl can take about
12 minutes on a large account against a 900 s derive budget), and PATTERNS found only the NAV-twin
precedent for reusing a crawl. This plan extracts the post-crawl assembly so any basis can be
assembled from retained rows, and adds the function plan 185-08 calls. It is basis-agnostic, so it
does not wait on founder question F1.

Output: the extracted assembly, the keyword, the report field, the options-basis function, a shared
SC-5 fixture and RED-first tests. With plans 185-08 and 185-09 this is PR-2.
