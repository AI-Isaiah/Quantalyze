## Decisions Made

1. **The apply-time block does not assert the widened literal.** See deviation 2 — this is the plan's one substantive design change and it is what makes arm P observable at all.
2. **Check 9/10 assert the ABSENCE of the two deliberately-excluded statuses** instead. That is the CTX-01 boundary ("admit this status and nothing else; do not mirror `ALLOWED_STRATEGY_STATUSES`") expressed as a machine check, it survives every mutation this corpus applies, and it genuinely needs the comment strip — the body's own comment names both excluded values, so an unstripped absence probe would false-FAIL on its own prose.
3. **Arm P's fixture is only moderately stale (30 days).** The fan-out orders candidates `ORDER BY last_return_date ASC` and hands out bounded slots; a century-old fixture would outrank the G1/G2 cohorts and steal them, and the g2_v1/g2_v2 stagger that makes arm G2's cap-neutering deterministic is built out of that same ordering.
4. **`COMMENT ON FUNCTION` re-issued unchanged.** It describes the cohort as "stale, non-composite, key-eligible" and names no status, so it stayed true across the widening.
