<objective>
Add the gate that makes this phase worth more than a one-line fix: a cohort-agreement arm proving
that the staleness view and the fan-out cannot disagree about which lifecycle statuses are in play.

**Purpose.** The defect being repaired is not "the fan-out forgot one status". It is that a status
shipped in July 2026 and a fan-out shipped in September 2026 and **nothing ever compared the two**.
`ledger_refresh_staleness` carries no lifecycle filter at all, by design, so it will surface any
status; the fan-out carries a hand-written literal, so it admits only what someone remembered. Six
stale rows, zero eligible. A future sixth status repeats it exactly, silently, and nobody finds out
until a production tick enqueues nothing.

**Output.** One `RED-UNDER` arm that derives both sets from catalogue metadata and fails when they
diverge, neuter-verified against a genuinely NEW status rather than against the status this phase
happens to be adding.

## The decision this plan implements

**CTX-06** — *"A cohort-agreement arm is added: no status may appear as stale in
`ledger_refresh_staleness` that the fan-out then refuses. This is the defect as a CLASS."*
Plus **CTX-07** (neuter-verified) and **CTX-08** (`ARMS_FLOOR` raised to the measured value) for
this arm. The `CTX-nn` mapping is in plan 01's objective; `D-01` in this repository always means
the composite/membership deferral and is a different thing.

## Two measurements that determine the arm's shape

**1. `strategies.status` is `text` plus a CHECK constraint, NOT an enum.** Measured in
`supabase/schema/baseline.sql`: `CONSTRAINT "strategies_status_check" CHECK (status = ANY (ARRAY[...]))`
over five values, and a repo-wide search for a `CREATE TYPE` naming a status enum returns zero
hits. So `enum_range(...)` — which RESEARCH.md sketched and flagged as unconfirmed — would not even
compile. The domain comes from `pg_get_constraintdef`.

**2. That constraint does NOT exist on a pg-lane today.** `scripts/pg-lane/fixtures/01-fixture-core.sql`
declares `strategies.status` as a bare nullable `TEXT` with no constraint, and this gate's apply
list does not carry anything that adds one. So an arm reading `pg_get_constraintdef` on today's
lane gets NULL and can only MEASURE_FAIL. Two files could supply it:
`scripts/pg-lane/fixtures/30-fixture-strategies-status-default.sql` (the stand-in, used by one other
gate) and `supabase/migrations/20260716130000_strategies_status_private.sql` (the real migration
that widened the constraint to five values in the first place).
⭐ **The real migration is the one to apply**, and the reason is the neuter: GRAMMAR rule 4 refuses
a mutation targeting anything under `scripts/pg-lane/fixtures/`, and the stand-in's own header says
so in as many words. Only a real migration file can carry the twin that adds a sixth status — which
is the only mutation that proves this arm catches a FUTURE status rather than merely the present
one.
</objective>
