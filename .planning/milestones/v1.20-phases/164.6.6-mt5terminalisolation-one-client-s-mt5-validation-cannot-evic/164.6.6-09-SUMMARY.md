# Phase 164.6.6 Plan 09: Park alert level, as the founder wrote it, plus the two other T-07 alert paths

**A glitch-class park failure now pages unless the validation itself hit a bridge glitch. A cleanly detected master password followed by a glitch park now reaches a human. An unexpected exception in the park block pages `unrecognised_cause`, and a cancelled park await pages `park_cancelled` and keeps propagating. All of this holds at both validate sites.**

## The gap this closes

`164.6.6-VERIFICATION.md` (gaps_found) and `164.6.6-SECURITY.md` T-164.6.6-07 (OPEN, blocking). The founder's decision (CONTEXT D-07 part 2, "Park alert level", 2026-10-03) reads: "A park that fails for the same bridge-glitch reason the validation hit logs a WARNING only, which respects D-15. Every other skip or failure still pages: house credentials unset, the account refused, the stage or deadline exits, a fenced probe." Plan 06 decided the WARNING from the park's own error class alone, and `park_on_house_account` was never told how the validation ended. So a trade-capable validation followed by a glitch park left the master session on the validation terminal and paged nobody.

## What changed

- `services/mt5_probe.py`
  - The private `_is_park_bridge_glitch` was renamed to the public `is_bridge_glitch`. The body is unchanged. The docstring now says which arms the predicate decides and which are glitch by decision.
  - `park_on_house_account` takes `validation_hit_glitch: bool`, keyword-only, with no default. In its `Mt5ClientError` arm:
    - a glitch-class error AND the flag set means WARNING;
    - a glitch-class error without the flag means `_alert_park_failed("bridge_glitch")`;
    - refused and other client errors are unchanged.
  - The comments above `_PARK_BRIDGE_GLITCH_CAUSE`, the `_warn_park_bridge_glitch` docstring and its log text now state the founder's rule. None of them still says "would answer" or "same class a validation answers".
  - `park_cancelled` joined `_PARK_ALERT_CAUSES`.
- `routers/exchange.py::_validate_mt5_key_probe`
  - `validation_hit_glitch = False` is declared in function scope and added to `_connect_and_probe`'s `nonlocal` line.
  - It is set from `is_bridge_glitch(e)` on the C5 arm, after the IPC-fault branch. It is set to `True` on the D-15 arm (`not operator_fault`), which lives in the outer body.
  - It is passed to the park.
  - The outer `except Exception as park_exc` keeps its class-only ERROR line and then calls `report_park_skipped("unrecognised_cause")`.
  - A new `except asyncio.CancelledError` arm calls `report_park_skipped("park_cancelled")` and then a bare `raise`.
- `services/ingestion/mt5.py::Mt5Adapter.validate` gets the same changes. The flag is assigned from `is_bridge_glitch(e)` just before the bare `raise` of the transient path, and set to `True` just before the synthetic D-15 `raise Mt5ClientError(0, ...)`.
- `164.6.6-06-SUMMARY.md` gets seven dated `CORRECTED 2026-10-03 (plan 09)` notes beside the drifted wording, which is kept as lineage. In the frontmatter each note is its own quoted list item, and the frontmatter still parses.
