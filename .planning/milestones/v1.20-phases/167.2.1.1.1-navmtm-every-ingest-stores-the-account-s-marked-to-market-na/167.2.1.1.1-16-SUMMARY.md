# Phase 167.2.1.1.1 Plan 16: NAVMTM allocated-capital strategies follow marked NAV Summary

**An allocated-capital Deribit strategy now keeps its allocated-capital history up to its first in-window mark, books the D-04 step over the NAV twin's level of the same crawl, follows its key's marked NAV by the day-P&L roll from then on, keeps a closed mandate's tail, and refuses by name (`mark_allocated_nav_unreconciled`, writing nothing) only when it has a mark it cannot apply.**

## What was built

All in `run_derive_broker_dailies_job` (`analytics-service/services/job_worker.py`), plus three small module-level names (`_MARK_ALLOCATED_NAV_UNRECONCILED`, `_marks_in_mandate_window`, `_mandate_window_contains`).

- **One definition of the returns basis.** `_returns_are_allocated = venue == "deribit" and denominator_config is not None` moved up beside the config parse (it was defined at plan 07's seam). The Deribit branch and the seam both read it.
- **The twin request (before the crawl).** For an allocated strategy the derive reads the key's stored marks and requests `nav_twin=True` on the CASH-pass build only when an in-window mark exists, or `mark_day_for(_utc_now())` lies in the mandate window (this derive writes that mark before the seam re-reads). The keyword is passed only on this path.
- **The apply seam.** It now reads the marks for every strategy, filters them to the window for an allocated one (logging `outside=N inside=M` when any lie outside), and applies the survivors. An allocated strategy takes a new branch: the three helpers (`native_ledger_realized_terminal`, `_day_pnl`, `_composed_flows`) on `report.nav_twin_ledger`, then `apply_marks(..., roll_basis="day_pnl")`. The old `if not _returns_are_allocated:` wrapper was removed, so the seam body is dedented by four spaces (`git diff -w` of the plan is 186 lines of `job_worker.py`; the raw diff is larger).
- **W1 / L3.** No in-window mark: nothing to apply, the derive continues as before; if the twin was requested and is absent, one warning `nav_twin_unbuildable (error=<class> id=...)` is logged. An in-window mark and (a) no twin, or (b) a `NavReconstructionError` from a helper (section 5 included), is `mark_allocated_nav_unreconciled` through the existing refusal return: nothing written, one failed stamp with `heal_series=False`, a token-only detail, FAILED permanent. `MarkApplyRefusal` from the apply takes the same return with its own token.
- **W2 tail.** After the apply, when the allocated mandate has a `mandate_end`, `mandate_end < mark_day_for(_utc_now())` and `mandate_end` is after the last applied mark day, the combiner's own rows dated in (last applied mark day, `mandate_end`] are appended verbatim. While the window is open nothing is appended (the next mark's single return covers those days, D-05).
- **The simple-sum rule (NAVMTM D-14).** The derive's cumulative method is NOT changed. The rule is named in a comment at the seam and pinned by a test.

## The words for plan 14 (founder readout)

**The simple-sum rule (NAVMTM D-14), in the must-haves' words.** An allocated-capital strategy keeps its configured cumulative_method, and every persisted row, the post-S NAV returns and any W2 tail rows included, is summed: cumulative return = the sum of every row's return, max drawdown on that running sum, Sharpe and Sortino on the rows as today. From S on a day's return is the change in marked NAV over the prior marked level net of flows, not P&L over the allocated capital, and the sum of those is not the compounded NAV change.

**The window rule.** Marks dated after `mandate_end` are not applied (nor marks before the schedule's first `effective_from`). A closed mandate keeps its allocated rows after the last in-window mark through `mandate_end`. While the window is open, the days after the last mark are covered by the next mark.

**The refusal token for plans 11 and 14.** An allocated strategy whose NAV twin cannot be built or cannot pass section 5, while it has a stored in-window mark, is refused with **`mark_allocated_nav_unreconciled`** and refuses on every derive until the twin can be built (T-167.2.1.1.1-48: a named, nothing-written denial of service). The apply's own tokens (`mark_base_nonpositive`, `mark_base_unrollable`, `mark_terminal_day_mismatch`, `mark_input_nonfinite`, `mark_days_not_ascending`) also reach an allocated strategy. A twin that fails with no mark to apply is invisible except the one `nav_twin_unbuildable` warning.

## Plan 13's stated limit: an extraction on a currency's first or last activity day

Plan 13 said the twin reuses marks built from the allocated `native_pnl` span, so an extraction day that is a currency's first or last activity day lies outside that span, and asked plan 16 to confirm the twin's roll finds a mark for every day it values. Six cases run the REAL builder (over a faked crawl and feed), the REAL allocated combiner and the REAL helpers (`test_the_roll_on_a_currencys_last_activity_day`, `..._first_activity_day`). In every case the outcome is "valued to the hand-computed number" or "refused by name, nothing written":

| Case | Outcome |
|---|---|
| Extraction on the LAST day (03-06, terminal day), feed carries the day | Valued. Base `NAV(03-05) = 1.99 * 60,000 = 119,400`; the persisted returns equal `(121,380 - 119,400)/119,400` and `(122,000 - 121,380)/121,380`. |
| Same, feed has no price for the day | Refused: `mark_allocated_nav_unreconciled` (the twin's helper raised `InceptionReconciliationError`: the section-5 gate prices each currency on its own last balance day and a missing price is NaN). Nothing written, stale rows untouched. |
| Extraction liquidates the whole currency on the last day, price present / absent | Present: valued, same base. Absent: still refused by name (the section-5 gate again), although no BTC price is needed for the day's value. This is a conservative over-refusal, never a guessed price. |
| Extraction on the FIRST day (with the deposit), feed carries the day | Valued. The first day's value telescopes out of the base, which is `NAV(03-05)` only. |
| Same, feed has no price for the day | Refused earlier, by the BUILDER, permanent: the deposit is valued at that day's index, the same source the mark comes from, so a day without a mark is a day whose deposit cannot be valued. A first day cannot reach the twin without a mark. Nothing written. |

Why it holds: the roll steps back by `L(d-1) = L(d) - P_d - F_d` off the twin's own levels, so a mis-valued day inside the roll span cancels between the terminal and that day's P&L; what matters is the level on S-1, which needs a price, and the twin's valuation refuses when a day it must value has none.

## Known limits (recorded, not fixed)

- **A twin that cannot be built refuses an allocated strategy on every derive from its first in-window mark** until it can be built (T-48; named, nothing written). The failure that matters live is a price hole on a day the twin must value (the section-5 gate then refuses). Plan 11/14 read the risk and the outcome.
- **Conservative over-refusal.** A missing price on a currency's last balance day refuses even when that currency is liquidated that day (see the table above).
- **The mark-to-market second pass and the smoothed pass keep their realized (allocated) basis**: `mtm_returns` and the smoothed series are built by their own `combine_native_ledger` calls and are not marked. As in plan 07.
- **The twin's smoothed_mtm path** has no dedicated test (plan 13 limit, unchanged); the tests here use cash_settlement.
- **The derive persists no scalar metrics**: `metrics_json_by_basis` is null on the prestamp, so the simple-sum test reads the cumulative return off `derive_basis_series`' own result (spied). The published figure is the series runner's recompute from the persisted rows; plan 08 pins it.
- **Tail timing.** A derive that runs on the `mandate_end` day with its own mark refused has an open window by the rule, so its tail row appears the next day. The series is rewritten every derive, so it self-heals.
