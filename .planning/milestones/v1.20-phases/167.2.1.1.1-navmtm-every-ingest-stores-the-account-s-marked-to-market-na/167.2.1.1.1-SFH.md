## Findings

### F1. HIGH, user-facing and data-integrity: once marks exist, a dust, non-positive or errored current balance turns the derive into a permanent FAILED, every day
`analytics-service/services/job_worker.py:7486-7507` (terminal build), `analytics-service/services/nav_marks_apply.py:297-299` (the refusal), `job_worker.py:7515-7540` (the permanent FAILED plus strategy stamp). Composite sibling: `job_worker.py:10149-10172` (`_ccxt_apply_inputs`).

For ccxt, MT5 and sFOX the realized terminal is only built when `not balance_error and equity is not None and float(equity) > dust`. Otherwise `_apply_terminal` stays `None`. `apply_marks` with any pre-start input day (always true after history exists) and `term_day is None` raises `mark_base_unrollable`. The seam turns that into `error_kind="permanent"`, stamps the strategy analytics FAILED ("marked-to-market values could not be applied... needs nothing from you") and returns before any csv write.

Failure scenarios:
1. A manager winds down and withdraws, so equity falls to dust or zero. The mark itself is refused (`dust` or `nonpositive`), but stored marks from earlier days remain. Every later derive now FAILS permanently and the published factsheet flips to failed. Before this phase the same derive completed (the dust anchor path degrades with a flag).
2. One transient venue hiccup (`balance_error=True` from `fetch_account_equity_and_upnl_usd`, which returns the flag instead of raising) on any day after marks started becomes a permanent failure with no retry, and the strategy shows a failed status until the next good day. The job is classed permanent, so it is not retried that day.
3. Composite: a ccxt member whose key is emptied or rotated out but still has an in-window mark (`mark_day < window_end`) makes `_ccxt_apply_inputs` return `_terminal=None`. `_apply_member_marks` raises and `_refuse_member_marks` fails the WHOLE composite permanently.

The user-facing copy says "needs nothing from you" while the cause is the venue read, not the marks. No test covers the dust-after-marks or balance_error-after-marks case (the only dust test, `test_no_marks_strategy_series_is_unchanged`, has no stored marks).

Minimal fix: when the missing terminal is caused by `balance_error`, `equity is None` or dust, do not refuse. Either return `error_kind="transient"` for `balance_error` (no stamp, retry), or for dust/closed accounts skip applying and leave the stored series untouched with one named warning (`mark_terminal_unavailable`). Add a test per case, including the composite member.

### F2. MEDIUM, user-facing: My Allocation names a "Recorded equity from <date>" step from raw stored marks, not from marks the curve actually applied
`src/lib/queries.ts:4159-4190` (`readMarksStart`), called at `queries.ts:6167-6178`.

The factsheet reads `marks_since` from the flag the derive stamps only after `apply_marks` succeeded. My Allocation instead takes the earliest `mark_day` in `api_key_nav_marks` for the included keys. If a key's apply is refused (F1, `mark_unit_mismatch`, `mark_base_nonpositive`), its key_inputs and returns stay realized-basis, yet the dashboard draws the marker and the caption "Recorded equity, including unrealized P&L, begins on <date>". The caption asserts something the curve does not contain.

Minimal fix: read `key_inputs:<api_key_id>`'s `marks_since` (already written present-only by the key-mode epilogue, `job_worker.py` around `_key_inputs_payload["marks_since"]`) and take the earliest of those over the included keys, instead of the marks table.

### F3. MEDIUM, not user-facing: a key refused every day (permanent refusal class) is invisible beyond one warning line per night
`job_worker.py:7058-7063` (the only record of a strategy-mode refusal token is a `logger.warning`); key mode records `nav_mark_refusal` in key_inputs, strategy mode records nothing.

Tokens that are structural for a given account never clear: `wallet_basis_mismatch` (Binance or Bybit holding any non-USDT asset), `option_value_unverified` (every OKX or Binance options holder, by D-19/D-20), `borrow_present`, `account_mode_unsupported`, `uta_wallet_mismatch`. Such a strategy silently never gets marks, shows no `marks_since`, and keeps the realized basis the phase exists to replace. Nobody is told which accounts are permanently excluded; the runbook's "refusals by token" reading is one night of grep over worker logs.

Minimal fix: persist the last refusal token and its first-seen day per key (key_inputs already has `nav_mark_refusal` in key mode; write the same field in strategy mode, or add a count to the job result), and have the post-deploy readout list keys refused N days running.

### F4. LOW: `_write_nav_mark_for_this_derive` returns `(None, None)` with no token and no log when `_mark_read_at is None`
`job_worker.py:7014-7018`.

For a uniform or marked-equity venue this means "no mark, no reason". Today it is a defensive dead branch, but if a future venue path skips the stamp, marks stop silently and `nav_mark_refusal` stays absent. Fix: for a venue in either set, return `(None, "balance_error")` (or a new token) and log, or raise.

### F5. LOW: MT5 `marked_at` is stamped after the deal-history download, not after the equity read
`job_worker.py:6060` versus the read at `6042-6058`.

`account_info` is taken inside `read_mt5_deal_ledger`; a fresh login waits for history to settle (up to `_MT5_HISTORY_WAIT_S`) and downloads deals before `_mark_read_at` is stamped. CONTEXT D-03 and the module doc say `marked_at` is the instant the equity read finished. A derive that reads equity at 23:5x and stamps at 00:0x labels the wrong day, and `price_live_balance` prices at the stamp. sFOX already stamps before its long crawl (`5691`); do the same for MT5 by having the reader return the `account_info` instant.

### F6. LOW: timeout of the Bybit/Binance mark read is labelled `positions_unreadable`
`job_worker.py:3886-3896`. Bybit makes no positions call (D-17), and a Binance timeout may be in the options reads. Cosmetic but it misdirects the nightly token count the runbook relies on. Fix: use a distinct `mark_read_timeout` token in `MARK_REFUSAL_TOKENS`.

### F7. LOW: runbook claims the platform retries a failed 00:05Z benchmark refresh
`docs/runbooks/navmtm-chain-move-go-live.md` ("The benchmark" paragraph), route `src/app/api/cron/refresh-benchmark/route.ts`, `vercel.json`.

Vercel cron does not retry a non-2xx. The route does page (502 plus Sentry), so the failure is loud, but a missed close means every BTC-unit MT5 key refuses `native_unpriced` that night (no older close is used, correct) and the day is only covered by the next mark's single return. Fix the runbook sentence, and consider a second invocation (for example 00:20Z, still before the 00:40Z fan-out) or a check at P1 that the refresh logged before the sync starts.
