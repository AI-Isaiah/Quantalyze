## The correction this plan exists to record

The plan mandated a blast-radius paragraph asserting that activating with `MT5_ENABLED` false
"downgrades the whole MT5 cohort at once, on the FIRST tick, before anyone reads a log line."
**Measured at HEAD by reading the code, that is false**, on two independent counts:

1. **A disabled venue writes nothing to the analytics row.** The derive arm's MT5 kill-switch
   (`job_worker.py:3685-3690`) returns a permanent `FAILED` **before any stamp**. The
   `process_key_long` mirror (`long_fetch.py:285-291`) does the same. A flag-off tick mints
   failed `compute_jobs` rows and touches `strategy_analytics` not at all.
2. **A wedged gateway never stamps either.** The `-10005` IPC-timeout class arrives as an
   `Mt5ClientError` that `classify_mt5_login_error` does not call `auth` → transient, no stamp
   (`:3962-3969`). Hung read → `asyncio.TimeoutError` → transient, no stamp (`:3790-3815`).
   `Mt5SessionAbandoned` (`:3816-3870`) and `Mt5AccountMismatchError` (`:3871-3902`) likewise,
   each commented "no user-blame analytics row for a fault of ours."

Only a genuine credential rejection stamps — and that is the one path plan 02's D-15 guard
covers. **Both of the runbook's two named hazard scenarios are therefore non-destructive.**

I wrote what is true instead. Publishing the mandated paragraph would have put a catastrophe in a
founder-facing document that the founder could disprove in five minutes — and a reader who
disproves one claim discards the surrounding constraints with it. That is the
falsifiable-and-false failure the ROADMAP fences at lines 267-273 and that D-16 names.

### But the residual is real, and it is NOT where the plan looked

The D-15 guard covers **one** of the terminal-failure stamps a tick can reach. Enumerated at HEAD
and written into the runbook as a table:

row unguarded. This is `T-161.1-21`, recorded ACCEPTED in plan 02. The remediation section
therefore ships as required content, for the right reason.

## ⛔ Both gates this plan mandated were VACUOUS as written — measured, not suspected

This is the fifth and sixth unfalsifiable check found in this phase.

**Vacuity 1 — `;`-separated greps under `bash -c` never fail.** The task-1 gate is
`bash -c 'test -f "$f"; grep -q …; grep -q …; echo RUNBOOK_OK'`. There is no `set -e`, so every
failing `grep -q` is discarded and `echo` always runs. **Measured:** with the view-migration
filename deleted from the file, the plan's gate printed `RUNBOOK_OK` and exited **0**. It cannot
fail for any reason. Same defect in the task-2 gate.

**Fix:** `bash -euc` with one command per line. Both corrected gates were then observed RED under
neutering (table above) and green on restore.

**Vacuity 2 — both of the task-2 gate's tokens already existed in the pre-edit file.** The
mandated check is `grep -qi "471-472\|stored" TODOS.md`. Measured against `HEAD:TODOS.md`:

- `stored` — **14 occurrences** before this plan touched the file.
- `471-472` — **1 occurrence** before, in entry 0.1's own CORRECTED bullet.

So *both* branches of the alternation pass on the unedited file. Even after the fail-fast fix,
that gate would assert nothing about this plan's work. **Fix:** three tokens, each measured at
**0** in the pre-edit file and each unique to one of the three mandated edits:
`ledger-refresh-go-live.md`, `per_strategy_stored.items()`, `D-COMP`.

**Generalisable lesson, and the reason this is written up rather than quietly fixed:** a gate token
must be counted against the PRE-EDIT artifact before it is trusted. A token chosen by reading the
finished file is guaranteed to match it.
