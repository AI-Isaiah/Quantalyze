# Phase 164.9.1 Plan 02: M1, the ten-arg enqueue writes the fan-in status Summary

**Migration `20260924230827_fanin_initial_status_10param` makes the TEN-arg `public._enqueue_compute_job_internal(uuid, uuid, text, text, uuid[], text, jsonb, uuid, uuid, timestamptz)` compute `v_initial_status` and INSERT it. The value is `done_pending_children` when `p_parent_job_ids` has an element, and `pending` otherwise. On the local lane a parented child now lands `done_pending_children`, is not claimable while its parent runs, and is released to `pending` by the parent's mark-done.**
