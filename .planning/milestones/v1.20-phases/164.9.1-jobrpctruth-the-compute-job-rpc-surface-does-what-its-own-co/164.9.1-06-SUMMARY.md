# Phase 164.9.1 Plan 06: Pins for the Normalisation Fragment's Position and Byte-Identical Modes Summary

**`src/__tests__/restore-test-from-baseline.test.ts` now fails if `build_transaction` moves the analytics_service_url emitter call or its concatenation out of the slot between the reference-data gate and the ledger DDL, or reads `$mode` anywhere between that gate and the COMMIT/ROLLBACK block. Each pin has in-memory calibrations and was seen RED against a neutered live script. The arm-ratchet literals now read the script's measured 34.**

## What changed (commit `bb0fd0b7f`)

- **L-01 order pin (D-16a).** Two new `STEPS` entries sit between "the gate" and "the ledger DDL": the emitter call (`bash "$REARM_EMITTER" --emit-restore-sql`) and the concatenation (`printf '%s\n' "$rearm_sql" >> "$out"`). A new assertion pins the call below the gate heredoc's terminator line and pins the concatenation to exactly one live line. The order check is also written as an `inOrder` predicate, and three calibrations run it on mutants: calibration 3 moves the concatenation above the gate, calibration 4 moves it below the ledger DDL, and calibration 5 pastes the call inside the gate heredoc. Each one flips the predicate or the terminator pin.
- **Byte-identity pin (D-16a), new `it`.** The window runs from the `TXN_REFDATA_GATE` terminator line to the `if [ "$mode" = "restore" ]; then` / `echo "COMMIT;"` block. It must contain the emitter call and no live `$mode` / `${mode` reference. Calibrations: the whole emitter block wrapped in `if [ "$mode" = "restore" ]`, an `if [ "$mode"` placed after the call, and `--mode "$mode"` passed to the emitter. The pin detects all three.
- **Arm ratchet (D-18).** E was read from the script with `sed -nE 's/^EXPECTED_ARMS=([0-9]+)$/\1/p'`, which gives 34. Fifteen `EXPECTED_ARMS=32` literals became `=34`. The bump calibration's `=33` became `=35`, and `movedArms` became 35. The required `MEASURED 2026-09-21` became `MEASURED 2026-09-25`, the date on the comment beside the constant. The bump, comment-out, double and interim-line calibrations are unchanged in shape and pass.
- **D-28.** E2 and `src/__tests__/test-restore-workflow-wiring.test.ts` were not edited. The wiring test shows no diff against PHASE-BASE (`git diff --quiet` exit 0, inside the verify).
