**Gathered:** 2026-10-02

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

Close the one SQLSTATE 40001 raise site that Phase 164.9.3.2 left unmeasured: the enqueue race-loss `RAISE ... USING ERRCODE = 'serialization_failure'` in `_enqueue_compute_job_internal` (latest definition: `supabase/migrations/20260924230827_fanin_initial_status_10param.sql`). Phase 164.9.3.2 plan 01 (`164.9.3.2-01-SUMMARY.md`) refuted the two-target shape A2 (22023 at the entry guard), and measured the conflict-wait path converging through PostgREST v14.7 in 2–4 s. It could NOT induce the race-loss raise itself in five attempts, so its behaviour through PostgREST is reasoned, not measured.

This phase measures that raise deterministically and then fixes on the evidence. The deliverable is either a measured "it converges, keep 40001" with a gate that pins it, or a fix if it does not converge.

</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Fix shape (founder, 2026-10-02, AskUserQuestion)
- **D-01:** Measure first, then decide. Induce the race-loss raise deterministically on the local-stack lane (`scripts/local-stack/run.sh up`), both in psql and through the lane's PostgREST. Suggested mechanism, not binding: test-only triggers on `compute_jobs`, installed only on the throwaway lane. A row-level BEFORE INSERT trigger inserts a competing in-flight job for the same target. A statement-level AFTER INSERT trigger then moves that competitor to `done`, so the INSERT ... ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING conflicts and the in-flight re-read misses. Statement-level AFTER triggers fire even when DO NOTHING inserts 0 rows. The planner/researcher must confirm the mechanism actually reaches the RAISE (a `enqueue race lost` server-log line, or SQLSTATE 40001 in psql) before measuring PostgREST.
- **D-02:** The decision rule is binding. If the PostgREST call converges (HTTP 200 with a job id inside a bounded time, the race lost at least once in the server log), keep 40001: no migration, no PROD apply. Then add a gate that pins the converging behaviour, and correct the stale comments (D-04). If it hangs or does not converge, apply the 164.9.3.2 fence remedy to this site (errcode 55006, re-based on the LATEST definition across ALL migrations, three migration reviewers before merge) plus whatever app-side retry that requires.
- **D-03:** The measurement is recorded with its raw numbers (HTTP code, wall time, count of race-lost log lines) in the plan SUMMARY, in the same form as `164.9.3.2-01-SUMMARY.md`.

### App-side 40001 retries (founder, 2026-10-02, AskUserQuestion)
- **D-04:** Keep the app-side retry-once helpers: `src/lib/supabase/retry-serialization-failure.ts`, the 40001 branches in `src/app/api/strategies/csv-finalize/route.ts` and `src/app/api/allocator/holdings/sync/route.ts`, and the Python classifiers (`_is_serialization_failure`). If the measurement shows PostgREST retries the 40001 itself, correct their comments so they state why the helper still exists: PROD's PostgREST version may differ from the lane's v14.7, and a direct-Postgres caller can still get a 40001. The comments must not claim the branch fires under PostgREST. Do not delete code.

### Binding constraints carried from 164.9.3.2
- **D-05:** Any `CREATE OR REPLACE` is re-based on the function's LATEST definition across ALL migrations. Any migration gets migration-reviewer + rls-policy-auditor + silent-failure-hunter BEFORE merge, because merge auto-applies to TEST and then to PROD with no human gate.
- **D-06:** No agent queries PROD for this phase. The test-only triggers exist only on the lane and are never written as a migration.

### Claude's Discretion
- Gate placement (a lane-only SQL gate under `supabase/tests/` vs a lane measurement script), and whether the PostgREST measurement lives in a script or a vitest lane test, follow the patterns 164.9.3.2 used (`test_compute_job_fence_errcode.sql`, the lane measurement in plan 01).
