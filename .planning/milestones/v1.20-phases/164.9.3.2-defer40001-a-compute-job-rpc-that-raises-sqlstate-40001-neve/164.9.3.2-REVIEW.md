## Info

### IN-01: Both classifiers treat a bare SQLSTATE 55006 as a fence preemption, with no literal required

**Files:** `analytics-service/main_worker.py:403-408`, `analytics-service/services/job_worker.py:938-939`
**Issue:**
- `code == "55006"` alone returns True. 55006 is a generic class (`object_in_use`) that Postgres itself raises, for example "is being used by active queries".
- None of these raises is reachable from the three RPCs or from `sync_strategy_analytics_status` today.
- A future 55006 on a mark path would be swallowed silently as `LATE_MARK_IGNORED`. The job would then sit `running` until the watchdog reclaims it.
- This is narrower than the pre-phase rule, which accepted a bare 40001. Two tests pin the current rule as intended: `test_apierror_with_code_55006_detected_without_literal` and `test_defer_lost_ownership_code_55006_alone_classifies`.

**Fix (optional hardening):** Classify on the literal alone: `return _PREEMPTED_MESSAGE_LITERAL in str(exc)`. Alternatively, keep the code-alone match and record the reachability assumption next to the check.

### IN-02: Self-verify block reports this migration's failures under another migration's name, and carries a dead duplicate role guard

**File:** `supabase/migrations/20261001120000_compute_job_fence_errcode_55006.sql:542-677` and `:599-603` (carried forward, not in this iteration's diff)

**Fix:** Rename the prefixes in lines 542-677 to `compute-job-fence-errcode:`. Delete the duplicate guard.
