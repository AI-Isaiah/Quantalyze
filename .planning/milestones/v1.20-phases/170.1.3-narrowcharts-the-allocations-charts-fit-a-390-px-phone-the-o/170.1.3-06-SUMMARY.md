**2. Rule 7, for the founder (recorded, not acted on).** The calendar's 13th "Jan" labels the first column of the NEXT year (day-of-year 366 in a non-leap year) in a row that draws no day of that year, and only a non-leap year starting on a Sunday puts it in the last column. Dropping it would be a truer calendar, but it is a hidden label (D-02) and a desktop text change on every non-leap calendar (D-04), so only the founder can choose it. This plan keeps the label and gives it room. Plan 05's `minPatternTexts: 2` for `^Jan$` and the `heatmap-2023` floor (16) depend on the label staying; if the founder drops it, both must change in the same plan on purpose.

**3.
