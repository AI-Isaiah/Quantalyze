**Which existing approach, and why (Claude's discretion per CONTEXT: "whether label fitting
reuses an existing helper").** The phase has two edge-fit mechanisms. (1) The inward-anchor flip,
`fitTickLabel` / `EdgeFitXTick`, is for a label centred on a tick. (2) Gutter growth from the
widest label, `estLabelWidth` + `Math.max(minimum, Math.ceil((widest + gap) / 4) * 4)`, is for a
label drawn at a fixed anchor beside a plot (`rightAxisPad`, `ReturnQuantiles`' left gutter,
`TimeSeriesChart`'s `padLeft`). Both gap labels are of the second kind: the EOY values are
start-anchored beside the bars, and the calendar's month label is start-anchored at its own week
column. Flipping either to `end` would draw it over the bars or over December's columns (plan 04
recorded the same defect for end-anchored y ticks: "lays '+1000.00' across the plot"). So this
plan reuses mechanism (2) inline, in the house shape, and adds no helper to `chart-ticks.ts`
(its class guard bans `Math.max` there anyway). The UI-SPEC Spacing line "the fix there is
anchoring, not padding" predates the round-2 and plan-04 gutter precedents; the literal stays the
minimum, as it does in those two emitters.

**Locked decisions.** D-02: "every label's box sits inside its SVG. Labels are not hidden." D-03a
/ D-03c: `DistributionPanels.tsx` and `HeatmapPanels.tsx` are shared emitters fixed once, with no
composer-only prop. D-04 (amended): desktop unchanged apart from labels already cut on desktop.

**Recorded, not acted on (Rule 7, for the founder).** The calendar's 13th "Jan" labels the first
column of the NEXT year (day-of-year 366 in a non-leap year) in a row that draws no day of that
year. Dropping it would be a truer calendar, but it is a hidden label (D-02) and a desktop text
change on every non-leap calendar (D-04), so only the founder can choose it. This plan keeps the
label and gives it room. Name it in the SUMMARY's findings.
