## Summary

**Round 2 WR-01 (the canvas still drew out-of-year cells) is resolved.**

- **The code change.** The draw loop in `YearCalendarCanvas`
  (`HeatmapPanels.tsx:412-433`) now skips every slot whose date falls outside the calendar's year,
  before it draws either the filled box or the "missing day" box (fill plus outline). That covers
  the leading days before Jan 1 and the trailing next-year days. The check is the same expression as
  the month-label loop (`:286-291`), so the labels and the cells cannot disagree.
- **What is still drawn.** An in-year day with no data keeps its missing box. A leap year that starts
  on a Sunday keeps day 366, because that day is Dec 31 of the same year.
- **The effect dependencies.** `year` is now in the effect's dependency list. That is correct,
  because the loop reads `year.firstWeekOffset` and `year.year` directly.

**The new jsdom pin can fail, and it measures the right slots.**

- **How it records.** It stubs `HTMLCanvasElement.prototype.getContext` with a context that records
  each `fillRect` and `strokeRect` position. The stub is restored by the file's
  `afterEach(vi.restoreAllMocks)`.
- **What it checks.** It asserts that nothing is drawn at any weekday of week 53 (1 to 7 Jan 2024)
  or at weekdays 0 to 5 of week 0 (26 to 31 Dec 2022). As controls, it asserts that in-year empty days
  (1 Jan 2023 and 31 Dec 2023) keep their box and that the one real value still paints.
- **Coordinates.** The ±1 tolerance takes in the `+0.25` offset of the outline.
- **Can it fail?** If the new check is removed, the week-53 and week-0 assertions go red.

**Data path.** Every `dailyHeatmap` comes from `dailyReturnsByYear` (`build-payload.ts:499` and
`scenario-factsheet-payload.ts:403`). Because that function files each date under its own year, a
skipped slot can never hold a value. The change hides only placeholder boxes, never data.

**Pixel goldens (checked, not a finding).**

- **Which goldens see this.** `e2e/svg-chart-parity.spec.ts` runs in CI's seeded e2e job. It holds
  committed `daily-returns-heatmap` goldens at desktop, 320 and 2560. Its seeded window (about 2025-08
  to 2026-04-14) renders the 2025 calendar first. Under D-05 that calendar loses one "Jan" label and
  about 13 placeholder boxes.
- **Why the goldens should still pass.** The placeholder colours (#FBFCFD fill and a 0.5-unit #E2E8F0
  outline on white) differ from white by less than the per-pixel `threshold: 0.2`, so pixelmatch does
  not count them. The removed label covers well under 1% of a roughly 770 × 112 crop, against
  `maxDiffPixelRatio: 0.02`. `e2e/demo-screenshot.spec.ts` allows 0.05. I worked this out on paper and
  did not run it. If a golden does go red, regenerate it for D-05 rather than read it as a
  regression.
