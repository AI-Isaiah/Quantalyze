- Copy, exact: `We leave out {n} {day|days} on which an account was nearly empty (under {TINY_BASE_PERCENT}% of its highest balance over the previous {TINY_BASE_WINDOW_DAYS} days): {formatUnpricedDays(days)}.`, where `days` is the validated list and `n` its length. For key C's two days it reads `We leave out 2 days on which an account was nearly empty (under 5% of its highest balance over the previous 30 days): Oct 27, 2026 – Oct 28, 2026.` "nearly empty" is D-02's own wording; "an account" holds on a strategy (its account) and on a book (one of its accounts, plan 08).
- D-06 "naming the near-empty days": the count stays, so a reader learns how many days without expanding a range, and the days follow the colon, the shape of the one day list this view already prints (`UsdDisclosure`: `No BTC close for 3 days: Oct 3, 2026 – Oct 5, 2026.`).
- Date format: `formatUnpricedDays` from `src/lib/factsheet/returns-unit.ts`, which `FactsheetView.tsx` already imports for that note, reused unchanged: `formatCloseDay`'s `Oct 27, 2026`, consecutive days collapsed into one spaced-en-dash run, runs joined by `; `, all UTC. No new formatter and no new date format.
- Length, decided here: every run is printed, none truncated. That is `formatUnpricedDays`' own pinned contract (`returns-unit.test.ts` "lists every run, none truncated") and the convention of the day list this view already prints; a second, truncating convention ("the first few and N more") in the same view would blend two patterns, and a note whose job is to name the days must not hide some. The rule keeps the list short: D-08 leaves out every calendar day under the threshold, so an episode is one contiguous run; it ends at most 30 days after its peak, when the near-empty level becomes the new normal (D-03); a new episode needs a fresh fall below 5%. Key C's 2026-10-27 and 2026-10-28 are one run.
- DESIGN.md "Voice / microcopy": declarative, sentence case, active voice ("We leave out", as the flow-timing note's "we count"), the limitation stated with its threshold attached, no adjective standing in for a number (the count and the dates are the figures).
- DESIGN.md colour semantics: muted (`--color-text-muted`, the token DESIGN.md gives "labels, captions, timestamps") because a left-out day is an honest, steady-state fact, not a recoverable warning (amber) and not a failure (red). No new colour, no glyph (the amber caveats carry the warning glyph; this note does not).
- Type: the existing neutral data-quality paragraph's classes and border, unchanged, so the v2 single-strategy 4-size / 2-weight contract (`tests/visual/strategy-v2-type-scale.test.ts`) holds. public, so naming the days tells any viewer on which days its account held under 5% of its highest

founder's D-06 ("naming the near-empty days"), recorded here as a consequence. The note shows the dates
and the fixed threshold only, never a balance, an amount or the peak (T-188-13).
