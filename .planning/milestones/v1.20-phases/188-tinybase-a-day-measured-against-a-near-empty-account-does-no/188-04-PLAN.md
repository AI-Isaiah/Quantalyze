## Decided by the founder (2026-11-03, recorded in CONTEXT)

- **Decided: D-08 (founder 2026-11-03), "Yes, leave them out too": "Every calendar day under the threshold is treated the same, active or quiet."** A realized-era day with no trade, funding or flow on a near-empty account, which the combiners store today as a gap-filled r = 0.0, becomes NaN and its stored row goes. Consequences, recorded so the executor and the verifier expect them:
  - (a) Stored history: every gap-filled 0.0 key-day inside a tiny stretch leaves `csv_daily_returns`, at most 29 per key and withdrawal (the roll-off). RESEARCH's blast radius already counts such days without telling them apart from measured ones: episode E1's flat days are about 34 of its 36 key-days (two Deribit keys, 17 each beside the one +8.2% day, r about 0), and E2 and E3 were not split either, so how many vanish only because of this plan is first measured by plan 11. In a book, the drained key sits out the day (plan 05's 0.0 `dropped_day_pnl` row) instead of adding r = 0 at its tiny weight, and a book day on which it is the only key with a return loses its return row (a gap, never 0.0).
  - (b) Strategy headline, through D-04 (plan 02: a realized-era NaN is a chain break): a strategy whose key was drained and then left idle has no break today and gets one. Its headline (return, CAGR, volatility, Sharpe, drawdown) restarts on the day after the last quiet tiny day, at most 29 days after the withdrawal, and everything before that leaves the headline, where today those days read flat and the earlier history stays in. Where plan 02's measured tiny days already break the chain, the "since" day moves later by up to 29 days.
  - (c) SC-4 (plan 11): the independent recompute judges every stored key-day, a stored gap-filled 0.0 included, so it agrees with the derive because this plan runs.
  - (d) KL-3 widens (plan 03): where `_pre_start_levels` rolls by returns with no day P&L (`roll_basis="returns"`: OKX, Bybit, Binance), the roll stops at a quiet tiny day this plan leaves NaN instead of rolling through today's 0.0, so a peak before that day is not seen by the first marks' windows.
