Apply D-08 and D-10 to the two remaining dollar totals that merge keys: the scenario commit's server AUM
and the match scorer's `portfolio_aum` (Phase 167.1.1's routed R-15-1 and R-15-2), without letting
owner-writable evidence into the persisted figure (plan-checker N1).

D-08, quoted from CONTEXT: "holdings from different API keys are never merged. ... Holdings, Open
Positions and Exposure carry every live key's position, and their totals equal the sum over keys."
Purpose: the commit route sizes every audit row's `size_at_decision_usd` from a sum that keeps one row per
venue:symbol:type across ALL dates and keys, and the scorer does the same; on a two-key book both
under-count. The dashboard's reader cannot simply be reused: it reads the owner-forgeable audit log as
clean-poll evidence, and the route's trust comment and forged-audit test forbid that for this figure. So
the reader gains an audit-free mode and both callers share plan 11's tie-break.
Output: the reader option, the route and scorer moved onto per-key, one-account-once sums, two-key tests,
the known-limit test and neuters.
Containment (for the report): the route replaces one query, its local marker read and its dedup with the
reader call plus the shared function; the scorer replaces its collapse loop with a per-account read plus
one owner-filtered `api_keys` read. If either grows beyond that, stop and report rather than widen.
</objective>
