<objective>
Make the Exposure panel's "what I hold now" read the same per-key latest holdings as Holdings and Open
Positions (D-01), and leave the over-time charts alone (D-03).

Positions were empty. Exposure read the allocator's single latest `asof` filtered only by `native_unpriced`;
the tables read `fetchLatestHoldingsPerKey`, which also drops a key at a newer clean poll and at a newer key
on the same exchange account. Two readers, two answers. After this plan there is one.
Output: a pure builder, the panel wiring, the card's stamp and empty copy, and the old read retired.
</objective>
