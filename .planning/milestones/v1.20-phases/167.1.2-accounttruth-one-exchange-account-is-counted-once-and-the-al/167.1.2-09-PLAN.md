<objective>
Keep a disconnected or revoked account's history in the rebuilt series up to the day it left, with
an overview where the owner includes or excludes each departed account (D-05, the founder's own
words; D-09 for the end day and the default).

Purpose: without this, plan 05's series is "the accounts connected now", and a key the founder
disconnected simply vanishes from the past, which is the opposite of what he asked for. The default
excludes only what the compose cannot prove is a different account, so D-05 never re-opens the
double count D-01 closes. The founder confirmed that default on 2026-09-25 (CONTEXT D-09).
Output: departed keys composed up to their end day, flow-neutral exits, retained inputs, one shared
rule, and a switch on each departed key card.
Ship unit: PR C, with plans 05 and 11.
</objective>
