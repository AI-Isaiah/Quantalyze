  `labelById: ReadonlyMap<string, string>` and sets `name: labelById.get(apiKeyId) ??
  CONNECTED_KEY_FALLBACK_NAME` ("Connected key"). ScenarioComposer passes `apiKeyLabelById` (built
  from `dataSourceLabel` in `src/lib/api-key-label.ts`, plan 04's module; not re-implemented), so the

  stays, now redundant. The SSR helper `liveBaselineMetricsFromPerKeyDailies` names its per-key unit
  "Connected key" (only that line of queries.ts changed).
- **SC-5b, observation warning.** MetricsColumn reads `payload.periodsPerYear` from the `usePayload()`
  it already had (no second source). The warning condition, the years figure and the threshold
  sentence ("Conventional reliability threshold is ≥ {periodsPerYear} observations (1 year).") use
  it; with no basis the whole block is hidden. `periodReturn` and the 3 Year / 5 Year rows are byte
  for byte unchanged (D-14; Phase 169 plan 07 owns them).
- **W2.** `buildScenarioFactsheetPayload` returns `periodsPerYear` (the returned object's last field,
  `scenario-factsheet-payload.ts` line 524), the same resolved value its `compute()` calls use.
  `grep -c periodsPerYear` on that file went 12 → 13.
- **SC-5c, tab switch.** `changeTab` and the canonicalisation effect call
  `window.history.replaceState(null, "", url)`; `router.refresh()` polling untouched; the effect's
  "shallow replace does not refetch" comment is corrected.
