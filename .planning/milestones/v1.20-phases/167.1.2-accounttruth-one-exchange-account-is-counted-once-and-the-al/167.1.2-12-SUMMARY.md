## Which arms guard what (Task 2 acceptance)

- **N5 is the guard D-17 (c) requires.** A book that already has snapshots is never sent a bootstrap reconstruct. Without that guard, the 30-day reap of `done` compute_jobs rows would turn the arm into a monthly re-reconstruct of every allocator key.
- **C, B and S are the guards against a stranded key (revision 1).**
  - C pins the per-run cap.
  - B pins that a book beyond the cap gets no job of either kind until a later call takes it whole.
  - S pins that a book is never split across runs.
  
  Together they ensure that no first snapshot row can close the zero-snapshot gate before every qualifying key's reconstruct exists.
