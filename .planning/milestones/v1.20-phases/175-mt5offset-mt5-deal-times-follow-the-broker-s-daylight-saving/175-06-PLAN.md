days actually moved") and the live half of D-01 (the rule is chosen by a worker setting). Deadline
carried from RESEARCH: if the rule is not live by 2027-03-14 (US spring) / 2027-03-28 (EU spring),
the interim integer must be flipped by hand again.

outcomes, in this plan's SUMMARY.
