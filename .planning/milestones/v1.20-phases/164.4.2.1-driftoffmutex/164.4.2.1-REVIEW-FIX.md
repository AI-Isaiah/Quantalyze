### SFH-02 / IN-05: `measureHolders` saw only the literal step name or the literal key

**Files modified:** `src/__tests__/critical-regressions.test.ts`
**Commit:** 6806e1c9e
**Status:** fixed: requires human verification. This is detector logic: a wrong rule would either miss a holder or red on a job that holds nothing.
**Applied fix:** In a job's own YAML, any of the following marks the job as a holder:
- the literal key;
- any `Acquire…mutex` step;
- a `MUTEX_KEY` assignment;
- the mutex's PGAPPNAME;
- any advisory-lock call other than one on a different numeric literal. A call on a variable argument counts.

The detector also walks every `scripts/*.sh` on the job's non-comment lines, plus the files those scripts `.`/`source`. Inside a script, only these count as taking the key:
- `MUTEX_KEY=`;
- the mutex's PGAPPNAME;
- an advisory-lock call on the key literal or on `MUTEX_KEY`.

A mention does not count as a take, and that distinction is load-bearing:
- The wait's sourced `shared-test-db-keys.sh` names the key as a comparison operand and try-locks the in-flight flag key.
- `test-ledger-drift-check.sh` names the key in a comment.

`scripts/pg-lane/` and `scripts/local-stack/` are exempt because each runs a private cluster on the runner. The pg-lane drill `mutex-dead-holder-lane.sh` takes the literal key on purpose; without the exemption, `sql-mutation` reads as a holder.

A new calibration `it` runs the detector on synthetic jobs that call the repo's real scripts:
- Must be seen: four spellings, plus a key taken only through a sourced file.
- Must not be seen: `test-db-drift`'s two real scripts, a commented-out invocation, and the pg-lane drill.

The Topic B report flagged the raw-index directory computation. It now uses `posix.dirname`, and `test-restore-workflow-wiring.test.ts` passes (117/117).
