### Task 1 — the interleave falsifier (commit `b36afbc1`)

`test_CRITERION_4_a_monitor_tick_cannot_land_inside_a_live_jobs_terminal_window`

A job takes `mt5_terminal_lease(key)` in the JOB SITES' own form (`wait_s=None`, UNBOUNDED —
the asymmetry IN-04 names is the hazard, so the stand-in must not quietly bound itself),
announces `enter` through an `asyncio.Event`, and parks BOUNDED until the tick's first touch
is observed. Only then does the tick run. Under the real registry the tick's bounded acquire
expires and it skips; the job times out at its ceiling and its terminal window stays
contiguous.

**THREE ORACLES, because they are three different failures:**

| # | Oracle | The failure it alone can see |
|---|--------|------------------------------|
| 1 | no `tick` index falls between the job's `enter` and `exit` | an interleave on the shared terminal |
| 2 | `round_trips == []` — ⛔ EMPTY, never merely short | a tick that QUEUED and ran AFTER the job: it passes (1) perfectly while having made the job wait for up to the whole budget |
| 3 | the bounded-acquire INFO skip, logged ONCE, naming the caller | a tick that made no round-trip because the CLIENT failed to build |

**The bound itself is asserted, not assumed** — `_install_lease_counter` WRAPS the real lease
(its release discipline still runs), and the tick is asserted to take EXACTLY ONE acquisition,
keyed through `mt5_terminal_key` and carrying a finite `wait_s` equal to the shipped knob's
per-call value. `wait_s is None` is called out by name as the BATCH form that would queue.

**The teeth, in the same test.** A fresh `asyncio.Lock` per call serializes nothing. The patch
targets `services.mt5_concurrency._mt5_terminal_lock_for` — the registry's OWN module, the one
`mt5_terminal_lease` resolves the name from — and is accompanied by a structural assertion that
`mt5_terminal_lease.__wrapped__.__globals__ is vars(mt5_concurrency)`, so a future refactor that
moved the reader is reported rather than silently making the control a no-op.

⭐ **ONE CONTROL, ONE VARIABLE.** The arm isolates the LOCK; `bump_mt5_terminal_epoch` is
patched to a no-op on `services.mt5_concurrency` (the READER, not `mt5_client` which DEFINES it)
so the abandoned-session FENCE is held still. ⛔ The abandonment is NOT caught in the harness —
the shipped `_run_two_concurrent_mt5` forbids exactly that, and for the reason it gives: a
harness that swallows a refusal stays green when a future change makes the fence fire where it
should not.

  into a single commit, the Task 1 intermediate state was rebuilt programmatically (removing
  Task 2's two contiguous regions), asserted to compile, asserted to retain every Task-1 symbol
