## Decisions Made

### 1. Cause 2 writes NO row (the plan's recorded discretion, exercised)

| cause | today | writes a row |
|---|---|---|
| 1 `flag_read_failed` | `RAISE WARNING`, then the NOTICE | **YES** |
| 2 `flag_false_by_design` | the NOTICE | **no** |
| 3 `flag_row_invisible_or_absent` | the NOTICE, byte-identical to cause 2's | **YES** |

Cause 2 is the healthy dormant state and is already legible from the flag row itself. **Retention note:** no purge of `public.cron_runs` exists anywhere in this repo (measured at HEAD), so instrumenting cause 2 would accrue 2 functions × 24 ticks/day = 48 rows/day for the whole pre-activation period to restate a fact one `SELECT` already gives. Causes 1 and 3 are both *defect* states, so a growing count IS the signal — but if either becomes chronic it accrues at the same 48/day, and the disposition then is a retention policy on `cron_runs`, **not** a quieter instrument. Recorded in the migration header, not closed.

### 2. The instrument is NOT wrapped in an exception handler

If `public.cron_runs` were absent, the dormant path now RAISES instead of returning 0. That is deliberate: an instrument that swallows its own failure is the exact shape this phase exists to remove. The table has existed on PROD since `20260408113029` applied, and the pg-lane gets it from fixture 33.

### 3. Three of the four needles are CONCATENATED, one is written whole

`v_flag_needle` (`'FROM public.' || 'system_flags'`), `v_instrument_needle` (`'INSERT INTO public.' || 'cron_runs'`) and `v_guc_needle` are assembled so this file is never a second raw occurrence of what it asserts. `v_null_needle` is written whole **and that is safe by measurement**: arms A and K are designed to PRESERVE `IS DISTINCT FROM TRUE` when they mutate the guard line, precisely so the apply survives and the arm — not the migration — is the first failure.

### 4. The cause literals are gate-mutable and deliberately NOT needles

Plan 03's instrument twins prove the row carries the RIGHT cause by mutating the cause literal. A needle on a cause literal would abort the apply instead of reddening the gate, and `WAIVED_CEILING` is 0.
