# Phase 169.1 Plan 07: The bootstrap CI and the stress windows agree with the headline beside them Summary

The Bootstrap CI now resamples the series the headline's risk statistics run over: the one
`metricsBasisSeries` result in `deriveSeriesBundle`. It scores each resample, and the point
estimate, under the headline's method. So the point Sharpe, Sortino and Max DD equal the headline
in three cases: on an active-basis strategy, on a gapped calendar composite and on an arithmetic
composite. That holds at full history and in a selected range. On an arithmetic series, a stress
window's strategy return is the sum of the window's returns, and its drawdown is the running-sum
trough. The benchmark stays geometric. The public factsheet cache key moved v10 -> v11 once, here.

## What was built

- **bootstrap.ts.** `bootstrapCI` has an optional sixth parameter `cumulativeMethod`, default
  `"geometric"`. It passes the parameter to the private `headlineStats`. There, the drawdown is
  `arithmeticUnderwater(rets)` under arithmetic and `drawdowns(cumEq(rets))` otherwise.
  `arithmeticUnderwater` is imported from `./compute`, beside `cumEq` and `drawdowns`. The one
  Sharpe call (the shared `sharpe`) and the one Sortino expression are untouched.
- **stress-windows.ts.** `computeStressWindows` has an optional sixth parameter
  `cumulativeMethod`, default `"geometric"`. Under arithmetic, after the shared loop:
  - the strategy's `stratReturn` is `arithmeticEquity(slice)[last] - 1`;
  - its `stratMaxDD` is the minimum of `arithmeticUnderwater(slice)`.

  The benchmark's accumulation, its null handling (169.5 CR-01), the coverage rule, the catalogue
  and the drop counters are unchanged.
- **build-payload.ts (`deriveSeriesBundle`).**
  - The bootstrap call is now
    `bootstrapCI(basis.returns, 2000, 5, 42, periodsPerYear, isArithmetic ? "arithmetic" : "geometric")`.
    `basis` is 169.1-06's one `metricsBasisSeries` result, and the helper is not called a second
    time.
  - The stress call is
    `computeStressWindows(dates, stratRet, btcAl.returns, "BTC", markets, isArithmetic ? "arithmetic" : "geometric")`.
  - `windowView` (plan 169.1-02) re-derives through the same function, so a selected range
    follows automatically. `basis-context.tsx` was not edited.
