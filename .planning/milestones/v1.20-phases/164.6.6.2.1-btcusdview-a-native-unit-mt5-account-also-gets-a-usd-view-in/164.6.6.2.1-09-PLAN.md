<objective>
Compose the BTC key into the USD allocator book, and when a day cannot be priced, leave the key
out of that day by name.

Purpose: D-01 (dashboard totals in USD), D-09 (omitted by name that day, never a stale close),
D-20 (a drop in the total is never silent: the dashboard names the account and the day, plan 17
renders it from this payload). The mechanism is the one plan 03 measured.

Output: the compose arm, the derive's wiring, the payload list, and tests.
</objective>
