## `computed_at` independence (orchestrator note)

The stamp does not depend on `computed_at` moving, and does not write it. Measured: neither payload the `_stamp_strategy_analytics_failed` closure builds (the loud `_upsert` and the D-15 `_upsert_error_only`) contains `computed_at` (`grep` over the closure's line range prints nothing). The wizard's terminal read, `useStrategySyncPoller`'s status poll, selects `computation_status, computation_error` only; `computed_at` appears there only in the kickoff freshness probe. The stamp passes `heal_series=False`. So a later phase that holds `computed_at` on failed writes (NAVBREACH) cannot change whether this card appears.
