<objective>
Create `src/lib/diversification.ts` — a pure-TS, zero-dependency SAMPLE-convention math library that derives the diversification view's numbers from the frozen engine's already-emitted `correlation_matrix` + the de-aliased constituent return series + normalized weights. It computes: per-constituent re-alignment (mirroring the frozen engine), a SAMPLE covariance matrix, per-constituent σ, the Choueifaty Diversification Ratio, per-constituent percent-contribution-to-risk (PCR), the risk-based Effective Number of Bets (ENB), an average-linkage hierarchical cluster order, and the ρ≥0.85 "too similar" pairs.

Purpose: This is the novel surface of Phase 41. The engine (`scenario.ts`) is FROZEN and emits ρ but NOT the per-constituent σ, the covariance matrix, or the aligned-on-commonDates per-constituent series that DR/PCR need. This lib reconstructs those (re-alignment) and computes the four diversification formulas + one clustering algorithm, all SAMPLE-convention so they stay internally consistent with the displayed correlation matrix. Pinned by a consistency golden test that rebuilds ρ from the lib's own cov+σ and asserts equality with the engine matrix to 3 decimals.

Output: `src/lib/diversification.ts` (math) + `src/lib/diversification.test.ts` (golden tests). NO new dependency. NO edit to `scenario.ts`.
</objective>
