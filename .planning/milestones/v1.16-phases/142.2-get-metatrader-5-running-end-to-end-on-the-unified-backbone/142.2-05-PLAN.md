verdict stories for producers 2 (stitch composites) and 3 (keyless CSV uploads). RESEARCH assumption
A4 ("one producer") is FALSE — there are THREE `csv_daily_returns` producers (PATTERNS Class-Fix
Site Ledger item 3): `run_derive_broker_dailies_job` (upsert job_worker.py:4507),
`run_stitch_composite_job` (upsert :6550), and `csv-finalize/route.ts` → the
`persist_csv_daily_returns` SECDEF RPC. This plan covers all three, each by the mechanism that fits:

- **Producer 1 (derive):** runtime assert at the seam — the open-ended venue registry is where a
  future combiner could omit the verdict.
- **Producer 2 (stitch):** unconditional `composite_stitched` stamp in `headline_payload` (the
  strategy_analytics headline write, constructed at job_worker.py:6706-6718 and upserted by
  `_write_headline_and_by_basis` at :6816-6820) + regression test. It is ONE closed code path, not
  a registry — a stamp plus a pinning test is the right enforcement. ⚠️ NOT in `merged_flags`
  (:6638-6700), the data_quality_flags carrier — see Task 2.
- **Producer 3 (keyless CSV):** stamped in `analytics_runner.run_csv_strategy_analytics`, NOT in
  TypeScript. Planner decision, stated for review: csv-finalize's only strategy_analytics writes are
  FAILURE placeholders (verified at HEAD — :724, :763); the success path's first analytics-side
  touchpoint is the runner, and strategies reaching `run_csv_strategy_analytics` with no api-key
  linkage are keyless uploads BY CONSTRUCTION (composites never route through this function —
  analytics_runner.py:1527). The keyless discriminator is sound at the PRODUCER because the producer
  knows the pipeline; it was unsound at the GATE because the gate could not distinguish "keyless" from
  "verdict missing". The CSV path is therefore covered explicitly — not exempted by omission.
