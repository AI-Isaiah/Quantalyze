**Notes — the founder challenged the premise, and was substantially right:**
> *"They should all take dailies, then unified backbone, and from the unified backbone, it should
> be the same, thus I do not understand the last question."*

Verified rather than argued. The architecture largely holds:
- `job_worker.py:6043` states surfaces agree "by construction" via shared `strategies.asset_class`
- MT5's annualization is already correct — `create-with-key/route.ts:510` stamps
  `isCryptoExchange(exchange) ? "crypto" : "traditional"`; `finalize-wizard/route.ts:731`
  explicitly says "stamp `traditional` for mt5 (forex/CFD)"
- `portfolio-stats.ts` **defaults** to `periodsPerYear = 252`, so a caller that forgets the basis
  still lands on the right clock for MT5. The fragile direction is crypto, not MT5.

Residual justification, narrowed: the **backbone-bypass surfaces** logged in `TODOS.md` —
`_compute_portfolio_analytics` (`portfolio.py:628`), `equity_reconstruction.py`, and the bespoke
TS stacks — re-derive rather than read. They are the one place the invariant could be false today.

The decision was re-put to the founder with this corrected framing (all five / detail + one
spot-check / detail only) and **all five was reaffirmed**.
