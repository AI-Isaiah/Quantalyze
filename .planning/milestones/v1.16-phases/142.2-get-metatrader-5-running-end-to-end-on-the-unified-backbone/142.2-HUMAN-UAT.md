## Why this cannot run locally or in CI

- The MT5 gateway is **Railway-internal** (`mt5-gateway.railway.internal:8001`) — unreachable from a
  laptop or a CI runner.
- The shared **TEST** database has **no worker**, so a connect would never sync.
- The requirement is an **outcome**, not a sum of MT5-01..04: *"a founder completes the connect flow
  without needing to know an internal error code, a server name, or a flag."* No automated assertion
  can stand in for a human not needing to ask a question.

## ⛔ What passing this checklist DOES and DOES NOT prove

**PROVES: reachability and honesty.** The founder can connect an MT5 account and get to a rendered
strategy, and every rejection along the way names its true cause.

**DOES NOT PROVE: that the numbers are right.** ⛔ **Reaching a rendered strategy is NOT evidence
that the rendered performance is correct.** Do not record the equity curve, period return, Sharpe,
drawdown or any daily figure as verified on the strength of this checklist, and do not let any
artifact of Phase 142.2 claim it.

**Number-correctness is Phase 142.3's gate** (MT5-06..10, decisions D-07..D-11, preserved verbatim
in `142.2-CONTEXT.md` as the 142.3 seed): the **MT5 terminal itself** as an **external** oracle,
against the **live funded account**, on a **trading day**, with the server-UTC offset **measured**
rather than assumed.

⚠️ The specific reason a "it rendered, so it works" reading is dangerous here: a wrong server-UTC
offset misassigns near-midnight deals to the adjacent day, and then **period totals still reconcile
while the daily series is wrong** — which corrupts Sharpe, max drawdown and every risk metric
derived from the series. That failure renders perfectly and is invisible to everything 142.2 built.
