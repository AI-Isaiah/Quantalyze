## Verdict up front

**The phase goal is achieved.** All three clauses of the goal statement were verified
first-hand against source and against a live `TestClient` driving the real `main.app`:

- *tells the truth about whose fault a failure is* — the four-class contract exists as a

  both service-role read sites are tenant-scoped and an ownership gate precedes them.

  bucket 429s on request 31 and a tenant call immediately after still returns 200.

The two gaps below are at the **margin of two requirements' wording**, not at the goal.
Neither blocks Phase 140.2. Both are already known to the executor and recorded in
`140.1-TS-OBLIGATIONS.md`; the disagreement is only whether `REQUIREMENTS.md` should carry
an unqualified `[x]`.

one of the five "known partial delivery" items I was told to re-derive first-hand checked
out exactly as described — including the uncomfortable ones (the tenth IP-keyed route, the
over-broadened 403, the `main.py` mypy exclusion). I found no instance of a SUMMARY claiming
