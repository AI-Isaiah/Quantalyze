<objective>
Make the multi-call route declare its real worst case, and bound the fan-out at the query.

Purpose: `SEAMCORE-10` (A-06, A-29).

The composite branch of `finalize-wizard` reads its members with an ordered
`.from("strategy_keys").select("api_key_id")` that has **no `.limit()`**, then loops
`for (const member of members ?? [])` issuing a sequential, cache-bypassing scope-broadening probe per
member. Each probe reaches `resilientFetch("keys-permissions", …)` at a 15 000 ms budget — while the
route's `SEAM_ROUTE_BUDGETS` row declares `{ key: "keys-permissions", calls: 1 }`. The invariant
computes its worst case from that same `1`, so it asserts a fixed figure against the 300 000 ms
ceiling **forever, regardless of N**. Roughly twenty members reaches the ceiling.

**Two independent fixes are required and neither substitutes for the other.** A cap alone leaves the
table lying about the branch; a corrected table alone leaves the fan-out unbounded.

⚠️ **A-29's sharper half, which is easy to miss:** the composite and single-key branches are
MUTUALLY EXCLUSIVE, and the composite branch spends **zero** `process-key-enqueue` — it returns
through `runLegacyFinalize`. So today's two-leg row describes the SINGLE-KEY path and the composite
path is not modelled at all.
</objective>
