      `no-raw-analytics-fetch` to `"off"` on both warmer paths, and the only exclusion test fires
      solely if the author ALSO edits `SEAM_ROUTE_BUDGETS`. This clause is ADDITIVE work — a new
      guard, not a tightening of an existing one.

Output: 13/13 budget-key pins, a MECHANISM that keeps the count honest, a leaf-purity guard, a
warmer-exclusion guard, all registered.

⚠️ **The class is 13 budget-key BINDINGS, not 5 call sites.** A call-graph enumeration reports 5
`resilientFetch(` invocations and is WRONG about the class that matters, because two of the five
take their budget key from a variable: `analytics-client.ts` fans out through nine exported
wrappers, and `process-key-client.ts` computes its key from `budgetKeyFor(args.flow_type)`. Fixing
a subset here is the instance-not-class defect that cost this programme 37 scrapped commits.

⚠️ **Closing the class at 13 is only half the job.** Thirteen pins prove the thirteen bindings that
exist today are correct. They prove NOTHING about a FOURTEENTH. A new wrapper added next month that
reuses an existing budget key adds no pin, breaks no pin, and reddens nothing — the class quietly
re-opens and the docblock census that "closed" it becomes a historical note. **A census is a
convention; this plan must ship a mechanism.** Task 1 therefore delivers the other half of the
analog it already cites: `src/lib/api/limiter-ordering.test.ts` walks a directory, classifies what
it finds, and FAILS on anything unclassified — forcing a conscious decision rather than trusting one.
