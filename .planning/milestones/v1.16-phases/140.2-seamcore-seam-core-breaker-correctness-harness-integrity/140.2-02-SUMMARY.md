## Deviations from Plan

### 1. [Rule 7 — Conflicting instruction, surfaced not averaged] The behavioural loop bound

- **Found during:** Task 2, designing the M14b falsifier.
- **Conflict.** `140.2-VALIDATION.md` §2's checklist says: *"No loop bound is read from the module
  under test. A loop of 'threshold − 1 iterations' is written as a hand-typed `4`, never as
  `BREAKER_FAILURE_THRESHOLD - 1`."* The PLAN requires the opposite outcome: M14b must redden a
  **behavioural** trip-count test driven through the fake.
- **These cannot both be satisfied, and the reason is structural.** The double fully replaces
  `@upstash/ratelimit`, so once its threshold is hand-typed, production's constant enters the
  behaviour through *no other path*. If the loop bound is ALSO hand-typed, the test is decoupled
  from production entirely and M14b cannot redden it — it would become a test of the fake, by the
  fake, which is the "second pair of mocks agreeing with each other" VALIDATION itself names.
- **Resolution: followed the PLAN.** The loop bound stays `mod.BREAKER_FAILURE_THRESHOLD`. The
  checklist item exists because that bound was *tautological* when the fake harvested the same
  number; with the fake independently pinned at a literal 5, the two sides now come from
  **independent sources** and the assertion is a genuine DIVERGENCE detector. The loop bound is the
  test's INPUT, not its expected value — no expectation is read from the module.
- **Documented in the code**, not just here: an explicit ⚠️ block above that test explains why the
  bound is production's and names it as ledger row M14b, so a future reader applying the checklist
  literally does not "fix" it and silently delete the phase's headline falsifier.
- **Surfaced rather than blended.** If VALIDATION's wording was meant to bind here too, this is the
  one place to revisit — `src/lib/resilient-fetch.test.ts`, the `trips the breaker once the failure
  allowance is exhausted` case.

### 3. [Rule 2 — Missing critical functionality] The plumbing observation at F-1, and the 8th grep hit

- **Found during:** Task 2.
- **Issue.** The plan says to keep `slidingWindow` so a test can still OBSERVE what the core
  constructs. But **no such assertion existed** — the options object was consumed by the harvest and
  never asserted. Removing the harvest without adding an assertion would have left the core's

- **Fix.** F-1 records `{ tokens, window }` into a hoisted array (reset in `beforeEach`) and a new
