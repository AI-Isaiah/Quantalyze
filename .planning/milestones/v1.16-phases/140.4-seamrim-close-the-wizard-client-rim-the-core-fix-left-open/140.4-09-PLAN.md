## The rule, stated as a PROPERTY (so it is row 1, not a roster)

> **Taint propagates to an alias only through an expression that can carry the caught value's own
> TEXT** — which is what undici embeds the outgoing headers into. A classifier call returning a
> boolean, or a constructor returning one of our own typed objects, does not.

Text-carrying channels: the bare identifier · `.message` / `.name` / `.stack` / `.cause` ·
`.toString()` · `String(x)` / `JSON.stringify(x)` · a template interpolation · a conditional or binary
operand whose other side is one of those. Everything else does not propagate.

This is a **property**, not a hand-typed exemption list, which is what makes it **coverage-law ROW 1**.

## Also in scope: the `Error` global-constructor false positive

because `/^(error|[\w$]*(Err|Error))$/` accepts it. So `console.error(…, err instanceof Error ? … )`
reports a bare `Error` — the **global constructor**, which carries nothing. It surfaced at 3 sites
under the widened roster. Exclude the exact identifier `Error` from the errorish half, with the
justification written down and a self-test in both polarities.
