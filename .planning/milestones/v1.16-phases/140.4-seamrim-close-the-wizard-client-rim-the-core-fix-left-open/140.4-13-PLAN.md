## The measured state, re-derived at plan time

Comment-stripped scan over the 15 routes in `SEAM_ROUTE_BUDGETS`:
```

429-ONLY                                       = 11
NO LIMITER                                     = 1   admin/match/eval  (checkLimit count 0; its two

```
The 11 carry **12** `checkLimit` call sites — `keys/sync` has two (a per-user bucket and a
per-user-per-strategy bucket) and **both** need the split.

`rateLimitDenyJson` / `rateLimitDenyText` are the **canonical deny-response builder for exactly this
503-vs-429 split**, and they are **live and adopted**: **9 production route call sites plus 2 wrappers**
(`withAdminAuth`, `withAuthLimited`). **Zero of them are seam routes.**

And the builder's own docblock says why:
> *"Routes whose tests `vi.mock("@/lib/ratelimit")` still inline the branch to keep their existing
> mocks intact — this helper is for the admin surface where the real ratelimit module loads."*

**Test-mock convenience blocked adoption of the artefact.** That is a P6-shaped fact — the fix's own
justification is what left the class open — and CONTEXT §5c settles the mechanism:

> *"`rateLimitDenyJson` ALREADY EXISTS — 9 production call sites, 2 wrappers, and zero on the seam.
> ⇒ the C-5 remedy is **adoption of an existing chokepoint (coverage-law ROW 1)**, not 11 new predicate
> calls (row 3)."*

## ⚠️ Blanket adoption would be a REGRESSION. Read this before writing code.

`rateLimitDenyJson` returns a **fixed** body (`{error: "Rate limiter unavailable" | "Too many requests"}`)
and only a `Retry-After` header. The seam routes' 429s are **not** interchangeable with that —
measured at plan time:

| route | today's 429 body | headers |
|---|---|---|
| `strategies/create-with-key` | `{ code: "KEY_RATE_LIMIT", error: "Too many requests" }` | `NO_STORE_HEADERS` + `Retry-After` |
| `keys/sync` (×2) | `{ error: "Too many requests", code: "RATE_LIMITED" }` | `NO_STORE_HEADERS` + `Retry-After` |
| `strategies/csv-validate` | `csvErrorEnvelope("CSV_RATE_LIMIT", …)` — the v0 CSV envelope | `NO_STORE_HEADERS` + `Retry-After` |
| `verify-strategy` | `{ error: "Too many requests" }` | `Retry-After` only |

Dropping those `code`s breaks the wizard's rendering, and dropping `NO_STORE_HEADERS` re-opens a
cache-contract finding. `bridge/route.ts`'s own docblock says this in as many words:
> *"converging onto withAuthLimited's default `rateLimitDenyJson` would drop this route's bespoke
> 429/503 copy AND regress the NO_STORE_HEADERS added below (`rateLimitDenyJson` does not stamp it)."*

⇒ **Extend the chokepoint so a caller can keep its own body and headers, then adopt it.** The artefact
owns the **decision** (503 vs 429, and the `Retry-After`); the route owns its **body**. That is still
row 1 for the property that matters — *"a limiter misconfiguration is never rendered as throttling"* —
and it costs no route its contract.

- **The adoption sweep across 12 call sites — ROW 3, PARTIAL BY CONSTRUCTION** (measured 1/8 · 2/56 ·
  0/32). **Say so in those words.** What closes it is Task 3's **derived-population** guard: a new seam
  route that inlines a deny arm instead of calling the builder reddens on the day it is written.
