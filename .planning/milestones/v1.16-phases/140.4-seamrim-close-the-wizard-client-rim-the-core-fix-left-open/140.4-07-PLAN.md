## Why these are genuine offences, not a widened guard crying wolf

`src/lib/resilient-fetch.ts` rethrows the **original** error: *"Rethrow the ORIGINAL error. Both
clients map `err.name` onto their own typed errors … wrapping here would silently reclassify every
timeout in the codebase."* So the value reaching these catches is the original undici error, whose
`message` — and in one shape whose `name` — embeds the **outgoing headers**: `Authorization: Bearer
<INTERNAL_API_TOKEN>`, `X-Service-Key`, and on the CSV finalize flow `X-User-Access-Token`, a **live
end-user Supabase JWT**.

⚠️ **`err.message` is banned too, not just `err`.** The guard flags `err instanceof Error ? err.message : err`
at all three of its references, deliberately — `message` is exactly where undici puts the headers.
