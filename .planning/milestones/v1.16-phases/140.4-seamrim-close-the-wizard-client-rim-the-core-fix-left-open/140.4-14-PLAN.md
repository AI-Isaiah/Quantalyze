## Why this exists, and what it does NOT claim

Plans `140.4-01` and `140.4-02` closed **14 reads** across the two `checkStrategyGate` consumers —
**7** on the admin approve path (of 12 reads, 5 of which already bound `error`) and **7 of 7** in the
wizard's single-key `Promise.all` — using a per-site edit. That is coverage-law **ROW 3**, and its measured coverage in this programme is
1/8 · 2/56 · **0/32**. Both plans say *"partial by construction"* because they are.

The repo-wide class is far larger, and **the number depends entirely on the predicate** — CONTEXT §5c
is emphatic:

> *"UNCHECKED-SUPABASE-READ POPULATION IS DEFINITION-DEPENDENT — DO NOT PICK A NUMBER, STATE A
> PREDICATE. Three honest measurements disagree because they measured different things: **32**
> (synthesis, `src/app/api/**` only) · **73** (mapper, repo-wide destructure-never-reads-`.error`) ·
> **139** (researcher, 372 total sites − 233 checked). A plan that quotes one of these three without
> its definition is repeating the defect this phase exists to fix."*

**This plan quotes none of them as a target.** It states its own predicate, applies it to a
**proven-clean** glob, and lets the ratchet do the rest. **It does not close the backlog and must not
claim to.**

⚠️ **The class has TWO carriers, and this rule covers one** (CONTEXT §5b). Three of the review's
"client members" — `MatchQueueIndex.tsx` ×2 and `StrategyActions.tsx` — are unchecked **`fetch()`**
calls, not Supabase reads. `MatchQueueIndex`'s are `await fetch("/api/admin/match/kill-switch", …)`
with **no `res.ok` read**, so a 403/500 makes the match-engine kill-switch pill snap back and the
founder believes the engine is OFF when it is ON. **A Supabase-shaped rule is row 1 for the Supabase
carrier and row 3 for the `fetch` carrier — say "partial by construction" for the second, in those
words.** `140.4-PATTERNS.md` §12 records that **no analog exists** in this repo for an unchecked-`fetch`
census; do not invent one here.

## The predicate, published (this is the plan's contract)

> **Flag an `await`ed PostgREST call expression whose result is destructured — in EITHER the object
> form (`const { data } = await …`) or the ARRAY form inside a `Promise.all` — where no binding for
> `error` is introduced, and the chain is not terminated by `.throwOnError()`.**

**The array form is not optional.** The mapper's object-destructure predicate returns **zero** on
`SyncPreviewStep.tsx:1053-1114` — the phase's top finding — because that block array-destructures.
*Object form closed, array form open, in the same file.* A rule that only sees the object form would
report the converted files clean while the exact defect class walks back in.

go down there. **The existing backlog outside the glob is untouched and remains PARTIAL BY
CONSTRUCTION.** Both statements go in the SUMMARY, in those words.
