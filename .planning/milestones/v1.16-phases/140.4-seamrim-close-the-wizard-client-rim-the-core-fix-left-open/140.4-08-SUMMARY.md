## A judgement call the plan did not anticipate: the three PROPERTY-read sites

Sites **2, 5 and 10** do not log the caught value — they log a **property** of it
(`err.status`, a `number`; `err.seamCode`, a `string | null` machine code from a
closed set — `analytics-client.ts:66,78`). Both are our own facts, in the same
category as the guard's `SAFE_PROPERTIES` (*"non-string facts from types WE
define"*), and the scrub is a **rendering no-op** on each. They are nonetheless
reported by the predicate, because its allowlist is `retryAfterS` /
`deadlineExceeded` / `code` and nothing else.

Three ways to clear them; I took the third and recorded why:

1. **Widen `SAFE_PROPERTIES`.** Forbidden — `seam-log-coverage.test.ts` is out of
   scope, and weakening the guard in the same commit as the scrubs is a worse
   version of the named warning sign the plan already bans for the roster.
2. **Bind the reads to non-error-shaped locals.** This clears the grep and closes
   nothing: it is precisely the **one-hop alias hole** plan `140.4-09` exists to
   close, deliberately introduced to evade a guard. Rejected outright.
3. **Route each read through the leaf.** Output bytes identical (`scrubSeamError(424)`
   → `"424"`; `scrubSeamError("SEAM_X")` → `"SEAM_X"`), no operator information
   lost, no `err.message` pulled into a line whose own comment exists to keep it
   out. **Chosen**, with the reasoning written into the source at `eval:217` and
   cited (not restated) at the other two.

**Handed to plan `140.4-10` as a roster-adjacent question it may want to settle:**
whether `status` and `seamCode` belong in `SAFE_PROPERTIES`. I did not decide it,
because deciding it means editing the guard.
