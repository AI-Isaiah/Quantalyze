## Finding 8 is DISPOSITIONED, not code-fixed

Stated plainly because the phase sign-off depends on it: **the retry→limiter amplification
remains LIVE in the code.** No limiter-aware retry path was built, no limiter constant moved,
and nothing refuses a retry to protect the `/process-key` ceiling. What changed is exposure, not
mechanism: post-D-01/D-03 the retry-eligible population is `onboard`-with-a-key only, and
`resync` — the dominant historical share — retries no more, so the worst case finding 8
arithmetic'd against the ceiling applies to an order of magnitude less traffic. It is recorded in
the `retriesForFlow` docblock, booked in `TODOS.md`, and carries explicit re-raise conditions
(a new YES flow verdict, a `resync` re-grant, or `RetrySafeEntry.retries` widening past one).

**Phase sign-off wording, which must not be shortened:** all 13 findings are CLOSED **per their
dispositions** — twelve remediated in code or prose, one (finding 8) dispositioned. "Closed"
must never be read, or written, as "all 13 fixed in code".

**Advisory-gate discipline:** every CI gate here is advisory at merge. The pins added across this
phase **would have caught** these regressions at 141.1; nothing in CI *did* stop them, because
they did not exist.

### 3. [Criterion interpretation, recorded] "No surviving reference to the deleted assertion"

Task 1's criterion reads "no surviving reference to the deleted derived assertion anywhere in the

met: `grep "threshold/2"` returns nothing outside those two corrections and no code path or
