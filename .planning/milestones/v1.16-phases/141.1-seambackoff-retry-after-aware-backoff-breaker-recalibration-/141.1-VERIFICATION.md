## 6. The criterion-wording problem — my recommendation

### Were the four judgements correct?

**Yes, all four. Unambiguously, and for a stronger reason than "it's just a comment."** I
re-ran each grep at HEAD and confirm the reported counts exactly (3, 1, 3-excluding-comments,
2, and 7 for the DISCHARGED count).

| Plan | Criterion | Extra hit is | Correct to refuse? |
|------|-----------|--------------|--------------------|
| 01 | `grep "hasContractualWait"` = 2 | The symbol anchor `// \`Retry-After\` (see \`hasContractualWait\`)` at `:2327`, ~400 lines from the helper | **Yes.** `resilient-fetch.ts` is ON `SEAM_CITATION_SURFACE`, where a cross-region reference is *required* to be a symbol anchor. | 09 | `grep "unreachable today" TODOS.md` = 0 | Both hits are the correction text, quoting the phrase to identify what was corrected | **Yes.** Verified both: `:497` is the corrected 140.5 bullet, `:525` is G3's verdict naming its obligation. The false *assertion* is gone; both survivors assert the opposite. |

In every case the number and the intent point in opposite directions, and in three of four
the "fix" would have been to delete a navigational aid the same phase mandates elsewhere.
Editing code to satisfy a grep integer is the failure mode this phase's own thesis names —
so refusing four times, and surfacing each refusal, is the behaviour I want to see, not a
deviation to be logged away.

### Recommendation

**Yes — reword these criteria to be code-only, and prefer construct assertions to integers.**
Concretely, in descending order of value:

1. **Stop expressing symbol-occurrence criteria as raw grep counts. State the construct.**
   In all four cases the criterion *meant* something structural: "exactly one call site",
   "the assignment has no fallback", "all three `it.each` sites are fenced", "no surviving
   claim that the path is unreachable". Write that. Plan 04's criterion already did
   ("returns 0 hits **inside the retries assignment**") and was internally consistent — it
   was the accompanying grep that was wrong, not the sentence.

2. **When a count is genuinely wanted, make it code-only using tooling the repo already
   owns.** Plan 01 already produced the right form and got exactly the plan's stated 2:
   `grep -n "X" f | grep -vE "^[0-9]+: *(//|\*|/\*)"`. For anything more than a spot check,
   `source-scan.ts`'s line-preserving tokenizer and `extractComments` in
   `seam-citations.invariant.test.ts` are the sanctioned strippers — a criterion that reuses
   them cannot disagree with the guard it lives next to.

3. **Count occurrences, not lines, in `TODOS.md`.** Plan 09's
   `grep -Ec "DISCHARGED|RE-BOOKED" ≥ 4` counts *lines*, and TODOS.md's house style is one
   bullet per very long line. That criterion scored 2 while five obligations were genuinely
   discharged. The executor restructured the section into five per-obligation bullets to
   satisfy it — which happened to be an improvement, but the pressure ran the wrong way and
   is not safe to repeat. Use `grep -o … | wc -l`, or assert the presence of one verdict
   token per named obligation.

4. **Add a standing planner rule.** *Any acceptance criterion expressed as an exact grep
   integer must either exclude comments explicitly, or be replaced by a named-construct
   assertion.* A phase that simultaneously (a) mandates symbol anchors in comments and
   (b) counts raw symbol occurrences has put two of its own rules in direct tension. This
   phase hit that tension **four times in nine plans** — that is a systematic planning
   defect, not four coincidences, and it is cheap to close at plan time.

5. **Optional, and I would not block on it:** the citation guard's known blind spot (the
   self-relative `(:NNNN)` forms) is what let gap 1 through. Closing it needs the second,
   containing-file-scoped predicate that `seam-citations.invariant.test.ts` already names as
   a residual. Worth booking; it would have caught the one citation this phase missed.
