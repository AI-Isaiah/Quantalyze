## Accomplishments

- **D-08 landed by construction, not by convention.** `ResilientFetchInit.retriesOverride` is a required `0 | 1`, the `init` parameter lost its `= {}` default, and `const retries = retriesOverride;` has no fallback. ## Decisions Made

- **Kept `SEAM_BUDGETS[*].retries`** and rewrote its docblock as ACCOUNTING in the same commit (OQ-3's recommendation). Deleting it would have reddened SC-4b and `EXPECTED_RETRIES`, and the accounting is genuinely needed — `process-key-enqueue` really does perform two attempts in production, and the route's lambda ceiling must be charged for both.
- **Removed the `init = {}` default.** Not in the plan's letter, but required by its intent: a defaulted parameter means `resilientFetch(key, path)` compiles again and the axis is only half-closed.
- **Ran the full 10,707-test suite** rather than the plan's three files. A required property on `ResilientFetchInit` is precisely the change whose failures land outside the file you edited.
