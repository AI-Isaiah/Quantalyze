## ⭐ PYAPIFIX2-01 — THE ENUMERATION (the deliverable)

### The behavioural predicate I ran

A site is a member of the **venue-transient class** iff ALL THREE hold:

> **P1 — CLASSIFIED UPSTREAM.** The site sits on a path that has already decided *which* upstream
> condition occurred: it is lexically inside an `except` handler whose caught type is a venue /
> transport class (`ccxt.*`, `SfoxApiError`, `Mt5ClientError`, `Mt5AccountMismatchError`,
> `Mt5ValidationError`, `asyncio.TimeoutError` around a venue probe), **or** its `detail` reads a
> classification produced by a venue call (`result["error"]` / `validation["error"]` from
> `validate_key_permissions`).
> **P2 — RETRY COULD CLEAR IT.** The condition is not a permanent caller fault
> (`AUTH_FAILED`, `PERMISSION_DENIED`, `TRADE_SCOPE`, `WITHDRAW_SCOPE`, `MISSING_SCOPE`,
> `MT5_MASTER_PASSWORD`, `MT5_WRONG_SERVER`) and not a server-side feature gate.
> **P3 — HUMAN STRING IS THE ONLY CARRIER.** The emitted body carries no machine `code`, no
> `retryable`/`recoverable` — i.e. `HTTPException(status, detail=<scalar str>)`, never
> `service_error(...)`.
