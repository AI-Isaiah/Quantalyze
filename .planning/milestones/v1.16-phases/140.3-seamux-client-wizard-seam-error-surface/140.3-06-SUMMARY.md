## ⚠️ Hard rule #8: is *"changing the status alone buys nothing, because the status is discarded"* still true?

**Partly — and that is exactly why it was misleading rather than simply wrong.** Verified first-hand
rather than assumed:

- **TRUE, still, of `classifyKeyValidationError`.** After `140.3-05` it branches on `body.code`
  (`seamCode`) and returns its *own* status; it reads the upstream status **nowhere**. The wizard's
  rendered copy for these seven sites is byte-identical before and after this plan. Confirmed by
  reading `wizardErrors.ts:1179-1215` and by the parity test passing unmodified.
- **FALSE as a general claim.** `seamBreakerVerdict` in `src/lib/seam-discriminator.ts` **does** read
  the status: `424 → attributability:"caller-exchange", counts:false, breakerKey:null`. So the status
  is load-bearing for **attributability** and for breaker accounting even though the render is
  unchanged. And the wire contract itself (`STATUS_CONTRACT.md` §5) is the point: a 400 *asserted a
  falsehood about whose fault it was*, independent of what any client rendered.

The docstring was therefore **corrected to say precisely that**, not deleted — the bullet explains why
the flat shape came FIRST, which is still valuable history; it just stopped being a reason the status
does not matter.
