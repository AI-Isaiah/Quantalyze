## What this plan built

### Two negative pins — and, honestly, what they do not prove

Both are source-scanning tests in the `seam-log-coverage.test.ts` shape. Every count and every
exclusion is a **hand-typed literal**; neither file contains a self-referential count
(measured: 0 in both).

**`src/lib/seam-ssr-exposure.pin.test.ts` (7 cases).** Zero server directives under `src/`, pinned to
a hand-typed `0`; and no server-rendered module outside `src/app/api/` imports `analytics-client`,
`resilient-fetch` or `process-key-client`. **This is the property that means a breaker trip can never
500 a page — no user is ever locked out by an analytics outage.**

The allow-list is exactly two entries, hand-typed: `src/lib/analytics-client.ts` and
`src/lib/process-key-client.ts` — both seam modules themselves, importing `resilient-fetch` because
that is the transport they are built on. Excluding them excludes the subject from its own rule.

> 🔎 **A measured correction to the plan's premise.** The plan expected an allow-list also covering
> `wizardErrors.ts` and various `"use client"` components. **Measured: only TWO files outside
> `src/app/api/` import any seam module at all.** Fourteen others *mention* them — every one in a
> comment, several in docblocks explaining why they must NOT import them. A substring grep reports
> all fourteen as violations and the natural "fix" is to weaken the guard, so the scan matches the
> **import edge** and strips comments first, and the regex is itself pinned against a prose mention.

**Does NOT prove:** (1) direct import edges only — a server component importing a helper that imports
the seam is invisible; (2) it is a source scan, not a runtime proof; (3) nothing about whether the
seam calls that exist are *correct*; (4) a dynamic `await import()` would evade the regex.

**`src/lib/seam-poll-disjointness.pin.test.ts` (6 cases).** The wizard sync-poll path makes no seam
call and its stall backstop is wall-clock. **This is why no retry storm is possible from the poll** —
a poll that reached the seam would, during an outage, record failures on a 3–10s cadence in every
open tab, re-arm the breaker continuously, and extend the outage through our own recovery UI.

> 🔎 **Stronger than CONTEXT claimed.** The poll chain — `useStrategySyncPoller.ts` (the engine),
> `SyncPreviewStep.tsx`, `SyncProgress.tsx` — contains **zero `fetch(` calls of any kind**. Every
> read is `@/lib/supabase/client`. `SyncProgress.tsx` is on the roster deliberately: it is the
> engine's other consumer, the one a reader thinking only about the wizard would forget.

**Does NOT prove:** (1) **the roster is hand-typed and three files long** — a fourth poller added
tomorrow is invisible until someone adds it; this buys falsifiability at the cost of coverage and
both halves are stated in the file; (2) direct calls and edges only; (3) nothing about poll cadence
or Supabase load — only that the poll cannot feed the breaker; (4) source, not runtime; (5) the
wall-clock assertion is structural.
