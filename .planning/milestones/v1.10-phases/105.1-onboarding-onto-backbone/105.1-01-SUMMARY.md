## qs-Convention Discovery

`qs.stats.sharpe(stat_returns, periods=periods_per_year)` = `mean(r) / std(r, ddof=1) × √periods` with `rf=0`. On `day_basis="calendar"` (`stat_returns IS returns` byte-for-byte, metrics.py:703-705) the paper oracle reconciles to the derive output at **rel 0.0** — no tolerance widening needed.
