## PRIORITY CHALLENGE — answer: (b), with precision

**The plan's stated premise is FALSE, and the decisive sub-question resolves against the plan:
`computation_status='failed'` rows' KPIs ARE surfaced.** Evidence, traced in src/ this session:

1. **The premise "with no compute job, strategy_analytics has no row at all (route.ts:1640-1654)"
   is false two ways:**
   - The cited comment describes the WR-04 empty-series *rejection* rationale, not a structural
     invariant about analytics rows.
   - The route ITSELF writes `strategy_analytics` rows with `computation_status='failed'` and no
     job success: `writeFailedStrategyAnalyticsPlaceholder` (`route.ts:1229-1305`, upsert at
     `:1280-1293`). PROD confirms: 18/18 zero-dailies csv strategies carry an analytics row; 7
     carry non-null `sharpe` AND `cagr` (all `computation_status='failed'`, computed 2026-05-27
     under older code).

2. **Failed rows' KPIs are READ and DISPLAYED:**
   - `src/lib/utils.ts:171-176` `extractAnalytics` does NO status gating.
   - `src/components/strategy/StrategyTable.tsx:1091-1092` renders
     `formatNumber(s.analytics.sharpe)` UNGATED by `computation_status` (only the *chip* at
     `:896-904` is status-aware). This is the discovery/browse listing surface.
   - `src/lib/queries.ts:141-183` `getPercentiles` selects `sharpe` etc. with NO
     `computation_status` column at all and folds every published row into percentile ranks.
   - (Contrast: strategy-v2 detail `queries.ts:1197,1212` and both factsheet PDF routes DO gate
     on complete — the gating is per-surface, not systemic.)

3. **Why this bites the trades-FILL arm specifically:** FILL writes `category_id` — which
   PROMOTES a previously discovery-invisible row (WR-04) into the `discovery_categories!inner`
   listing join — together with `asset_class='crypto'`, while the empty-series arm fires NO
   recompute. If the row belongs to the historical failed-with-KPIs class, its √252-computed
   Sharpe is then displayed under a crypto label with nothing reconciling it. That is exactly
   the hazard constraint B (the ECONOMIC ORACLE) exists to forbid.

**Mitigating measurements (honest, but not an invariant):** the placeholder writer never writes
KPI columns (only status/error/flags, `route.ts:1283-1291`); under CURRENT code no new
zero-dailies strategy can acquire KPI values (both enqueuers are dailies-gated; the worker needs
a job); all 7 KPI-bearing failed rows are historical (pre-A4, 2026-05-27) and an echo against
them requires the same user resubmitting a ~3-month-dead wizard_session_id. So the hazard
population is small and shrinking — but "practically unreachable" is a census fact, not a
structural pin, and this codebase's own rule is that money-math must not rest on usually-true.

### BLOCKER-1 (plan 01, must_haves truth 4 + Task 1 trades-FILL arm + objective evidence chain)
- The sentence "`trades` strategies carry NO stored KPIs — structurally pinned" and "with no
  compute job, strategy_analytics has no row at all" must be DELETED — both are refuted by
  PROD and by `route.ts:1229ff`.
- **Fix (either):**
  (i) Condition the empty-series FILL on stored-KPI absence: before FILLing on an echo whose
  enqueue will not fire, read the strategy's `strategy_analytics` row; if it carries any
  non-null stored KPI (`sharpe`/`cagr`/…), REFUSE (or FILL `category_id` only, never the
  clock) — one extra owner-scoped read, and the trades-FILL test arm gains a sibling
  KPI-present → no-clock-move arm with its own neuter; OR
  (ii) FILL unconditionally but gate the `asset_class` component on "the recompute fires"
  (`dailyReturnsSeries.length > 0`), leaving `category_id`-only FILL on empty-series echoes —
  simplest, loses nothing economically (a truly KPI-less trades row has no clock reader today).
- Either way, re-ground the objective's FILL-safety chain on the TRUE premise: "no KPI-value
  writer exists without a completed compute job under current code (enqueuers dailies-gated;
  the route placeholder writes no KPI columns), and the historical failed-with-KPIs class is
  excluded by the guard above" — not on "no row exists".
- The currently-planned trades-FILL test arm (`FILL applied AND no enqueue`, unconditional)
  would PIN the hazardous behavior. It must be re-cut to match the chosen guard.
