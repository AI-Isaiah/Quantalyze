- Single weight-1 constituent reduces the blend to portDaily = L·r (renorm by UN-levered mass, scenario.ts:417-431) → its max_drawdown IS the sleeve's standalone levered max-DD (founder lock).
- One −0.05 day among zeros ⇒ |maxDD(L)| = 0.05·L exactly (0.05·L < 1).
