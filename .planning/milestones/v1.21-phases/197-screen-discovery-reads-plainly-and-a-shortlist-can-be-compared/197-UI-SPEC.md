Gates (DESIGN.md Semantic-color gates):
1. **Stale chip is amber, not red, not muted (U-03).** A stale record is recoverable (the next successful sync clears it), and it must be seen; red would call it a permanent failure, muted would hide it. Same warning pairing as the owner "Syncing" chip in this file.

4. **Track carries no tone.** A short record is a fact, not a verdict: `text-text-secondary` for a finite value, `text-text-muted` for `—` and `<1`.
5. A `—` cell (null, withheld, not ranked) never carries a semantic colour.

## Interaction contract 1: column headers (CLAR-01, D-05)

The header itself states the period and whether the figure is annualised. Each metric header is two lines inside the existing sort button: a **label line** (today's `HEADER_LABEL`, sort glyph after it) and a **qualifier line** (same mono uppercase, `text-text-muted`). The `Tooltip` wraps the sort button and carries the definition. All strings live in ONE exported constant map (planner names it, suggested `DISCOVERY_COLUMNS` beside `COLUMNS`), read by the table header and the grid card footer, so the two views cannot disagree.

The periods are what the stored figures actually cover. The discovery list reads `strategy_analytics` headline scalars, which are computed over the full record (from the last break when the record is chain-broken, `metrics.py` headline suffix). So the headers say "Full record", not "1y": D-05's "Return 1y, ann." is the format, and this contract does not recompute any figure (U-01).

Order (Track is new and sits first among the metrics so a reader sees how long the record is before reading figures from it, U-02):

| # | Label line | Qualifier line (visible) | Accessible name of the sort button | Tooltip (definition) | Cell |
|---|-----------|--------------------------|------------------------------------|----------------------|------|
| 1 | `Strategy` | none | `Strategy` | none | unchanged |
| 2 | `Track` | `Months of record` | `Track, months of record` | `How long the record is, in whole months. Counted over the days its returns cover.` | see Track below |
| 3 | `Return` | `Full record · not ann.` | `Return, full record, not annualized` | `Total return over the whole record. From the first return day, or from the last break in the record.` | unchanged figure (`formatPercent`, signed, sign tone) |
| 4 | `CAGR` | `Full record · ann.` | `CAGR, full record, annualized` | `Compound annual growth rate over the whole record. From the first return day, or from the last break in the record.` | unchanged (`annualizedCell`, 194 gate) |
| 5 | `Sharpe` | `Full record · ann.` | `Sharpe, full record, annualized` | `Annualized return divided by annualized volatility over the whole record. From the first return day, or from the last break in the record.` | unchanged (`annualizedCell`) |
| 6 | `Max DD` | `Full record · not ann.` | `Max DD, full record, not annualized` | `The largest fall from a peak over the whole record. From the first return day, or from the last break in the record.` | unchanged |
| 7 | `Volatility` | `Full record · ann.` | `Volatility, full record, annualized` | `How much daily returns vary, annualized over the whole record. It is never negative, so it carries no sign.` | **unsigned**: `formatPercent(volatilityShown, 2, { signed: false })`; `—` and `<1y` unchanged |
| 8 | `Return` | `Last 6 months · not ann.` | `Return, last 6 months, not annualized` | `Return over the 182 calendar days that end on the record's last day.` | unchanged (`six_month_return`) |
| 9 | `AUM` | `Stated by manager` | `AUM, stated by manager` | `Assets under management as the manager states it. It is not measured from the exchange.` | unchanged |
| 10 | `Return curve` | `Full record` | (not sortable, plain header) | `Cumulative return over the whole record.` | unchanged sparkline |
| 11 | `Underwater` | `Full record` | (not sortable, plain header) | `Distance below the previous peak over the whole record.` | unchanged sparkline |
| 12 | `Actions` | none | n/a | none | unchanged |

Rules:
- **Annualisation token:** visible `ann.` / `not ann.` is `aria-hidden`; an sr-only `annualized` / `not annualized` sits beside it. The visible separator ` · ` is `aria-hidden` with an sr-only `, ` (the `SyncBadge` `Separator` idiom).
- **Wrapping:** the qualifier may break only at the ` · ` separator, so each unit ("Full record", "not ann.") stays whole (`whitespace-nowrap` per unit). A header is at most three lines (label + two qualifier units). Right-aligned columns right-align both lines.
- **AUM and Track carry no annualisation token** (U-05): neither is a rate. Their qualifier states what the figure is instead.
- **Sparkline header rename** `Return` to `Return curve` (U-06): otherwise three headers would read "Return".
- **Tooltip trigger:** hover or keyboard focus of the sort button, existing `Tooltip` behaviour. On touch, tapping sorts (as today) and the qualifier is already visible, so nothing depends on the tooltip.
- `aria-sort` stays on the `<th>`; the button's accessible name is label + qualifier.
- The filter panel and its "Not shown" line keep their labels (`Return %`, `6 Month`, ...): that line reports on a filter and names it the way the filter panel does.

### Track column

| State | Condition (`rowRecord(s).spanDays`, the 194 D-20 count) | Cell | `title` |
|-------|----------------------------------------------------------|------|---------|
| months | span ≥ 30 days | integer `floor(spanDays × 12 / 365.25)`, `formatNumber` with 0 dp, `.font-metric` | `{N} days of record, {first} to {last}.` (dates `formatIsoDay`) |
| under a month | 0 < span < 30 | `<1` | same |
| unknown | span null (no readable dates) | `—`, muted | none |

Sortable (`desc` first, like every metric); unknown sorts last in both directions (`compareWithMissingLast`). No percentile suffix. Counts from `headline_since` when the record is chain-broken, because that is the record the figures beside it are computed on (same record the 194 gate reads).

### Grid view footer (same list, U-07)

`StrategyGrid` footer labels read from the same map: `Return · full record` and `CAGR · full record · ann.` (DM Sans `text-xs` muted label as today, value unchanged). At `max-[375px]` the two footer items stack.

## Interaction contract 2: stale strategies (CLAR-02, D-03)

**Field read:** the newest stored return day, `strategy_analytics.returns_series` last date, already projected to the list as the scalar alias `series_end` (`returns_series->-1->>date`). **Never** `computed_at` (a recompute on old data is not fresh data), never `trades_fetched_at`.

**Threshold (D-03):** stale when the newest return day is more than **7 calendar days** (crypto, `annualizationPeriods(asset_class) === 365`) or **10 calendar days** (everything else) before the current UTC day. Measured once per mount from the existing `mountedAtMs` instant. Two named constants (placement is the planner's), never a restated literal.

| Row kind | Rank cell | Stale chip | Ordering |
|----------|-----------|-----------|----------|
| current (displayed, newest day within threshold) | `#n` (n counts current rows only) | none | first partition, active sort |
| stale (displayed, older than threshold) | `—` muted, `title` + sr-only reason | `Data from {YYYY-MM-DD}` amber | second partition, same active sort |
| age unknown (displayed, `series_end` null or unreadable) (U-08) | `—` muted, `title` + sr-only reason | `Data date unknown` amber | second partition, with stale rows |
| undisplayed (no computed analytics, existing STALE-01) | `—` (unchanged) | none | last partition (unchanged) |

Rules:
- Stale rows are **never removed or hidden**, under any filter, sort or scope.
- "Sort below all current rows within whatever sort is active": the partition is direction-independent and applies to every key, `name` and `track` included (the same shape as the existing STALE-01 partition, ahead of `compareWithMissingLast`).
- A stale row keeps its figures (they are real figures of an old record) and its `<1y` / `—` gating, but shows **no percentile suffix** and **no ordinal**: an ordinal or `Pnn` is a ranking claim (U-09).
- The chip sits in the Strategy cell's second line, after the strategy-type badges and before `SyncBadge`, using `DATA_STATE_CHIP` + warning tokens (uppercase, like the existing chips). Grid view: the same chip under the card's badge row.
- `SyncBadge` is not changed. On a stale crypto row it can still show its own red series dot beside the amber chip; that colour belongs to the shared badge's ladder and is out of scope here (booked observation, see Assumptions A-4).
- **The RED test (SC-3):** a fixture with a strategy whose `series_end` is older than the threshold and whose Sharpe is the highest in the set must render below every current row and without `#1`. On today's HEAD it ranks `#1`, so the test is RED before the change.
- Applies on the published-only mounts (`/discovery`, `/browse`), not on the owner mounts (U-04).

## Interaction contract 4: selection and the sticky Compare bar (ROUTE-05, D-04)

**Selection column** (discovery mount only):
- A new leading column, before `#`: 44px wide (`w-11`), header with an sr-only `Select to compare` and no visible text (the star column's idiom). Pinned in the `@3xl` sticky stack at `left-0`; `#`, star and Strategy shift right by the measured width (extend `usePinnedStackOffsets` to measure the select cell too; no px literal). Below `@3xl` it scrolls away with `#` and star, and Strategy stays pinned at `left-0` (unchanged rule, 170.6.1 D-09).
- Cell: a native `<input type="checkbox" class="rounded accent-accent">` (16px) centred in a 44 × 44 `<label>`. Inset focus indicator (2px accent, the clip-proof `GHOST_ROW_ACTION` idiom), because the cell lives in the `overflow-x-auto` region.
- `aria-label`: `Select {strategy name} to compare`.
- **Cap of 4 (D-04):** with 4 selected, every unchecked checkbox is `disabled`, its label carries `title="Up to 4 strategies can be compared. Remove one to add another."`, and the checked ones stay operable.
- Selection is held in the table's state, keyed by strategy id, in the order the boxes were ticked. It **survives** paging, sorting, search, filters, the watchlist scope switch and the table/grid switch. It resets when the category changes (the page remounts the table by slug). A selected strategy that a filter hides stays selected and stays in the bar.
- Grid view: the same checkbox as an overlay sibling of the card link (never inside the `<a>`), top-right, left of the star, 44 × 44; the card's reserved right spacer widens to fit both.
- Placeholder rows (owner mount) never render a checkbox.

**Sticky bar:**

| Selected | Bar | Screen reader (polite `role="status"`, sr-only, always mounted) |
|----------|-----|------------------------------------------------------------------|
| 0 | absent | `""` |
| 1 | absent (D-04: appears from 2) | `1 selected. Select one more to compare.` |
| 2-3 | shown | `{n} selected for comparison.` |
| 4 | shown, with the cap line | `4 selected, the maximum.` |

Layout:
- Rendered by `StrategyTable` after the pagination row, `sticky bottom-0 z-40` (above the `z-30` pinned header corner). In normal flow it docks at the end of the list, so it never covers the last row or the pagination.
- Square data-panel edge: `border border-border bg-surface`, card shadow `0 1px 3px rgba(0,0,0,0.04)`, `px-4 py-2`, its own `@container`.
- Left group (`flex items-center gap-3 min-w-0`): `{n} selected` (body, `text-text-secondary`); then, from `@2xl` (672px) up, one chip per selected strategy in selection order: `rounded-sm border border-border bg-surface px-2 py-1 text-caption text-text-primary`, the name `truncate max-w-64` with `title`, then a `×` button (`aria-label="Remove {name} from the selection"`). At 4 selected, the muted caption `Up to 4. Remove one to add another.` follows the chips.
- Right group (`flex items-center gap-3 shrink-0`, pushed right): `Clear` (`Button variant="ghost" size="md"`, `aria-label="Clear the selection"`), then the primary CTA **`Compare ({n})`**, a `next/link` `Link` with `Button` `primary md` classes, `aria-label="Compare {n} strategies"`.
- Href: `/compare?ids={id1},{id2},...` in selection order. This is the format `/compare` already parses (`page.tsx:63`, comma-separated, first 4). Plain navigation; the selection is not persisted after leaving the page.
- Phone (bar container under 672px): the chips are hidden; the bar shows `{n} selected`, `Clear` and `Compare ({n})` on one line.
- No enter or exit animation (DESIGN.md Motion: no decorative animation).

Focus:
- `Clear`: the selection empties, the bar unmounts, focus moves to the first visible row's checkbox.
- Chip `×`: focus moves to the next chip's `×`, else the previous one; if the bar unmounts (fewer than 2 left), focus moves to that strategy's checkbox when its row is visible, else to the first visible checkbox.

## Decisions taken in this contract (no founder available; each is the option most consistent with DESIGN.md and the code)

- **U-01 Headers say "Full record".** The stored list figures are computed over the whole record (or from its last break), so the header states that period. D-05's "Return 1y, ann." is read as the header format; a 1-year figure would need new analytics and is not in the phase.
- **U-02 Track sits right after Strategy.** A reader sees how long the record is before reading figures from it.
- **U-03 Stale chip is amber.** Recoverable and must be noticed (Color gate 1).
- **U-04 Stale ordering applies on `/browse` too.** It is the same shared published-only table and the same ranking claim. Owner mounts are unchanged.
- **U-05 AUM and Track have no annualisation token.** Neither is a rate; the qualifier states what each is instead.
- **U-06 Sparkline header `Return` becomes `Return curve`.** Otherwise three headers read "Return".
- **U-07 The grid footer reads the same label map.** The grid is the same list's other view; it would otherwise contradict the table.
- **U-08 An unreadable newest day ranks with the stale rows.** Unknown age must not rank as current (the cautious direction); it is shown as `Data date unknown`, never dropped.
- **U-09 Stale rows lose `#n` and `Pnn`, keep their figures.** The ordinal and percentile are ranking claims; the figures are facts about an old record.
- **U-10 `/compare` gets a per-column Remove link.** Without it, a comparison at the cap of 4 cannot change, which would make "Add strategy" a dead end.
- **U-11 Partner names are plain text.** Keeps the pinned cell narrow and adds no new link target to an admin-only line.
- **U-12 Search failure uses the sibling search's inline idiom.** `StrategyBrowseDrawer.tsx:598` searches the same catalogue and renders its failure inline with `role="alert"`; an `ErrorEnvelope` inside a dropdown would not fit. Conformance over taste (CLAUDE.md Rule 11).
- **U-13 Selection lives in table state, not the URL or storage.** The smallest mechanism that meets D-04. Cross-category comparison is covered by `/compare`'s own search.
- **U-14 Disabled checkboxes at the cap.** A box that cannot be ticked says why in its `title`, and the bar states the cap; ticking a fifth box is never silently ignored.
