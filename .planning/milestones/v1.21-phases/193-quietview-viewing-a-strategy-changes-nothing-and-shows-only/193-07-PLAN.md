- `useNoteAutoSave(scope_kind, scope_ref, initialLastSavedAt)` returns `{ saveState, lastSavedAt, save }`;
  `saveState` goes `"saving"` then `"saved"` (then `"idle"` after 2 s) or `"error"`.
- The idiom to copy, `DashboardNoteCard`: `lastSavedContent` state seeded from the initial content,
  `pendingSaveRef`, an effect advancing the baseline when `saveState === "saved"`, and a blur that
  returns early when nothing changed.

  editing to true. `HoldingNoteRow` and `BridgeOutcomeNoteSection` GET the note in a mount effect and
  render a loading gate (`if (!initialLoaded) return …`); `HoldingDetail` GETs on the Notes tab and
  keeps `noteLoadError` as its fence.
