<objective>
Gate the Sharpe deltas and lifts that 194's pages show from the portfolio components: the discovery list's Simulate Impact panel, and the Holdings optimizer, under D-11 as superseded by D-15 (withheld only when a compared record fails the minimum; a short window qualifies).

Purpose: D-11 "Sharpe deltas / lifts ... gated on the shorter of the spans of the figures they compare", as superseded by D-15. Census R3 lists both sites. The simulator response carries no annualisation basis today, so without one field from the producer the client could not tell a crypto book (90 calendar days) from a traditional one (60 observations).

**Shared with Phase 195: `src/components/portfolio/PortfolioOptimizer.tsx` and `src/components/portfolio/PortfolioOptimizer.test.tsx` (on the 195 branch).** Task 2 runs only after 195 has merged (its precondition). Known limits, stated: a suggestion stored before plan 13 ships has no state and shows as today until the optimizer next runs; a simulator response from an analytics deploy older than this plan has no `annualization_periods` and shows as today.

Output: the producer field, the gated impact panel, the passed-through lift fields, the gated lift cell, tests.
</objective>
