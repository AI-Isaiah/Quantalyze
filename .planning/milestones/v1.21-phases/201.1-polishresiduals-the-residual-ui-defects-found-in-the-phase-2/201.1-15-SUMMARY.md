## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] CoverageTimeline end label spilled 9.2px out of the section at 320px**
- **Found during:** Task 2 (the as-built `flex-wrap justify-between` row from plan 201.1-13).
- **Measured at 320px:** the track is 70px wide (193 to 263, and the section's right edge is also 263), while the start label is 72.6px and the end label 79.2px. The wrapper's `flex-1` has `min-width: auto`, i.e. one whole label, so it was 79px wide and pushed the end label to 272.2, 9.2px past the track and the section (the start label ended at 265.6). The UI-SPEC premise was "the track is about 118px at 320px".
- **Fix:** `min-w-0` lets the wrapper shrink to the track; `justify-end` with `mr-auto` on the start label packs toward the right edge, so a label wider than the track spills LEFT over the empty `aria-hidden` spacer instead of right out of the section. At 390 and 1280 the boxes are identical to the as-built ones. Within Interaction contract 2a (spacer, font classes and `formatDay` endpoints kept).
- **Measured after:** 320: start [190.4, 263.0], end [183.8, 263.0] on separate lines, both right edges at the track's right edge (263.0); 390: start [193.0, 265.6] on line 1, end [253.8, 333.0] on line 2, right edge 333.0 = track right edge; 1280: both on one line, start [469.0, 541.6], end [1127.8, 1207.0], track [469.0, 1207.0].
- **Files modified:** `CoverageTimeline.tsx`, `CoverageTimeline.test.tsx`, `e2e/layout-narrow.spec.ts`
