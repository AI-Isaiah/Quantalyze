## Rewrite shape

Only the spaces inside a helper-printed date changed: month to day, comma to year, month to year, and in a day-with-time the time to `UTC`. The spaces around a range's en dash, the one after the year's comma in a day-with-time and the ` · ` separators stay ordinary. No fixture input changed, no matcher was loosened, no expected value is built with a helper. The `AddAllocationFlow` regex carries ` ` exactly where the date's two spaces were.

## Deviations from Plan

None - plan executed as written. Notes the reader needs:

- **Absence assertions kept live (see key-decisions).** `StrategyBrowseDrawer.test.tsx` lines with `not.toContain("Aug 4, 2026")`, `"Jul 20, 2026"` and `"Jan 1, 1970"` were not red (an absence passes trivially) but would have become vacuous after the helper change, so they now use the no-break form.
- **Month-label filter in `CustomRangePicker.test.tsx`.** The two `/^[A-Z][a-z]+ \d{4}$/` filters that select the calendar month labels got the same ` `; a label printed with an ordinary space now selects nothing, so the `toEqual` on the labels still fails on a regression.
- **Left unchanged on purpose, not red and not weakened by me:** `CustomRangePicker.test.tsx` `getByText("Feb 1, 2024 – May 1, 2024")` and `getByText(/Mar 2024/)` / `(/Apr 2024/)`, and `deletion-requests/page.test.tsx` `getByText("Oct 9, 2026, 14:05 UTC")` / `"Oct 10, 2026, 08:30 UTC"`. Testing-library's default normaliser folds U+00A0 to an ordinary space in the node text before comparing, so these pass with the ordinary-space matcher and would FAIL with a ` ` matcher. They are pre-existing normalising matchers (the plan forbids introducing one, and I did not). They do not pin the no-break rule; the sibling `textContent` assertions in the same files do. A follow-up that wants them to pin it needs a custom `normalizer` or a switch to `textContent`, which is outside this plan's rule.
- `ScenarioCompareTable.test.tsx` line 36 (a doc comment quoting `Jan 2, 2024 – Jun 1, 2024`) was left as prose.
