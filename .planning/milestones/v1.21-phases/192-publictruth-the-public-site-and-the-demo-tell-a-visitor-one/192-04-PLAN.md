## Decision notes

- **Copy is the UI-SPEC's, verbatim, except where 192-EVIDENCE.md disagrees.** The Fernet description is copied from the cryptography docs line recorded in the evidence (closes RESEARCH A1); if that line differs from the UI-SPEC wording, the evidence wins and the SUMMARY records it.
- **Retention (orchestrator resolution 5).** At HEAD the packet says raw fills are kept 30 days and hard-deleted by a daily job, while /security says fills and aggregates are kept for the life of the connection. A planning-time grep found no scheduled deletion of trade fills; plan 192-01 measures it. Both documents state only what `Retention at HEAD:` records; if it reads `undeterminable`, the retention claim is removed from both and the two sentences that advertise "retention windows" are trimmed to match.
- **Packet transport row.** The UI-SPEC asks the packet to carry "the same facts as the two rows above" (In Transit and At Rest). The packet has no transport row today, so one is added.
- **Static list.** The packet HTML cannot import `EXCHANGES`; its list is typed to match and a parity test reads `EXCHANGES` so the two cannot drift silently under the default flags.
- The signed-in wizard steps' false cipher text is out of this plan's scope (Phase 200, booked by plan 192-10).
