Form rules (UI-SPEC Interaction contract 1; D-19; U-01):
- Form A (`Term`, `src/components/ui/Term.tsx`) goes around the word `Scenario` only, keeping it as Term children, and never inside a button, link, summary or tab.
- Form B wraps an existing control in `Tooltip` (`src/components/ui/Tooltip.tsx`) with `termDefinition("scenario")`. Use the function-children form, so `aria-describedby` lands on the control itself and the wrapper spans carry no `aria-*` attribute. Plan 201-18's `ProfileTabs` "Mandate" tab is the in-repo precedent: a `term` field on the item, a typed `TermKey`, and a wrapper with `className="flex shrink-0 [&>span]:flex"` so the trigger sits in its strip as before.
- Census marker: the scanner accepts a `term` property beside a `label` property in the same object literal, or a `term` attribute on the element, as covered.

Why the tab wrapper is safe, measured 2026-11-11: `AllocationsTabs` warns that re-nesting tabs re-introduces axe `aria-required-children`. Read in `node_modules/axe-core` 4.14.0, `getOwnedRoles` looks through an owned element that has no role, no global `aria-*` attribute and is not focusable, and checks its children. So the function-children wrapper is transparent to that rule; a wrapper carrying `aria-describedby` would not be. The tab test pins that the wrapper spans carry no role, no `aria-*` and no `tabindex`. The seeded `e2e/composer-axe.spec.ts` gate checks it in CI; no Playwright runs locally.
