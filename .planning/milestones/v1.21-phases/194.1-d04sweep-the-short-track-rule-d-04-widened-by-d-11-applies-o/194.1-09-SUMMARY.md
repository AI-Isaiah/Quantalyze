# Phase 194.1 Plan 09: Scenario constituent figures on their own record Summary

**An added Scenario constituent's detail-row CAGR and Sharpe are withheld, marked `<1y`, or shown on that strategy's own total record, for book legs and drawer-added legs alike, with all logic in a new helper and 14 one-line hunks in the shared composer.**

### Plan behavior not reachable through the composer UI

The plan's composer case "a BOOK leg whose lazy fetch answered a 400-day record: book wins" cannot be built through the UI. Every composer fetch seam (the add seam, the Bridge seam and the hydration effect) is guarded by `!strategyById.has(id)`, so a book leg never has a lazy entry. Book-wins is pinned at the helper instead (`constituent-figure.test.ts`, both directions, plus a book with no scalars staying unknown), and neuter 2 goes RED there. The composer carries three book-leg cases (40-day withheld, 400-day full, hand-built payload without scalars).

## Limits recorded, not fixed

- The three scalars cannot carry the record's `unreadable` flag, the same limit the dashboard book payload has. An unreadable rankable record is reported once at the route (Sentry, reason `figure-record-unreadable`); the consumer sees a null span, an unknown record, and shows the stored figure as for a stale deploy. No fourth field was invented.
- A stale deploy that omits the scalars reads as an unknown record (`"null"` state): the figure shows as today, no marker.
