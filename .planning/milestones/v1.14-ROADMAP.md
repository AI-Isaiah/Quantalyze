## Core value

Options-trading Deribit strategies get an HONEST daily factsheet. An un-smoothed
options book lumps a whole session's P&L onto the single settlement day (live:
94%/day, +3305%/month — total correct, daily worth false) and `mark_to_market`
is honestly gated OFF for it. `smoothed_mtm` — a THIRD selectable basis — spreads
each lump across the days it accrued via daily option marks (`Book[d]−Book[d−1]`),
total-preserving, so the daily worth is real. Existing `cash_settlement` (headline/
peer-rank) and `mark_to_market` stay BYTE-IDENTICAL (SC-4).
