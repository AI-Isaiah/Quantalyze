This plan (the tracer of the composer chain) derives the one pending boolean, holds the strip's Sharpe cell through `gatedBlendMetrics`, and makes a failed probe terminal (CONTEXT clarification F5). Measured (checker round 1): `KpiStrip` paints YTD TWR, Sharpe, Max DD 12m and Avg |ρ| only; the blend's volatility, Sortino and CAGR have no strip cell. A real basis flip needs a traditional book leg plus a drawer leg answered traditional: while pending the drawer leg's null class derives 365 (RANK-06), and on settle the blend derives 252. A crypto drawer leg would derive 365 both before and after, so it exercises no flip.

Purpose: a drawer-added leg can no longer flip √365 → √252 under a number the user is reading.
