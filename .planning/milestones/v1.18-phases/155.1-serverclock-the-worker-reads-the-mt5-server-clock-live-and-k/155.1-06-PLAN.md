<objective>
Harden the contract of the one tick-time method Plan 01 admitted (D-D). An AST pin keeps the committed remote expression to tick times only, and offline behaviour proofs cover the far-side result, every error shape and the session fence.

Purpose: D-D admits exactly one exception to the read-only surface. That exception holds only while the committed remote expression reads no price (D-04) and changes no terminal state (D-05). Without a pin, either could be added to the string silently, because `test_read_only_surface_no_trade_methods` inspects client-side attributes and cannot see inside the string.
Output: the `test_SERVER_TICK_TIMES_*` family in `analytics-service/tests/test_mt5_client_contract.py`.

This plan was split out of Plan 01 on 2026-11-17 (checker scope_sanity: Plan 01 was estimated at 1.20x the per-plan budget). It was appended as 06 rather than renumbered, so the cross-references in Plans 02-05 stay valid. Its files are disjoint from Plans 02, 03 and 04, so it runs in wave 2 beside them.
</objective>
