<objective>
Count each skip of the no-advance weekly cap, per tick, with its reason (ROADMAP SC-2, second half).

Decisions this plan implements:
- CONTEXT SC-2 (Q1 = A): "Each skip is counted per tick with its reason in public.cron_runs, under an error/cause tag that cannot collide with candidate_enqueue_failed".
- CONTEXT "Claude's Discretion": the exact tag and metadata shape, and whether a zero-skip tick writes a row, provided the prober's counts are not disturbed.
- RESEARCH addendum decision (1): count the new cap's skips only, with their reason.

Discretion choices made here:
- `status 'ok'`, `error NULL`, `metadata.cause = 'no_advance_weekly_cap'`;
- one row per tick listing the targets;
- no row when nothing was skipped (no hourly noise rows, and the table is never purged);
- the cohort excludes marks-paused strategies, so the reason is unambiguous.

Purpose: a cap with no counter is a silent exclusion as far as an operator can see. The counter makes every capped strategy visible each hour with its age.

Output: the skip-count statement and check-7 update in the plan 03 migration, three arms with twins, the regenerated snapshot and a runbook paragraph.
</objective>
