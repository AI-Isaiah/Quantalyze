<objective>
Wire the proven 131-01a/01b smoothed core into persistence: a THIRD basis pass in BOTH worker
routes (single-key + composite), the smoothed KIND in basis_series.py, the
`metrics_json_by_basis["smoothed_mtm"]` write, and — the point of the phase — OPENING the
`unsmoothed_options_book` gate for the smoothed basis (mark_to_market stays honestly gated
OFF on unsmoothed options books; smoothed_mtm is the basis that CAN serve them).

Purpose: the frontend (131-03) reads `strategy_analytics.metrics_json_by_basis.smoothed_mtm`
and the persisted smoothed series — nothing renders until the worker persists them.
Output: both routes persist the third basis; gate opened for smoothed only; SC-4 holds at the
worker layer (no option activity ⇒ no third pass ⇒ byte-identical persisted rows).

Constraint carried from 131-01a/01b: NOTHING on the cash_settlement / mark_to_market passes
changes. The third pass is ADDITIVE — cloned from the MTM second pass, gated on option
activity, never replacing or reordering the existing passes.

FOUNDER-VISIBLE TRADE-OFF (surfaced per plan-check MEDIUM-3/MEDIUM-4, accepted under locked
D-07): an options key whose held instrument's life comes to STRADDLE the ~2.5yr mark-retention
horizon flips from green to HARD-FAILING at the job level — the smoothed pass raises
LedgerValuationError (partial marks are never interpolated, never bucketed as pre-retention)
and the whole job fails loud, so ALL persisted analytics for that key stop refreshing on
future recomputes as the retention window moves. This is the deliberate consequence of
no-interpolation/no-fallback: the alternative (silently smoothing a partial book, or silently
degrading to two bases) fabricates a basis. If it fires in production it is an operational
signal (persistent mark cache is the named follow-up knob), not a defect.
</objective>
