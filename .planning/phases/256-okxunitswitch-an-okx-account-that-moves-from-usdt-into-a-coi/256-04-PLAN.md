`build_switched_series(*, switch: UnitSwitch, split: SplitPnl, funding_rows: Sequence[Mapping[str, Any]], external_flows: Sequence[ExternalFlow], anchor_usd: float, open_unrealized_usd: float, closes: pd.Series, latest_close_day: str, usd_full_history: bool) -> SwitchedSeries | str`.
`SwitchedSeries` NamedTuple: `headline: pd.Series` (USD daily returns, whole history), `meta: dict[str, Any]` (the USD segment's NavTWRMeta keys, plus the coin segment's guard flags merged by union), `usd_returns: pd.Series`, `coin_returns: pd.Series` (native, index from first_coin_return_day), `coin_day_pnl: pd.Series` (native), `coin_nav_levels: pd.Series` (native realized levels from S to latest_close_day), `coin_flow_by_day: dict[str, float]` (native), `coin_base_level: float` (level at end of S, in the coin), `unpriced_days: tuple[str, ...]`.
Refusals (strings from plan-01 UNIT_SWITCH_REFUSALS plus one new): `switch_day_unpriced`, `coin_segment_unpriced`, and `SWITCH_ANCHOR_UNPRICED = "switch_anchor_unpriced"` (the latest completed close is not stored yet; the derive retries transient, MT5 D-17 rule).

## Marks (planner decision, flagged)
This builder applies no marks. The derive applies the stored USD marks once, to the whole USD headline, with its existing unchanged block (precedent: a BTC MT5 key "has been converted to dollars at the stored daily closes above, so its marks apply as nav_usd", job_worker marks apply block). RESEARCH suggested re-basing per segment; not taken, because the apply block's downstream (key payload terminal, writer basis, tiny-base days, marks flags) binds to one MarkedSeries and per-segment application would fork it. Known limit, recorded for the founder: the native coin line follows the coin bills and the anchor, so a stable residual's coin-value drift reaches the USD headline through the marks but not the coin line.
