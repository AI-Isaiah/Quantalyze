# Phase 256 — UI Design Contract

> Visual and interaction contract for the presentation half of OKXUNITSWITCH (RESEARCH plan 04
> PRESENT). Generated by gsd-ui-researcher, verified by gsd-ui-checker. DESIGN.md governs; this
> file only says how its existing tokens and idioms apply to a strategy whose unit switches once.

**Scope.** Presentation only. The phase adds no new token, colour, font, size, weight or spacing value.
It changes four things:
1. One chart, the factsheet's Cumulative Returns track, draws two native segments with a real break,
   and a unit label at the switch.
2. A switch sentence, built from the derived days, appears wherever the unit appears.
3. The headline KPIs stay whole-record USD, with a note that names the switch.
4. Two list and share surfaces carry the sentence.

**Sources of decisions.**
- CONTEXT "Readers": the founder's answers of 2026-11-27, quoted verbatim:
  - "Whole record in USD (Recommended)"
  - "One chart, marked switch (Recommended)"
  - "USD, BTC part converted (Recommended)"
  - the copy "In USD to 14 Nov, in BTC from 15 Nov"
- RESEARCH "Reader census" (TypeScript table) and "Storage verdict".
- The 164.6.6.2.1 BTCUSDVIEW UI-SPEC is the precedent for the disclosure line, the compact chip and
  the curve break.

## The rules this phase adds

1. **The data the presentation reads.**
   - **The headline series is USD.** For a switched strategy `payload.returnsUnit` stays unset, so
     the strategy shows no `Returns in BTC` masthead chip, no BTC/USD unit toggle and no
     `convertedFrom`.
   - **The new fields.** The presentation reads two additive payload fields:
     - `unitSwitch`, the parsed `data_quality_flags.unit_switch`.
     - `unitSegments`, the parsed `strategy_analytics.unit_segments` sidecar.
     The planner may rename them; the shape they carry is fixed by rule 2.
   - **Absent or malformed fields.** If `unitSwitch` is absent or malformed (a unit failing
     `RETURNS_UNIT_RE`, a day failing the strict ISO parse, or a first coin day not exactly one UTC
     day after the last USD day), the strategy renders as a plain USD strategy. That render is
     byte-identical to today's, and nothing in it is guessed. This follows `parseReturnsUnit`'s
     posture.
2. **The label binds to two named days, never to one `switch_day`.**
   - The composer takes `{ fromUnit, toUnit, lastFromDay, firstToDay }`:
     - `fromUnit` is the stable unit, rendered `USD`.
     - `toUnit` is the coin.
     - `lastFromDay` is the last USD return day, S.
     - `firstToDay` is the first coin return day, S+1.
   - RESEARCH and CONTEXT use `switch_day` for different days. RESEARCH means S; the revised
     CONTEXT "Boundary day" bullet means S+1. The UI never reads a field called `switch_day` alone.
   - Fixture (required): `lastFromDay = 2026-11-14`, `firstToDay = 2026-11-15` renders the
     founder's dates, 14 and 15 Nov.
3. **The no-join rule applies only where native units are drawn.** That is one place: the factsheet
   Cumulative Returns chart. Every other curve draws the headline USD series unbroken (surface table
   below).
4. **No figure is computed across the boundary in mixed units.**
   - The drawn segments are each compounded within their own unit, and each restarts at 1.00×.
   - Every number shown as a KPI, a window KPI or a hover value either comes from the all-USD
     headline series or is a native value labelled with its unit.
5. **Tone.**
   - A unit switch is a steady fact about the account, so every new line and mark is neutral (muted
     or secondary text). It is never amber and never red (DESIGN.md semantic-colour gates; the
     164.6.6.2 D-08 rule that a denomination is not a warning).
   - Returns keep their existing sign tone (`signColor`).
6. **Print.** Every sentence this phase adds sits in a printing HTML region (masthead, figcaption,
   metrics note). None lives only in a `title` attribute or only inside the SVG. `title`s are
   additions for hover and exported SVGs, never the only carrier.
7. **No quote pair is named** (164.6.6.2.1 D-21).
   - Conversion copy says "the stored daily BTC close", the wording `UsdDisclosure` already uses.
   - It never says "BTC/USD", "BTC-USDT" or "BTCUSD".

## A. The Cumulative Returns chart: two segments, one break

**Geometry (prescriptive).**
0. **X axis.**
   - The chart keeps indexing `view.dates`, the headline series' dates, because `xRange` is shared
     page state in `factsheet-context.tsx` across every chart.
   - Native segment values are looked up by date onto those indices.
   - A native coin day that is absent from `view.dates` (for example, a day without a stored close)
     gets no x of its own. Its native return is still compounded into the next plotted native
     point, so the coin level drawn at every plotted day is the true native cumulative. No value is
     interpolated or invented.
1. **USD segment.** It runs from the series start through `lastFromDay` (S), in USD growth (1.00× at
   its own base), exactly as today.
2. **Coin segment.** Its first vertex is its BASE at 1.00×, drawn at the x of `lastFromDay`; the
   base is the coin level at the end of S. Its first return point is `firstToDay` (S+1). From there it
   compounds the native coin returns.
3. **The break.** The USD path ends at `(x(S), USD level)` and the coin path starts at
   `(x(S), 1.00)`. They are two separate `<path>` elements, so the SVG draws no vertical or diagonal
   segment between them. This is a real lift, NOT the FS-02 gap seam: the seam joins its neighbours,
   and the founder's "no line joining the two units" forbids that join.
4. **Marker.** One vertical line at `x(S)` across the plot height:
   - stroke `var(--color-text-secondary)`, `strokeWidth={1}`, solid, the marks-start idiom;
   - in a `<g pointerEvents="none" data-testid="unit-switch-marker">`;
   - behind the series;
   - pan/zoom-synced and export-safe like `segmentMarkers`;
   - with `<title>` set to the full switch sentence (Copywriting), so an exported SVG carries it.
5. **Unit labels.** Short codes only, never the sentence. The coin segment is about 5% of the plot
   width at full range, so a full sentence would clip.
   - `fromUnit` (`USD`) sits at `x(S) − 4`, `textAnchor="end"`.
   - `toUnit` (`BTC`) sits at `x(S) + 4`, `textAnchor="start"`.
   - Both sit at `y = PAD.top + 37`.
   - If the `toUnit` label would cross the plot's right edge, it moves to `x(S) − 4`, end-anchored,
     on the next row (`PAD.top + 50`). The two codes then stack left of the marker, `USD` above
     `BTC`. Nothing is truncated.
6. **Y axis.**
   - Both segments share the existing growth axis, which reads in ×.
   - The axis title keeps its existing text. `unitTitle` stays as today, since no `returnsUnit` is
     set.
   - The figcaption says that the two parts are separate growth measures.
   - The default log scale holds; both segments are positive growth.
7. **Rebase on zoom** (`rebaseOnZoom`):
   - A window starting inside the USD segment rebases the USD part at the window start. The coin part
     stays at its own base.
   - A window starting inside the coin segment rebases the coin part at the window start. No USD
     part is drawn.
   - A rebase never crosses `x(S)`.
8. **Hover readout.**
   - The value carries its unit: `{formatDay(date)} · {growth}× in {unit}`, using `unitTag` for the
     `in BTC` text.
   - The base vertex at `x(S)` reads the USD point (the day S belongs to the USD chain).
   - The coin base is not hoverable as a separate day.
9. **Comparator (A5).**
   - The comparator line is drawn from the window start through `lastFromDay` and stops there. Its
     path ends at the same x as the USD path.
   - It is not drawn over the coin segment: a USD benchmark beside a BTC-measured line invites a
     cross-unit comparison (SC-3).
   - The figcaption names the stop (Copywriting).
   - The Cumulative vs Benchmark chart is unaffected; it is all USD.

**States.**

| State | Render |
|-------|--------|
| Switch inside the visible window, coin segment has ≥1 return day | Full geometry above, plus the figcaption |
| Switch left of the window (window wholly in the coin segment) | Coin path only. No marker. A single `toUnit` code at the top-left of the plot (`x = padLeft + 4`, `y = PAD.top + 37`, start-anchored), plus the figcaption |
| Switch right of the window (window wholly in the USD segment) | USD path only. No marker. A single `fromUnit` code at the top-left, same position, plus the figcaption |
| Coin segment has only its base (no coin return day yet) | USD path only. No marker, no coin label. The figcaption carries the sentence plus the "no BTC day yet" clause (Copywriting). |
| `unitSwitch` absent or malformed, or `unitSegments` absent | Today's single-series chart, byte-identical. No marker, no figcaption. |
| A coin-segment day with no stored BTC close | The native coin path does not need a close, so it is drawn. The day is absent from the headline USD series, and the masthead unpriced note (B) names it; **that note is the only carrier**. The other charts get no seam, because `segmentMarkers` is set on `cumulative` alone (the 164.6.6.2.1 precedent). **Reverse case:** a headline `missingSegments` run that falls inside the coin segment is NOT drawn as a `{days}d — no data` seam on this chart, because the native line has that day. Seams from runs inside the USD segment render as today. |
| Existing FS-01 boundary markers or the marks-start marker on the same chart | All render. Each owns its own annotation row (11, 24, 37). |

**Accessibility.**
- **Screen readers.** The chart's existing sr-only help keeps its text. A new sr-only sentence is
  appended inside the same `<figure>`, before the plot: the switch sentence plus "The chart draws
  two separate lines; the BTC line starts again at 1.00×."
- **The marker.** It is `pointerEvents="none"` and is not focusable.
- **Keyboard.** Pan and zoom behaviour is unchanged.

renders with a `—` inside it.

**⚠ Named open conflict, DATE FORMAT (Rule 7: surfaced, not averaged).**
- **The two sides.**
  - The founder's copy is yearless and day-first: "In USD to 14 Nov, in BTC from 15 Nov".
  - Phase 201 D-11 / U-14 makes `formatDay` (`Nov 14, 2026`, year included) the one UI date
    format. `returns-unit.ts` says "No factsheet date is yearless (Phase 201 U-14)".
    `src/app/factsheet/[id]/trade-fetch-lines.test.ts` pins the factsheet's date helper to
    `formatDay` mechanically.
- **Chosen.** Keep the founder's sentence frame verbatim, and render the dates through
  `formatCloseDay`/`formatDay`.
- **Why.**
  - CONTEXT says "Code uses the DERIVED switch date; the founder's wording uses this account's
    dates", which reads as fixing the frame and not the date format.
  - A yearless date on a printed factsheet becomes ambiguous after a year, which fails the DESIGN.md
    dated-document test.
  - A second date formatter would break the one-format rule.
- **Not done.** No second formatter and no yearless variant.
- **Founder confirmation.** Route this to the founder at UI review / UAT. If the founder wants the
  literal `14 Nov`, that is a DESIGN-level exception to U-14. It needs its own decision recorded in
  CONTEXT and the ROADMAP; the executor must not make it inline.

**Voice checks (DESIGN.md).** The copy is:
- declarative and sentence-case;
- free of adjectives;
- free of exclamation marks and of words like "unfortunately";
- explicit about its limit, with the day attached ("The comparator is shown to Nov 14, 2026").
