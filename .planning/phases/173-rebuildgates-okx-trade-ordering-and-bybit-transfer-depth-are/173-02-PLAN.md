<objective>
Make the probe count Bybit deposits and withdrawals older than 365 days per kind, record corroboration evidence beside them, and print a strict per-account `bybit_transfers_gate` derived in code.

D-02, verbatim from CONTEXT: "The strict rule applies: a per-kind count of 0 older than 365 days keeps that kind `unmeasured` and the gate stays shut for it. The probe records corroboration evidence (for example whether the account's oldest fill is older than its oldest deposit) for the founder to judge at the checkpoint; that evidence does not open the gate by itself. The probe carries per-kind counts (deposits and withdrawals older than 365 days) as permanent record fields; the pooled `transfers_older_365d` stays for lineage."
SC-2, verbatim from ROADMAP `### Phase 173`: "Bybit deposit and withdrawal depth older than 365 days is measured per kind on a 730-day account."

Every piece is an aggregation of data the probe already records: `_TradeRecorder` stores every transfer the rebuild reads as `(kind, id, timestamp)` and every trade call's `oldest_ts`. No venue call is added. RESEARCH Pitfall 3 is why the corroboration exists: the rebuild's transfer read is on-chain only, so a 0 can come from the funding route and is not a depth finding.

Purpose: SC-2 can be met on a6 on the next deployed probe run, and the founder gets the evidence D-02 asks for. Output: the probe and its tests changed (same two files as plan 01, so this plan runs after it).
</objective>
