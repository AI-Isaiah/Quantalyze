# Phase 164.5.2.1 Plan 03: the in-bridge lock, plus the founder's hold-the-date decision Summary

**One-liner:** `sync_strategy_analytics_status` now takes the mark RPCs' own two-integer per-strategy
advisory lock as its first statement after the NULL guard. A new LANE-ONLY dblink gate proves it: a
direct bridge call waits on that exact lock (namespace oid, objsubid 2) behind a mark, where before
it waited on `transactionid`. Per the founder decision of 2026-10-03, a row that branch (a) KEEPS
now holds `computed_at`, `computation_error` and both provenance markers, so nothing that was not
recomputed reads as fresh.

   already reads what branch (a) resolves it to, meaning `complete_with_warnings`, or plain `complete`

   The migration header's D-18 paragraph is narrowed and dated, with the 2026-09-27 text kept as
   lineage. **Plan 05's TODOS and runbook closeout of D-18 should cite the narrowing.** No gate arm

   NULL,` lines. They are now the ELSE arms of hold CASEs. The carried P2d count is now 1, not 2
