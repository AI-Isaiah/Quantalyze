## Founder decision surfaced: D-18 PRE corner

**Default (a), "accept and record", ACCEPTED 2026-09-27, ratified by the founder the same day.**

**The corner.** A SIBLING's bridge call lands while X is still `running`, after the Python honour
write and before X's own mark. That call takes branch (a) and blanks the provenance. After a marker
retraction, X's failure then cannot be tied to its writer, and `computation_warned` stays up until
the runner's next success writer.

**Conditions.** It needs BOTH of these inside one window:
1. a marker-retraction race, AND
2. a sibling terminal transition between the honour write and X's mark.

It was measured OPEN under every SQL shape considered.

**Narrowed 2026-10-03.** On a `complete_with_warnings` row, the founder's hold-the-date keep makes
the sibling's call a keep that holds X's provenance, so X's loud mark clears the warning. A pg-lane
probe read warned `t` before `failed` and `f` after.

**Exits the founder can still take:**
- (b) route a Python-side item, because the honour path is the only place that knows X's writer
  reached the row.
- (c) a SQL redesign in which branch (a) preserves sentence and provenance for a still-non-terminal
  job. That changes Phase 164.2's "branch (a) clears both markers" contract.

**Where it is recorded:** the migration header, the `[164.6.7-COMPOSITE-REREAD-RESIDUE]` closeout,
runbook item 7 and the CHANGELOG `### Notes`.
