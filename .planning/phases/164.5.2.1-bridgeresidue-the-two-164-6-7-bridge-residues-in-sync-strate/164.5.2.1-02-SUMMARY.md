# Phase 164.5.2.1 Plan 02: RETRY-PLAIN-COMPLETE closed in SQL Summary

**One-liner:** Branch (a) of the bridge now keeps a published plain `complete` row at `complete`, with
the reaper stamp NULL, when every in-flight job carries an in-scope refresh marker and no unprotected
failure is live. The rule rests on an unmarked-job count folded into read 1, in the same snapshot. Six
gate arms, each with a layered twin that bites, prove the keep is no wider than D-05. The residue gate
is now sealed on `sql-tests` with `ALL 13 ARMS EXECUTED`.

## ⚠️ Open items (pre-merge)

**[164.5.2.1-02-KEPT-ROW-COMPUTED-AT] needs a decision before merge.** The full write-up is in
`deferred-items.md`. On a kept row, branch (a) still rewrites three things:
- `computed_at` to `now()`
- `computation_error` to NULL
- both provenance markers to NULL

The status and the stamp are the only two columns the keep holds. The `computed_at` behaviour is
inherited from the `complete_with_warnings` keep arm, which has always worked this way. It does not
affect the staleness verdict, which keys on `returns_series` dates. It does advance the FreshnessChip
and PDF vintage on a row whose refresh keeps failing transiently. There are three options:
1. Accept it.
2. Narrow fix: one `computed_at` CASE plus one arm. The sentinel moves to 14 and `ARMS_FLOOR` to 254.
3. Class-wide fix: the narrow fix plus the warned arm. This changes PROD behaviour for the warned cohort.

I did not fix it inside this plan. D-05 is locked at two arms, and an honest fix applies to the whole
class.
