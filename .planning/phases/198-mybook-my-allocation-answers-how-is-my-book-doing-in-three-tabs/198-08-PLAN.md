## Entry point for Phase 196

| URL | Result |
|-----|--------|
| `/allocations?add=<id>`, a strategy the picker lists | The flow opens over the Overview at step 2, pre-filled, with focus on Amount. `add` leaves the URL. |
| `/allocations?add=<id>`, a strategy already in the book | Step 1 with `{Strategy} is already in your book. Change its amount on Holdings.` and `Open Holdings`. |
| `/allocations?add=<id>`, malformed or unlisted | Step 1 with `That strategy is not available to add. Search for another below.`, plus a `warnAudit` breadcrumb. |
| `/allocations?add=<id>`, unlisted while the 200-row list is truncated | Step 1 with `That strategy is not in the list we loaded. Search for it below.`, plus a breadcrumb. |
| `/allocations?add=` | Step 1, no notice. |
| `/allocations?tab=holdings&add=<id>` | Over Holdings. The URL keeps `?tab=holdings`. |
| reload after any of the above | No flow. |
