**Corrected premise.** The ROADMAP's item (3) assumed a Bybit key's identity could be missed by the UTA wallet parse. It cannot: a Bybit key's account identity is read by `venue_account_id_from` from the `/v5/user/query-api` body, `result.userID` (never `parentUid`, because a sub-account has its own userID). `_extract_bybit_unified_walletbalances` is a holdings parse (per-coin `walletBalance` from the unified-account `info`), unrelated to identity. A key whose venue answers without an id is already flagged three ways: the stamper's `no_id` outcome (warning, then ERROR via the `_unstamped_for_too_long` path), `escalate_if_unchecked` with `UNSTAMPED_ESCALATION_DAYS` = 3 (ccxt and MT5 keys older than three days and still unstamped log at ERROR so Sentry sees them), and the Overview's `identityStillPending` hold, which surfaces `account_identity_pending` while a key's identity is unknown. A departed unstamped key falls under D-01 instead (the new TODOS entry).
