the phase's original deliverable was a no-op against its own target fault (criterion 1(a), probed
live: `PROBE {"initialize": false, "last_error": [-6, "Terminal: Authorization failed"],
"terminal_info": null}`). The heal verb is the CREDENTIALED form of `initialize()`. ⛔ And the
moment a password is passed to it, `mt5linux` 0.1.9's `f'mt5.initialize(*{args},**{kwargs})'`
passthrough makes a raw rpyc remote traceback a real credential disclosure (T-134-01). Today only
`login`'s `login` arm runs the by-value redaction loop; its `initialize` arm carries
`scrub_freeform_string` alone. Routing credentials through `initialize()` without that loop
REGRESSES A SHIPPED SECURITY CONTROL.

Output: two fenced public methods and one module-level key helper on `analytics-service/services/mt5_client.py`;
a derived, calibrated credential-redaction gate in `analytics-service/tests/test_mt5_client_contract.py`.
</objective>

<ordering_is_the_point>
## ⛔ WHY THIS PLAN IS WAVE 1 AND WHY IT CARRIES NO END-TO-END SLICE

This phase opts OUT of tracer-first decomposition, deliberately and for a stated reason:
criterion 1 carries an **ORDERED security precondition**. The thinnest end-to-end slice would
route an env credential from `main.lifespan` through `initialize()` before the redaction that
covers it exists. That is the one ordering the ROADMAP forbids. So the phase is decomposed
security-first: this plan lands the redaction and its falsifier; **plan 02 is the first plan that
routes a credential, and it cannot execute until this one is green** (`depends_on`).

The credentialed verb and its redaction land in the SAME commit — they are inseparable, and a
commit carrying one without the other is the regression this ordering exists to prevent.
</ordering_is_the_point>

RESEARCH's Pitfall 1 recommends a shared by-value redaction helper rather than a second copy of
the four-line loop. Extracting one means rewriting `Mt5Client.login`'s except arm.

**Rejected, on D-07 and on CLAUDE.md Rule 3.** D-07 is a founder decision whose stated reason is

live job processing. Rule 3 says do not refactor what is not broken. A four-line duplication is

hazard is that a THIRD credential-carrying call site lands one day without the loop. A shared
helper does not prevent that — a new author simply would not call it. Task 2 fences the CLASS

property enforceable.

method redacts by value on BOTH arms (transport raise AND falsy return). `Mt5Client.login`'s falsy
