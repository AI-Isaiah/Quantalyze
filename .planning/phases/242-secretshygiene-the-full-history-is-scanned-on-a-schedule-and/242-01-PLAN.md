## Interfaces and traps for this plan (read before writing a line)

- **gitleaks 8.30.1 semantics, measured in RESEARCH "Pattern 1":** the git-mode fingerprint is `Commit:File:RuleID:StartLine`. A three-part `file:rule:line` line is a GLOBAL fingerprint, checked first, that suppresses that site in every past and future commit; never write one. A line that does not split into exactly 3 or 4 parts on `:` is logged at warn level as `Invalid .gitleaksignore entry` and is inert. An inline comment after a fingerprint therefore voids the line silently (measured: 29 of 31 suppressed). Comments are whole lines beginning `#`; blank lines are skipped. gitleaks finds `.gitleaksignore` in the source directory even when the cwd is elsewhere. It scans only added lines of each commit's patch, so a commit that lists a fingerprint is not itself a finding.
- **Plan-time measurement (2026-11-27, this planner):** `gitleaks git . --config .gitleaks.toml --log-opts=origin/main --redact --no-banner --report-format json --report-path <scratch>/main.json --exit-code 7` at origin/main `45d7b4e5c` printed `2743 commits scanned`, `leaks found: 31`, exit 7: 30 `generic-api-key` + 1 `jwt` across 22 files, 31 distinct fingerprints, the only distinct Secret value `REDACTED`. The RESEARCH table "The 31 findings" names every site by commit, file, rule and line. Re-measure; suppress what the scan reports then.
- **Analogs (read by symbol, never by line number):** the gitleaks install step is the step named `Install gitleaks (sha256-pinned release binary)` in `.github/workflows/supabase-migrate.yml`; copy it byte for byte including its comment on why the checksum file is never fetched at runtime. The `report` job of `.github/workflows/sql-mutation-nightly.yml` (`needs`, `if: always() && needs.<job>.result != 'success'`, `permissions` with `issues: write`, `actions/github-script` at its pinned SHA, `listForRepo` by label, comment-or-create) is the issue-filing template; its header block (WHAT THIS IS / WHY IT FAILS RED AND FILES AN ISSUE) and its D-11 sentence are the header template. `anchorIndex` and `jobSlice` in `src/__tests__/sql-mutation-nightly-parity.test.ts` are the text-pin helpers to copy; `gitleaksAvailable` and `HAS_GITLEAKS` with `it.skipIf` in `src/__tests__/gitleaks-allowlist.test.ts` are the real-binary pattern.
