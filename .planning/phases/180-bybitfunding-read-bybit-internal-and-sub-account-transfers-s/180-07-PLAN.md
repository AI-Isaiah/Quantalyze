<objective>
Refuse a forced Bybit rebuild by name when its Unified flow read cannot be done, make the unforced failure permanent rather than retried, and move the closed refusal vocabulary together.

D-04 (founder 2026-10-28), verbatim: "Wave 2: the rebuild's read, with tests that go RED on the on-chain-only read and on a dropped page (SC-2)." This plan finishes D-04's wave 2 (plans 04, 05 and 06 built the reader, the page fix and the rebuild's branch).
D-03 and orchestrator reading 2 as quoted in `must_haves`.

Why the classifier arm: `BybitUnifiedFlowReadError` is a `RuntimeError`, and `classify_exception` (`services/job_worker.py`) returns `unknown` for one, which the queue retries, about 210 requests per retry for a fault no retry can clear. Orchestrator reading 2 says permanent. The smallest change in the classifier's own pattern is one `isinstance` arm, as `Mt5GatewayMisconfigured` has; the reader's text is static by contract (plan 04), so the message can be shipped as it is. It changes no flow kind and no reader (D-02).

Purpose: SC-2's "never a silent 0" on the rebuild's read, forced and unforced. Output: the refusal wrapper, the 17th token, its pins, the runbook list, the classifier arm and their tests.
</objective>
