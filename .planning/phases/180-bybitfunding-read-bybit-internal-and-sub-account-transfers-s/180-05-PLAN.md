## What ccxt hands back (ccxt 4.5.88, verified in RESEARCH Q3)

`fetch_deposits` / `fetch_withdrawals` call `add_pagination_cursor_to_result`, which writes `nextPageCursor` onto the `info` of the FIRST raw row (`data[0]`) when the venue returned one; `parse_transactions` then re-sorts the page ASCENDING by timestamp, so the cursor sits on the `info` of one row (the newest, for a newest-first venue). `params["cursor"]` is passed through to the request and `params["until"]` becomes `endTime`. The same mechanism carries the trade walk's cursor (`_next_page_cursor` in `services/equity_reconstruction.py`). Bybit's documented page size for these endpoints is 1 to 50. The repo's existing transfer doubles (`_make_capped_transfer_fetcher` in `tests/test_exchange_pagination.py`) answer ascending and use a `MagicMock` exchange whose `id` is not "bybit", which is why the old defect never showed (RESEARCH Pitfall 5); they stay as they are for the generic path.

Do NOT import `services.equity_reconstruction` here (it imports this module); copy its cursor scan as a private helper.
