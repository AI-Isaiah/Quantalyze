## Vendor facts (`GET /v5/account/transaction-log`, read 2026-10-28; RESEARCH Q1)

`accountType` "UNIFIED"; `type` is ONE value per request; `startTime`/`endTime` in ms, "If both are given, the span can be at most 7 days"; `limit` 1 to 50; `cursor` = the previous `nextPageCursor`; "The endpoint supports up to 2 years of data". No sort order is documented. A row carries `id`, `type`, `currency`, `transactionTime` (string ms), `change` ("cashFlow + funding - fee"), `cashFlow`, and `""` in numeric fields it does not use (`"funding": ""` on a transfer row). The sign of a transfer row is not documented (RESEARCH A2): the direction comes from the requested type and `amount` is the absolute value. ccxt 4.5.88 method: `private_get_v5_account_transaction_log` (verified present in the pinned version and in the local 4.5.85).

Output row shape (what `_compute_daily_equity` and `ccxt_rows_to_dated_flows` read): exactly the keys `id` (str), `type` (`deposit` or `withdrawal`), `currency` (upper case), `amount` (a finite float, the absolute value), `timestamp` (int ms), `fee` (None). NEVER a key named `kind`: the replay builds its events as `{"kind": "deposit", **row}`, so a `kind` key on the row would overwrite the event kind (RESEARCH Pitfall 4). No raw `info` either: the module hands nothing from the venue onward but these six values.
