## Promotion criteria (stated in advance, before any PROD stitch runs)

They mirror the probe's pass criteria C1 to C4 and C6 (`252-06-PLAN.md`, PASS CRITERIA), evaluated on every stitch of an option-active composite. None is a numeric plausibility bound (Phase 194 D-05 holds).

| # | Criterion | Mirrors | How the stitch reads it |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | The composite has option activity | C6 | `smoothed_mtm_available(member_signals)` is True (identical to `mark_to_market_available` returning `MTM_REASON_OPTIONS`) |
| P2 | Every Deribit member's smoothed build computed, and the stitched smoothed series derived | C1, C2, C4 | `smoothed_metrics_json is not None` after step 6b. That is False after a budget refusal, a bounded-pass timeout, a `_PERMANENT_LEDGER_ERRORS` re-raise (`LedgerValuationError` covers a daily-mark hole (C2) and a refused row; `NavReconstructionError` covers the reconcile (C4)), fewer than two interpretable days, a `CompositeOverlapError` or a derive `ValueError` |
| P3 | No option day is valued at cash because its marks aged past retention | C3 | no entry of `smoothed_member_metas` carries a truthy `pre_mark_retention_option_dailies` |
| P4 | The smoothed headline has no interior chain break | new | `_smoothed_basis_result.headline_since is None` |

P4 is new and is the planner's choice, recorded here. The factsheet's dated-headline machinery (`headlineCoversFrom`, the "measured since" notes) is keyed to the cash basis. A chain-broken smoothed headline would therefore publish suffix-measured figures with no "measured since" note on the factsheet while the lists date them. A chain-broken smoothed series keeps the withhold, and the reading goes to the founder (see "Post-deploy" below). It is the fail-safe direction the ruling names.

## Design (settled at planning; the executor implements it as written)

1. **Vocabulary, in `services/stitch_composite.py`** beside `smoothed_mtm_available` (the single admissibility vocabulary owner):
   - `HEADLINE_BASIS_FLAG = "headline_basis"` and `HEADLINE_BASIS_SMOOTHED = "smoothed_mtm"`, each a module-level literal assignment on one line (plan 10's cross-language pin parses them with a regex). Do NOT name either `MTM_REASON_*`: the wave-1 census counts every `MTM_REASON_*` assignment as a gate reason.
   - `SMOOTHED_HEADLINE_NOT_PROMOTED_CAUSES`, a closed tuple: `"smoothed_basis_not_computed"`, `"retention_bucketed_option_days"`, `"smoothed_chain_broken"`.
   - `smoothed_headline_promotion(*, smoothed_computed: bool, retention_bucketed: bool, chain_broken: bool) -> tuple[bool, str | None]`: pure, returns `(True, None)` or `(False, cause)`, checking P2, then P3, then P4 in that order. P1 is the caller's guard. The docstring cites the ruling, the criteria table and the fail-safe direction.
   - Extend the `MTM_REASON_OPTIONS` comment: since Phase 252 wave 1 every TypeScript reader of this value reads it as "the headline is the cash wallet of an options book", so a promoted row does not carry it. Its MTM meaning survives in the absent `mark_to_market` by-basis key, which is what disables the MTM toggle.

2. **Flag-independent composite pass (step 6b).** `smoothed_ok` becomes `smoothed_mtm_available(member_signals)` alone. Do not read `is_smoothed_mtm_enabled()` at this site. The single-key third pass and the RT-4 refusal in `run_derive_broker_dailies_job` keep their reads, unchanged. Rewrite the 6b "Phase 134 kill-switch" comment to say why: the founder's 2026-11-25 ruling makes the smoothed basis the headline candidate for an option-active composite; GLB-3 already degrades every structural, budget and timeout failure of this pass; and the population is exactly the rows the wave-1 withhold hides, so a degrade leaves them as they are today. Precedent: 185 D-04 builds the key-mode options headline without the flag. A TRANSIENT reconstruction result (429, geo-block) still surfaces as a whole-job retry, as it does with the flag on. Update the kill-switch docstring in `services/closed_sets.py` above `is_smoothed_mtm_enabled` (comment only, no code) so it no longer claims the flag gates the composite route.

3. **Honest smoothed derive (step 6b).** The smoothed `derive_basis_series` call passes `scalar_returns=gap_fill_daily_returns(stitched_smoothed)` and `densify_policy="zero_fill"`. These are the cash derive's arguments minus the covered-day and tiny-day exclusion, because `_reconstruct_all` runs the smoothed pass with `apply_member_marks` False, so it has no covered days. Reason: the default scalar input (`gap_fill_daily_returns` of the sparse series) drops a member-guard NaN day and then fills it with 0.0. That invents a flat day and hides the break P4 checks. Confirm by test that an interior NaN in a smoothed member series sets `_smoothed_basis_result.headline_since`. The smoothed by-basis object and the smoothed series row are this same result, so the toggle view and the promoted headline agree by construction. PROD has never stored a smoothed row (CONTEXT), so no stored value moves.

4. **Decision (after step 6b, before step 7).** Only when `smoothed_ok` is True, call `smoothed_headline_promotion` with `smoothed_computed = smoothed_metrics_json is not None`, `retention_bucketed = any(m.get("pre_mark_retention_option_dailies") for m in smoothed_member_metas)` and `chain_broken = (_smoothed_basis_result is not None and _smoothed_basis_result.headline_since is not None)`. Emit one `logger.info` line, `run_stitch_composite_job: smoothed headline promoted for strategy %s`, or `... smoothed headline not promoted for strategy %s (%s)` with the cause literal. Nothing else goes in the line: no amount, no date, no member id. When `smoothed_ok` is False, there is no call and no line.

5. **Step 7 on a promoted row.** The rule: every output whose contract is to describe or agree with the HEADLINE follows the smoothed result. Every output whose contract is to describe the CASH basis stays cash.

| Output | Promoted row | Not promoted (byte-identical to HEAD) |
