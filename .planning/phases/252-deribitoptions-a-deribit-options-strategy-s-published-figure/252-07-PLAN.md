## Design (settled at planning; the executor implements it as written)

### The three subclasses (all in `services/deribit_txn.py`, beside `OptionRowFieldMissingError`)

| Class | Raised by | Closed attributes |
|---|---|---|
| `OptionMarkHoleError(LedgerValuationError)` | the D-07 hole raise in `option_mtm_daily` | `vs_marks`, `vs_retention` |
| `OptionExerciseRowError(LedgerValuationError)` | the D-09 `exercise` raise in `replay_option_positions` | `change_shape`, `position_shape`, `siblings` |
| `OptionExpiryRowError(LedgerValuationError)` | EVERY refusal inside `_expiry_close`: nonzero change, nonzero position, and the non-numeric change or position that `_coerce_float` refuses | `change_shape`, `position_shape`, `siblings` |

The constructor takes the message positionally and the attributes keyword-only. The keyword-only pattern follows `InceptionReconciliationError` in `services/native_nav.py`, but the message is passed in, never rebuilt, so `str(exc)` and `exc.args` stay byte-identical to HEAD. The `_coerce_float` paths inside `_expiry_close` keep the text `_coerce_float` writes today; one way is to catch its `LedgerValuationError` and re-raise the subclass with `str(e)`, chained. The executor chooses the mechanism, and Task 3 pins the bytes. None of the three subclasses `OptionRowFieldMissingError`.

### Closed vocabularies (module constants in `services/deribit_txn.py`, imported by the probe for validation; one definition)

| Attribute | Words | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| `vs_marks` | `no-marks`, `before-first-bar`, `between-bars`, `after-last-bar` | Over the keys of that instrument's marks map: empty means `no-marks`. A day before the earliest key is `before-first-bar`, a day after the latest key is `after-last-bar`, and anything else is `between-bars`. ISO day strings order as calendar days. |
| `vs_retention` | `older`, `not-older`, `unknown` | `unknown` when `option_mtm_daily` is given no cutoff (its pure-core direct callers). `older` when the missing day is strictly earlier than the cutoff, otherwise `not-older`. |
| `change_shape`, `position_shape` | `absent`, `non-numeric`, `zero`, `nonzero` | `absent` means the key is missing, null or a blank string, the same reading `_expiry_close` already applies to `position`. `non-numeric` means float() refuses it. `zero` and `nonzero` follow the float value. The helper never raises. |
| `siblings` | keys `trade`, `delivery`, `assignment`, `expiry`, `exercise`, `other`, each an int of at least 0, every key always present | Counts every row of the `rows` argument to `replay_option_positions` whose `instrument_name` string equals the refusing row's, the refusing row included, bucketed by `type` with any other type counted as `other`. It is computed only when refusing. |

`option_mtm_daily` gains a keyword-only `retention_cutoff: str | None = None`. Its default keeps every direct caller byte-identical. `_build_smoothed_option_mtm` passes the hard `retention_cutoff` it already computes (today minus `_option_mark_retention_days()`), not the soft tolerance band. `_expiry_close` gains the `rows` sequence its census needs. Its only caller is the per-instrument loop of `replay_option_positions`.

### Probe output (`scripts/deribit_options_smoothed_probe.py`)

`RECORD_KEYS` gains `refusal_shape`. It takes one of four values:
- `null` — a record whose build did not raise one of the three, which includes every cash record and every successful build.
- `{"kind": "hole", "vs_marks": <word>, "vs_retention": <word>}`.
- `{"kind": "exercise" | "expiry", "change": <word>, "position": <word>, "siblings": {<six keys>: <int>}}`.
- `"unreadable"` — one of the three fired but an attribute was off its closed set. The record is still emitted and is never refused (252-06 deviation 1 is the precedent).

The validator accepts exactly these shapes and nothing else. `_refuse_floats` already recurses, so a float anywhere inside is refused. The build word and detail need no new code: once the three names are in the tuple that `LEDGER_ERROR_CLASSES` derives from in `scripts/navbreach_anchor_probe.py`, `_class_word` prints `build-error:OptionMarkHoleError` and `classify_ledger_error` prints `OptionMarkHoleError:position`. The criteria C1 to C6, the member verdicts and the verdict line do not change.

### What the reading can and cannot say (record it in the probe docstring and the SUMMARY)

The probe names the FIRST refusal per member only. `replay_option_positions` refuses before any mark is fetched, and the hole guard stops at the earliest missing day of the first holed instrument in grid order. So a hole reading does not rule out a D-09 row later in the ledger, and an exercise reading says nothing about marks.
