### Shared pattern — Scrollable Tab Strip (one pattern, three consumers)

A strip of tabs that does not fit scrolls **inside its own strip**. It never wraps into a second row of tabs, and it never widens the page.

- Strip element: `flex flex-nowrap min-w-0 max-w-full overflow-x-auto snap-x [scrollbar-width:none] [-webkit-overflow-scrolling:touch]`.
- Each tab: `snap-start shrink-0 whitespace-nowrap`, focus ring `focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-inset focus-visible:ring-accent` (an outset ring would be clipped by `overflow-x-auto`).
- No edge-fade overlay. The tab cut off at the right edge is the scroll affordance (NAV-02; DESIGN.md hairline-clean).
- The active tab is scrolled into view on mount and on change. `AllocationsTabs` does this already via `computeTabStripScroll`. `Tabs.tsx` calls `scrollIntoView({ inline: "nearest", block: "nearest" })` on the active trigger, and honours `prefers-reduced-motion`.

| Consumer | Contract |
|----------|----------|
| `AllocationsTabs` tablist (`role="tablist"`, `aria-label="Allocation surfaces"`) — item (a) | Keep the SAME element with the SAME direct `role="tab"` children (JOURNEY-03: re-nesting is a critical axe violation). Tablist classes become `flex flex-nowrap items-center gap-1 min-w-0 basis-full overflow-x-auto snap-x [scrollbar-width:none] [-webkit-overflow-scrolling:touch] sm:basis-auto sm:flex-wrap sm:overflow-x-visible`. Its wrapper (today `ml-auto flex items-center gap-1`) becomes `ml-auto flex min-w-0 max-w-full flex-wrap items-center justify-end gap-1 sm:flex-nowrap`. The separator becomes `hidden sm:inline-block`. Export, Tweaks (see N-TWEAKS) and `+ Allocation` get `shrink-0`. Result below `sm`: row 1 is the h1 block, row 2 the full-width scrolling tablist, row 3 the right-aligned actions |
| `TabsList` underline variant, `src/components/ui/Tabs.tsx` (consumed by `ProfileTabs`, `AdminTabs`) — `/profile` tab row | List: `flex gap-1 overflow-x-auto [scrollbar-width:none] shadow-[inset_0_-1px_0_var(--color-border)]`. The hairline is drawn INSIDE the scroll box as an inset shadow, replacing `border-b border-border`, because `overflow-x:auto` forces `overflow-y:auto` and clips the trigger's `-mb-px` overlap. Trigger: drop `-mb-px`, keep `border-b-2` (active = accent), add `shrink-0 whitespace-nowrap`, ring becomes inset. The `Tabs.test.tsx` pin on `"flex gap-1 border-b border-border"` is updated intentionally with a dated comment. The segmented variant (`WatchlistTabs`) is untouched |
