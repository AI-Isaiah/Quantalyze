**Why stale is red, not amber (research Section 4, DESIGN.md gates).** Amber means "recoverable,
the system or a one-click action fixes it". A key the tick no longer reads, or a disconnected key
(D-10), does not heal itself. Red is the existing stale token on this ladder
(`FRESHNESS_COLORS.stale`). The trade-fetch ladder has two states (current, stale) and so never
uses the `warm` amber dot.

**Contrast.** `#DC2626` on `#FFFFFF` 4.83:1 and on `#F8F9FA` 4.6:1, AA for 10px and 12px text.
`#64748B` on white 4.85:1 (DESIGN.md 2026-04-30). `#4A5568` on white 7.5:1.

**Gates honoured.** Tone renders only on a dated, finite fact. Never-fetched, not-applicable and
an unreadable timestamp carry no tone. The named gap is absence, so it is never red.

## Single source of truth (binding on the planner)

All of the following live in `src/lib/freshness.ts` and are imported, never re-typed (the
`quantalyze/no-raw-staleness-derivation` lint fails an ad hoc comparison):

- `TRADE_FETCH_STALE_DAYS = 2` (D-05).
- `TRADE_FETCH_VENUES = ["okx", "bybit", "binance"]`: the venues `/cron-sync` fetches trades for.
  It mirrors `fetch_daily_pnl`'s branches. `deribit` is in `EXCHANGE_CLASSES` but has no
  `fetch_daily_pnl` branch, so it is not-applicable. MT5, sFOX, CSV and composite are
  not-applicable.
- `resolveTradeFetch(tradesFetchedAt, venue, now)` returns one of `current`, `stale`, `never`,
  `not_applicable`, `future`, plus the parsed date. Rules, in order:
  1. `tradesFetchedAt` non-null and unparseable: `not_applicable` (no claim; `console.warn` once,
     the `deriveSegmentMarkers` malformed-shape idiom).
  2. `tradesFetchedAt` non-null and ahead of now by more than `CLOCK_SKEW_TOLERANCE_MINUTES`:
     `future`.
  3. `tradesFetchedAt` non-null and `now - fetchedAt > TRADE_FETCH_STALE_DAYS * 86_400_000` ms:
     `stale`. This is the literal D-05 reading, "more than 2 days old".
  4. `tradesFetchedAt` non-null otherwise: `current`.
  5. `tradesFetchedAt` null and `venue` (lower-cased) in `TRADE_FETCH_VENUES`: `never`.
  6. `tradesFetchedAt` null otherwise: `not_applicable`.

A non-null `tradesFetchedAt` is authoritative whatever the venue prop says, because only the cron
writes it. The venue only separates the two null cases.

**Props.** `SyncBadge` gains two REQUIRED props, compile-forcing every mount to decide, exactly as
`seriesEnd` was (HONEST-08):
- `tradesFetchedAt: string | null`, from `strategy_analytics.trades_fetched_at`.
- `tradeFetchVenue: string | null`: the strategy's venue as the mount knows it, or `null` when the
  surface cannot resolve one. `null` with a null `tradesFetchedAt` resolves to not-applicable, the
  cautious direction: no claim rather than a "not yet fetched" claim.

`tradeFetchVenue` is separate from the existing optional `exchange` prop on purpose. `exchange`
also renders the visible venue label, and making it required would add an "OKX" label to the
three allocator mounts that show none today.

**`computedAt` stops driving the badge (D-07).** It no longer sets the dot, the sentence or the
render gate. It survives only inside the tooltip, labelled "Analytics last computed …". The
`if (!computedAt) return null` gate is replaced by the render rules below.

## Badge states (`SyncBadge`)

The badge states ONE sentence, and that sentence names its subject (HONEST-08). Two facts are
judged: the trade fetch (`resolveTradeFetch`) and the series end (the existing
`bucketSeriesAge` 3d/7d ladder via `resolveEffectiveRecency`'s series half). Precedence:

1. Trade fetch `stale`: the trade sentence ALWAYS binds. Stale is the D-06 claim the reader must
   see, and it is the cause when the series is old too.
2. Trade fetch `future`: the trade sentence binds (muted), as a data defect.
3. Otherwise, the series sentence binds when its verdict is worse than the trade verdict. Ranks:
   `current` = fresh (0); `never` = unknown (1, the existing `TONE_RANK` slot); series `fresh` 0,
   `warm` 2, `stale` 3, `future` 4. Ties go to the trade sentence.
4. Trade fetch `not_applicable`: only the series arm exists. A resolvable series end renders the
   series sentence. An unresolvable one renders nothing (no claim, no placeholder).

| State | When | Dot | Visible text (exact) | Text colour |
|-------|------|-----|----------------------|-------------|
| **Current** | trade `current`, series not binding | `bg-positive` | `Trades fetched {age}` | `text-text-muted` |
| **Stale** | trade `stale` | `bg-negative` | `Trades fetched {age}` + ` · ` + `stale` | sentence `text-text-muted`; the word `stale` `text-negative` |
| **Never fetched** | trade `never`, series not binding | `bg-text-muted` | `Trades not yet fetched` | `text-text-muted` |
| **Future** | trade `future` | `bg-text-muted` | `Trades fetched` + ` · ` + `future — check data` | `text-text-muted` |
| **Not applicable** (MT5, Deribit, sFOX, CSV, composite, or venue unknown) | trade `not_applicable`, series end resolvable | series tone, unchanged (`FRESHNESS_COLORS[...]`, or `FUTURE_SERIES_DOT` on a future series) | `Track record ends {age}` | `text-text-muted` |
| **Not applicable, no series** | trade `not_applicable`, series end null | none | the badge renders `null` | n/a |
| **Series binds** (any trade state except stale/future) | series verdict outranks the trade verdict | series tone, unchanged | `Track record ends {age}` (existing copy, byte-unchanged) | `text-text-muted` |

`{age}` is the existing `timeAgo()` output, unchanged: `just now`, `{m}m ago`, `{h}h ago`,
`{d}d ago`, `in the future`. Example: `Trades fetched 5h ago`; `Trades fetched 9d ago · stale`.

**What happens to "Synced Nd ago".** The word "Synced" is retired from `SyncBadge`. It was a claim
about a job that every reader took as a claim about the strategy; "Trades fetched" names the fact
the badge now reads (D-07). The component keeps its file name and export name; renaming it is
churn outside this contract. The venue label (`OKX`, `Bybit`, `Binance`) still renders before the
sentence wherever a mount passes `exchange`, unchanged.

**Separator markup.** The ` · ` is `<span aria-hidden="true"> · </span>` followed by
`<span className="sr-only">, </span>`, so a screen reader hears "Trades fetched 9d ago, stale".

### Tooltip (`title` attribute on the badge's outer span)

Supplemental only: every claim is already in the visible text, so nothing depends on hover.

| State | `title` (exact) |
|-------|-----------------|
| Current | `Trades last fetched from the exchange {age}. Fetched daily; marked stale after 2 days without a fetch.{computedSuffix}` |
| Stale | `No trades fetched from the exchange in more than 2 days. Last fetch {age}.{computedSuffix}` |
| Never fetched | `No trade fetch is recorded for this strategy yet. Trades are fetched daily.{computedSuffix}` |
| Future | `The recorded trade fetch time is in the future, so no fetch age is shown.{computedSuffix}` |
| Not applicable | `Track record ends {seriesAge}.{computedSuffix}` |

`{computedSuffix}` = ` Analytics last computed {age}.` when `computedAt` is non-null, otherwise
empty. The figure `2` in the copy is interpolated from `TRADE_FETCH_STALE_DAYS`, never typed.

## Factsheet trade-fetch line (`TradeFetchLine`, new, public)

**Placement.** In `FactsheetHeader`, inside the existing wrapper `<div>` that holds
`<FreshnessChip>` and `<SeriesRecencyLine>`, as the next sibling directly after
`<SeriesRecencyLine>`. Right-aligned at `sm` and up, inheriting the wrapper's alignment. It is a
sentence of its own, like `SeriesRecencyLine`, never part of the chip. `FreshnessChip` itself is
NOT edited by this phase: its "Computed" eyebrow names `computed_at` correctly and was never a
"Synced" claim.

**Audience (D-06, D-10).** Rendered for every viewer who can see the factsheet: anon on a
published strategy, an allocator, the owner. No owner-only branch. Not hidden in print (no
`factsheet-v2-no-print`).

**Data.** The payload gains `tradesFetchedAt: string | null` and `tradeFetchVenue: string | null`
(the strategy's api-key exchange), resolved through `resolveTradeFetch`. A disconnected key whose
`trades_fetched_at` the runbook seeds (D-10) renders through the ordinary current/stale rules. It
will read stale within 2 days and stay stale, which is the intended statement.

| State | Render (exact) | Classes |
|-------|----------------|---------|
| Current | `Last trades fetched {n} ago · {date}` | `mt-1 text-caption text-text-muted` |
| Stale | `Stale: last trades fetched {n} ago · {date}` | `mt-1 text-caption text-negative` |
| Never fetched | `Trades not yet fetched` | `mt-1 text-caption text-text-muted` |
| Future | `Trade fetch time is in the future — check data` | `mt-1 text-caption text-text-muted` |
| Not applicable | renders `null` | n/a |

- `{n}` is whole elapsed days, `Math.floor((now - fetchedAt) / 86_400_000)`: `0` renders
  `less than a day`, `1` renders `1 day`, otherwise `{n} days`. So the current line reads
  `Last trades fetched less than a day ago · Oct 9, 2026`, and a stale one reads
  `Stale: last trades fetched 9 days ago · Sep 30, 2026`.
- `{date}` is `formatIsoDate(tradesFetchedAt)`, the factsheet's one date formatter (UTC,
  `Mon D, YYYY`), the same one `SeriesRecencyLine` uses.
- The verdict ("Stale") and the stale threshold are decided by `resolveTradeFetch` only. The
  day count is display, never a second threshold.
- `now` comes from a `React.useState(() => Date.now())` initialiser, the `FreshnessChip`
  precedent, so render stays pure.
- The `·` in this line is plain text inside one `<p>`. A screen reader reading it as a pause is
  acceptable here because the verdict word leads the sentence.

## Named unrecovered gap (D-02)

**What it states.** A span of days that neither the tick nor the venue can supply. It stays
missing: it is never zero-filled or interpolated, and metrics run over the days that exist. This
phase names it; Phase 167.1.2.2.2.1 OKXHISTORY may later fill it, and the line then disappears
because the span is gone.

**Placement.** Factsheet header, in the same wrapper, directly after `TradeFetchLine`. One `<p>`
per gap, newest gap first. Tearsheet: directly after its trade-fetch line. It belongs in the
document header because it is a statement about the whole record, and the header is what survives
printing. It is not a chart-only seam.

| Element | Copy (exact) | Classes |
|---------|--------------|---------|
| One gap | `No trade data {from} – {to} ({days} days). The exchange no longer serves these days; metrics use the days that exist.` | `mt-1 text-caption text-text-secondary` (tearsheet: `text-fixed-10`) |
| One-day gap | `No trade data {from} (1 day). The exchange no longer serves this day; metrics use the days that exist.` | same |
| More than 3 gaps | the 3 newest render as above, then `{k} more gaps without trade data.` | same |

- `{from}` and `{to}` are `formatIsoDate` of inclusive UTC dates. `{days}` is the inclusive day
  count, the same arithmetic as `inclusiveDayCount` in `src/lib/factsheet/build-payload.ts`, so
  this line and the chart seam's `{days}d — no data` can never disagree for one span. Example:
  `No trade data May 6, 2026 – Sep 22, 2026 (140 days). The exchange no longer serves these days; metrics use the days that exist.`
- The range separator is an en dash with a space either side.
- Muted-secondary, never red, never amber: a gap is absence (DESIGN.md: red is "never for
  absence"). It carries no dot and no glyph.

**Data contract (the UI renders server truth only).** The payload carries
`unrecoveredGaps: { from: string; to: string }[]` (YYYY-MM-DD, inclusive both ends), read from a
persisted per-strategy record that the planner picks. RESEARCH.md recommends range-level
provenance in `strategy_analytics.data_quality_flags`, the D-03 precedent. The runbook's
per-strategy pair supplies the values: pre-tick high-water + 1 day to post-tick earliest new
row - 1 day. Absent, empty or malformed: nothing renders. Malformed also logs a `console.warn`,
the `deriveSegmentMarkers` idiom. The client NEVER infers a gap from holes in `payload.dates`.
A holed date list is not proof that the venue no longer serves those days.

**Chart.** Out of scope. `TimeSeriesChart`'s gap seam stays composite-only (GUARD-02: a single-key
SVG stays byte-identical). The header line is the disclosure.

## Accessibility

- **Never colour alone.** Every tone has a word: stale carries the visible word `stale` (badge)
  or `Stale:` (line); current carries a dated sentence; never-fetched and future carry their own
  sentences. The dot is `aria-hidden="true"` (it already is on the chip; add it on the badge).
- **No interaction added.** The badge and lines are static text, not focusable. The `title` is
  supplemental, so a keyboard or touch user loses nothing.
- **No live region.** These values are SSR-rendered and do not change while the page is open.
- **Contrast.** See Color: every text pair is at least 4.5:1.
- **Screen-reader separators.** See the badge separator markup above.
- **Print.** Lines are not hidden in print; the stale red prints as text plus the word.
