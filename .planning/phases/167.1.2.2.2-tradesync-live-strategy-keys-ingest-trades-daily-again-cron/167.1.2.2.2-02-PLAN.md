**D-03 mechanism decision (Claude's discretion, recorded here).** The range-level provenance lives in a dedicated `series_provenance` column, not in `data_quality_flags`: `run_csv_strategy_analytics` rebuilds `data_quality_flags` wholesale, so a runbook write there is erased by the very re-derive it accompanies, and a code-side carry-over would spread to every writer of that field. The column's documented shape is an object with two optional arrays: `legacy_ranges` (each `{from, to, source, recorded_at}`, `source` = `returns_grid_carry_forward`) and `unrecovered_gaps` (each `{from, to, recorded_at}`), dates `YYYY-MM-DD`, inclusive. OKXHISTORY (167.1.2.2.2.1) may later add a `reconstructed_ranges` array under the same column. The database enforces object-ness only; the reader (plan 10) validates the arrays and renders nothing on a malformed value.
