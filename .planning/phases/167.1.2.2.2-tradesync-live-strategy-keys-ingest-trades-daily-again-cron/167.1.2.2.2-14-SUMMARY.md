## Deviations from Plan

**1. [Rule 3 - design] Five shared fragments added beyond the 27 listed forms**: `strat-universe`, `tick-scope`, `d08-scope`, `gap-scope`, `rederived-cut`. Detection, write, readback, oracle and rollback read one definition instead of seven copies. Renderer extensions: fragment lines on a write-tagged line inherit the tag, and the DRY scan also refuses `cron_sync_tick(`.

**2. [Rule 1 - premise re-measured] The tick population is 12 keys, not 4.** Connected keys backing an eligible strategy: okx 3, bybit 1 (the four that fetch) and 8 `mt5` keys that take the benign `deferred` path. `stepb-response`'s `expected_keys` is the route's own selection and reads 12 today; plans 17 and 21 must compare `total_keys` with it, not with 4. The `mt5` group adds one inter-batch delay (about 2 s) to the run; plan 06's budget test census omits it, immaterial against 180 s.

**3. [Rule 2] `d08-rollback` cutoff.** The plan says "only while no re-derived row shares the date". Carried and re-derived rows are indistinguishable in the table (same arbiter, `updated_at` not bumped by the derive's upsert), so the form takes the first re-derived day per strategy as `REDERIVED` (the `earliest_new_row_date` the worklist prints, or `none`) and never deletes on or after it. A legacy range reaching the cutoff stays recorded and is decided by hand.

**4. `clamped_keys` is empty when counters come from `cron_runs`**: the heartbeat metadata is a closed counter set without the clamp report. A pg_net timeout therefore cannot be judged for clamping from that source; the downstream read's `earliest_new_row_date` against Step A's floor is the cross-check.

**5. ADR-0008** gained one clause citing the runbook next to the mechanism 5 note (permitted by plan 13's summary; the file is not in this plan's `files_modified`).

**6. Commit-assertion branch name.** The branch `wt/167.1.2.2.2-p14` is outside the executor's `agent-*` allow-list; committed on it as assigned. It is not a protected branch.
