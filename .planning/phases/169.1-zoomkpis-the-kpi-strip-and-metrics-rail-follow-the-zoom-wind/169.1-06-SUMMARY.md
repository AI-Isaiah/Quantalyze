# Phase 169.1 Plan 06: A gapped composite's risk statistics and the rolling Sharpe run over the engine's days Summary

On a composite with interior calendar gaps, `metricsBasisSeries` now returns the zero-filled
calendar series, the one `gap_fill_daily_returns` hands the engine. So a selected range's
Ann. Vol, Sharpe and Sortino on the strip are the engine's figures. `deriveSeriesBundle` feeds
the same helper's series to the rolling Sharpe, which therefore follows the headline's day
basis. On a calendar composite it also feeds that series to the rolling volatility and the
rolling Sortino. Each result is mapped back onto `dates`. With no conventions, and on a
single-key series, every figure is unchanged.

## What was built

- **compute.ts.**
  - `metricsBasisSeries` has a new branch for the calendar basis with `calendarDense`. It
    walks the dates and pads each absent calendar day with 0.0. Each present day keeps its
    return, and `positions[i]` is the day offset of `dates[i]`. It throws when a date is
    unparseable, a duplicate or decreasing. The message reads
    `metricsBasisSeries(): a calendar-dense series needs strictly increasing dates (...)`.
  - The active and pass-through branches are unchanged. The day count is one private
    `calendarDayOffset`.
  - `compute()` is unchanged apart from the arithmetic CAGR. It now divides the basis sum by
    `basisN` in every branch. The old separate calendar-day count is gone, because on a
    dense series `basisN` IS that count. The frozen `compute.conventions` block stayed green
    unedited.
  - The comments on the helper, on `ComputeConventions.calendarDense` and in `compute()` now
    cite D-32 and mirror `gap_fill_daily_returns` and `stat_returns`. 14b's note that "the
    calendar-density branch is plan 169.1-06's" is gone.
- **build-payload.ts (`deriveSeriesBundle`).**
  - It makes one `metricsBasisSeries(stratRet, dates, { dayBasis, calendarDense })` call,
    and `onBasis` maps a rolled array back through `positions`.
  - The rolling Sharpe always runs on `basis.returns`.
  - The rolling volatility and Sortino stay on `stratRet` under `dayBasis === "active"`.
    In every other case they run on `basis.returns`, mapped back the same way.
  - The window is still `pickRollingWindow(stratRet.length)`.
  - Plan 169.1-07 can reuse the same `basis` constant for the bootstrap.
  - `rolling.ts` is not edited.
