<objective>
Finish D-31 for single-key strategies, trimmed to what HEAD still lacks (D-83). Phase 169's review
round 1 (SFH H-2 / M-2) already made a single-key `simple` strategy draw its arithmetic curve, by
reading the method from the live `returns_denominator_config` inside `readSingleKeyBasisOpts`, and
already withholds the leverage what-if for an arithmetic or active-day row
(`dataQuality.returnsConventionOverride`). Two things are still wrong: the live config wins over the
frozen `cash_settlement` conventions echo the stored headline was actually computed under (the drift
D-30 ordered the tiers to prevent: the config can be edited after a run), and a single-key payload
carries no `dayBasis`, so the window arm (169.1-02, 169.1-03) computes an active-day strategy's
window on the calendar basis.

Purpose: one resolver decides the conventions for every strategy, composite or single-key, from
where they are stored (D-30), so a page never shows a curve and a headline computed two ways.
Output: `readSingleKeyBasisOpts` routed through `resolveMetricsConventions`, the build-only echo read
in the resolve stage, and tests.
</objective>
