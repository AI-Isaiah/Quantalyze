### MD-03 (= SFH MEDIUM-1): an active-basis window with fewer than two non-zero days showed Ann. Vol "0.0%"

**Files modified:**
- `src/lib/factsheet/compute.ts`
- `src/lib/factsheet/compute.conventions.test.ts`
- `src/app/factsheet/[id]/v2/basis-context.window.test.tsx`

**Commit:** `08ea9396f`
**Status:** fixed (requires human verification: this is a numeric-logic change)

**Applied fix:** `annVol = basisN >= 2 ? basisSd * √P : NaN`. This is the engine's rule: `stat_returns.std()` of 0 or 1 points is NaN, and `_safe_float` stores it as None.

**Readers checked** (a full sweep of `ann_vol` and `volMatchedLabel` outside tests, run after the commit):
- Calendar basis: `basisN = n >= 2` on every rendered path. The build gate `MIN_FACTSHEET_SERIES_POINTS = 2`, `computeStyleDrift` returns early below 4 so each half has 2 or more, and the Scenario adapter gates fewer than 2 points before `compute()`.
- The comparator vol-match: `vmScale = stratAnnVol / bench.ann_vol` turns NaN in that same case. The vol-matched comparator series then becomes NaN, and `TimeSeriesChart` skips non-finite points (`:134`, `:166`). The line is therefore absent rather than the fabricated flat 0-vol curve it was before. Within a window the comparator series are not drawn: charts read the base view.
- Every displayed `ann_vol` (`FactsheetView.tsx:1499`, `MetricsColumn.tsx:176`, `BatchDPanels.tsx:33/351`) goes through `pct` / `pct1`, which render NaN as "—".
- **One unguarded reader found: `comparator-block.ts:151`** builds `volMatchedLabel: \`${short} × ${vmScale.toFixed(2)}\``. MD-03 makes it read `"SPX × NaN"` (measured with a 40-day active bundle that has one non-zero day). It happens only in a bundle built without `comparatorAnnVol` (the MTM or smoothed bundle) whose series has fewer than two non-zero days. The label reaches the screen only through the Volatility Matched chart legend under that basis. Before the fix the same case read "× 0.00" over a fabricated flat curve. The fix belongs in `comparator-block.ts`, outside topic B: decision 2 below.

**Evidence:**
- New cases: `compute.conventions.test.ts` covers 0 and 1 active day under both methods, plus a 2-day floor case. `basis-context.window.test.tsx` covers `windowView` over a 5-day idle range with 0 and with 1 non-zero day.
- With the guard neutered, the 4 new cases fail and the 31 others pass. The file was restored with `cp`, and `cmp` confirmed it identical.
- The snapshot did not move.

### MD-02: an active-day-basis composite rolled vol and Sortino over the present days

**Files modified:**
- `src/lib/factsheet/build-payload.ts`
- `src/lib/factsheet/build-payload.rolling-basis.test.ts`

**Commit:** `477092a20`
**Status:** fixed (requires human verification: this is a numeric-logic change)

**The Python, checked first:**
- `compute_all_metrics` builds `sibling_kinds` with `rolling_volatility_*` and `rolling_sortino_*` from `returns` (`metrics.py` ~2645-2659).
- The day basis moves only `stat_returns` (`:1692`) and `_rolling_basis`, which feeds the rolling Sharpe only (`:2033`).
- For a composite, `derive_basis_series` passes `gap_fill_daily_returns(sparse)` into `compute_all_metrics` (`basis_series.py:246`). So `returns` is zero-filled under every day basis.

**The D-39 premise needed correcting.** "Under active they stay on `stratRet`, as the engine's do" held for a single-key strategy only. The CONTEXT entry was left unedited; this report records the correction.

**Applied fix:**
- Vol and Sortino now roll on `dense = metricsBasisSeries(stratRet, dates, { calendarDense })`, which ignores the day basis, and are mapped back through `dense.positions`.
- `dense` is `stratRet` with identity positions when the series is not calendar-dense. Single-key payloads and every calendar-basis payload are therefore byte-identical.
- The in-code comment was rewritten to match.

**Evidence:**
- New case: an active-basis gapped composite. At the last date, rolling vol and Sortino equal the rolling functions over a hand-built zero-fill twin and differ from their present-days values. The rolling Sharpe stays on the active days.
- With the old active arm restored, the new case fails: `rollingVol: 0.16360705716946394 vs 0.1526629925891488`. Restored with `cp` and confirmed with `cmp`.
- The snapshot did not move, because no snapshot fixture is both active and composite.

### MD-01: every brush pointer-move re-ran the whole series bundle

**Files modified:**
- `src/app/factsheet/[id]/v2/basis-context.tsx`
- `src/app/factsheet/[id]/v2/MasterBrush.tsx`
- `src/app/factsheet/[id]/v2/basis-context.window-gesture.test.tsx` (new test file)

**Commit:** `aa699e2ab`
**Status:** fixed for the MasterBrush. TimeSeriesChart is still open (see the residual below).

**Measured before choosing.** One `deriveSeriesBundle` with `benchmarkPrices` unavailable, mean of 5 runs, Node, on this branch:

| obs | bundle | bootstrapCI alone | compute() alone |
|---|---|---|---|
| 365 | 37.2 ms | 32.3 ms | 0.5 ms |
| 1500 | 148.1 ms | 136.8 ms | 2.2 ms |
| 3000 | 292.4 ms | 256.8 ms | 4.8 ms |

**Decision: option (c), commit on release.**
- **(a) rejected.** A narrower derive would save only ~12%, because the bootstrap the window displays is ~88% of the cost. It would also be the second derive function D-27 rules out (169.1-02 SUMMARY, Known limits: "A narrower derive would be a new function, which D-27 rules out").
- **(b) rejected.** The bootstrap would settle after the strip, so mid-drag the bootstrap point would disagree with the strip Sharpe. 169.1-07 made those two equal.
- **(c) chosen.** It keeps every displayed figure equal to today's settled values, with no new derive path.

**Applied fix:**
- `BasisProvider` carries a range-gesture hold. `useRangeGesture()` exposes `begin(startRange)` and `end()`. Their identity is stable, so an unmount cleanup keyed on `end` cannot fire on `begin`. Outside a provider both are no-ops.
- `MasterBrush` calls `begin(xRange)` on pointer down, after the `button !== 0` return and before any `setXRange`. It calls `end()` on pointer up, cancel and lost capture (all of which route to `onPointerUp`), and in an unmount cleanup.
- While a hold is active, `useWindowedView` reads `held ?? deferredLiveRange` for both `view` and `scope`. The "Selected range" eyebrow therefore always names the range its figures describe (the WR-01 rule). `AnalyticalPanels`' and `MetricsColumn`'s `scope.kind` reads inherit the held scope.
- The charts and the brush keep following the live `xRange`.

**Evidence** (the new `basis-context.window-gesture.test.tsx`, timing-free, counting `bootstrapCI` through a pass-through `vi.mock`):
- A 6-move pan runs **0** derives during the drag and **1** on release. Strip and rail share it, and `rail.view` is the same object as `strip.view`.
- The settled `strategyMetrics`, `bootstrapCI` and `strategyWorst10` equal `windowView(payload, s, e)` for the final range.
- Pointer cancel releases the hold, and so does unmounting the brush mid-drag.
- A range set outside a gesture still derives at once (1 derive).
- With the hold neutered: **6** derives during the drag (one per move), and 3 of the 4 cases fail. With the unmount cleanup neutered, the unmount case fails. Both files were restored with `cp` and confirmed with `cmp`.
- Per drag at 3000 obs: before, 6 × ~292 ms; after, 1 × ~292 ms on release. A click-outside re-anchor also derives once, on pointer up.
