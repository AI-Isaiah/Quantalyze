**Facts measured at plan time (2026-09-26; re-read at HEAD).**
- `bootstrapCI(rets, n_resamples, block_len, seed, periodsPerYear)` resamples `rets` in blocks and
  scores each resample, and the point estimate, with the private `headlineStats`: one Sharpe and
  one Sortino expression (the Phase 166.1 matcher counts them; Sortino is a count-pinned allowlist
  entry of exactly one in this file) and a Max DD from `cumEq` then `drawdowns`. `n` is `rets`'
  length; `AnalyticalPanels.tsx` reads it as `lowN` and in the sentence "Bootstrap resamples are
  drawn from {n} observations."
- `computeStressWindows` compounds `stratRet` and `benchRet` over each named window's indices, with a
  peak seeded at 1 at the window start, and drops a window whose observed-day coverage is below
  `MIN_COVERAGE_RATIO`.
- After 14e, `deriveSeriesBundle` holds one `metricsBasisSeries(stratRet, dates, conventions)`
  result and the bundle's `cumulativeMethod` / `isArithmetic`; it calls
  `bootstrapCI(stratRet, 2000, 5, 42, periodsPerYear)` and
  `computeStressWindows(dates, stratRet, btcRet, "BTC", markets)`. Plan 169.1-02's window arm takes
  `bootstrapCI` from the slice's bundle (its follow-list) and keeps the base view's
  `stressWindows` (named historical windows, full history).
- `arithmeticUnderwater` and the geometric `drawdowns` are both invariant to an inserted or removed
  0.0 return (the sum, the product and the running peak do not move), so the basis series moves no
  Max DD. The `build-payload` arithmetic fixture's span (2025-03 to 2025-05) contains the
  "Apr 2025 tariffs" window, so that fixture's stress windows move by construction.
