# Phase 169.1 Plan 05: An arithmetic series' calendar windows, buckets, heatmap and Calmar by Year are sums Summary

On a series computed under arithmetic compounding, `compute()` now sums the calendar windows
(MTD, YTD, 3M, 6M, 1Y, 3Y, 5Y) and the weekly, monthly, quarterly and yearly buckets. The
engine's `_bucket_return` and its summed monthly grid do the same. `monthlyReturnsMatrix` and
`calmarByYear` take the same method, and `deriveSeriesBundle` passes it to both. On one
arithmetic page, the months of a year now add up to that year's heatmap YTD, its Calmar by Year
return and the `yearly` figure. Without the method, every function returns exactly what it
returned before.
