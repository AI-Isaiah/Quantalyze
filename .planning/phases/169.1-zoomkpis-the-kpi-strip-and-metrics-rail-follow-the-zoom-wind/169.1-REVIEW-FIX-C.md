### SFH MEDIUM-2: on a chain-broken row, a selected range compounds across the TWR break and the coverage caveat is hidden

**Files modified:** `src/app/factsheet/[id]/v2/MetricsColumn.tsx`, `src/app/factsheet/[id]/v2/FactsheetView.tsx`, `src/app/factsheet/[id]/v2/FactsheetView.window-kpis.test.tsx`

**Status:** fixed: requires human verification

**Applied fix:**
- New `windowCoverageCaveat(dataQuality, basis, rangeStart, subject)` in `MetricsColumn.tsx`, next to `headlineCoverageCaveat`.
  - It uses the same gates: cash basis, `twrChainBroken === true`, and `headlineCoversFrom` present.
  - When `headlineCoversFrom` is a date and the range starts before it: "This range starts before {date}, where the record resumes after its last break in the return chain. Its {figures} compound returns from before that date, which the full-history figures leave out."
  - When the range starts on or after that date: no caveat.
  - When `headlineCoversFrom` is `null`, or the date cannot be formatted, every range gets the caveat with no date: "The record has a break in the return chain at a date this view cannot name, so this range may span it. Its {figures} compound across any break inside it."
- While a range is selected, the KPI strip (`FactsheetView.tsx`) and the rail's Main Metrics (`MetricsColumn.tsx`) show this caveat in place of the stored-headline one. At full history nothing changed.
- Each surface names its figures by its own labels (IN-R2-02), the same three as the full-history caveat:
  - Strip: "Cum. Return, CAGR and Calmar".
  - Rail Main Metrics: "Cumulative Return, CAGR and Calmar".
- **Max DD is not named, on purpose (follow-up `ea519364e`).** The reviewer listed it, and `64f3818d4` named it on the strip. But `compute_all_metrics` (analytics-service/services/metrics.py, the geometric `max_dd`) builds max drawdown from the WHOLE record's wealth curve (`returns.fillna(0)`), not the post-break suffix. So the full-history Max DD already spans the break, and "which the full-history figures leave out" was false for it. The engine confines only the compounded return, CAGR and Calmar (CAGR over that max DD) to the suffix.
- The in-file comments that cited the leverage arm as precedent for dropping the caveat are corrected.

**The boundary was checked, not assumed.**
- `types.ts` documents `headlineCoversFrom` as "the first day (ISO date) of the span that headline covers".
- `nav_twr._last_interior_break_suffix` returns the suffix starting at that first valid day.
- `windowView` compounds the return dated `view.dates[startIdx]`.
- So a range starting exactly on `headlineCoversFrom` compounds only days the engine also compounds, and it gets no caveat. Both sides are compared as `.slice(0, 10)` ISO strings.
- The sentence is worded so it is true whether or not the range also reaches past the date. A range lying wholly before the break still "compounds returns from before that date".

## Decision: caveat, not withholding (with reason)

The reviewer offered two options: keep a caveat that names the break, or withhold the compounded fields. I chose the caveat.
- **The factsheet already handles a chain-broken row this way at full history.** It shows the figure and states which span it covers (Phase 169 SFH H-1, `headlineCoverageCaveat`). A window caveat continues that pattern on the same surface.
- **The D-46 withholding does not apply here.** 169.4.1 D-46 (founder) withholds CAGR as "—" only on the OG card, because "the card has no room for a caveat". The factsheet has room, and D-46 itself records that the factsheet shows the figure with a "covers from" note.
- **DESIGN.md, checked:** `## Voice / microcopy` asks for declarative sentence-case captions, active voice, and "state limitations ... don't hide a gap — name it". The anti-slop table permits `⚠` only as a semantic glyph coloured by a semantic token and adjacent to text, which is how both surfaces already render the caveat. The null-date sentence was switched from passive ("are compounded") to active ("compound") in `ea519364e`. The "null renders as em-dash, never a fabricated value" rule bears on withholding, and does not forbid a real figure that comes with a stated limit.

## Scoping decisions (kept narrow on purpose; flagged for review)

1. **Cash basis only, and only when `headlineCoversFrom` is present.** These are the same gates as the full-history caveat.
   - On a chain-broken row with no stored overlay (`headlineCoversFrom` absent), the full-history figure is computed in TypeScript over the whole series and carries no caveat today.
   - The same is true under mark_to_market and smoothed.
   - A window caveat in those cases would be stricter than the full-history view it zooms into, so I did not widen it.
   - Whether those full-history cases should carry a break caveat too is a separate, pre-existing question. It is not part of MEDIUM-2.
2. **Only the last break is placed.** The payload carries only the first day after the last break. A range starting after it is clean. A range before it may also span earlier breaks, and the sentence makes no claim about those.
