<objective>
Close the second pre-existing number-truth defect the last revision found (orchestrator decision 4
(b), CONTEXT D-31): on an arithmetic composite the rail's calendar windows (MTD, YTD, 3M, and on)
come from `compute()` and are compounded, while the engine sums them. The same hard-coded product
sits in `compute()`'s weekly / monthly / quarterly / yearly buckets, in `monthlyReturnsMatrix` (the
Monthly Returns heatmap and its YTD column) and in `calmarByYear`, so on one page the monthly cells
of an arithmetic composite do not add up to its own year or its own headline. The whole class is
closed here (Fix-campaign discipline), not only the rows the finding named.

Purpose: every bucket a page shows on an arithmetic series is computed the way the engine computes
it, by the same shared functions, so the cells, the rows and the headline agree.
Output: the three functions following the method, the call sites passing it, and tests.
</objective>
