# Phase 169.1 Plan 03: The zoom window follows the strategy's compounding method and day basis Summary

`compute()` now takes one optional conventions argument that mirrors `compute_all_metrics`.
Under ARITHMETIC compounding, the equity curve and underwater curve are running sums, so the
cumulative return, Max DD, Calmar and the drawdown-derived figures follow them. The CAGR is
the mean daily return times periodsPerYear. Under the ACTIVE day basis, vol, Sharpe and
Sortino run over the non-zero days. The composite read path resolves both conventions from
where they are stored: the persisted flag, then the `cash_settlement` conventions echo, then
the config. The payload carries them only when they are not the defaults, and `rederiveArgs`
passes them to both browser re-derive arms. On an arithmetic composite, a selected range's
Cum. Return now equals the rise the arithmetic equity chart draws across that window.

## What was built

- **compute.ts.**
  - New exported `ComputeConventions` type, and an optional fifth parameter that defaults to
    `{}`.
  - Under arithmetic: `eq = arithmeticEquity(rets)` and `dd = arithmeticUnderwater(rets)`.
    Every figure computed from them keeps its existing form.
  - New exported `metricsBasisSeries`. Under the active basis it returns the non-zero finite
    returns, with null positions on the excluded days. Otherwise it returns `rets` itself
    with identity positions.
  - The basis statistics get their own mean and sd from a second `dispersion(basisReturns, 0)`
    call. That series also feeds the existing `sharpeRatio(...)` call, and the existing
    `ddDev` / Sortino line now takes the basis series, its count and its mean. No second
    ratio or Sortino expression was written.
  - Arithmetic CAGR is `(sum / count) * periodsPerYear`. The count depends on the case:
    - active basis: the basis count;
    - `calendarDense`: the calendar days from the first date to the last, inclusive;
    - otherwise: the observation count.

    An empty active basis gives NaN.
  - Geometric CAGR, skew, kurt, VaR, CVaR, win rate, profit factor, the windows and the
    buckets are unchanged.
- **types.ts.** `FactsheetCommon.cumulativeMethod?` and `dayBasis?`, each with a doc comment.
- **build-payload.ts.**
  - `BuildFactsheetOpts.dayBasis` is new.
  - `deriveSeriesBundle` accepts `dayBasis` and `calendarDense`, passes the conventions to
    `compute()`, and reads `strategyEquity` / `strategyDrawdowns` from `compute()`'s own
    `eq` / `dd`. The two `isArithmetic ?` ternaries are gone.
  - `buildFactsheetPayload` passes the same conventions to the top-level `compute()` and to
    all three bundles. `calendarDense` is `opts.dataQuality.composite === true`.
  - It emits `cumulativeMethod` only for `"arithmetic"` and `dayBasis` only for `"active"`,
    by conditional spread.
- **basis-context.tsx.** `rederiveArgs` now passes three fields:
  - `isArithmetic: view.cumulativeMethod === "arithmetic"`;
  - `dayBasis: view.dayBasis`;
  - `calendarDense: view.dataQuality?.composite === true`.

  The "Geometric is right for the leverage arm" comment is replaced by the D-27 / H-2 / D-83
  sentence. Nothing else in either arm changed.
- **composite-read-path.ts.**
  - Exported `readCashConventions` selects `conventions:payload->conventions` from the
    `cash_settlement` row. It reuses `CASH_SETTLEMENT_SERIES_KIND`. An error result and a
    thrown chain both log `console.error` and return null.
  - Exported `resolveMetricsConventions` holds the HARD-03 block (moved, same behaviour),
    with the conventions tier inserted between the persisted flag and the config. It also
    resolves the day basis from the echo, then the config's `metrics_basis`, then calendar.
    Strict literals throughout; a present unexpected value gets a warn and falls through.
  - `readCompositeFactsheet` reads the echo inside the existing `Promise.all` and returns
    `dayBasis` in `buildOpts`.
  - `activeDayMetricsBasis` and `readSingleKeyBasisOpts` are untouched; they are 169.1-04's.

**Probe cost, stated (D-83 (a) scope):** every composite resolve now makes ONE more
