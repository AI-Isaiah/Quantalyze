<objective>
Carry D-02 to "what allocators see" beyond the factsheet: the discovery and owner lists, the detail shapers that share `shapeRowAnalytics`, the strategy page's benchmark joint, and the /compare overlay's span, by the same evidence rule and with no new UI marker.

Purpose: D-02's option text says allocators keep seeing the last computed numbers during a re-run. Today `shapeRowAnalytics` blanks a computing row's figures and `StrategyTable` drops its `#n`. RESEARCH Open Question 1 recommends lists in this phase; `shapeRowAnalytics`'s own comment says the premise behind blanking computing rows is under review, and Phase 166.5 removed it. UI-SPEC rules that list rows carry no updating line.

/compare (plan-check round 2, 2026-11-03). /compare has no status gate: it draws every published row's stored figures and curve, so during a re-run they are the last success's. Today it pairs them with the live `twr_chain_broken` and `cumulative_method` aliases, which the derive hop's prestamp rewrites, and for a chain-broken row with a covered-from date read from the stored `cash_settlement` series row, which the derive hop's `_persist_cash_series` rewrites even before the prestamp. So between that persist and the success write the overlay can draw the last success's curve from the new derive's last-break date, under the old headline. Task 3 moves the three onto one flags generation, plan 03's effective flags, and takes the date from that generation's `headline_since`. That is the field the single-key and composite factsheet already date the same headline by (since Phase 164.6.6.3.3), and Python writes it in the same run as the headline it dates. The rule is status-independent on purpose. Every chain-broken row is `complete_with_warnings` (`twr_chain_broken` is a guard key). Plans 03 and 08 record that the status bridge holds such a row at that status through the derive hop, until the compute hop's `_mark_computing`. A rule keyed on pending or computing would therefore miss the window this defect is about.

D-04 (founder 2026-11-03, locked). Plan 03 makes the factsheet fall back to the queued state for a terminal-success row whose derive prestamp left no bound snapshot, detected by `hasUnboundDerivePrestamp`. Task 2 makes the joint follow the same rule. It reads the flags blob and computes figures from those flags, so the same mix would otherwise reach the strategy page. The composite joint (`src/app/strategy/[id]/v2/composite-joint.ts`) and the discovery detail page build through plan 03's resolve stage, so they follow it by construction. The lists and /compare do not follow it, by decision. They read scalar aliases only, and a scalar alias cannot see the null-valued key that marks such a prestamp. Keying on the missing marker alias alone would also blank every legacy row at rest, and /compare has no placeholder state to fall back to. The shaper's recompute arm cannot meet that state, because a pending or computing row in it fails plan 03's check (c).

Output: one shaper arm, four ranked-projection aliases, the optional fields, one table predicate, the joint gate with its D-04 withhold, the compare projection aliases and its one-generation covered-from date, and their pins.
