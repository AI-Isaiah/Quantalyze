Honour D-02's literal wording on the one production surface where the strategy gate's pending and computing arms decide something during a re-run: admin approval. A strategy with a stored success result passes the gate while it re-computes; without one, or when the run failed, the gate refuses exactly as today.

Purpose: RESEARCH found that `checkStrategyGate` has two production callers, the admin approval route and the wizard's SyncPreviewStep, and that the factsheet does not call it. D-02's answer was "a strategy with stored analytics passes the gate during a re-run". Output: a required gate input, the recompute-aware arms, the admin route's evidence on both reads, and tests including a can-fail proof.

Founder decisions and remaining defaults:
- D-05 (founder 2026-11-03, locked), which answers RESEARCH Open Question 1: the approval gate is in the reach. The gate change covers admin approval and the SyncPreviewStep caller per RESEARCH.
- D-04 (founder 2026-11-03, locked) is a display rule, and this plan does not apply it to the gate. Take a terminal-success row whose derive prestamp left no bound snapshot. The gate shows no figures, and its analytics arms pass such a row on its terminal status exactly as today. What an approval publishes is the factsheet, which plan 03 already withholds in that state until the success write. A pending or computing row in that state fails plan 03's check (c), so the gate refuses it as today. Plan 08 records this.
- The PUB-01 defence in the admin route (a composite needs its latest stitch_composite job to be done) is NOT relaxed: it guards against a laundered composite status by reading terminal queue state, and relaxing it is a separate decision. A composite approval during a re-stitch therefore still 409s; plan 08 names it in the VERIFICATION hand-off as not yet holding.
