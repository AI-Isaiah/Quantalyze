<objective>
Make the wizard's finalize step queue an analytics re-run only when one is needed (SC-2), on the legacy arm that every contribution and every composite takes. A test goes RED on today's unconditional enqueue first.

Purpose: Defect 1. RESEARCH measured 4 of 4 finalize-sourced sync_trades jobs in the retention window landing after the strategy's first computation had already finished, all on private contributions. `enqueue_compute_job` dedupes only onto an in-flight job of the SAME kind, so once the wizard's SyncPreviewStep chain is done, a new sync_trades row re-runs the whole chain and bounces a complete row to computing. The re-run rule is Claude's Discretion in CONTEXT ("must not drop a run that is needed"); this plan implements RESEARCH's recommended rule plus one refinement: a dead in-flight job is not evidence the run will finish.

Output: a pure-ish decision helper with a truth-table test, the route wiring on both legacy arms, and route tests including the SC-2 RED test and a can-fail proof.
</objective>
