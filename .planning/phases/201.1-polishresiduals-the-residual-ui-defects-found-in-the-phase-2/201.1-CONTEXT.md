## Implementation Decisions

### Locked: the seven success criteria (founder decision 2026-11-12, "One polish phase now")

Quoted from ROADMAP `### Phase 201.1`, one per residual:

- **D-01 (SC-1) WR-13:** in the one-line key-row layout, a failed key row with a long `sync_error` (about 150 characters) never squeezes the label below a readable width. Measured: the allocator profile at 1440px, with a 60-character label, gave a label column 20.2px wide showing "U…" and metadata at 7 lines; at 1920px the column is 120px. Reviewer hint: `@4xl:basis-[12.5rem]`, plus a failed-state row in the 1280px floor test, extended to 1440 and 1920.
- **D-02 (SC-2) No ISO dates in UI:** the Scenario composer CoverageTimeline endpoints (`CoverageTimeline.tsx`, which prints `2025-09-17` / `2026-11-10`) and the compare chart x-axis ticks (SVG tspans) use the shared date helper. The copy census rule C catches SVG tick text, or the gap is stated.
- **D-03 (SC-3) One range separator:** the composer "Coverage window" uses the spaced en dash, not "→".
- **D-04 (SC-4) No year orphans:** a date never breaks before its year ("Nov 11, / 2026"). The helper output keeps the date together with no-break spaces, and a test proves it.
- **D-05 (SC-5) Full name in the matrix:** the correlation matrix shows "My allocation" whole, not "My allocati…". The cap is at `build-payload.ts:530`. A previous attempt at cap 13 clipped V390 by 4.81px, and widening the mobile `LABEL_W` 128→136 changes the 320 golden `correlations-matrix-portrait-320-chromium-linux.png`, which needs a re-bake via the dispatch-only `bake_svg_goldens` workflow. The two NARROWCHARTS fixtures that currently pin "My allocati…" are reverted to "My allocation".
- **D-06 (SC-6) Sticky bar wrap:** at 390px the Scenario composer sticky bar does not wrap "No material change yet." one word per line.
- **D-07 (SC-7) Composer opening range:** the composer opening on `SELECTED RANGE: <d> – <same d>` (1 day of overlap; KPIs show dashes until "Reset to full history" is pressed) is debugged to its root cause, and fixed if real or recorded as a seed/headless artifact with evidence. It reproduces before 201, at `4a65c8843`.

The file and line anchors above (`build-payload.ts:530`, `CoverageTimeline.tsx`, the golden name) were measured on 2026-11-12. Re-resolve them at HEAD before a plan cites them.
