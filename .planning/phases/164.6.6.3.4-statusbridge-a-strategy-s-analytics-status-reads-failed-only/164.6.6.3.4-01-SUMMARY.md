## VERIFY-CARRY CMD finding (orchestrator question: why "added 1" for two inserted lines)

Cause: the printed `added` is `n.length - j` (new-block lines minus lines matched), so on a passing run it equals the NET line delta (inserted minus deleted), never the count of insertions. A prototype that inserted two lines while another line was removed or replaced reads `added 1`. It is not a greedy subsequence match being fooled by a duplicated line.

Proof it cannot be fooled by duplicates: I ran a sweep over every carried line of the 736-line verify block against the final migration, at the same acceptance logic as the CMD:
- delete any single carried line (733 candidates, including every repeated `END IF;` and `RETURN;`): 0 undetected;
- delete a carried line and re-add the identical line at the tail before the NOTICE: 1 undetected, a blank line (cosmetic, and the same text);
- delete a carried line and re-add it at the head of the DECLARE: 1 undetected, the first declared variable (the result is the identical file, a no-op);
- modify any non-blank carried line in place: 0 undetected;
- swap any two adjacent distinct lines: 0 undetected.
Reproduced on the real CMD: two inserted lines in the tail, one a duplicate `END IF;`, printed `added 3 ... -> true`; two DECLARE lines printed `added 2 ... -> true`; deleting the first carried `END IF;` and adding a tail `END IF;` printed `63/736 ... misplaced 19 -> false`; deleting the LAST carried `END IF;` and adding a tail one printed `733/736 ... misplaced 3 -> false`. No matcher change was needed.

Committed-tree result: `verify carried: 736/736 added 63 misplaced 0 revoke 1 -> true`.
