UI-SPEC's Item 6d says to wrap α in its own `<span className="normal-case">`. Research measured
that the span breaks six existing tests (`FactsheetBody.joint-floor.test.tsx` x3,
`FactsheetBody.basis.test.tsx` x3), because `getByText("α vs SPX")` / `getAllByText(/α vs/)` match
an element's own text nodes. This plan uses `first-letter:normal-case` on the label `<p>`, gated
to labels that start with α. It renders "α VS BTC" in Chromium with zero DOM change, and it
satisfies D-09's "normal-case on the glyph" and the UI-SPEC's "DOM text content stays `α vs BTC`".
Gating matters: applied to every label, "Sharpe" would render "sHARPE". Cross-browser support of
`text-transform` on `::first-letter` is assumed (RESEARCH A2); the 320 px look is human_needed.

Output: one className edit and an extended kpistrip test; three extended page test doubles.
