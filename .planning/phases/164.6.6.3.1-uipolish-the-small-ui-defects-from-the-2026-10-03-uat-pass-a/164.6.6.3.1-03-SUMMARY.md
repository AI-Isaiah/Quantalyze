# Phase 164.6.6.3.1 Plan 03: One dashboard disclaimer, a dated batch chip, a bottom nav that wraps whole Summary

/recommendations and /portfolios/[id] each print the legal footer once instead of twice (a census keeps every dashboard route there), the recommendations batch chip now states which batch it is ("Batch: Fresh · Oct 6" style, never "Invalid Date"), and the bottom-nav label wraps whole on up to two centred lines in both cell arms.

## What changed

`src/components/strategy/FreshnessBadge.tsx`
- New `showDate?: boolean` (default false; JSDoc: opt-in, pill arm only). A local `toTimestamp` reads `computedAt` the way `computeFreshness` does (Date to `getTime()`, number as is, null or undefined to NaN, string through `Date.parse`).
- `date = showDate && Number.isFinite(ts) ? formatAbsoluteDate(new Date(ts).toISOString()) : null`. The finite check precedes the formatter because `formatAbsoluteDate` prints the literal "Invalid Date" for a bad input.
- Pill arm only, after the freshness word: `<span aria-hidden="true">·</span><span className="font-metric">{date}</span>`. The pill's own `gap-1.5` spaces them; no literal spaces; the date takes the pill's text colour. The dot arm ignores `showDate`.

`src/app/(dashboard)/recommendations/page.tsx`: `showDate` on the batch chip only (`grep -c showDate` prints 1); the page's footer `Disclaimer` element and its import are gone, no comment names the removed tag (`grep -v '^\s*//' | grep -c "<Disclaimer"` prints 0).

`src/app/(dashboard)/portfolios/[id]/page.tsx`: the bare `<Disclaimer />` and its import are gone; nothing else touched, its `FreshnessBadge` is byte-identical. Same grep prints 0.

`src/components/layout/MobileNav.tsx`: `min-w-0` after `flex` in both cell class strings (`grep -c "flex min-w-0 flex-1"` prints 2); the label is `<span className="text-center leading-tight">{item.label}</span>`. `leading-tight` is required: two 10 px lines at 1.25 stay inside the 64 px `pb-16` reservation in DashboardChrome. No `truncate`, `line-clamp` or `overflow-hidden`. The 44 px floor, focus outline, active accent and badge are unchanged.

Tests
- `FreshnessBadge.test.tsx` (new, 7 tests): default render carries no date, checked against hand-typed absences (independent oracle); `showDate={false}` equals the default by `innerHTML`; separator and `.font-metric` "Sep 25"; unparseable timestamp; null; Date and epoch number; dot variant ignores `showDate`.
- `page.mandate-gate.test.tsx` (+2): zero `disclaimer` test ids on /recommendations; the "Batch:" pill's text contains "Sep 25" and the middle dot (the freshness word is not asserted, the fixture is Stale against a real clock).
- `dashboard-disclaimer-once.test.ts` (new, 7 tests): 4 matcher self-tests (code hit; props hit; line, block and JSX comments yield 0; `<DisclaimerBanner` and a bare import yield 0), a population fence (more than 100 non-test files, contains both pages, no test path), the DashboardChrome positive control (exactly two `<Disclaimer variant="footer"` in comment-stripped source), and the negative with a `file:line` report. Registered in `vitest.node-env.ts` beside its alphabetical neighbours; vitest reports it as `|node|`.
- `MobileNav.test.tsx` (+3): every link and the "Add a Strategy" button carry `min-w-0`, `flex-1`, `min-h-[44px]`; the label has `text-center` and `leading-tight`; no cell or label carries `truncate`, `overflow-hidden`, `text-ellipsis` or a `line-clamp` token. Tokens are compared by splitting on whitespace.
