<objective>
DBOOK-02: a Deribit correction row carrying a capital reason never classifies as trading P&L, including one that carries a trading token and no capital word, and each correction shape has its own fixture.

Purpose: today `correction_is_trading` returns True for any reason that clears the capital denylist and contains a trading word. A capital adjustment worded `funding account correction` contains `funding` and no capital word, so it is summed into realized trading P&L with no backstop (the balance-identity oracle derives from the same predicate, so it cannot see the misroute). This plan closes the TODOS bullet `Deribit correction residual` (REQUIREMENTS DBOOK-02, covers L2219).

Decision traceability, by CONTEXT label (CONTEXT has no D-NN ids):
- "DBOOK-02: the correction classifier" (decided by the phase agent, accepted by the founder 2026-11-24): trading only with a structural trading shape that names an instrument; a trading word alone is not enough; every other correction fails loud through `assert_correction_classifiable`; one fixture per shape including a capital reason with a trading token and no capital word.
- "Decided autonomously [DBOOK-02]": the structural shape reads an instrument token in the reason OR the row's `instrument_name`; the capital denylist keeps precedence. Recorded reading: "a non-null `instrument_name`" is implemented as a non-blank string that fullmatches the same anchored instrument pattern. This is narrower than "non-null" (a value like `n/a` is not an instrument), which is the safe direction: a narrower gate can only add a fail-loud.
- RESEARCH assumption A6, adopted: the gate is a conjunction (a trading word AND an instrument), not an instrument alone. CONTEXT's "Matching a trading word is not enough" does not say a word stops being needed; narrower is the safe direction.
- "PROOF-01".
- CONTEXT Phase Boundary: no change to the derive writer (seam S1), the compose (seam S2), `ops_staleness_check()` (seam S5), no backfill and no migration. This plan touches one pure function in `deribit_txn.py`.

Known risk, accepted by orchestrator decision (RESEARCH A1/O5): raw Deribit ledgers are not stored, so "no PROD key holds a correction whose reason lacks an instrument token" was not measured. No worker-side census runs before deploy, because it needs a founder go for a Railway start-command swap. If a PROD key does hold such a row, its nightly derive now fails loud with `LedgerValuationError` (the founder's fail-loud rule) and surfaces as a FAILED DERIVE through the job-failure path. The phase 233 freshness arm cannot see it: a derive that stops entirely freezes its inputs and its returns together. Record this paragraph in the SUMMARY under `## Known risk (A1)`.

Output: the two modified files and `233-01-SUMMARY.md`.
