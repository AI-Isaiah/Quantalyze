Where the compose zeroes an emptied level, stated from the code (the executor re-reads each symbol before writing):
- `allocator_equity_derive.replay_key_equity`: the emptied scan runs on the RAW levels, chronologically (prior peak over earlier non-emptied positive levels; two-sided band `-EMPTIED_NEGATIVE_FRACTION * peak <= level < _emptied_ceiling(peak)`; a positive flow right after an emptied day that lifts the level to the ceiling or above ends the stretch and restarts the peak). The forward self-check runs on the raw levels (Pitfall 6). THEN every emptied day's level is set to 0.0 in the returned series. That series is both the level the book sums on the next day and the level carried past the key's own end (`portfolio_returns._level_on` reads it).
- The last day: `peak_at_last_day` is the prior peak before the last day is scanned. When the last day is emptied and the anchor is inside the band of `peak_at_last_day` (`anchor_in_band`), the level stays 0.0; otherwise the live anchor replaces it. A counted key whose key_inputs `anchor_usd` is null is the job's no-capital exit (`run_derive_allocator_equity_job`: `anchors_by_key[_k] = _nc_level`, the stored realized terminal), so its effective anchor is the realized terminal, which is in band whenever its raw last day is emptied.
- `allocator_equity_compose.portfolio_returns`: `numer += equity_prev * ret` and `denom += equity_prev`. A participant whose previous-day level is 0.0 therefore adds nothing to EITHER sum, whatever its stored return or P&L. It is still a participant (its level is not None). After the key loop: a non-finite sum is `nonfinite`; then `if excluded and denom == 0.0: continue` (a key sat out and nothing is left: absent, not counted as skipped, the compose raises `missing_return_inside_coverage`); then `not (denom > 0.0)` is `skipped_nonpositive_denominator`.
- `compose_allocator_equity`: payload `flags` is `sorted(flag_tokens)` (an array of tokens); `inputs` carries `n_keys`, `anchor_asof`, `composed_at`; `failing_key_exits` and `no_capital_exited_keys` entries are `{api_key_id, reason, day}`; `unpriced_native_keys` entries are `{api_key_id, day}` with an optional `reason` (`day_unknown`) and optional `hole_days`. The fourth bucket (`null_anchor_without_returns`): a key with a null anchor, reason `dust`, and no return series is omitted silently and never counted in `inputs.n_keys`.
- `job_worker.run_derive_allocator_equity_job`: a no-capital exit always departs on its listed day (`departed_end_by_key[_k] = _nc_end`); a failing exit departs on its listed day only when that day is before the book's last day, a marked key that reaches the book's last day is named with that day and stays in, and `day: None` names a key with no return day that was left out of the compose. `_load_allocator_daily_returns` reads `csv_daily_returns` (`api_key_id`, `date`, `daily_return`) by `allocator_id` and `api_key_id`; a key with no writer basis in its key_inputs row is replayed on the legacy roll from those returns.
