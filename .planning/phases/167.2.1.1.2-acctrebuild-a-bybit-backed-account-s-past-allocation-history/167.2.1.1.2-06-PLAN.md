## Decisions recorded by this plan

- **AR-7 (one forced path).** Every forced job takes the account path, whatever its key count. A sole-key forced
  rebuild therefore also keeps older days (D-01) and records the boundary (D-03), and the persist-time sibling
  fall-through disappears. The unforced paths (sole-key `replace_equity_snapshots`, multi-key DO NOTHING) are not
  touched (RESEARCH pitfall 6).
- **AR-4 (RESEARCH OQ-2; founder-visible, the founder may revisit).** Rows a forced rebuild writes inside a clipped
  OKX window carry `pre_terminus_balance_unknown = False`: the 89-day start never hits the terminus, and the curve is
  anchored to the venue's `totalEq`. Consequence the founder should know: those rows are no longer excluded from
  level-derived surfaces the way an unforced OKX reconstruct's flagged rows are, so the BaselineUnknownBanner can stop
  showing for them; and an anchored offset spreads one constant, which cannot repair the level of a position opened
  before the window (RESEARCH A5). Recorded here and pinned by the OKX test, so a change of mind is a visible diff.
- The audit counts on a forced completion (counts and booleans only, no ids, no amounts): `account_rebuild` True,
  `account_key_count` (every api_keys row of the user), `account_exchange_accounts`, `account_zero_contribution_accounts`,
  `account_days_incomplete`, `window_days`. The started audit is unchanged.
- Accepted residual (T-167.2.1.1.2-21): a key added between the persist-time key re-read and the window call is not
  seen; such a key has no reconstructed history, and closing it needs an in-function key-count guard (RESEARCH OQ-4,
  recommended no).
