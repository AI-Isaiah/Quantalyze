## Decisions copied for the founder

- **AR-4 (RESEARCH OQ-2; founder-visible, the founder may revisit).** Rows a forced rebuild writes inside a clipped OKX window carry `pre_terminus_balance_unknown = False`: the 89-day start never hits the terminus, and the curve is anchored to the venue's `totalEq`. Consequence the founder should know: those rows are no longer excluded from level-derived surfaces the way an unforced OKX reconstruct's flagged rows are, so the BaselineUnknownBanner can stop showing for them; and an anchored offset spreads one constant, which cannot repair the level of a position opened before the window (RESEARCH A5). Recorded here and pinned by the OKX test, so a change of mind is a visible diff.
- **AR-7 (one forced path).** Every forced job takes the account path, whatever its key count. A sole-key forced rebuild therefore also keeps older days (D-01) and records the boundary (D-03), and the persist-time sibling fall-through disappears. The unforced paths (sole-key `replace_equity_snapshots`, multi-key DO NOTHING) are not touched (RESEARCH pitfall 6).

## Things worth knowing (recorded, not changed)

- A rate limit raised inside any account crawl is stamped on that key and then, because the gather loop is the only converter, ends the job FAILED/permanent as `account_key_read_failed` rather than a retryable transient. This follows the plan text literally. If the founder wants a 429 on a forced rebuild to retry, it is a one-line change in the gather loop.
- An MT5 key's `_mt5_fetch_window` failure arm emits its own `reconstruct_failed` audit before the account refusal `account_key_read_failed` is audited, so that rare path writes two failed audits. Not covered by a test.
- Accepted residual T-167.2.1.1.2-21: a key added between the persist-time key re-read and the window call is not seen.
