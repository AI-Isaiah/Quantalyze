## Decided by the founder (recorded in CONTEXT)

- **D-02:** a tiny-base day's return is null: no stored row, never 0.0, never chained.
- **D-04:** from a key's first mark on, a left-out day is skipped, not a chain break. This is why
  `apply_marks` gives such a day no index entry, and why the fix widens the delete span instead of
  putting NaN rows into the marked series.
- **D-11 (2026-11-05), "Gap closure in 188 (Recommended)":** the reconcile delete covers only the
  span of days still measured, so tiny days at the edge keep the rows an earlier derive stored; fix it
  in this phase.

## Design decisions (planner, Claude's discretion; reversible unless rated)

- **The hull, not a second delete.** The span becomes `[min, max]` over the payload series' days and
  the stated days, and the delete keeps its rule: every calendar day of the span the payload lacks.
  An unmeasured, unstated day that the widened span now includes (for example a mark-gap day between
  a stated leading-edge day and the first measured row) is deleted. That is the treatment the same day
  already gets inside the old span, so the rule is one rule. A second delete naming only the stated days
  would leave that day treated differently by position.
- **One helper for the stated list.** `_tiny_base_days_stored(meta, returns, applied, *, key_mode)`
  returns exactly what each mode stores today. Key mode: `tiny_base_day_list(meta["tiny_base_days"],
  applied.marks_since, applied.tiny_base_days)`. Strategy mode: the realized days kept only when they
  are NaN days of the final series (R2-02) when marks were applied, then
  `tiny_base_day_list(realized, None, applied.tiny_base_days)`. The strategy pre-stamp's
  `tiny_base_guard` logic keeps its own `_realized_tiny` reading unchanged.
- **Gap 2's design question (VERIFICATION):** no new degrade reason. `KEY_INPUTS_MISMATCH` already
  makes such a book untrustworthy, which is loud. After this fix the invariant holds by construction
  and the book arm pins it. A new reason would need new copy, and it would edit `DegradeReason` and
  `_BLOCKING_REASONS`, which this phase's PATTERNS keeps out of bounds.
- **No deletion guard applies.** `csv_daily_returns` carries only the `BEFORE INSERT OR UPDATE`
  owner-coherence trigger (`supabase/migrations/20260624120000_csv_daily_returns_per_key_axis.sql`).
  The `quantalyze.sanitize_in_progress` convention belongs to deletion-cascade guards, and there is
  none on this table. The delete stays the existing service-role delete, scoped by its axis.
- **Out of scope, recorded only:** the key-mode `< 2` interpretable-days short-circuit writes and
  deletes no `csv_daily_returns` row by design (it persists only `key_inputs`), so a stated day there
  would keep an earlier row. It is not reachable for keys C, k1 or k2 (years of measured rows), and the
  VERIFICATION does not name it. Record it in the SUMMARY under "Observations"; do not change it.
