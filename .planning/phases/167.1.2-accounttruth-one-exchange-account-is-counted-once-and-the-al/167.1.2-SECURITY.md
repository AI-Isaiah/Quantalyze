### PR C3 residual notes (audit 2026-09-29, HEAD `b233e99b9`)

Both threats close on their mitigation AS DECLARED. The review findings below are recorded here as
measured. No disposition (fix, route, accept) was recorded for any of them at audit time, and this
note does not supply one. They stay open in the C3 review files.

1. **T-167.1.2-51: the ready-branch warm-up promise is off by one** (`167.1.2-C3-REVIEW.md` WR-01, a Warning;
   `167.1.2-C3-REVIEW-SFH.md` SFH-C3-03, MEDIUM). "Ready" needs at least one return,
   the writer yields at most N-1 returns for an N-day curve, and the factsheet builder needs two returns.
   So `FactsheetWarmupNote` renders only when a 2-day curve is already on screen, while its sentence
   says panels appear "once at least two days" are available; the panels need a third day. The
   promise comes true a day later, so this is not the indefinite timer D-15 removed. It is the same
   class, though, and plan 14's truth D2, the D-15 text in CONTEXT.md and the rewritten
   `FactsheetWarmupNote` JSDoc all say "its timer is true", which is false whenever the note
   renders. The new "ready" arm mocks the builder and pins the sentence verbatim. The copy was outside
   plan 14 by hard constraint, and the review sends it back as a founder copy call. Related: the
   note's count line reads the legacy `snapshotCount`, not the curve shown above it (review IN-01).
2. **T-167.1.2-52: the two surfaces agree on WHETHER, not on WHY** (`167.1.2-C3-REVIEW-SFH.md`
   SFH-C3-01, MEDIUM). The composer reads the state and never the reason
   (`equityHistoryRebuildReason`: 0 hits in `ScenarioComposer.tsx`). For `history_read_failed`,
   `duplicate_account`, `key_not_syncing` and `shared_account_no_working_key`, the Overview names
   the cause or the action, while the Scenario says the history "is being rebuilt". Direction,
   traced at HEAD (a failed derived read leaves `derivedPayload` null, so
   `equityCurveSource` is `"legacy"` in `derivePhase07Fields`): before C3 a `history_read_failed` book with 0 legacy snapshots got no Scenario
   disclosure (a silent gap); after C3 it gets the "being rebuilt" sentence, a false cause that did
   not render before. The declared mitigation (one state gate, arms on both) claims gate parity, not
   reason parity, so the threat closes as declared. Also recorded: parity holds only in book mode,
   D-15's own blank-mode carve-out (SFH-C3-04, LOW), and `src/lib/queries.ts` comments still say the
   warm-up gate and the Scenario disclosure read `equityCurveSource` (SFH-C3-02, review IN-02), so a
   reader following them could restore the removed source gate. The "do not restore" lineage lives
   only in the test files.
