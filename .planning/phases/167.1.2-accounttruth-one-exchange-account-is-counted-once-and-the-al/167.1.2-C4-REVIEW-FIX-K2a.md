### SFH-C4-07: a later key that ends first cut the earlier key's tail (MEDIUM, data integrity)

**Status:** fixed pending founder ratification of the D-09 refinement (see below).

**Commits:** `cfbdee2c9`, plus the follow-up `95d20b3e5`
**Files:** `analytics-service/services/job_worker.py` (`departed_history_inclusion`),
`src/lib/departed-history.ts` (`departedHistoryInclusion`),
`analytics-service/tests/fixtures/departed_history_inclusion.json` (rows 18 and 19, rule prose),
`analytics-service/tests/fixtures/shared_account_resolution.json` (four cells and the notes),
`analytics-service/tests/test_allocator_equity_departed.py`

**Fix.** The fix covers the gap and does not only flag it. The compose takes one end day
per departed key (`departed_end_by_key`, `_clip_through`), and `account_identity_collisions`
models one `[first, last]` window per key. So giving A two windows (Jan 1 to Jan 31 and
Feb 16 to Mar 1) would mean redesigning the compose and the collision model. The rule
stays single-window instead. Among the counted departed keys on one known account, in the
D-09 order, a key is COVERED when its last countable day (min(end day, last returns day))
is before the last countable day of a key ordered ahead of it. That earlier key reads the
account over every day of the covered key. A covered key counts zero days, gets the reason
`same_account_as_earlier_key` and bounds nothing. Every other key is bounded by the next
uncovered key. A: Jan 1 to Mar 1. B: zero days. Every day is counted once.

- Where the successor ends on or after the predecessor, the behaviour is unchanged. All 17
  original fixture rows kept their expected values byte for byte.
- A new invariant test, `test_no_day_of_a_known_account_is_dropped`, runs over every
  fixture row. It checks that every day a counted-eligible departed key read, before a live
  key on the account takes over, is counted exactly once. It is the other half of B4.
- **Follow-up `95d20b3e5` (knock-on, measured).** Four cells of the holder matrix had
  pinned the same hole: holder revoked or inactive, marked key disconnected. H ends on its
  last returns day (06-03) and M on its disconnect day (06-02). The old rule gave H zero
  days and M the account, so 06-03 counted nowhere. Under the fixed rule, H counts the
  account through 06-03 and M counts zero days. The cells now read `counted/terminal:
  [O, H]`. The reader's side of every cell is unchanged (`ready`).
- **Process slip.** I committed `cfbdee2c9` after running only the departed suite. The
  wider `-k holder` gate then went red on those four cells. The follow-up commit is the
  correction.

**Spec refinement: the founder must decide, and the orchestrator must record it.** This
refines D-09 case (2), which the founder confirmed. The phrase "that key carries the
account's history from then on" now holds only for a successor that does not end first. A
successor that ends first is covered instead. This needs the founder's ratification, not
just a CONTEXT edit. I did not edit CONTEXT.md or ROADMAP.md. The deviation policy asks for
the decision to be recorded in both.

**Recorded RED.** The new fixture rows ran against the pre-fix rule:
- pytest: `5 failed, 79 passed`. The failures were rows 18 and 19 in the rule and job-level
  tests, plus row 18 in the new coverage invariant.
- vitest: `Tests 2 failed | 19 passed`.

GREEN after the fix: pytest `84 passed`, vitest `21 passed`.

### SFH-C4-05: the switch read ON beside "History not available" (MEDIUM)

**Commit:** `e40a6f768`
**Files:** `departed-history.ts` (+ test), `AllocatorExchangeManager.tsx` (+ departed-history test)

**Fix.** `departedHistoryCard(key, keys, anchor)` returns `{sentence, checked, toggleTo}`.
The component renders that result and no longer decides anything itself. Without a usable
anchor, wherever the book could otherwise hold the history, the switch is OFF and disabled
(`toggleTo: null`). The line names the real cause. Because the switch cannot fire, no
include is invited and no recompute is promised.

Five existing component tests drove an unanchored switch: the RPC failure test, both 55006
tests, the 55000 test and the unknown-default test. They now seed an anchor, because their
subject is the RPC mapping and that needs a switch the book can act on.

**RED:** I neutered the unanchored gate in `departedHistoryCard`. That gave `7 failed` across
the lib tests and the component tests, including the pre-existing "History not available:"
component test. Neutering the reason-specific copy gave `2 failed`. After each neuter I
restored the file from a byte backup, `cmp` matched, and the tests were GREEN (`64 passed`).
