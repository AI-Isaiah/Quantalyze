<objective>
Rebuild the allocator's equity history as a per-account, flow-neutral series and persist its own
returns (success criterion 3, D-06). Refuse to persist it while any account is counted twice. This is
the Python compose and job slice. The TS reader that switches My Allocation from "rebuilding" to this
series is plan 11 (split from this plan by the plan check, W3). It reads the payload contract stated
in must_haves.

Purpose: Phase 115.1 already replays each key's $-equity from a live anchor with flows and
carry-forward. Three defects keep it from being the answer: two keys on one account are summed
twice, only a $-curve is persisted so the dashboard turns deposits into returns, and any gap falls
back to the legacy patchwork. This plan closes all three.
Output: `returns` + `version: 2` in the compose payload, and an identity gate over the counted set
in the job.
Ship unit: PR C. ⛔ Plans 09 (departed history, D-05) and 11 (the TS reader) ship in the SAME PR.
Without plan 09 the curve would drop departed accounts, against the founder's scope. Without plan 11
nothing renders it.
</objective>
