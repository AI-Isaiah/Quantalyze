### WR-01 / SFH-C3-03: the ready-branch warm-up note promised panels after two days; they need three

**Files modified:** `src/app/(dashboard)/allocations/AllocationDashboardV2.tsx`,
`src/app/(dashboard)/allocations/AllocationDashboardV2.rebuilding.test.tsx`,
`src/app/(dashboard)/allocations/AllocationDashboardV2.warmup.test.tsx` (new)
**Commit:** `a48200f48`

**Applied fix:**
- Copy now reads "Aggregated factsheet panels appear once at least three days of blended equity
  history are available." (no em dash).
- The day count is **cited, not imported**: the builder's minimum is the inline literal
  `dailyReturns.length < 2` in `buildAllocatorPortfolioFactsheetPayload`
  (`src/lib/factsheet/allocator-portfolio-payload.ts`), not an export, and that file is outside
  this fixer's scope. The JSDoc names it, plus the writer's `portfolio_returns`
  (`range(1, len(union))`, confirmed by reading `allocator_equity_compose.py`) and the reader's
  refusal of a zero-return series. Together they give three curve days.
- The JSDoc's "so its timer is true" claim is replaced. The new text says when the note renders
  and why the count is three.
- **Known limit, written into the JSDoc:** a day the writer skips (`skipped` / `nonfinite` in
  `portfolio_returns`) adds no return. The panels can then need more than three days. "At least"
  stays true; the promise is a floor.
- **Test:** the mocked-builder arm in `rebuilding.test.tsx` is deleted and replaced by a pointer.
  The new arms live in `AllocationDashboardV2.warmup.test.tsx`, which does NOT mock the builder.
  - **Why a new file:** `rebuilding.test.tsx`'s `vi.mock` of the builder module is hoisted and
    file-scoped. An unmocked arm cannot live there without changing what every other arm exercises.
  - **Fixture:** producer-shaped ready payloads, as `derivePhase07Fields` emits them: N curve days,
    returns on days 2..N (N-1 returns), `equityHistoryState: "ready"`, reason null, source
    `"derived"`, N in {2, 3, 4}. N=1 is excluded because the reader never makes a zero-return
    book ready.
  - **Oracle:** for each N the test calls the real builder with the arguments the Overview passes.
    It asserts the note renders exactly when that call returns null, and the factsheet body
    renders exactly when it does not.
  - **Copy binding:** the test computes the smallest N the real builder accepts, maps it to a
    word, and asserts the sentence says `at least <word> days`. A non-vacuity check requires both
    outcomes in the measured range.
- The header comment in `rebuilding.test.tsx` no longer quotes the two-day promise or claims the
  timer is true. The `:313` IN-01 lineage comment still quotes the old copy, and that quote is
  accurate as history, so it stays.

**Test-first evidence:**

   days of...", received "...at least two days of...".
2. **Neuter 1, after commit.** Copy sed back to "two days" gives RED with the same diff,
