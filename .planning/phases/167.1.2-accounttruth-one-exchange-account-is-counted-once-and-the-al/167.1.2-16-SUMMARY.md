## Routed to C2/C4 replans

1. **How the history rebuild treats a still-connected key that is is_active false, sign_in_failed or error.** This covers whether it counts as departed for the rebuild at all, whether the unknown-identity default-exclude applies to it, and what its end day is. It is owned by the **C2 replan of plans 05 and 10** and the **C4 replan of plan 09**. It is OPEN. The "last returns day" end day for such a key is a planner derivation, not a decision. The history_inclusion COMMENT states no default for such a key until the replans land. Its end-day and default-exclude sentences apply, as written, to disconnected and revoked keys only.
2. **Resetting an owner's choice when a failing or inactive key recovers on an ordinary worker tick.** This is owned by the **C4 replan of plan 09**, which also ships the only product caller of `set_departed_key_history_inclusion`. The gap cannot open before plan 09 lands. A reset trigger was deliberately not added, because the worker cycles syncing then error on every failed attempt.
3. **Plans 05, 08, 09 and 10 must add `167.1.2-16` to their `depends_on` when replanned.** Plan 09's must_haves still say "disconnected or revoked" and need rewriting against D-18.

- **Commit:** d8ff7a7a7
- **Why this matters:** in the main checkout, which IS linked to PROD, the same mistake would have run `supabase db push` against production. Any generated SQL or prose that quotes a CLI command in backticks must go through a quoted heredoc or the Write tool.
