## The rule, sourced (measured by the planner at this branch's HEAD)

- **D-18 wording** (CONTEXT, end of file): "a key that is ACTIVE and whose last sync is not
  `sign_in_failed` or `error`", replacing "not disconnected, not revoked", "so an account whose only
  other holder is inactive or failing is counted by a healthy sibling".
- **"ACTIVE" is the repo's one eligibility predicate**: `is_active AND sync_status IS DISTINCT FROM
  'revoked' AND disconnected_at IS NULL`, stated identically by `eligible_key_predicate` in
  `analytics-service/services/allocator_equity_derive.py`, by the COMMENT in
  `supabase/migrations/20260717233529_allocator_equity_derived_surface.sql`, and by the TypeScript
  `isPerKeyDailiesEligibleKey` in `src/lib/queries.ts`. No conflicting source was found.
- **The D-18 working-holder predicate is therefore**
  `is_active AND disconnected_at IS NULL AND (sync_status IS NULL OR sync_status NOT IN ('revoked', 'sign_in_failed', 'error'))`.
  The explicit `sync_status IS NULL OR` leg is required: `NULL NOT IN (...)` is NULL, which an `IF`
  treats as false and which would silently make a never-synced key non-working. NULL counts as
  WORKING, as it does today in `isWorkingHolder` and in the eligible predicate
  (`IS DISTINCT FROM`). `api_keys.is_active` is `boolean NOT NULL DEFAULT true` (baseline.sql), so
  it needs no NULL leg. `rate_limited`, `syncing`, `computing`, `complete_with_warnings` stay
  working: D-18 names only the two failing statuses.

## The owner's choice across a recovery: ROUTED to plan 09 (PR C4), by name

D-18 widens "departed" for the RPC: a connected key that is `sign_in_failed`, `error` or
`is_active = false` now accepts an include/exclude choice. Today only `reconnect_allocator_api_key`
resets `history_inclusion` to NULL, and plan 09 owns the rotate-secret route's reset (its must_haves:
"add `history_inclusion: null` to that same update"). A key that recovers from `error` or
`sign_in_failed` on an ordinary worker tick passes through neither, so a choice made while it was
failing would sit dormant and re-apply at its next failure.

**Disposition: routed to Phase 167.1.2 plan 09 (PR C4), stated in the `history_inclusion` COMMENT
with the "owned by … until it lands" pattern the COMMENT already uses. NOT fixed in this migration.**
Why, measured:
1. The gap cannot open before its owner lands. `set_departed_key_history_inclusion` has no caller in
   `src/` other than the generated `src/lib/database.types.ts`: the toggle that calls it is plan 09's.
   Until plan 09 ships, no product path can store a choice on a failing key.
2. A "reset on recovery" mechanism is not small or sourced. The worker writes `sync_status =
   'syncing'` when a sync starts and `'error'` when it fails, so a key that keeps failing cycles
   syncing then error on every attempt. A trigger that reset the choice on "became working" would
   wipe the owner's choice every tick; a narrower "reset on a success status" trigger is a semantic
   decision (which statuses mean recovered) on a hot table with no source in CONTEXT. That decision
   belongs to the replan of plan 09, which also owns the toggle's behaviour for a key whose status
   flaps between working and failing.

## OPEN: how the history rebuild treats a still-connected failing or inactive key (NOT decided here)

D-18 widens the RPC's departed test, so an owner may now record a choice for a key that is still
connected (disconnected_at NULL) but is_active false, sign_in_failed or error. CONTEXT does not
decide how the C2 history rebuild treats such a key. Two readings the planner derived are recorded
here as OPEN, not decided:
- **End day.** The planner's first draft extended D-09's "last returns day" end day, which D-09
  gives for a revoked key, to every failing or inactive key. That is a planner derivation with no
  source in CONTEXT.
- **Default-exclude.** Applied literally, D-09's unknown-identity default-exclude would drop the
  whole history of a still-connected key with a NULL venue_account_id after a single failed sync
  tick. That would be a silent loss of a working account's history.

Neither is written into the COMMENT as a rule. The `history_inclusion` COMMENT says the question is
owned by the Phase 167.1.2 C2 replan of plans 05 and 10 and the C4 replan of plan 09, and that until
it lands the column states no default for such a key. The end-day and default-exclude sentences keep
applying, as written, to disconnected and revoked keys only. This is the orchestrator's disposition
of plan-check warning 1 (revision 1).
