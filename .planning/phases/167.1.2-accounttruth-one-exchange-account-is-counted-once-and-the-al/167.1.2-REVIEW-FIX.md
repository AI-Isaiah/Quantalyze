### CR-04 — an emptied account contributes $0

**Brief precondition, verified as asked.** An empty holdings list does reach `_update_ok`. `persist_allocator_holdings` returns 0 without raising (`allocator_positions.py`, `if not holdings: return 0`), and `_update_ok` runs next in the same `try`. So no MT5-style marker was built.

**⚠️ Deviation from the brief: the evidence column.** The brief named `api_keys.last_sync_at` with a success `sync_status`. Measured, that column cannot identify a holdings poll:
- `routers/cron.py` `cron-sync` selects every active key (`.eq("is_active", True)`, no strategy filter).
- It advances `last_sync_at` whenever `(not trades) or synced_count > 0`, and it writes no `sync_status`.
- So an idle or emptied key's `last_sync_at` moves on every cron tick.
- Under the briefed rule, any key whose 04:00 holdings poll has not landed by the 05:00 refresh (worker backlog, or a circuit-breaker DEFERRED) would read `complete` + today + yesterday's `asof` as empty. It would then persist a false $0, first-writer-wins. That is the D-07 defect again, for every key.

**Applied fix.** The rule is unchanged: a successful poll newer than the account's latest row means $0. ~~The evidence is now the durable record of the poll itself: a `compute_jobs` row with `kind='poll_allocator_positions'`, `status='done'`, for a group member, whose **`claimed_at`** falls on a day after the group's latest `asof`.~~
⛔ **CORRECTED 2026-09-29 (C2 round 2, `167.1.2-C2-REVIEW-FIX-R2.md`; the struck text is kept as lineage):** the evidence is the proving poll's OWN recorded outcome: the `allocator.holdings.sync_completed` audit event, which now carries `final_status` beside `row_count`, created after the latest `asof`, with `final_status` `complete` and `row_count` 0 (R2-CR-01). The job row records no outcome.
- Why `claimed_at`: the handler computes `asof` after the claim, so a poll that wrote rows has `asof` on or after its claim day. `updated_at` can cross midnight.
- `mark_compute_job_done` leaves `claimed_at` alone.
- Done jobs are retained for 30 days (`20260417110539` retention cron), and a daily poll keeps a fresh one.
- ~~The proof is admitted only from a member on a ccxt venue whose `sync_status` is exactly `complete`.~~ `complete_with_warnings` means a derivative read failed. MT5 persists its own empty-equity row, and sFOX / unsupported venues return no rows with a warning (`NON_CCXT_VENUES`).
  ⛔ **CORRECTED 2026-09-29 (C2 round 2, `167.1.2-C2-REVIEW-FIX-R2.md`; the struck text is kept as lineage):** gating on the key's CURRENT status re-carried a proven-empty account on any refresh that read `rate_limited` or `syncing` (R2-CR-01). The ccxt-venue gate stays; the status gate is gone. Two further gaps are closed: a poll landing between the two reads is counted, not zeroed (SFH-R2-01), and a book whose every account is proven empty writes a $0 row (SFH-R2-02).
- With no proof, the D-07 carry holds. The count is reported as `emptied_accounts` in the audit and the log.
