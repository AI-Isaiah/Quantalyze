<objective>
Close **F6** — gate condition 4's first half.

Both existing cache controls scan **a single file's bytes**. `src/lib/factsheet/fetch-and-build-payload.ts`
and its 37 transitive dependencies are UNGUARDED, and the builder's own docblock says
*"It imports `next/cache` nowhere"* as though that were enforced. It is not — it is a comment.

The failure mode Team 4 described: a perf PR wraps a composite read in `unstable_cache([…, id])`
somewhere inside the builder's dependency graph. The entry is now **id-keyed and shared between
lanes** — the token lane fills it with an unpublished payload, and the next anonymous reader of
that id is served the unpublished factsheet for the full TTL. **Every existing gate stays green.**
The failure is silent, TTL-long, and invisible in CI. Live exposure today is zero (no mint route,
empty table); it stops being zero at 164-03, which is why Team 4's words were "must land before
164-03."

Output: the guard pinned over the whole transitive graph, proven RED by a planted import in a DEEP
dependency, and a docblock that cites its enforcement rather than claiming it.

⛔ NOT in scope: `page.cache-isolation.test.tsx`, the ORDERED acceptance spec. That is
**164-05-03** and it stays there. This plan closes the *static* half of gate condition 4; 164-05
closes the *behavioural* half. Neither substitutes for the other.
</objective>
