## Booked observation (RESEARCH Open Question 1, no change)

For a constant-yield one-strategy book, the optimizer's `sharpe_lift` and `corr_with_portfolio` are `None`, the score is `0.3 * dd_improvement`, and the top suggestion is the least-bad dip. After plan 01 its `dd_improvement` is negative, so `WhatWedDoCard` drops the drawdown clause, falls back to "... to diversify the portfolio", and prints a negative optimizer score. The sentence is not false, only thin; whether to hide the card when the only statistic is a worsening drawdown is a founder question. The new WhatWedDoCard pin renders exactly this shape and asserts the fallback. Plan 03 carries this to the CHANGELOG `### Notes`.

A second observation, not a defect of this plan: `portfolio-analytics-adapter.ts` coerces a missing `dd_improvement` to `0` (`?? 0`). A stored row with no value therefore renders as a neutral 0.00%, not as "not computable". Pre-existing, unrelated to the sign, left alone.
