<objective>
The key-mode options basis, end to end (D-04, SC-2, SC-5): an allocator key with Deribit options is
built on the founder's F1 basis from its one crawl, every writer quantity reads that ledger, the
first marked day joins it with no day counted twice, and a structural failure falls back to cash
with the caveat.

Purpose: `job_worker.py` keeps key mode on `cash_settlement` (`DEFAULT_PNL_BASIS`), so the level a
key-mode return divides by is the cash wallet and an option round trip prints -48% then +333%
(RESEARCH Q10). The bases exist; key mode needs them as its HEADLINE, not as an additive pass
(RESEARCH Q1).

Pre-mark history and marked days (the orchestrator's required statement). `apply_marks` is the only
join. Days before `S` (the key's first mark) keep the writer's series exactly, which after this plan
is the options-basis series for an option-activity key. Day `S` is `(M_S - L(S-1) - F_S) / L(S-1)`
where `L(S-1)` is the writer's own realized level rolled from its terminal through its own day P&L
and flows (G-01), so with the options ledger the base includes the option book and `r(S)` no longer
absorbs it. Days after `S` are mark days only; gap days are absent and listed in `mark_gap_days`.
Marks win from `S`; a day's flow is placed once. Task 2's seam test pins all of it.

Captures that stay as the cash pass took them (stated, not implied; plan-checker revision
2026-11-01). The three 167.1.2.2.1 D-03 captures in `key_inputs` are NOT recomputed from the
options ledger. `ledger_digest` hashes the one crawl's rows and `account_summary` is the derive's
one `get_account_summaries` read, so both are identical for the cash and the options ledger.
`native_inception_diagnostics` is intentionally the CASH ledger's: MD-01 requires it to be taken
before anything can refuse, so a derive whose options step later degrades still stores it; the
cash `combine_native_ledger` and its section 5 gate run first on every derive, so the numbers
always belong to a gate that ran; no gate reads them; and recomputing them would make a stored
diagnostic depend on whether the options step succeeded. Step (c) leaves all three captures where
they are and adds one comment line at the inception-diagnostics capture saying so.

Output: the constant, the options step, the narrowed caveat rule, the `options_basis` field, the
rewritten key-mode contract, and RED-first tests. With plans 185-07 and 185-09 this is PR-2;
this plan is never shipped without 185-09 (the budget bound).
</objective>
