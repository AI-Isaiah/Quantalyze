<objective>
Make the key-mode options step safe inside the derive budget and decide its edge days: bound it,
degrade it to cash with the caveat on refusal or timeout, keep transients retryable, and apply the
founder's F2 answer for days the option prices no longer cover.

Purpose: RESEARCH Q4 and Pitfall 4: the options step adds one public chart request per held
instrument plus the dense index marks inside a fixed 900 s budget, and an unbounded step that runs
over would turn a healthy cash derive into `failed_final`. RESEARCH Q5 and Pitfall 5: the ~913-day
chart window advances daily, so some option days fall back to cash and the caveat must follow them.

Output: the budget slice and arms, the F2 rule, the final caveat rule, and RED-first tests with
neuter drills. Last plan of PR-2.
</objective>
