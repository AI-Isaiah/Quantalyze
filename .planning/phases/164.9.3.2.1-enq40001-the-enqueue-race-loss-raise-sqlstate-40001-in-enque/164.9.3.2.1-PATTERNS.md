## Shared Patterns

### Fail-loud arm identity
**Source:** `supabase/tests/test_compute_job_fence_errcode.sql:127-129`, `supabase/tests/test_ledger_refresh_fanout.sql:1838-1848`
**Apply to:** the new gate. Every failure path raises `TEST FAILED (<arm>): ...` naming the arm, the observed SQLSTATE and message, and why the expected value matters. A raw trigger or RPC error must never escape as the first failure.

### Layered twins with guard stand-down
**Source:** `supabase/tests/test_compute_job_fence_errcode.sql:41-50` (rationale) and :126 (shape). The grammar is in `scripts/mutation-runner/GRAMMAR.md`.
**Apply to:** R1. Step 2 always stands down the migration's own self-verify guard, which here is `IF v_body10 !~ c_serfail_re THEN`. The edit touches the runner's scratch copy only.

### Own rows only, nothing survives
**Source:** `test_compute_job_fence_errcode.sql:26-33, 65, 213`
**Apply to:** the gate. Use one `BEGIN; DO $$ ... $$; ROLLBACK;`, no dblink and no psql meta-commands. Every count is scoped to the gate's own fixture ids (dedupe gate :44-47 states the same rule for shared TEST).
