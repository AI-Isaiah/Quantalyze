<objective>
Replace the hand-typed six-name staging allowlist with a derivation: the stager enumerates what the
two producers' own source text declares they write, unions it, and stages that.

Purpose: the list agrees with the producers TODAY by coincidence of two lists holding the same names,
not by construction. An auditor measured it clean and that changes nothing — a hand-maintained list
that agrees with its source right now is precisely the state this defect passes through on its way to
disagreeing, and a channel added later is then silently ABSENT from the artifact.

Output: a small testable derivation script, the staging step wired to it with its default-DENY
discipline intact, and a three-armed calibration proving a new channel arrives with no list edited
while an undeclared file still does not.
</objective>
