  verbatim `<worktree_branch_check>` block with `EXPECTED_BASE` pinned to the phase tip
  (`execute-phase.md:519,573`). 4/4 spawns in 141.1 came up 56–79 commits stale.
- **Dispatch ONE `Agent()` per message** — concurrent `git worktree add` races on
  `.git/config.lock` (`execute-phase.md:531-537`).
- **An interrupted executor's uncommitted diff may be a MUTATION, not progress** —
  141.1-08's orphaned diff was its own acceptance mutation caught mid-probe.
- **Every pin must be proven to fail**, driven through the REAL call site. D-01/D-03 are
  seam-crossing; a helper-level test cannot see them.
- **Prose moves with code** — docblocks + `docs/runbooks/seam-breaker.md` describe the
  D-16 semantics and the current retry verdicts.
- **CI gates are ADVISORY at merge** (branch protection deferred until paying clients),
  so say "would have caught", never "did stop".
