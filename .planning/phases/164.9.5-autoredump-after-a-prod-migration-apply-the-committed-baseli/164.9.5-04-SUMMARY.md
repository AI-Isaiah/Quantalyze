# Phase 164.9.5 Plan 04: redump workflow wiring Summary

**`supabase-migrate.yml` now carries `redump-dump` (PROD credential and a read-only token: it re-dumps PROD after a succeeded `apply` on main, gates the dump with the script and a sha256-pinned gitleaks 8.30.1, and uploads a 1-day artifact only on a change) and `redump-pr` (write token and no PROD credential: it composes onto current main, refuses a human commit on the bot branch, and makes one leased push to `automation/baseline-redump` before opening or editing the PR). Both sit above `apply`, which is still the last job.**
