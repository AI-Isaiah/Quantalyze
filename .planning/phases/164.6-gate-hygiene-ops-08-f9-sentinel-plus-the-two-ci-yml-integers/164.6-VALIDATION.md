## Sampling Rate

- **After every task commit:** the quick vitest command, plus the lane run of any touched gate file.
- **After every plan wave:** `npm test`; `node scripts/mutation-runner/run.mjs --parse-only`; `--file` on both ledger gates; `node scripts/lint-sql-gates.mjs`; `npm run schema:functions:check`; the ledger pytest; lint and typecheck on changed files.
- **Before `/gsd-verify-work`:** a full `node scripts/mutation-runner/run.mjs` exits 0, and the floors, parser, meta, contract and drift vitest files are green. `baseline-content-drift` is EXPECTED to report exactly the two fan-out findings until the post-apply re-dump — recorded, never allowlisted.
- **Max feedback latency:** quick loop under 10 s.
