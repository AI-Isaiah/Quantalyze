## Fixed Issues

### HIGH-1: No automated reader of the failure row. An all-fail tick is recorded as `succeeded`.

**Status:** fixed: requires human verification (logic)
**Files modified:** `supabase/migrations/20260924120000_ledger_fanout_failure_count.sql`, both `supabase/schema/functions/enqueue_ledger_*.sql` snapshots, `supabase/tests/test_ledger_refresh_fanout.sql`, `supabase/tests/test_ledger_refresh_composite_arm.sql`, `scripts/prod-prober/arms/cron-obs.mjs`, `scripts/prod-prober/run.mjs`, `scripts/prod-prober/fixtures/cron-obs/ledger-fanout-failed.json`, `src/__tests__/prod-prober-wiring.test.ts`
**Commits:** `0dd123d41`, `21a8e2818`
**Applied fix:**
- Both fan-outs now `RAISE EXCEPTION '<fn>: every candidate this tick failed to enqueue …'` when `v_enqueued = 0 AND v_failed > 0`. The raise happens after the unlock, so pg_cron records the run as `failed`. Arm U (every candidate fails, so the call must raise) and arm R (rewritten to accept the raise) cover this, and each has a RED-UNDER twin.
- **Confirmed by symbol that cron-obs did not surface it.** Before this fix, `scripts/prod-prober/arms/cron-obs.mjs` had no step for the ledger fan-out job. This round extends it minimally with step (4): `LEDGER_FANOUT_SQL` and `parseLedgerFanoutRow`, which raise the defect kind `cron-ledger-fanout-failed`. The step counts the job's runs and errored runs over 3 hours by matching `position(<function name> IN return_message)`, and never reads `status`.
- The prober self-test grew from 83 to 84 scenarios and passes 84/84. A neuter of the new step was seen RED, then restored.
- The `return_message` shape was **measured on the local lane (pg_cron 1.6.4)**. A failed run records the ERROR plus a `CONTEXT: PL/pgSQL function <name>()` line. A succeeded run records `1 row`. Measured with the job still scheduled: 2 runs, 2 errored for a failing fan-out; 2 runs, 0 errored for a healthy one.

**Recorded trade:** the RAISE rolls back that tick's own `cron_runs` failure row. On an all-fail tick the 20-hour cooldown (HIGH-2) therefore does not engage, and the tick fails loud every hour instead. That is the chosen direction, because a loud repeat beats a silent skip. The migration header carries it.
