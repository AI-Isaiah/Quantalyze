  - The function keeps the same signature, `SECURITY DEFINER` and the pinned `search_path`.
  - REVOKE from PUBLIC, anon and authenticated, then GRANT to service_role, are re-issued.
  - Every DO block reads only the catalogue and the function body, so none can refuse on TEST.
  - Each COMMENT re-issue is guarded.
  - A negative anchor over the comment-stripped body refuses any `computed_at` write.
- **Rollback in `down/`** (T-15):
  - `db push` never applies files in `down/`.
  - It has a catalogue-only precondition and re-issues SECDEF, `search_path` and the ACL.
  - It writes no data.
- **Runner stamps** (T-01, T-02):
  - The literal `"now"` stamp appears only in the two success payloads: `analytics_runner.py` `_mark_complete` and the composite `headline_payload` in `job_worker.py`.
  - The AST census `test_computed_at_stamp_census.py` enforces both rules, success-only and literal `now`, and its synthetic defects bite.
- **`.neq("status","done")` guards** (T-10, T-11):
  - Present in both `long_fetch.py` and `ledger-refresh-marker.ts`.
  - After a zero-row update, each re-reads once. A `done` job is a quiet skip; a lost row throws in TS, and logs an error plus a Sentry signal in Python.
- **Sentry signal in `long_fetch.py`:**
  - The message is static. The job id and marker go in tags only, and the marker comes from a closed set.
  - It is wrapped in try/except, so telemetry cannot fail the enqueue.
