## Pitfall 6 decision

**Cache not exposed, PDF exposed, window recorded not closed.**

Grep evidence (run at HEAD):

- `git grep -n -e fetch_strategy_lazy_metrics -e upsert_strategy_analytics_series_batch -- src analytics-service/services`: in `src` the lazy RPC is read only by `queries-client.ts` / `queries.ts` (the client-side lazy panels) and in `analytics-service/services` the batch RPC is called only at `analytics_runner.py` (the CSV sibling upsert) and `job_worker.py` (the composite sibling upsert) and `basis_series.py` (the basis series writer). The cached builder (`composite-read-path.ts`) reads kinds `MTM_DAILY_RETURNS_SERIES_KIND`, `SMOOTHED_MTM_DAILY_RETURNS_SERIES_KIND` and `CASH_SETTLEMENT_SERIES_KIND` only, never a lazy-panel sibling kind.
- Write order, read from the code: CSV runner `_persist_cash_series` then `_mark_complete` (the stamp) then `_upsert_siblings`; composite `persist_basis_series` (three calls) then `_write_headline_and_by_basis` (the stamp) then the sibling upsert. So the cash, MTM and smoothed-MTM series the cached builder reads are written BEFORE the stamp: **the `unstable_cache` is not exposed.**
- PDF route (`route.ts`): ETag `"${id}:${analytics.computed_at}"`, `If-None-Match` equal returns 304, both responses carry `Cache-Control: public, s-maxage=3600, stale-while-revalidate=86400`, puppeteer `waitUntil: "networkidle0"` (lazy panels fetch the sibling series). **Exposed.**

**Exposure.** A PDF rendered between the stamp and the sibling write shows the new scalars beside the previous run's lazy panels, carries the NEW date, can be served by the CDN for up to 3600 s (stale up to 86400 s more), and stays 304-valid until the next real recompute moves `computed_at`. Before this phase the bridge stamped AFTER the siblings, so such a render carried the OLD date and was replaced on revalidation.

**Measured width (local stack, loopback).** One `upsert_strategy_analytics_series_batch` round trip through PostgREST as the service role, on a strategy this step seeded and deleted (`strategies_left 0`, `series_left 0` read back). Payload: 12 kinds, 560 034 bytes, built from the real `compute_all_metrics` output over 1 100 daily points (10 kinds) plus `exposure_series` and `turnover_series` in the same shape (no existing test fixture carries a 12-kind payload, see Deviations). 5 calls: 41.6, 25.2, 23.5, 24.1, 21.2 ms. **Median 24.1 ms, max 41.6 ms.** A lower bound for PROD (no network hop, small stack). `strategy_analytics_series` read 12 rows after the calls, as expected.

**Closing option** (booked, not taken): write the sibling series BEFORE the success write, as the cash series already is, folding a sibling failure into that write's flags. The width, the exposure and the option are in `TODOS.md` `[166.5-PDF-MIXED-VINTAGE-WINDOW]` and are for plan 08's Known limits and CHANGELOG.
