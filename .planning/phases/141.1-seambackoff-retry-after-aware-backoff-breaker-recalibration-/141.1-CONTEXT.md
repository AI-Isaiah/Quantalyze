<domain>
## Phase Boundary

Phase 141 shipped a bounded seam retry (`retries: 1` on five `SEAM_BUDGETS` rows) gated on a committed retry-safety audit (`src/lib/seam-retry-registry.ts`). An 8-agent review campaign found **zero user-facing defects and zero data-integrity defects** — every blocking claim was refuted under adversarial scrutiny.

141.1 delivers three things, none of which is a bug fix:

1. **Consume a design input that was built and never used.** Phase 140.5 shipped `Retry-After` propagation as an explicit hard prerequisite for 141. The retry loop ignores it.
2. **Turn an accidental threshold into a decided one.** `BREAKER_FAILURE_THRESHOLD = 5` predates per-attempt failure counting.
3. **Repair a defective deliverable.** The SEAM-05 audit artifact's *evidence* is wrong in several places even though every *verdict* is right. The registry's whole premise is "the evidence IS the entry"; wrong evidence is a defective deliverable because a future engineer extends the allowlist by reasoning from these strings.

**Nothing is live.** `origin/main` has no `seam-retry-registry.ts` and no `retriesOverride` — production performs zero seam retries today. Branch `feat/v1.16-141-jobs-rate-retry` is ahead with no open PR, so 141.1 lands **alongside** 141 in the same PR rather than shipping a knowingly-imprecise audit artifact and correcting it afterwards.

### Out of scope (hard fences)
- **Do not re-litigate the RULED OUT table** in `141-REVIEW-CONSOLIDATED.md`. Each of those 8 entries died on specific evidence; re-opening them burns the campaign's main value.
- **Do not change any retry verdict.** No `SEAM_BUDGETS` row is un-flipped, no YES becomes NO. Only the justifying prose is wrong.
- **Do not redesign the retry mechanism.** It is sound.
- **No Python-side request-cancellation work** (see D-03).

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### A. Design (founder-decided 2026-07-31)

- **D-01 — `Retry-After` ⇒ fail fast, do not sleep.** When a retry-eligible 503 carries a `Retry-After` header, the loop must **not** retry: it surfaces attempt 1's response immediately (preserving the 503 body — `dependency`, `human_message`) and does not spend a second breaker failure. Rationale: `analytics-service/services/error_contract.py:131-152` makes `Retry-After` mandatory on every SERVICE-TRANSIENT 503 from `RETRY_AFTER_SECONDS = {"mt5-gateway": 30, "supabase": 15}`; no route budget is worth burning billed Vercel wall-clock sleeping for 15–30s, and retrying in 375ms is near-certain to fail. The existing jittered `SEAM_RETRY_BACKOFF_MS + jitter` backoff **stays unchanged** for the transport/timeout class, which is the dominant Railway-blip class. 429 is already correctly excluded (`attributability: "caller"`, `counts: false`) and stays excluded. Covers A1.
- **D-02 — `BREAKER_FAILURE_THRESHOLD` stays 5; the decision gets recorded.** Faster containment under per-attempt counting is ratified as *intentional*, not accidental: a Railway blip reaching 5 counted failures is a real outage, and tripping the global `breaker:railway` key in ~2.5 user requests instead of 5 is the right direction. No numeric change to `BREAKER_FAILURE_THRESHOLD`, `BREAKER_WINDOW`, or `BREAKER_COOLDOWN_S`. The reasoning must be pinned in a comment **and** a test so it stops being an undecided tradeoff. Covers A2.
- **D-03 — Server-side cancellation is out of scope; the cost is recorded.** No `request.is_disconnected()` work in the analytics service. The doubled-compute consequence (a retry adds a second concurrent full compute on the 4 heavy budgets while attempt 1 still burns CPU) is documented in the registry/runbook as a known accepted consequence and booked to `TODOS.md`. Covers A3.

### B. SEAM-05 deliverable — evidence re-derived from traced source

- **D-04 — Re-derive every analytics evidence string from its own handler.** The three analytics YES verdicts that claim *"no persisted server-side write on the request path"* are false: `bridge` (`portfolio.py:2047,2123,2167`), `simulator` (`simulator.py:442,490`), `portfolio-optimizer` (`portfolio.py:1836-1838` UPDATE + `:1863` audit). Only `optimize-weights` is accurate. Each entry states its **actual traced finding** and **why the retry is still safe given that write** (append-only `audit_log` with no counting consumer for these entity types; deterministic whole-value overwrite by row id). Verdicts stay YES; rows stay flipped. Also correct `runPortfolioOptimizer` and `findReplacementCandidates`, whose descriptions currently describe a *different endpoint*. Covers B1, B6.
- **D-05 — Re-derive every flow evidence string.** `resync` must stop claiming "The SEQUENTIAL-retry class is closed" — it is open for the 15s-timeout sub-case (the worker's 30s tick advances SV#1 to `validated` with ~40% probability inside the blip, the `status='draft'` pre-check misses, a 2nd draft row is inserted; still exactly one job, index holds, self-heals). `onboard` must stop stating a conditional as fact and instead record **why** `wizard_session_id` is guaranteed non-NULL (the `isUuid` 400 gate at `create-with-key:194`, the two stamping RPCs, the 7-day draft purge), so a future NULL producer trips the recorded reasoning. Both must qualify "exactly one SV row and one job" as holding only while the job is in the 3 indexed statuses (`failed_retry` is outside the index predicate). Covers B2, B3, B4.
- **D-06 — Fix rotted citations and stop them rotting again.** The migration id on both flow verdicts is wrong (`20260418194206` creates the **allocator** index; the real one is `20260416125430` — prefer citing the constraint name, which does not rot). Stale line coords: `resilient-fetch.ts:502`→`548`, `:1934-1937`→`1953-1955`, `keys/sync/route.ts:563-599`→`610-625`. Add `src/lib/seam-retry-registry.ts` to `SEAM_CITATION_SURFACE` (`seam-citations.invariant.test.ts:106`) — the repo's existing guard banning bare `file:line` on the seam surface — and convert coordinates to symbol anchors. The most citation-dense file in the phase being outside that guard is exactly how these shipped. Covers B5, B7, B8.
- **D-07 — Correct now-false prose in shipped code and fixtures.** Three comments in `resilient-fetch.ts` (`:1482`, `:1919`, `:2001`) still say every `SEAM_BUDGETS` row carries `retries: 0`; five rows are 1. `:553-559` overstates the belt ("NOT what turns retry on" is true only for `postProcessKey`, which always passes an explicit override). `test_resync_retry_single_job.sql:40` claims all fixture work runs inside `BEGIN…ROLLBACK`, but the 4 pre-clean DELETEs precede `BEGIN;` and commit under psql autocommit — a false invariant claim inside a file whose purpose is pinning invariants. Covers B9, B10.

### C. Registry-bypass axis

- **D-08 — Close the transport-boundary fallback.** `retriesOverride?: number` is optional and falls back to `SEAM_BUDGETS[key].retries` (`resilient-fetch.ts:1921`), so "absence ⇒ no-retry by construction" holds only inside the two client wrappers that spell `?? 0`. A new `resilientFetch("process-key-enqueue", …, { body: { flow_type: "teaser" }})` inherits `retries: 1` with the registry never consulted, and it typechecks (`validate-and-encrypt:221` is a live precedent for hand-picking a budget key while hand-writing a flow_type). No live caller today. Close it by making `retriesOverride` **required** `0 | 1` and dropping the row fallback, **or** by exposing `retriesForFlow` / `retriesForAnalytics` accessors that hold the single `?? 0` — implementation choice is the planner's, but the axis must be closed by construction, not by convention. Covers C1.
- **D-09 — Add a call-site census on the retry axis.** `resilient-fetch.wiring.test.ts` exists precisely so an unclassified NEW call site cannot "be discovered by nothing" for budget keys (`EXPECTED_BINDINGS` B-1…B-13). Phase 141 added a **second** classification axis with no equivalent census. **The fix pattern already exists in-repo and was not applied — this is the instance-not-class shape that has bitten this codebase before.** Extend the existing census rather than inventing a new mechanism. Covers C2.
- **D-10 — Pin the belt's INDEPENDENCE, not just its value.** MEASURED: deleting BOTH `retriesOverride` lines (all of plan 141-04's wiring) leaves **558 tests green across 11 files**, because the rows were flipped to mirror the registry exactly. Add a test that puts row and registry in **disagreement** (force `process-key-sync.retries = 1`, assert the teaser is still single-fetch). Covers C3.
- **D-11 — Freeze the maps and make exhaustiveness real.** `RETRY_SAFE_FLOW_TYPES` / `RETRY_SAFE_ANALYTICS` are exported mutable with a type annotation, not `as const satisfies`: `RETRY_SAFE_FLOW_TYPES.teaser = {retries:1, evidence:""}` and `delete …onboard` both typecheck clean. `FlowType` exhaustiveness is not truly pinned (both sides hand-typed to the same list of 4), and `budgetKeyFor` is a ternary with fall-through, so a 5th flow silently lands on `process-key-enqueue` (row = 1). The docblock at `:37-39` claims an enforcement that does not exist. Either make the claim true or delete it. Covers C4.

### D. Test hardening — every fix gets a regression test that fails without it

- **D-12 — Pin the per-attempt deadline and attempt-2 request identity.** MEASURED: hoisting the per-attempt deadline above the loop leaves **125 tests green**, yet in production that mutation is severe and silent — attempt 2 fires with an already-fired abort signal, making the retry a **no-op for the timeout class** (the dominant Railway-blip class) while the mock still sees 2 `fetch` calls. Add `calls[0][1].signal !== calls[1][1].signal`, a fake-timer case proving attempt 2 gets a fresh full budget, and `expect(calls[1]).toEqual(calls[0])` minus `signal` (same URL / Authorization / X-Service-Key / body) — which closes attempt-2 identity and catches the deadline mutation together. Covers D1, D7.
- **D-13 — Pin the sleep→re-check ordering.** MEASURED: swapping `sleep()` and the pre-attempt-2 `isBreakerOpen` re-check leaves 21/21 green, though the comment claims the order is load-bearing (the breaker may open DURING the backoff via a concurrent caller). The harness already has `shared.mode.staleReadOnce` to script it. Covers D2.
- **D-14 — Close the vacuous and missing test cases.** `seam-constants.pin.test.ts:335` uses `it.each(Object.keys(RETRY_SAFE_ANALYTICS))` — emptying the map yields ZERO cases and stays green; add a length assertion. Add literal pins for `SEAM_RETRY_BACKOFF_MS` / `SEAM_RETRY_JITTER_MAX_MS` alongside every sibling constant. Add a BEHAVIOUR pin for the `status='draft'` filter: a COMPLETED resync verification exists ⇒ a genuinely new resync must still queue, not answer `WIZARD_DUPLICATE`. Add a `bridge` case at its production configuration in `resilient-fetch.test.ts`. Covers D3, D4, D5, D6.

### E. Accounting

- **D-15 — Recharge SC-4b for the retried leg.** SC-4b's `storeMs` term was not multiplied by `(1 + retries)` though `requestMs` was, and `STORE_COMMANDS_PER_SEAM_CALL.failing = 3` is stale — a retried failing call issues up to **6** commands (attempt-1 mget + trip get/set, pre-attempt-2 mget, attempt-2 get/set). That is a ~12,750ms under-charge per retried leg, in the **unsafe** direction. No ceiling is breached (worst route ~56,000ms vs 300,000ms), so this is an accounting correction, not a limit change. The file's own guard comment says "a future edit that adds a store round trip has to come back here" — 141 was that edit. Also correct `:81` ("with every row at `retries: 0`"). Covers E1.

### F. Observability / ops

- **D-16 — Correct the flag-monitor denominator bias.** `cron/flag-monitor/route.ts:49-51` is the repo's only error-rate alert (`ALERT=0.005`, 15-min window, emails founder): numerator = Sentry `level:error`, denominator = `audit_log` rows. A transient failure that now succeeds on attempt 2 emits zero Sentry events and zero 5xx while the denominator is unchanged, so **retries suppress the alert precisely during the degradation that triggers them**. Compounding: the `process_key.entry` audit emit at `process_key.py:1002` precedes BOTH dedup pre-checks, so a retried onboard/resync **deterministically** writes a 2nd `entity_type='process_key'` row into that denominator even on the duplicate short-circuit, biasing `errorRate` downward. Also fix `path:/api/process-key`, a route that does not exist under `src/app/api/`. Covers F1, F5.
- **D-17 — Stop discarding attempt-1's diagnosis.** The counting-5xx arm (`resilient-fetch.ts:2154-2163`) has no `console.error` and no Sentry capture — it does `recordOnce` then `continue`, and the discarded attempt-1 diagnosis vanishes; pre-threshold degradation is now materially quieter than on 2026-07-30 with nothing recording the shift. Separately, when attempt 1's own failure is the threshold-th, attempt 2's re-check throws `CircuitOpenError` and the caller loses attempt 1's 503 body (`dependency`, `human_message`) — the freshest diagnosis, which the caller *did* receive pre-141. Log the discarded status alongside the throw. Covers F2, F4.
- **D-18 — Give the seam breaker an ops surface.** 25 runbooks exist in `docs/runbooks/`; the Vercel→Railway breaker appears in none. The only "circuit breaker" hit is `compute-queue.md:160`, a **different** breaker (Python per-API-key 429 cooldown), so on-call gets the wrong mental model. Document fail-open semantics, the Upstash keys, `seam.breaker.open`, the up-to-2-attempts behaviour, the `Retry-After` fail-fast rule from D-01, and the D-03 doubled-compute cost. Covers F3.

### G. Undischarged obligations booked to 141

- **D-19 — Discharge or explicitly re-book every 141 obligation.** `TODOS.md:121` (TS-35 W-4: 141 converts `recoverable` from a render hint into an automated retry input, so the `unknown ⇒ true` polarity must be RE-DERIVED at that moment — no re-derivation artifact exists); `TODOS.md:365` (LO-02 / TS-39, `decodeBreakerLock` unbounded span → `Retry-After: 1e17`, routed to 141, not done); `TODOS.md:483` now factually false ("the path 141 adds (unreachable today)" — 141 added it); `TODOS.md` has **no phase-141 deferrals section**, unlike every prior v1.16 phase; `141-REVIEW.md` was left untracked — the phase's own review artifact never committed. Covers G1, G2, G3, G4, G5.
- **D-20 — Version/CHANGELOG at ship time.** CHANGELOG/VERSION/package.json are untouched. This matches `CONTRIBUTING.md:68-71` (ship-time step), but 140 earned `[0.50.0.0]` and 140.3 `[0.50.1.0]`, and 141 turns retry ON for five budgets — so the bump belongs in the same commit as the ship, per project convention. Covers G6.
