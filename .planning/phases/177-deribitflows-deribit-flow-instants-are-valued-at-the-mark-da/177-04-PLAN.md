<objective>
Complete D-05's failure vocabulary in the derive, each disposition RED first: a failed read keeps the day's mark and
retries, a last-attempt 429 is stamped once, a structural no-data refuses by name, a store fault retries, a prediction
miss retries, and this derive's own mark is part of the prediction.

D-05 verbatim from CONTEXT: "No fallback: a structural no-data read refuses as `mark_flow_index_unavailable`; a
transient read retries; a 429 re-raises to `_stamp_429`." And from the coordinator brief carried in CONTEXT D-02: "A
failed read is a named refusal or retry in the house vocabulary, never a silent fallback that writes a wrong number."

Known limit, accepted under D-05 and stated rather than hidden (RESEARCH Open Question 8, settled by the orchestrator):
a moved flow in a minute where the perpetual printed NO candle refuses `mark_flow_index_unavailable` on every derive,
indefinitely, because such a minute has no price to store. There is no recovery path in this phase (no override, no
fallback); the plausible cases are thinly traded `{CCY}_USDC-PERPETUAL` minutes of non-BTC/ETH coins (RESEARCH A5). It
goes in this plan's SUMMARY under known limits.

Budget (plan check, scope_sanity W2): the prefetch runs after the full-history crawl, so plan 01's fixed
`_BROKER_CRAWL_TIMEOUT_S` (810 s) bound, plus up to 255 s of reader backoff, could outrun the 900 s outer budget and
lose the day's mark. Task 1 bounds it by the remaining derive budget minus `_DERIVE_POST_CRAWL_RESERVE_S`, the way the
MTM second pass bounds itself (`_mtm_remaining`, `_MTM_SECOND_PASS_MIN_SECONDS`), and captures `timeout` below a floor.
The floor `_MOVED_FLOW_PRICE_MIN_SECONDS` is 10.0 s (Claude's discretion). It is not a latency measurement, and
correctness does not rest on it: a read the bound cuts off is captured as `timeout` and the mark is kept either way. The
floor only avoids starting a read that cannot finish one attempt.

Purpose: SC-2's refusal half and SC-3's store-fault half. Output: the capture, the token, the seam split, and the tests.
</objective>
