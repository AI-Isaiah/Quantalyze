## Founder follow-up: MM-2x's actual shape (2026-10-08, from his MT5 screenshots)

1. **Exact oracle.** `test_mm2x_shape_every_small_balance_day_is_measured_and_exact` builds the ledger in `Fraction`: deposit 0.1 (2025-10-16), deposit 1.0, five trading days, a near-total withdrawal (derived so the ledger closes exactly to 0.00961461), then ten small-balance trading days with P&L from 2e-5 to 5e-4. Expected return per day is `pnl / prior NAV` in `Fraction`. Every day after the second ledger day is measured (never `dust_nav_guard` NaN) and matches to 1e-12. Two lower-balance variants of the same shape (terminal 5e-4 and 3e-6 BTC, trades scaled) are measured too; those are the ones the old floor guarded.
2. **Pre-inception.** The reconstruction's index starts on the first ledger day (deposits and trades are unioned into it, `gap_fill_daily_returns` reindexes only `[first, last]`), so no row exists for any earlier calendar day. Day 0's base is `NAV_0 - pnl_0 - F_0`, the opening capital, which is zero in theory and only float noise (about 1e-17) or an incomplete ledger leaves a residue R there. Measured sweep of R in {0, 5e-8, 1e-7, 3e-5, 5e-4, 0.02, 0.2}: day 0 is NaN for every R up to the deposit (negative or dust guard for float noise; `flow_dominated_guard` once R >= 1e-7 because the 0.1 deposit is at least the base, `FLOW_DOM_RATIO` 1.0), and for R = 0.2 (larger than the deposit) it is exactly 0.0, a deposit-only day with no P&L. **The code does not emit a residue-return at 1e-7**, so the stop condition did not trigger. The range the old floor hid as dust (3e-5, 5e-4) is stopped by the flow guard instead. `test_no_return_is_emitted_before_the_first_deposit` pins it for all seven residues.
3. **Floors against MM-2x's scale** (balance 9.6e-3, smallest trade 2e-5): `material_equity` 1e-4 is an account-level equity test (the balance is 96 times it) that never looks at a single trade, so it cannot drop a day. `residual_abs_tol` 1e-6 is a whole-series roll self-check on float residuals of about 1e-16; it drops no day, and a dropped 2e-5 flow is 20 times the band so it still raises (`test_reconcile_residual_tolerance_catches_the_smallest_mm2x_trade`). Neither distorts a real MM-2x day. Neither changed.
