## SFH-1 unpriced native leg

**Finding:** a native-unit (BTC) leg with no BTC price source dropped out of a blend silently while the UI said it was blended.

**Cause:** `readBtcCloses` returned null on an empty or all-dropped price read with no log. The returns route sent `daily_returns_usd: []`, the dashboard gave the book row `daily_returns = []` with `series_state` derived before conversion ("available"), and the composer's warm-up gate excluded the empty series while still printing "Blended in USD at the daily BTC price."

**What changed** (commit `0ba0eb9b0`):

- `src/lib/factsheet/native-to-usd.ts`: new `isNativeLegUnpriced(series, unit, converted)`, true only for a native unit with a series of two or more points whose conversion priced nothing. It covers both "no price source" and "closes cover none of the series' days". A native leg with no series stays `series_state`'s absence. The conversion math is untouched, and there is no fallback to raw BTC returns.
- `src/app/api/strategies/[id]/returns/route.ts`: the body carries `native_unpriced: boolean` (always present; false for USD).
- `src/lib/queries.ts`: `getMyAllocationDashboard` strategy rows carry `native_unpriced` (optional on the type, always set by the producer). The conversion runs once per row, so the series and the flag come from the same call. `series_state` is unchanged.
- `ScenarioComposer.tsx`: reads the flag (strict `=== true`, book wins whole, written on settle, purged on remove). An unpriced native row renders a muted `text-xs text-text-muted` note, `No BTC price available, so this strategy is left out of the USD blend.` (`data-testid="scenario-detail-usd-unpriced-{id}"`), in the blend line's slot, instead of the blend line. The chip and the `CAGR IN BTC` eyebrow stay.
- `src/lib/factsheet/benchmark-source.ts`: `readBtcCloses` logs one `console.error` (`[benchmark-source] BTC closes unusable: ... dropped=N`) when it returns null with no read error.
