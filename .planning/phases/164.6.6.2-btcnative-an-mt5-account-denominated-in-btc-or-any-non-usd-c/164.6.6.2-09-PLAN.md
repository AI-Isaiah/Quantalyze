Convert a BTC strategy's series to USD at the server seams that feed blends: the allocator

the composer's lazily-added legs (plan 11 blends what it serves).

conversion is called, so no surface blends raw BTC returns with USD ones. D-18's arithmetic is the
function from plan 08; this plan only calls it.

Output: server-side BTC closes helper, the converted dashboard payload with `returns_unit`, the
returns-route field, the portfolio-page conversion, and parity tests.
