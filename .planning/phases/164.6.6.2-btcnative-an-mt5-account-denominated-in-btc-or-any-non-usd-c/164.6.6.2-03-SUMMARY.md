## Deviations from Plan

**1. [Plan premise] Tracer equity is 0.1105, not 0.0105.** The plan says equity 0.0105 with the canonical deals scaled by 1e-6. That anchor gives an initial NAV of `0.0105 - 0.0005 - 0.01 = 0`, so no hand-literal series can exist. 0.1105 is the canonical 110_500 times 1e-6 and gives the plan's stated oracle (the USD canonical series). The persisted-balance assertion is therefore 0.1105. Files: `tests/test_mt5_derive_branch.py`. Commit `59ac608f0`.

**2. [Rule 3 - blocking] Two suites outside `files_modified` needed a fixture-only `currency`.** Removing the USD-sized floor from the settle wait makes a currency-less `account_info` skip the wait (correct), which failed `tests/test_equity_reconstruction.py` (4 tests via `_mt5_account`) and `tests/test_mt5_handover.py` (1 test). Added `"currency": "USD"` to those two builders, nothing else. **For plan 04:** its read_first says the equity_reconstruction MT5 fixtures "already carry `currency`"; only the `_mt5_account` builder does now. The other literals in that file (login-mismatch, NaN equity, missing balance) still omit it and are unaffected only because they refuse before the settle wait. Commit `23b7e5c22`.

**3. [Census-forced] Curated stamp sentences are inline, not constants** (see Census section). The plan said to add them as module constants "so the census can see them"; the census that enumerates stamp messages reads the call's own literal. `_MT5_CURRENCY_BLANK_MESSAGE`, `_MT5_CURRENCY_CHANGED_MESSAGE` and `_MT5_CURRENCY_REFUSED_MESSAGE` (the `error_message` texts) remain constants. Commit `037fcb328`.
