### Matcher decisions to review

1. **R2 extended to `if (...) return`** (see key-decisions). Self-tested both ways.
2. **Collection sizes are not sign tests.** `CMP_ZERO` skips an operand ending in `length`, `size` or `count`. Without it, `DrawdownChart.tsx`'s `hasScenario = scenarioDrawdownData.length > 0` was an R7 finding although the colour it picks is "scenario overlay or not", not a sign.
3. **R7 ignores colours inside a JSX branch.** `RiskAttribution.tsx`'s `assessable ? <span>…` was a finding because the branch's own `overweight ? "text-negative" : "text-positive"` sits inside it. `assessable` is `share >= 0` as a validity guard (a hedge's share gets no word), and the colour comes from `overweight`, a different comparison. R7 now reads only the colours a boolean picks directly. A boolean that picks between two JSX elements of different colours is therefore not seen by R7; R2 and R7 still scan each element's own expression where it is written.
4. **Import statements are blanked before R5**, so `import { CHART_NEGATIVE, … }` is not a tone. Self-tested.

Decisions 2 and 3 narrow a matcher after a finding. The plan says never widen a matcher to make a real finding pass; both findings were proven not to be sign tests by reading the code, and each exclusion has a red and a green self-test. Flagged here for the orchestrator's review.
