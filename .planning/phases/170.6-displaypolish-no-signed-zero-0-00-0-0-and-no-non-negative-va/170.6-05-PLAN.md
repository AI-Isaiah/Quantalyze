<objective>
Apply the one rule to the factsheet's v2 chart components (ticks, tooltips, labels, the heatmap's printed text).
The two /strategy/[id]/v2 panels that were here moved to plan 14 (plan-check iteration 2, W2: budget).

Founder decisions, quoted from 170.6-CONTEXT.md:
- D-02: "A value that rounds to zero at its display precision prints unsigned and in a neutral tone. `-0.00`, `-0.0%` and `+0.0%` never appear."
- D-03: "Red and green follow the sign of the ROUNDED value, never the raw one. A raw `-0.0004` shown as `0.00` is neutral."
- D-05: "There is one rule, in one place. `src/lib/factsheet/display-sign.ts` owns it."
- D-06: "Charts and text labels follow the same rule: axis ticks, tooltips, the OG card and KPI tiles. Heatmap cell FILLS are unchanged. They encode magnitude, not a printed sign."

Census rows owned (170.6-01-PLAN.md "Phase census"): B — TimeSeriesChart 3, HistogramChart 1,
SignaturePanels 1, CrossSignaturePanels 1, HeatmapPanels 2; C — HeatmapPanels 2, TimeSeriesChart bar fill 1.
Unchanged by D-06 (row E): heatmap cell fills, regime bands, event-signature `tone` props, streak colours.

magnitude tint, so D-03 governs it: it takes the tone of the value at the precision its tooltip prints,
and a bar whose tooltip reads a rounded zero is drawn in the neutral muted colour.
