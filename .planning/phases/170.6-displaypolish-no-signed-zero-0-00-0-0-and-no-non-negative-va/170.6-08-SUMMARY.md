The case asserts `/^[+-]?0\.00%$/` plus the colour (the part this plan owns, RED before and GREEN after). **Once plan 02 is merged, the text is `0.00%`; the orchestrator can tighten the regex to the literal in the merged tree.** The `+1.00%` and `-3.00%` cases assert the exact strings.
