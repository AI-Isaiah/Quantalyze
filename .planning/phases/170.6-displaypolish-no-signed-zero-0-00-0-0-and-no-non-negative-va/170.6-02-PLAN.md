<objective>
Make `src/lib/utils.ts`, the third module D-05 names, delegate to the one rule, and move its tone helper
onto the rounded sign at every caller. Fix the factsheet tearsheet's own monthly cells and Max DD hero, and
the portfolio benchmark comparator panel's private copy of the tone helper.

Founder decisions, quoted from 170.6-CONTEXT.md:
- D-02: "A value that rounds to zero at its display precision prints unsigned and in a neutral tone. `-0.00`, `-0.0%` and `+0.0%` never appear."
- D-03: "Red and green follow the sign of the ROUNDED value, never the raw one. A raw `-0.0004` shown as `0.00` is neutral."
- D-04: "Max drawdown is never green."
- D-05: "… `src/lib/utils.ts` delegate[s] to it and do[es] not re-implement it."

Judgment recorded for the orchestrator: `utils.ts` is shared app-wide (`formatPercent` has 114 calls in
40 files, `formatNumber` 44). Delegating it means a rounded zero loses its sign on every page that uses
it, not only on factsheets. That is what D-02's "never appear" and D-05's "delegate" say, so this plan
does it (orchestrator call O-1), Task 1 sweeps the tests for pinned signed zeros with `grep -a`, and the
full suite in plan 13 catches anything the sweep missed.

Purpose: SC-3 ("`format.ts` (both) and `utils.ts` delegate to `display-sign.ts`; no second implementation remains").
The bridge-outcome label's private `toneOf` (raw `ratio > 0` / `ratio < 0`) is folded in here by plan-check
iteration 3 (W-A): it is D-03's own example (a raw -0.0004 printed `0.0%` and drawn red by
`src/app/(dashboard)/allocations/components/OutcomeRecordedRow.tsx`), in scope per O-7, and its file pairs with
the case 13 pin this plan already moves. `deriveOutcomeStatusPill` is untouched: its win/loss pill prints no
figure (census table G).

Output: utils delegating, the outcome label's tone on the rounded sign, `metricColor` taking the printed unit and precision, its 13 calls and the
BenchmarkComparison copy migrated, the tearsheet tones fixed.
</objective>
