## ⭐ `[VAULTTICK-EMPTYKEY-01]` deserves separate notice

It is a defect in the guard **this phase shipped**, on the callable **Phase 164.5.1 is about to
point the live cron row at**. `match_engine_cron_tick()` compares `v_key = ''` where it means
"empty"; a vault secret of pure whitespace is not `''`, so it passes and is sent as an
`Authorization` header, producing a 401 against the analytics service. ⚠️ Phase 164.5.1 should know
this before it repoints jobid 1 at that function — a 401 there is precisely the failure mode
`CRON-DRIFT-01` exists because of (a PROD cron job 401'd hourly for seven days behind a green cron
history). Routed to 164.5.1's attention in `164.7-07-SUMMARY.md`.
