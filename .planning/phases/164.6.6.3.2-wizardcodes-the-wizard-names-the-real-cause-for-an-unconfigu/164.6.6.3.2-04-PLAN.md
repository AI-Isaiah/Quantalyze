  `supabase/schema/functions/claim_compute_jobs.sql`). So the handler's `job` dict carries the

  `compute_intro_snapshot` (RESEARCH A4, measured here with a red-first test).

  that, so this plan adds no SQL.

  `grep -rn 'reconstruct_allocator_history' src`. This is not a gap: nothing in the wizard can show it.

  reachable on the single-key strategy-mode path. Re-measure with
  `grep -n 'Mt5HistoryUnsettledError' analytics-service/services/job_worker.py`; expect only the
  derive arm and its import.

  `MT5_HISTORY_NOT_SETTLED_COMPUTATION_ERROR`.
