## Decisions Made

1. **`FRONTIER_EXEMPT_CEILING = 3`, on an observed 0.** Pinning at the observed value (0) would red every migration-adding PR; pinning above the ceiling the founder framed (~3) would widen the hole. 3 is the smallest value that tolerates the ordinary apply-on-merge artefact.
2. **Bare `NAME=<digits>`, never `${…:-3}`, never `local`.** Two independent reasons, both recorded in the constant's comment: `findThresholdSites` only recognises that spelling (so any other form escapes SC-9 entirely), and an env-overridable ceiling hands a runner a knob that raises the tolerance without a reviewed diff — the exact inverse of `WAIVED_CEILING`'s intent.
3. **The two-layer rule is scoped honestly rather than faked.** `mutation-runner-floors.test.ts` catches a stale-low floor by re-deriving the corpus from disk. That is impossible here: the exempt count is a property of a live shared ledger. The comment and the `KNOWN_THRESHOLD_SITES` entry both say so plainly instead of implying a coverage the tests do not have.
4. **The vitest `EXPECTED_ARMS` pin derives the count and enforces prose agreement** rather than hardcoding the sibling's needle. Rationale in the block's own comment: a hardcoded needle reds on any bump but says nothing about whether the dated sentence beside the constant moved with it — and that drift is precisely the defect the sibling had to add its agreement leg to catch.
