that calls `initialize()` + `terminal_info()` (never a log-in) and classifies `-10004` (bridge not

the pinned floor of four.

green `/health` and a gateway log with no ERROR line; the only instrument was a human clicking
connect. The arm makes that state loud within one hourly tick and names which of the two remedies
applies.

Output: `arms/mt5.mjs`, `realSshRunner`, six fixtures, the final four-arm registry, and
`--self-test` at 50/50.
