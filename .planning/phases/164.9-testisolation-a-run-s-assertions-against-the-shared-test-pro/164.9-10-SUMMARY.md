## Deviations from Plan

### 1. [Rule 3 — blocking] The plan's ratchet number was stale; the LIVE value was read by symbol

**Found during:** Task 1. **Plan said:** 416 → 417. **Shipped:** 422 → 423. Plan
04 landed six further arms in a fix round after this plan was written. Reading
the constant by symbol is the repo's own standing rule and it is what caught it.

### 2. [Rule 1 — bug] D1's twin NARROWS the allow-list; it does not DROP the constraint

**Found during:** Task 1, before the first run. **The plan asked for** a `sql`
step dropping the allow-list constraint — "the same lever the file's existing
constraint arm already uses". **That cannot work.** The drop is arm **U2's** twin,
U2 lives in Part 1 and runs FIRST, and the runner requires the FIRST
`TEST FAILED (…)` to name the arm under test. Two arms cannot share one mutation:
whichever runs first steals the bite, and the other scores `WRONG-ARM`.

The shipped twin swaps the CHECK for one admitting deployed hosts but **not** the
loopback sink. Measured consequences: U2 still gets its 23514 on a foreign host
(green); arm C1's loopback INSERT happens after Part 1 has dropped the constraint
itself (green); D1's "the allow-list admits the sink" leg reddens alone. An
`edit`-kind twin was also rejected: the allow-list literal is spelled THREE times
in `20260907120000` and its own verify block asserts all three are each other, so
an edit aborts the apply and the runner scores a defect rather than a bite.

### 3. [Rule 3 — blocking] The completion sentinel stays at 13, and the plan did not anticipate it

**Found during:** Task 1. Raising the sentinel to 14 would require editing
`.github/workflows/ci.yml`'s per-file arm derivation **and** its two mirrors
(`src/__tests__/drift-check-scripts.test.ts`,
`src/__tests__/contracts/ci-anti-skip-gate.contract.test.ts`). ⛔ `ci.yml` is
owned by a concurrent sibling executor this wave and is explicitly out of bounds.
D1 is pinned by its machine-executable twin and by the runner's own arm ratchet —
a different instrument — and Part 2's completion notice deliberately avoids the
`ARMS EXECUTED` phrasing so the sentinel read stays unambiguous. Recorded in the
gate file itself so the next reader meets the reasoning rather than the gap.

### 4. [Rule 2 — missing critical functionality] The third branch could never have been taken

Covered in full under **"The RED that was not manufactured"** above. Keyed on
`NULL` alone, it was dead code on every lane. Now three spellings, with the stock
comment matched EXACTLY rather than by substring so a hand-set marker that
happened to contain that sentence is not mistaken for an unmarked cluster.

### 5. [Rule 1 — bug] The self-test's mutex arm was killing the client, not the backend

**Found during:** Task 2, by a self-test that went RED in eleven places at once.
`kill` + `wait` reaps the psql CLIENT; the BACKEND sits inside `pg_sleep` still
holding the advisory lock, because the server does not notice a departed client
until it next tries to write. Every arm after it refused on a mutex nobody was
holding any more, each failing for a reason that had nothing to do with what it
was asking. Fixed by terminating the backend **by the lock it holds** and then
WAITING for the lock to go — and that wait is now its own named check, so the
failure cannot recur silently.

### 6. [deliberate] Two disclosure-shaped literals survive in the remediation script

The plan's Task 2 `<verify>` requires **zero** host-, connection-string- or
home-path-shaped strings on the added lines. Two survive, both in the
**self-test** and both safe by construction, with the reasoning recorded in-file:

⛔ **Neither is QUOTED here, and that is the rule doing its job: a proof of
absence must not publish what it proves absent.** They are named by location and
by kind; read them in `scripts/test-only-normalize-analytics-url.sh`'s `self_test`
function, where each carries its own ⚠️ note.

- **A loopback DSN**, for a cluster the self-test itself just created on a
  freshly-allocated port with trust auth. No password, no host that exists after
  the process exits. `scripts/restore-test-from-baseline.sh`'s own self-test uses
  the identical shape for the identical reason.
- **A deliberately SYNTHETIC hostname** under the allow-listed suffix. The fixture

⛔ **Neither is spelled in pieces to dodge the grep.** A scan you route around is a
