<objective>
ROADMAP criterion 2 asks for a calibration: seed a FOREIGN row and show the assertion stays GREEN
where a global one would have gone RED for someone else's work.

⭐ RESEARCH measured that this criterion is SMALLER than the ROADMAP implies and that its shape is
different. Most of the SQL gate corpus is already isolated by construction — each gate wraps its
writes in a per-part transaction that rolls back and scopes every assertion to locally generated
fixture ids, so a foreign row cannot reach it. Only a handful EXECUTE a real deployed global
`ORDER BY … LIMIT` sweep, and one of those is a false positive for this concern. For the three that
remain, the mitigation ALREADY EXISTS as a seeding discipline — seed this run's rows at an extreme
timestamp so they dominate the deployed budget regardless of foreign candidates.

**What is missing is the PROOF, not the mechanism.** Nothing in the repo today seeds a competing
foreign row and shows the assertion survives. This plan supplies exactly that, and supplies the
mutation that proves the proof can fail.

Purpose: make "isolated" a measured property of these three gates instead of an unproven claim
about a passing test.

Output: one foreign-row calibration part and one calibrated mutation arm per gate, plus a pin sweep
that moves only the ratchets that must move.
</objective>
