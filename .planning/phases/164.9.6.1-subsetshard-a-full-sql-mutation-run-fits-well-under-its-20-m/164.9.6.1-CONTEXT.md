<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### D-01 Split mechanism (founder, AskUserQuestion 2026-10-03: "In-job concurrency")
- The mutation runner runs arm lanes CONCURRENTLY inside the existing single `sql-mutation` job.
  The target is 4 lanes in flight (ubuntu-latest has 4 vCPUs), which should bring the mutate step
  to about 5-6 min.
- There is still ONE job, ONE runner log and ONE floor/ratchet verdict, with no matrix and no
  artifact merge.
- The runner prints its concurrency on a new `lane-concurrency:` line.
- The two independent arm tallies (`arms:` executed vs `lane-invocations:`) must still agree under
  concurrency, and `biting` must still count each arm exactly once.
- Each concurrent lane keeps its own throwaway PostgreSQL cluster. No two lanes may share a data
  directory, port or socket.
- Applies to EVERY full run: PRs, FULL pushes to main and the nightly. SUBSET runs use the same
  runner, so they get the same concurrency.

### D-02 Drift-tolerant predecessor (founder, AskUserQuestion 2026-10-03: "Drift rows only")
- `predecessorVerdict` (`scripts/classify-changed-paths.mjs`) treats a red predecessor as GREEN only
  when BOTH of these hold:
  - every failing check run on it is `sql-gate-lint` or the `frontend` aggregator;
  - `sql-gate-lint`'s job log shows only `content-drift — DRIFT <fn>` rows as its errors, plus the
    fixed summary and exit lines.
- Anything else stays RED and forces FULL with the reason named. That includes:
  - a cancelled or failed `sql-mutation`, or any other failing check;
  - a log that cannot be read or is truncated;
  - any other `::error::` line;
  - an `allowlist-*` finding.
- The tolerated case prints its own reason (for example "predecessor red only with tolerated
  baseline drift: <fn list>") on the `scope-reason:` line.

### D-03 Time guard (founder, AskUserQuestion 2026-10-03: "No guard")
- No new wall-time warning or failure is added. The 20-minute `timeout-minutes` stays the only
  limit.

### Claude's Discretion
- How to implement concurrency in `run.mjs`, for example async spawn with a bounded pool replacing
  the serial `spawnSync` loop. It must keep output deterministic: per-arm results are collected and
  printed in corpus order, never interleaved.
- Whether concurrency is a flag or a constant (`--concurrency N`, defaulting to the CPU count capped
  at 4), and how the self-test proves the parallel and serial runs agree.
- How `predecessorVerdict` fetches the `sql-gate-lint` job log (the `gh api` job-logs endpoint,
  with `GH_TOKEN` already reaching the step via `env:`), and its self-test rows.
