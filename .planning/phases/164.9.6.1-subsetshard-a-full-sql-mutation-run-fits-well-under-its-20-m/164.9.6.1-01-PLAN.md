<objective>
Make the SQL mutation runner run gate files concurrently inside the existing single `sql-mutation`
job, per D-01, so a FULL run's mutate step drops from about 12.9-14.3 min of serial lanes toward
about half of that, with no change to the 20-minute cap (D-03).

Purpose: two consecutive FULL pushes to main were cancelled at the 20-minute timeout (9c1dc3137,
b2c59c720). Every lane today is a serial `spawnSync`. RESEARCH measured the recommended
file-level pool at 2.94x locally (45.2 s serial vs 15.4 s at four in flight, identical verdicts).
Output: an async runner with a bounded file pool, per-worker ports, ordered output, a
`lane-concurrency:` line, and tests proving serial and parallel runs agree.

Derived requirements this plan serves (no REQUIREMENTS.md IDs were assigned; plan 03 writes them into
the ROADMAP): SS-1 FULL mutate step about half the serial time under the unchanged cap; SS-2 tallies
agree and biting counts once; SS-3 deterministic corpus-order output; SS-4 lane isolation; SS-5 the
`lane-concurrency:` line.

(1) `runLane` becomes `async`. (3) `runCorpus` becomes `export async function runCorpus({` and gains one parameter, `concurrency = defaultLaneConcurrency()`, inside its own parameter list (the CLI never passes it). Reject a non-integer or `< 1` value with a thrown usage error. Then:
- Await the probe lane BEFORE the pool starts (its result gates `lane-blocked-stale`).
- Allocate N distinct ports in-process with `node:net` (`createServer().listen(0, "127.0.0.1")`, read the port, close, repeat until N distinct values).
