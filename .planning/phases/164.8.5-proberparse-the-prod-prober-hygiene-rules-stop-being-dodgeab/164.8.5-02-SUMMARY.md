## Live-behaviour note the next reader needs

Three NEW ways for the hourly prober to go red, all of them refusals of the ORACLE rather than
verdicts about PROD:

1. A PROD job whose `username` or `database` moves away from the manifest is now `cron-drift`. That
   is the point — it is a privilege change — but it is a new red.
2. A manifest row missing `active`, `schedule`, `username` or `database` is now `manifest-invalid`
   and **no drift verdict is issued**. A hand-trimmed manifest stops the comparison instead of
   quietly agreeing with PROD.
3. A manifest row whose `command` is edited without re-capturing its sha is now `manifest-invalid`.
   ⚠️ This is the one a well-meaning human will hit: tidying whitespace or a comment in the committed
   command text is now a refusal. The remedy is in the message — re-capture, or use the withhold
   procedure. Normalisation is `ws-collapse-v1`, so pure whitespace changes still hash equal; a
   comment or a rename does not.

In all three the PROD credential scan still runs and still reports (section (0), plan 01), which is
now proved for the ABSENT, STALE, marker-MISMATCHED **and HAND-EDITED** oracle states.
