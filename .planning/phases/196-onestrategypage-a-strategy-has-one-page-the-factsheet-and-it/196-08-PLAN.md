<objective>
Make `/browse` session-aware so a signed-in user never sees a visitor prompt there: the header swaps Log in / Sign up for "Go to Discovery", and the root and category sign-up prompts hide.

Purpose: ROUTE-02 "a signed-in user never sees 'Sign up' prompts on /browse". The detail-page prompts went with plan 02's redirects and the deletion of their pages (plan 17 for `/browse/[slug]/[strategyId]`, plan 18 for `/strategy/[id]`); this plan covers the layout, the root and the category page (RESEARCH open question 11 adds the root's "Get Started Free").
Output: a cached session helper, a session-aware layout with a client "Go to Discovery" link, two page edits and their tests.
</objective>
