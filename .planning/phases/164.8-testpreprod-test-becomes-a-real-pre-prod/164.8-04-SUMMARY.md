## The halt, and how it ended

**This section is history now. Every refusal it records was correct at the time, and one of them was vindicated rather than merely tolerated.**

Task 2's amendment was CONDITIONAL delegation — *"Delegate, but only if TEST is measurably quiet"* — with three conditions on HOW the measurement is taken: acquire the shared-TEST advisory lock FIRST, probe while holding it, count `idle in transaction` as ACTIVE. All three need a session-mode Postgres connection to TEST, and the executor had none (`TEST_SUPABASE_DB_URL` exists only as a GitHub repo secret; `.env.test.local` and `.env.local` carry no DSN; `~/.pgpass` and `~/.pg_service.conf` do not exist; the `quantalyze-test` Keychain item is a login account).

Three routes were considered and each refused, and **the refusals stand**:

1. **A PostgREST probe with the service-role key** — refused because every PostgREST request is its own pooled session, so a SESSION advisory lock cannot be held ACROSS the read. Condition (a) is unsatisfiable there by construction, and the probe would have printed a green-looking `0 active sessions` with nothing behind it.
2. **Adding a probe step to CI to reach the secret** — refused as a source edit outside the plan's `files_modified` (Rule 4), and because a CI job releases the mutex at teardown, minutes before a dispatch.
3. **Dispatching anyway on the green preflight** — refused because the preflight proves the *transaction* is correct and says nothing about whether other people's CI is mid-run.

⭐ **Refusal 1 was vindicated by the run that resolved the halt.** The gate, run properly inside the held mutex, printed `active [holder-pid] x1, idle x14` before its zero. Fourteen idle sessions were present on shared TEST at that moment. A pooled REST probe could not have held the lock across that reading, and the discrimination that makes `0 other active` meaningful — excluding this job's own mutex holder BY PID — has no analogue outside the job. The substitute would not merely have been weaker; it would have measured a different database state and called it quiet.

**Task 3's refusal is discharged, not overturned.** It was withheld because the 31 entries were still MEASURED ABSENT, so emptying the file then would have been the mute button its own header forbids. CI run `34274161551` changed that fact and nothing else: the 31 are now measured PRESENT, VAC-08 took its hard-failure branch and printed the remedy `delete these lines`, and deleting them obeys the ratchet rather than silencing it.

**The founder decision is recorded in the file itself**, dated 2026-09-08, in the shape of the 2026-09-07 precedent entry above it, and it names the RESTORE as the cause.
