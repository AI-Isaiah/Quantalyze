## RED calibration — every assertion proven able to fail

Two independent layers, both run on every CI invocation.

**Layer 1 — in-process twins.** Every text predicate goes through `calibrate()`, which asserts the mutation genuinely differs, the predicate holds on the real file, and the predicate **flips** on the mutant. A pin that cannot fail fails the suite by construction, on every run, not only when someone remembers to check.
