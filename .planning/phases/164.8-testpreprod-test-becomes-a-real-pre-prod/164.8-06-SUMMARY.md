The plan's B4 revision was right, and the evidence bore it out rather than merely permitting it.

The verdict does not depend on it alone — the two genuine measurements (the migration appearing in NEITHER VAC-08 list; `0 absent` printed on a named sha) carry it.

**GENERAL — open, and it is a different hole than the one the ROADMAP anticipated.** The restore was schema-only (`BASELINE.md`: 0 data statements).
