# Phase 170.2 Plan 05: CSV masthead venue label (SC-4) Summary

A CSV-ingested strategy with a declared venue now shows `Binance · self-reported` in its factsheet masthead, keyed on the stored `strategies.source = 'csv'` (payload field `source`) and not on `ingestSource`, which reads `api` for every PROD strategy.

## What changed

- **Payload.** `fetch-and-build-payload.ts` selects `source` from `strategies` and passes it beside `supportedExchanges`. `build-payload.ts` accepts `source?: string | null` and emits `...(strategy.source === "csv" ? { source: "csv" as const } : {})` in `common`, in the `returnsUnit` style. `FactsheetCommon.source?: "csv"` is optional so fixtures that spell out the common fields keep type-checking. The `fetch-and-build-payload.test.ts` source-grep tokens are untouched.
- **Masthead (M-1 to M-7).** `FactsheetHeader` computes `venueLabel = payload.source === "csv" ? exchanges : null` (stored order, verbatim, joined `", "`). The label is one `<span data-testid="masthead-venue-label" className="text-caption text-text-secondary">` text node between `OwnershipTag` and the chip line. When it renders, the exchanges member leaves the chip line; an emptied chip line is omitted instead of printing the dash; the amber pill keeps its trigger on the reduced chip list and its `title` drops "exchanges". Mastheads without the label are unchanged.
- **Cache key.** `factsheet-v2-payload-v12` became `v13` in `page.tsx` (with a "Bumped v12→v13" comment line), the two pinned tests (typed by hand) and the keyParts sentence in `fetch-and-build-payload.ts`.
