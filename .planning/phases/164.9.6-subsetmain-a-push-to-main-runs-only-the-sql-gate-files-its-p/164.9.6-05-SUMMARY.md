# Phase 164.9.6 Plan 05: the nightly full-corpus sql-mutation run Summary

A new workflow, `.github/workflows/sql-mutation-nightly.yml`, mutates the FULL SQL gate corpus daily at 03:00 UTC and on dispatch. It runs under its own 45-minute job cap and a 40-minute cap on the mutate step. Its lane and assert steps are byte-for-byte copies of ci.yml's `sql-mutation` steps, and a vitest pin keeps them that way. When the job does not succeed, it stays red, and a separate `report` job files one deduplicated issue carrying the reading. ci.yml's `timeout-minutes: 20` on `sql-mutation` is unchanged.

## What changed

**`.github/workflows/sql-mutation-nightly.yml` (new)**
- Triggers: `schedule` `0 3 * * *` and `workflow_dispatch`, nothing else. There is one top-level `permissions: contents: read` and a `concurrency: sql-mutation-nightly` group that does not cancel a run in progress.
- The header comment covers D-05 (its own 45, and 20 stays ci.yml's ceiling), D-06/D-11 (red plus an issue, and the Railway coupling), why it is a separate file (`nightly.yml`'s preflight is accepted-red), why steps are copied rather than shared (vitest executes ci.yml's job text), and why there is no shared-TEST lock (the lane is a throwaway cluster). It names neither the key number nor any secret.
- Job `sql-mutation-full`, `timeout-minutes: 45`, with no `needs:` and no `if:`:
  - checkout (`persist-credentials: false`) and setup-node, with the same SHA pins as ci.yml;
  - `Record the nightly start time`, which writes `NIGHTLY_START` to `$GITHUB_ENV`;
  - the six lane steps (pg_cron, dblink probe, binaries probe, dead-holder drill, meta-command preflight, runner self-test), with the same names and `run:` text and no `if:`;
  - the mutate step: `timeout-minutes: 40`. Its `env:` is `RUNNER_LOG` plus `SQL_GATE_REASON: "nightly full corpus (${{ github.event_name }} event, sql-mutation-nightly.yml): …"`. Its run is `set -uo pipefail` followed by ci.yml's four FULL-tail lines verbatim;
  - the assert step, copied byte for byte, with `env:` `RUNNER_LOG` only;
  - three `if: always()` steps. One prints the wall time against 2700s, with a `::warning::` at 2160s or more, or `wall time not measured` when the start step did not run. One captures the reading. One uploads `reading.txt` and `wall-time.txt` for 14 days.
- Job `report`: `needs: [sql-mutation-full]`, `if: always() && needs.sql-mutation-full.result != 'success'`, job-level `contents: read` + `issues: write` (the workflow's only write scope), `timeout-minutes: 5`, and no checkout.
  - The download step uses `continue-on-error: true`, so a cancelled run with no artifact is reported in the issue instead of failing here.
  - The github-script step reads `reading.txt`, or writes `no reading captured: the mutation job concluded <result>`. It caps the text at 60,000 chars, deduplicates on `nightly-canary-failure:sql-mutation`, and comments on the open issue or creates `Nightly full SQL mutation run failed on <date>` with labels `nightly-probe` + the dedupe label. The body carries the run URL, the job result, the reading in a fence, and the D-11 sentence.

## Deviations from Plan

1. **[Rule 1] The capture step's "floors from" line does not exist.** `scripts/mutation-runner/run.mjs` prints no `floors from` line; the floors are read by the assert step, and its output goes to the job log, not the runner log. The capture greps the real prefixes (`scope`, `scope-reason`, `coverage`, `arms`, `biting`, `lane-invocations`) plus every line naming `ARMS_FLOOR`/`FILES_FLOOR`/`WAIVED_CEILING`.
2. **[Rule 1] The derivation-read pin was narrowed** to `${{ needs.` expressions and `SQL_GATE_MODE`/`SQL_GATE_FILES` env keys. The first draft matched the substring `needs.` and went red on the real file, because the byte-equal assert body names `needs.changed-paths.outputs.sql_gate_files` inside an error message. A calibration (a `SQL_GATE_MODE` env key) proves the narrowed pin still bites.
3. **[Rule 2] Extra calibrations and pins** beyond `<behavior>`:
   - an unlisted extra step, a raised ci.yml timeout, a push trigger, a secret, a derivation env key, a lane-step `if:`, a write scope lent to the mutation job, and a `success()`-gated capture step;
   - no checkout in `report`, and no permissions block on the mutation job.
4. **The capture step does not `cat` the reading into the job log.** It prints only a line count. Echoing it back would annotate the runner's `::error::` lines a second time.
5. **[Rule 1] Unchecked narrow in the parser** (`c04b06c0a`). The repo-wide narrowing scan in `test-restore-workflow-wiring.test.ts` caught `uses.indexOf("@") + 1`, where a miss gives `0` and silently keys an unpinned action. It now goes through `anchorIndex`. This was found only by the full suite, because the plan's verify list does not include that file.
6. **The worktree pre-commit allow-list was not followed**, as in plans 01, 03 and 04. The checkout is on `feat/164.9.6-subsetmain`, which the orchestrator pinned. HEAD was confirmed attached and not on a protected branch before each commit.
