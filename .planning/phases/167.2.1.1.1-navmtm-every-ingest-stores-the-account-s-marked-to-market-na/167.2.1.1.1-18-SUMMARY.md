## Docs re-read (2026-10-26)

Fetched the three cited Bybit V5 pages. Confirmed: `totalEquity` = sum of asset equity; coin equity = `walletBalance - spotBorrow + unrealisedPnl + Asset Option Value`; `totalWalletBalance` sums `walletBalance` (gross of spot borrow, so with borrowing the two totals are on different bases, and `borrowAmount` includes derivatives liabilities); "All account wide fields are not applicable to isolated margin"; `/v5/account/info` returns `unifiedMarginStatus` (integer) and `marginMode` (ISOLATED_MARGIN, REGULAR_MARGIN, PORTFOLIO_MARGIN); UTA 2.0 integrates inverse, USDT/USDC perps and futures, spot and options, while UTA 1.0 keeps inverse in a separate CONTRACT account. ccxt 4.5.85 `is_unified_enabled` defaults the cached status to 6 when the field is absent (line 1579).

## Ladder (order implemented)

1. A raised read, or no list / non-dict body -> `balance_error` (exception class logged, no text).
2. status not a venue-reported 5 or 6 (int or digit string; bool, float, text, null, missing refused) -> `account_mode_unsupported`.
3. `marginMode` ISOLATED_MARGIN -> `isolated_margin_unsupported`; missing or not a documented mode -> `uta_totals_unreadable`.
4. coin list not a list, or a coin's `borrowAmount` missing or not finite -> `uta_totals_unreadable`; then any non-zero borrow -> `borrow_present`.
5. `totalEquity` / `totalWalletBalance` missing or not finite -> `uta_totals_unreadable`.
6. Only now our wallet is priced (a refused account costs no ticker read): any failed ticker -> `wallet_unpriced`; then `totalWalletBalance` vs our wallet beyond `max(1.0, 0.005 x |our wallet|)` -> `uta_wallet_mismatch`.
7. D-04 `wallet_basis_mismatch` against the derive's own wallet, in `build_mark_read`, unchanged.

## Call count per Bybit key per UTC day

One `fetch_balance()` (ccxt adds its own cached query-api and account-info reads on a fresh exchange object, as before), one `/v5/account/info` read of our own, one `fetch_ticker` per held non-stable coin, no positions read. W3 still skips all of them when the day's mark is stored (tested: zero calls on the second derive).

Plan 11's dry-run call sequence for Bybit: `read_marked_equity_usd(exchange, "bybit")`, then the derive's own USDT wallet figure, then `build_mark_read(..., marked_equity=<tuple>, usdt_wallet_usd=<that figure>)` (pinned by `test_the_dry_run_sequence_for_a_uta_key`).

## What this does and does not accept (coordinator W3)

The derive's Bybit wallet figure is USDT-only, and D-04 compares `totalWalletBalance` (all coins) with it. So an account whose non-USDT collateral (BTC, USDC, inverse-margin coins) exceeds `max(1.0, 0.5% of the derive wallet)` is still refused by name, `wallet_basis_mismatch`. This plan removes the false `positions_unreadable` refusals for USDC-settled, inverse and option holdings; it does NOT make "any mix of collateral" markable. Pinned by `test_non_usdt_collateral_beyond_tolerance_stays_refused_by_name` (a derive-level case) and the `derive-usdt-only` row. The stored-mark tracer uses an account with 6.0 of BTC beside USDT 1600.0 (inside the 8.0 tolerance). The `1600.0` rows in `test_bybit_mark_goes_through_build_mark_read` and the dry-run test are labelled as the arithmetic pass edge, not a figure production passes for the mixed account.
