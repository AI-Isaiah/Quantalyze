<objective>
The compute half of consumption: the factsheet's headline and published flags read the marked series correctly and
carry the marks-start date.

CONTEXT D-12: "The compute writes the marks-start date into the strategy's analytics data, which published factsheets
already read (like `headline_since`). The owner-only marks table is not exposed." D-05: "A day with no mark gets no
marked value and no return of its own. The next stored mark's return covers the whole gap, and the gap days are named."

RESEARCH did not catch this consumer: `run_csv_strategy_analytics` re-densifies every broker series at its load
boundary and turns every absent in-span day into a NaN chain break (MEDIUM-1, DQ-03). Without this plan, one missed
mark after deploy would break the factsheet chain and move `headline_since`, the opposite of D-05. The runner learns
the boundary from the derive's prestamp (plan 07 stamps `marks_covered_from` = apply_marks' `covered_from`), so the two
cannot disagree.

Purpose: SC-2 for the published strategy figures, and the D-12 date the factsheet note (plan 10) reads.
Output: runner changes and one test module.
</objective>
