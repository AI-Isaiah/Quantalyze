<objective>
The storage half of NAVMTM, proven at the database boundary before any writer exists.

CONTEXT D-01: "New append-only table (Recommended). One row per key per day holding NAV, open profit, mark time,
venue and owner. The migration needs migration-reviewer and rls-policy-auditor clean before merge, because PROD
applies automatically after TEST." D-06: "Write-once (Recommended). The first writer of a day wins. Rebuilds read
marks and never replace them. Key deletion follows the existing key-delete cascade." D-07: "Store the native amount
and unit, the USD figure, and the close day used to price it." D-11: "A run that writes the mark later the same UTC
day, for example a retry at 05:00Z, still writes it. `marked_at` records the real time."

Purpose: SC-1 needs a place that holds NAV including open profit, the open profit itself and the mark time per key per
day. A table whose rows cannot be updated or deleted by the worker is what makes D-06 and L3 ("a rebuild or re-derive
never silently replaces or drops a stored mark") structural rather than a code convention.
Output: the migration, its manual rollback, and the 11-arm SQL gate.
</objective>
