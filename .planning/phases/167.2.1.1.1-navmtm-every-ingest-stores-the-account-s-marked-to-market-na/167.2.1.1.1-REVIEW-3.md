## Warnings (MEDIUM)

### WR-01: A rotated-out allocated composite member is flagged "Marks paused" for ever, and its tail before `window_end` is dropped [MEDIUM, user-facing; the tail half is data-integrity; introduced by the SFH-2 N2 fix]

**File:** `analytics-service/services/job_worker.py:9950-9954` (`_note_trailing(..., closed=mandate_end < label)`)
with `_note_trailing` at `:9780-9791`. The marks come from `_allocated_member_marks`
(`:9877-9878`), which keeps only marks before the member's own `window_end`. The tail rule is at
`:9955-9971` and is keyed on `mandate_end` alone.

**Issue:** for an allocated (Deribit, `returns_denominator_config`) member, the "closed" test reads
only the mandate. It ignores the member's own composite window. Take member 1, windowed
`[01-01, 03-01)`, which rotated out of an open-mandate composite after marks through 02-28, the
last day of its window. Its terminal is 02-28 and `closed` is False, so
`since = max(03-01, 01-01) = 03-01 <= label` and the member is recorded as trailing. The composite
publishes `marks_trailing_since: 2026-03-01` for as long as the composite exists, and its
factsheet draws "Marks paused from Mar 1, 2026". That is so even though member 2's marks reach the
day the run labels. It happens for every allocated member that rotates out after its marks, not
only for a member whose marks were refused.

Reproduced on the real `run_stitch_composite_job` with the plan-17 harness
(`test_alloc_member_false_trailing.py`): member 1 has marks 02-27 and 02-28 inside
`[01-01, 03-01)`, and member 2 has marks through 03-07 on a run that labels 03-07. Output:
`marks_since: 2026-02-27 marks_trailing_since: 2026-03-01` (the test asserting no trailing fails).

The same branch has a second gap. Suppose the member window has closed, the mandate is still open,
and the marks stopped before `window_end - 1` (refused). The W2 tail never runs, because
`tail_to` is `mandate_end`. The member's realized rows in `(last mark, window_end)` are then
dropped, and the stitch 0.0-fills them. That is round-1 WR-03 for the allocated member, which
round 1 judged already covered by W2. W2 covers a closed mandate, not a closed member window.

**Fix (minimal, one branch):** close the allocated member on whichever ends first, its window or
its mandate, and run the tail to that end:

```python
label = mark_day_for(_utc_now()).isoformat()
window_end = _window_by_seq[seq][1]
ends = []
if config.mandate_end is not None:
    ends.append(config.mandate_end.isoformat())                      # inclusive
if window_end is not None:
    ends.append((date.fromisoformat(window_end) - timedelta(days=1)).isoformat())  # last in-window day
tail_to = min(ends) if ends else None
closed = tail_to is not None and tail_to < label
_note_trailing(seq, marked.terminal[0], closed)
if closed and marked.terminal[0] < tail_to:
    tail = returns[[marked.terminal[0] < ts.date().isoformat() <= tail_to for ts in returns.index]]
    if len(tail):
        tail = _tail_without_open_profit(tail, marks[-1].nav_usd, marks[-1].open_pnl_usd)
        out = pd.concat([out, tail]).sort_index()
```

Add the repro above as a test, plus one in which the member's marks stop two days before
`window_end` and its realized rows for those days are kept with D-24 applied.

### WR-02: MT5 flow instants and days use a fixed server offset, so outside summer time a flow made in the hour after the read is filed on the mark day and fabricates a large, permanent return [MEDIUM, data-integrity, user-facing; since round 1, and D-23 now depends on it]

**File:** `analytics-service/services/job_worker.py:6711-6714` (`deal_utc_day(..., int(os.getenv("MT5_SERVER_UTC_OFFSET_S", "0")))`)
and `:6728-6735` (`deal_utc_instant_ms(_deal, <same env>)`). Both feed `_mt5_flow_by_day` and
`_event_flow_instants`, which reach the apply at `:7530-7531` (strategy mode) and `:7442-7449`
(key mode). The known limit is recorded at `services/mt5_deals.py:277-280`, and
`TODOS.md` (v1.15 MT5 item) records `MT5_SERVER_UTC_OFFSET_S=10800` (EEST) on the worker.

**Issue:** the mark is a true-UTC observation (`marked_at` comes from our own clock, around 00:10Z
under D-03). An MT5 flow's day and instant come from broker server time minus one fixed integer. The
known-limit note says the offset error "affects day-bucketing only" and that "order ... is all the
fold uses". That was true of the realized deal ledger, where P&L and flows move together. It is
false once a mark is compared with a deal. Once the broker is on UTC+2, the worker's 10800 makes
every computed instant one hour early:

- A deposit made at true 00:10-01:00Z on day D+1, after mark D's read, is filed on D. It lands
  outside the D+1 check in rule 10 and sits in mark D's numerator or base, although mark D's NAV
  does not hold it.
- A deposit made at true 01:00-01:10Z gets an instant before the read and is moved into D. This
  narrow window is new in round 2.

Reproduced with the real `deal_utc_day` and `deal_utc_instant_ms` feeding `apply_marks`
(`mt5_offset_dst.py`). The deposit is 50k at 00:30Z on 11-11, mark 11-10 is read at 00:10Z
(NAV 102k, open profit 2k) and mark 11-11 has NAV 152k:

```
offset right (7200):        flow day=2026-11-11 rows={11-10: 0.02, 11-11: 0.0,      11-12: 0.0} cum=0.020000
offset as deployed (10800): flow day=2026-11-10 rows={11-10: 0.04, 11-11: 0.490196, 11-12: 0.0} cum=0.549804
truth: one +2% step on 11-10, flat after
```

When the misfiled day is not the first mark, the pair is -48% then +49% (cum about -22.5% for the
same numbers). Marks are immutable, so the error is re-published on every derive. It reaches the
factsheet, the persisted metrics and the My Allocation curve.

**Exposure:** it is UNMEASURED which DST rule the broker follows. Under EU rules the offset has
been wrong since 2026-10-25. Under US rules it is wrong from 2026-11-01. It stays wrong until
March in either case, unless the broker keeps UTC+3 all year. Any MT5 deposit or withdrawal in
roughly the 50 minutes after the nightly read is affected. One MT5 account is live.

**Fix:** this needs a decision, not a code edit, so do not improvise it in the fixer round.
- **Now (ops):** measure the broker's current offset and set `MT5_SERVER_UTC_OFFSET_S` to it
  before the first nightly marks. The spike's estimator and the live-tick confirmation are already
  named in TODOS.
- **Book:** a DST-aware offset, or one measured per derive from the gateway's server clock, owned
  with the existing MT5 DST item. Update the `mt5_deals.py:277-280` note: the offset now decides
  which mark a flow belongs to, not only which day it is bucketed on.
- If the founder wants an in-phase guard instead: do not place MT5 flows by instant, and do not
  apply MT5 marks to a day carrying a flow whose server-time instant is within one hour of the
  read. That is a scope decision.

## Info

### IN-01: For two venues, D-23's "instant" is not the moment the balance moved, so a flow can be moved into a mark that does not hold it [LOW, data-integrity; sFOX is not live]

**Files:** `job_worker.py:5716-5719` (the sFOX `_mark_read_at` is our clock after the
balance-history crawl) with `sfox_read.py:149-156` (`interval=86400` daily `usd_value` points;
the NAV is the last point, `job_worker.py:5805-5806`). Also `ccxt_flows.py:299-300` (the instant is
the ccxt row `timestamp`).

**Issue:**
- **sFOX:** the NAV is the last daily `usd_value` point, whose own timestamp
  (`_row_timestamp_ms`) can be earlier than `marked_at`. A flow between that point and `marked_at`
  is moved into the mark day but is not in the NAV, which gives the same shape as WR-02. Before
  round 2 such a mark was skipped. It is UNMEASURED which instant the last point represents.
  sFOX go-live is still founder-gated (TODOS item 3), so nothing is exposed today.
- **ccxt deposits:** the ccxt `timestamp` on OKX is when the deposit record was created, not when
  it was credited. A deposit created before the read and credited after it is moved into the mark
  day. The day bucket has the same limit on the realized basis at midnight, so this is the old
  class at a new boundary.

**Fix:** for sFOX, place against the last balance point's own timestamp (pass it as the
`AppliedMark.read_at` for the apply, and leave the stored `marked_at` alone), or pass no sFOX
instants until this is measured. For ccxt, book it, and measure the gap between created and
credited on the first post-deploy readout.

### IN-02: MT5 BTC key: a flow dated the last mark's read day is neither in the flows nor in the instants until the next derive; a moved flow is priced one close later than the mark [LOW]

**File:** `job_worker.py:6784-6790`. Instants without a close are dropped, and
`benchmark_prices` never holds today's close (`native_to_usd.latest_completed_close_day`).

**Issue:** at 00:10Z on D+1, a BTC deposit made at 00:05Z sits inside mark D but has no D+1 close.
It is in neither the converted flows nor the instants, so mark D publishes +F/base for one night.
The next derive prices it and moves it, so it heals. There is also a lasting second-order error:
the moved flow is valued at close(D+1), while M_D is `nav_native x close(D)`. The difference,
`F_btc x (close(D) - close(D+1))`, stays in r_D. It is far smaller than the pre-round-2 error.
**Fix:** none required. Name it in the post-deploy readout. A price rule for a moved flow belongs
to MARKSOURCE.

### IN-03: `native_flow_instants` raises a bare KeyError for a Deribit currency whose only activity nets to zero on a day with no mark, a permanent crash on every derive with marks [LOW]

**File:** `analytics-service/services/native_nav.py:1126-1144`
(`ledger.marks[fi.currency].loc[pd.Timestamp(fi.day)]`). It is called outside any try at
`job_worker.py:7513` (strategy mode), `:7444` (key mode), `:7496` (allocated twin, after the try)
and `:9922` / `:10076` (composite; the try there catches `NavReconstructionError` only).

**Issue:** the docstring says every day the function values "already passed the density contract".
The contract requires a mark only where the balance or the net flow quantity is nonzero
(`native_nav.py` `_value_over_calendar`, `required = (b_eff != 0) | (flow_eff != 0)`). Suppose a
BTC deposit is returned the same day for the same quantity, on an account that holds no BTC. The
composed flows then pass with no BTC mark, but the two per-row instants are nonzero and the lookup
raises. Reproduced at helper level (`deribit_cancelling_flows.py`):
`composed flows ok ... native_flow_instants raised KeyError 'BTC'`. A KeyError escapes the
derive's typed dispositions, the job retries to `failed_final`, and because history does not
change, every later derive fails the same way. It is UNMEASURED whether the live builder ever
produces this exact shape (a withdrawal fee breaks the cancellation).
**Fix:** when an INDEXED instant's currency or day has no mark, skip it. By the density contract
that day's composed flow in that currency is zero, so no move can be owed. Or value only the
instants whose day is some mark's read day, which is all that rule 10 reads.

### IN-04: For writer-basis venues the event-time `flows` in `key_inputs` are not restated, while `composed_flows` are [INFO]

**File:** `job_worker.py:7653-7685`. Only the non-writer branch rewrites `external_flows`.
**Issue:** for Deribit and MT5 keys the stored event-time flows keep a moved flow on D+1. The only
reader in writer mode is `build_allocator_ledger`, through `mwr_and_dietz_from_ledger`, whose
scalars are not rendered (`allocator_equity_compose.py`, F5 note). The per-key replay and the book
returns use `composed_flows` and the stored returns, which agree. No user-facing effect today.
**Fix:** none now. If those scalars are ever rendered, restate both fields.

### IN-05: D-24 reverses the open profit even when the last mark booked no row [INFO]

**File:** `job_worker.py:610-625` and its three callers.
**Issue:** when the last applied mark has no row (rule 4: an unusable base), its open profit `U`
was never booked as a step on that day. The tail still books `-U/M` on its first row. This needs a
closed window or mandate together with an unusable base on the last mark, which is very rare.
**Fix:** reverse only when the last mark day has a row, that is when it is in
`marked.writer_day_pnl` or in `marked.returns`.
