<objective>
D-17 and D-18 for Bybit: the Bybit mark is the Unified Trading Account's own total, option value included, refused by
name whenever the account mode, margin mode, borrowing or fields make that total untrustworthy.

CONTEXT D-17: "Research ... found that `fetch_positions()` on Bybit reads only `category=linear, settleCoin=USDT`, so an
account also holding USDC-settled or inverse positions was refused every day. ... The Bybit mark takes NAV and open
profit from those totals with no position walk." D-18: "The Bybit NAV is `totalEquity` ... The stored open profit is
`totalEquity - totalWalletBalance`."

Sources (cited, fetched 2026-10-26 by the research in the coordinator's scratch file):
- https://bybit-exchange.github.io/docs/v5/account/wallet-balance (`totalEquity` "Account total equity (USD): ∑Asset
  Equity By USD value of each asset"; coin `equity` "Asset Wallet Balance + Asset Perp UPL + Asset Future UPL + Asset
  Option Value"; "All account wide fields are not applicable to isolated margin"; coin `borrowAmount`).
- https://bybit-exchange.github.io/docs/v5/acct-mode and https://bybit-exchange.github.io/docs/v5/enum
  (`unifiedMarginStatus` 1 classic, 3/4 UTA 1.0, 5/6 UTA 2.0; UTA 1.0 keeps inverse outside UNIFIED).
- https://bybit-exchange.github.io/docs/v5/account/account-info (`unifiedMarginStatus`, `marginMode`).

Purpose: SC-1 for every Bybit UTA 2.0 key, not only USDT-linear ones.
Output: the reader, the tokens, one new test module, the retired Bybit cases of plan 15's module.
