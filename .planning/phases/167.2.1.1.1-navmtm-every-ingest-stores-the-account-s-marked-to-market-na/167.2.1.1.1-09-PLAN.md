<objective>
Composite strategies use their members' stored marks too.

ROADMAP SC-2: "The strategy return series ... use the stored marked NAV for every day it exists ... with the boundary
visible and no fabricated past marks." A composite strategy is a strategy with a published factsheet. RESEARCH 5.4
assumed composites "inherit members' marked series" from csv_daily_returns; that is not how the stitch works:
`run_stitch_composite_job` reconstructs each member live through `_reconstruct_ccxt_member` / `_reconstruct_deribit` and
never reads the members' stored rows. Without this plan every composite factsheet would stay realized-basis while the
single-key ones moved to marks. This plan applies the same rule (plan 03) per member, inside the stitch. D-14 ("Apply
marks to them too") reaches an allocated-capital composite the way it reaches a single allocated-capital strategy:
through plan 13's NAV twin of the member's own crawl and plan 16's day-P&L rule; that member is plan 17's (split from this
plan in revision round 2 so each stays inside the context budget).

Purpose: SC-2 for composites, D-05 and D-12 on the composite factsheet.
Output: member apply for every member whose returns are NAV changes, the composite covered set, coverage_mask's new
argument, one test module.
</objective>
