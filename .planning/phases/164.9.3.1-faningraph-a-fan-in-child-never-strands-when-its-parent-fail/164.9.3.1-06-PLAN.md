<assumption_delta_decision>
Primary noun: the decision identity of a `bridge_outcomes` row.
Decision: `add-alongside`. `match_decision_id` stays the live link (FK, `ON DELETE SET NULL`, per-decision
unique constraint); `former_match_decision_id` is added beside it to remember which decision an orphaned
row came from once the live link is gone. Rationale: the live link must stay nullable and FK-backed (the
admin delete is a first-class action and outcomes outlive their decision), so a single column cannot carry
both "current decision" and "decision it was recorded under".
Accepted debt: two identity columns, exactly one of which is non-NULL for a cascade-orphaned row, both NULL
for a born-NULL row; readers that want "the decision this outcome was recorded under" must read
`COALESCE(match_decision_id, former_match_decision_id)`.
What would force a later promote to one column (`recorded_match_decision_id` NOT NULL, no FK, plus a
separate live FK): a reader or report that needs the recorded decision for every row, or a policy that keys
on it.
Invariant pinned by tests: at most one of the two columns is non-NULL on any row (the trigger sets
`former_match_decision_id` NULL whenever `match_decision_id` is non-NULL); gate C OD6 and OD2 observe both
directions.
</assumption_delta_decision>
