option Y (a nullable `former_match_decision_id`, recorded by the sync trigger, the SAME-named index
re-keyed on it). The gate is written against the Y contract now so plan 06's migration has a target.

**Lanes: `pg-lane`** (gate C, Tasks 1-2) **and `local-stack`** (gate C on the real tables plus vitest arm
(d), Task 3, under `$LANE_LOCK`).

**Planner decision recorded here (security hardening inside option Y, not a new decision):** the research
sketch lets the trigger record the former id only on the cascade update. Because `bridge_outcomes` carries
a table-level UPDATE/INSERT grant to `authenticated` plus the owner policies `bridge_outcomes_insert_own`
and `bridge_outcomes_update_own`, an allocator could otherwise write `former_match_decision_id` directly and
defeat the born-NULL duplicate guard Y exists to keep. So the column is trigger-owned: an INSERT always
stores NULL, an UPDATE that keeps `match_decision_id` NULL keeps the OLD value, and the trigger also fires
on `UPDATE OF former_match_decision_id`. Arms OD4-OD6 pin that contract.

Output: fixture 40, gate C (6 arms), vitest arm (d), and the pasted RED transcripts.
</objective>
