Wire MT5, the one venue whose fetched data carries both flow instants and position-open instants
(D-10 research measurement), into the timed core: the fold builds a `DayTiming` per flow day, the
combiner opts into the inception rule, and the MM-2x reference account's deposit day becomes a
position-relative return.

Purpose: D-02/D-03/D-04/D-09 on real MT5 deals; D-07 where an open cannot be resolved; D-01/D-11/D-12
for every MT5 account ("every MT5 and CSV account" is full-history). D-08: instants come from the
plan-03 seam using the same `server_utc_offset_s` as the day.
Output: an extended fold, three consumers passing the same timing, the MM-2x timed fixture.
</objective>
