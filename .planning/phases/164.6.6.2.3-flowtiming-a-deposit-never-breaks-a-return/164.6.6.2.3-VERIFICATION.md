## Warnings carried (none is a must-have failure)

All are MEDIUM, booked in TODOS with owners; none changes a verified truth.

- **D-18 / `164.6.6.2.3-D17-GUARD-DISCONTINUITY`** (founder-accepted limit): a vanishingly small
  flow on a day whose carried position lost about 90% or more of the account turns that day into a
  flagged NaN where the no-flow day was measured.
- **`mt5_soak.py` live-gate is weaker than the production path** (`164.6.6.2.3-SOAK-BTC-FLOORS`
  R3-02: judges USD floors, so a BTC-unit account reads permanently red; `...-SOAK-TIMED-DAYS-UNCHECKED`
  R3-03: a timed day certifies green for any finite value or NaN; `...-SOAK-DAY-ATTRIBUTION`). The
  soak is a founder-run script, not a production code path, so the risk is a missed regression on
  the next live soak, not a wrong published number. Do not read a green soak as proof of timed-day
  arithmetic; the unit-test oracles and the neuter carry that proof.
- `164.6.6.2.3-D15-THRESHOLD-BAND`: the "returns can be extreme" caveat fires at `|flow| >= prev`,
  so a day-level flow of, say, 50% of the prior balance can still inflate a return unmarked.
- `164.6.6.2.3-COMPOSITE-DAYLEVEL-NOTE`: a stitched composite does not propagate a member's
  `flow_timing_day_level` note.
- `164.6.6.2.3-INCEPTION-DAY0-PNL`, `-MT5-BASIS-SILENT-NONE`, `-EMPTY-COHORT0-REFUND`: edge shapes
  (day-0 P&L dropped without a flag on the day-level inception skip, a writer basis that returns
  None silently, an emptied account re-funded on a timed day).
- Deribit still has dust-guarded days after the change (`dust_nav_guard` x29 across sized keys, 11
  distinct): these are real sub-floor bases (D-06), not flows.
- Deribit end-of-day returns on flow days can be large (largest 529.34%, 2 distinct series with a
  day at least 100%). They are kept by D-05/D-07 and now marked by D-15; intraday Deribit timing is
  booked as `164.6.6.2.3-DERIBIT-INTRADAY-TIMING`.
