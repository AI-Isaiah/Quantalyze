## The `small_base_measured` resolution (plan-checker note, R = 3e-5)

The plan asserted `small_base_measured` absent on every row. With D-05, day 0 at R = 3e-5 is now a MEASURED day whose prior NAV (3e-5 BTC) is under the BTC material equity (1e-4), which is exactly the D-25 rule: a measured day on a very small balance is kept exact and counted. D-12 says day 0 is measured; D-05 says no flow warns; D-25 is an informational annotation that never promotes the status and never breaks a chain. The honest reading is therefore that the annotation fires, and the test now asserts it for R = 3e-5 only: `small_base_measured is True`, `small_base_measured_days == 1`, `small_base_measured_min_nav` within rel 1e-6 of R (3e-5). For R = 5e-4, 0.02, 0.2 (base at or above 1e-4) and for the guarded rows R = 0, 5e-8 (day 0 not measured, so not counted) it is asserted absent. Measured outputs agreed with the hand reasoning (`small_base_measured_min_nav` = 3.0000000000279803e-05). Status stays `complete` in the measured rows, so nothing warns.
