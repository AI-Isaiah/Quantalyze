# Phase 164.6.6.2.3 Plan 05: MT5 timing fold and full-history combine Summary

The MT5 fold now builds a per-day `DayTiming` from the same single deal pass, every MT5 combine declares full history, and the MM-2x deposit day is measured on the capital its positions opened on: 0.011 / 1.1 = 0.01, where the end-of-day formula said 0.11.

## What changed

**`analytics-service/services/broker_dailies.py`** (no other `services/` file touched)
- `_fold_mt5_deals` returns `(daily_pnl_series, flows, timing)`. The single classification loop (still the only `classify_deal(` call site) now also RECORDS: flow deals with their amounts per day, trading deals as `(deal, position_id, entry_code, type_code, cash)` per day, and every position's `DEAL_ENTRY_IN` deals with the UTC day the loop already computed. It never calls `deal_utc_instant_ms`.
- `_mt5_flow_day_timing` (after the loop, flow days only) resolves instants and builds the `DayTiming`:
  - flows are sorted by instant, stable on fold order for equal instants (a flow deal whose instant is `None` makes the day unresolvable);
  - a commission-family deal (7..11) with no position is placed by its own instant;
  - a BUY/SELL deal needs a position id with recorded IN deals and a known entry code, else the day is unresolvable;
  - a position whose earliest IN deal is on an earlier UTC day is carried overnight: open `None` (D-04), no instant resolved;
  - otherwise the open is the earliest IN-deal instant of the day; it must resolve and must not be later than the closing deal, else the day is unresolvable.
  An unresolvable day gets no record and falls to the day-level rule with the `flow_timing_day_level` annotation. No time is ever invented. Keys are `pd.Timestamp(day).as_unit("us")`, asserted to be members of the NAV index in the same unit.
- `combine_mt5_deal_ledger` and the meta call inside `reconstruct_mt5_nav_levels` both pass `timing=timing, full_history=True` (D-11: every MT5 account is full-history; `history_deals_get` is a full-history fetch). `mt5_day_pnl` unpacks and ignores the timing. The levels themselves never depended on either.

## Deviations from Plan

**1. [Interpretation] A flow-only day is a timed day, not a day-level one.** The plan builds a `DayTiming` "for every day with at least one flow". A day holding only a deposit (no trading deal) has all its instants, so it gets a record; its value is the same exact 0 either way. Consequence: the plan's wording "the legacy MM-2x fixture keeps the deposit day at day level" holds for the legacy fixture only through its withdrawal day (a trade without a `position_id`); its 1.0 deposit day is timed and its `flow_timing_day_level_days` is 1, not 2. The day-level behaviour of a legacy deposit day that carries a trade is pinned separately by `test_legacy_deals_without_position_ids_keep_the_day_level_rule` (canonical ledger, 300/100400, annotation present). Founder-visible effect: none on values; `flow_timing_timed_days` counts deposit-only days.

**2. [Test fixture] Oracle semantics for a no-P&L day.** The first oracle draft returned `None` for any deposit-only day; the core (plan 01) measures cohort 0 at exactly 0 whenever capital existed at the start of the day and only the inception day has no return. The oracle was corrected to that definition (from D-03/D-12), not from the code's output.

**3. [Process] Branch namespace.** The pre-commit allow-list expects `agent-*` branches; this worktree is the orchestrator-named `wt/164.6.6.2.3-p05` (attached HEAD, not protected/default, cwd sentinel checked). Committed on it as plans 01, 03 and the caller directed.
