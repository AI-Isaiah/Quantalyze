## MEDIUM

### SFH-R3-01 - D-23 "sized at the 0.5 ceiling" book entry is recorded when no sizing took place (screening mode / no eligible candidate)

- File: `analytics-service/routers/match.py:1659-1698` (`add_weight_fallback_entry`), appended at
  `analytics-service/routers/match.py:~1875` (`holding_flags_list.append(add_weight_fallback)`), with
  `scoring_aum` swapped at `:~1810`.
- Severity: MEDIUM. User-facing: no (the dashboard reader keeps only flagged entries that carry a candidate,
  and the book entry has `flagged: false`). Data-integrity: yes (the persisted `match_batches.holding_flags`
  audit record asserts a sizing that did not happen).
- Evidence: the entry is due when `ctx["aum_not_positive"]` is true and the merged ticket is positive. Both are
  properties of the context, not of the scoring result. The engine only computes a ticket-based `add_weight`
  when `mode == "personalized"` (`services/match_engine.py:998-1007`), and `mode` is `"personalized"` only if
  `portfolio_strategies` is non-empty (`services/match_engine.py:885`). `aum_not_positive` is true for a book of
  holdings whose AUM is <= 0 even when no strategy and no priced (eligible) spot holding exists. For example a
  derivative-only book with net losses, or a book of only withheld spot plus derivatives, has
  `ctx["portfolio_strategies"] == []`. The engine then runs in screening mode, never sizes a ticket (0.10 is
  unused there too), and the batch still persists `{"kind": "book", "add_weight_fallback":
  "aum_not_positive_ceiling_0_5", "add_weight": 0.5}` plus a WARNING saying the candidate was sized at the
  ceiling. The same false claim appears when the engine returns early with zero eligible candidates. No test
  covers the screening case: `tests/test_match_aum_not_positive_sizing.py` has no screening or empty-book test.
- Hidden error: an operator or later reader comparing batches cannot tell a real ceiling-sized batch from a
  screening batch that carries a stale claim. Nothing else downstream is affected (the swapped 1e-6 AUM is
  unused in screening).
- Fix: gate the entry (and the AUM swap) on the engine's own condition, and on the result for the eligible
  case. In `add_weight_fallback_entry` add `if not ctx.get("portfolio_strategies"): return None`. In
  `_score_one_allocator`, append the entry only when `result["mode"] == "personalized"` and
  `result["candidates"]` is non-empty (or decide the entry after `score_candidates` returns, passing the AUM
  swap in before). Add two tests: a derivative-loss-only book (screening) records no fallback entry and logs no
  `add_weight_fallback`; a personalized book whose candidate list is empty records none.
