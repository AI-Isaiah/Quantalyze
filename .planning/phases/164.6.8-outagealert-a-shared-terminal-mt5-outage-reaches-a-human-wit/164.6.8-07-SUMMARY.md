# Phase 164.6.8 Plan 07: OUTAGEALERT relaunch-debt lease wait and page Summary

While a relaunch debt is owed the heal's lease acquire waits `_MT5_RELOGIN_LEASE_WAIT_CEILING_S` (no debt: `_relogin_lease_wait_s()` unchanged), the busy-with-debt arm keeps its byte-identical ERROR line and now also pages source `mt5_relaunch_debt`, and the first heal that pays a paged debt sends one recovered notice. SC-2 and `MT5-RELAUNCH-DEBT-LEASE-WAIT-01`.

## What was built

- `mt5_relogin.heal_mt5_terminal_session`: `terminal_key`, `debt_owed_at_start` and `lease_wait_s` are computed once before the "starting" line (inside the existing guard). The starting line carries the chosen wait and its reason in closed text (no host or port). The lease uses `terminal_key` and `lease_wait_s`. The busy arm's comment keeps the 164.6.6.1 sentence "the lease wait is NOT lengthened" as dated lineage and records the amendment (D-14). After `record_mt5_heal_outcome`, `debt_owed_at_start and not mt5_relaunch_debt(terminal_key)` triggers the recovered notice. Both notifiers are awaited through `_page_for_outage_alert`, a wrapper that cannot raise.
- `mt5_outage_alert`: `note_relaunch_debt_lease_busy(*, detail)` records the first busy time and a tick count, sets `debt_paged`, and calls `ops_alert.page("mt5_relaunch_debt", "lease_busy_with_relaunch_debt", event="outage", since=<first tick>, ...)` on every call (the helper's hourly window bounds it, D-05). `note_relaunch_debt_settled()` clears the state first, then sends `recovered_from_lease_busy_with_relaunch_debt` only if a page went out (D-06). Both never raise (IN-06 handler shape). State is reset by `_reset_outage_state_for_tests`.
- `tests/test_mt5_relaunch_debt_alert.py` (new, 21 tests): tracer; no-debt arm (default, env override, out-of-window env falls back to default); debt arm ceiling for every env value; starting-line reason; page cadence and first-tick `since`; page content free of host, port and credentials; source and cause admitted by the helper; recovery after a page; no recovery without a page; no recovery while the debt is still owed; a second incident gets its own `since`; busy notifier raising, tracker `page` raising, settled notifier raising; monitor floor still >= lease ceiling + budget ceiling.
- `.planning/ROADMAP.md`: dated correction under criterion 2 (original sentence kept) and the Requirements line filled. Diff confined to the 164.6.8 section.
