## N1 - MEDIUM - the record is closed after `page()` regardless of delivery, so a failed recovered notice is lost for good. Confidence 7
mt5_outage_alert.py `_send_request` (the `elif await ops_alert_open.close_open_page(...)` branch), `note_relaunch_debt_settled` (close after the settled page), mt5_validation_liveness.py `read_unscrubbed_validation_terminal` (close after a recovered page).
`ops_alert.page` returns None and the email is a background task, so the caller cannot know the recovered notice failed. F2's fix releases the window and logs at ERROR, but nothing re-sends. The caller then closes the durable row and adds the source to `_DURABLE_CHECKED`. Scenario: redeploy during an incident, new process sees the first authorized reading, finds the open row, sends the recovered notice while the Next.js route returns 502 or 401 (rotated SERVICE_KEY) and Sentry has no DSN; the row is closed and the check is marked done. The founder's inbox ends on OUTAGE, which is the exact loss WR-01 exists to prevent, and the module docstring's "at-least-once, never lost" is false for delivery failure. Only an ERROR log remains.
Fix: make `page` report delivery (return the email task or a bool resolved by the same `sentry_ok or email_ok` rule) and close the row only on success; on failure leave it open so the next healthy reading retries. Add a test: recovered page whose delivery fails leaves the row `running`.

## N2 - MEDIUM - open-page record failures are WARNING only, so the loss of restart recovery is invisible. Confidence 6
