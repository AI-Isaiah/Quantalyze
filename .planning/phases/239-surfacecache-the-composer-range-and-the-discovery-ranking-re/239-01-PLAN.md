<objective>
Make the shared `FactsheetProvider` hold a manual range as dates and resolve it to indices on the ACTIVE basis axis each render, so a range the user set by pan, zoom, brush or keyboard keeps its exact dates when the series grows at either end (CACHE-02, the manual half of founder Q4 "Presets roll, manual stays (Recommended)").

Purpose: `userRange` holds axis indices today, so when the composer's series grows at the FRONT every index points at a different day (TODOS `201.1-COMPOSER-RANGE-AS-DATES`). The change lives in the shared provider, so the real factsheet's pan, zoom and brush keep their dates too, with no composer-only fork (CONTEXT, Claude's Discretion).

Design (a refinement inside Claude's Discretion, recorded so the executor does not rediscover it):
- The provider sits ABOVE `BasisProvider` and cannot see the basis, while every consumer indexes the active basis view's dates (RESEARCH Pitfall 1: `MasterBrush.basis.test.tsx` uses a 150-day MTM axis dated 2031 against a 100-day cash axis dated 2020).
- RESEARCH Pattern 4 had `BasisProvider` read the payload, but `usePayload()` throws outside `FactsheetProvider`, and seven `basis-context.*.test.tsx` files mount `BasisProvider` without one (measured at planning time). So `BasisProvider` publishes ONLY the basis, through a nullable `ActiveBasisContext` exported from `factsheet-context.tsx`; `useXRange()` derives the axis from the provider's own payload with the pure `basisAxisDates(payload, basis)`, falling back to cash when no `BasisProvider` is above it.
- Presets are NOT in this plan: plan 02 adds the rolling preset member, `subtractMonths` and `setPresetRange` together with their only caller, `PeriodControl`.

Output: `range-dates.ts` with its pure test, the date store in `factsheet-context.tsx`, the one-line basis bridge in `basis-context.tsx`, the re-pinned and new provider arms, and a new active-axis test.
</objective>
