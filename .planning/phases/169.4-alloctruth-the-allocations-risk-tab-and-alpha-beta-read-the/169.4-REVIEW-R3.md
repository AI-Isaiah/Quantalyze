## Warnings

### WR-01: the floor sentence names record length as the cause when the cause is the comparator's prices ending, so "this record has 0" can sit on a record of 120+ days

**Files:**
- `src/app/factsheet/[id]/v2/FactsheetView.tsx:1504-1505` (`pairedFloorReason(cn, jointWithheld.paired, "record", …)`, where `cn` is the undated `cmp.shortName`)
- `src/app/factsheet/[id]/v2/MetricsColumn.tsx:272-275` (§IV is named `Benchmark — vs ${bn}`, undated, with the same sentence)
- `src/lib/factsheet/joint.ts:29` ("Alpha and beta need at least 10 days paired with {cmp}; this {noun} has N.")

**Failing scenario:** take any api or CSV strategy whose first date is after 2026-05-08 (every strategy
connected in the last five months), open its factsheet, and pick SPX. The SPX fixture ends 2026-05-08,
so the record pairs 0 intervals with SPX. The KPI strip reads "α vs SPX —" and "IR vs SPX —" above
*"Alpha and beta need at least 10 days paired with SPX; this record has 0."* §IV repeats the sentence
under nine "—" rows. The record may hold 150 days. The sentence reads as "this record is too young", and
a reader will expect the figures to appear after 10 more days. They never will: the cause is that SPX
has no prices after May 8, and no amount of record length fixes that. A record that starts between
roughly 2026-04-24 and 2026-05-08 reads "has 1" to "has 9" and never improves either. The coverage is
dated elsewhere on the page: the picker caption reads "SPX prices through May 8"
(`ComparatorPicker.tsx:39-40`), and the §I/§II bench headers read "SPX to May 8" (`MetricsColumn.tsx:113,144`).
The two lines that carry the new sentence (the KPI strip and §IV's header) are undated, though, and the
sentence itself names only the count. The fix report frames this as "one cause told consistently". The
count is true, but the implied cause is wrong for this whole population. This is the wrong-cause class
that round 2 raised on the signature panels.

**Severity: MEDIUM.** The copy is user-facing and misleading. No figure is wrong, and the dated coverage
is visible elsewhere on the same page. It is not HIGH.

**Fix (if it is ever taken):** when the comparator's coverage ends before the record does, name that
instead. The block already carries what is needed: `jointCmp.through` and `isPastCoverage`, which
`comparatorPartialThrough` wraps.
```ts
// MetricsColumn.tsx / FactsheetView.tsx — beside pairedFloorReason
const endsEarly = comparatorPartialThrough(block.through, view.dates.at(-1), COMPARATOR_CALENDARS[cmpKey]);
const reason = endsEarly
  ? `${pairedFloorReason(cn, w.paired, "record", w.floor)} ${cn} prices end ${isoToMonthDay(endsEarly)}.`
  : pairedFloorReason(cn, w.paired, "record", w.floor);
```
