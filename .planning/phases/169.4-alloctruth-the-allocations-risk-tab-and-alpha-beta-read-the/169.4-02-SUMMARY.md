## Task 3: alpha/beta of the book against BTC

- **Basis constant.** `ALLOCATOR_PORTFOLIO_ASSET_CLASS = "crypto"` is exported from `allocator-portfolio-payload.ts`. The build uses it for `assetClass`, and the widget uses it through `annualizationPeriods`.
- **Widget schema.** `alphaBetaWidgetDataSchema` is `riskWidgetDataSchema.extend({ equityHistoryState, equityDailyReturns, btcBenchmarkPrices }).loose()` and carries the B9 eslint comment. It is passed to `withWidgetBoundary`. `widget-data.ts` is not edited.
- **`computeBookAlphaBeta`** (exported, pure) follows `buildFromBuildableSeries` and `comparator-block.ts` step for step:
  1. `normalizeDailyReturns` on the book's returns;
  2. `alignCoveredReturns(prices, dropped, dates, COMPARATOR_CALENDARS.btc)`;
  3. keep the indices where `paired[i]` holds and the value is non-null;
  4. `jointMetrics(strat, bench, 0, 365)`.
  It returns no figure when the history is not ready or the book has fewer than 2 returns, and a BTC-unavailable result for `null`, `undefined` or `{ unavailable: true }`. D7 now keys on non-finite alpha or beta, because `jointMetrics` returns NaN where `computeAlphaBeta` returned null.
- **`AlphaBetaDecomposition.book-btc.test.tsx`**, 8 cases:
  - **parity:** alpha and beta are `toBe`-equal to `buildAllocatorPortfolioFactsheetPayload(...).comparators.btc.joint`, and the callout renders that alpha;
  - **gap:** 58 paired indices. The figures equal `jointMetrics` over hand-listed indices (all except k and k+1). They are also pinned as literals, alpha 0.08745351150053449 and beta 0.07311557987410754. The case also checks that the date-intersection reading gives a different beta. The literals were measured from this implementation. The independent check is the `jointMetrics`-over-listed-indices assertion.
  - **BTC unavailable, null, or absent on a ready book:** the new line shows and there is no figure;
  - **not ready:** the "Insufficient data" line;
  - **wrong shape** (an array of daily points): the boundary alert shows and there is no figure;
  - **loose schema:** extra payload keys still render.
  Before the fix all 8 were RED: `computeBookAlphaBeta` was not exported, and the widget rendered no BTC path.
