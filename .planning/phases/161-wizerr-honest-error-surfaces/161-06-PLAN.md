Detector fired (`optional` family): a REQUIRED-by-omission constructor shape gains a fourth
OPTIONAL parameter. Decision: **add-alongside**, deliberately, not promote.

Rationale, measured: there is exactly ONE production construction site
(`analytics-client.ts:~556`), so a trailing-options-object refactor is cheap at the producer —
but it would force rewriting every positional construction across **seven** test files
(including five that declare their own class), which is a large blast radius for zero
correctness gain and would violate CLAUDE.md Rule 3. The type hazard booked in TODOS §4a
(positional adjacent same-typed params) does NOT apply here: the new parameter is
`number | null`, following two `string | null`s, so a transposition is a `tsc` error rather
than a silent swap.

**Accepted debt + what would force a promote:** a FIFTH optional field, or any second
`number | null` field, removes the type-distinctness that makes this safe — at that point the
trailing-options-object form becomes mandatory. Recorded here so the next carrier inherits the
trigger rather than rediscovering it. Task 3's parity law is the mitigation that keeps the
add-alongside honest.
</assumption_delta_decision>
