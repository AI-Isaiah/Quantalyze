## ⭐ MEASURED: the producer semantics, and TWO DEFECTS in the proposed WIZERR-10 copy

161-UI-SPEC § Copy Spec WIZERR-10 carries its own ⚠️ truth obligation: *"these two clauses are
my rendering of the verdict names — the executor MUST validate each clause against the
producer's actual verdict semantics before shipping."* That validation was done at plan time.
**The producer registry contradicts both proposed clauses.** Correcting them is honoring the
UI-SPEC's instruction, not overriding it (its preamble: "the copy and its invariants are the
contract" — and a false clause fails the contract's first principle).

Truth source, read first-hand: `analytics-service/services/broker_dailies.py` § "Who stamps
what (all five values, one producer each)" (~:110-135). Measured semantics:

| Verdict | Producer | Actual condition |
|---|---|---|
| `sampled_gapped` | `combine_sfox_balance_history` | `nav_gap_days > 0` — a SAMPLED NAV with **any** interior hole. "Computable, but not a complete record." |
| `fill_derived_unproven` | `combine_realized_and_funding` (binance / bybit / okx) | **ALWAYS and unconditionally** — a constant property of the derivation method, not a finding about this series |

**Defect 1 — `sampled_gapped`.** The proposed clause says the sampling has "gaps **too large**
to verify". The producer's threshold is `nav_gap_days > 0`, not a magnitude judgment. "Too
large" asserts a size test that does not exist, and would be false for a one-day hole.

**Defect 2 — `fill_derived_unproven`.** The proposed title "This data source was **examined and
refused**" implies a producer looked at THIS series and found it wanting. The verdict is stamped
unconditionally for every binance/bybit/okx fill-derived series. "Examined" is true only in the
gate's weaker sense — *a producer ran and stamped a verdict*, as opposed to the NULL "nobody
looked" case — never in the sense of a per-series finding.

Candidate corrected wording (executor may refine, must not regress):

- `sampled_gapped` reason: "The return series is built from sampled balance snapshots with
  interior gaps, so it is not a complete record."
- `fill_derived_unproven` reason: "The return series is derived from individual fills, which
  does not establish a complete record for this venue."
- Wizard `title`: "This track record could not be verified from its source data."
- Wizard `cause`: "The venue's return series carries a completeness verdict we do not admit —
  the data was found wanting, not missing."

⚠️ Both reason sentences render RAW on the admin surface behind `Cannot approve: ` (A5,
measured below), so each must read as a complete sentence after that prefix.
