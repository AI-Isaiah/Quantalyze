`createAdminClient()` instead of the user-scoped `createClient()`, and fail **closed** when the
service key is absent.

what is passed as `p_exchange`. **What is missing is that a browser can bypass the route entirely.**

Output: two route handlers, and plan 02's failing assertions turning green on both.
