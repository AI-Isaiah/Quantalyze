**Decisions taken here, recorded (Rule 7).**
- Sort order (orchestrator O-1, CONTEXT "Orchestrator calls"): the reader returns distinct lowercase
  codes; SyncBadge orders the venue LABELS case-insensitively without a locale
  (`a.toLowerCase() < b.toLowerCase()`), so OKX plus Bybit renders `Bybit, OKX`. D-05's
  `"OKX, Bybit"` was an illustration, not an order.
- Mismatch (orchestrator O-3): the cue renders when venues are non-empty, the listed set is
  non-empty, and at least one venue is NOT in the listed set (case-insensitive). The mounts pass the
  whole `supported_exchanges` as a new optional `listedExchanges` prop. The sentence keeps D-05's
  form: `Listed as {label of listedExchanges[0], falling back to exchange}; trades come from {every venue label}`. With no
  listed set there is nothing to disagree with, so no cue.
- Display names (orchestrator O-2): SyncBadge's private three-entry label map (`binance`, `okx`,
  `bybit`) conflicts with `EXCHANGE_DISPLAY` in `src/lib/closed-sets.ts`, which carries every
  `SupportedExchange` and makes a missing label a compile error. `EXCHANGE_DISPLAY` becomes the single
  label source for both the venue and the listed label (fallback: the raw code). This IS a visible
  change, not a no-op: a listed `deribit`, `sfox` or `mt5` that rendered raw today renders `Deribit`,
  `sFOX` or `MT5` on every mount that renders a SyncBadge label. ⚠️ Measured at re-plan: of the
  mounts that pass `exchange`, StrategyHeader passes `tradesFetchedAt={null}` and `seriesEnd={null}`,
  so its badge resolves to `bound === "none"` and renders NOTHING today; CompositionDonut,
  StrategyBreakdownTable and /recommendations pass no `exchange` at all. So the label fix is visible
  on the StrategyTable and StrategyGrid mounts only, and StrategyHeader's pin asserts what it can:
  that its badge still renders nothing (no label, raw or display), plus a SyncBadge-level pin that a
  listed `deribit` renders `Deribit` given a renderable state. Flagged to the orchestrator: the
  literal O-2 pin "StrategyHeader renders Deribit" is unreachable without changing StrategyHeader's
  badge states, which D-07 forbids.
- Field name: `tradeVenues?: readonly string[]` on the ranked row (optional, set only by
  `getStrategiesByCategory`), and an optional `tradeVenues?: readonly string[] | null` SyncBadge
  prop, separate from `tradeFetchVenue` exactly as `exchange` is (D-07).
