## Decisions Made

- The inner-join oracle is built by date lookup on the strategy's own sorted index (`isin` plus `reindex`). Both legs carry the index shape the engine's pair legs carry, and the oracle never calls `_benchmark_pair`.
- treynor's oracle divides the engine's own top-level `cagr`, computed on the full series, by the oracle beta. This mirrors the fan-out, which uses the full-series cagr.
- The D-06 inequality is `new != pytest.approx(old, rel=1e-9, abs=0.0)`, as the plan specifies.
