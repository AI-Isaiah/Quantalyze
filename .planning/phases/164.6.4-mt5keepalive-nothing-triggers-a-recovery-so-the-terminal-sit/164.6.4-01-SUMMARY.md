# Phase 164.6.4 Plan 01: `_raise_last` redacts BY VALUE at the one shared site Summary

`[164.6.2-RAISE-LAST-SHAPE-ONLY]` is closed: `Mt5Client._raise_last` takes an optional
keyword-only credential triple and redacts the three values by value at all three of its raise
points, so `login`'s falsy arm and `initialize_with_credentials`' falsy arm are now SYMMETRIC —
and the gate that pinned the disclosure became the gate that pins the symmetry.

## What was built

**Task 1 — `analytics-service/services/mt5_client.py`** (commit `4f1963fd`)

- `_redact_with_optional_credentials(text, credentials)`, a new module-level helper beside
  `_redact_credential_values`, with the same two arms as
  `mt5_relogin._describe_exception_for_log`: by value with the triple, `scrub_freeform_string`
  without it. It DELEGATES to the shipped loop — a second copy was the measured live disclosure
  `[164.6.2-LOGIN-ESCAPE-BLIND]`.
- `_raise_last(self, *, credentials: tuple[int, str, str] | None = None) -> NoReturn`, applying
  that helper at all THREE raise points: the converted transport raise, the empty-`last_error`
  sentinel, and the final `Mt5ClientError(code, text)`. The code is preserved through the
  redaction; only the freeform text is rewritten.
- Call sites: `login`'s two falsy arms (after `initialize`, after `login`) and
  `initialize_with_credentials`' falsy arm pass the triple. The other five callers
  (`assert_session_authorized`, `account_info`, `terminal_info`, `history_deals_get`,
  `order_check`) are unchanged — they hold no credential and there is nothing to redact by.
  Measured: EIGHT `_raise_last` call sites, 3 credentialed + 5 not.
- `initialize_with_credentials`' `try/except Mt5ClientError` + `removeprefix` + re-raise block
  collapsed to one parameterised call.
- The docstring paragraph calling the asymmetry "measured and deliberate" — and claiming closing
  it "would mean editing `login()`, which D-07 forbids" — became false in the same commit and was
  rewritten to record the close and why D-07 was not broken.
- ⛔ Both direct `_redact_credential_values` calls on the transport-raise arms were KEPT, as the
  plan requires.

**Task 2 — `analytics-service/tests/test_mt5_client_contract.py` + the two ledger files** (commit `5ce04acd`)
