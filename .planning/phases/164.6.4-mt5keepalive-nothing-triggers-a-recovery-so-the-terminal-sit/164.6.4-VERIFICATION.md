### Criterion 2 — something NOTICES, and the latency is MEASURED

**Wired, not merely built.** `main.lifespan` creates
`asyncio.create_task(mt5_session_monitor_loop(), name="mt5_session_monitor")` as the sixth entry,

**The goal sentence, demonstrated behaviourally.**
`test_CRITERION_2_a_dark_reading_drives_the_heal_with_no_human_and_no_restart` drives the **real
loop** against a fake terminal returning `-6` and asserts four things: the credentialed heal

**Latency MEASURED rather than assumed — and the honesty here is the good kind.** The module
refuses to claim what it cannot know: true detection latency is unobservable because nothing
watches the lapse itself. What it records is a measured **bound** —
`_stamp_reading_and_measure_gap()` returns a **monotonic** wall-clock gap since the previous
measured reading (never wall clock; NTP steps would produce negative durations on a table whose
whole purpose is durations), and `None` on the first reading of a process rather than the cadence
constant. `test_first_reading_after_boot_is_true_EXACTLY_when_the_gap_is_None` pins it. ⛔ The
configured cadence is explicitly refused as a substitute — "an assumed number wearing a measured
name is the defect class this whole phase is about."

**A blind instrument cannot look like a healthy one.** A run of `not_measured` readings escalates
the log level once it covers the independent instrument's own window, with the threshold
**derived** from the supplied cadence rather than hand-typed, so a retune carries through.
`KIND_DISABLED` (a genuine operator decision) is counted but exempt from escalation;
`KIND_KILL_SWITCH_UNPARSEABLE` (`1`/`on`/`yes` — the operator meant ON) is a misconfiguration and
still escalates. That distinction is WR-14, and it is the right cut.

properly.
