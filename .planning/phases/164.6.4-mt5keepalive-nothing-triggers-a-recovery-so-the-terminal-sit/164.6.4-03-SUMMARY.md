The monitor's two safety properties are now STRUCTURAL and each is proven to BITE against its
own mutant table: the task can never escape to `_crash_handler` (P-outer, the ONE shared
predicate imported and widened), and one failing tick can never end the loop (P-inner, new).

ceiling are recomputed from the quantities they descend from, and criterion 1's prohibition on

**P-inner, new.** `_loop_survival_defects(source)` — parameterised by SOURCE TEXT for the same
stated reason the shared predicate is, and naming a defect when:

1. the guard body does not hold EXACTLY ONE `while`;
2. a statement in the `while` body is not itself a `try`;
3. a per-tick guard's FINAL handler does not catch bare `Exception`;
4. ANY handler of a per-tick guard contains a `break` or a `raise`;
5. the final handler's body is not EXACTLY one nested `try` with a `BaseException` handler and
   no `finally:`/`else:` (IN-06);
6. the source carries a bare `asyncio.sleep`, or the `while` body has no shutdown-aware wait and
   no `wait_for` bound.

⚠️ Rule 4 is checked over EVERY handler and rules 3/5 over the FINAL one only — the shipped sleep
guard legitimately carries a narrow `except asyncio.TimeoutError: pass` ahead of its broad arm,
and that ordering is itself load-bearing (`asyncio.TimeoutError is TimeoutError`, whose MRO runs
through `OSError`).

**P-inner's calibration — nine mutants, BOTH directions.** The narrowing, the `break`, the
`raise`, the unguarded statement, the narrowed IN-06 fallback, the bare `asyncio.sleep`, the
excised `while`, **and the two widenings the plan insists on**: the per-tick arm widened to
`BaseException` (it wraps an `await`, and `asyncio.CancelledError` is not an `Exception`
subclass on this venv, so it swallows shutdown cancellation and hangs the 10 s gather) and the
same arm widened to `OSError` (it eats the budget expiry).

## Outstanding — one observation this plan did NOT close, recorded rather than silently absorbed

**A persistently failing interval WAIT would spin the loop hot.** The sleep guard's broad arm logs
and (correctly) does not `break`, so if `asyncio.wait_for(SHUTDOWN.wait(), …)` failed on every
iteration with something other than `TimeoutError`, the `while` would re-enter without delay. The
plan's 1(e) drives the reachable case — a failing TICK — and that case is bounded and gated.

⚠️ Scope honestly: the failing-WAIT case is **believed unreachable** rather than proven so.
`session_poll_interval_s()` is never-raising by construction (it goes through the shipped
`_env_float`, whose own gates are in `tests/test_mt5_relogin.py`) and `asyncio.Event.wait()` does
not raise. ⛔ No fix was attempted, and deliberately: the only remedies available are a fallback
`asyncio.sleep` — which P-inner's own rule 6 forbids for the reason `lifespan`'s 10-second gather
gives — or a `break`, which is the silent-death class this whole plan exists to prevent. A real fix
needs a decision about what a loop that cannot sleep should DO, which is a design question, not an
in-plan auto-fix.
