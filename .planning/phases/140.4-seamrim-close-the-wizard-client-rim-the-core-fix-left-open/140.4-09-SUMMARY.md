## Task 2 — the one-hop alias rule, and the global `Error` exclusion

### The rule, as a property

> **Taint propagates to an alias only through an expression that can carry the caught
> value's own TEXT.**

Text is the whole of what is defended: undici embeds the outgoing headers into
`err.message`. A classifier returning a **boolean**, or a constructor returning one of our
own **typed objects**, cannot carry a header no matter what it was derived from.

Channels: the bare identifier · `.message` / `.name` / `.stack` / `.cause` (and the
`["message"]` element-access form) · `.toString()` · `String(…)` / `JSON.stringify(…)` · a
template span · a conditional operand · a **text-forwarding** binary operand (`+`, `??`,
`||`, `&&` — **not** `===`, `instanceof`, or arithmetic, which yield booleans and numbers).
Everything else, including **every other call**, is opaque.

This is **coverage-law ROW 1**: the predicate is the artefact and it is a property, not a
roster.

### The three measured alternatives

| implementation | offences on the current 8-member roster |
|---|---|
| scope-blind regex alias rule | **51 hits, ~47 FALSE POSITIVES** (name collisions across scopes) |
| scope-aware AST, taint on **any** reference | **3 offences, ALL THREE FALSE** — `resilient-fetch.ts` ×2 (`const deadlineExceeded = isDeadlineError(err)`, a boolean) and `keys/[id]/permissions` (`const seamFailure = readSeamFailureCause(err)` → `.retryAfterSeconds`, a number) |
| **text-carrying channel (shipped)** | **0** |

The file's own warning, and why the third was chosen: *"a guard that cries wolf gets
widened until it catches nothing."*

### The `Error` exclusion

`/^(error|[\w$]*(Err|Error))$/` accepts the identifier `Error` itself, so
`console.error(…, err instanceof Error ? … )` reported the **global constructor**, which
carries nothing. Excluded by **exact name** (`GLOBAL_ERROR_CONSTRUCTOR = "Error"`), not by
pattern — `readError` is still flagged, and that is pinned by `ERROR-EXCLUSION −`. Nothing
in this codebase declares a binding named `Error`, so the exclusion cannot hide a real
caught value behind a rename. It surfaces at 3 sites under `140.4-10`'s widened roster
(`keys/sync`, `portfolio-optimizer`, `csv-finalize`) and at **0** on the current one.

### The vacuity fence — new, and specific to the AST path

`stripComments` runs **before** the parse and `ts.createSourceFile` **recovers** from a
malformed input rather than throwing, so a corrupted strip would yield a tree with no catch
clauses and `aliasOffences` would return `[]` for every file forever. Two hand-typed floors
(never `xs.length`): catch clauses **≥ 6**, and tainted aliases **≥ 2**.

The taint floor of 2 names its members: **`process-key-client.ts`'s `message`** and
**`keys/[id]/permissions`'s `rawMessage`**. Neither reaches a console sink — which is *why*
the roster scores zero. The engine is following real aliases on real source and finding them
clean; it is not finding nothing.
