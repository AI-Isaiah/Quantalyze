### Accepted deviation (recorded as instructed)

The round-2 Topic A fixer did not make DO-body DELETE/upsert on a replayed table a flat refusal.
It made them declinable only under a machine-checked `freshKeyProof`: the write must be keyed by a
block-local variable declared once as `uuid := gen_random_uuid()` and never reassigned (`:=`, `=`,
FOR/INTO/GET DIAGNOSTICS, shadowing, quoted spellings and labels are all checked). The reason: the
literal rule would permanently red `sql-gate-lint` on three applied, uneditable migrations. The
orchestrator accepted this. If the founder prefers the literal rule, that is a decision, because
those three migrations would then have no remedy.
