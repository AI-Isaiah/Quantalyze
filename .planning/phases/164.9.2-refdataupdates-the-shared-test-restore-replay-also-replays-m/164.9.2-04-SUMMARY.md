## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] The grep rc>1 arm could not be reached by pointing `REFDATA_AUDIT_LOG` at a directory**
- **Found during:** Task 2
- **Issue:** The plan's behaviour line says to reach the rc>1 branch by pointing the log at a
  directory. Under both workflows' step shapes, `node … > "$REFDATA_AUDIT_LOG"` fails first on
  the redirection, and `|| status=$?` catches it. So the step reports `the reference-data allowlist
  audit failed (exit 1)` and never reaches a grep. That arm would have asserted a branch it cannot
  reach.
- **Fix:** A pass-through `grep` stub in the harness bindir exits 2 when its argv contains
  `STUB_GREP_FAIL_ON`, and otherwise execs the real grep. Every rc>1 branch in both workflows'
  audit and self-test steps is reached by name, with an `unbind` calibration.
- **Files modified:** src/__tests__/test-restore-workflow-wiring.test.ts
- **Commit:** 1ed444bd5

**2. [Scope note] Worktree branch namespace**
- This checkout is a git worktree on `feat/164.9.2-refdataupdates`, not an `agent-*` branch, so
  the executor's worktree allow-list would refuse to commit. The orchestrator named this branch and
  checkout explicitly, and plans 01-03 committed here. Commits went to that branch with explicit
  paths (`git commit -- <paths>`). Nothing was pushed.
