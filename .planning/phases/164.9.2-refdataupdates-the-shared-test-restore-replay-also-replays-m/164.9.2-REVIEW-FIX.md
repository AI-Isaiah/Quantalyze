## Measurement that contradicted the reviewers

Both reviewers measured **0** DO-body writes on replayed tables. The first `--audit` that could tell
a DO body from a function body measured **4 on public tables** (plus 9 on `auth.users`, all
self-test fixtures deleting their own uid):

- `20260407164606_perfect_match.sql:37`: a DO-body `UPDATE profiles SET is_admin` reading an
  app setting and `auth.users`
- `20260602183000_b5b_api_key_delete_atomicity.sql:406/:408`: DELETEs of its own probe rows
- `20260713120000_cleanup_abandoned_wizard_drafts.sql:188`: an upsert of its own probe row inside a
  rolled-back subtransaction

Each got a `decline:` line whose reason says why it reaches no replayed row (D-02: decline, never
skip). **`auth.*` DO-body writes are deliberately not counted.** `auth.users` survives the DROP, so a
write a DO body made there is still on TEST after a restore. This is asymmetric with the top-level
`auth.users` UPDATE refusal, which is stricter by design, and the asymmetry is stated in the allowlist.
The C5 census moved by measurement: `C5_ENTRY_COUNT` 6 → 10, decline pin 2 → 6, C5 files 6 → 9.

## Judgement calls, stated

- **lower()/upper() not admitted** (WR-03 proposed them). They are real functions resolved on the
  search_path, and the replay runs under `public, pg_catalog`, so a `public.lower` would win.
  COALESCE/NULLIF/GREATEST/LEAST/ROW are grammar constructs with no lookup.
- **decline: now also counts DO-body writes**, rather than using a new line kind. It matches the
  allowlist's own definition ("accounted for, not replayed") and needed no parser or restore-awk
  change.
- **IN-03's audit cross-check not built.** When it fired, the only remedy would be dropping the
  `update:` line. The audit then refuses the literal UPDATE as unlisted and refuses a decline over
  it, so the check would have no way through. It needs a founder decision on how a reviewed
  trigger is accepted.
- **Top-level `SELECT fn()` / `CALL` and dynamic SQL stay untraced.** SFH-02 offered either
  refusing them or stating the limit. This fix states it: on the `C5 scope:` line and in the
  allowlist's limitation 2.
