# Phase 167.2.1.1.1.1 Plan 04: the holdings poll under the price rule Summary

**The allocator holdings poll now writes a missing ticker price as a named gap row with fixed warning copy, values USDE/PYUSD/USDB at 1.0 by the anchor's rule, and reads OKX spot from the wallet `cashBal` so open profit is counted once.**

## Performance

- **Duration:** about 45 min (start time not captured to the second)
- **Completed:** 2026-10-27T20:05Z
- **Tasks:** 2 (a tracer and an auto task, both TDD)
- **Files modified:** 2 (`analytics-service/services/allocator_positions.py`, `analytics-service/tests/test_allocator_positions.py`)

## Accomplishments

- **OKX counted once (R-1, D-01, D-05 forward half).** `_extract_okx_cash_balances` reads `info["data"][0]["details"][*].cashBal`, and `_fetch_spot_rows` overrides ccxt's `total` (OKX `eq`, which is `cashBal + upl`) with it for `exchange.id == "okx"`. The tracer runs a hand-typed payload (invented round numbers in the recorded cassette's shape) through ccxt's real `okx.parse_trading_balance`: the USDT spot row is 100000.0 (not 104000.0) and the sum of `holding_equity_contribution` is 134000.0 (typed: 100000 + 0.5 x 60000 + 4000), where the `eq` read at plan base gave 138000.0.
- **Gap rows, not measured zeros (D-02 row 5).** A non-stable asset whose ticker gives no usable `last` (omitted by the bulk call, None, 0, negative, NaN, or bulk and per-symbol both raising) is written with `mark_price` 0.0, `value_usd` 0.0 and `raw_payload["price_gap"] = True`. `usable_price` from `services/price_rule.py` is the one predicate. The per-symbol fallback no longer invents `{"last": 0.0}`.
- **Fixed copy.** `SPOT_PRICE_GAP_NOTE` ("Couldn't read a price for some {venue} balances — sync will retry automatically.") is returned as the warning; when the derivative arm also failed the derivative note comes first and both survive, joined by one space. No symbol, amount or venue text.
- **One stable-value rule.** `is_stable_value` replaces the STABLECOINS-only test, so USDE, PYUSD and USDB skip the ticker at 1.0, matching `_price_wallet_totals`.
