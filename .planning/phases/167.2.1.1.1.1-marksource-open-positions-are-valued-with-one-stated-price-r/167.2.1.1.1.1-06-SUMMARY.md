# Phase 167.2.1.1.1.1 Plan 06: the two allocator curves agree on a book with open positions Summary

**One new test module, `test_marksource_cross_curve.py`, runs one hand-typed venue state per marked day through the real poll and the real refresh (legacy curve) and through the venue's real equity reader, the real key derive and the real compose (derived curve), and proves OKX, Bybit, Binance, MT5 USD, MT5 BTC and sFOX agree on every marked day within `max($1, 0.5% of level)`; each venue family's case is shown able to fail by a drill that was restored byte-equal.**

## Performance

- **Tasks:** 2 (a tracer and one auto task), 3 commits
- **Files:** 1 created (`analytics-service/tests/test_marksource_cross_curve.py`, six cases), no production file changed

## Accomplishments

- **Tolerance, once.** `TOLERANCE_USD = 1.0`, `TOLERANCE_REL = 0.005` and `agree(legacy, derived)` are typed once (`grep -acF 'TOLERANCE_REL = 0.005'` prints 1). `assert_curves_agree` names the case, the day and both levels on a miss.
- **Legacy leg, real.** Each venue's typed payload goes through `fetch_allocator_holdings` (ccxt's real balance parser for OKX, Bybit and Binance; the real MT5 facade over a recording transport; the sFOX read facade double), the returned rows are stored as the holdings of the read day D + 1, and the real `run_refresh_allocator_equity_daily_job` runs at 01:30Z of D + 1. It labels its one row D (plan 05). `legacy_levels` reuses `carry._seed_key`, `carry._seed_holding` and `carry._run_refresh` by setting the helper module's `TODAY` to each read day; nothing was copied.
- **Derived leg, real.** The venue's own equity comes from the real reader over the same payload: OKX `fetch_account_equity_and_upnl_usd` (`totalEq` / `upl`), Bybit and Binance `read_marked_equity_usd`. That figure is the stored mark. The real key derive (`derive_key_ccxt`, `derive_mt5_key`, `derive_sfox_key`) persists the marked series and the real compose (`compose_curve`) draws the level, which every case asserts equals the typed venue equity.
- **R-1 is a case.** The OKX open profit is 4000 on 134000 and 5000 on 137000, against tolerances of 670 and 685, so the `eq` double count (138000 and 142000) is far outside it.

## Deviations from Plan

None to a rule. Four notes the orchestrator should know, none of which weakens a verify:

1. **[Drill-forced test change] The sFOX case first went RED on the wrong assertion.** The first D-N24 run failed at `assert warning is None` in the poll loop, a side assertion, before the curves were compared. The plan's rule is that a drill must turn the agreement assertion RED. The warnings are now collected and asserted last; D-N24 re-run: RED at `agree(800.0, 1045.0)`. Commit `e2cb12e11`.
2. **Typed figures differ from the plan's `<behavior>` suggestions for MT5 USD, MT5 BTC and sFOX, and the days are the roundtrip scenarios'.** The roundtrip harnesses (`derive_mt5_key`, `derive_sfox_key`) carry fixed deal ledgers and NAV series, so the cases use their scenarios: MT5 USD equity 108500.0 / 113600.0 (balance plus a floating 3000.0 / 3100.0, above 542.5 / 568.0) instead of 103000.0; sFOX 1045.0 / 1110.0 (USDC 245.0 / 260.0, above 5.225 / 5.55) instead of 12500.0; Bybit, Binance and OKX use their own invented figures. The plan's real requirement, that each neutered difference sits above `max(1, 0.5% of level)`, holds for every case (the drill readings above), and the module asserts the sFOX balances equal the roundtrip module's `SFOX_MARKS`.
3. **The derive's own equity read is fed, not exercised.** `derive_key_ccxt` patches `services.exchange.fetch_account_equity_and_upnl_usd`, so the OKX second mark is written by the real derive from `(equity, upl)` that this module read with the real reader over the same payload. The reader and the derive are both real; the call between them is the harness's.
4. **Marks for Bybit, Binance, MT5 and sFOX are stored with `seed_mark`** (as the roundtrip module does) from the real reader's figures, not written by the derive; the OKX second day is written by the derive. The mark WRITER's own refusals are out of scope, and the module docstring says so: a Bybit or Binance book holding BTC or ETH beside a USDT-only derive wallet would be refused `wallet_basis_mismatch` by `build_mark_read` (plans 15 and 18), so that day carries no mark and is not a marked day. The Bybit and Binance cases hold non-USDT assets on purpose (the plan asks for them, so the poll's pricing is exercised); they prove rule agreement on days that do have a mark.
