# Phase 167.2.1.1.1.1 Plan 15: the refresh names what it left out for want of a price Summary

**A balance the legacy refresh leaves out because it has no price (dust below the floor, or unsizable) is now named on the stored snapshot as `__price_gap_left_out__ = {count, symbols, dust, unsizable}`, kept through truncation and never added to a level, and a book left out entirely writes no snapshot and says `price_gap_left_out` in its audit.**

## Accomplishments

- `services/equity_reconstruction.py`: `"__price_gap_left_out__"` added to `_BREAKDOWN_PROTECTED_KEYS` (comment names D-06); stamped on `breakdown_to_store` after the empty-book gate and before `_cap_breakdown`, beside `__pnl_missing__`, when `price_gap_dust_rows + price_gap_unsizable_rows > 0`, with `symbols` taken from `list(latest.price_gap_left_out_symbols)`; `_sum_breakdowns` docstring names the new record; the empty-book gate chooses `derivative_pnl_unreported`, else `price_gap_left_out`, else `no_holdings_today`. `value_usd` is still computed from `total`.
- Stored shape, as the test pins it: `{"count": 1, "symbols": ["XYZ"], "dust": 1, "unsizable": 0}` for the dust book (USDT 100000.0, XYZ 1.0 unpriced, newest XYZ price 50.0 = 50.00 against a floor of 100.00); `{"count": 1, "symbols": ["XYZ"], "dust": 0, "unsizable": 1}` for a never-priced XYZ under the recorded `unsizable: left-out` choice.
- Tests: 3 new cases in the carry-forward file (`test_dust_left_out_is_named_on_the_snapshot`, `test_unsizable_left_out_is_named_on_the_snapshot`, `test_price_gap_left_out_only_book_writes_no_snapshot`), one truncation pin in `test_equity_reconstruction.py`, and the `_sum_breakdowns` pin in `test_reconstruct_whole_account.py` now carries a `__price_gap_left_out__` record beside the `__pnl_missing__` one and asserts it absent from the sum. That sum pin passes before and after the production change (`_sum_breakdowns` already drops non-numeric entries); it is a guard against a future regression, not a RED.
