## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed / design additions

**1. [Rule 2 - W9 critical functionality] `ledger_digest_error` field on `CompletenessReport`**
- **Issue:** The plan computes the digest in `build_deribit_native_ledger` but guards it in the worker. A raise inside the build would fail the ledger build itself, contradicting "the capture and the digest can never fail a working derive".
- **Fix:** The build catches and records the class name on the report (`ledger_digest_error`, defaulted `None`); the worker writes `{"ledger_digest_error": ...}` and logs one WARNING. A writer refusal in the worker is caught separately.
- **Files:** `deribit_ingest.py`, `job_worker.py`. **Commit:** `8eb48a557`.

**2. Tie-break on the digest sort** adds the serialised row as the third sort key, because two subaccount scopes can share `(timestamp, id)` and the hash must not depend on page order. Tested.

**3. The unvaluable-collapse return of `fetch_deribit_native_account_state`** (the `ValueError` path) also carries the capture fields. It is a readable response, so the stored snapshot is still the honest read.

No other deviations. The plan's `read_at_iso` is a wall-clock stamp, so two reads of the same account are not byte-equal in that one field by design.
