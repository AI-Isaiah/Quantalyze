<objective>
Ship work item (A), part 1: the additive capture. The derive already reads Deribit's account summary once. This plan stores that read, a per-currency identity verdict and a digest of the crawled ledger in the key's `key_inputs` payload. Nothing it computes changes.

Purpose: SC-1 says the identity is confirmed first with one account-summary read at the account's next derive. The dev machine cannot take that read (Deribit keys are decrypted only in the Railway worker), and a second exchange read is exactly the live-state leak D-03 removes. So the read is the stored snapshot of the derive's existing single read. Plan 05 reads it on PROD after the next 05:30 UTC derive.
Output: new defaulted fields on `DeribitNativeAccountState`, a payload writer/reader pair for `account_summary` and for `ledger_digest`, the Deribit-gated epilogue fields, and one new test file.

Scope fence (PATTERNS finding 8): the composite path (`_reconstruct_deribit` in `job_worker.py`) also reads live account state and stores nothing. D-03 and RESEARCH OQ-8 scope the capture to key-mode `key_inputs`. Composites stay recompute-only (Wave 5). This is deliberate, not an omission.
</objective>
