## Deviations from Plan

**1. [Design] `_inception_reading` signature.** `ledger` dropped (unused) and `venue` added (the orphan refusal needs it). Plan 06 only passes `dust_cap_usd=` to it, which is unaffected. No behavior change.

**2. [Design] Balance-anchored wedge merges rather than replaces.** The plan said `dataclasses.replace(ledger, terminal_upnl_native=alt)`; replacing would zero the wedge of every currency without a balance read. The merge is identical when `alt` covers every currency with a wedge, and honest when it does not. Tested.

**3. [Rule 3 - blocking] `_roll_buckets` and `_union_calendar` extracted from `_native_nav_levels`.** The plan says to roll "through the exact functions `_native_nav_levels` uses" and forbids a second roll implementation; calling the levels function would run the gate and raise on a breaching ledger. A behavior-preserving extraction was the only way to share the roll without raising. `tests/test_native_nav.py` is unchanged and green.

**4. [Test tolerance] USD-valued oracles use 1e-6.** The residual of a 2000 BTC roll carries about 1e-13 BTC of float noise, which is 3e-9 USD at a 30000 mark, so the plan's illustrative 1e-9 tolerance on dollars was too tight. The native residual itself is asserted within 1e-12 as planned.

**5. [Test oracle correction] The material-residual fixture's ratio.** The residual there is 0.499988 BTC (terminal 0.5 less Σpnl 6.479214 plus the 6.479202 withdrawn), not 0.5; the test oracle was worked forward by hand and corrected before the commit.

**6. Merge of `origin/main`** was done although the plan text only says to re-pick the version (see key decisions).

No other deviations.
