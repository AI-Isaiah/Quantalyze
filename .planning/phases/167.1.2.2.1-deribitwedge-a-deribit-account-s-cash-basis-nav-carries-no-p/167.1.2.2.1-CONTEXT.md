<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Emptied stretch (SC-3, founder D-18(b))
- **D-01:** A day counts as "emptied" when the account's reconstructed level is **below 1% of its prior peak**. The founder chose this on 2026-10-09 (Recommended) over a fixed `$25` floor and over a combined rule. The threshold is relative so that it behaves the same for any account size.
  - While emptied, the account composes as **zero capital with no return**: no daily return is written for those days, and the account adds no weight to the book.
  - The book continues normally on re-deposit.
  - The stretch is neither refused nor degraded.
  - **Reversibility:** costly. Changing the band later changes which days carry returns, so every affected book has to be recomputed.
- **D-02 (orchestrator default):** "prior peak" means the highest level reached before that day, never a later one. Using a later peak would let a future deposit reclassify past days.

### Derive determinism (new scope, founder 2026-10-09)
- **D-03:** Two derives over the same stored inputs must produce identical returns. Today they differ on 372 of 1187 days, because the derive reads live account state (debug note).
  - The founder chose to **fix this in this phase** (Recommended) rather than book it separately. The reason: the SC-4 re-baseline is only trustworthy if a re-run reproduces it.
  - Acceptance: a test runs the derive twice on frozen inputs and asserts byte-equal returns. It fails on today's code.
  - Any live read that is legitimately needed, such as SC-1's one account-summary identity read, is captured as an input and stored. It is never read silently during a derive.
  - This item must also be added to the ROADMAP section as success criterion 6, through `/gsd-phase --edit`.

### Carried from 167.1.2.2 CONTEXT D-18 (locked)
- The fix is the writer: the cash_settlement wedge in `build_deribit_native_ledger`. It is not a compose workaround.
- The recompute joins the Wave 5 consolidated PROD recompute, so no row is recomputed twice.
- PROD readings are taken marker first, and record counts and verdicts only.
- 2026-10-09, orchestrator: the 05:30 UTC cron re-derives each connected key with the deployed code, so merge-and-deploy of PR-2 is the keys' re-baseline (plan 08 brackets it); the Wave 5 consolidated recompute covers the two private composites only, so no row is recomputed twice.

### Founder decisions after research (2026-10-09)
- **D-04 (amends D-02):** the "prior peak" restarts after a re-deposit ends an emptied stretch, so a small re-deposit is not read as still empty. Founder choice (Recommended).
- **D-05:** the refused allocator's book reads "rebuilding" until the SC-5 +1.3% inception residual is measured and fixed or routed. Founder choice (Recommended).
- **D-06 (founder 2026-10-10, after plan 06 Task 1 measured `sc5-verdict: H-D`):** the SC-5 +1.3% residual sits in the compose-versus-writer gap, not in the writer's ledger (options-holding account: compose implied start c = 0.01311 of the first-return level; writer prev0 ratio -8.5e-9 under both the current wedge and the balance anchor). Fix it IN THIS PHASE, in PR-2: the writer stores its own composed flows and day P&L and the allocator compose reads them (RESEARCH 6.4), via gap planning. Plan 06 Task 2 (the inception dust cap, precondition `cap-verdict: passes` already met) still ships. Founder choice (Recommended).
- **D-07 (amends D-04, founder 2026-10-10):** a re-deposit restarts the prior peak only when it lifts the level to at least `EMPTIED_FRACTION` of the prior peak; a smaller (dust) deposit leaves the emptied stretch running. Measured on PROD by plan 06: one ~1e-8-of-peak deposit restarted the peak and left 38 near-zero days classified live (717 emptied instead of RESEARCH's 755). Founder choice (Recommended).
- **D-08 (amends D-07, founder 2026-10-10, after PR-2 review WR-03):** a re-deposit also ends an emptied stretch and restarts the prior peak when it lifts the level to at least an absolute floor of 100 USD, even if that is under `EMPTIED_FRACTION` of the old peak. Reason: the old peak never decays, so without a floor a real small restart (e.g. 5k USD after a 1M peak) would compose as zero capital indefinitely. The PROD dust deposit was cents, so it stays dust and the 755-day reading holds. Founder choice (Recommended).
- **D-09 (founder 2026-10-10, after PR-2 review round 2; amends D-08 and overrides the worst-MEDIUM-no-fixer rule):** fix the round-2 MEDIUMs before ship. (a) A level counts as emptied only when it is below the SMALLER of `EMPTIED_FRACTION` of the prior peak and the 100 USD floor, on replayed levels and on the last-day live anchor alike, so a balance of 100 USD or more is always live capital whether or not a deposit follows (replaces D-08's deposit-only test). (b) A materially negative last-day live anchor refuses, as on main. (c) A stored return explains an emptied stretch's first day only when it is a loss that accounts for the drop, not any non-zero return. (d) The stitched legacy-roll flag is raised only for venues that write a writer basis (Deribit). Founder choice (Recommended).
