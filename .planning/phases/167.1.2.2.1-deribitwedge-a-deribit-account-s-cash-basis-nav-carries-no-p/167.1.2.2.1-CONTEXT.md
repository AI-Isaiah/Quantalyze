<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Emptied stretch (SC-3, founder D-18(b))
- **D-01:** A day counts as "emptied" when the account's reconstructed level is **below 1% of its prior peak**. The founder chose this on 2026-10-09 (Recommended) over a fixed `$25` floor and over a combined rule. The threshold is relative so that it behaves the same for any account size.
  - While emptied, the account composes as **zero capital with no return**: no daily return is written for those days, and the account adds no weight to the book.
  - The book continues normally on re-deposit.
  - The stretch is neither refused nor degraded.
  - **Reversibility:** costly. Changing the band later changes which days carry returns, so every affected book has to be recomputed.
- **D-02 (orchestrator default):** "prior peak" means the highest level reached before that day, never a later one. Using a later peak would let a future deposit reclassify past days.

### Derive determinism (new scope, founder 2026-10-09)
- **D-03:** Two derives over the same stored inputs must produce identical returns. Today they differ on 372 of 1187 days, because the derive reads live account state (debug note).
  - The founder chose to **fix this in this phase** (Recommended) rather than book it separately. The reason: the SC-4 re-baseline is only trustworthy if a re-run reproduces it.
  - Acceptance: a test runs the derive twice on frozen inputs and asserts byte-equal returns. It fails on today's code.
  - Any live read that is legitimately needed, such as SC-1's one account-summary identity read, is captured as an input and stored. It is never read silently during a derive.
  - This item must also be added to the ROADMAP section as success criterion 6, through `/gsd-phase --edit`.

### Carried from 167.1.2.2 CONTEXT D-18 (locked)
- The fix is the writer: the cash_settlement wedge in `build_deribit_native_ledger`. It is not a compose workaround.
- The recompute joins the Wave 5 consolidated PROD recompute, so no row is recomputed twice.
- PROD readings are taken marker first, and record counts and verdicts only.

### Claude's Discretion

- Whether the +1.3% `inception_unreconciled` residual (SC-5) is fixed here or routed by name. The choice follows its measured cause.
- When the SC-1 identity read happens, at the account's next derive. It is taken read-only.
