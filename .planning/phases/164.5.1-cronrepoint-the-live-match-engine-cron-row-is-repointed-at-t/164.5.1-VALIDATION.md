named explicitly.

in `.github/workflows/ci.yml` BY SYMBOL, not from a line number restated here
(`[164.7-CITATION-DRIFT-01]`).

## Sampling Rate

- **After every task commit:** the matching quick-run command above.
- **After every plan wave:** full Python suite AND full vitest suite.
- **Before `/gsd-verify-work`:** both full suites green.
- **Wave B is NOT CI-gated.** Its PROD statements are gated by D4 — three reviewers
  (`migration-reviewer`, `rls-policy-auditor`, `silent-failure-hunter`) plus founder execution.
  That gate cannot be automated and must not be reported as if CI covered it.
