<objective>
The phase's TRACER: wire ONE path — the kill-switch read — end to end through every layer this
phase modifies, so a Supabase gateway 504 produces a loud, distinct, FAIL-CLOSED engine stop
instead of the documented fail-open `True`.

`_engine_is_enabled()` is fail-OPEN by documented design; its own comment gives the rationale

WITHOUT RETRY, and retry is what dissolves it: after exhausting retries a failure is no longer a
blip. Sentry `QUANTALYZE-1D` (5 events, 2026-09-12) is the dated production sighting of the guard
failing open.

seam in `services/db.py`, never inside `db_execute`; 3 attempts with ~1/2/4s exponential backoff

status logged at ERROR; fail-closed applies to `_engine_is_enabled_cached` too; the mid-run
re-check at the UNCACHED accessor stays). This plan also ANSWERS the open structural fork CONTEXT.md
requires the plan to answer rather than inherit — see the decision block below. D4 (D-04) does not
gate this plan: nothing here writes to any database.

## The structural fork, ANSWERED

CONTEXT.md: *"either `_engine_is_enabled` becomes `async def` and those call sites drop `to_thread`
for a plain `await`, or the retry helper stays sync and sleeps with `time.sleep`."*

**Answer: `_engine_is_enabled` and `_engine_is_enabled_cached` become `async def`, and
`db_read_with_retry` is `async` and wraps `db_execute`.** Three reasons, measured at HEAD:

1. **One seam, not two.** `_claim_priority` (`main_worker.py:746`) and `_reset` (`:1066`) already
   call `await db_execute(fn)`; plan 05 wires them to the same retry. A sync helper would force a
   second async sibling, i.e. two notions of "retry a Supabase read".
2. **The house idiom is async.** `_okx_bills_fetch_with_backoff` is `async def` with
   `await asyncio.sleep(delay)` — the idiom CONTEXT.md names as the one to copy.
3. **The read joins the bounded executor.** Today the guard's Supabase call runs on
   `asyncio.to_thread`'s DEFAULT pool; routing it through `db_execute` puts it on `_DB_EXECUTOR`
   with its 80%-saturation WARNING. That is a gain, not a side effect.

**Cost, stated so it is not discovered mid-implementation:** measured 2026-09-16, `_engine_is_enabled`
is referenced by 3 production call sites (`match.py:1713`, `:1954` via the cached accessor, `:2029`)
and by ~20 `monkeypatch.setattr` sites plus 3 direct calls across
`analytics-service/tests/test_match_router.py`,
`analytics-service/tests/test_maybe_single_none_guards.py` and
`analytics-service/tests/test_status_contract_match_sim_portfolio.py`. All of them move in this
plan. ⛔ The mid-run re-check at `match.py:2029` keeps the UNCACHED accessor and keeps polling fresh
every iteration — that is a safety property (F1/MED8), not a style choice, and the async conversion
must not collapse it into the cached one.

Purpose: make the only manual stop control in the system provable.
Output: a retry seam, a tri-state guard, a distinct closed status, and a test that has been
observed to go RED when the disposition is neutered.
