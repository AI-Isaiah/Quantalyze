# Phase 164.5.1 Plan 09: the live cron repoint and the ledger-refresh activation

The 161.1 ledger refresh was then activated, and the oracle re-captured to match.**

## Accomplishments

- **The repoint (Step 2).** `cron.schedule` UPSERTs on `(jobname, username)` and PRESERVES the jobid, so jobid 1 kept its identity, its `0 * * * *` schedule and `active`. `command` went 546 → 39 characters, `command_sha256` `1400dddd…` → `0a0a0729…`. Verified by read-back, not by the write's return value.
- **The first tick, which is the whole phase working in production.** `cron.job_run_details` shows the command shape genuinely changed in execution — 06:00Z returned `DO` (the old inline block), 07:00Z returned `1 row` (a function call). That `succeeded` proves only ENQUEUE, so the verdict was read from `net._http_response`: `status_code=200`, `timed_out=false`, empty `error_msg`, and a body of `{"status":"partial","processed":2,"skipped":8,"failed":0,"retention_deleted":2,"duration_s":8.94,"next_cursor":"aaaaaaaa-…001"}`. Batching engaged (`partial` + a cursor rather than running at the gateway wall), nothing failed, and the run came in roughly 5× under the 44.67 s baseline and far under the 60 s gateway ceiling. The cursor was CHECKED rather than assumed: it resolves to a real `profiles` row, and 3 of 35 profiles carry patterned UUIDs.
- **`[VAULTTICK-EMPTYKEY-01]` closed on a production reading** of the live command after the repoint. Before the repoint jobid 1's command contained no `btrim` call at all, so the empty-key guard was unreachable in production while being green in the repo — exactly the gap the entry existed to name. A repo-side measurement never settles a production claim in this phase, and did not here.
- **The activation (Step 3), its DEFER lifted on measurement.** `ledger_refresh_enabled = true`; `ledger_refresh_fanout` registered as jobid 40 on `25 * * * *`, active. Preconditions were read BEFORE the write so the session could not register a job that would fire into a dormant system.
- **The oracle re-captured twice, by script.** After the repoint: 14 jobs before and after, exactly one moved (jobid 1). After the registration: 14 → 15, exactly one added (jobid 40, `SELECT public.enqueue_ledger_refresh_for_strategies();`, sha `c0d4cda3…`), no other job's sha moved. Both captures came from `prod-prober.yml`'s `capture-manifest` mode (the second is run `35197830300`, `captured_at 2026-09-17T08:05:56Z`, `database_marker` reading PRODUCTION) and were reviewed job by job before commit.
- **P3-C falsified, in writing.** The gate's core claim does not hold, and the falsifying measurement is in the record. This is reported as a defect in the gate, not routed around.
