## Design

The Withheld verdict now has two scopes, both built on one hook, `useFullHistoryWithheld()` (`MetricsColumn.tsx`): `!leverageApplied && isHeadlineWithheld(dataQuality, basis)`, with no window term.

- Full-history surfaces (EOY Returns, Calmar by Year, the EOY bar chart, the Monthly Returns YTD cell) take it as is, so a zoom does not switch the dash off. They show the whole record inside a zoom too.
- The rail's slice figures and the rolling charts' avg label keep the slice-aware gate, `!selected && useFullHistoryWithheld()` (D-78).

`MetricsColumn` calls the hook once and passes the flag down. Panels outside it (`EndOfYearBarsPanel`, `MonthlyReturnsHeatmap`) take it as a required prop; small wrappers in `FactsheetView.tsx` (`EndOfYearBars`, `MonthlyReturns`) read the hook.
