### R2-02 (LOW, confidence 6): The new SFH-01 `console.warn` fires on every resolve, probes included, for ordinary non-rankable states

**File:** `src/lib/factsheet/composite-read-path.ts:879-882`

**Issue:**

- **It reads as a defect but fires on routine states.** The warn is unconditional on the `else if` arm. That arm covers the not-rankable case, which is routine: `computing` during every recompute of a chain-broken strategy, `pending`, and `failed` indefinitely.
