<objective>
Close the one verification gap of Phase 164.6.6.3.3 (review round 1 WR-04, decided by the founder as D-15 on 2026-10-15, "Withhold them too"): on a Withheld row the KPI strip's α and IR cells read "—" with no tone, so nothing under the "Headline figures withheld" note shows a number.

Purpose: today `KpiStrip` in `FactsheetView.tsx` fills the α/IR cells from the whole-record joint (`const shown = suppressRelative ? null : j`) without consulting the headline verdict, so a Withheld row prints numeric α and IR, which compound across the very break the note names, directly under that note. WR-01 already nulls the trailing windows on a Withheld row; this applies the same rule to the two joint cells.

Output: one exported predicate in `MetricsColumn.tsx` shared by the note and the strip, the strip gate in `FactsheetView.tsx`, and render tests seen RED on today's code, GREEN after, and RED again under a neuter restored from a byte backup.

Decisions taken in this plan (stated so the executor does not re-decide them):
- Scope of "Withheld" = every Withheld cause (unmeasured, `recomputing`, `failed`), because the predicate is `headlineWithheld === true` and the D-14 tests already assert "in every cause the figures read '—' and no whole-record figure is shown". The `failed` cause has no strip note, and its cells are dashed anyway.
- Window behaviour unchanged (D-78): the Withheld note never renders inside a selected window (`spanNote` takes `windowCoverageNote` there), the seven cells are the slice's own re-derived figures, and the α/IR cells keep the slice joint. The gate is therefore `!selected`.
- Leverage: the gate also requires `!leverageApplied`, mirroring exactly when `spanNote` uses `headlineCoverageNote` (a chain-broken row is not leverage-eligible, so this is a mirror, not a behaviour change).
- Basis: the predicate requires `basis === "cash_settlement"`, as the note does; under mark-to-market or smoothed the strip shows that basis's own figures and `suppressRelative` keeps its existing rule.
- Flat-leg line: gated off on a Withheld full-history strip (UI-SPEC section 2: "only when the α/IR cells are shown"; the cells no longer show the joint). The paired-floor line (`jointFloorReason`) is left unchanged: its gate is the joint being withheld, which is independent of this decision, and it stays a true statement.
- `stripRelative`: no copy change. The Withheld arm of `headlineCoverageNote` returns the same sentence for `strip` and `stripRelative`, and that sentence names neither α nor IR. Chosen over the alternative of adding an α/IR clause to the Withheld sentence, per D-15.
</objective>
