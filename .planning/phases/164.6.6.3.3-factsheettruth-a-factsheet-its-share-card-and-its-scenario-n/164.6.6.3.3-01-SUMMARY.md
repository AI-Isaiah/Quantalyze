## D-13 (founder decision) and its implementation

The checkpoint returned the contradiction; the founder chose to judge a composite's `twr_chain_broken` on its stitched series (D-13 in CONTEXT.md). Implemented in `run_stitch_composite_job` (`job_worker.py`):
- the member-union loop skips `twr_chain_broken`; every other `NAV_TWR_GUARD_KEYS` flag, the allocated-capital keys and `used_heuristic_capital` still union;
- the flag is set (and `member_warned` promoted) when `_cash_basis_result.headline_since` is not None, the same predicate `compute_all_metrics` uses to set the date;
- a clean re-stitch pops a stale `twr_chain_broken` next to the stale `headline_since` (the path merges into existing flags, so without the pop the flag would outlive its date and D-03 would withhold the row forever).

**Behaviour change:** a composite is `complete_with_warnings` for a chain break exactly when its stitched series has an interior break. Before, a member meta flag promoted regardless of the window, and an in-window NaN with no member flag did not promote. Single-key parity is now exact. `headline_since` alone still promotes nothing.
