### Implementation

`managerRows?: Record<string, { editHref: string; actions: ReactNode; note?: ReactNode }>`, no default. Header: keyed `Fragment` per `COLUMNS` iteration, Status th emitted when `i === 0 && managerRows` (plain `scope="col"`, `sticky top-0 z-20`, no button, no aria-sort, no `left-*`). Body: Status td right after the Strategy td. In-name Badge guard `!managerRows && s.status !== "published"`. OwnershipTag keeps `visibility === "owner-all-statuses" &&` immediately before it with `!managerRows &&` after (P8 window). Actions td: `managerRows ? <div flex flex-nowrap ...>{Edit link when an entry exists}{slot}</div> : ownerRowActions`, and `s.status === "published" && !managerRows` in front of `<SimulateImpactButton` (pin 7). `emptyRowColSpan` adds `+ (managerRows ? 1 : 0)` with its comment extended. Placeholder rows get a Status td (`—`).
