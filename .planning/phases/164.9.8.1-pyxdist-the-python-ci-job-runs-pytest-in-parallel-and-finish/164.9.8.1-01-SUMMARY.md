## Root cause (RESEARCH Flake 1, confirmed on this tree)

The conftest hook ran after xdist's `WorkerInteractor` hook had already read `iter_markers("xdist_group")`, so no nodeid ever received the `@shared_test_db` suffix and each live DB test was its own work unit. The live fence tests therefore raced across workers. No test assertion, sentinel or selection changed.
