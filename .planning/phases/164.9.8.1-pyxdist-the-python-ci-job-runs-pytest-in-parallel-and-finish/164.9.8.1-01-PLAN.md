<objective>
Make the `shared_test_db` xdist group real, and prove it with a gate that can fail.

Implements D-01 (fix the grouping, audit for ungrouped lane writers) under D-03/D-04.

Output: one decorator plus a dated lineage note in `analytics-service/tests/conftest.py`, and the new gate `analytics-service/tests/test_xdist_group_is_real.py`. The lane soak for this flake (D-01b) is plan 04, because it needs the lane.
