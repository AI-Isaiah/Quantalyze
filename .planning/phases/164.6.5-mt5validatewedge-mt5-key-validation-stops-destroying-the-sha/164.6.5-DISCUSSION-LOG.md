## Decisions left deliberately open

**D-05, the supervision remedy.** Three candidates (wrap the image / external
supervision from the existing session monitor / container restart policy), each with a
real cost, and the choice only became visible once D-04 was discovered. Picking it from
the orchestrator seat without research would be the assumption-instead-of-measurement
this milestone exists to stop. Fenced by D-06 (the probe must exercise the IPC bridge —
a process-liveness probe would have passed for the whole 1h39m outage) and D-07 (the
restart must preserve the saved Wine-prefix login that makes the 2.0 s unattended
recovery possible).
