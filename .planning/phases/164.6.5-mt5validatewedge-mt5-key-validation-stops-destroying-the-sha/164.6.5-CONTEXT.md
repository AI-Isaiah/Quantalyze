# Phase 164.6.5: MT5VALIDATEWEDGE - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-09-22
**Status:** Ready for planning

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

A client can add an MT5 key, repeatedly, without taking MT5 validation down for
everyone — and if the terminal does wedge, it recovers WITHOUT a human.

⛔ **THE SCOPE FENCE, restated from the ROADMAP because it is the thing most likely to
be lost:** this phase does **NOT** fix the eviction. That is Phase 164.6.6. Do not let
"MT5 is back" read as "the finding is closed" — **the outage was the symptom; the
shared mutable terminal is the defect.** This phase makes the shared terminal survive
and self-heal; it does not stop it being shared.

⛔ **A SECOND FENCE, discovered during the codebase scout and NOT anticipated by the
ROADMAP — it changes what criterion 2 can even mean.** See D-04.

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### A. Root cause — the evidence bar, and what happens if it resists

- **D-01: "Named" means a stated mechanism that PREDICTS THE ASYMMETRY, not a
  plausible story.** The binding fact is that a switch *succeeded* cleanly the same
  day (`04:08:06 disconnected` → `04:08:07 authorized`, ~1 s) while the 11:02 and
  ~12:52 switches wedged. Any candidate root cause that cannot say why those runs
  differ is **not** the root cause and must be recorded as a rejected hypothesis, not
  adopted.
  — **Reversibility:** reversible — a documentation and evidence standard, not code.

- **D-02: ⛔ Sleeping, retrying, or serialising around the switch is NOT an acceptable
  close for criterion 1.** A retry that happens to work still leaves a shared terminal
  that dies on an account switch. If the investigation lands on "retry makes it go
  away", that is a FINDING TO RECORD, not a fix to ship under criterion 1.
  — **Reversibility:** reversible.

- **D-03: IF THE ROOT CAUSE RESISTS, THE PHASE STILL SHIPS — but it says so.** The
  phase is NOT blocked on criterion 1. Criteria 2–6 are independently valuable and
  each removes real harm (a 1h39m manual outage, a lying error message, an
  unmeasurable prober). If criterion 1 is not met, the phase ships with criterion 1
  **explicitly open**, a named residual routed to its own entry, and ⛔ **no
  redefinition of "root cause" downward to make it closeable.** Recording an honest
  open criterion is the success path; quietly restating a mitigation as a diagnosis is
  the failure this decision exists to prevent.
  — **Reversibility:** reversible.

- **D-03a: ⭐ THE FIRST HYPOTHESIS TO TEST, surfaced by the scout and cheap to check.**
  `deploy/mt5-gateway/railway-gateway.md` records, in its own words, that pinning the
  image digest pins the **base only**, because *"The MetaTrader terminal binary
  self-updates from the broker independently of the image and CANNOT be frozen."* A
  terminal that self-updated between 04:08 and 11:02 is a mechanism that PREDICTS THE
  ASYMMETRY (D-01) rather than merely tolerating it. ⚠️ This is a HYPOTHESIS with a
  documented mechanism, not a finding — it is written here so the researcher starts
  from it, and ⛔ it must be confirmed or rejected against evidence like any other.
  — **Reversibility:** reversible.

### B. Supervision — ⛔ the image is NOT OURS, and that is the phase's biggest surprise

- **D-04: ⛔⛔ THERE IS NO DOCKERFILE FOR THE TERMINAL IN THIS REPOSITORY.** MEASURED
  2026-09-22 at `89cc0358`: the gateway runs the **third-party prebuilt image**
  `gmag11/metatrader5_vnc:2.3`, deployed via Railway's *Deploy from Docker Image*
  (`deploy/mt5-gateway/railway-gateway.md`, `deploy/mt5-gateway/docker-compose.yml`).
  The only MT5 gateway artifacts in the repo are deploy descriptors, a diagnostic
  script (`scripts/mt5-diag.sh`) and the prober arm. The two Dockerfiles present
  (`analytics-service/Dockerfile`, `fly-egress-proxy/Dockerfile`) build other things.

  ⇒ **The ROADMAP's criterion 2 — "`terminal64.exe` runs under supervision" — cannot be
  delivered by editing an s6 service file in this repo, because this repo does not
  build that image.** The ROADMAP's own correction #4 is still exactly right about the
  DEFECT (s6 supervises desktop/VNC/nginx/cron/audio; the terminal is started once
  from the openbox autostart and nothing watches it) — what changes is WHERE a fix can
  live. ⛔ A planner that assumes an editable in-repo s6 tree will produce tasks that
  cannot be executed.
  — **Reversibility:** reversible as a finding; the remedy it forces is rated below.

- **D-05: THE SUPERVISION REMEDY IS AN OPEN CHOICE FOR RESEARCH — this context
  deliberately does NOT pick one, and it fences what a pick must satisfy.** The three
  live candidates, each with a real cost:
  1. **Wrap the image** — our own thin Dockerfile `FROM gmag11/metatrader5_vnc:2.3`
     adding an s6 service for `terminal64.exe`. Most faithful to criterion 2; ⛔ makes
     us the builder of an image we currently only consume, and the pin/self-update
     caveat in D-03a becomes ours to own.
  2. **External supervision** — the analytics-service session monitor (already running
     every 10 min) detects the wedge and triggers a restart through an existing
     channel. No new image; ⚠️ restart authority from outside the container is the
     hard part and must not become "a human with `railway redeploy`" by another name.
  3. **Container-level restart policy / healthcheck** — cheapest; ⛔ restarts the whole
     container rather than the process, and must be checked against D-06 before it can
     count.
  ⛔ **Whichever is chosen must satisfy D-06 and D-07, and the choice must be recorded
  with the reason it beat the other two.**
  — **Reversibility:** costly — option 1 makes this repo the owner of a container image
  it does not build today, and unwinding that means re-pointing the deploy and
  re-establishing the upstream pin.

- **D-06: ⛔ THE LIVENESS PROBE MUST PROBE THE IPC BRIDGE, NOT THE PROCESS.** MEASURED:
  throughout the 1h39m outage `terminal64.exe` was RUNNING and its UI was RESPONSIVE —
  the founder clicked its own Login dialog three ways and the Journal wrote nothing. ⛔
  A probe that checks "is the process alive", "is the window up", or "does the UI
  respond" **would have passed for the entire outage** and is therefore disqualified by
  construction. The probe must exercise the thing that actually died: the IPC/rpyc path
  that returns `-10005`.
  — **Reversibility:** reversible.

- **D-07: ⛔ THE RESTART MUST PRESERVE THE UNATTENDED AUTO-LOGIN.** MEASURED: a restart
  of `terminal64.exe` under the same Wine prefix, container and volume reached
  `authorized` in **2.0 s with no human**. ⚠️ The recorded belief that a restart lands
  on `-10004` needing an operator is WRONG here (ROADMAP correction #3) — but it is
  wrong *because the password is saved in the persistent Wine prefix*. Any remedy that
  discards or rebuilds that prefix destroys the very property that makes self-healing
  possible. ⛔ Restart the PROCESS; do not reset the PREFIX.
  — **Reversibility:** one-way — a remedy that wipes the persistent Wine prefix
  destroys the saved login, and restoring it requires a human entering a credential at
  the VNC console, which is precisely the 1h39m manual step this phase exists to
  remove.

### C. The heal, and the two causes of -10005

- **D-08: THE HEAL MUST ACT ON `ipc_fault`, AND ITS FIRST PRODUCTION VERDICT IS
  NEGATIVE.** Phase 164.6.2's heal reported `not_healed:ipc_fault` five consecutive
  times because it cannot drive a terminal whose IPC is dead — it never reaches a login
  attempt. ⛔ Do not read those five as the heal being broken in itself; read them as
  the heal being **pointed at the wrong layer** for this cause. Acting means escalating
  to the supervision remedy chosen in D-05, not adding another login attempt.
  — **Reversibility:** costly — `analytics-service/services/mt5_relogin.py` builds its
  verdicts through `_not_healed` and downstream keys off the `not_healed:` prefix, so
  changing what a verdict means touches every consumer of that prefix.

- **D-09: ⛔ `-10005` HAS AT LEAST TWO CAUSES WITH OPPOSITE REMEDIES, AND THE SHIPPED
  REMEDY STRING NAMES ONLY ONE.** MEASURED at `89cc0358` in
  `scripts/prod-prober/arms/mt5.mjs`: the `mt5-ipc-timeout` remedy tells the reader to
  *clear the MODAL LOGIN DIALOG* via VNC and states *a redeploy does NOT fix this*.
  For the modal-dialog cause that is correct and proven. For THIS incident it is
  **false in both halves** — there was no dialog (VNC clean, Alerts tab empty) and a
  restart fixed it in 2.0 s. ⇒ The arm needs to distinguish the two causes, or the
  remedy must stop asserting a single one. ⛔ **Cross-link and REFINE
  `MT5-WEDGE-OBS-01`; do NOT reopen it** — its modal-dialog mechanism is proven and
  still correct for its own cause.
  — **Reversibility:** reversible — the arm's own self-test asserts the remedies differ
  and each carries its distinguishing word, so a split is additive.

### D. The prober must actually measure the terminal

- **D-10: ⛔ `mt5-ipc-timeout` HAS NEVER ONCE FIRED IN PRODUCTION, AND THE
  CLASSIFICATION `MT5-WEDGE-OBS-01` WAS CLOSED ON IS THEREFORE UNPROVEN.** The arm
  reported `mt5-ssh-transport` — whose own remedy text concedes *"the terminal was
  never reached and nothing about it was measured"* — while `railway ssh` into the same
  container worked fine with a WORKSPACE-scoped token. ⚠️ This is the vacuous-gate
  class this milestone exists to close: a gate that reported a verdict having measured
  nothing. **REQUIRED in this phase, not deferred.**
  — **Reversibility:** reversible.

- **D-11: ⛔ CALIBRATE AGAINST A REAL WEDGE, NOT ONLY A FIXTURE.** A fixture proves the
  classifier can emit the string; only a real wedge proves the arm reaches the terminal
  and distinguishes the causes. ⚠️ Without D-10 and D-11 the phase **cannot honestly
  prove criterion 1 held**, because it would have no instrument that measures the
  subject. The token-scope failure mode is already documented in the arm's own
  `mt5-ssh-transport` remedy — start there.
  — **Reversibility:** reversible.

### E. Wizard copy — mint an arm, do not repurpose one

- **D-12: MINT A DISTINCT ARM; ⛔ DO NOT DELETE OR WIDEN `KEY_NETWORK_TIMEOUT`.** The
  shipped copy claims a *"temporary exchange issue or a network blip"* and says *"Try

  them with correct credentials. `KEY_NETWORK_TIMEOUT` remains CORRECT for a genuine
  transport failure and its honest tail is deliberate
  (`src/app/api/strategies/finalize-wizard/route.ts` keeps it *"for exactly what it
  honestly"* covers). The `KEY_SCOPE_CHECK_UNREADABLE` precedent is the pattern to
  follow for a new, narrower arm.
  — **Reversibility:** costly — a new user-visible error code is a published contract
  across the wizard, its tests and support copy.

  ⛔ **CORRECTED 2026-09-22 (pattern-mapping, BEFORE any plan was written) — THE
  PRECEDENT NAMED ABOVE GOVERNS THE WRONG STAGE.** The original wording is kept as
  lineage and is still correct about copy SHAPE. But `KEY_SCOPE_CHECK_UNREADABLE` lives
  on the **finalize-time scope re-probe**, not on the **initial MT5 key validate** where
  the founder's two measured retries (45 s and 55 s apart, one with correct credentials)
  actually happened. Pointing a planner at it as *the* precedent would have aimed D-12/
  D-13 at the wrong route.
  ⭐ **The more precise mechanism, verified at HEAD:** a machine-readable wire-code chain
  already runs at the initial-validate stage — `service_error(...)` in
  `analytics-service/routers/exchange.py` → `VENUE_WIRE_CODE_TO_VERDICT` and
  `classifyKeyValidationError`'s `seamCode`-first check in `src/lib/wizardErrors.ts`,
  already live for `MT5_GATEWAY_UNCONFIGURED`. **That is the concrete mechanism for
  D-13's "the server already knows `ipc_fault`, carry it through"** — it carries a code,
  not re-sniffed message text.
  ⛔ **BOTH stages stay on the table and the planner picks deliberately** once D-01's
  root-cause timing is pinned; the pattern-mapper documented both rather than silently
  choosing, and this correction does not choose either.

- **D-13: THE SERVER ALREADY KNOWS — the new arm must carry `ipc_fault` through, not

  instructing a retry that cannot work. ⛔ Read `DESIGN.md` before ANY copy decision.
  ⚠️ The copy must not promise a self-heal that D-05 has not yet shipped.
  — **Reversibility:** reversible.

### F. correlation_id

- **D-14: `correlation_id` MUST BE PER REQUEST, NOT PER WIZARD SESSION.** MEASURED
  identical across attempts HOURS apart, so two different failures are
  indistinguishable in support — which directly undercuts the "email us with the
  correlation id" instruction in that same copy. ⚠️ Scout note: the wizard's id is
  client-minted in `src/lib/wizard/localStorage.ts`, documented there as a
  *"telemetry-correlation id, not a security token"* with `Math.random` entropy, while
  server paths mint per-call ids with `crypto.randomUUID()`
  (`src/lib/analytics-client.ts`, `src/app/api/strategies/csv-finalize/route.ts`). ⛔
  Decide deliberately whether the per-session id is KEPT AS A SESSION id alongside a
  new per-request id, or replaced — do not silently change the meaning of an existing
  telemetry field that other consumers may join on.
  — **Reversibility:** costly — the id is already emitted into funnel telemetry, so
  changing its cardinality changes the meaning of historical rows.

### G. The settings landmine — PIN, do not fix

- **D-15: ⛔ PIN IT WITH AN ASSERTION; DO NOT TICK THE BOX.** The terminal's Experts tab
  has *"Disable algorithmic trading when the account has been changed"* **UNCHECKED**.
  Validation IS an account change, so ticking it would **silently disable algo trading**
  on the shared terminal. It is correct today only by default, and nothing in the repo
  asserts it. The deliverable is a CHECK that fails loudly if it ever flips — not a
  change to the setting.
  — **Reversibility:** one-way in the dangerous direction — ticking the box silently
  disables algorithmic trading on the shared terminal serving every client, and the
  failure would be silent rather than loud.

  ⭐ **STRENGTHENED 2026-09-22 (pattern-mapping) — a real analog exists, and it is not
  the prober.** The setting itself is NOT live-observable: `[Experts] Account=1` only
  reaches disk on a CLEAN terminal exit (`scripts/mt5-diag.sh` warns of exactly this),
  so only the CONSEQUENCE is observable. ⚠️ The prober arm prints `trade_allowed` and
  explicitly **"NEVER JUDGES"** it, so it is the wrong host for an assertion.
  ⭐ `classify_trade_capability` and `terminal_trade_permission_off` in
  `analytics-service/services/mt5_validation.py` ALREADY judge `trade_allowed`
  post-validate with the fail-safe shape D-15 needs. ⛔ Extend that vocabulary; do not
  mint a second, competing judge, and do not start judging the prober's deliberately
  unjudged branch without saying why.

  ⭐⭐ **THE APPARENT CONTRADICTION IS ALREADY RESOLVED IN THE REPO, AND THE RESOLUTION
  IS A GATE — found 2026-09-22 while surveying the queue, recorded here so plan 06 does
  NOT send the founder to re-measure what was already measured.**
  `164.6.4-UAT.md` § *"R2 / question THREE — a login did NOT re-clear the Expert-Advisors
  options"* carries a founder-captured live before/after from **2026-09-16**, with **a
  REAL per-account authorization between the two readings**, and the options came back
  **byte-for-byte the same** — `Allow algorithmic trading` ✅ on both sides.
  ⭐ **The mechanism it names is what reconciles the two records:** the re-clear hazard is
  **GATED** by the `Disable … when the account has been changed` checkbox, which is OFF on
  this terminal — consistent with `scripts/mt5-diag.sh`'s own header, which conditions its
  warning on `[Experts] Account=1` being *armed*. So the four files asserting a re-clear on
  EVERY account change are **conditionally right and unconditionally wrong**, and D-15's
  measurement of the box as UNCHECKED is the other half of the same fact. ⛔ They are not
  two competing claims to average — one is the gate, the other is the gate's position.
  ⭐ **This is exactly why D-15 says PIN, DO NOT TICK:** ticking the box ARMS the re-clear.
  ⚠️ **The UAT states its own two limits and they bind here too:** (a) ONE login on ONE
  terminal — enough to refute an "every one" claim, ⛔ **not enough to assert the
  converse**, so plan 06 must still confirm the CURRENT position rather than inherit it;
  (b) its evidence form is the terminal's own Journal plus the options dialog, not an
  analytics log line.
  ⚠️ **LABEL COLLISION, do not be caught by it:** that UAT calls this "D-09", which is
  **`164.6.4`'s D-09, a different decision from `164.6.5`'s D-09** (the `-10005` split).
  Same label, different phase.
