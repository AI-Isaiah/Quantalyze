# Phase 164.6.6.2.1 Plan 09: BTC key in the USD allocator book Summary

A priced BTC key now sits in the USD allocator book at its USD levels, a key whose live close is missing is composed through its last priced day and exits by name (`native_unpriced_key_omitted` plus `unpriced_native_keys`), and an interior unpriced stretch leaves the key out of those days without shifting any level before the hole.

## What was built

- **Compose, trailing (T1).** `compose_allocator_equity(..., unpriced_close_day_by_key=)`. A key with an anchor equal to its realized USD level on `P`, `departed_end_by_key[k] == P` and an entry in the new map is composed through `P` and rotated out after it by the existing exit seam and ledger outflow. A key in the map with no departed end is rotated out after its last return day, so a caller can never get a flat carry on a price no close backs. A key with a NULL anchor and the reason `native_unpriced` is omitted from the unanchored-return and fourth buckets exactly as `native_unit` is (no `DROPPED_KEY`). Constants `_NATIVE_UNPRICED_KEY_OMITTED` and `_NATIVE_UNPRICED_REASON`. `read_native_key_inputs` is the defensive reader of the worker-written fields.
- **Compose, interior (I2).** `unpriced_days_by_key` and `segment_terminals_by_key`. `hole_segment_ends(event_days, unpriced_days)` names the priced day before each hole. The key is replayed as units: each segment before the last rolls back from the realized USD level the writer stored on its last day, the last keeps the key's own id and its realized terminal. A segment before a hole is a hole-exit unit (an exit seam on the curve, an outflow of its last level in the ledger); the next unit enters with an inflow of its first level unless a coverage seam already books that handoff. `inputs.n_keys` counts keys, not units.
- **Derive (allocator).** The key_inputs read loop: for a native row it reads the native fields; an unpriced-anchor key with a readable realized terminal on `priced_through` gets `anchors_by_key[k] = realized USD`, `departed_end_by_key[k] = priced_through` and its flows and dropped-day P&L after that day dropped; a key with nothing priced stays NULL `native_unpriced`; a malformed native field leaves the key out by name (NULL anchor, `native_unpriced`), never priced on a guess. It passes the three new maps to the compose.
- **Derive (writer).** `_convert_native_key_to_usd` also returns `segment_terminals` (the native realized NAV level on each `hole_segment_ends` day times that day's close), persisted as `segment_terminals: [{utc_day_iso, level_usd}]` beside `unpriced_days`.

## MEASUREMENT verdicts quoted (acceptance I1)

```
VERDICT-M4: LAST-DAY-IS-LAST-DEAL-DAY
```

`unpriced_native_keys[].day` is ALWAYS the ISO date of the missing close, which is exactly what plan 08 writes as `unpriced_close_day` (the latest completed UTC day, `latest_completed_close_day(_utc_now())`). It is not the curve's last date: by M4 the curve's last date is the last deal day of any key, so the missing-close day can be later than it (tracer test: curve ends 2026-06-10, `day` is 2026-06-09; a quiet book can be the other way round). Plan 17's note reads that field and nothing else.

## Deviations from Plan

**1. [Rule 2 - Missing critical functionality] The writer had to persist the level before each hole (`segment_terminals`)**
- **Found during:** Task 2 design, from MEASUREMENT M3's own note ("a compose input that merely names the unpriced days ... is not enough on its own, because the pre-h levels are already mis-rolled").
- **Issue:** the stored returns plus `unpriced_days` cannot recover the USD level on the far side of a hole: the return across it was never formed, and `replay_key_equity` rolls over the days present, so the earlier history is scaled by an unknown factor. The plan's `unpriced_days_by_key` alone would have shown the key out of the unpriced days but left every level before them wrong.
- **Fix:** `segment_terminals` is written by plan 08's converter (the native realized NAV on the last priced day before each hole times that day's close, the same basis as `realized_terminal`), read defensively, and passed as `segment_terminals_by_key`. `hole_segment_ends` is the single definition of "a hole" imported by both sides. `segment_terminals_by_key` is a compose keyword beyond the plan's artifact table. A hole with no stored level composes unsplit and blocks as `key_inputs_mismatch`.
- **Files modified:** `analytics-service/services/job_worker.py`, `analytics-service/services/allocator_equity_compose.py`, `analytics-service/tests/test_derive_allocator_equity_job.py`
- **Commit:** `05d619902`

**2. [Rule 1 - Bug] A flow or dropped-day P&L dated after `priced_through` would have blocked a T1 key**
- **Found during:** Task 1 design (plan 08's handoff: flows are persisted for any flow day with a close, even after `priced_through`).
- **Issue:** the replay would read the series as running past its realized terminal: `realized_terminal_day_mismatch` and `out_of_window_flows`, both blocking.
- **Fix:** the derive drops such flows and dropped-day P&L for a T1 key (they happened while the key was already out of the book).
- **Commit:** `95a8e64df`

**3. [Plan wording] The planned RED for the priced case does not exist.** The base already composes a priced BTC key correctly (plan 08 made its inputs). The case is kept as a characterization pin; the real RED is the unpriced half (above).

**4. [Plan wording] `unpriced_native_keys` is present only when non-empty.** The plan says "emit ... a list"; an empty list on every USD book would change every payload for nothing. A reader treats absent as empty.

**5. [Plan wording] The day after a hole is also left out.** Plan 08 lists `h+1` (it has a close but no formable return) in `unpriced_days`, so B contributes nothing on it either, as the plan's "key contributes nothing to that day's dollar total" for every listed day requires.

**6. A segment with no return row is dropped and flagged.** It cannot be blended or replayed from a level; the omission is named by `native_unpriced_key_omitted`, not hidden. Not reachable from the converter's output in the cases tested; documented for the contract.

## Handoff notes for plan 17 (the D-20 line on the dashboard)

- Payload field: `payload["unpriced_native_keys"]` = `[{"api_key_id": str, "day": str | None}]`, sorted by key id, ABSENT when empty. `day` is the missing close's ISO date and is `null` only when the row's native fields were malformed (the key is then left out whole and the day is unknown); the TS reader must tolerate it.
- It lists a key only when it is left out of the curve's LAST day (a T1 key whose priced-through day precedes the last curve date, or a key omitted whole). A key left out of INTERIOR days only (a priced last day) raises the flag `native_unpriced_key_omitted` but is NOT in the list, so the list cannot name an interior-only gap. If D-20 needs the interior days named too, that is a follow-up to this plan's payload (the compose knows them: they are the unit gaps), not something plan 17 can read today.
- No amount enters the list; the flag is benign (does not flip `is_trustworthy`).
