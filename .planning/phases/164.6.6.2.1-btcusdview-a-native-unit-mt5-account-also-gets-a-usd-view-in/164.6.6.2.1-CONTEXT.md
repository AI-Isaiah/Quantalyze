# Phase 164.6.6.2.1: BTCUSDVIEW - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-10-09
**Status:** Ready for planning

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

A native-unit MT5 account (BTC today) also gets a USD view. Its native NAV is converted at the daily BTC benchmark price. Allocator equity snapshots and the dashboard include the account instead of skipping it, and `api_keys.account_balance_usdt` is populated. A day without a benchmark price stays null.

This phase also owns, per ROADMAP:
- the "in BTC" chip on the strategy list surfaces (Discovery, `StrategyTable`; 164.6.6.2 D-19);
- three known limits: `strategy_matching.find_matched_strategy` and `bridge_scoring.find_replacement_candidates` correlate a BTC-unit series as if it were USD, and `getOwnCapitalStrategies` shows an unlabelled own-capital MTD;
- the first caller of `nativeAmount`.

</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

- **D-01 (default unit):** the factsheet and the strategy surfaces show the account's **native unit (BTC) by default**, with a BTC/USD toggle for the converted view. Returns are earned in BTC, so the native unit is the truth. **Allocator dashboard totals are in USD**, because they sum across accounts. Founder choice, 2026-10-09 (Recommended), over a USD default.
- **D-02 (carried, locked):** never invent data. A day with no BTC benchmark price has no USD value (null, rendered "—"). It is never interpolated or carried forward.
- **D-03 (carried from 164.6.6.2):** "BTC only first, USD later" (D-15). The native-BTC reporting from 164.6.6.2 (D-13, D-14) stays the source of truth; USD is derived from it.
- **D-04:** the two correlation consumers stop correlating a BTC-unit series as USD. They convert to USD first, or compare in a common unit. A test fails on today's behaviour.
- **D-05:** the "in BTC" chip appears wherever a native-unit strategy is listed, so a BTC return is never read as a USD return.

### Founder decisions after research (2026-10-09)
- **D-06:** an interior BTC-price hole **breaks the USD curve**. The USD view still shows, with a visible break at each unpriced day, returns computed only over priced days, and a note naming the missing days. It never silently understates. The converter must not drop the day after a hole without saying so. Founder choice, over disabling the toggle.
- **D-07:** the owner's exchange key card shows **both** the native balance and the USD value, e.g. "0.4213 BTC · ≈ $26,100", or the native amount alone when no price exists for the day. This is `nativeAmount`'s first caller. It needs a GRANT migration for `account_balance_native` / `account_currency` (owner-only read; migration review applies). Founder choice (Recommended).

### Orchestrator resolutions (from locked rules)
- **D-08 (from D-02, strict pricing):** live-balance pricing is strict. A balance is priced at the same UTC day's stored close, or it is NULL. Nothing is ever carried forward. The post-deploy acceptance reading is conditioned on a close existing (PROD is a day behind today; 170.2 SC-2 fixes the refresh).
- **D-09:** a key on an unpriced day is omitted by name from the composite allocator book that day, never priced at a stale close. A short spike confirms the compose accepts a key that ends a day early.
- **D-10 (D-04 scope):** fix `find_matched_strategy`, and also the third consumer research found (the inline matching block in `verify_strategy`, `routers/portfolio.py`). Pin `find_replacement_candidates` with a regression test only, since it is already converted by #969. Correct the ROADMAP wording.
- **D-11:** `getOwnCapitalStrategies` MTD converts to USD, matching the positioned half and the dashboard rule (D-01).
- **D-12:** the "in BTC" chip is limited to StrategyTable and Discovery in this phase. Its anonymous read alias needs the security pin at `queries.test.ts` amended with a dated reason. The plan is sequenced after 170.2 merges.
- **D-13:** a UI-SPEC is required before the UI wave (toggle, compact chip, USD disclosure line, curve-break note).
- **D-14:** add a TODOS line for ranking honesty on Discovery (BTC returns ranked beside USD). It is out of scope here.

### Founder decisions after the UI-SPEC check (2026-10-09)
- **D-15:** benchmark-vs-BTC figures stay hidden in the USD view, exactly as in the BTC view: α/IR vs BTC, BTC correlation rows/cells, event studies, the stress BTC column. Reason: a BTC-denominated account's USD series carries ~1 BTC beta by construction, so these figures would measure the denomination, not the manager. Founder (Recommended).
- **D-16:** the leverage (L×) control is disabled in the USD view, with a reason line. Scaling the USD series would also lever the BTC price leg. Founder (Recommended).
- **D-17 (refines D-08):** a live balance is priced at the latest completed daily close (the previous UTC day's) and the label says so, e.g. "≈ $26,100 at Oct 8 close". If that close is missing, only the BTC amount shows. Nothing older is ever used. Founder (Recommended).
- **D-18:** `/strategy/[id]/v2` stays native-only (unit chip, no toggle) in this phase. 170.5 is reworking v2. A v2 toggle follow-up is booked in TODOS by the planner. Founder (Recommended). This narrows D-01's "strategy surfaces" to the factsheet family, by founder decision.

### Orchestrator calls (from the UI check)
- **D-19:** the masthead stays BTC in both views. That is consistent with D-15, so the chip's "measured in BTC" tooltip stays true.
- **D-20:** on the dashboard, when the BTC key is left out of the book total on an unpriced day, a visible line names the account and the reason. A drop in the total is never silent (D-09 "by name" is user-visible, not just a flag).
- **D-21:** the USD disclosure copy claims only what the data guarantees, e.g. "converted from BTC at the stored daily BTC close", never "BTC/USDT close". `benchmark_prices` has no source column, and the CoinGecko fallback is BTC/USD.
- **D-22:** a leading price hole (a record predating the stored closes) gets a "Priced from {date}" clause. It never starts late silently.

### Claude's Discretion
- Whether the toggle state persists per viewer, using browser storage as a convenience only.
- Which benchmark-price fixing time (daily close) is used for the conversion. Use the same daily series the benchmark metrics use.

</decisions>
