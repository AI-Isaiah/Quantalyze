<objective>
Measure, before any code changes, how the existing composite allocator book behaves when a
BTC key's USD series ends early or has an unpriced interior stretch, and select the mechanism
plans 08 and 09 build.

Purpose: CONTEXT D-09: "a key on an unpriced day is omitted by name from the composite allocator
book that day, never priced at a stale close. A short spike confirms the compose accepts a key that
ends a day early." RESEARCH A6 and Pitfall 6: the compose carries a key-day with no return inside
coverage at r = 0 under a benign flag, which for a BTC key is a stale-price day. UI-SPEC F: the
dashboard note covers the book's latest day only, earlier unpriced days are omitted by name in
the curve. D-17: a live balance is priced at the latest completed close.

**The design under test (pre-registered, plans 08 and 09 build exactly this unless a verdict
says otherwise):**
- Writer (plan 08): a BTC key's per-key returns are `convert_native_returns_to_usd` of its native
  returns; its realized terminal is (P, native realized level at P x close(P)) with P its last USD
  return day; its live anchor is `price_live_balance(equity, ...)` (D-17), or NULL with reason
  `native_unpriced` when that close is missing; interior unpriced days are listed.
- Mechanism T1 (trailing): when the live anchor is unpriced, the key is composed through P and
  rotated out after P exactly as `departed_end_by_key` rotates a departed key out (an exit seam,
  never a return, never a carried level), under a new benign token.
- Mechanism I2 (interior): a new compose input names a key's unpriced days; on those days the key
  contributes nothing to the dollar curve and leaves both sums of `payload.returns` (an exit and a
  re-entry, never r = 0 at a stale price).

Output: `164.6.6.2.1-MEASUREMENT.md` with M1-M4, each VERDICT line, and the MECHANISM lines.
</objective>
