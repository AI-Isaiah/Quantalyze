<objective>
Ship the schema and the owner-only GRANT that the phase's two owner surfaces need, as PR 1, ahead
of every reader.

Purpose: D-07 needs `account_balance_native` and `account_currency` readable by the key's owner.
D-17 needs the stored date of the close that priced `account_balance_usdt` ("≈ $26,100 at Oct 8
close"); UI-SPEC E forbids taking that date from the viewer's clock, so it must be a stored field.
UI-SPEC F requires the Holdings native row's unit to come from a stored unit ON THE ROW, never
inferred from the symbol or the mark. Merging `supabase/migrations/**` applies to TEST then PROD
with no human stop, so every review (`migration-reviewer`, `rls-policy-auditor`) happens before
this PR merges, and nothing that reads these columns ships in it.

**Planner-named fields (UI-SPEC "Holdings row unit; key card close date — unresolved"):**
- key card close date: `api_keys.account_balance_usdt_close_date` (date).
- Holdings row unit: `allocator_holdings.quantity_unit` (text). Chosen over the row's
  `raw_payload->>currency` because sFOX rows already store an ASSET code under that key (a BTC
  spot row would read as a native-unit account row), and over a join to the key's
  `account_currency` because UI-SPEC F binds the unit to the row itself.

Output: the migration, its rollback, a gate file with mutation arms, and every census move.
</objective>
