<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

- **D-01 (founder 2026-10-07):** Own phase, Wave 1. Fix the trigger rather than changing the page's promise.
- **D-02:** The cron is registered through a runbook step, never a migration (164.7 rule:
  "A migration that schedules is a scope violation"). The registration is a PROD live op the orchestrator
  runs (PROD writes are authorized), marker query first, with the statement and its readback recorded.
- **D-03:** Before registering, the load the fan-out adds is MEASURED (eligible key count, per-job duration,
  what else the worker runs between 04:00 and 06:00 UTC) and the fan-out is shown unable to wedge the worker
  the way the July phase35 backfill did. If that needs code (a bound, a lane, a stagger), it ships first.
- **D-04:** The FLIP runbook's gating steps (dedicated backfill worker cutover, pilot, E2 gate) are
  re-evaluated against today's code, not assumed: research states for each whether it is still needed, already
  satisfied by later phases, or obsolete, with evidence. A step judged still needed is planned, not skipped.
- **D-05:** The ROADMAP and TODOS notes saying "Do NOT re-register the `derive-allocator-key-dailies` cron"
  (ROADMAP ~394, TODOS ~786) are amended with dated lineage notes, not deleted.
- **D-06:** Success is measured on PROD after the first 05:30 UTC run: counts of version-2 `equity_curve`
  rows vs eligible allocators, job outcomes, no worker backlog; then the browser check of My Allocation.
  ⏳ **D-06 clarification (planning 2026-10-07, pending founder confirmation at plan 06):** "eligible allocators" means census e's owner population minus the held set (`rebuilding:duplicate_account`, `rebuilding:key_not_syncing`, `rebuilding:account_identity_pending`), held owners listed by reason and count; per D-09 okx bound owners are counted under the okx follow-up. Counts are bounded to `computed_at` from 05:30Z of the run day to 24 hours later.

- **D-07 (founder 2026-10-07, research OQ-1):** FLIP Step 4 (the E2GT-01 harness) is WAIVED as a pre-gate.
  It cannot run for Deribit key-mode and the legacy fallback it protected was removed in 167.1.2 plan 11.
  The rehearsal (D-08) and the D-06 next-morning reads replace it as the safety evidence. The waiver is
  recorded in the runbook's Step 4 with a dated note.
- **D-08 (founder 2026-10-07, research OQ-2):** Rehearse first: one manual
  `enqueue_derive_broker_dailies_for_allocator_keys()` fan-out, watched to completion on the backfill worker
  (per-venue durations incl. okx, reclaims, crawl-bound hits), with no merge to `main` while it runs. The cron
  is registered only after a clean rehearsal. Because the rehearsal writes rows, SC-3 / D-06 are judged on
  `computed_at` at or after 05:30Z of the first cron day, never on row existence.
- **D-09 (founder 2026-10-07, research OQ-4):** If the rehearsal shows okx crawls hitting the 810 s bound,
  register anyway (a bound hit ends the job with an error and the next day retries) and book okx crawl speed
  as a follow-up phase.
- **D-10 (founder 2026-10-07, research OQ-6):** The backfill-worker staleness alarm is NOT built here. It is
  booked in TODOS.md and routed to OUTAGEALERT (Wave 6).
