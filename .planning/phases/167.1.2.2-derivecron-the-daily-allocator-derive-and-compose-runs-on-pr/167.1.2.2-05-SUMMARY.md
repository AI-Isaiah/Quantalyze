## Blocks the cron

1. **Bybit key bound-IP rejection persists (D-11 re-check failed).** Until one key-mode derive for that key reads `done` from the backfill service, that key fails every day, its allocator's compose reports INCOMPLETE every day and that book never renders. Someone must establish the egress address the backfill service really uses at the time of a call and compare it with the key's allow-list as saved at the venue (a changed or non-static Railway egress, an IPv6 versus IPv4 mismatch, or an unsaved list are the candidates). The address must not be written into tracked text.
2. **Compose refusal on the Deribit plus MT5 allocator.** The D-13..D-16 code replaced the day-1024 self-check divergence with a refusal that the reconstructed equity is non-positive on 755 of 1200 days, a "flow dominates prior capital" check that refuses rather than fabricates a floor. This needs a root-cause decision (is the reconstruction wrong for this account's flow history, or is the refusal correct and the account genuinely cannot be reconstructed, in which case the honest outcome is a labelled degraded book or a documented exclusion rather than a daily failed job). It is the D-12 question again, one layer deeper, and nothing in this plan fixes it.
3. SC-3 would read `FAIL` for 2 of 7 eligible owners (1 more is held by design), so a cron registered today would not meet the success criterion on its first morning.

No other blocker: queue behaviour, role isolation, bounds, watchdog and MT5 gating were clean on this run, as on plan 02's.
