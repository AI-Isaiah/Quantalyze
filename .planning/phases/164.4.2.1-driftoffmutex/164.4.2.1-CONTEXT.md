## Decisions

### D-01 — SC-3's clause (a) is judged by the Goal (FOUNDER DECISION, 2026-09-25)

- **Question put (AskUserQuestion, Q1, 2026-09-25):** which bar does Success Criterion 3 mean for
  164.4.2's clause (a)? The research (`## Open Questions` Q1) measured that on non-degenerate
  merge-push runs `e2e-seeded`, which this phase does not touch, is the critical path at about
  18 min, so dropping the key cannot put a run-total below clause (a)'s strict 16m18s. The ROADMAP
  Goal ("no slower than before Phase 164.4.2") and title ("falls back inside its BEFORE band") and
  SC-3 ("meet clause (a)") therefore disagree.
- **Founder answer, verbatim:** "Judge by the goal"
- **What it means, operationally:** success for this phase is (i) `test-db-drift` no longer waits
  on the advisory key, and (ii) non-degenerate merge-push runs land back near the pre-164.4.2 band,
  read as the ROADMAP title states it: a non-degenerate run-total **inside or below 16m18s-18m50s**
  (164.4.2's BEFORE non-degenerate band). The strict clause (a) reading (run-total BELOW 16m18s) is
  still computed and recorded for every admissible run, and its result is booked as **evidence for
  Phase 164.9 TESTISOLATION**. It is NOT a pass/fail gate for this phase.
- **Reason:** the Goal is the founder's stated intent. Getting below the band requires
  un-serializing `e2e-seeded` from `python`, which is Phase 164.9's scope; keeping the strict
  reading would make this phase unfinishable without absorbing 164.9 work.
- **Reversibility:** reversible. It is a grading reading of numbers the measurement records either
  way; re-reading them against the strict bar needs no code change and no re-run.
- **Where it is recorded:** here AND as a dated one-line note in the ROADMAP `### Phase 164.4.2.1`
  section (CLAUDE.md deviation policy: CONTEXT and ROADMAP, never just one).

### D-02 — SC-1's choice: Option A, drop the key from `test-db-drift` (research recommendation)

Delete exactly three steps from the `test-db-drift` job: `Acquire shared-test-db mutex`,
`Release shared-test-db mutex (best effort)` and `Verdict — shared-test-db mutex holder died mid-job`.
Keep everything else byte-identical: `needs: [python, changed-paths]`, the job `if:`,
`timeout-minutes: 90`, `permissions:`, `Install psql client`, the
`Wait for the TEST schema apply to conclude (merge pushes only)` step (from its `- name:` line through
its `run:` body), `actions/setup-node`, and the `VAC-08 - repo-vs-TEST ledger and function body drift`
step.

**Reason (SC-1 "the choice is recorded with its reason"):** SC-2's ordering ("reads shared TEST
after `apply-test`'s schema apply") is delivered by the wait step, which already runs before the
acquire and checks a fact (the same-commit apply concluded, or none exists after the grace, AND the
schema-apply in-flight flag is clear). The key adds only mutual exclusion against a schema writer that
starts after the wait concluded. Those writers are transactional, and every remaining interleaving
yields a LOUD red, never a false green (research `### Interleavings once the key is gone`), and the
same false-red interleaving exists today under arbitrary lock order. `python` and `e2e-seeded` write
rows, never the ledger or the three bodies. So the key buys VAC-08 nothing it relies on.

**Rejected:** B (hold the key only around the reads) does not shorten the queue ahead of the hold;
C (leave the aggregator / run later) violates SC-4 or still blocks the aggregator; C′ (drop
`needs: python`) makes the job queue behind `python` from t=0 and weakens the wait's appearance-grace
assumption. A private key for this job is banned by the job's own ⛔ block.

### D-03 — No read-fence (Option D is NOT built)

### D-05 — Measurement is written before code; the 5-run AFTER is a verification obligation

`164.4.2.1-MEASUREMENT.md` (BEFORE, empty AFTER protocol, clause texts per D-01, the prediction) is

### D-06 — Clause (c) is made non-vacuous

After this phase `test-db-drift`'s acquire-wait is zero BY CONSTRUCTION, so it is written as
"0 (no acquire step — never evidence)" and never counted as a win. Clause (c) is graded on

### D-07 — The holder-set pins move by measurement, in the same commit as the `ci.yml` change

`critical-regressions.test.ts` `DB_JOBS` goes from three jobs to `["python", "e2e-seeded"]`; a new
negative pin asserts `test-db-drift` is not a holder; a new pin asserts `test-db-drift` runs the
ordering wait BEFORE VAC-08 (with the key gone, that wait is SC-2's only ordering control) and keeps

under a neuter before it is kept.

### D-08 — The long ⛔ block on `test-db-drift` stays in place as lineage

`python` and `e2e-seeded` reference it BY NAME ("See the ⛔ block on `test-db-drift`"). Moving it
would re-point several references for no behavioural gain. It gains a dated header saying this job no
longer takes the key, and every literal occurrence of the key number inside the `test-db-drift` job is
replaced by its NAME ("the shared-test-db key"), because the holder-set pin reads comments.

### D-09 — Docs currency is dated addenda; the VAC-08 script is not edited
