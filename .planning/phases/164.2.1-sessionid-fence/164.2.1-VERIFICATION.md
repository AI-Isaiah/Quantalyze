### The dead end, traced end to end at HEAD (verify item 1)

Abandoned key-A draft → `localStorage` payload `{ source:"api", wizardSessionId:S_A, apiKeyId:"A" }`
(or a legacy payload with no `apiKeyId`). Owner clicks "Finish setup →" on key B →
`ContributionWizardOverlay.tsx:302` passes `preselectKey={activePreselect}` → `WizardClient`
mounts with `wizardSessionId = newWizardSessionId()` and, in the post-mount effect
(`WizardClient.tsx:443-471`), calls `deriveWizardResumeOverrides(loaded, "api", null,
initialDraft?.api_key_id ?? preselectKey?.id ?? null)` → `sessionKeyMatches("A" | undefined, "B")`
→ `false` (`localStorage.ts:584-590`) → `out.wizardSessionId` stays unset → the fresh mint
survives → `setHydrated(true)` (`:493`, same IIFE, after the overrides) → Continue POSTs
`wizard_session_id = fresh`. The insert on `(user_id, fresh, 'wizard')` cannot collide with
draft A's row, so the 23505 never fires and re-pressing Continue carries the same fresh,
non-colliding token. Established by: SC-1c's POST-body assertion at HEAD; neuter B proving that
passing `null` at that exact call site restores `S_A` into the POST (the original bug,
reproduced and then removed); and the route read at `route.ts:686-790`, which now (Phase
164.2) also distinguishes a same-key collision (`DRAFT_ALREADY_EXISTS`) from a different-key
one (`DRAFT_SESSION_COLLISION`) — copy, not the fence; the fence is that the collision no
longer happens.
