<objective>
Founder F-04 retires "Bridge" and makes "Scenario" the registered term (F-08). After the wave-5 plans removed every user-visible Bridge string and every Term with the `bridge` key, this plan makes the change permanent where the phase enforces vocabulary:
- The registry swaps `bridge` for `scenario`, with the UI-SPEC definition.
- The copy census retires `Bridge` (case-sensitive, U-30) with its one count-pinned exception.
- The registry test admits that one mention and nothing broader.
The new `scenario` form makes rule B report the existing "Scenario …" labels. They are pinned at exact counts for plan 201-34, which places each one per U-27.

Purpose: without this plan, nothing stops the retired word from coming back, and "Scenario" is a tab name with no definition behind it.

Output: the registry swap, the census policy change, re-pinned census tests, rule B pins for plan 201-34, and RED proofs that the census and the registry test fail on the word.
</objective>
