# Phase 194.1 Plan 04: Holding card Sharpe and Vol gate (B11) Summary

**The `/compare` holding card now withholds Sharpe and annualised volatility under 90 days of price record and marks them `<1y` under one year, through the same `figureRecord` / `gateAnnualized` / `annualizedCell` path a strategy uses; return, drawdown and the 30-priced-day floor are untouched.**

## Accomplishments

- Adapter: `HoldingCompareAnalytics` gained optional `first_priced_day` / `last_priced_day`, set from `marks.points` (the spot priced days, the derivative usable directed days), the same set whose length is `priced_days`. `analyzePriceSeries`, `analyzeSignedReturns`, the floor and every withheld reason are unchanged.
- Card: `HoldingFactsheet.tsx` builds `figureRecord({ seriesStart: first_priced_day, seriesEnd: last_priced_day, headlineSince: null, observations: priced_days, periodsPerYear: annualizationPeriods("crypto") })`, takes `gateAnnualized(record)` (forced `"null"` under the floor), and renders Sharpe and Vol through `annualizedCell(fmt(gatedValue(...)), state, record)`.
- Assumption A1 applied: a holding is judged at the crypto convention, matching the adapter, so the span (90 days) decides, not the observation count.
