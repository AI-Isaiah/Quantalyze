## Known divergence (deliberately outside the fixture)

Producers only write real `yyyy-mm-dd` days. Pinned in pytest by `test_calendar_overflow_day_freezes_unreadable_the_documented_divergence` and named in the fixture's `_comment` and the helper's docstring.
