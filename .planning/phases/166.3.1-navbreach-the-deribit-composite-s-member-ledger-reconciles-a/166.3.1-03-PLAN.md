<objective>
Fix the root cause of the breach without touching the gate (D-01, D-02). The anchor handed to the
native core is the venue's `equity`, which Deribit defines as `balance + futures (session uPnL +
session RPL) + options value`; the ledger books session PnL only at the 08:00 UTC settlement. The
wedge strips session uPnL and the options value and never reads the realized term, so whenever the
account has realized-but-unsettled PnL at crawl time the anchor exceeds the ledger by exactly that
amount and the gate (correctly) refuses. The same defect makes member 3's stored series depend on
the time of day it was computed (116 to 117 differing days between three computes of the same
history, RESEARCH). The cash wedge also adds `options_session_upl` on top of `options_value`, which
the repo's own M5 identity (`test_smoothed_terminal_wedge_and_inception_closure_open_book`) and the
venue docs say double-strips (H1b). That second change ships only when the probe record shows it
live (`H1b: evidenced`, W5); otherwise it is booked and the realized-term fix ships alone.

Purpose: SC-1 and SC-2. The composite recompute in plan 04 can only be trusted if the reconciliation
closes for the right reason.
Output: the fix in `deribit_ingest.py`, tests that are RED on today's code, guards, and the known
limits booked.
</objective>
