Why: `replay_key_equity` counts a flow dated after the key's last RETURN day as
`OUT_OF_WINDOW_FLOW` (blocking). Two of the three keys have a terminal day one day after their
last return day, because the writer's return for that final day is dropped (a dust prior NAV, or a
withdrawal that dominates the prior NAV), and the flow of that day sits there. This is not a
property of the exit rule; it is what composing "through the terminal day" means when the terminal
day has no return. The MT5 dust key's inception residual is a stored-data fact the exit rule
cannot change.

level on L (the level the replay itself reaches on L when rolled from the terminal through the
dropped days between L and the terminal day; nothing is invented), trim every flow, dropped-day
P&L and basis day after L, and exit after L.

## Options for the founder (D-08)

How does a connected key whose live equity read found no capital (anchor NULL, token `dust` or
`nonpositive`), but which has real stored return history, enter its allocator's book?

- **A (plan recommendation, as specified):** compose the key at its own stored levels through its
  last measured day P (its stored realized terminal), then let it leave the book after P as an
  exit. Pros: only stored numbers (no invented capital); the same mechanism already ships for a BTC
  key whose close is missing (164.6.6.2.1 T1); an exit is never a loss; emptied stretches inside the
  history stay zero capital (DERIBITWEDGE D-01); compose-only, reversible by revert; no edit to the
  derive epilogue FLOWTIMING also changes. Cons: the live remainder after P (at most the dust floor,
  or nothing) is not in the book's dollar total, only named by the flag and the payload list; a key
  whose replay blocks keeps `dropped_key`; the rule trusts the live read's token, so a wrong
  transient zero on a funded account would exit it while the book still reads trustworthy.
  **Measured on today's PROD inputs: 0 of 2 books trustworthy.**
- **A2 (sub-option of A):** also require the key's stored realized terminal to be at or below the
  dust floor (or inside its emptied band), so a key whose live read says empty but whose writer
  says funded keeps `dropped_key`. Cost on today's data: none (3 of 3 keys qualify, all three
  terminals are at or below the floor). It closes the transient-zero direction.
- **A3 (refinement of A, found by this measurement):** end the key on its last return day L
  (at the writer's level on L) when the terminal day is later, instead of on the terminal day.
  **Measured: 1 of 2 books trustworthy** (book 2). Costs: the job rolls the terminal level back
  across the dropped days after L (one more line of arithmetic over stored inputs), and a
  Deribit key ending on L composes on the legacy roll or a zero-padded basis day. Plan 09's guards
  and trims would carry it.
- **B:** keep the key live to today and read its NULL anchor as zero capital through the D-01
  last-day band. Measured: the stored last level is inside the emptied band for 1 of 3 keys; the
  other two sit above it (a dust remainder written as 0 books a loss that did not happen, or the
  replay refuses). Cannot work as stated.
- **C:** store the measured remainder in the key_inputs row and compose it as the live anchor.
  The derive stores no remainder today (the anchor is NULL by design and only the token survives),
  so this changes the derive epilogue FLOWTIMING also edits and rewrites every key's row at the next
  derive. Costly to reverse; materially more code for a sub-floor amount.
- **D:** change nothing and record why. Both books stay untrustworthy while the keys stay
  connected; the owner's only way out is disconnecting the keys, which drops their whole history
  under `departed_history_unavailable`.

Recommendation: **A with A2 and A3.** Plain A reaches none of the books on today's data; A3 makes
book 2 trustworthy, A2 costs nothing today and closes the one failure direction A opens. Book 1
stays untrustworthy for a reason that is recorded (the MT5 dust key's inception residual), which
is what SC-5 allows.

## Founder answer

Option: **A with sub-option A2 and refinement A3** (2026-10-13, AskUserQuestion, "A + A2 + A3 (Recommended)").

- A: compose through the stored levels, then exit, named.
- A2: qualify only when the stored realized terminal is at or below the dust floor or inside the emptied band.
- A3: when the terminal day falls after the last return day L, the key ends on L at the writer's own level rolled back through the dropped days, everything after L is trimmed, and the key exits after L.
- Book 1 stays untrustworthy for the recorded reason (the MT5 dust key's inception residual, 0.15% against a 0.01% band; the gate is not loosened). The founder chose to route that residual to a phase.
