## Related finding (recorded, not acted on): the same cash-basis wedge term, seen from another root cause

A separate debug (branch `debug/derivecron-compose-refusal`, commit dc754758c, file
`.planning/debug/derivecron-compose-refusal.md`) root-caused a refused allocator book to the same term this
probe calls H1b: `options_session_upl` is subtracted twice by the cash-basis terminal wedge. The founder routed that
fix to the DERIBITWEDGE phase (decision D-18). Phase 131's review had already recorded the term as a
known residual and left it frozen.

What this probe's evidence says about it:

- Direction and size: AGREES. On m3's BTC bucket the residual, divided by the options session uPnL,
  reads -1.1 under `cash_settlement` and -0.0744 under `mark_to_market`. The cash reading minus the mtm
  reading is about -1.03, i.e. the cash basis carries one extra subtraction of the options session
  uPnL, with the sign and unit weight the debug predicts. The residual is also about 14 times larger on the cash basis
  (1.16e-05 of throughput against 8.45e-07 under mtm).
- The debug's own falsification test (is the options session uPnL nonzero on an account with an open
  options book at read time): m3 passes it. The options session uPnL is nonzero and `has_open_options_book`
  is true. This probe does not establish that m3 is the same account as the debug's account B.
- Consequence at the gate: CANNOT TELL. The cash residual on m3 BTC is about a tenth of the dust floor
  (1.16e-05 against 1e-4 of throughput), so `gate_today` passes and `gate_candidate_rpl_only` passes.
  By the plan's mechanical rule that is `H1b: not-evidenced`. The term was measured 0.3 h after the
  session reset, when the options session uPnL is smallest; a gate-level effect needs a run later in a
  session with the options book moving. That is also what the debug found: the term is invisible to the
  gate on an account with large lifetime throughput.
- OQ-2's own rule applies as written: the cash-basis `options_session_upl` change ships in this phase
  only if the record shows `H1b: evidenced`; it does not, so it is booked rather than shipped here. The
  routing between this phase and DERIBITWEDGE is the orchestrator's call.
