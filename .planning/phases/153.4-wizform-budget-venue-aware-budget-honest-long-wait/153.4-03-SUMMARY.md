# Phase 153.4 Plan 03: The client-safe budget and the honest long wait Summary

**The browser gets a hand-typed copy of the 120 s budget it was granted, pinned to `SEAM_BUDGETS` with both sides typed out, and `ValidateWaitCard` spends it: one label per venue class, an escalation ladder keyed to fractions rather than milliseconds, and a `Stop waiting` control that costs nothing to press.**

## Accomplishments

- **The browser can read its budget without the seam core coming with it.** `src/lib/wizard/validate-budget.ts` carries `VALIDATE_KEY_BUDGET_MS` (30 000), `VALIDATE_KEY_SERIALIZED_BUDGET_MS` (120 000), `validateBudgetMsFor` / `validateBudgetSecondsFor`, `WAIT_CARD_MOUNT_DELAY_MS` (300), the two ladder fractions (0.4 / 0.75) and `WAIT_ABORT_GRACE_MS` (15 000). Its ONLY import is `venueIsSerialized` — asserted, not asked for.
- **The duplication is safe because an assertion says so, in both directions.** The new pin in `seam-constants.pin.test.ts` asserts `SEAM_BUDGETS["validate-key"].timeoutMs === VALIDATE_KEY_BUDGET_MS` and the serialized equivalent, AND re-pins the two client figures against bare `30_000` / `120_000` literals typed in that file. Either half alone is a guard with a hole: the equality alone is satisfied by both sides moving together to a wrong value; the literals alone say nothing about agreement.
- **The ladder is genuinely a fraction, and that is proven by mutation.** The same two constants resolve to 48 s / 90 s on the serialized arm and 12 s / 22.5 s on the default one. Replacing `budgetMs * WAIT_QUEUE_FRACTION` with a literal `48_000` reds the default-arm boundary test by name — a ccxt user would otherwise get no escape control until 36 s after their own deadline had passed.
- **The queue disclosure is a CLASS check and it can be shown to be one.** Rewriting the gate as `exchange === "mt5"` reds two assertions: a mixed-case `MT5` case (the wizard's own display form, so the instance check drops the disclosure for the only venue that needs it) and a bounded body scan that reds BY NAME. The plan predicted the plain non-serialized test would stay green under that rewrite, and it did.
- **Nothing red inside budget, nothing invented, nothing that floods AT.** `text-warning` at 75% with the card's whole rendered class string asserted free of `text-negative` and of any raw amber class; the dot and the elapsed counter both `aria-hidden`; no `progressbar`, no `<progress>`, no `aria-valuenow`, no element whose text ends in `%`; the live region announces only on mount and at crossings (a 61 s → 62 s tick leaves the announced sentence byte-identical while the visible counter moves).
- **`Stop waiting` is honest about costing nothing.** It appears only at ≥ 40%, fires its callback on click, renders no dialog, is the card's only button, and carries `focus-visible:ring-2 ring-inset ring-accent` at full opacity with `ring-accent/50` and `/20` asserted absent.
- **Full suite green:** 772 files / **11 572 passed / 287 skipped / 0 failed**; `npx tsc --noEmit` clean; `npm run lint` 0 errors.

## Deviations from Plan

### Acceptance criteria met by rewrite rather than by surrender

**1. The bundle-safety grep — `grep -nE "resilient-fetch|analytics-client|next/server|@upstash" src/lib/wizard/validate-budget.ts` returns 7 lines, not the 0 the criterion demands.**

The criterion and its own task's action text contradict each other. The action mandates a docblock stating *"WHY the duplication exists: `SEAM_BUDGETS` lives in `resilient-fetch.ts`, which imports `next/server` and `@upstash/redis`"* — which cannot be written without those four tokens. This is the third time in this phase a bare substring grep has collided with prose the same task requires (153.4-01 deviation, 153.4-02 deviation).

Here the repo settles it, and not by preference: **`seam-ssr-exposure.pin.test.ts` is the house guard for this exact property**, and its docblock reads *"Fourteen files under `src/` name these modules in prose — `wizardErrors.ts` and `seam-errors.ts` each carry a docblock explaining, at length, why they must NOT import them. A substring grep reports all fourteen as violations, and the natural 'fix' is to weaken the guard until it stops complaining."* That file's own self-test pins `SEAM_IMPORT_EDGE.test('// do NOT simplify this to @/lib/resilient-fetch') === false`.

So `validate-budget.test.ts` scans **import edges over comment-stripped source** and asserts the extracted specifier list is exactly `["@/lib/closed-sets"]`, with a self-test proving the extractor matches a specifier and not a prose mention, plus a vacuity floor. The check a reader can run by hand is:

```
grep -nE '^import|from "' src/lib/wizard/validate-budget.ts   →  one line, "@/lib/closed-sets"
```

Two independent guards now cover the property: this one, and `seam-ssr-exposure.pin.test.ts`, which already scans every non-client file under `src/` for the three seam modules and therefore covers this module for free. The added value here is `next/server` and `@upstash`, which that pin does not look for.

**2. `grep -c 'text-amber-600' ValidateWaitCard.tsx` returns 0 — and the warning against it is still in the file.** The same collision, but this one WAS satisfiable both ways, so neither was surrendered: the comment names the failure ("the raw amber Tailwind class the `SyncPreviewStep.tsx:2377` site uses") without spelling the class, and says in the same breath why it avoids the literal. `grep` returns 0 and the guard can still fail on real code.

### Design decisions taken inside "Claude's Discretion" (CONTEXT §Claude's Discretion — exact long-wait presentation)

**3. The wait-promise line is retired at the 75% rung.** UI-SPEC's ladder table does not say whether earlier rungs persist. At 75% the slow line already says *"We will wait until 120s, then tell you what we found"* — the same deadline as the promise, plus what happens next. Rendering both puts two sentences about one number in a three-line card. The house analog (`SyncPreviewStep`) uses the same exclusive shape (`showSlowHint && !showWarn`). The queue line and the promise DO coexist between 40% and 75%, because they say different things.

**4. An unrecognised venue gets `Checking your key...`, not a fabricated name.** UI-SPEC specifies `Checking your key with {VenueName}...` and the plan asked for "a safe fallback". Echoing an unknown `exchange` back into the sentence is T-153.4-11; substituting a generic noun ("your exchange") reads as filler. Dropping the clause states exactly what is known. Asserted with a `<script>`-shaped venue string, which renders neither `kraken` nor `script`.

### Known and accepted

**5. `hover:bg-page` on the `Stop waiting` button is a visual no-op** — the card's own surface is `bg-page`. This is deliberate: the classes are the shared `ghost` variant's own (`text-text-secondary hover:bg-page`) copied verbatim so that the day `ui/Button.tsx:35`'s ring is fixed, this plain `<button>` becomes a drop-in `<Button variant="ghost" size="sm">` with zero visual delta. Diverging now to buy a hover state would forfeit that. The keyboard affordance — the one carrying the WCAG 1.4.11 argument — is correct and asserted. Cosmetic, below the founder stopping rule, recorded here rather than fixed.

## What the next plan needs to know

- **⛔ The `SEAM_DEADLINE_EXCEEDED` gate is STILL UNPAID, and it is now unambiguously 04's and 05's.** 153.4-02 handed it forward as "153.4-03's". It is not: this plan emits no code and renders no envelope, so there is no commit here that could have paid it. The obligation binds **whoever starts emitting `SEAM_DEADLINE_EXCEEDED`** — the abort path in `ConnectKeyStep` / `MultiKeyConnectStep`. That commit must pass `surface: "connect"` **and** `budgetSeconds` to `buildEnvelope`, or 153.1-04's `REQUIRES_CONNECT_SURFACE` absence-suppresses rule silently withholds *"Your key details are still on this page."* A user who waited two minutes and is then told nothing about their typed credentials is the worst outcome this phase can produce.
- **The card is PURE and does not time.** The caller owns the clock (`elapsedMs`), the abort controller, and the 300 ms mount gate. `MultiKeyConnectStep` mounts one per validating member panel — that is why the card holds no state; N cards with N intervals would disagree about "now", and a global spinner would freeze panels waiting on nothing.
- **`WAIT_ABORT_GRACE_MS` (15 000) is the browser's margin OVER the promise, not an extension of it.** Abort at `validateBudgetMsFor(exchange) + WAIT_ABORT_GRACE_MS`, never at the budget itself: the seam deadline fires inside our route and the reply still has to travel back, so aborting at exactly the budget cuts off a verdict already on the wire and re-creates the silent UNKNOWN.
- **The cancelled state's copy is specified and NOT built here.** UI-SPEC Surface 1 §Cancel affordance: on click, abort → unmount the card → keep every field value → **move focus to the submit button**, and render a NEUTRAL `text-caption text-text-secondary` line where the card was: *"We stopped waiting. Nothing was saved and your key details are still on this page."* ⛔ Not an `ErrorEnvelope`, ⛔ not red. That belongs to the surface that owns the abort.
- **Do not add a second escalation ladder.** If a composite member panel needs a variation, give `ValidateWaitCard` a prop; a copy in either step is the duplication this component exists to prevent.
- **Two copy strings this plan ships are asserted BYTE-EXACT in `ValidateWaitCard.test.tsx` and are ALSO in the UI-SPEC copy table.** Before changing any of them, grep `e2e/` as well as `src/` (the v1.10 lesson: e2e grep-gates scan `src/` only).
- **WIZFORM-05 stays PARTIAL.** Both client legs now exist — the budget selection (02) and the honest wait (03) — but neither is reachable by a user until 04/05 mount them, and D-27 leaves 120 000 ms provisional until Phase 155.
