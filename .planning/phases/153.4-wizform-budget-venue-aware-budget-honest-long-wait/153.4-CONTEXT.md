<domain>
## Phase Boundary

**Delivers.** The client grants an MT5 validation a budget its honest verdict fits inside,
and a long wait is **legible and abortable** rather than a silent stall.

Concretely:

- The `validate-key` budget goes **venue-aware** — an MT5 arm at `120_000` ms selected by a
  `budgetKeyFor(exchange)` that maps **unknown → the default row** rather than throwing
  (D-01, D-26; RESEARCH Pattern 1).
- `BREAKER_LOCK_TOMBSTONE_S` moves 60 → 90 in the **same commit**, so A-25 holds exactly:
  `(30 + 90) × 1000 = 120 000` (D-26).
- The A-25 pin is re-cut to **derive** the longest budget from `SEAM_BUDGETS` and compare it
  to hand-typed breaker constants — the existing literal-vs-literal assertion is kept
  *beside* it, not replaced (D-19).
- The 16 pin sites (RESEARCH Table C) move together, `seam-budgets.invariant.test.ts`
  SC-4a/b/d/e/f stay green, and the new legs are modelled as mutually exclusive `branch`
  legs so SC-4b takes a MAX, not a SUM (D-04; RESEARCH Pattern 2).
- The long wait becomes honest: in-progress copy, the escalation ladder, a `Stop waiting`
  affordance, and a "waiting for the connection" state distinct from "validating" — never a
  silent spinner (D-05).
- `Validating...` (ASCII) is the one spelling at every call site (D-21).
- The `120_000` is recorded as **PROVISIONAL** and tightened from data in Phase 155 (D-27).

**Requirements:** WIZFORM-05 (**client leg**).

**Depends on:** Phase 153.1 (`SEAM_DEADLINE_EXCEEDED`, `serialized`); best **AFTER**
Phase 153.3 — a budget cannot fix a structurally censored verdict (D-24).

**Owns:** `resilient-fetch.ts`, `analytics-client.ts`, `seam-constants.pin.test.ts`,

**Does NOT deliver — and who owns each excluded item:**

  green at HEAD, precisely so this sub-phase's budget raise cannot pass a pin that cannot
  fail. This sub-phase makes it hold at the new value; it does not invent it.
- `VENUE_CAPABILITIES`, `MIN_DESCRIPTION_CHARS`, the nine `validatePayload` codes, the
  derived roster, the D-34 reorder, the copy table — **Phase 153.1** (WIZFORM-CODES). The
  long-wait card's copy strings live in the table 153.1 owns.
- Inline field validation, the `MetadataStep` / `AllocateDialog` guards, the submit-path
  probe gate and catch-all, the MT5 chip set and preselect — **Phase 153.2**
  (WIZFORM-FIELD).
- Every Python change — the end-to-end deadline, the `initialize()` inversion, the terminal
  lease and its bounded acquisition timeout, `terminal_info()` fail-closed, per-stage
  instrumentation, the runbook single-replica pin — **Phase 153.3** (WIZFORM-GW).
- **Tightening `120_000` from the instrumentation 153.3 adds** — **Phase 155** (MT5-VERIFY),
  live-gated. D-27 makes the number provisional on purpose.
- Any retry loop on the submit path — D-07's prohibition is 153.2's; nothing here reopens
  it. `seam-retry-registry.ts` is owned for the **budget/breaker** contract, not to add a
  new retry.

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Timeouts and budgets (WIZFORM-05) — founder-locked, do not re-derive

- **D-01: The client `validate-key` budget goes venue-aware and LONG on the MT5 arm —
  target ~90–120s, NOT 35s.** Founder 2026-08-07, verbatim: *"why not give it more than
  40 seconds? I don't need absolute exact time data. if it is 2 minutes old, that is
  fine."* ⛔ **35s is a trap number.** It appears three times in REQUIREMENTS.md and
  ROADMAP.md, but always as a description of the *existing server-side bug*
  (`_MT5_PROBE_TIMEOUT_S`), never as a target. A slow MT5 broker login legitimately
  takes **35–70s+** to fail, so a 35s client ceiling would still abandon the request
  before the honest verdict lands and would keep producing `UNKNOWN`.
- **D-04: The invariant to satisfy is `client ceiling > server worst case`**, sized
  END-TO-END so lock-queue wait sits *inside* the client budget, not on top of it.
  Every change must survive `src/lib/seam-budgets.invariant.test.ts`, which recomputes
  per-request sums together with `encrypt-key`. That test is the thing that stops a
  naive bump.
- **D-05: A long budget requires honest in-progress copy, never a silent spinner.** A
  ~2-minute wait with no explanation is a worse product than a fast wrong answer.

- **D-26: the client budget is `120_000` ms, and `BREAKER_LOCK_TOMBSTONE_S` goes 60 → 90 in
  the SAME commit.** 120 s is the founder's stated tolerance (*"if it is 2 minutes old,
  that is fine"*), and the UI-SPEC's `Stop waiting` affordance makes a long wait
  user-abortable, so the cost of generosity is low. A-25 then holds exactly:
  `(30 + 90) × 1000 = 120 000 ≥ 120 000`. The tombstone raise is a one-line change and was
  never a reason to pick a smaller timeout.

- **D-27: this number is PROVISIONAL, and the phase must make it measurable.** No
  uncensored successful MT5 validation exists anywhere — not in Railway logs, not in
  Sentry, not in `docs/evidence/`. Elapsed time is **not instrumented** on the wizard's
  path (`mt5_client.py` and `routers/exchange.py:222-470` contain zero timing).
  ⛔ Do not cite "35–70 s" from `153-UI-SPEC.md:226` as evidence — that line is derived
  from the timeout constants and says so itself. This phase must add per-stage
  `stage` + `duration_ms` instrumentation so the real p50/p95 exists; the budget is then
  tightened from data during **Phase 155** (MT5-VERIFY), which is already live-gated.

- **D-19: the A-25 pin must be re-cut to DERIVE from the longest budget.** Today
  `seam-constants.pin.test.ts:713-718` asserts `60_000 >= 60_000 - 30_000` with **both
  sides hand-typed literals**, so it stays green while its premise goes false. A pin that
  cannot observe the coupling it guards is decorative. Deriving it is in scope.

### Copy

- **D-21: `Validating...` (ASCII) wins; the UI-SPEC's `Validating…` is CORRECTED, not
  blended.** Rule 7 — there is a recorded superseding decision plus four live call sites.
  One spelling, chosen, everywhere.

### Superseded decision — carried for the record, NOT to be implemented

- **D-18 (SUPERSEDED by D-24 — kept for the record):** an earlier reading set the budget at
  `90_000` ms because that was the largest value needing only ONE constant changed under
  the A-25 breaker invariant. **That reasoning was backwards** — it let a test invariant
  pick a production timeout. Founder challenge 2026-08-08: *"I don't know if 90s is the
  right number… maybe you do some research how much is enough time."* Superseded.
  ⚠️ Practical consequence for this sub-phase: **`90_000` is not the number.** D-26's
  `120_000` is, and it is paid for by moving `BREAKER_LOCK_TOMBSTONE_S`, not by shrinking
  the budget to whatever the pin already tolerated. RESEARCH **Table B** computes SC-4b
  under a 90 000 ms arm — read it as arithmetic method, not as the chosen value.

### Inherited but NOT owned here

- **D-24 (owner: Phase 153.3)** — ⭐ the root cause is the Python nested-timeout inversion,
  not the client budget. **Sequencing is binding**: raising the ceiling before 153.3 lands
  only moves where the pile-up happens.
- **D-03 / D-25 / D-29 (owner: Phase 153.3)** — the server's end-to-end deadline, the
  unchanged worker constant, and the bounded lease-acquisition timeout together define the
  "server worst case" D-04 must exceed. Size the ceiling against what 153.3 actually
  ships, not against today's ~105 s + unbounded queue.
- **D-29's queue state (owner: Phase 153.3 for the server, this sub-phase for the UI)** —
  "waiting for the connection" is a *different state* from "validating"; the long-wait card
  and `Stop waiting` are the affordance the server-side decision assumes exists.
- **D-32 (owner: Phase 153.3)** — the instrumentation that turns D-27's provisional number
  into evidence.
- **D-09a / D-34 (owner: Phase 153.1)** — `ConnectKeyStep.tsx` and `MultiKeyConnectStep.tsx`
  host `KNOWN_CREATE_WITH_KEY_CODES` / `KNOWN_ADD_KEY_CODES`, which 153.1 converts from
  hand-listed sets to a derived roster. This sub-phase owns those **files**; do not
  re-hand-list the rosters while editing them for the long-wait card.
- **D-17 / D-22 (owner: Phase 153.1)** — venue-shaped remedies are gated on the capability
  record; the long-wait card must not author a venue-shaped remedy of its own.
- **D-07 (owner: Phase 153.2)** — no retry loop. Touching `seam-retry-registry.ts` for the
  budget contract must not become "add a retry".

## Specific Ideas

**Binding traps (verbatim from `.planning/ROADMAP.md` §Phase 153.4):**

- ⛔ **D-26: the `120_000` budget row and `BREAKER_LOCK_TOMBSTONE_S` 60 → 90 are the SAME commit.** A-25 then holds exactly: `(30 + 90) × 1000 = 120 000`.
- ⚠️ **`budgetKeyFor` must diverge from its analog deliberately.** `process-key-client.ts:123-134` throws on `default:` via a `never` assignment; this one takes a caller-supplied string, so `default:` **returns `"validate-key"` and never throws**. Write the divergence down or a reviewer will "fix" it back. Never interpolate a wire value into a breaker key (T-140-01).
- ⚠️ 16 pin sites (RESEARCH Table C) must move together, including the prose restatements that stay green while their premise breaks.

**Also binding, from the parent phase's ROADMAP trap list:**

- ⚠️ WIZFORM-04: a naive retry loop multiplies the budget `src/lib/seam-budgets.invariant.test.ts` recomputes, and retrying into an open breaker is how one slow venue takes down every other user's submits. The fix starts with "is the call needed", not "add a loop".
