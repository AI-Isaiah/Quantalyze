# Phase 153.4 Plan 05: The composite connect step waits honestly, per panel Summary

**Every composite member panel now owns its wait outright — its own `AbortController`, its own frozen venue, its own budget, its own `Stop waiting` and its own deadline verdict — driven from ONE step-level interval, keyed by panel id so a reorder cannot redirect an abort, and asserted isolated four independent ways of which three were observed to red when the mechanism was neutered.**

## Accomplishments

- **The composite surface stopped being the one that spins.** `MultiKeyConnectStep` mounted nothing before this: a member panel validating against a serialized venue got a disabled `Validating...` button for up to two minutes, which is exactly what D-05 forbids. It now renders `ValidateWaitCard` inside the panel it describes, with that panel's label, that panel's ladder and that panel's escape control.
- **⭐ Per-panel isolation is a property, not a hope, and it is asserted four ways that a step-level implementation fails.** Scope (an idle sibling renders no card, no ladder, no `Stop waiting`, no `aria-busy`), budget (at 12 000 ms the ccxt panel is past 40% of ITS 30 000 ms while the serialized one is nowhere near 40% of ITS 120 000 ms — a shared budget cannot be both), abort target (cancelling panel 0 leaves panel 1's SIGNAL unaborted) and reorder (a panel moved mid-flight cancels the request it actually started). Three of the four were run against a deliberate mutation and observed red.
- **⭐ The abort is keyed by identity, and so is every outcome write.** Controllers and abort reasons live in `Map<panel.id, …>`; the plan required that much. The half the plan did not name is that the OUTCOME arms had the same disease: `updatePanel(idx, …)` writes to whatever panel now sits at the captured index, so a reorder during a validate would have written one member's failure — or its verified `api_key_id` — onto another. A new `updatePanelById` fixes the class inside the function this plan owns, and the reorder test asserts the cancelled line follows the panel rather than the position.
- **ONE interval, not N.** A single `setInterval(1000)` runs while ANY panel is validating; it reads `panelsRef` (this file's "sync in an effect, never write during render" rule), evaluates each validating panel's deadline against ITS OWN venue budget, and patches only the rows that are actually running. `grep -c setInterval` is 1. N timers for a once-a-second render is T-153.4-22, and it is the shape a naive per-panel copy of `ConnectKeyStep` would have produced.
- **A cancel and a deadline are told apart before anything classifies them.** Both arrive as the same `AbortError` on the same catch. The catch branches on the recorded reason FIRST: a user cancel renders a neutral line, logs nothing and fires no `wizard_error`; the deadline emits `SEAM_DEADLINE_EXCEEDED` and fires exactly one event carrying it, on the COMPOSITE funnel step (`connect_key_multi`) — merging it into `connect_key` is what made the multi-key path invisible before 140.3-13a. Both directions asserted, in separate cases.
- **⭐ The `REQUIRES_CONNECT_SURFACE` gate is paid at this surface too, in the commit that created the obligation.** The per-panel `buildEnvelope` now receives `surface: "connect"`, `budgetSeconds` (from the frozen attempt venue, `undefined` for every other code) and `venue`. Without the surface, 153.1-04's absence-suppresses rule silently withholds *"Your key details are still on this page."* from the user who most needs it. Without the venue, a serialized-venue member reads *"switch to a different exchange"* about an account that IS the venue (D-17). Both have their own `it`.
- **The browser gives up LAST, per panel.** Each panel aborts at `validateBudgetMsFor(itsOwnVenue) + WAIT_ABORT_GRACE_MS` — 135 000 ms on the serialized arm, 45 000 ms on the ccxt one — never at the budget itself. A composite that mixes the two would, under a shared deadline, cut a two-minute grant off after forty-five seconds and re-create the silent UNKNOWN.
- **Full suite green:** 791 files / **11 605 passed / 287 skipped / 0 failed** (11 587 → 11 605, the 18 new cases); `npx tsc --noEmit` clean; `npm run lint` 0 errors.

## Deviations from Plan

### Acceptance criteria reconciled rather than silently satisfied

**1. The `299/301 ms` mount-gate assertion contradicts the plan's own Task-1 action text, and the action text wins.**

Task 2 asks to port "the mount gate at 299/301 ms". Task 1 mandates that this step drive every panel from ONE 1 s interval and says so explicitly: *"At a 1 s tick, `waitElapsedMs` is 0 until the first tick, so a sub-second answer never reaches 300 — which satisfies the render gate without a second timer. Say so in a comment so a reader does not 'fix' it by adding one."* Under that design the card cannot appear at 301 ms; it appears at the first tick.

The resolution is the house one: assert the PROPERTY UI-SPEC states rather than the literal boundary another step's mechanism happens to produce. The case asserts no card at 299 ms, **no card at 301 ms** (the tick gate is strictly later than the 300 ms floor, so the render gate is cleared a fortiori) and a card at 1 000 ms. The gate is still falsifiable — deleting the `>= WAIT_CARD_MOUNT_DELAY_MS` comparison alone would not red it, but deleting the `p.status === "validating"` / `waitStartedAt !== null` guards would, and the fast-path case covers the flash the gate exists to prevent. This is the **fifth** time in this phase an acceptance grep or literal has collided with its own task's mechanism (153.4-01, -02, -03, -04 all recorded one); the resolution is the repo's each time.

**2. `grep -c $'…'` returns 1, not the 0 the criterion demands — and it returned 1 at HEAD too.**

The pre-existing occurrence is the rehydrate banner's `Loading your saved keys…` (a different surface, untouched by this plan). My own first draft added a SECOND occurrence inside a new comment (`updatePanel(idx, …)`); it was rewritten to `updatePanel(idx, patch)`, so the count is 1 before and 1 after — **this plan added none.** The property the grep proxies for is D-21: no U+2026 in the RENDERED busy label. That is asserted rather than grepped — the busy-label case walks the panel through three rungs, asserts the button reads ASCII `Validating...` at each, and asserts the card's `textContent` contains no `…`, which a comment can never satisfy. The banner is logged in TODOS.md for the repo-wide ellipsis sweep already opened by 153.4-03.

### Deliberate divergences from the plan's action text

**3. A FOURTH `PanelState` field: `waitExchange`, the venue frozen at validate.** The plan lists three. The exchange cards stay clickable while a panel's validate is in flight, so a mid-flight card click would re-time that panel's client deadline, re-cut its ladder and re-label its card — advertising a budget nobody granted (T-153.4-12), and cutting a 135 000 ms deadline to 45 000 ms mid-request. 153.4-04 froze the same thing for the same reason and its summary hands the requirement forward explicitly (*"Freeze the venue per panel at submit"*). It is deliberately NOT cleared when the wait ends, because the failure envelope is rendered after the attempt and must name the budget that attempt was granted.

**4. `updatePanelById` — the outcome arms no longer write by index.** The plan requires the abort MAPS to be identity-keyed and gives the reorder-safety assertion as the falsifier. Making only the maps identity-keyed leaves a second, identical bug one line away: `updatePanel(idx, …)` in the outcome arms writes to whatever panel occupies the captured index at resolve time, so a reorder during a validate lands one member's failure — or its verified `api_key_id`, which is worse — on another panel. That is precisely T-153.4-19 (one panel's state rendering under another). The fix is scoped to the function this task rewrites; `updatePanel(idx, …)` is untouched for the synchronous UI edits, where the index is current by construction. Asserted: after a reorder-then-cancel, the cancelled line is on the panel that started the request and NOT on the position it used to hold.

**5. Focus restoration uses a ROW ref plus a pending-id ref, not a direct button ref.** Two measured facts, both inherited from 153.4-04: `ui/Button.tsx` accepts no `ref` (TS2322) and this phase's UI-SPEC ⛔ forbids editing it; and at the instant of the click the panel's validate button is still `disabled`, so `focus()` would be a no-op dropping focus to `<body>`. The step registers each panel's validate ROW by id, records the panel to focus when `Stop waiting` is pressed, and an effect focuses the button inside that row once the panel is back to `editing`. Asserted by `document.activeElement`.

**6. `venue` / `surface` / `budgetSeconds` are paid at ONE of the five `buildEnvelope` sites, not four.** The spawn brief says "the same envelope context at its four `buildEnvelope` sites"; the PLAN's own interfaces block ⛔s `handleContinue` and the step-level `continueError` envelope out of scope (*"That is `composite/set-members`, not a validate — it spends no venue budget and gets no wait card"*), its acceptance criterion requires `git diff` to show no hunk touching `handleContinue`, and 153.4-04's handoff names `:1320` — the per-panel one — as the site that will emit the code. Conflict surfaced, not averaged (Rule 7): the plan and the predecessor summary agree with each other. There is also a substantive reason the step-level envelopes must NOT receive a venue — a composite has N venues and no single one to name, so passing panel 0's would answer panel 3's question with panel 0's venue. The obligation is bound to the site that emits `SEAM_DEADLINE_EXCEEDED`, and that site is paid.

### Out of scope, logged not fixed

**7. `MultiKeyConnectStep` has no MT5 exchange card.** Its `EXCHANGES` array stops at deribit. The only path that produces a serialized member panel is the UAT/F-4 draft carry-over — pick MT5 on the single-key form, click "+ Add another key window", and panel 0 inherits `exchange: "mt5"` — which is **reachable in production** and is the path this plan's mixed-venue assertions take. The wait behaves correctly on it, but the panel renders four cards with none pressed and would POST `passphrase: null` (the broker server), so the SUBMIT would fail for a reason the user cannot see. Fixing it is a product decision (add the card with its three-field labelling, or refuse to carry a serialized draft into a composite panel), not a defect this plan could take. Logged in TODOS.md; natural neighbour is Phase 155.

**8. `e2e/api-key-flow.spec.ts` still has the two pre-existing failures 153.4-04 MEASURED.** Both are `request`-fixture POSTs to `/api/keys/validate-and-encrypt` expecting 401 and receiving 403, because `withAuth` runs `assertSameOrigin` before authenticating. No file this plan touches is involved — neither case renders a wizard step. Already logged in TODOS.md by 153.4-04; not duplicated.
