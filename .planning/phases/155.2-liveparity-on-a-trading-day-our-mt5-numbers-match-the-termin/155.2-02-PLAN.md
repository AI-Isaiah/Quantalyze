<objective>
Fix the engine defect that makes every real USD account's Gate A unmeasurable. `_native_unit_present` in `services/mt5_parity.py` treats ANY non-NULL `native_unit` as a unit mismatch. The marks migration documents that a USD-unit MT5 account stores `native_unit 'USD'`, so `start_level`, `end_level`, `cum_return` and `day_placement` read `not_measurable:unit_mismatch` for all three founder accounts. That is what the Phase 155 dress rehearsal printed, and that record wrongly blamed the synthetic oracle (RESEARCH section 2, Pitfall 1).

Purpose: CONTEXT locks that "any Gate A discrepancy is fixed at its root in shared money-math, never scoped to exchange === 'mt5'". This plan fixes the measurement blocker before the real Gate A run (plan 03), for MT5-07.
Output: the corrected predicate with a USD/BTC/non-USD-currency test trio, a Gate-A-only final_green test, and a RED proof.
</objective>
