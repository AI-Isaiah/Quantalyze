<objective>
Root-cause findings F-155-01, 02 and 03 (cross_store, group G1 in RESEARCH section 3) per CONTEXT "Discrepancies and flags". The leading hypothesis (RESEARCH A2) is that the harness compares a series derived by differencing the stored cumulative curve against per-key rows with exact `===`, so float round-off near 1e-16 reads as a divergence. This plan diagnoses first, on the sealed capture: it splits every differing day into missing, sub-tolerance and material. Only then does it replace exact equality with a documented absolute tolerance. A missing day or a material gap stays a difference.

Purpose: MT5-09 and MT5-10. A harness defect must not pose as a product defect, and a real divergence must not hide behind float noise.
Output: harness changes with tests and a RED proof. The decomposition and re-run outcomes go to the capture directory, and the counts go into the SUMMARY.
</objective>
