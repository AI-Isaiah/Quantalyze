# Phase 155.2 Plan 04: cross_store round-off versus real divergence Summary

Float round-off from differencing the stored cumulative curve was 98.4 percent (362 of 368 differing days) of the cross_store differences on the sealed capture (RESEARCH A2 confirmed); a tolerance of 1e-9 on a daily return removes it, and what remains is a small set of missing days that stays counted.

## Decomposition on the sealed capture, before the rule changed (Task 1)

Counts only. `material` is 0 for all three labels, so the material-gap ratio is 0 (3 s.f.: 0.00) and no real value divergence between the per-key rows and the strategy series exists beyond round-off.

| Label | days_compared | missing | below_tol | material | material ratio to MT5_PARITY_TOLERANCE |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| MM2 | 243 | 1 | 114 | 0 | 0.00 |
| MM3 | 243 | 1 | 113 | 0 | 0.00 |
| FX-AI_V | 177 | 4 | 135 | 0 | 0.00 |

The baseline findings (exact equality) reported 115, 114 and 139 differing days for MM2, MM3 and FX-AI_V. Those are exactly missing plus below_tol (1+114, 1+113, 4+135), so the decomposition accounts for every differing day with nothing left over.

## Where the missing days sit (read-only look at the sealed slices, dates and store names only)

- MM2 and MM3: one day each, 2026-03-21, the first of 243 days, present in the per-key and strategy-linked rows and absent from the strategy series. The strategy series here is curve-derived (no stored `daily_returns`), and `equityCurveToDailyReturns` emits no return for the curve's first point, so the series starts one day after the curve's first date.
- FX-AI_V: four days. 2026-05-26 (the first of 177 days) is the same first-curve-point case. 2026-11-16, 2026-11-17 and 2026-11-18 are the last three days: present in the per-key rows, absent from both the strategy series and the strategy-linked rows (the strategy series is three days behind the per-key store).

Whether those days are a product defect or the expected shape of the derivation is NOT ruled on here. The plan fixes the rule: a missing day stays a difference, and no tolerance was widened to absorb it.

## Tolerance change and RED proof (Task 2)

`differing` now counts a day when it is missing from any compared store or when any compared value differs from the stored series by more than CROSS_STORE_ABS_TOL (a non-finite gap also counts). Block comment updated; the old `m.get(d) === stored.get(d)` expression is gone (grep count 0).

- Neuter: the filter set back to exact equality (`gap !== 0`) in the live file, from a byte backup in the scratchpad.
- RED: 1 failed, 17 passed. The failing test is "155.2-04 cross_store tolerance: a 1e-12 per-key gap is not a divergence; a 1e-6 gap and a deleted day still are", with `expected 1 to be +0` on the 1e-12 case.
- Restore: copied back from the byte backup; `cmp` printed nothing (clean); suite back to 18 passed. No git checkout, restore or stash was used.

The existing "cross_store counts every day ... finds the two stores equal" test, WR-05 and the NEUTER test stayed green unchanged.
