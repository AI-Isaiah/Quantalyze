<objective>
Close founder ruling G4-1 (b), recorded verbatim in `155.2-RULINGS.md`: "a pipeline defect. Enqueue the analytics recompute after a key-mode derive, as a gap-closure fix with a test that can fail."

The gap (F-155-08, F-155-12, and through them F-155-07, F-155-09, F-155-11 and the three trailing days of F-155-01; F-155-15, F-155-17, F-155-21 and F-155-26 are the same lag, absent on the sealed capture): the per-key series runs ahead of the strategy analytics. On the sealed capture, FX-AI_V's per-key series ended 2026-11-18 while its strategy series ended 2026-11-15.

Mechanism, read from code during planning (this replaces RULINGS' "cannot be read from the capture" for the code half):
- The nightly key fan-out `enqueue_derive_broker_dailies_for_allocator_keys` (05:30 UTC) derives EVERY eligible key in key mode. Per the handler's docstring, key mode "does NOT enqueue compute_analytics_from_csv".
- The daily strategy-mode re-entry is `routers/cron.py` `cron_sync` (D-02 of Phase 167.2.1.1.1: "EVERY ELIGIBLE STRATEGY OF A HEALTHY KEY IS DERIVED EVERY DAY"). But `_sync_single_key` defers any key whose exchange is not in `EXCHANGE_CLASSES` (binance, okx, bybit, deribit) or not in `DAILY_PNL_VENUES` (okx, binance, bybit). So an mt5, sfox or deribit strategy never gets that daily derive.
- The only other strategy-mode refresh for those ledger venues is the single-key ledger fan-out. It admits a strategy only once `ledger_refresh_staleness` reads it stale, which needs a series older than 4 days. A lag of 1 to 4 days is therefore invisible to every refresher, and the 3-day FX-AI_V lag sits inside that window.

What "the analytics recompute" is for a broker strategy, and why this plan does not enqueue `compute_analytics_from_csv` alone. `run_csv_strategy_analytics` reads the STRATEGY-keyed `csv_daily_returns` rows (keyset read by strategy_id in `services/analytics_runner.py`), while a key-mode derive writes only `api_key_id`-keyed rows. Enqueuing compute alone would re-publish the same stale strategy rows and close nothing. The repo's own name for the broker recompute is `_recompute_kind = "derive_broker_dailies"` in `cron_sync`: the strategy-mode derive rewrites the strategy-keyed rows and chains `compute_analytics_from_csv` through `_enqueue_csv_analytics`. This plan enqueues that kind, as `cron_sync` does, carrying the single-key fan-out's refresh marker (Task 1 gives the reason). It is the ruling's recompute expressed through the chain head, not a different fix. The SUMMARY repeats this paragraph so the founder can read it.

No database migration: `enqueue_compute_job` already accepts `p_strategy_id` + `p_kind` from the service role and dedups on (target, kind) over in-flight jobs (latest `_enqueue_compute_job_internal`, migration 20261114130000). The reads (strategies, strategy_analytics, compute_jobs) are service-role reads. If execution finds a migration IS needed, stop and report: migrations need migration-reviewer and rls-policy-auditor, and they auto-apply to PROD on merge.

Ship order: its own PR after the plans 01-10 PR lands, before plan 155.2-12, whose recapture is the closure evidence for F-155-08 and F-155-12. Plan 12's `depends_on` names this plan.
