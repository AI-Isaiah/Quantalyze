## Decisions taken as defaults (no founder available)

- D17-1 Outcome: a new bucket `oracle_account_mismatch` in `services/mt5_parity.py`, not in `PASSING_BUCKETS`. Every window of the account becomes one RED `AccountVerdict` with that bucket, no legs and reason `oracle_account_mismatch:<arm>`. It is not routed through `_refused_verdict`, which stamps `unexplained`.
- D17-2 Capture: `capture-keymap.sql` gains two columns, `account_shared` (`account_shared_with_api_key_id IS NOT NULL`) and `venue_account_sha256` (hex sha256 of the login text, NULL when the login is NULL). The exact-column contract means a capture sealed before this plan (including the 2026-11-22 recapture) is refused at load by name. That is deliberate: ruling (a) needs a fresh capture with MM3 relabelled to k10 anyway.
- D17-3 Hash limit: an unsalted sha256 of a short numeric login is brute-forceable, so the hash is treated as identifying. It lives only in capture directories, which are outside the repo and never committed. The brief allows a hash; recorded as a known limit.
- D17-4 Report login: `report_account_matches(data, sha256_hex)` in `mt5_parity_report.py` returns only a match verdict (`True`/`False`/`None` for unreadable); the login never enters `ParsedReport`. The login is the first token of the `Account:` header cell (text before the first whitespace or `(`), stripped as `mt5_venue_account_id` strips, never `int()`.
- D17-5 Scope: the gate sits in the verdict path (`scripts/mt5_parity_run.py` `_prepare`, through `evaluate_windows(account_mismatch=...)`), the only path that binds a Report to a label. The records/findings path compares product surfaces with each other and binds no Report, so it has no login arm; G5-2 makes a duplicate key visible there through `keys_sharing_login`.
- D17-6 Sharpe: three decimals is stricter than 3 significant figures for |Sharpe| >= 1 and looser below 0.1. A re-run may surface Sharpe rows that 3sf hid, or clear rows it raised.
