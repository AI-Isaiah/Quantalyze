# Phase 155.2 Plan 05: per-key leg asset class derives from the key's venue (G2) Summary

A per-key composer-book leg now takes its asset class from its key's exchange through one shared helper, so a pure MT5 book annualizes risk on 252 instead of the hard-coded crypto 365, while mixed, crypto and unknown-exchange sets stay on 365.

## Task 1: assetClassForExchange (commit 0ec070c9d)

- `assetClassForExchange(exchange)` sits directly below `isCryptoExchange`: a crypto venue gives `crypto`, any other supported venue gives `traditional`, absent, empty or unsupported gives `null`. Case-insensitive; it never compares against a venue name.
- Tests were written first and were RED (4 failed, `assetClassForExchange is not a function`) before the helper existed, then green. They pin: crypto venues and case, mt5 as traditional, null for undefined, null, empty, an unknown name and the inherited name `constructor`, and the blend: two mt5 legs 252, mt5 with a crypto leg 365, mt5 with an unknown leg 365.
- Acceptance: `export function assetClassForExchange` count 1; `"mt5"` literal count in closed-sets.ts stays 5; `=== "mt5"` stays 1 (the helper adds none).
- `closed-sets.mt5-flag.test.ts` and `closed-sets.test.ts`: 66 passed.

### Reader census for a per-key unit's class (step 5)

Every consumer of a per-key unit's class goes through `blendPeriodsPerYear` (scenario-compare.ts `computeMetricsForDraft` basis, ScenarioComposer.tsx basis, the harness). No reader applies `annualizationPeriods` to a per-key unit. The other `annualizationPeriods` call sites in non-test source read a strategy row's class or a fixed class, not a per-key unit: `annualized-cell.tsx` (a `Strategy` row's `asset_class`), `compare/page.tsx` (a strategy), `AlphaBetaDecomposition.tsx` (the book constant), `HoldingFactsheet.tsx` and `holding-compare-adapter.ts` (the literal `crypto`), and the factsheet, figure-record, queries, finalize and freshness modules (a strategy or analytics asset class). So a key whose exchange is missing moves from the old literal `crypto` to null, and both read 365 in the blend: no change for any existing caller, and nothing needed stopping.
