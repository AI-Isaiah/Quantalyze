<objective>
MT5D-01, the fourth attempt-ending path. The hourly terminalizer `retention_compute_jobs_orphaned_running`
ends a running job as failed_final ('orphaned') when no worker completed it within its 4-hour claim window,
or when it has no claim stamp after 48 hours. That ends an attempt, and the founder ruling for MT5D-01 asks
for a record of every failed attempt's cause. This plan records it with a narrow trigger on that transition.

Purpose: RESEARCH Open Question 2 is decided IN, with cause class `orphan_reaped` (plan 01 DEC-01-A).
RESEARCH proposed re-registering the terminalizer cron, and that is barred: under the Phase 164.7 rule (D-02
in docs/runbooks/derivecron-go-live.md, D-10 in outagealert-migration-schedules-nothing.test.ts) a migration
never schedules or re-registers a job. A re-registration would also re-point the terminalizer gate's 28
twins. A WHEN-qualified AFTER UPDATE trigger records the same fact without touching the cron body.
Output: one migration, its rollback, one gate, one snapshot.
</objective>

## Decisions this plan records

- **DEC-07-A A trigger, scoped by WHEN.** `AFTER UPDATE OF status ON public.compute_jobs FOR EACH ROW WHEN
  (OLD.status = 'running' AND NEW.status = 'failed_final' AND NEW.error_kind = 'orphaned')`. Only the
  terminalizer writes error_kind 'orphaned' (mark_compute_job_failed admits transient, permanent and unknown
  only), so the trigger fires for exactly its rows. Postgres evaluates the WHEN before calling the function,
  so every other compute_jobs update pays a column check and nothing more. This is the one trigger-written
  cause class. The other three are written in-function (plans 01 and 02), because those functions can be
  re-based by a migration and the cron body cannot. Recorded so the two mechanisms read as a decision and not
  as drift.
- **DEC-07-B What the row carries.** It copies from OLD: claim_token, attempts and claimed_by. It copies from
  NEW: id, kind, strategy_id, api_key_id, exchange, created_at, error_kind and last_error. ON CONFLICT
  (job_id, claim_token, cause_class) DO NOTHING. The 48-hour arm's rows have a NULL token. NULL tokens never
  deduplicate, and the terminalizer transitions a row out of running only once, so each reaped attempt gives
  one row.
- **DEC-07-C No catch-up sweep.** A backfill of past reaps would double-write the NULL-token arm, and RESEARCH
  census (e) found no orphaned_running_reaped markers in PROD's retained window anyway.
