Purpose: TODOS `FANOUT-COHORT-SIBLING-POLL-01` asks to "decide both conjuncts together, with a fresh
measurement". RESEARCH Summary point 4 found the real exclusion is the second conjunct, not the status list.
`/cron-sync` stores daily P&L through the `sync_trades` SQL RPC and then enqueues a strategy-mode
`derive_broker_dailies` job per strategy that stored rows (docs/runbooks/cron-sync-go-live.md, steps 2 and
3). It never writes a `sync_trades` compute job. RESEARCH's assumption A3, "/cron-sync writes no
compute_jobs row", is wrong in the letter but right where it matters here. A strategy kept current by
/cron-sync alone never has the "done sync_trades job in 30 days" the cohort requires. PROD census (b) shows
5 sync_trades jobs in 30 days. Widening the status list alone would enqueue almost nothing. HEAL-05's shape
is Claude's Discretion (CONTEXT).
Output: one migration re-basing the cohort only, its rollback, one gate, one snapshot, and a recorded fresh
PROD measurement.

## Decisions this plan records

- **DEC-06-A Both conjuncts, decided together.**
  - Status becomes `IN ('published','pending_review','private')`.
  - The key-health conjunct becomes `EXISTS (done sync_trades job for the strategy in the last 30 days) OR
    EXISTS (strategy_sync_cursors row for the strategy with last_sync_at > now() - interval '30 days')`.
  - The noun is "a successful sync of this strategy's key in the last 30 days". The cursor advances only on a
    non-degraded fetch (routers/cron.py), so it means the key worked.
  - Add-alongside, not a replacement. The user-triggered sync path still writes a compute job and not the
    cursor, so replacing the job conjunct would drop strategies that path keeps current. This is accepted
    debt: promote to the cursor alone once every sync path writes it.
- **DEC-06-B Key liveness.** It adds `ak.is_active AND ak.disconnected_at IS NULL`. Today's cohort polls a
  disconnected key that synced in the last 30 days. The new cohort is a superset of today's except for those
  keys, which the prohibition requires dropping.
- **DEC-06-C Venues.** The cursor is written only for the venues /cron-sync syncs (DAILY_PNL_VENUES in
  services/exchange.py: okx, binance, bybit). Those are exactly the venues `fetch_positions` supports
  (services/positions.py). So the widening adds no strategy the poll cannot serve. The job conjunct keeps its
  old reach unchanged.
- **DEC-06-D Nothing else moves.** The loop body, the in-flight pre-count, the enqueue_compute_job call, its
  idempotency key and metadata, the return value and the worker caller all stay byte-identical. That is what
  keeps the enqueue live (F-8).
