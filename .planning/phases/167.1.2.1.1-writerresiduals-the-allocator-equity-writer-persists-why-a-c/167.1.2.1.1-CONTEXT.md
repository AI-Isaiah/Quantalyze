## Phase Boundary

The allocator equity writers are:
- the derive: `run_derive_allocator_equity_job` in `analytics-service/services/job_worker.py`, with the stitch and grouping helpers in `services/allocator_equity_derive.py`;
- the daily refresh: `run_refresh_allocator_equity_daily_job` and `_fetch_latest_holdings_per_eligible_key` in `services/equity_reconstruction.py`.

This phase makes those writers persist why a curve was refused. It also flags a failing, never-polled or over-age key instead of carrying it flat or at $0, and counts each account once. A book that reads "ready" is then never silently diluted or missing a key. Scope is ROADMAP SC-1 to SC-9 for this phase. The reader changes are limited to D-04's mapping onto existing lines. Every other reader item belongs to 167.1.2.1.2 HISTORYREADER.

Measured at HEAD `24065b26b` (2026-10-16), by reading code:
- The snapshot is first-writer-wins on `(allocator_id, asof)` (`persist_equity_snapshots`), so a short or stale total stays wrong for that day.
- The Overview curve no longer reads `allocator_equity_snapshots`; it reads `allocator_equity_derived`, with `equityHistoryReadiness` in `src/lib/queries.ts`.
- Three readers still use the snapshots: `/compare` per-holding metrics, the dashboard's per-holding returns and live baseline, and the match scorer `analytics-service/routers/match.py`.
- The derive's refusal paths call `_delete_equity_curve_row()`. These are identity (duplicate/collision), B2 empty, F1b incomplete, F2 corrupt input, compose refused and the read cap. With no row, the reader names `awaiting_derivation` ("recomputed daily"), which is false for a refusal.
- `no_working_groups` counts only groups of 2+ eligible keys (`if len(members) < 2: continue`).
- `stitch_shared_account` takes a day's flows from the owning key only.
- `_fetch_latest_holdings_per_eligible_key`:
  - a never-polled group adds nothing and only `never_polled += len(eligible)` records it;
  - the carry has no horizon, and `CARRY_AGE_WARN_DAYS = 3` only logs;
  - `_identity_unknown_and_not_working` covers only NON-working unstamped groups (R2-CR-02).
- The reader already holds the Overview under `account_identity_pending` / `key_not_syncing` for any unstamped eligible ccxt key (`identityStillPending`).

## Implementation Decisions

### Founder decisions (2026-10-16, relayed by the orchestrator; every answer was the recommended option)

- **D-01 (SC-4, SFH-08, founder Q1 A): hold the write.** No `allocator_equity_snapshots` row is written for the day while an eligible account has never polled AND one of its eligible keys is WORKING (`working_holder_predicate`). The hold is named in the audit (`refresh_held_*` reason plus counts) and in a Sentry event (`capture_message`, warning, static message, tags only, no ids or USD). A never-polled account whose eligible keys are all failing (`sign_in_failed` / `error`) does NOT hold the book. It is left out of the total, with a named count in the audit and a Sentry event, so a permanently broken new key cannot stop the book's snapshots.
- **D-02 (SC-5, SFH-09, founder Q2 A): 7-day carry cap, then hold.** A carried account whose latest `asof` is more than 7 days before today is not carried, and the day's write is held through the same mechanism as D-01. A Sentry event names the count of over-age accounts and the oldest age; the audit carries the same counts. `CARRY_AGE_WARN_DAYS` keeps its log role or is folded into the cap; that is a technical choice. No stale or short total is ever persisted.
- **D-03 (SC-2 / SC-3 / SC-6, founder Q3 A): blocking, under the conditions below.**
  - **SC-2:** a single eligible, FAILING key (not working, account id known) whose return series ENDS before the book's last return day is a blocking degrade reason. The book reads "rebuilding" with a named reason. A failing key whose series still reaches the book's last day is not flagged, because nothing is diluted.
  - **SC-3:** in a stitched shared account, when the members' flows DISAGREE on a day the older member owns, the curve takes a blocking reason. A newer key lists a deposit the owning key does not, or the per-day sums differ. Agreement keeps today's behaviour.
  - **SC-6:** two or more eligible WORKING keys on one stamping venue (`VENUES_WITH_ACCOUNT_ID`) that are both identity-unknown (unstamped and unmarked) mean the derive marks the curve blocking with the reader's existing reason `account_identity_pending`, and the refresh holds its write (D-01 mechanism) until the stamper groups them. Once stamped, `account_groups` counts them once. Any grouping change moves Python `account_groups`, TS `accountIdentityTokens`/`keyAccountOf`, `one_key_per_account_day`/`oneKeyPerAccountDay` and the `queries.ts` reader together (167.1.2.1 WR-03). This decision adds a blocking reason and a hold, not a grouping change; if the plan does touch grouping, all four move.
- **D-04 (SC-1, SFH-05, founder Q4 A): the writer stores the reason token, and the reader maps it to existing lines.** A derive refusal persists a reason token where the reader reads it, and the false `awaiting_derivation` / "recomputed daily" line goes away for a refusal. The reader maps each token to a line it already has (`duplicate_account`, `derivation_rejected`, `history_read_failed`, or the named shared-account lines). **No new copy.** The storage shape is technical (see Claude's Discretion). It must never render as a curve to any reader of `equity_curve`.
- **D-05 (SC-9, founder Q5 A): research first, the tolerance is never loosened.** Research reads the MT5 BTC-unit `dust` key's stored series on PROD (read-only, marker query first, counts and verdicts only in tracked text) to find why the implied start is 0.15% against the 0.01% band. If a writer defect causes it, the fix lands at the writer with a RED-first test. If the stored data is right, the measurement goes back to the founder through the orchestrator before SC-9 is closed by decision. The `inception_unreconciled` band is never loosened (NAVBREACH D-02).

### Technical decisions (mine, do not change what users see or what data means)

- **D-06 (SC-7):** the derive's `departed_history_unavailable` is also captured as a Sentry EVENT (`capture_message`, warning, static message, tags only, the same pattern as the identity refusal). A count field, for example `departed_history_unavailable_keys`, is written by `job_worker.py` into the payload or flags, so the number of accounts with missing history is readable.
- **D-07 (SC-8, landing constraint):**
  - Every writer change in this phase deploys outside 03:30-05:00 UTC (R3-WR-02 measured about 03:30-05:00, wider than the roadmap's 04:00-05:00).
  - The ship/deploy note records it, and the merge is timed so Railway's deploy lands outside the window.
  - If a plan removes the constraint instead (for example, the emptiness proof stops depending on the poll's audit timing), the plan states it and a test pins it. Default: the timing constraint stays and is honoured at landing.
- **D-08:** every SC is fixed with a test written first that fails on today's code (pytest from `analytics-service/` with the CI command, vitest for the reader mapping, or a SQL gate arm if a migration lands). RED is proven by neuter-and-restore from a byte backup.
- **D-09:** every hold or flag is counts-only in logs, audits and Sentry: no key id, venue id or USD figure (T-167.1.2-22 / -32). New audit actions need members in both `services/audit.py` and `src/lib/audit.ts` (`test_action_literal_matches_ts_union`).
- **D-10:** a new blocking `DegradeReason` member joins `_BLOCKING_REASONS`. Its token is mapped by the reader to an existing line (D-04), never to a new string the owner sees.

### Decisions after research (2026-10-16)

- **D-11 (SC-9, after D-05's research; PENDING founder confirmation, planned on the recommended option): see below.** Research re-measured PROD read-only on 2026-10-16, after the marker query named PROD:
  - all 8 connected MT5 keys replay clean on their stored writer basis, with an implied start of at most 1.3e-14 against the 1e-4 band;
  - book 1 reads trustworthy;
  - 0 curve rows carry `inception_unreconciled`.

  The 0.15% residual of 2026-10-13 was the shape of the rows before the 164.6.6.2.3 recompute (they had no writer basis), and the derives since have rewritten them. It is neither a writer defect nor a true property of the data. The proposed close is that SC-9 is closed by recorded decision as "cleared upstream, re-measured clean". There is no writer change, and the band stays untouched. Execution re-runs the read-only measurement and records counts and verdicts in the SUMMARY, and the stale docstring of `test_no_capital_exit_inception_residual_stays_dropped` is refreshed.
- **D-12 (D-02 refinement; PENDING founder confirmation, planned on the recommended option): see below.** D-01's working/failing split also applies to the 7-day cap. An over-age account whose eligible keys are ALL failing is left out of the total, with a count and a Sentry event, and does not hold. An over-age account with a WORKING eligible key holds. The point is that a dead key never freezes the owner's snapshot store. If the founder instead keeps D-02 as worded (every over-age account holds), only the over-age branch's working check is removed.
- **D-13 (technical, from research): see below.** For D-01 and D-04, "never polled" means: no `allocator_holdings` row for any key of the group AND no `allocator.holdings.sync_completed` audit event for any key of the group. Measured: 5 Deribit keys on PROD have an empty-poll event and no rows. A group with an event and no rows is "polled, no rows" and does not hold. A failed read raises.
- **D-14 (technical): see below.** One new audit action, `allocator.equity.refresh_held`, with the reason in its metadata. It is added in four places: `services/audit.py`, the `src/lib/audit.ts` union, the `src/lib/audit.ts` entity map, and the `AllocatorEquityAction` Literal in `services/job_worker.py`.
  - Every refresh job writes its own audit row.
  - The Sentry event is raised by one leader job per allocator (the smallest eligible key id), because the fan-out runs one refresh job per key.
  - No migration is needed: the `allocator.equity.%` prefix is already worker-only.
