<objective>
The daily refresh's holds: founder decisions D-01 (SC-4, never polled), D-02 with the D-12 refinement (SC-5, the 7-day carry cap) and the refresh half of D-03 (SC-6, unstamped working keys). A held day writes no snapshot row, names its reason in one new audit action (D-14), and raises one Sentry event per allocator.

Purpose: the legacy snapshot store never records a short or stale total for a day because a key was never read, has gone stale, or is not yet grouped.
Output: the hold branch, the "polled" evidence read, the cap, the audit action in four places, tests.

Known consequence to carry into the SUMMARY: the snapshot table is first-writer-wins with one refresh job per key per day, so a held day is a permanent gap in `allocator_equity_snapshots` by design. Its readers are the dashboard counts, the GDPR export and a non-gating script; the Overview curve reads the derived row.
</objective>
