## Residuals (booked in TODOS.md, unfixed by founder instruction)

None of these makes a success criterion false; each is a WARNING.

- **MR-01 (MEDIUM, USER-FACING), `167.1.2.1.1-NEWBOOK-HELD-BACK-LINE`.** I reproduced it at HEAD: `compose_allocator_equity({}, {'k1': []}, {'k1': 1000.0}, {})` returns an empty, untrustworthy curve with `dropped_key`, and `refusal_payload('compose_empty_blocked', ...)` stores `['compose_empty_blocked', 'dropped_key']`. The reader maps that to `derivation_rejected`, "did not pass its checks". A single-key or all-new book in its first day or two (a key with under 2 days of history) used to read the true "being rebuilt, recomputed daily" line and now reads a held-back line. SC-1 removed one false line and introduced another, narrower one, on the onboarding path. Fix is a small B2-split change plus a fixture row.
- **M-1 / LR-01 (MEDIUM), `167.1.2.1.1-HOLD-SENTRY-LEADER`.** The hold's Sentry event is raised only by the job of the smallest eligible key id (D-14 as decided), which can be a failing key whose job defers or fails preflight. The hold is then audit-only for that book.
- **M-2 (MEDIUM), `167.1.2.1.1-LEFTOUT-SENTRY-NO-WRITE`.** The `left_out_*` events sit after `persist_equity_snapshots`, so a book whose only accounts are never-polled-failing or over-age-failing takes the `no_holdings_today` return with an audit count and no Sentry event. This is a literal shortfall against D-01/D-12's "left out with a Sentry event" in that case; the audit row still carries the counts.
- **M-3 / LR-03 / LR-06 (MEDIUM), `167.1.2.1.1-IDENTITY-HOLD-DERIBIT`.** The SC-6 refresh hold counts Deribit keys although Deribit rows add $0 to the refresh total, and the hold has no bound if the stamper never reads an id. Transient for Deribit, but it freezes the legacy snapshot meanwhile.
- LOW, not booked: LR-02/L-5 (benign refusal rows raise a reader `console.error` and Sentry warning), LR-04/L-1 (SC-7 counts dropped on the empty-compose row), LR-05 (SC-3 flags a pre-window flow the newer key lists), L-2, L-3, L-4, L-6 (test-strength notes: shared mock scope in `_sentry_calls`, no SC-3 parity cell, vacuous no-event assertions without a positive control).
