SC-1 (SFH-05 writer half), per founder decision D-04: a derive refusal persists its reason token in the same `(allocator_id, 'equity_curve')` row the reader already reads, and the reader maps each token onto a line it already has. Today the refusal paths delete the row and the reader falsely says the history is "recomputed daily".

Purpose: a book whose curve was refused names why, instead of claiming it is waiting.
Output: `DeriveRefusal` + `refusal_payload()`, `_persist_refusal` at all refusal sites, the reader mapping, the census mirror, one cross-runtime fixture with Python and TS consumers.

Storage shape (Claude's Discretion, decided here): the SAME row, a version-2 payload with `is_trustworthy: false`, `curve: []`, `returns: []`. Reasons: one atomic upsert replaces a stale trustworthy curve, the reader already routes an untrustworthy v2 row to `untrustworthyRebuildReason`, and RESEARCH Finding 1 enumerated every reader of `kind='equity_curve'` and found `getMyAllocationDashboard` is the only application reader (`match.py` reads neither table at HEAD). A separate `kind` would need a non-atomic delete plus insert and a second read.
