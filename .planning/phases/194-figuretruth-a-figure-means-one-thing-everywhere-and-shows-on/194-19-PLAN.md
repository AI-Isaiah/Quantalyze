<objective>
Re-base `get_verified_cohort_rank` so the Scenario peer cohort leaves out strategies whose total record fails D-15's minimum (crypto: under 90 days; non-crypto: under 60 observations, or under 90 days while the stored count is absent), with a rollback, a lane fixture, a regenerated snapshot and a cross-language bound pin.

Purpose: D-11 "A strategy whose figure is withheld is also left out of the peer set it is ranked against", as superseded by D-15 (withheld only on the total record), for the cohort the Scenario blend is ranked against (census R3: the cohort RPC needs a migration). Shared with Phase 195: none.

Canonical record predicate (D-15). It is written character-identical in the count query and the rank query, directly after the RANK-01 gate line, on ONE line, and the DO block counts this exact text:
`AND NOT COALESCE(CASE WHEN s.asset_class IS DISTINCT FROM 'crypto' AND (a.metrics_json->>'record_observations') ~ '^\d{1,9}$' THEN (a.metrics_json->>'record_observations')::int < v_min_obs WHEN (a.returns_series->0->>'date') ~ '^\d{4}-\d{2}-\d{2}$' AND (a.returns_series->-1->>'date') ~ '^\d{4}-\d{2}-\d{2}$' THEN (a.returns_series->-1->>'date')::date - GREATEST((a.returns_series->0->>'date')::date, CASE WHEN (a.data_quality_flags->>'headline_since') ~ '^\d{4}-\d{2}-\d{2}$' THEN (a.data_quality_flags->>'headline_since')::date END) < v_min_span_days END, false)`
`CASE` fixes the evaluation order: the observation arm runs only for a non-crypto row carrying an integer-shaped count of at most nine digits (so the `::int` cast cannot overflow), every other row falls to the span arm, a cast runs only on a date-shaped string, and `GREATEST` ignores a null `headline_since`. If neither arm can decide, `COALESCE` keeps the row.

Output: the migration, the rollback, the snapshot, the pin, and the review gate.
