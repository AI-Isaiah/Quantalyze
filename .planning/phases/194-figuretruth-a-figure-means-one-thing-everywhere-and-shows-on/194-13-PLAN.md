<objective>
Make the Python producers emit the span each Sharpe lift is measured over, and gate the two places Python itself turns a lift into words.

Purpose: D-11 "Sharpe deltas / lifts ... gated on the shorter of the spans of the figures they compare", as superseded by D-15 (withheld only when a compared TOTAL record is under the minimum: 90 calendar days crypto, 60 observations non-crypto; a window under one year carries the note). A stored optimizer suggestion has no dates, so the display (plan 22) cannot gate it unless the producer records the verdict and the span. `match_engine`'s reasons are rendered on 194.1 pages, but the text is produced here, so it is gated once at its source (assumption A-194-D11-05; 194.1 renders what this emits).

Shared with Phase 195: none.

Known limit, stated not hidden: suggestions and match reasons stored before this ships carry no span and keep today's text until the optimizer or the match batch next runs; plan 09's recompute does not re-run either.

Output: `ONE_YEAR_DAYS`, `annualization_state`, the span on every suggestion and fit component, the gated reason and narrative sentence, tests.
</objective>
