Planner decisions (vocabulary and reads, from RESEARCH "D-01: what a read-only Railway-worker diagnostic can reuse"):
- Reads per key, all GET, through the wrapper: wallet balance with accountType UNIFIED; if ccxt raises a
  `BadRequest` or `ExchangeError` for it, or its `result.list` is empty, one more read with accountType
  CONTRACT; then one position-list read with category linear and settleCoin USDT. No `fetch_balance()` (it adds
  `load_markets` and two account-info calls). No retry. Per-key bound `READ_TIMEOUT_S = 60.0` via
  `asyncio.wait_for`.
- The ccxt-total comparison uses a separate credential-free `ccxt.async_support.bybit({})` instance, created
  once per run and closed in `finally`, calling `parse_balance` on the same raw wallet response; the keyed
  client is never asked to parse.
- Equality of two venue decimals: `decimal.Decimal` on the venue strings; equal when the absolute difference is
  at most `_FIELD_EQ_ABS = Decimal("0.00000001")` (one unit of Bybit's 8-decimal strings) or at most
  `_FIELD_EQ_REL = Decimal("1e-9")` times the larger magnitude. Constant names avoid the substrings dust and tol.
- Key record keys, exactly: record ("key"), key (`^k[0-9]{1,2}$`), run_date (`^20[0-9]{2}-[0-9]{2}-[0-9]{2}$`),
  read_ok, positions_read_ok, failure_class, account_type, ccxt_total_equals_wallet,
  coin_equity_minus_wallet_equals_upnl, account_equity_minus_wallet_equals_perp_upl, upnl_ratio, upnl_nonzero,
  borrow_present, non_usdt_coins_present, non_usdt_upnl_present, positions_upnl_matches_totalperpupl.
  Vocabulary: read_ok and positions_read_ok are booleans; failure_class is one of none, AuthenticationError,
  PermissionDenied, RateLimitExceeded, RequestTimeout, NetworkError, BadRequest, ExchangeError, EmptyResponse,
  Timeout, other; account_type is one of UNIFIED, CONTRACT, other, field-absent; upnl_ratio is a sig3 float or
  field-absent; every other measurement is a boolean or field-absent. The coin-level fields read the USDT coin
  row (equity, walletBalance, unrealisedPnl); the account-level field reads totalEquity, totalWalletBalance,
  totalPerpUPL; borrow_present reads any coin's spotBorrow or borrowAmount; upnl_ratio is (equity minus
  walletBalance) divided by unrealisedPnl for the USDT coin when unrealisedPnl is nonzero.
- Summary record keys, exactly: record ("summary"), run_date, keys (count), keys_read (count),
  keys_informative (count with upnl_nonzero true), keys_identity_holds (count of informative keys whose coin
  identity is true), keys_ccxt_total_is_wallet (count). Counts are non-negative ints and never booleans.
- Exit codes: 0 every key read; 1 any key failed (after every key and the summary are printed) or any other
  failure (exception TYPE only on stderr); 2 refused before any decrypt (no active bybit key, or a selected
  row that `refusal_reason` rejects). `main` takes no options and rejects any argument.
