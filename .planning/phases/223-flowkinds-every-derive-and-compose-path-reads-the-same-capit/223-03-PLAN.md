## Facts measured for this plan (HEAD f9d6d770e, 2026-11-24)

- `fetch_funding_bybit` computes `now_ms` from `datetime.now(timezone.utc)` once and calls
  `walk_paginated(contract, since_ms=since_ms, now_ms=now_ms, fetch_page=_fetch_page)`, with
  `inst_types=("linear", "inverse")`, `window_max_days=7` and
  `default_lookback_days=BYBIT_FUNDING_DEFAULT_LOOKBACK_DAYS` (365).
- `walk_paginated` loops categories OUTER and windows inner; with `since_ms=None` it starts at
  `now_ms - 365` days. `_windows` yields `(w_start, min(w_start + 7 d, now_ms))` and advances
  `w_start = w_end + 1` while `w_start < now_ms`. So a call with `until_ms - since_ms` at most 7 days
  sends exactly one window per category: linear, then inverse.
- Every current caller passes `(exchange, label, since_ms)` positionally and optionally
  `on_malformed=`; a defaulted keyword-only addition breaks none of them.
- The C27 census pin calls `fetch_funding_bybit(exchange, "s", None)` and asserts at least 106
  requests and `TIMEOUT_PER_KIND["sync_funding"] == 180`; it must stay green unedited.
