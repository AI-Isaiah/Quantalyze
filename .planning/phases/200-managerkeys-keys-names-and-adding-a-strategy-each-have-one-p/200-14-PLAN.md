<objective>
Close gap 3 of `200-VERIFICATION.md`: the confirm dialog's Close (aria-label "Close") is 20x20px at
320px and 1280px. It is the header Close of the shared `src/components/ui/Modal.tsx`, a bare
`<button>` around a 20px svg, so every dialog in the app carries it. The fix goes in `Modal`, the root
cause, so every dialog is fixed at once.

**Should a gate have caught it? Yes, and none could.** (1) `tap-target-minimums.test.ts` is a source
scan of a NAMED list of controls fixed after the 2026-06-30 device sweep. It catches regressions on
listed controls only, and the shared Modal was never listed. (2) `e2e/target-size.spec.ts` with
`assertTargetSizes` measures scoped regions only (the legal footer, chart tap-rects) and never an open
dialog. (3) The axe passes (`buildAxe`: `wcag2a`, `wcag2aa`, `best-practice`) do not enable axe-core's
`target-size` rule, which is tagged `wcag22aa`, and no axe pass runs with a dialog open. This plan
adds the two gates that would have caught it: the shared primitive joins the source scan, and the
seeded spec measures real boxes of two open dialogs. Known limit, recorded and not done here: the
whole class (any sub-24px control anywhere) would be caught by adding `wcag22aa` to `buildAxe`. That
re-baselines every axe spec, so it is outside this gap. The SUMMARY records it for the orchestrator
to book.

**Size decision (Rule 7, recorded).** The gap's text offers the repo's 32px dismiss idiom
(`inline-flex h-8 w-8 shrink-0 items-center justify-center`) as an example, and it also asks for
the open dialog to be measured with `assertTargetSizes`. Those two conflict: `assertTargetSizes`
enforces 44px (`MIN_TARGET_PX` in `e2e/helpers/reflow.ts`), and its own comment forbids lowering the
bar. The Close is therefore sized 44x44 (`h-11 w-11`). That matches the `ui/Button` `md` size
(`min-h-[44px]`) the same dialogs use for Cancel and confirm, clears the 24px minimum the gap's truth
names, and needs no helper change. A `-my-3 -mr-3` margin keeps the 20px glyph and the header height
where they are today. No negative LEFT margin: at 320px the title can wrap and fill the header row,
and a left margin would lay the 44px box over the title's last 12px. The rejected alternative (32px, plus a separate 24px measurement) would need a second
target-size helper or a lowered bar.

Purpose: every dialog's dismiss control is operable on touch (WCAG 2.5.8): the re-point confirm
(ROUTE-09), the Rename dialog (ROUTE-10) and the 14 other importers.
Output: the Modal fix, its source pin, and two live dialog measurements.
</objective>
