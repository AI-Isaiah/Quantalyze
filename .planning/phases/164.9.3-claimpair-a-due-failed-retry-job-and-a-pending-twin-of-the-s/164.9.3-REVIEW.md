### IN-11: The probe still counts a due `pending` intro row that C39 drops beside a running intro sibling (new; outside D-11's failed_retry scope)

**File:** `supabase/migrations/20260927120000_claim_pair_pre_rank_exclusion.sql:365` (5-arg) and `:591` (2-arg). The exclusion is scoped by `status = 'failed_retry' AND (...)`.
**Issue:** `compute_jobs_one_inflight_per_kind_strategy` does not cover `compute_intro_snapshot`, so a `pending` intro row can coexist with a `running` intro row on the same strategy. C39's strategy clause has no carve-out, so it drops the pending one, while the probe counts it.

- Measured, shape S3: `cand(pending)=0 unrelated_low=0` on HEAD, under the literal widening, and under the split form.
- For every other kind and column the inflight unique index prevents a `pending` row beside a `running`/`dpc` one, so this is the only pending-candidate gap.
- It is bounded while the sibling is `running`, because the hold ends when the sibling finishes. It is pre-existing (C39 and the counting probe both date from 20260601193000). It is latent, because no writer sets `low`.

**Fix:** Either record it as the residual (i) "what stays", or make the probe exactly the union by lifting the C39 half out of the `failed_retry` scope:

```sql
AND NOT ((status = 'failed_retry' AND <pending-sibling disjunction>) OR <C39 running/dpc disjunction>)
```

The second option is a larger block rewrite and needs its own arm, so the record-it route is the proportionate one for this phase.

### IN-01: Every count arm assumes its rows fall inside `LIMIT 1000` (carried)

**File:** `supabase/tests/test_claim_compute_jobs_failed_retry_pending_pair.sql` (every claim call, including W-LOWTWIN's at `:851-856`)
**Issue:** The claims are queue-wide and ordered by priority, then `next_attempt_at`, then `id`. The arms seed at `now() - 1..10 minutes`. If a lane holds more than 1000 due claimable committed rows that sort earlier, an arm false-reds with a zero count.
**Fix:** Seed arm rows at `now() - interval '10 years'`, or assert a bound on foreign claimable due rows before each claim. Any new W-C39* arm should follow the same rule.
