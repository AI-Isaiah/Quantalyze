## Summary

The defect is exactly as booked, and it is wider than a single tick. Measured this session on a throwaway pg-lane with the three claim bodies copied verbatim from `supabase/schema/baseline.sql`: a `failed_retry` row due 10 minutes ago plus a `pending` twin of the same `(kind, partition)` makes `claim_compute_jobs` and the 5-arg `claim_compute_jobs_with_priority` raise `23505` on **all four** partitions (8 of 8 cells). It does so whether the twin is not yet due, or due later than the retry. The whole batch aborts: an unrelated, due job on another partition is not claimed either. The wedge never clears by itself. Four consecutive ticks, including one after the pending twin was made due, all raised `23505`. The only case that does not raise is a twin that is due EARLIER than the retry, because it then wins the per-partition rank.

The naive fix booked in the ROADMAP is wrong: adding `'pending'` to the C39 `x.status` list with `x.id <> ranked.id`. It stops the error and replaces it with a **silent, permanent** wedge. The C39 guard runs AFTER `row_number()` ranking, so the retry (rank 1) is dropped by the guard and the pending twin (rank 2) is dropped by `rn = 1`. Neither is ever claimed; measured as `tick1..tick4 = []` on all 8 cells. It also starves `compute_intro_snapshot` completely. That kind is carved out of the strategy index, so two `pending` rows may legitimately share a strategy, and the naive guard excludes each against the other: measured, 0 of 3 intro rows claimed, where today's tree claims 3 of 3. The fix that works is a **pre-rank exclusion** in the `ranked` CTE's `WHERE`: a `failed_retry` row whose partition holds a `pending` row is not a candidate at all. Measured on all 8 cells: no `23505`, the unrelated job is claimed, the pending twin runs (at once if due, otherwise when it falls due), and the retry runs after the twin finishes. The intro carve-out still claims 3 of 3.

The 2-arg `claim_compute_jobs_with_priority(integer, text)` overload is **unreachable** while the 5-arg overload exists. Positional, explicitly typed and named calls all raise `42725 ... is not unique` (measured). Its body is the 2026-04-28 migration-090 body, with no C39 guard, no claim-token fence and no `last_error` clear. Called in isolation (5-arg dropped inside the transaction), it raises the same `23505`. With only the pre-rank exclusion added it still raises `23505` on the next tick, because it lacks C39. So the 2-arg must get both the pre-rank exclusion and the C39 guard. Dropping it instead would redden VAC-04 (`SNAPSHOT_MISSING`, which has no acknowledgment path).

**Primary recommendation:** ONE migration that `CREATE OR REPLACE`s all three claim bodies, each copied byte-for-byte from its latest source migration, and adds one thing to each: four per-partition pre-rank clauses (`status <> 'failed_retry' OR NOT EXISTS (pending sibling)`), the strategy clause carving out `compute_intro_snapshot`. The 2-arg overload also gets the C39 block ported verbatim. No enqueue change. It does not touch `defer_compute_job`, `mark_compute_job_done`, `mark_compute_job_failed` or `_enqueue_compute_job_internal`. Its self-verify block is catalog-only.

### Why the naive guard starves (root cause)

`deduped` applies `rn_x = 1` and the C39 `NOT EXISTS` in the SAME `WHERE`, after ranking. When the retry ranks first, the new guard drops it, and the twin was already dropped by `rn_x = 1`. Nothing re-ranks, so the partition is dead until a human intervenes, and nothing is logged. For `compute_intro_snapshot` the strategy index does not apply, and the rank is skipped by the H-1235 carve-out `(strategy_id IS NULL OR kind = 'compute_intro_snapshot' OR rn_s = 1)`. So each pending intro row matches the other as a `'pending'` sibling, and both are excluded forever.

### D-08 Recommendation

**(a) only: claim side, as a PRE-RANK exclusion.** It is not the post-rank form the ROADMAP booked. Add to the `ranked` CTE's `WHERE` of each body, after its last existing predicate:

```sql
      -- CLAIMPAIR (164.9.3): a failed_retry row whose (kind, partition) holds a
      -- pending row is not a candidate. Applied BEFORE row_number(), never in
      -- `deduped`: excluded there it would rank first and take its pending twin
      -- down with it (measured: a silent, permanent partition wedge).
      AND (portfolio_id IS NULL OR status <> 'failed_retry' OR NOT EXISTS (
        SELECT 1 FROM compute_jobs x
         WHERE x.kind         = compute_jobs.kind
           AND x.portfolio_id = compute_jobs.portfolio_id
           AND x.status       = 'pending'))
      AND (strategy_id IS NULL OR status <> 'failed_retry' OR kind = 'compute_intro_snapshot' OR NOT EXISTS (
        SELECT 1 FROM compute_jobs x
         WHERE x.kind        = compute_jobs.kind
           AND x.strategy_id = compute_jobs.strategy_id
           AND x.status      = 'pending'))
      AND (allocator_id IS NULL OR status <> 'failed_retry' OR NOT EXISTS ( ...allocator_id... ))
```

## Common Pitfalls

1. **Post-rank guard = silent permanent wedge.** Measured. The exclusion must be in `ranked`'s `WHERE`. A verifier must include an arm where the twin is due AFTER the retry (−5 min) and assert the twin IS claimed. Asserting "no 23505" alone passes the naive fix.
2. **Forgetting the intro carve-out** on the strategy clause starves nothing with the pre-rank shape (the clause touches only `failed_retry` rows), but it would needlessly hold back an intro retry that cannot collide. Keep `OR kind = 'compute_intro_snapshot'`, and add an arm asserting 3 of 3 intro rows claimed.
3. **Applying the new exclusion to `pending` candidates** (the `x.id <> ranked.id` idea) reintroduces intro starvation. Condition it on `status <> 'failed_retry'`.
4. **2-arg with only the pre-rank clause** still raises `23505` on the next tick (measured). Port C39 too.
5. **2-arg red-first reads 42725.** Expected; the arm must DROP the 5-arg in-transaction to reach the body.
6. **Bare `COMMENT ON FUNCTION`** → `42725` on any lane with both overloads.
7. **Data-dependent verify block** → the TEST apply refuses and PROD deploy is withheld. Catalog-only.
8. **Census moved by N+1** → calibration pins break. Grep, run, set to measured.
9. **Arms whose uncaught 23505 aborts the file** → no arm identity → `MEASURE_FAIL`. Trap, then raise after the block.
