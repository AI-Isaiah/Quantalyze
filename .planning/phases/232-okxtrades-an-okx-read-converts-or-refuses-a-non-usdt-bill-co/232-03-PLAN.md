<objective>
VREAD-06: an OKX daily P&L bill in a currency other than USDT is converted at a stated rate or refused
by name, never summed at face value.

Purpose: the OKX aggregation sums `pnl + fee` per day without ever reading `ccy`, so a BTC bill would be
added to USDT P&L at face value (`[167.1.2.2.2.1-OKX-DAILY-PNL-MIXED-CCY]`; exposure measured nil so far).
Stable coins count at 1.0 under the shared rule; anything else refuses the whole read by a closed token
that every flag reader names, so no caller can store or publish a mixed series.
Output: the refusal in `fetch_daily_pnl`, the token registered at every reader, a new gate file, the
grown parity matrix, the derive refusal test, the cron flag list, re-fixtured `ccy`-less test bills,
PROOF-01 records and the Phase 223 hand-off.
</objective>

## Decisions this plan applies (quoted from CONTEXT and its orchestrator rulings)

- "A bill whose `ccy` is a stable coin under the existing shared rule `services/price_rule.is_stable_value`
  is counted at 1.0 [...]. Any other `ccy`, including a blank or missing one, refuses the OKX daily-P&L
  read by name: a new closed token (for example `daily_pnl_non_usd_okx`) recorded through
  `_record_dq_flag`, carried by `daily_pnl_read_verdict` and the realized refusal set exactly like
  `daily_pnl_truncated_okx`, never summed. No price source is added."
- "The whole read, permanent class (the same history fails the same way), like a cap hit."
- Ruling Q3: "a bill refuses when its `ccy` is not stable under `is_stable_value` AND its finite
  `pnl + fee` is nonzero. A zero-amount non-stable bill contributes 0 at any rate [...]. Blank/missing
  `ccy` with a nonzero amount refuses." Therefore the check sits AFTER the `_finite_float` parse (it needs
  the finite amount), which moves it later than RESEARCH and PATTERNS section 2 placed it; a non-finite
  amount keeps the existing malformed path (D-12: malformed is a data-quality flag, not a refusal).
- Ruling Q4: `okx_archive_job._check_live_realized_flags` (Phase 223's file) is not edited; the hand-off
  line goes to the coordinator.
- The composite member read (inside `run_stitch_composite_job`) never drains flags and would read the
  empty list as flat; it is out of v1.22 by founder ruling F-2 ("Forget about composites",
  `186-COMPOSITE-MEMBER-READ-GAPS` out of scope): record it, do not route it.
</context>
