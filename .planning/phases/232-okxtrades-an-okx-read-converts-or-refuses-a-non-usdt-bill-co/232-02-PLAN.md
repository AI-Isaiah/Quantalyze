contiguous oldest prefix the next tick resumes from. Plan 232-04 wires the cron to store that prefix.
Output: the window-major walk with self-checks and age routing, three constants, one clock seam, one
named exception, one page helper, a new gate file, the re-fixtured OKX tests and PROOF-01 records.
