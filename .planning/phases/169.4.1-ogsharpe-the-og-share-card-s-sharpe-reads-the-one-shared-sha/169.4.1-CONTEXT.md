### D-46: a chain-broken row's card shows CAGR as "—" (founder, AskUserQuestion 2026-09-30)
- **The case.** Review round 1 (CR-01 = SFH-01) found the stored `cagr` is annualized over the stretch
  after the last chain break, while the card's 0.95-year gate measures the full record. Fix `134f353dd`
  hides the stored CAGR when `data_quality_flags.twr_chain_broken` (or `insufficient_window`) is `true`.
  The factsheet shows the same figure with a "covers from" note, and SC4 lets a surface differ only if it
  says why.
- **Decision (founder, "Keep \"—\""):** the card keeps the bare "—". The card has no room for a caveat, and
  hiding a number never misleads. The alternatives offered and declined were: show the stored CAGR when the
  post-break stretch is itself at least 0.95 years, or show it with a small "since <date>" label.
- **Consequence for SC4:** a "—" on the card beside a factsheet figure with a "covers from" note is the
  accepted difference for chain-broken rows (composites included, since they inherit the flag).
- **Reversibility:** reversible (one display condition in `og-metrics.ts`).
