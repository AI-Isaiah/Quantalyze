## The sync budget ledger (why 840.0; DERIVED, the constant's comment carries it in prose)

- `TIMEOUT_PER_KIND["sync_trades"]` is 900 s; the dispatcher wraps the whole handler in `asyncio.wait_for` with it.

- At the deadline, requests already past the check still finish: at most the five semaphore slots, each bounded by ccxt's request `timeout`, whose Binance default is 10,000 ms at 4.5.89 and which `create_exchange` does not set.
