<objective>
SC-1 (R2-02, the silent stall): a Binance raw fills read walks every window from its start to now, proven end to end by promoting 250-01's strict-xfail reproduction to a passing gate.

Purpose: today `_fetch_raw_trades_binance` sends one `fetch_my_trades(symbol, since, limit=1000)` per page with no `until`, so for a linear market ccxt caps the request at `since + 7 days`, a quiet week returns nothing, and the cursor never moves again. A `since_ms` of None sends no startTime at all (the venue's last 7 days), and a seed past the venue's 3-month reach is sent as is. This plan replaces the per-symbol read with a since-aligned window walk that carries an explicit end on every request, starts from the venue's reach when it has no seed or an older one, reads spot-resolved names in 24-hour windows, refuses a row the venue served outside its window, and gives every way a symbol's walk can stop early (page cap, a page it cannot page past) a stop kind and a read-through bound instead of a bare log line. The cursor gate is unchanged here: once the walk returns the newer fill, the existing stamp (newest persisted fill) moves to it.

Output: the walk in `services/exchange.py`, its constants, the window helper and the refusal class; the promoted e2e arm; the 13 migrated Binance tests; the N1 record.

Interim state, recorded (review info item; the phase ships as ONE PR, so no PROD state ever holds it): until 250-05 loads markets before the reverse map, an exchange whose `markets` is unloaded (the reconcile path) leaves a stored linear name unmapped, ccxt routes the raw name to the spot market, and `_binance_fills_window_ms` (which finds no spot market under the raw name) returns the 7-day window, so the walk sends a 7-day span to the spot endpoint, which the venue refuses (24-hour rule). The symbol lands in the holding `binance_partial_symbols`, and an all-symbols refusal raises `BinancePerSymbolFetchError`: louder than HEAD's silent zero-fill spot read. 250-05 closes it.

Spec-less probe fallback: skipped visibly. This phase has no requirement IDs and no SPEC; plans map to SC-1..SC-5 of the ROADMAP `### Phase 250` section.
