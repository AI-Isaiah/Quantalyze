### 2. [Rule 1 — bug] The `IS NOT DISTINCT FROM` ban was placed where it could never fire, and was moved

- **Found during:** Task 1, designing the neuter for that arm.
- **Issue:** As first written, the ban sat AFTER the six positive CASE anchors. Every
  one of those spells `computation_error_job_id\s*=\s*v_…_job_id`, so an edit to
  `IS NOT DISTINCT FROM` breaks a positive anchor first and the ban is never reached.
  That is the file's own named vacuity class: "an assertion that contributes nothing
  while READING as independent coverage."
- **Fix:** Moved to `(P2a-neg)`, immediately after the aggregate anchors and above
  every positive CASE anchor, with the reasoning recorded in place. Arm 7 above is the
  measurement that it now fires and names the actual regression.
- **Commit:** `f1eafd76`
