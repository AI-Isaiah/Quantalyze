```
title:  A draft from an earlier session is still open.

        is that draft — not the key you picked — that our records clash with. Nothing was
        created by this attempt and none of your stored keys changed.
fix[0]: Press “Continue with this key” once more. If the clash was a race with your own first
        press, we hand that draft back and carry on from where it stopped — nothing is created

fix[1]: If it is refused a second time, the open draft belongs to a different key of yours and
        this screen cannot reach it. Email security@quantalyze.com with the correlation id

docsHref: /security#draft-resume
actions:  ["request_call", "expand_log"]       ← neither is in RECOVERABLE_ACTIONS
```
