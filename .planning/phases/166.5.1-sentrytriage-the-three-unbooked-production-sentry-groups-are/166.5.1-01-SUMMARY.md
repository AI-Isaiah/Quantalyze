## Dead-branch stop rule (read end to end before deleting)

`cron_recompute` was read from the cursor read to the final return. After the `if not allocators` guard a non-empty page always enters the loop. Its first iteration either sets `mid_run_kill_switch_state` and breaks (that state returns before the boundary branch) or runs the allocator and sets `last_handled_id` (a raise from `_should_skip_allocator` / the lock propagates out of the handler and never reaches the branch; `_score_one_allocator` failures are caught and still reach `last_handled_id = allocator_id`). No other path reaches `if has_more or budget_exhausted:` with `last_handled_id is None`, so the branch was deleted, not kept.
