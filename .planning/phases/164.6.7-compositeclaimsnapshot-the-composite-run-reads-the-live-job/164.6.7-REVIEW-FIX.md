### Caller trace: nothing between the closure and `dispatch` swallows the raise

I did this with an AST walk of `services/job_worker.py`. For every call to
`_stamp_strategy_analytics_failed` and to its one wrapper `_dispose_broker_nav_error`, it
listed each enclosing `try` whose BODY contains the call, with that try's handler types, plus
any enclosing `with` block. The walk found 21 loads of the two names and 21 calls, so neither
name is passed around as a callback. `RefreshMarkerRereadUnavailable` subclasses `Exception`
directly, so only a bare `except`, an `except Exception` or an `except BaseException` on the
path could catch it.

There are 17 direct stamp calls and 4 calls of the disposer, grouped by what encloses them.
"Outer crawl try" means the one big `try` in `run_derive_broker_dailies_job` whose only
handler is `except ccxt.RateLimitExceeded` (it calls `_stamp_429` and re-raises) and whose
`finally` closes the exchange.
