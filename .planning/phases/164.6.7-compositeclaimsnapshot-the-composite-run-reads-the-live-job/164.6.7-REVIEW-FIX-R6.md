## Alert volume (runbook, re-counted in `c17d048ec`)

Rows changed:
- **Heal programming error:** `unknown` (retried) → **permanent, terminal**. Still +2.
- **Composite stamp, non-object flags:** a new row. **+2**, added to whatever row the attempt then
  reaches.
- **Composite success path, non-object flags:** a new row. **2**, and the job continues.

The bound was re-derived from the code:
- **Single-key, or a composite whose flags are an object:**
  - A retried attempt is at most 3 events and the last attempt at most 4. So one job produces
    **at most 10**. Round 5's figure was 12.
  - With a provenance refusal, the figures are 4 and 5. So one job produces **at most 13**. Round 5's
    figure was 15.
  - These match the reviewer's figures.
- **A composite whose `data_quality_flags` is not an object:**
  - `_flags_object_or_dropped` runs after the status read and BEFORE the marker re-read and the
    write. So when the write fails, nothing replaces the value, and every retry pays the +2 again.
  - A retried attempt is at most 5 events: the drop (2), the breach line, `_stamp_io`'s ERROR and
    its capture.
  - So one job produces **at most 15**, and **at most 18** with a provenance refusal on the write.
  - This needs a malformed value, a marked job whose row is gone, and a write that fails on every
    attempt. The reviewer's 10 (13) did not count the capture it recommended in R6-02.

The round-5 values are kept as dated lineage in the runbook.
