<objective>
Show the harm on the local lane BEFORE anything is fixed (ROADMAP success criterion 1, D-01).

Build `analytics-service/scripts/probe_composite_claimtime.py`, a standalone script that enqueues a
marked `stitch_composite` job, claims it through the real claim RPC (so the handler receives the
claim-time snapshot exactly as the worker does), retracts the marker on the live row with the same
write shape the TypeScript helper uses, runs the real `run_stitch_composite_job` on the snapshot,
reads what the handler wrote, recomputes the SQL bridge's `is_protected` over the live row, then
marks the job failed through `mark_compute_job_failed` and reads the end state.

Purpose: criterion 1 says the harm is shown first or the phase shrinks. Per D-02 the fix ships
either way; this plan records the decision disagreement and sizes the end-state harm.
Output: the committed probe and the pre-fix verdict lines in this plan's SUMMARY.
</objective>
