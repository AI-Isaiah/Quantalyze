### Informational (recorded, not counted)

- **Plain-`complete` retry gap (WR-01), T-05 family.** On `failed_retry`, the SQL bridge rewrites a plain `complete` row to `computing`, so the transient retry protects only `complete_with_warnings` or warned rows. When the gap bites, the result is a loud failure, never a silent one. It is routed to Phase 164.5.2 and documented above `MarkerLiveState` and in runbook items 2 and 4.
- **`_read_existing_failed_row` reads through bare `db_execute`.** If it raises, nothing is written and the exception reaches `dispatch`. Nothing is suppressed and no protection is granted.
- **Sentry volume.** A failed re-read emits up to 3 events per attempt, so at most 9 per job. The runbook's alert-volume bullet records this. It is not a disclosure issue.
- **01-SUMMARY has no `## Threat Flags` section.** The section is missing rather than declared "None". 02 and 03 declare "None".
