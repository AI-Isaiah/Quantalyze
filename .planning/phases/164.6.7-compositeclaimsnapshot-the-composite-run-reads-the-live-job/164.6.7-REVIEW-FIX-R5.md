- no write.

**Seen RED** with the arm deleted: no line carried the cause.

### R5 IN-02 (LOW): the tail mirror failed a job whose work had landed. Fixed, `9a14a3b67`

**The decision, from the code:** failing the job is wrong.

1. The error is deterministic, so every retry re-crawls the venue and re-writes the hop-1 rows.
   Nothing the retry does changes hop 2.
2. `sync_strategy_analytics_status` reads a marked derive that ends `failed_final` as a live
   failure. Its `live_failures` CTE counts it until a newer `done` job of the same kind exists.
   Over a published row it takes the protected branch and writes `computation_error_copy` for the
   derive kind. Over an unpublished row it stamps `failed`. Either way it lands on a row whose hop
   2 may just have succeeded.
3. The capture already carries the "needs a human look" signal.

So the fix keeps ERROR plus the capture, and the job finishes DONE.

**How.** `_refresh_marker_live_state` takes a keyword-only `raise_programming_errors`, with no
default so every caller must choose.
- The chain edge passes `True`. It is unchanged: log, capture, re-raise, nothing enqueued.
- `_refresh_marker_still_on_row`, whose one handler caller is the tail mirror, passes `False`. Its
  docstring now says it never raises for a failed read.

The policy block names the tail mirror as its one deliberate exception, and says why the heal
differs: the heal's job is failing anyway, while the tail mirror's job succeeded.

**Tests.** `test_at_the_tail_mirror_it_is_unknown_after_the_one_enqueue` became
`test_at_the_tail_mirror_it_is_logged_and_the_job_is_done`. It asserts DONE, one enqueue, 2 ERROR
lines (the first names the programming error) and 1 capture.

**Seen RED** with the tail mirror passing `True`. The class docstring's neuter claim was also
re-measured: deleting the programming-error branch turns both cases RED.
