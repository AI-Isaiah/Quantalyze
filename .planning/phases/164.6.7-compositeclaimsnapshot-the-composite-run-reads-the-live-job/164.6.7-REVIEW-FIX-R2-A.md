### WR-01 / SFH-R2-03: plain-`complete` rows (item 4, OPTION TAKEN: QUALIFY, NO MIGRATION)

**Commit:** `227cafdf4`

**File:** `analytics-service/services/job_worker.py`. This change is comments only.

**Why I did not keep the snapshot in job metadata:** a Python-only snapshot cannot close the gap.
1. On the first `failed_retry`, the bridge's branch (a) has already rewritten a plain `complete` row to `computing`. Neither hop-1 honour path writes `computation_status` back:
   - the single-key error-only upsert omits it;
   - the composite one omits it too.
   So an attempt-2 honour driven by metadata would leave the row at `computing`.
2. On exhaustion, the bridge decides in SQL from the live column (`v_publish_healthy`), and no Python code runs.

Closing it for real needs a bridge migration: the non-terminal branch must keep a healthy publish state for a job that carries a refresh marker. Under the brief, I did not write that migration.

**What changed:**
- A canonical "what the transient retry does and does not preserve" note now sits above `MarkerLiveState`. It names the `complete_with_warnings` / `computation_warned` condition and the mechanism.
- Every comment that overclaimed now states the limit and points to that note:
  - the D-09 comment on the entry read;
  - the D-09 comment on the single-key stamp closure;
  - the D-09 comment on the composite stamp closure (worded to avoid the M-3 scan's banned token);
  - the chain-edge comment.

**For the orchestrator:** the TODOS entry and the runbook sync are yours and fixer B's to book. I did not re-measure the live-cohort figure (`complete` 0 / `complete_with_warnings` 5). It is cited as dated.
