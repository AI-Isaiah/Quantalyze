## SC-6 record

The source is RESEARCH Finding 9, measured on the private local-stack lane at planning:
- **Signature:** axe `page-has-heading-one`, target `html`, "Page must have a level-one heading". It matches CI run `36984352063`.
- **Repro:** 3 of 3 lane failures (`--repeat-each=3`), with **0 page errors** in all three, which rules out #418.
- **Mechanism:** just before `buildAxe(page).analyze()`, the masthead `<h1>` was in the DOM with computed `display: block` but a `0×0` rect. Its only hidden ancestor was `DIV#S:1[hidden=true] display=none`, React's streaming-SSR hidden segment, delivered but not revealed. Three `loading.tsx` `.animate-pulse` skeletons were still present. A second axe scan 3 s later found **no** violations. The spec's waits (`toHaveText` / `toContainText`) do not require visibility, so they matched text inside the hidden segment.
- **Verdict: not the same class** as the hydration mismatch. It is a test-side streaming-reveal race, and the tick fix does not touch it (it still failed after the fix, with 0 page errors).
- **What this phase changed:** the cash-basis test asserts `#factsheet-main h1` has count 1 and waits for it to be visible before the scan (founder "Fold it in", 2026-10-02). The `mark_to_market` sibling is unchanged because it already waits on a visible element (`BASIS · MARK-TO-MARKET` toBeVisible after the toggle click). Whether the CI flake is gone is read from `e2e-seeded` on the pushed head.
