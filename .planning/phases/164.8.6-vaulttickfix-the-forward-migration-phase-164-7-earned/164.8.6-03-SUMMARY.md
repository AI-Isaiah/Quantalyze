## Decisions Made

### 1. The roster is spelled out, not ranged (Deviation 1)

`(A-L,M1,M2,S1)` is the form the plan asked for and the form a reader would prefer. It is also the form the shipped CI parser scores as 4. Taking the parser's form is not a compromise — a roster exists so that removing an arm has to be written down twice, and a roster the checker mis-counts pins nothing at all.

### 2. M2 is one arm with its read and its assertion in different places (Deviation 2)

Splitting a probe from its assertion looks like a smell until the alternative is spelled out: the row lives only inside a subtransaction that is about to be rolled back on purpose, and asserting where the row exists would steal the enclosing arm's identity. The plpgsql local is the bridge, and arm L already documents that bridge for `v_ret_l`. Both halves carry a ⛔ block saying why they are where they are.

### 3. `cron_runs` moves with the function in arm J (Deviation 3)

The alternative — `GRANT INSERT ON public.cron_runs TO <drift role>` — does not work: fixture 33 enables RLS with no policies, so a non-owner with INSERT is still refused, and the arm would have kept dying on a raw error. Ownership is what exempts, which is the same reasoning the four existing tables carry.

### 4. `ci.yml`'s own `ARMS_FLOOR=195` was NOT raised

`arms_declared` is now **203** (measured), comfortably over the floor, so nothing is red.
