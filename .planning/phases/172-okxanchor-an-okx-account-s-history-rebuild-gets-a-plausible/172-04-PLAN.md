<objective>
Wire plan 01's opening check into the forced OKX anchor, as a second opinion behind the existing 5x trigger, with the
tolerance the founder chose from a4's reading in plan 03.

D-01 item 2 verbatim from CONTEXT: "If the cause is money held before the 89-day window, the implied opening balance
(`anchor - replay_final`) is checked against an independent source (OKX bill balances); the rebuild anchors when they
agree within a stated tolerance and refuses `anchor_skipped_implausible` when they do not."
Carried, verbatim: "UPNLVENUES 167.2.1.1 PD-H: a forced rebuild writes only a curve anchored to today's venue equity
including open profit; otherwise it fails permanent with a named reason and writes nothing. PD-I: no automatic
re-crawl." and "The forced-refusal vocabulary is a closed, pinned set."

CONTEXT discretion, answered here explicitly: the rule applies to the FORCED path only. The unforced path keeps its
unanchored zero-cash curve: its OKX window is 730 days, so the terminus arm fires before the 5x arm is ever evaluated, the
bills archive reaches three months and cannot prove an opening two years back, and PD-B keeps the unforced anchor
advisory. A test pins that the unforced path makes no bills call even when the bills would agree. The anchor stays the
live `totalEq` read; NAV marks are not used (CONTEXT discretion; the NAV-mark comparison is a Deferred Idea).

Shape (RESEARCH "Anti-Patterns": keep the 5x gate as the cheap trigger, the oracle as the second opinion; no new token):
inside `_fetch_and_price_window`'s anchor block, the `elif _implausible_anchor:` arm refuses only when
`_okx_forced_opening_agrees(...)` is False. That helper returns False at once unless `require_anchor` and the venue is
OKX, so every other caller is byte-for-byte on the old behaviour. On agreement the existing anchoring `else` branch runs
unchanged and sets `anchor_opening_agreed`.

Out of scope, recorded: a replay ending at or below zero is not gated by the 5x arm today (RESEARCH Open Question 2,
Pitfall 5). This plan pins that behaviour with a test and plan 05 books the follow-up; it does not change it.

One departure from RESEARCH Open Question 4, recorded: the check reads its OWN balance row (one extra
`GET /api/v5/account/balance`, only when the 5x gate fires on the forced OKX path) instead of sharing the anchor's
response. `fetch_okx_total_equity_and_upl_usd` is advisory and swallows every exception, while the check must let a venue
error propagate (Pitfall 3), so sharing it would mean changing an advisory reader every OKX anchor uses. The check takes
only `cashBal` of currencies with no bill since the opening day from that row, which only trading in the seconds between
the two reads could move; the USD floor and the tolerance absorb that.

Purpose: SC-2. Output: the rule, its telemetry and audit flag, and its pins. Nothing is run against TEST or PROD.
