<objective>
Extend the TRADEPAGES read-only probe so that, for an OKX member refused `anchor_skipped_implausible`, it measures the
refusal's cause and the bills oracle's verdict inputs, within the probe's existing redaction contract. This is the
instrument plan 03 runs on a4 (SC-1).

D-01 item 1 verbatim from CONTEXT: "A read-only diagnostic run on a4 on the backfill worker (probe pattern, counts,
booleans and 3-significant-figure ratios only: `totalEq / replay_final`, replay sign, non-stable share, `upl / totalEq`,
flip-from-flat closes, negative-quantity assets, in-window flows, skipped symbols) measures the cause (SC-1)."

How (RESEARCH Pattern 3, "Recommended mechanics", with plan 01's oracle): the forced read is unchanged and still
refuses. Only then, in `_probe_member`'s refusal branch, a SECOND, advisory pass of the rebuild's own read
(`er._fetch_and_price_window` without `require_anchor`) recovers the unanchored replay (its last value is
`replay_final`, its first row's day is the opening day), one raw balance row is read through
`obal.read_okx_balance_row`, and `obal.read_okx_opening` reads the bills oracle at that opening day. The probe prints
ratios against the anchor, never the anchor itself. Agreement is NOT decided here: the tolerance does not exist until
plan 03 has this reading (CONTEXT: "Tolerance value, chosen from the measurement"), so the probe reports
`opening_check` as `measured` with the residual, or `unavailable` with its reason.

Why not change `_read_member`: it carries Phase 173's ordering read (`_busiest_inst_type`, `okx_newest_first`), which runs
only after a successful forced read; the planning brief requires that plumbing intact. Once plan 04 lets a4 anchor, that
read happens on its own.

Purpose: SC-1. Output: the extended probe and its tests. Nothing is run against TEST or PROD here.
</objective>
