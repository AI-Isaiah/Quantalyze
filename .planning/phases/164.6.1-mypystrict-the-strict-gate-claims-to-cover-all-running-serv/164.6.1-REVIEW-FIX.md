## Fixed Issues

### CR-01: A folded YAML continuation line hides `|| true` or `--exclude=` on the gate command

**Files modified:** `src/__tests__/contracts/ci-mypy-strict-surface.contract.test.ts`
**Commit:** `83ca045b0`
**Applied fix:** `stepSwitchProblems` now reports any content line in the gate step that is indented deeper than the step's keys. YAML folds such a line into the `run:` value, where the single-line extractor cannot see it. Legs: (w) `|| true` and (w2) `--exclude=services/ingestion/`.

### WR-01: Quoted or spaced YAML keys switch the gate step off with every pin green

**Files modified:** `src/__tests__/contracts/ci-mypy-strict-surface.contract.test.ts`
**Commit:** `069f863c0`
**Applied fix:** the named-bad-key reader `gateStepKeys` is replaced by a POSITIVE rule. After `- name:`, every content line at the key indent must be a literal `run: ...` line, and there must be exactly one of them. Job-level and workflow-level keys are now read through `yamlKeyOf`, which strips quotes and the space before the colon. `keyBlock` also returns every block of a duplicated key, and the `shell` regex accepts a quoted key. Legs: (k2) `"if": false`, (k3) `if : false`, (k4) a second `run:`, (l2) a quoted job-level `continue-on-error`, (l3) a spaced one.
**Interpretation:** the disposition says a job-level key normalising to `continue-on-error` **or `defaults`** "is a problem regardless of its value". The real `python` job carries `defaults: run: working-directory:`, so refusing `defaults` outright would redden the real tree. The fix therefore applies the "any value" rule to `continue-on-error`. For `defaults`, it keeps the existing `shell:` check, but now finds the key in any quoting or spacing.
