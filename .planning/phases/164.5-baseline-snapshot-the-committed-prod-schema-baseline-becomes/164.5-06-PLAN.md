`supabase db push`, `supabase db reset`, `--project-ref` or `--db-url` from this directory. The
DROP reaches PROD by MERGE, after the founder's gate, never by a CLI push from an agent.

**Why no `IF EXISTS` and no `CASCADE` (D-01):** `IF EXISTS` silently no-ops on a signature
mismatch — a vacuous pass — and `CASCADE` silently removes dependents. Both convert a loud failure
into a quiet wrong outcome.

aborted because the measurement was unavailable. It is NEVER rendered as "zero calls."
