## ⚠️ P-01 — criterion 5's two halves do not agree, and this plan resolves it explicitly

The criterion says "applied to TEST **or** dispositioned by name; VAC-08 reports zero unledgered
migrations on its next credentialed run." Those two clauses cannot both be satisfied by the
disposition route, because the gate STRIPS comments before comparing:

- `new drift = measured-missing MINUS baselined` -> dispositioning makes this **0**.
- The gate's own diagnostic still reads `ledger presence: 31 absent, all 31 baselined …; 0 NEW drift`.

So dispositioning yields **zero NEW/undispositioned drift**, NOT **zero unledgered migrations**.
Only applying all 31 to shared TEST would yield the latter.

**Decision: take the DISPOSITION path, and pin the criterion's wording to what the tool prints.**

Reasoning, stated so it is not re-litigated:

1. Applying the 31 is **explicitly forbidden to an agent**. TODOS `[VAC08-LEDGER-32]`: *"⛔ Do NOT
   hand-apply the remaining 31 to TEST to shorten the list — TEST is shared with other people's
   CI; that stays a founder decision, not an agent one."*
2. The ROADMAP's own criterion permits it: "applied to TEST **or** dispositioned by name."
3. TODOS.md records the exact misreading this guards against: grepping a CI log for the token
   `VAC-08` matches only the prefixed summary lines and reads as "clean" — *"grepping a CI log by a
   token tests the token, not the gate."* Leaving the criterion phrased as "zero unledgered
   migrations" would institutionalise that misreading as a passing condition.

⚠️ **Inherit the caveat, and do not let prose slide past it: LEDGER ABSENCE IS NOT OBJECT
ABSENCE.** These migrations have no `schema_migrations` row; whether their objects exist in TEST
(hand-applied, or installed by a later migration) is a different question this gate does not
answer. Nothing written here may say "TEST is missing 31 features."

⭐ The precedent that a real shrink is possible is instructive and must be cited in the file:
`20260823120000_revoke_api_keys_insert`'s census guard demanded POSITIVE evidence of which
database it was on, TEST supplied the non-PROD signature, and its four structural post-verifies
passed on TEST exactly as on PROD. "May never be applicable" was a guess about a guard nobody had
run.
</objective>
