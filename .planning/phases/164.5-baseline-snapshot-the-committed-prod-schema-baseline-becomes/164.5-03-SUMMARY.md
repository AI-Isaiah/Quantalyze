### One decision beyond the plan's letter, and why

`sql-body-normalize.mjs` documents `SNAPSHOT_ONLY` as *advisory*. **A status that is merely
advisory to a GATE is a status the gate is blind to**, which is this repository's named defect
class, so it is a finding here. It is ZERO on today's tree, so the strictness cost nothing and
bought the claim in the header that **all five statuses are dispositioned and none is silently
ignored**. Self-test arm 7 builds a two-overload fixture that actually produces the status and
asserts `counts.SNAPSHOT_ONLY === 1` before asserting it fails — so the arm cannot pass
vacuously on a fixture that never produced the status.
