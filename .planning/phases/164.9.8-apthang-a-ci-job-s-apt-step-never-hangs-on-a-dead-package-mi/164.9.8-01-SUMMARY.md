# Phase 164.9.8 Plan 01: ci-apt.sh wrapper and the sql-tests psql tracer Summary

One bash wrapper bounds every apt network phase by wall clock (update 150 s, fetch 120 s) inside a 400 s retry budget, skips apt when the tool is already on PATH, and ends as a named MEASURE_FAIL instead of a hang; ci.yml's sql-tests psql install now goes through it with an 8-minute step limit.

## Commits

- `abaa493a6` feat(164.9.8-01): tracer, sql-tests psql install goes through scripts/ci-apt.sh (script with a stub self-test, ci.yml edit)
- `ea1ee8904` test(164.9.8-01): ci-apt.sh --self-test proves cap, budget, retry, loud failure and skip

## What was built

- `scripts/ci-apt.sh` (mode 755): `install [--provides CMD] [--budget S] [--update-timeout S] [--fetch-timeout S] PKG...` and `--self-test`. Defaults 400 / 150 / 120, kill-after 10, retry sleep 3, minimum-phase floor 10 (the last three overridable only through `APT_KILL_AFTER`, `APT_RETRY_SLEEP`, `APT_MIN_PHASE`).
- The three Acquire options live in one array (`APT_NET_OPTS`). No signature-weakening option exists (`grep -Eic 'allow-unauthenticated|AllowInsecureRepositories'` prints 0).
- The wall-clock prefix is in one helper (`net_phase`): `sudo <resolved timeout> --kill-after=.. <cap> apt-get <opts> ...`. The unpack (`--no-download`) is unwrapped.
- ci.yml `sql-tests` > `Install psql client` (the step at the sql-tests job, not the `test-db-drift` twin): `timeout-minutes: 8` inserted directly after `- name:`, the two raw apt lines replaced by `bash scripts/ci-apt.sh install --provides psql postgresql-client`, `set -euo pipefail` and `psql --version` added. No step added, removed or reordered.
