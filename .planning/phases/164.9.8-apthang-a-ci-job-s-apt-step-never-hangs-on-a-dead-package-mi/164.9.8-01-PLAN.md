<objective>
Build the one apt entry point the whole phase routes through and prove it end-to-end on ONE workflow site (the tracer), per the D-02 correction dated 2026-10-07 in 164.9.8-CONTEXT.md.

Purpose: the 2026-10-07 hangs survived the runner image's own 15 s / 1-retry apt settings because apt's timers are inactivity timers and a trickling fetch never trips them (RESEARCH Findings 2). What ends the hang is three things: a wall-clock cap per network phase, a bounded retry in a fresh apt process, and skipping apt entirely where the image already ships the tool (D-04). This plan creates that script and wires it into ci.yml's `sql-tests` `Install psql client` step with its own step time limit (D-03).

Output: `scripts/ci-apt.sh` (with `--self-test`), one converted ci.yml step.
</objective>

## Measured basis for the caps and the budget (planner revision, 2026-10-07)

Source: GitHub jobs API over the last 600 ci.yml runs (2026-09-11 to 2026-10-07), read-only, plus the logs of the 53 slowest successful apt steps. Details are in RESEARCH "Planning addendum".
- Successful `Install psql client` steps: n=1920, median 10 s, p99 39 s, max 91 s. Successful `Provision pg_cron` steps: n=525, max 41 s.
- Slowest successful UPDATE phase (Mirrorlist line to `Fetched`): 37.7 s. Slowest successful FETCH phase: 69.2 s (job 112943975486, two `Ign:` on azure at about 31 s each, then archive.ubuntu.com). In the stalled e2e-seeded run, the InRelease failover reached archive.ubuntu.com at +65 s.
- Slowest working throughput observed: a Playwright apt fetch of 32.1 MB took 4 min 47 s at 112 kB/s and succeeded (job 110490371136). At that rate the update's 10-12.6 MB of indices takes about 115 s.
- So: update cap 150 s (above both 65 s and about 115 s), fetch cap 120 s (1.7 times 69.2 s; the psql and pg_cron archives are 11.6 kB and 107 kB, so failover drives this phase, not throughput), kill-after 10 s, retry sleep 3 s.
- Total budget: default 400 s. Worst-case wall clock is the budget plus kill-after plus one sleep plus the unpack, about 430 s for a psql site, under the 8-minute step limit. Plan 02 gives pg_cron `--budget 360` and a 7-minute step limit. The sql-mutation arithmetic is in plan 02.
- Not covered, and accepted: a full dead-primary failover across every index file has never been observed succeeding in this sample. If one happens, it runs past the update cap, is retried inside the budget, and ends as a named MEASURE_FAIL, never as a hang.
</context>

    (e) Retry loop bounded by the budget. Record the start time with bash `SECONDS`. Each phase's cap is the smaller of its own cap and the remaining budget. Before starting a phase, if less than 10 s of budget remains, stop retrying.
    - While the lists are not yet updated, run the update phase (`update -y`); success marks the lists updated, and they are never re-fetched.
    - Then run the fetch phase (`install -y --no-install-recommends --download-only` with the packages).

    (f) When the budget is spent, print `::error::MEASURE_FAIL: apt could not fetch '<packages>' within the <budget> s budget (update cap <U> s, fetch cap <D> s). A dead or trickling package mirror, not a repo defect.` and exit 1.
