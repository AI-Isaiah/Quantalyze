- **Signature verification:** unchanged and fail-fast (T-164.9.8-01).

## Unregistered flags

`## Threat Flags` in the 01, 02, 03 and 04 SUMMARY files all say "None". Plan 05 has no SUMMARY. My own findings, none of them blocking:

- **U-1 (low): a carriage return in apt output survives into the annotation line.** `failure_detail` and `update_warning` quote apt lines with `cut` and never strip `\r`. Reproduced with a fixture: an `E: Failed to fetch ...` line containing `\r::error::...` comes out of `failure_detail` with the `\r` intact, and the wrapper then `echo`es it after its own `::error::` or `::warning::` prefix. If the runner treats a lone `\r` as a line break, a hostile or intercepted plain-HTTP mirror could emit a workflow command (spoofed annotation, `add-mask`). Whether the runner splits on `\r` I did not measure. Net new exposure is small: `run_capture` already tees the raw apt stream into the same log, and the runner has had `set-env` and `add-path` disabled since 2020. Suggested hardening, not applied (read-only audit): `tr -d '\r'` in `failure_detail` and `update_warning`.
- **U-2 (low): the guard's check for signature-weakening options is narrower than its description.** `r4Static` (`ci-apt-bounded.contract.test.ts:277`) matches only `allow-unauthenticated` and `AllowInsecureRepositories`. The CLI spelling `--allow-insecure-repositories`, `--force-yes`, `[trusted=yes]` and `Acquire::Check-Valid-Until=false` would pass R4. The wrapper and workflows are clean today, so T-164.9.8-01 holds; this is a future-edit gap.
- **U-3 (info): the wrapper self-test is timing-sensitive on a loaded macOS host.** At this sha it passed once, then failed or hung in 3 of 5 further standalone runs and timed out at the contract test's 55 s spawn limit once (scenarios with 1 to 3 s budgets, for example `unclassified-exit-100-no-mirror-blame` reported "not retried", and Homebrew `timeout` rejected an argument in one run). This is not a security gap, but it means the "self-test exits 0" evidence behind T-164.9.8-03 and T-164.9.8-04 is load-sensitive. No Linux CI run exists at this sha to compare; the REVIEW file recorded 11.7 s and green on the authoring run. Watch the first PR run.
