Purpose:
- Without a share floor, a run that certified one day of two hundred reads green.
- Without a tiny-base reading, the timed-NaN rule from 226-04 turns a legitimately left-out tiny-base day into a false RED. RESEARCH finding 2: production NaNs a day whose base is under 5% of the previous-30-day peak, and the soak models none of it.

Decision traceability, by CONTEXT label:
- "Minimum checked share": `ok` additionally requires `checked / (checked + timed + guarded)` at or above a named constant; the planner picks the value with a measured rationale; timed days that pass the exact cohort compare count as checked if judged sound, with the reasoning recorded.
- "Timed-day check": NaN must be explained by the soak's own guard reading.
- "Independence rule".
- "PROOF-01".

The share decisions, recorded:
- **Value 0.5.**
  - No live soak record exists to calibrate against: `docs/evidence` holds none (RESEARCH, checked).
  - The number is therefore set from fixture shares, not measured on a live account. The timed fixture is 2/3 and must stay green. A young account with 3 funding-guarded days then 4 measured days is 4/7 = 57% and must stay green. The TODO's case is 1/200 and must red.
  - 0.5 means a strict majority of the days that could have been certified were.
  - It is one reversible named constant. Moving it is a one-line change with no data effect.
- **Timed days that pass the exact cohort compare count as certified.** The cohort walk is an independent exact recomputation of the same quantity the untimed check recomputes. A timed day whose walk value is NaN certifies nothing, so it stays in the denominator only.
- **Quiet tiny-base days stay in `flat`, outside the denominator.** They carry no ledger information, exactly like any flat day, so a withdrawn account's quiet stretch cannot drag the share. Active tiny-base days are `guarded` and do count.
- **Vacuity is unchanged** (RESEARCH OQ3): `vacuous` stays `checked == 0`. The cost, stated in the docstring: an account whose every active day is a flow day stays not green.
