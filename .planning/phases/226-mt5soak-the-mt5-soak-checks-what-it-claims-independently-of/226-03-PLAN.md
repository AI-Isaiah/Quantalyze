**Soak-owned names (prefix rule, pinned by the 226-02 AST test).** Every function that must stay independent of the code under test is a module-level def named `_walk_*`. Soak-owned constants are `_WALK_*` and soak-owned types are `_Walk*`. From the soak module, a `_walk_*` function may reference only other `_walk_*` / `_WALK_*` / `_Walk*` names, builtins, and the stdlib modules `math`, `bisect`, `datetime` and `fractions`. It must never reference a name the soak imports from `services.*`.

**Types (created in 226-02, extended in 226-03).**
- `_WalkLedger`: a frozen dataclass. From 226-02 its fields are `pnl_by_day: dict[str, float]`, `flows_by_day: dict[str, float]`, `total_trading_pnl: float` and `total_flows: float`. 226-03 adds `timing: dict[str, _WalkDayTiming]`, defaulting to an empty dict.
- `_WalkDayTiming` (226-03): a NamedTuple with two fields.
  - `flows: tuple[tuple[int, float], ...]` holds the UTC instant in ms and the amount, ascending by instant, stable.
  - `pnl: tuple[tuple[int | None, float], ...]` holds the open instant in ms (None when carried overnight) and the cash, in deal order.

**Which P&L feeds which reading (from 226-02 on).**
- Level roll (`_forward_terminal_nav`): the FOLD's per-day P&L plus the walk's flows, with `initial = balance - walk.total_trading_pnl - walk.total_flows`.
- Published-return reading (`_reconcile_published_returns`): the walk's P&L and flows. From 226-03 also `timed_days = set(walk.timing)`; from 226-04 also `timing = walk.timing`.
- Attribution compare (`_walk_attribution_check`): the walk against the fold, day by day.
