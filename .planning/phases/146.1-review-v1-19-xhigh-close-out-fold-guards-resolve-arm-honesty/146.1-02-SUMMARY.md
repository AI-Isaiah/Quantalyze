## Decisions Made

1. **Set algebra instead of membership tests in the totality assertion.** The natural spelling `k in NO_LIMITER_QUARANTINE` would have tripped the plan's own anti-containment grep (`grep -qE '... in NO_LIMITER_QUARANTINE'`). Rewriting as `derived & limited & QUARANTINE` / `derived - limited - QUARANTINE` satisfies the grep *and* is genuinely better: it cannot be satisfied by an accident of iteration order and it surfaces the both-sets case explicitly.
2. **The docstring avoids the literal tokens `default_limits` and `SlowAPIMiddleware`.** The plan's `<verify>` greps *fail* on their presence anywhere in the file, so the "these were considered and rejected" prose is written descriptively ("no framework-wide fallback ceiling", "no slowapi request middleware"). The reasoning is preserved; only the exact tokens are avoided.
3. **`_import_app()` is called inside `_limited_keys()` too**, not just in the route walk. This makes every helper self-sufficient w.r.t. decoration-time population, so no test in the file depends on collection or execution order.
4. **Pinned `len(_dynamic_route_limits[key]) == 2`, not just key presence.** Dropping one of a pair of callable limits reads as a no-op in review and would leave the key — and therefore the partition — untouched.
