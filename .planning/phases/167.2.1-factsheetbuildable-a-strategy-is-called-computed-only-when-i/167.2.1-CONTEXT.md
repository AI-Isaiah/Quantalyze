<domain>
## Phase Boundary

**One claim: a surface that says "this strategy has a factsheet" agrees with what the share-link
recipient actually sees.**

1. **`/strategies` (S5 share note, 167.2 review WR-02).** A row whose analytics status is computed,
   but whose factsheet cannot build, shows no note today. Its recipient lands on the "not available"
   card. After this phase the list decides buildability with the builder's own code and shows a note
   that says what the recipient sees.
2. **The owner factsheet's pending panel (S7).** The same row shows the same note there, so the two
   owner surfaces cannot disagree about it.
3. **The key-card Delete warning (167.2 review R2-L4(b), ROADMAP criterion 5).** The composite
   membership read moves server-side, so an RLS policy that filters the read to an empty list,
   without an error, cannot hide the warning.

**Out of scope:** the share page itself (it is the ground truth and does not change); the public
cached lane's staleness (Phase 148 TODO, D-11); the discovery detail page's second assembly of the
builder (D-03, booked to Phase 169); scalar KPIs on discovery or my-strategies versus the factsheet
(Phase 169 SC4); the wizard's in-page preview (RESEARCH A5); any migration (D-01, D-04); any change
to what the analytics worker writes.

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

- **D-01 (2026-09-25, orchestrator, OQ1): criterion 5 is closed by a server route, not a migration.**
  A new `GET /api/keys/[id]/memberships`, wrapped in `withAuth`, proves ownership with an explicit
  `api_keys.user_id = <session user>` equality on the service-role client. It then reads
  `strategy_keys` joined to `strategies ( name, status )` on that same client, and answers
  `{ memberships: [{ name, status }] }`. `ApiKeyManager`'s `readKeyCompositeMemberships` fetches this
  route instead of reading `strategy_keys` in the browser. It treats any non-2xx answer, a body that
  is not JSON, or a missing or non-array `memberships` as `"unreadable"`, which renders
  KCS-DELETE-UNCHECKED.
  **Why:** both designs make the read immune to an RLS regression. The route needs no migration.
  So it avoids the three-reviewer gate, the SQL-census churn, and the auto-apply to PROD.
  The SECURITY DEFINER RPC `get_api_key_composite_memberships` specified in
  RESEARCH § Migration fallback stays the **recorded fallback** if the founder later wants the
  guard at the database layer.
  **ROADMAP wording:** criterion 5's "(a migration)" is superseded. It was written on the
  reviewer's assumption. A dated note records this in the ROADMAP `### Phase 167.2.1` entry, as
  the deviation policy requires (CONTEXT **and** ROADMAP).
  — **Reversibility:** reversible. One new route and one client function, no schema.

- **D-02 (2026-09-25, orchestrator, OQ2): a computed-but-unbuildable row gets its own precise note.
  It never gets KCS12-MINT-B verbatim.** MINT-B's tail, "The numbers appear there once a computation
  succeeds", is false for this row, because its computation DID succeed. The new rows join the
  curated KCS-12 family in `src/lib/status-surface-copy.ts`. Each is pinned by a hand-typed oracle in
  `src/lib/status-surface-copy.test.ts`, as every KCS-12 row is. The voice is DESIGN.md
  § Voice / microcopy: declarative, active, and the limitation stated with its threshold attached.

  | ID | Share mode | Condition | Copy |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | `KCS12-UNBUILDABLE-SHORT` | `mint-token` | a single-key row that is computed but has fewer than 2 distinct dated returns, AND the recipient arm is `not_available` | `Right now, a private link to this strategy shows that its factsheet is not available. Its last computation succeeded with fewer than 2 days of returns, and a factsheet needs at least 2.` |
  | `KCS12-UNBUILDABLE-COMPOSITE` | `mint-token` | a composite row that is computed but whose factsheet cannot build, AND the recipient arm is `not_available` | `Right now, a private link to this strategy shows that its factsheet is not available. Its last computation succeeded, but its results cannot be built into a factsheet. Contact support@quantalyze.com to have this composite checked.` |
  | `KCS12-PUBLIC-UNBUILDABLE-SHORT` | `public-url` | a published single-key row, computed, fewer than 2 distinct dated returns | `Right now, this strategy's factsheet link shows that the factsheet is not available. Its last computation succeeded with fewer than 2 days of returns, and a factsheet needs at least 2.` |
  | `KCS12-PUBLIC-UNBUILDABLE-COMPOSITE` | `public-url` | a published composite row, computed, factsheet cannot build | `Right now, this strategy's factsheet link shows that the factsheet is not available. Its last computation succeeded, but its results cannot be built into a factsheet. Contact support@quantalyze.com to have this composite checked.` |

  **Selection rule:** for an unbuildable row, a `public-url` row takes its PUBLIC-UNBUILDABLE line
  and needs no RPC, as 167.2 IN-04 already rules for published rows. A `mint-token` row reads its
  recipient arm with the existing `readRecipientArm`. Arm `not_available` takes the UNBUILDABLE line,
  because that is exactly when the share page shows its "not available" card. Arm `in_progress`
  keeps KCS12-MINT-A, because the recipient really does see "being prepared" while a recompute runs.
  Arm `unreadable` keeps KCS12-UNREADABLE.
  **The "2"** is `MIN_FACTSHEET_SERIES_POINTS`. A test pins the copy to the constant, so the sentence
  cannot drift from the gate. "yet" is dropped on purpose: waiting does not change this row.
  **The composite line names support** because the probe cannot tell a missing headline from a
  failed series read (D-07). "Support" is the remedy that is true in both cases, following the
  KCS-SHAPE-UNKNOWN precedent.
  — **Reversibility:** reversible. Copy only.

- **D-03 (2026-09-25, orchestrator, OQ3): the discovery detail page's second assembly of the
  builder is out of scope.** `src/app/(dashboard)/discovery/[slug]/[strategyId]/page.tsx` calls
  `resolveDailyReturnSeries`, `readCompositeFactsheet`, `readSingleKeyBasisOpts` and
  `buildFactsheetPayload` itself, instead of calling `fetchAndBuildPayload`. That is a drift risk,
  not a false "has a factsheet" claim: it serves published rows only and renders the honest KCS-10
  fallback. It is recorded as a `TODOS.md` drift-risk entry routed to **Phase 169 PAGETRUTH**, which
  owns factsheet KPI sourcing. Plan 04 writes the entry.
  — **Reversibility:** reversible. A backlog entry.

- **D-04 (research Pattern 1): buildability is decided at read time, on the server, by the builder's
  own code. Nothing is stored.** `fetchAndBuildPayload` is split into a module-private resolve stage
  (gates G0 to G3) and the build. Both it and a new exported `probeFactsheetBuildable(id, visibility)`
  call that stage. The G4 point-count predicate becomes the exported `hasBuildableSeries`, and
  `buildFactsheetPayload` itself calls it.
  A stored flag written by the Python job is rejected. It would re-implement the TypeScript gates
  in a second language, it would need a migration, and it could not be backfilled honestly.
  This satisfies ROADMAP criterion 2 ("or decided server-side for the list") and makes criterion 4
  vacuous: there is no migration.
  — **Reversibility:** reversible. A refactor inside one module, and the builder's output does not change.

- **D-05: a probe that errors is never read as "buildable".** A thrown probe, or a `read_error` or
  `not_visible` result, renders `recipientShareNote(mode, "unreadable")`. It is logged, and captured
  with tags only (`stage: "factsheet-probe"`). A `not_computed` result (the list's RLS embed and the
  admin read disagree, a race) takes the existing uncomputed path.
  — **Reversibility:** reversible.

- **D-06 (planner, 2026-09-25): the owner factsheet's S7 note uses the same derivation as the list.**
  The owner lane's pending panel renders `recipientShareNote("mint-token", …)`. For a computed but
  unbuildable row, that is MINT-B with the false tail. If the list moved to the D-02 line and the
  owner lane did not, the phase would create a new disagreement between two owner surfaces. On its
  null-payload path the owner lane therefore runs the same probe and the same selection rule.
  This is plan 04. The orchestrator's three-plan split did not include it, because the research
  assumed MINT-B would be reused verbatim, and D-02 overrides that assumption.
  — **Reversibility:** reversible.

- **D-07: every composite unbuildable case maps to one reason, `composite_unbuildable`.**
  `readCompositeFactsheet` folds a `csv_daily_returns` read error into an empty series and a missing
  headline into `null`, so the probe cannot tell an outage from missing data. Splitting them would
  mean editing `composite-read-path.ts`, which Phase 169 plan 04 edits. The single-key cases (G3
  empty and G4 below the minimum) map to `too_few_points`. They involve no read, so they are data
  facts.
  — **Reversibility:** reversible.

- **D-08: the list probes only its computed rows, with `Promise.all`, uncached** (RESEARCH A3 and
  Pitfall 7). Uncomputed rows are never probed. The probe is never routed through
  `buildFactsheetPayloadCached`.
  — **Reversibility:** reversible. Bound the concurrency if a list is measured to be large.

- **D-09: merge order with in-flight phases.** Phase 169 plans 04 and 05 edit `build-payload.ts`,
  `fetch-and-build-payload.ts`, `types.ts` and `composite-read-path.ts`. Phase 167.1.2 plan 04 edits
  `ApiKeyManager.tsx` and its test. **Whichever merges second rebases.** Each plan's first task
  re-reads its files at HEAD and re-resolves every symbol before editing. Hunks stay minimal, and no
  plan reformats untouched code.
  — **Reversibility:** n/a (process).

- **D-10: the Delete TOCTOU residual is accepted, not fixed.** A membership added between the read
  and the Delete still cascades without a warning. `api_keys_published_composite_delete_guard` still
  refuses the delete for a PUBLISHED composite. Closing the window needs a delete RPC that compares
  an acknowledged membership count, which is a migration. The founder's 2026-09-24 decision is
  "warn, never block".
  — **Reversibility:** reversible.

- **D-11: known limit, the public cached lane lags the probe** (RESEARCH Pitfall 4). A published
  row's public URL is served through `unstable_cache` with an id-only key and a 3600 s TTL. The
  probe answers for a fresh build now. For up to an hour the public page can therefore still serve an
  older payload or a cached `null`. That cache is the Phase 148 TODO and is not touched here.

- **D-12: no VERSION or CHANGELOG edit in any plan.** Both go in the ship commit, under the CLAUDE.md
  CHANGELOG mechanism. The bump is patch-level in meaning: a user-facing truth fix.

### Claude's Discretion
