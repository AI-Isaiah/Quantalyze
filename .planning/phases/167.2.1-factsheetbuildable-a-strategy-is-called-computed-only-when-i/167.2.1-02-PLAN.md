no longer turn the Delete confirm's membership read into an error-free empty list that shows no
composite warning.

regressed policy answers `[]`, the confirm stays silent, and the delete cascades a composite's
member away.

card reading it with every malformed or failed answer mapped to the existing
KCS-DELETE-UNCHECKED warning.
