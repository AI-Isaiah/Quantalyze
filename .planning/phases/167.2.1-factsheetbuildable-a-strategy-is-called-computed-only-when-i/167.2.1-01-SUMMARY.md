This is a raise, not a lowering: the floor only rises. The contract test's own RED-then-GREEN is the neuter evidence.
- **Files modified:** `scripts/live-db-lane-corpus.mjs` (not in the plan's `files_modified`)
- **Commit:** `a961cdd43`

**2. [Rule 1 - Plan text] The payload carries `strategyId`, not `id`**
- The plan's CONTROL arm says the payload's `id`. `FactsheetCommon` names the field `strategyId`, so every arm asserts `payload.strategyId`.

**3. [Rule 2 - Kept a log line's function] A NEW warn at G4 in the resolve stage**
- Before this plan, a single-key or composite series below the minimum reached `buildFactsheetPayload`, whose warn (`[buildFactsheetPayload] strategy series has fewer than 2 unique dated observations`) was the only log for that null. With G4 in the stage, the fetch path no longer calls the builder for that case. The stage therefore logs its own line: `[factsheet] fetchAndBuildPayload — return series has fewer than the minimum distinct dated observations; withholding the payload`. The builder's original warn is still there, unchanged, for its other caller (D-03). Every pre-existing log line keeps its message text.

**4. [Note] Log lines moved into the stage keep the text `fetchAndBuildPayload —`**
- The plan requires them verbatim, so they read the same when the probe triggers them. A reader of the logs cannot tell a probe's resolve from a build's resolve.

**5. [Note] The skip-reason advertisement is module-level**
- `advertiseLiveDbSkipReason` is called at module level, as `scenario-commit-rls.test.ts` does, rather than inside an always-passing `it(...)`. That avoids a test that cannot fail.

**6. [Note] The NaN parity row is "one finite point plus one NaN"**
- An all-NaN series is emptied by the shape normalizer and stopped at G3. The one-finite-plus-one-NaN row exercises G4, so neuter 1 can see it.
