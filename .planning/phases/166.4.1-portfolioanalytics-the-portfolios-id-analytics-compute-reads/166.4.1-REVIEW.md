- The AST test pins that the old `reindex(benchmark_rets.index)` join is gone.

### Correlation and TWR
- Correlation is `pairwise_correlation_or_none(aligned, b_aligned)` on the interval pair. The test oracle builds interval moves from price ratios on their own, so it cannot agree with the helper by construction.
- The weekday-only TWR oracle (`1.02**12 - 1`) would fail on the old inner join, which gives 0.0.
- Alpha spanning different windows is R-01. It was accepted and is not re-flagged here.
