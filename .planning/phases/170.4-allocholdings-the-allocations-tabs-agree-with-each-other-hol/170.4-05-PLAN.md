<objective>
Stop Scenario and the Diversification Optimizer asking an allocator to connect a key while keys are
connected (D-05, D-06), reading the one server-side predicate (D-07).

Purpose: on 2026-10-03 both surfaces showed "Connect Exchange" to an allocator with connected keys,
because Scenario keyed its front door on an empty book and the Optimizer on an empty `portfolios` list.
Third, D-08 ("One Key is One Strategy", CONTEXT): Scenario's live-holdings total and its
`holdingByRef` memo assume one holding per venue:symbol:type scope ref, so once plan 07 makes the payload
carry one row per key, a second key's row would overwrite the first in the map and drop out of the total.
This plan owns that fix because the memo lives in `ScenarioComposer.tsx`, the file its copy change edits.
Output: two key-aware empty states, the per-ref multi-row map, and their tests. Plan 03 threads `hasConnectedKeys` from the Holdings
tab into the Optimizer; until then the prop defaults to false, which is today's behaviour.
</objective>
