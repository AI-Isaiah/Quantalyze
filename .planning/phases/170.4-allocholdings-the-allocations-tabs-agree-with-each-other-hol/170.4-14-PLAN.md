  `getNetExposureSeries` and `getAllocationSeries` each call it and sum EVERY row per `asof`.
- `oneKeyPerAccountDay(rows, accountOf)` (src/lib/holdings-account-day.ts) keeps, per
  (account, holding_type), the rows of the key with the latest `updated_at` instant ON THE ACCOUNT'S
  NEWEST `asof` IN ITS INPUT, ties to the smallest lowercase `api_key_id`, and DROPS every row on an
  older day. That last property is why it must be called once per `asof` group here: called over the
  whole window it would erase all history but each account's newest day.
- The per-key reader (`fetchLatestHoldingsPerKey`, src/lib/latest-holdings-per-key.ts) learns which
  keys share an account by reading `api_keys` with the non-exported `KEY_IDENTITY_COLUMNS`
  (`id, exchange, venue_account_id, account_share_kind, account_shared_with_api_key_id`) under
  `.eq("user_id", userId).limit(HOLDINGS_ROW_CAP)`, failing on an error or on reaching the cap, then
  mapping them through `accountIdentityTokens` (src/lib/departed-history.ts) inline, with the fallback
  token `key:<id>` for a key the identity does not group. Its dollar callers (queries.ts
  `derivePhase07Fields`, the scenario commit route) apply `oneKeyPerAccountDay` with that identity.
