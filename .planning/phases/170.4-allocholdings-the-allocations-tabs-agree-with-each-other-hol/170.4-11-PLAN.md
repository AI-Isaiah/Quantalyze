SAME exchange account are one account. The rule is applied to dollar totals only (N2): the Scenario
fingerprint must keep the row set the commit RPC recomputes.
Output: the TS pure function, the Python pure function, one shared case file, the reader's account tokens,
and the refresh wired. Plans 07 (dashboard derive and fingerprint) and 12 (commit route, scorer) consume it.
