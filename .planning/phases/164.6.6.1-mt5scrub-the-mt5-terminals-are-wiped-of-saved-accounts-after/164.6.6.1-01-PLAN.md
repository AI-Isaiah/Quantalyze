<objective>
Build the one narrow verb every later plan relies on: terminate the terminal, refuse to delete unless
the terminate fully landed, then delete the saved-account database and, behind one int flag, the
per-account deal caches. Also build the two per-terminal registries the rest of the phase keys on:
relaunch debt and scrub owed.

Purpose: 164.6.6 D-03 reversed 164.6.5 D-07 for saved accounts and history. This is the only code
that acts on that reversal, and it must be unable to delete anything under a live terminal (A4),
unable to carry a credential, and unable to end a terminal without recording that it now owes a
relaunch (W-2, criterion 3).

Output: the verb, its contract tests (including an offline run of the committed body), the two
registries, and the runbook's posture paragraph for the new command class.

Spec-less probe: no requirement IDs are registered, so no edge probe ran. The edges here come from
the ROADMAP success criteria and the CONTEXT spike findings S-02, S-07 and S-09.
