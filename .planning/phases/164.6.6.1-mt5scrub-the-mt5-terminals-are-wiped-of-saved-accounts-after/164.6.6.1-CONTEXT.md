### MUST-FIX before any scrub ships (founder, 2026-09-27) — carried VERBATIM from the 164.6.6 plan-check round 2 report

Plan numbers inside the quotes use 164.6.6's ORIGINAL numbering: old 03 / 05 / 06 / 07 are now
164.6.6.1-01 / -02 / -03 / -04. The founder rated both HIGH if shipped; the checker had rated them
warnings. The re-plan must close each with a failing-first test, not by a note.

> **W-1. [key_links_planned] No scrub is sent on a connection whose probe thread may still be in flight. The site must be able to tell that exit apart by something other than the exception type.**
> - Plan: 06, Task 1
> - Evidence:
>   - Plan 06 skips the scrub on a probe timeout "because an abandoned probe thread may still be mid-call on the same rpyc connection".
>   - It sets `probe_timed_out` only on the outer `asyncio.TimeoutError` arm (`_MT5_VALIDATE_DEADLINE_S`, 75 s).
>   - In `routers/exchange.py::_validate_mt5_key_probe`, the inner probe-stage `wait_for` (`_MT5_VALIDATE_STAGE_TIMEOUT_S`, 60 s) catches its own `TimeoutError` inside `_connect_and_probe`. It re-raises it as a 424 `VenueTransientHTTPException`, the same shape the abandon arm and the mismatch arm raise.
>   - So on the stage-timeout path the nested finally sends the scrub while the `to_thread` probe is still running. About 15 s of the deadline is left at that point, so only the S-05-derived `_SCRUB_MIN_BUDGET_S` stands between that path and a concurrent send.
> - Example fix (non-binding): set a nonlocal "probe abandoned" flag on the stage-timeout arm inside `_connect_and_probe` and pass it as `probe_timed_out`. Add a behaviour bullet and a test for the stage-timeout exit.
>
> **W-2. [verification_derivation] The spike verdict that plans 05–07 branch on must require the relaunch time to fit the budgets those plans assume.**
> - Plans: 03 (Task 3), 06, 07
> - Evidence:
>   - Plan 03 S-03 has a timing clause for `bare` mode (the cold start must fit the `initialize` ceiling). The `credentialed` verdict rule has none.
>   - Plan 06 gates on `_SCRUB_MIN_BUDGET_S` (S-05, plus S-04 in credentialed mode) against the time left of a 75 s deadline (the worker gets `_MT5_PROBE_TIMEOUT_S`, which is 35 s).
>   - Plan 07 budgets the credentialed relaunch as one crossing of at most 30 s.
>   - The one relaunch measured so far, the recycle's, took 86 s from kill to `authorized` (comment near `_escalate_ipc_fault`'s budget table). That was a bare relaunch, so this is a risk rather than a contradiction.
>   - If the credentialed cold start is that slow:
>     - every wizard and worker scrub is `scrub_skipped_budget`, so criterion 2 on the validation terminal is never met, and only L2 would notice after deploy;
>     - every job-terminal `ipc_fault` scrub ends `relaunch_failed` after deleting the saved house login.
> - Example fix (non-binding): add a clause to plan 03 Task 3. `credentialed` (and `bare`) is recorded only when the S-05 scrub-plus-relaunch time fits the wizard remainder, the worker's 35 s and plan 07's single-crossing row; otherwise record `DISPROVED`, or a named budget finding that sends the phase back to planning. Plans 06 and 07 repeat that as a precondition.

⚠️ **What W-2 means for the re-plan, in the founder's terms:** no scrub path may delete the jobs
terminal's saved house login unless the spike has MEASURED a relaunch that fits the budget it runs
in; otherwise the jobs terminal is left logged out after a failed relaunch.
