terminal after every validation, the jobs terminal on the cadence D-04 sets — **without ever leaving
the jobs terminal logged out, and without a lock-up.**

measured how the scrub and the relaunch actually behave.

loud when it is unset, records job-terminal handovers, and closes H3), AND the founder's live scrub

## Founder-visible costs of the 2026-10-04 re-plan

Recorded at re-plan on 2026-10-04 under the CLAUDE.md deviation policy, and mirrored in the
ROADMAP's 164.6.6.1 entry. ⛔ **Shown to the founder at plan 02's Task 2 checkpoint, before any
code that deletes a saved login runs.** Each item is accepted there or named as rejected. A
rejection stops the phase and returns it to planning. The items are not numbered as decisions, and
none is decided by this list.

- **The queue-instead-of-fail rule from 164.6.6's D-29 is partly reversed** (plan 06). Suppose a
  wizard validation is already waiting on the validation lease when the one ahead of it finishes.
  It is refused with the recoverable 424 while the earlier validation's scrub runs. That refusal is
  a WARNING with no Sentry capture. A scrub that was genuinely missed stays ERROR with a capture.
- **A scrub holds the validation lease** (plans 05, 06). In the worst case it waits for the probe
  thread (`_SCRUB_PROBE_WAIT_S`, the wizard's rpyc bound plus 5 s, 60 s today) and then runs its
  own budget (`_VALIDATION_SCRUB_BUDGET_S`, 160 s today), so about 220 s. A wizard validation waits
  only 20 s for the lease (`_MT5_LEASE_WAIT_S`), so one that arrives during a scrub gets the
  existing `lease_busy` refusal. The typical scrub is far shorter: 2 s to kill and delete (S-05),
  2.8 to 3.4 s for the credentialed login (S-04, S-09), plus the launch time S-11 measures.
- **Validation now depends on the house credential** (plans 05, 06). If `MT5_LOGIN`,
  `MT5_PASSWORD` or `MT5_SERVER` is unset or wrong on the analytics service, every validation
  scrub ends skipped or unverified, and the scrub-owed mark stays set. Every wizard validation is
  then refused with the 424 until the credential is fixed. Each failure fires an ERROR alert.
- **The first validation after each analytics deploy is refused** (plan 06). The scrub-owed and
  relaunch-debt marks live in memory, so a restart loses them. The validation terminal is
  therefore marked owed at boot. The first wizard validation after a deploy is refused with the
  424 while a scrub runs. A worker validation runs that scrub inline first. Either way, the
  validation terminal is killed and relaunched once per deploy. Because no scrub is pending when
  that first wizard refusal happens, it is reported like a missed scrub: one ERROR with a Sentry
  capture per deploy (windowed by `_SCRUB_ALERT_WINDOW_S`).
- **164.6.5 D-08 is reversed for the relaunch only** (plan 03). The `ipc_fault` escalation now
  sends the house credential when it relaunches the jobs terminal. The terminate-and-delete verb
  itself still takes no credential.
- **164.6.2's rule of sending credentials only on `-6` is widened in two cases** (plan 04).
  - **D-09's boot case:** decided by the founder on 2026-10-04.
  - **The relaunch-debt case:** decided by the founder on 2026-10-04 as D-10. The planner added
    it and an earlier version of this bullet said it awaited confirmation. When this service has
    ended the jobs terminal and has not yet seen it house-verified, the next heal tick from any
    source, the session monitor included, sends one credentialed relaunch instead of the bare
    probe.
- **The jobs terminal's own relaunch is not measured before ship** (plans 03, 07). S-11 measures
  the validation terminal only, as the proxy. A slow jobs-terminal launch leaves it account-less
  until the next tick pays the relaunch debt. **Accepted by the founder on 2026-10-04 as D-11.**
  Plan 07's L-3 records the real jobs-terminal kill-to-authorized time.
- **`Mt5Client.recycle_terminal_process` is left with no production caller** (plan 03). Its
  constant stays byte-identical and pinned. Removing the verb is a separate, later decision.
- **The validation scrub keeps `logs`, `mail` and `subscriptions`** (plan 01). This is the
  planner's default under "Still OPEN" above, and the founder answers it at plan 07's Task 3.
- **The jobs terminal keeps its per-account `trades` caches** (plan 03) until 164.6.6.3 fixes
  Finding C. Owned by `TODOS.md` `MT5-JOB-TERMINAL-TRADES-SCRUB-01`. Whichever of the two phases
  merges SECOND makes the flip, judged at its own ship time against `origin/main`. Plan 08's pin
