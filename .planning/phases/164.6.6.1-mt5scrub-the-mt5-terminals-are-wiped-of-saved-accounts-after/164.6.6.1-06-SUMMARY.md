# Phase 164.6.6.1 Plan 06: the scrub wired into both validate sites Summary

**Both MT5 validate sites now schedule the validation-terminal scrub after their verdict, inside their own lease and after the release. Each passes the probe thread's own event, so no scrub crosses while an abandoned probe thread may still be logging in (W-1). A terminal still owed a scrub is paid for before the next probe: the wizard refuses with the existing recoverable 424 and schedules the scrub, and the worker runs it inline. `main.lifespan` marks the validation terminal owed at boot, so a restart cannot hide an account a validation left behind.**

## Accomplishments

- **Wizard (`_validate_mt5_key_probe`).** An outer `try/finally` around the existing park, release and `try/except/finally` block. If `login_attempted` is set, it calls `schedule_validation_terminal_scrub(host, port, site=SITE_VALIDATE_WIZARD, probe_thread_done=probe_thread_done)`.
  - It runs after `client.release` and still inside `async with mt5_terminal_lease(...)`.
  - The task is created and never awaited, because `asyncio.Lock` is not re-entrant.
- **Wizard owed gate.** It sits after the `lease_wait` emit and before the deadline starts. When `mt5_scrub_owed(terminal_key) or mt5_relaunch_debt(terminal_key)`, it does four things in order:
  1. `report_scrub_owed_refusal` first;
  2. then the schedule, with `probe_thread_done=None`;
  3. then `trace.outcome = "scrub_owed"`;
  4. then the existing `VenueTransientHTTPException(424, NETWORK_UNAVAILABLE, recoverable=True)`.

  Nothing is connected, logged in or probed. The comment covers:
  - decision 6, with the arithmetic (5 crossings at the 55 s interactive bound is 275 s);
  - the report-before-schedule reason;
  - the residue: while the scrub keeps failing, every wizard validation is refused;
  - the queued-B cost.
- **Worker (`Mt5Adapter.validate`).**
  - Before `_build_client`: `if (owed or debt) and not await run_owed_validation_scrub_in_lease(...)`, then `raise Mt5ClientError(0, "...owed a scrub that did not complete; retry later.")`.
  - The same outer `try/finally` schedule as the wizard, after `client.close`, inside the lease.
- **Boot mark.**
  - `mt5_terminal_scrub.mark_validation_terminal_owed_at_boot()` is synchronous and never raises. It has the shared never-raises shape (one `except Exception` whose body is one nested `try` with a `BaseException` fallback). It reads `read_env_validation_gateway_endpoint()`, returns on `None`, and otherwise calls `note_mt5_scrub_owed(...)` and logs one INFO line that carries no host, port or key.
  - `main.lifespan` imports it lazily beside the other `services.mt5_*` imports and calls it once, before the task list.
- **Timeout chain.** One dated line under "THE MT5 VALIDATE TIMEOUT CHAIN" says the 164.6.6.1 scrub adds no term to the 105 s worst case, and that an owed scrub refuses rather than running in-request. `tests/test_mt5_validate.py`'s chain assertions pass unchanged.
