## Open (blocking: severity >= high)

| Threat | Category | Sev | Mitigation expected | Found |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| T-15 | DoS: jobs terminal silently account-less | high | Debt branch ahead of the bare probe (present); D-09 boot branch (present); **ERROR on every unpaid tick (absent on one path)** | See below |

**T-15 evidence.**
- `_log_relaunch_debt_outstanding` (`analytics-service/services/mt5_relogin.py`, ~2083) is reached only from `_settle_relaunch_debt`, i.e. only after the heal WINS the terminal lease.
- The heal's acquire is bounded: `wait_s=_relogin_lease_wait_s()`, default 2.0 s (`_MT5_RELOGIN_LEASE_WAIT_DEFAULT_S`, ~620).
- The three job sites acquire with `wait_s=None`, and nothing in the job path reads `mt5_relaunch_debt`. The only readers are `routers/exchange.py:1052`, `services/ingestion/mt5.py:255` and `mt5_relogin.py`.
- When the acquire expires, the `Mt5TerminalBusyError` arm (`heal_mt5_terminal_session`, `mt5_relogin.py` ~2772-2806) logs **INFO** that a busy terminal "is itself evidence the session is fine". It then records `busy_skip` (`not_measured`) and returns. It never reads the debt and never logs ERROR.
- The discriminating check: a job that fails on an account-less terminal logs WARNING with a transient retry. See `services/job_worker.py` ~5116-5160: `logger.warning("derive_broker_dailies: mt5 read hit a transient/wrong-server client error ... retrying")` and `error_kind="transient"`. Nothing attributes the failure to the terminal or the debt.
- So the terminal can stay account-less with an outstanding debt, the heal reporting "fine" and the jobs retrying quietly. That is the silent shape T-15 names.
- The same gap was raised independently as WR-02 in `164.6.6.1-REVIEW.md` (MEDIUM, confidence 5-6) and was carried unfixed through re-review round 2. The review severity does not change this row's register severity of high.
- Residual that bounds it: the starvation needs a job backlog holding the lease through every tick. That is the S-03(b)/S-10 shape, where a bare `initialize()` on an account-less terminal blocks 25-30 s.

**Closure options.**
- (a) In the busy arm, read `mt5_relaunch_debt(mt5_terminal_key(host, port))` and log ERROR ("debt outstanding and the heal could not get the lease") instead of the INFO. Add one test. Optionally lengthen the heal's lease wait while a debt is owed.
- (b) The founder records this as an accepted risk in the log below. The founder has accepted similar costs before, with no clients yet.

- **Alerts and Sentry.** `_alert_scrub_failed` logs and captures only `cause` (closed set, else `unrecognised_cause`) and `site` (normalized). `sentry_init.py:370` sets `include_local_variables=False`, so a captured frame does not expose the credential tuple. No `capture_exception` was added.

- **Terminal command line.** The credential crosses as `initialize(login=..., password=..., server=...)` keyword arguments over the rpyc channel, never as argv. S-11(c) measured nothing beyond `/portable` for the credentialed launch (see T-06).

The scrub dials through the same `_default_connect`, which refuses any host `is_private_gateway_host` rejects before `mt5linux` is imported. Both endpoint readers re-check the host. The new runbook section states "No new network exposure is created".
- **Residual (unchanged, by design).** The channel itself stays unauthenticated arbitrary remote code (T-134-03), now with a deletion command class. This is recorded in the runbook and bounded by the committed literal.
