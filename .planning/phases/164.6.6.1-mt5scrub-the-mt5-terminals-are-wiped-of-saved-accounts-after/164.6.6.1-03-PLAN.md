<objective>
Criterion 2 on the validation terminal: **a validated account's saved data does not outlive its
validation.** Wire plan 05's scrub verb into both validate sites so the validation terminal is
scrubbed after every use (option (b), "pool of one plus scrub", RESEARCH decision table).

Purpose: under D-02 the validation terminal only ever sees un-onboarded accounts, including master
passwords the product then rejects (RESEARCH H3/H4). Scrubbing after each use bounds what a VNC
viewer or a container shell could read on it to the validation in flight when the scrub succeeds,
and to the LAST validated account when it does not (skipped, refused, partial or failed): that
residue stays until the next validation's scrub succeeds, so its bound is the next validation, not a
time. The residue is recorded (row plus WARNING) and its reader is the owner D-06 names; this plan
does not claim more than that.

Why inside the same lease and not after the response: a scrub scheduled after the lease releases
lets the next queued validation log in first, so two accounts' data would coexist. Inside the lease
the terminal never holds more than one validation's data. The cost is the scrub's time on each
validation's response, measured by the spike (S-05). Plan 08's L1 reads it live.

⚠️ Concurrency cost, named: the in-lease scrub lengthens each validation's hold on the validation
terminal, so a second user validating at the same moment reaches the 20 s `_MT5_LEASE_WAIT_S` bound
(`Mt5TerminalBusyError`) sooner.

Timeout budget, reconciled: the wizard's scrub is NOT given a budget of its own. It runs inside the
time left of the one end-to-end deadline `_MT5_VALIDATE_DEADLINE_S`, measured from a
`time.monotonic()` stamp taken immediately before the probe's `asyncio.wait_for`, and it is not
started when that remainder cannot cover the S-05-measured scrub time (plus the relaunch in
credentialed mode). So the server worst case documented in the `routers/exchange.py` timeout-chain
comment (end-to-end deadline, plus `_MT5_RELEASE_TIMEOUT_S` outside it, plus the bounded lease
wait) is unchanged, and no client-side timeout moves. The price is the residue above: a probe that
uses most of the deadline leaves the scrub skipped for budget.

Output: `services/mt5_terminal_scrub.py`, both validate sites calling it, and its tests.

Spec-less probe fallback skipped: phase has no requirement IDs to probe (visible skip). The
`requirements:` IDs above are RESEARCH's proposed IDs.
</objective>
