- PROD reads go through the Supabase MCP, one query per call, never chained.
- Enqueue: `enqueue_compute_job(p_strategy_id, p_kind, p_idempotency_key, p_parent_job_ids, p_exchange, p_metadata, p_allocator_id, p_api_key_id, p_run_at)`; an api_key-scoped `okx_archive_rebuild` passes `p_strategy_id := NULL`. The in-flight unique index is per api_key: an enqueue while another job of the kind is in flight on that key returns the existing row (readback `this_run_jobs` 0 means exactly that).
- The result lives at `compute_jobs.metadata->'member_progress'`; `last_error` reads `okx_archive_rebuild: <stop_class> ...` (counts and days only).
- OKX generates a file in about 2 h (up to 3 at peak); a link lives 5.5 h; the job polls every 15 min, gives up 8 h after OKX's request ts, and stops waiting 24 h after its own `created_at`.
- The chain (plan 03): a clean OKX `sync_trades` chains `fills_apply` when the strategy's first daily return is below the write bound, its earliest stored OKX fill is absent or more than 2 days after that, and no `fills_apply` job in 14 days on the account's keys is in flight, done, or failed with a named stop. After Phase 255 the three PROD OKX strategies hold old leftover fills from the one-page pager (ROADMAP Phase 255 result), so their earliest stored fill may sit within 2 days of the first daily return, in which case the chain does not fire and the runbook's direct `fills_apply` enqueue is the path.
- At booking: three PROD OKX strategies, apparently one OKX account; first daily returns 2026-02-23 or 2026-04-30; quarters needed 2026 Q1, Q2, Q3; Q4 2026 is the current quarter and outside the archive.
- The OKXHISTORY runbook keeps enqueues out of 00:00-01:45 UTC (the daily chain window); this runbook does the same.
