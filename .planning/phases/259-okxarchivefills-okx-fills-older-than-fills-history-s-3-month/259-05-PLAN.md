Design decisions recorded at planning time (Claude's Discretion in CONTEXT; flagged to the orchestrator):
- All or nothing per read: any file, mapping or reconcile stop writes nothing for any target and names the quarter, position and span. An operator can exclude a bad quarter or target with the optional `quarters` / `fill_strategy_ids` metadata on a manual job.
- At most 4 quarters per job (`MAX_FILLS_QUARTERS`), the newest kept, because the apply POST is budgeted at 12 per day per OKX user ID (RESEARCH A3) and one claim's budget is 840 s. This is the named known gap `259-ARCHIVE-QUARTER-CAP`: the left-out quarters are listed in `info.over_cap_quarters`, and a manual job with a `quarters` override reads them (runbook, plan 09).
- Write window per target is [anchor, write bound): archive fills before the anchor (first daily return) are counted (`before_anchor`), named as a span, and not written.
