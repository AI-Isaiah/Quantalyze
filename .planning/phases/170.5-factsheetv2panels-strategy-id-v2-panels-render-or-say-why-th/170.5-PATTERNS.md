#### Alternative path (call the builder) and why it is not the default

`fetchAndBuildPayloadWithReason(id, withPublishedOnly)` (`fetch-and-build-payload.ts:716-721`) returns the whole payload, `payload.comparators.btc.joint` / `.jointWithheld`, and `payload.returnsUnit`.
