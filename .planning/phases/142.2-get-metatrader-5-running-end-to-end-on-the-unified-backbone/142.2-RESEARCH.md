## Summary

The phase's centre of gravity, MT5-12, is smaller in *mechanism* and larger in *blast radius*
than the requirement text assumes. The mechanism is small because the pipeline already computes
everything needed: each of the four `broker_dailies.py` combine functions already knows whether
the series it just produced is ledger-complete, sampled-with-holes, or fill-derived-and-unproven,
and each already returns a `meta` dict that the worker already persists onto
`strategy_analytics`. Nothing needs to be *discovered* at derive time — it needs to be *stated*
and *carried*. The blast radius is large because the predicate that MT5-12 deletes exists in
**three** places, not two: `strategyGate.ts:181`, a hand-duplicated TOCTOU re-check at
`admin/strategy-review/route.ts:379-381`, and (for a different purpose) `long_fetch.py:63`. Any
plan that fixes two of three re-arms the exact drift class the founder refused to re-arm.

The recommended design deletes TypeScript's opinion entirely. Today two languages hold a venue
list and a comment asks a human to keep them in lockstep. After the change, **exactly one
component decides completeness — the combiner that built the series** — and TypeScript only reads
a persisted verdict it cannot compute. There is no second list to drift, and a new venue that
adds a combiner cannot ship a series without a verdict because a single fail-loud assertion at
the one persist seam refuses it. That is the structural difference between this and "widen the
frozenset", and it is the whole reason D-13 refused the one-line fix.

The safety oracle survives by construction rather than by care. `combine_realized_and_funding` —
the perp path, and the *only* one of the four that cannot prove its own completeness (venue
retention caps, the DQ-02 flow terminus, and the already-logged OKX-paginator silent truncation
all make its input stream unprovably whole) — stamps `fill_derived_unproven`. The gate takes the
daily-returns branch only on a positive `ledger_complete` / `user_supplied` verdict, so the
fills-gapped perp stays on the trade branch and is still refused. Absence of a verdict also
refuses. The change is behaviour-preserving for every ccxt venue and behaviour-changing only
where the founder was told to expect it: sFOX with interior NAV holes, and (as an explicit
decision, not a default) Deribit with a broken TWR chain.

**Primary recommendation:** carry the verdict in a **new nullable `strategy_analytics
.series_completeness` column**, stamped by the Python producer, enforced by one fail-loud
assertion at the derive persist seam, and read by the gate — and **split the phase at D-14**:
142.2 makes MT5 reachable and honest (MT5-03/04/05/11/12 + the offset *code*), 142.3 proves it
correct against the terminal on a trading day (MT5-06 live run, 07, 08, 09, 10). Keeping MT5-10's
explicitly-uncapped remediation in the same phase as a blocking architectural refactor is what
makes 142.2 unsizeable.

### Pattern 1: The completeness verdict is produced by the code that produced the series

**What:** Each `broker_dailies.py` combiner returns, alongside its `(returns, meta)`, a verdict
describing whether the series it just built is trustworthy as a complete record of the account's
performance over its own span. Nothing else in the system is allowed to have an opinion.

**When to use:** This is the MT5-12 mechanism. It is the answer to "make the daily series answer
for itself" that does not require inventing per-row provenance (D-16's supersession).

**Why this and not a shared helper:** a helper that any caller may or may not call is a
convention. A **required field of the return contract**, plus one assertion at the single persist
seam, is an invariant. The distinction is exactly what failed last time: `strategyGate.ts:69-71`
carries a comment reading *"keep the two in lockstep"* and that comment is the entire enforcement
mechanism. [VERIFIED: read at HEAD]

**Verdict values and their justification, per combiner:**

| Combiner | Venues | Verdict | Justification from code |
|----------|--------|---------|-------------------------|
| `combine_native_ledger:185` | deribit | `ledger_complete` | Reconstructs via `reconstruct_native_nav_and_twr` with a §5 inception-reconciliation gate; `UnmarkableCurrencyError` / `InceptionReconciliationError` propagate **typed and uncaught** so the job fails rather than emitting a partial series [VERIFIED: `broker_dailies.py:214-219`] |
| `combine_mt5_deal_ledger:403` | mt5 | `ledger_complete` | `history_deals_get(0, now + 1d)` fetches the **entire** account history with no retention cap [VERIFIED: `job_worker.py:3514-3515`]; `classify_deal` raises `Mt5DealClassificationError` **before any series is produced**; the anchor is the authoritative live `account_info().equity`; the docstring states outright that MT5 "is a ledger-COMPLETE venue, so a no-activity interior day is genuinely flat" [VERIFIED: `broker_dailies.py:422-425`] |
| `combine_sfox_balance_history:241` | sfox | `ledger_complete` **iff** `nav_coverage_gap_days == 0`, else `sampled_gapped` | The function already computes `nav_gap_days` and already sets `meta["nav_coverage_gap_days"]`; sFOX NAV is explicitly a **sampled** series where an unobserved interior day is UNKNOWN, not flat [VERIFIED: `broker_dailies.py:393-400`] |

Constant `fill_derived_unproven` is byte-equivalent to today's behaviour for every ccxt venue,
which is what makes the whole change auditable. Log the data-driven refinement to `TODOS.md`.

### Pattern 2: One fail-loud assertion at the single persist seam

**What:** Immediately before `csv_daily_returns` rows are written in
`run_derive_broker_dailies_job`, assert that `meta["series_completeness"]` is present and is a
recognised value. Absent or unrecognised ⇒ `DispatchOutcome.FAILED`, `error_kind="permanent"`,
with the same terminal `strategy_analytics` stamp the existing structural refusals use
(`_dispose_broker_nav_error` / `_stamp_strategy_analytics_failed` are the established idioms).

**When to use:** exactly once, at the seam. Do **not** repeat it per venue — a per-venue check is
a list by another name.

**Why this is the enforcement, not the comment:** a future engineer adding a fifth venue writes a
fifth combiner. If they forget the verdict, the *first* run of that venue fails loudly and
permanently with a message naming the omission, instead of silently inheriting a default. Pair it
with `mypy --strict` (already a repo requirement) by typing the meta key as required in the
`NavTWRMeta` contract, so the omission is caught at type-check time and the runtime assert is the
belt to that brace.

**Anti-pattern this replaces:** the current `strategyGate.ts:66-72` docblock —
*"Mirrors the analytics-service `is_ledger_backed = source == "deribit"` … keep the two in
lockstep. Deribit is the only such venue today."* The comment is stale in two ways at once (the
Python set is now three-valued; the claim "Deribit is the only such venue today" is false), and
MT5-11 correctly identifies the stale comment as **part of the defect**. Deleting the function
deletes the comment; nothing replaces it because TypeScript no longer holds the knowledge.

### Pattern 3: The gate reads a positive verdict; absence refuses

**What:** `StrategyGateInput.isLedgerBacked?: boolean` is replaced by
`seriesCompleteness: SeriesCompleteness | null`, and the branch becomes:

```ts
// src/lib/strategyGate.ts — replaces the current :181-184
const SERIES_TRUSTED_FOR_DAILY_BRANCH: ReadonlySet<string> = new Set([
  "ledger_complete",
  "user_supplied",
]);

const isDailyReturnsSourced =
  input.tradeCount === 0 &&
  csvRowCount > 0 &&
  SERIES_TRUSTED_FOR_DAILY_BRANCH.has(input.seriesCompleteness ?? "");
```

**Three properties this has that the current code does not:**

1. **Allow-list, not deny-list.** `null` (no row, stale row, a strategy derived before this
   column existed, a read that failed and was coerced) falls to the trade branch and is refused.
   The safe direction is the default.
2. **`apiKeyId` disappears from the branch.** The keyless-CSV case is now carried by
   `user_supplied` rather than by inferring trust from the *absence of a key*. The
   `NO_DATA_SOURCE` guard at `:154` still keys off `!apiKeyId` and is untouched.
3. **No venue name appears anywhere in `src/`.** A vitest source-reading invariant (the
   `seam-budgets.invariant.test.ts` idiom) can pin `"deribit"` to zero occurrences in
   `strategyGate.ts`, so a future "quick fix" cannot reintroduce the literal.

**⚠️ The third call site the requirement text does not name.** `admin/strategy-review/route.ts`
implements the predicate **twice**: once by calling `checkStrategyGate` at `:267`, and once
**inline** in the TOCTOU re-check at `:379-381`:

```ts
const isDailyReturnsSourced =
  recheckTradeCount === 0 &&
  (recheckCsvCount ?? 0) > 0 &&
  (!approveApiKeyId || isLedgerBacked);
```

Its own comment says *"Mirrors the first-pass gate's isDailyReturnsSourced predicate EXACTLY
(P72) … the two must never diverge."* That is the same enforcement-by-comment that failed. The
consolidation should **export the predicate from `strategyGate.ts`** and have both sites call it,
so the duplication is deleted rather than re-synchronised. [VERIFIED: read at HEAD]

**Reading the verdict costs zero new queries.** `SyncPreviewStep.tsx:1176` already selects from
`strategy_analytics` in the same `Promise.all`; widen the column list. `admin/strategy-review`
already reads `analytics?.computation_status`. Owner-side RLS on `strategy_analytics` is already
proven for client reads — `SyncPreviewStep.tsx:601` reads `data_quality_flags` from the browser
today. [VERIFIED: read at HEAD]

### Pattern 6: Measure the server offset from a live tick, not from the last deal (MT5-06)

**Current production state — verified, and it is not what the requirement text assumes.**
`MT5_SERVER_UTC_OFFSET_S` is **already set to `10800`** (UTC+3, EEST) on the Railway worker
[VERIFIED: `.planning/MILESTONES.md:25`, `TODOS.md:67`, `CHANGELOG.md:285`], read at
`job_worker.py:3735` via `int(os.getenv("MT5_SERVER_UTC_OFFSET_S", "0"))`. So the 135-row
production series was **not** bucketed at offset 0. What remains open is exactly what `TODOS.md`
says: *"a fixed env can't auto-switch EET↔EEST at the Oct/Mar transition"* — plus the unstated
second problem that a single worker-wide scalar is wrong the moment a **second broker** with a
different timezone connects.

**Why the last-deal estimator cannot be reused:** it was already built and already failed. The
v1.15 spike produced a *"misleading −810 estimate … root-caused (stale-deal artifact: estimator
assumed the latest deal ≈ now) and hardened to emit no candidate beyond ±13h."* [VERIFIED:
`TODOS.md:67`]. A ledger's newest deal is only ≈ now if the account traded in the last few
minutes. Do not re-derive this conclusion at plan time.

**The only reliable live clock:** a market tick, which the broker stamps in server time. On a
liquid symbol during a trading session, `tick.time − time.time()` **is** the offset, to within
the tick age. This is why D-08 (measure the offset) and D-09 (trading day) are the same
requirement wearing two hats — and it is a second, independent reason a weekend run proves
nothing.

**⚠️ This requires widening a deliberately locked surface — see Pitfall 4.**

**Recommended shape:**
- `Mt5Client.server_time()` — a purpose-named method returning a single materialized epoch
  scalar. Internally it reads a tick; it does **not** expose `symbol_info_tick` publicly.
- Persist the measured offset **and its measurement timestamp** per account (an `api_keys`
  metadata field, or a new small column). Both halves matter: an offset without a timestamp
  cannot be aged out or DST-reasoned about later.
- Assert the measurement against `MT5_SERVER_UTC_OFFSET_S`; disagreement is **loud** (a
  `complete_with_warnings`-class DQ signal at minimum, a permanent refusal at most — a decision
  for the plan, not for research to assume).
- Round the measured value to the nearest 15 minutes. Every real broker offset is a multiple of
  15 minutes; rounding removes tick-age noise without hiding a genuine hour-scale error.
- **Near-midnight regression test:** a deal at `23:30` server time with offset `+10800` must land
  on the **previous** UTC day. `deal_utc_day` is already the one seam and already has fixtures at
  `server_utc_offset_s = 7200` [VERIFIED: `test_mt5_deal_reconstruction.py:124`], so the test has
  a home.

### Anti-Patterns to Avoid

- **Widening `isLedgerBackedExchange` to `{deribit, sfox, mt5}`.** Explicitly refused (D-13). It
  re-arms the drift and satisfies nothing that a verdict does not satisfy better.
- **Making MT5 write `trades` rows so it passes the existing gate.** D-12 names this: a deal
  ledger has no fills, so synthesising them is a second venue-specific path *around* the dailies
  — the opposite of consolidation.
- **Treating `complete_with_warnings` as a gate signal.** It is a terminal *success* that both
  the gate (`strategyGate.ts:230-267`, a deny-list) and the admin re-check deliberately admit.
  Routing completeness through the warn channel makes it invisible to the gate.
- **Deleting `_LEDGER_BACKED_SOURCES` from `long_fetch.py`.** See Pitfall 2 — it governs a
  different question and removing it breaks ingestion.
- **Backfilling `series_completeness` for existing rows.** See Pitfall 6 — a backfill is a
  fabricated trust claim about series whose inputs were never examined. `NULL` is the honest
  value and the gate handles it safely.
- **Rendering the server's raw `error` string in the wizard** as the MT5-04 "fix". See Pattern 5.
- **Re-deriving the offset from the newest deal.** Already tried, already failed (Pattern 6).

| Problem | Don't Build | Use Instead | Why |
|---------|-------------|-------------|-----|
| Keeping TS and Python in agreement about venue trust | A codegen step, a shared JSON manifest, or a lint rule that greps both languages | **Delete TypeScript's opinion.** Only Python decides; TS reads a persisted verdict | A synchronisation mechanism still has two sources. **Key insight:** in this codebase the expensive mistakes have not been missing libraries — they

collapsing a pair into one owner. Where a pair genuinely cannot be collapsed (TS must *read*
something Python decides), the fix is to make one side incapable of computing it.

## Common Pitfalls

### Pitfall 1: The `data_quality_flags` bag looks like the obvious carrier and is a fail-open trap

**What goes wrong:** the verdict is added as a JSONB key, no migration is needed, everyone is
pleased — and then `analytics_runner.py:1439` runs and **rebuilds the dict wholesale**, wiping it
seconds after the derive stamped it.
**Why it happens:** the file's own comment describes exactly this hazard for `mtm_gated_reason`:
*"This rebuild REBUILDS data_quality_flags WHOLESALE (the fresh dict above), so an unbridged
reason is wiped seconds after being stamped."* [VERIFIED: `analytics_runner.py:1487-1490`]
**How to avoid:** use a real column, omitted from the runner's upsert payload so it survives by
construction. If a JSONB key is chosen anyway, the plan **must** include the explicit lift and a
test that the value survives a full derive → analytics round trip.
**Warning signs:** the phrase "no migration needed" appearing as the *reason* for a design choice.

### Pitfall 2: `_LEDGER_BACKED_SOURCES` looks like the same list and is not

**What goes wrong:** MT5-12 says *"no hardcoded venue set governs gate routing in **either**
language"*, a plan reads that literally, deletes `long_fetch.py:63`, and ingestion breaks for
deribit/sfox/mt5.
**Why it happens:** the two sets share a name and a membership but answer different questions.
`long_fetch.py:277` uses it to skip `fetch_raw` / `compute_fingerprint` /
`reconstruct_positions`, because those adapters **raise `NotImplementedError` by design**
[VERIFIED: `long_fetch.py:44-63,461`]. That is an *adapter capability*, and it is legitimately a
venue property. The `strategyGate.ts` mirror is a *trust judgement*, and that is not.
**How to avoid:** MT5-12 is satisfied by deleting the **TypeScript** mirror. State this
explicitly in the plan so a reviewer does not flag it as incomplete.
**Optional follow-up worth logging:** the ingestion set could become `adapter.fetches_fills` — a
property on the adapter that already knows the answer, removing the last venue literal. Cheap,
but not required by the invariant. Log to `TODOS.md`.

### Pitfall 3: A single persisted offset is still wrong across a DST boundary

**What goes wrong:** the offset is measured live on 2026-08-05 (EEST, `+10800`), persisted, and
asserted — and the phase closes believing MT5-06 is solved. On 2026-10-25 the broker moves to EET
(`+7200`) and every deal after that instant is bucketed an hour late, silently.
**Why it happens:** an offset is a *point-in-time* observation of a *timezone*. Historical deals
spanning a transition need the offset that was in force **at each deal's timestamp**, not the one
in force at measurement.
**How to avoid:** be honest that measuring does not solve DST, and say so in the phase's own
verification notes rather than letting a green test imply otherwise. Practical mitigations, in
increasing cost: (a) persist offset + measurement timestamp and re-measure on every derive, which
keeps *new* days right and only mis-buckets across a re-derive of history; (b) store an IANA
timezone guess per broker and apply it per-deal — correct, but nothing in MT5 publishes the
broker's timezone, so the guess is an inference; (c) accept the ≤1-hour day-boundary
misattribution and flag it. **Recommendation: (a) now, log (b) as an open item.** See Open
Questions.
**Warning signs:** any plan task worded "fix the server-time offset" rather than "measure and
assert the current offset".

### Pitfall 4: `Mt5Client`'s read-only surface is locked by two structural tests

**What goes wrong:** MT5-06 needs a live tick; the engineer adds `symbol_info_tick` to
`Mt5Client`; **two tests fail** and the fix looks like it is fighting a safety guard.
**Why it happens:** this is deliberate, not accidental.
`test_mt5_client_contract.py:719-736` parametrises a forbidden list that **includes
`symbol_info_tick`** and asserts `not hasattr(Mt5Client, forbidden)`;
`test_public_surface_is_exactly_the_contract` (`:748`) asserts the public callable surface is
**exactly** `{login, account_info, history_deals_get, order_check, …}`; and
`test_no_getattr_passthrough` forbids a forwarding facade. The rationale given is that
"mt5linux exposes the full trading surface; a trade method appearing here is a trust-integrity
footgun for the whole `api_verified` value prop." [VERIFIED: read at HEAD]
**How to avoid:** treat the widening as an explicit, argued decision, not a test fix. The
defensible position: `symbol_info_tick` is grouped as *raw surface*, not a *trade* method; add a
purpose-named `server_time()` returning one materialized epoch scalar, keep `symbol_info_tick`
itself absent from the public surface, and update `test_public_surface_is_exactly_the_contract`
to include `server_time` — leaving the forbidden-list test **untouched and still passing**. Any
plan that edits the forbidden list itself should be rejected at review.
**Warning signs:** a diff that deletes `"symbol_info_tick"` from the parametrised list.

### Pitfall 5: `database.types.ts` is stale and there is no regeneration script

**What goes wrong:** the migration lands, Python stamps the column, and the TypeScript read
does not compile — or worse, someone types the read as `any` to move on.
**Why it happens:** `database.types.ts` is missing `computing_started_at` and
`computation_warned` from migration `20260802120000`, and `package.json` contains no
`supabase gen types` script. [VERIFIED: grep at HEAD]
**How to avoid:** make the hand-edit an explicit plan task with the three sites named (`Row`,
`Insert`, `Update`), and run `npm run typecheck` as its verification.
**Warning signs:** `as any` or `@ts-expect-error` near a `strategy_analytics` select.

### Pitfall 6: A "helpful" backfill fabricates a trust claim

**What goes wrong:** someone reasons that `NULL` will block existing strategies and writes
`UPDATE strategy_analytics SET series_completeness = 'ledger_complete' WHERE …` for deribit rows.
**Why it happens:** it *looks* like preserving behaviour. It is asserting, on behalf of series
whose inputs no longer exist, that they were whole.
**How to avoid:** understand the actual blast radius first — **the gate runs at exactly two
moments: wizard `SyncPreviewStep` (a new/resumed connect) and admin approve.** It does **not**
run on render, so a `NULL` verdict cannot un-publish anything already published. The affected
population is: drafts awaiting approval, and re-syncs — both of which re-derive and self-heal.
**Do a read-only PROD census at plan time** (`khslejtfbuezsmvmtsdn`, SELECT only) to size that
population before deciding. If it is non-trivial, the right remedy is a **re-derive**, not a
backfill — re-derivation actually examines the inputs.
**Warning signs:** any `UPDATE … SET series_completeness` in a migration.

### Pitfall 7: The consolidation newly refuses sFOX and possibly Deribit — that is the point, and it still needs sizing

**What goes wrong:** the change ships, and a deribit strategy that would have been approvable is
refused because its `meta` carried `twr_chain_broken` (a DQ-03 interior chain break, where the
cumulative figure compounds only the post-break suffix).
**Why it happens:** MT5-12 states that deribit/sfox/mt5 "receive **no** completeness check at all
and are trusted purely by list membership", and that consolidation is the first time they are
actually checked. So new refusals are expected — but *which* ones is a decision, not a discovery.
**How to avoid — recommended positions, each to be confirmed at plan time:**
- **sFOX with `nav_coverage_gap_days > 0` → refuse.** Genuinely unknown interior days, and sFOX
  is dormant, so blast radius is zero. Ship it.
- **Deribit with `twr_chain_broken` → default to `ledger_complete` anyway (behaviour-preserving),
  and log the tightening as a decision.** Deribit is live (Zavara). Recommend a **read-only PROD
  census** of how many deribit `strategy_analytics` rows carry `twr_chain_broken` before
  tightening. Tightening without the census is exactly the kind of unsized change D-14 exists to
  catch.
- **MT5, Deribit clean, CSV → unchanged.**
**Warning signs:** a plan that tightens deribit without a census, or one that ships the whole
consolidation claiming "no behaviour change" (that claim is false for sFOX by design).

### Pitfall 8: `pytest` from the repo root makes live broker calls

**What goes wrong:** `pytest analytics-service/tests/...` from the repo root misses the VCR
`cassette_library_dir` and the suite hits real broker APIs, producing two bogus failures that
look like a ccxt version problem.
**How to avoid:** every pytest invocation in every plan must be `cd analytics-service && pytest …`.
This is a standing project constraint, not a phase-specific one.

## Phase Size — honest assessment and the D-14 recommendation

**Verdict: 142.2 as scoped is two phases of work, and the second one is unbounded by
construction.** Recommending the D-14 valve.

|-----------|------------------|-------------|

| MT5-12 behaviour decisions | sFOX/deribit census + tightening decision (Pitfall 7) | 1 |
| MT5-03 | 1 config key + 1 ternary + DESIGN.md read + tests | 0.5 |
| MT5-04 | 28 route sites + `WizardErrorCode` union + 2 recognised-code arrays + copy + 2 route test files | 2 |
| MT5-06 code | `Mt5Client.server_time()` + a pinned structural test update + persistence + assertion + near-midnight regression | 2 |
| MT5-06/07/08 live run | Human-gated, trading-day, gateway-only, founder reads the terminal | 1–2 (orchestrator) |

| MT5-10 | **Uncapped by founder decision** — a shared-backbone money-math root cause is explicitly in scope | **unbounded** |

Two things make the combined scope unsizeable rather than merely large:
1. **MT5-10 is uncapped by decision (D-10), not by oversight.** A phase containing an explicitly
   unbounded remediation cannot be planned to a boundary.
2. **The live run is human- and calendar-gated.** MT5-06/07/08 cannot start until a founder is
   at a terminal on a trading day with the account open. Blocking a merged architectural
   refactor behind that is what turns a large phase into a stalled one.

### Recommended split (D-14 valve)

**142.2 — "reachable and honest."** MT5-03, MT5-04, MT5-05, MT5-11, MT5-12, plus the **code** for
MT5-06 (`server_time()`, persistence, assertion, near-midnight regression test — all unit-testable
offline).
*Exit criterion:* the founder connects the real MT5 account through the wizard, passes the gate,
and sees a factsheet; and a fills-gapped perp fixture is still refused.
*This honours D-13* — MT5-11 still ships **through** MT5-12, never as a standalone widening.

**142.3 — "proven correct."** MT5-06 live measurement, MT5-07, MT5-08, MT5-09, MT5-10.
*Exit criterion:* the MT5 terminal's equity/period figures and all five rendered surfaces agree
within a stated tolerance, on a trading day, on the live funded account.
*This is where D-10's uncapped remediation lives*, which is the correct home for an unbounded
requirement.

**Why this boundary and not another:** it is the only cut where the dependency graph is already
one-directional. MT5-07/08/09 cannot run until MT5-12 unblocks publication (the factsheet, public
and browse surfaces all require a published strategy). Nothing in 142.2 depends on anything in
142.3. Every other candidate cut — e.g. "MT5-12 alone" — either strands MT5-11 (violating D-13's
"no standalone patch" only in spirit, but leaving MT5 blocked longer for no gain) or splits the
gate change across a deploy boundary.

**The alternative I considered and rejected:** keeping everything in 142.2 and treating MT5-10 as
a stretch. That is the "quietly shrink the scope to fit" failure mode the brief warns against —
it would produce a phase that reports done with its uncapped requirement unstarted.

### Known Threat Patterns for this stack

| Pattern | STRIDE | Standard mitigation |
|---------|--------|---------------------|
| **Publishing an understated track record as `api_verified`** (the D-15 threat — a funding-only perp series admitted by a fail-open gate) | Tampering / Repudiation | Positive allow-list verdict; absence refuses. Falsification fixture is the acceptance test, not a nice-to-have. |
| **Trust-signal drift across a language boundary** (the MT5-11 defect, generalised) | Tampering | Delete one side's ability to compute the signal; single producer, one fail-loud chokepoint. |
| Credential disclosure via a raw transport traceback | Information disclosure | Already mitigated: `Mt5Client._guarded_read` scrubs every raised transport error, and `login()` additionally redacts login/password/server **by value**. Any `server_time()` added for MT5-06 **must route through `_guarded_read`**. |
| Widening a read-only broker surface into a trade path | Elevation of privilege | The pinned forbidden-list test. Add `server_time`; never touch the forbidden list (Pitfall 4). |
| Account-size leak through a DQ/verdict channel | Information disclosure | Enum only, never a magnitude (V8 above). |
| An SQL migration auto-applying to production on merge | Tampering / DoS | Additive nullable column, no default, no constraint on existing rows, no backfill; applied to TEST via MCP first. |
| A destructive remedy offered on an unwinnable failure | DoS (user-inflicted) | ⚠️ **Already live:** `GATE_INSUFFICIENT_TRADES` offers "try another key", and `onTryAnotherKey` fires `handleDeleteDraft()`, destroying the draft and every `strategy_keys` member. ## Open Questions

1. **Does a single measured offset actually satisfy MT5-06, or does it only appear to?**
   - What we know: brokers stamp deals in server local time, which is a *timezone*, not an offset;
     `MT5_SERVER_UTC_OFFSET_S=10800` is EEST and becomes wrong at the October transition;
     `TODOS.md` already names this as the sole remaining v1.15 open item.
   - What is unclear: whether a per-account persisted scalar, re-measured on every derive, is
     acceptable — it keeps *new* days right but mis-buckets any **re-derive of history** that
     spans a transition, and MT5's derive re-fetches the **entire** ledger every run
     (`history_deals_get(0, now+1d)`), so a re-derive after October re-buckets the whole
     pre-transition history at the winter offset.
   - Recommendation: implement re-measurement (it strictly improves today), **state the
     re-derive-across-DST limitation explicitly in the phase verification notes**, and log the
     per-deal timezone model to `TODOS.md`. Do not let a green near-midnight test imply DST is
     solved.

2. **Is there exactly one `csv_daily_returns` write path?** (A4)
   - What we know: `run_derive_broker_dailies_job` is the strategy-mode writer, and there is a
     key-mode (allocator) variant in the same function.
   - What is unclear: whether the composite/stitch worker or the CSV finalize path writes rows by
     another route.
   - Recommendation: **a grep-and-confirm task before the assert is placed.** If there are two
     seams, the assert must sit at both, and that is worth knowing at plan time rather than at
     review time.

3. **Should the deribit `twr_chain_broken` tightening ship in this phase?** (Pitfall 7, A6)
   - What we know: MT5-12 expects new refusals for the three previously-unchecked venues; the
     gate does not run on render, so nothing already published is at risk.
   - What is unclear: how many live deribit strategies carry the flag.
   - Recommendation: read-only PROD census first; default to behaviour-preserving
     (`ledger_complete` regardless) and treat the tightening as a separate, evidenced decision.

4. **Does the client render the server's specific `error` string after MT5-04, or only copy by
   code?** (Pattern 5)
   - What we know: `TODOS.md` names the discarded server string as half the defect; the strings
     were not written for end users.
   - Recommendation: split the codes; render copy by code; log detail-passthrough separately.
     But this is a founder-facing product call, not a research finding — surface it at plan time.

5. **What tolerance counts as "matching" for D-07?**
   - What we know: the oracle is the terminal's equity/balance for a fixed window.
   - What is unclear: no numeric tolerance is stated anywhere in CONTEXT.md or REQUIREMENTS.md.
   - Recommendation: the planner must get a number from the founder before MT5-07 can have an
     acceptance criterion. A phase requirement that says "within a stated tolerance" with no
     stated tolerance is unverifiable.
