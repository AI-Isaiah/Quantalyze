<objective>
Make the key card stop claiming failure when only its own patience ran out (KCS-22). With stale
terminals now ignored (plan 03), a long healthy exchange fetch can outlast the panel's two-minute
budget; the panel must then say it stopped checking, not that the computation timed out, and must
not offer a Retry that could enqueue a second job during a live one.

Purpose: a false "timed out … retry" line pushes the owner to re-POST during failed_retry, which
inserts a second job (the F-3 note in src/lib/sync-progress.ts).
Output: a UI-only no_result state with two locked detail lines, the give-up reason threaded from the
poller, and the timeout sentence retired from every path.
</objective>
