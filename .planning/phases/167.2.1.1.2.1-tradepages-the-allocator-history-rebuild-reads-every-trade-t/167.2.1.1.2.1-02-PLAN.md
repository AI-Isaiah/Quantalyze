<objective>
Apply D-02's one stop rule to OKX. OKX's fills-history answers the NEWEST fills of its range (RESEARCH Q2, A1), so the
forward cursor the rebuild uses today (`since = newest + 1`) can never reach older fills, even once the short-page stop
is gone: every pass keeps 100 fills at most. This plan walks each instrument type backward with an explicit end.

D-02 verbatim from CONTEXT: "The rebuild's trade loop stops only on an empty page or when the read reaches the end of
its window, never on a page shorter than the requested limit. Bybit is read in 7-day windows with an explicit end time.
One rule for every venue the rebuild reads."

The end bound one millisecond past the oldest fill seen is decided here (Claude's discretion, "How the loop knows a
venue's page size and when it has reached the window end"). RESEARCH A2 leaves open whether OKX's `end` is inclusive or
exclusive. With the end at the oldest fill itself, an exclusive bound silently drops the older members of a
same-millisecond group split at the page edge; one millisecond later is safe under both readings, and id dedupe absorbs
the fills read twice. A group larger than a page still cannot be passed by any timestamp bound: that stop is loud.

Purpose: SC-1 ("The rebuild reads every page on OKX and Bybit").
Output: `_fetch_okx_inst_type_trades`, the real ccxt okx fake, the OKX pins.
</objective>
