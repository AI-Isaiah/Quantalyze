## Issues Encountered

**One measured observation, recorded rather than fixed — pre-existing, not introduced here.**

The two hand-kept by-name absence lists (A at `run.mjs:3716`, B at `run.mjs:3798`) sit at the tail of `&&` chains whose earlier links already pin `r.defects.length === 1` and `d.kind === "mt5-ssh-transport"` / `"mt5-probe-timeout"`. Given those priors, `noDefectOfKind` over a list not containing the pinned kind is **structurally always true**, and `&&` short-circuits before it on any earlier failure. So in their current scenario shape these two lists cannot go red at RUNTIME; their teeth is the non-comment source scan (calibrated three ways above), which is what task 2's own `<verify>` asserts on.

This is a property of the scenario shape that predates this plan — plan 02 only extended the lists the plan told it to extend, and the plan explicitly forbids adding or restructuring scenarios in task 2. It is recorded here so the verifier reads the lists as documentation-with-a-source-gate rather than as runtime controls. No roadmap deferral is opened for it: nothing is owed, and inventing one would put an unowned item in the queue.
