<objective>
Accept OKX quarter files on the UTC+8 cut and keep every daily total on whole UTC days. A quarter-edge UTC day counts only when both neighbouring files were read, otherwise it has no daily total at all.

Purpose: CONTEXT D-15. The D-14 calibrate re-run read `observed_min_day=2026-03-31` for the Q2 file, which shows that OKX cuts quarters at 00:00 UTC+8. The parser asserted calendar-UTC bounds, so it refused a correct file. Accepting the file on UTC+8 bounds without changing anything else would quietly turn each edge day into a partial day. This plan changes the bounds and the day rule together, in the two pure modules every consumer reads from.
Output: the UTC+8 bounds, whole-day `covered_days`, edge-day fields and `quarter_edge_days` in `okx_archive_files.py`; edge-gated sums, the edge-day readings and shared-edge adjacency in `okx_archive_classify.py`; two fixture instants moved inside the new bounds; a RED-first tracer and boundary, edge and gating tests, each guard shown able to fail.

Not in this plan: the job layer (`_windows`, the `census.edge_days` reading) and the runbook. Plan 19 owns both and depends on this plan. Also out of scope: shipping, the calibrate re-run (plan 14's follow-up after the ship), and any PROD statement.
</objective>

**Design fixed here (planner's decision under D-15).**
- The offset is ONE module constant, `_QUARTER_CUT_UTC_OFFSET_MS` = 8 hours in milliseconds, subtracted from both `start_ms` and `end_ms`. `quarter_bounds` and its tests are unchanged (calendar dates).
- `ArchiveFile` gains `head_edge_day: str` and `tail_edge_day: str`, placed right after `covered_days` and before `duplicate_rows_dropped` (the only defaulted field). `covered_days` becomes `[quarter start, quarter's last calendar day)` as UTC days. `head_edge_day` is the day before the quarter's first calendar day and `tail_edge_day` is the quarter's last calendar day.
- `quarter_edge_days(files: Sequence[ArchiveFile]) -> tuple[frozenset[str], frozenset[str]]` returns `(whole, missing)`. `whole` is the set of tail edges that are also head edges, and `missing` is every other edge day.
- `aggregate_archive` computes `covered` = the union of each file's `covered_days` plus `whole`, before the row loop. Classification counters and the three classification stops still read EVERY row. Three guards skip the sums for a row whose `day` is not in `covered`: in the NON_EXTERNAL branch after the non-USD handling, in the REALIZED-outside-the-live-instTypes branch before an `excluded_by_month` amount, and in the final branch before `summed.add` and the realized / funding / flow sums. `summed_ccys` therefore names only currencies whose money reached a covered day. The aggregate returns `covered_days=covered`, `edge_days_whole=whole`, `edge_days_missing=missing`. The two new `ArchiveAggregate` fields sit right after `covered_days`.
- `_adjacent_runs` sorts by `head_edge_day` and joins a file to the run when the run's last file's `tail_edge_day` equals its `head_edge_day`.
