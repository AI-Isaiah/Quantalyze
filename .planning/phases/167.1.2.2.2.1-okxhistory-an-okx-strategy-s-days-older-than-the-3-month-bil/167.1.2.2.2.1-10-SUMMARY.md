# Phase 167.1.2.2.2.1 Plan 10: OKXHISTORY operator runbook Summary

**A marker-guarded runbook that triggers the `okx_archive_rebuild` job for today's work list through `enqueue_compute_job`, reads counts and dates before, after and after-the-next-tick, rolls a rebuild back in one statement, and hands the recompute to Wave 5 through a booked TODOS entry.**

## Performance

- **Duration:** about 62 min (record start 17:28Z; SUMMARY written 18:30Z)
- **Tasks:** 3 (1 tracer, 2 auto)
- **Files:** 3 (1 created, 2 modified)

## Accomplishments

- `docs/runbooks/okxhistory-go-live.md` (about 900 lines): header with the Q3 overlap expiry (about 2026-12-30), the deploy-order rule and the scheduling rule; What this activates; Why it is safe now; Blast radius; Environment and the `form.mjs` installer (cloned from the TRADESYNC runbook); Pre-flight P0-P3; Steps 1 to 5; Rollback; What this runbook does not cover.
- Thirteen operator forms plus `guard-check` (the three-branch guard proof, which also prints `kind_registered` for the deploy order) and three fragments. Every read returns `marker_is_prod` first and at least one row.
- D-02 is a refusal in SQL, not a convention: each enqueue form raises when a rebuild target is missing, its key is not connected (active, not disconnected, not revoked: the worker's own `_key_is_connected`), or its key is on another venue account than the job key (which also refuses the disconnected-key ordinal). `enqueue-calibrate` adds the same check for the zero-check target. `enqueue-apply` refuses a fingerprint that is not 64 lowercase hex before it enqueues anything.
- `before` emits `gaps_param` (the stored gaps as `from:to` islands joined by `+`, or `none`) and the full `series_provenance` as the rollback reference; `after` counts rows inside the OLD gap ranges given by `O1_GAPS` / `O2_GAPS` (apply narrows the stored gaps, so counting inside them would undercount) and refuses a malformed value or an island that ends before it starts; `survival` counts days inside `reconstructed_ranges` with no row.
- D-10 and D-11 are stated at Step 2 and Step 3: any calibration day above one cent STOPS for the founder and is never widened, nothing is configurable, a miss writes nothing. Step 5 carries the over-claim note (a day a later live derive overwrote keeps its row and `survival` still reads 0).
- `TODOS.md`: `### 167.1.2.2.2.1-WAVE5-RECOMPUTE` (booked 2026-10-15, set by ordinal, Owner line naming the Wave 5 consolidated recompute, trigger, acceptance reading, the apply-timing note); `[167.1.2.2.2-OKX-PERSISTENT-TRUNCATION]` carries the dated measurement and the narrowed trigger and stays open; `[167.1.2.2.2.1-OKX-DAILY-PNL-MIXED-CCY]` names the calibrate census as its measurement and stays open.
