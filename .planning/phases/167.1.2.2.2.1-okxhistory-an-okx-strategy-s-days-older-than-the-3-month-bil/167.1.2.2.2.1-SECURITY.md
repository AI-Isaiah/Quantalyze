## Non-blocking observations

1. `ARCHIVE_ALLOWED_HOSTS` ships empty by design, so every real link is refused `host_not_allowed` until plan 13 adds a measured host. Fail-closed.
2. Zip central-directory parsing amplifies memory about 6.7x before the one-member check (a 64 MiB hostile zip could take on the order of 450 MB), and the decompressed cap peaks near 2x from the final copy. Bounded by the caps and needs an allow-listed host; note for plan 14's size calibration.
3. The fingerprint covers archive aggregates, census and planned dates, not the live-anchored return floats, as its docstring and T-20 state.
4. The generated `supabase/schema/functions/apply_okx_archive_rebuild.sql` omits the grant lines; the migration is authoritative.
