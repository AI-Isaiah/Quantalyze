# Phase 167.1.2.2.2.1 Plan 16: stateless archive wait and quarter ingest Summary

**`okx_archive_rebuild` now waits for OKX's multi-hour archive generation without storing anything: every claim re-applies and re-reads OKX, then defers (at most one hour, attempts untouched), re-applies once, or gives up from OKX's own request `ts` at 8 h, and `calibrate` downloads, parses and classifies each quarter file, recording the per-quarter readings and census, with a lost or expired link always a retry and never a partial result.**

## Performance

- **Duration:** about 1 h 5 min (roughly 18:20Z to 19:25Z)
- **Tasks:** 3 (1 tracer, 2 TDD)
- **Files modified:** 2 (`okx_archive_job.py`, `test_okx_archive_job.py`); nothing created

## Accomplishments

- **Stateless `_quarter_step`** (RESEARCH Pattern 1). Per claim and quarter: one apply POST on a fresh instance (spaced 11 s by `_ApplyPacer`), no status read while a not-yet-existing request is under 2 h old, then the state decides: `finished` with a link is ready; `ongoing` waits 15 min or gives up at 8 h of OKX `ts`; `failed` or `finished` without a link is re-applied once and re-read, and a second failure is permanent. No link or `ts` is stored between claims (pinned by a module-state test and by captured-output leak assertions).
- **Waiting is `defer_compute_job`** through `_defer_job`: claim token, clamp to the SQL cap of 3600 s, a short class-and-counts reason, SQLSTATE 55006 yields DEFERRED, any other error propagates. A venue rate limit (RateLimitExceeded or DDoSProtection) anywhere in the OKX section defers at least 900 s and never reaches `_stamp_429` or `stamp_api_key_429`.
- **`calibrate` accepted** with the contract's per-mode key rules (`apply` stays refused). After every quarter is ready: `_download_quarter` (one fresh status read and one retry on 403/410), `_ingest_quarter` (download, then parse in a thread), `_census_of` (`aggregate_archive` in a thread). The per-quarter readings and the census go into Result shape v1; `reconcile`, `excluded`, `info`, `d03`, `strategies` and `fingerprint` stay null for plan 08.
- **Preflight shares the wait** (`TestPreflightSharesWait`): three claims with fresh fakes, deferring until both quarters read `finished`, then one v1 block with `href_scheme` and `href_host` set, ingest keys null, and zero downloader calls on any claim.

## Issues Encountered

None blocking. Two things the next plans should know:

- **Diagnostic gap for classification stops.** `non_usd_ccy` and `unknown_subtype_nonzero` stop the job with only `parse=<label>` in `last_error` and `census: null`: the aggregator raises before it returns a census, and its stop detail (which ccy, which subtype, which months) is deliberately not echoed (file-derived labels, and amounts must never leave the database object). RESEARCH Section 1 asked the non-USD stop to "carry the per-ccy census (counts and months, no amounts)". Plan 08 (or plan 14's runbook form) should decide where that goes in Result shape v1; this plan did not invent a key.
- **Unmeasured OKX behaviour (coverage D6).** Whether a re-apply of a `failed` request resets `ts`, and whether a status read straight after a re-apply can still say `failed`. If `ts` resets and OKX keeps failing, the cross-claim loop is bounded only by OKX's real generation time (at least 2 h per cycle, because the first-read rule applies to the new request). If a read straight after a re-apply can still show the stale `failed`, the job stops permanently when it could have waited; the operator re-enqueues. Both fail loud rather than guess.
