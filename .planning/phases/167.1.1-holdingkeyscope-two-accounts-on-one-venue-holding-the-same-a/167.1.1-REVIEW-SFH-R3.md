**Zero-priced-days branch.** Reachable only for a spot, non-fixed-mark holding that HAS rows, after `dailyMarkPrices` has validated every row. So:
- A malformed mark or `asof` still throws `HoldingMarkContractError` and then `HoldingCompareLoadError`; it is never turned into `no_price`.

**console.warn content.** The message carries `params.holding_ref` (already validated by `parseHoldingCompareId` against `/^[A-Za-z0-9_-]+$/` per segment, so no log injection) and `rows.length`.
