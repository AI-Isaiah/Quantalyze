### SFH-03 (data-integrity): a non-number mark or non-ISO asof is a contract violation, not a gap

**Commit:** `ce2072f57`
**How:** `dailyMarkPrices` now checks every row first. A mark that is not a finite number, or an `asof` that is not a `YYYY-MM-DD` string, throws `HoldingMarkContractError` (counts only, never a value). `fetchHoldingCompareItem` catches it, logs the holding ref and both counts via `console.error`, and throws `HoldingCompareLoadError` (same path as a bad `updated_at`). A finite mark at or below 0 stays a silent gap (the documented `0.0` failed-ticker sentinel); the existing sentinel tests are unchanged and a new fetch-level arm pins "negative mark: no throw, no log".

### SFH-01 (user-facing): that holding must not come back as null; it is withheld with a named note

**How:** `HoldingCompareItem` gains a third member `{ historyState: "withheld"; analytics: null; withheld_reason: "fixed_price" }`.
