<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Scope (founder, 2026-10-15, AskUserQuestion: "Close-out incl. /compare (Recommended)")
- **D-01:** 167.1.1 is a close-out phase with four parts:
  1. One test across the AUM, Open Positions and the 167.1 untrusted-key marker in which two keys
     hold one asset on one venue. It is observed RED with the scope key the phase was booked
     against (`holdingScopeKey` alone, no `api_key_id`), via a byte-backup neuter that is restored
     and `cmp`'d, then GREEN.
  2. The census of every consumer of `holdingScopeKey`, pinned by a test that enumerates them by
     symbol and fails when a new one appears unclassified. Each consumer is either re-keyed or
     shown not to need it. The 2026-10-15 reading: `queries.ts` is re-keyed per key. Three merge
     across keys on purpose: the commit route sums per ref over keys; `scenario-state.ts` builds a
     fingerprint over a set of tokens, matching the commit RPC's `DISTINCT ON`; and
     `holding-outcome-adapter.ts` builds the Bridge scope_ref, which `match.py`'s summed
     pseudo-strategy also uses.
  3. The D-02 rule built.
  4. The `/compare` per-holding metrics (D-03).

### Soft-disconnected keys (founder, 2026-10-15, AskUserQuestion: "Count, disclose (Recommended)")
- **D-02:** a soft-disconnected key's holdings stay IN the total. They are disclosed as "keys
  needing attention" the same way an untrusted key's are: the "needing attention" predicate is
  widened to cover a key with `disconnected_at` set, whatever its `sync_status`. This answers
  SC 3 and founder queue item 11's second question. It is the question 167.1's CONTEXT
  (`## Known limits`) and 167.1-REVIEW booked for the founder.
  - The rule matches the per-key reader's existing rule: every key the allocator owns counts,
    departed ones included (`src/lib/latest-holdings-per-key.ts` docblock). Anything not
    trusted goes into a disclosed part and is never hidden.
  - PROD on 2026-10-15 (read-only, marker = PRODUCTION): 13 soft-disconnected keys with sync
    status `complete` hold 146 `allocator_holdings` rows from the last 30 days, so the case is
    reachable today.

### /compare per-holding metrics (founder, 2026-10-15, AskUserQuestion: "Price-based, spot only (Recommended)")
- **D-03:** each holding's return, Sharpe, max drawdown and vol are computed from that holding's
  daily PRICE change (mark price day over day), never from its dollar value. That way buying,
  selling or moving more of an asset is not read as a gain or loss.
  - Spot holdings go `"ready"` once that source exists.
  - Derivative holdings stay `"rebuilding"` until a signed basis is defined.
  - A test fails on the dollar-ratio read: the value-level ratio over
    `allocator_equity_snapshots.breakdown` in `reconstructAndAnalyze`
    (`src/app/(dashboard)/compare/lib/holding-compare-adapter.ts`).
  - PROD on 2026-10-15: 26 of 36 spot holdings (per key and asset) have at least 30 priced days.
    For derivatives it is 141 of 2025.

### Founder decisions after review (2026-10-15, AskUserQuestion)
- **D-04 (founder, "Keep excluded, named (Recommended)"):** in the composer's book-mode AUM a
  soft-disconnected key's dollars stay OUT of the modelled AUM and are named "Excludes $Y from
  keys needing attention". On Holdings and Open Positions they are summed into the total and
  chipped "Key disconnected". This settles the WR-03 reading below; no summed key set moves.
- **D-05 (founder, "Yes, named, no metrics (Recommended)"):** on `/compare` an owned spot holding
  whose recorded mark is not a market price gets NO metrics and a named note: MT5
  (`account_balance`), stablecoins and sFOX (`fixed_price`), and an asset with no priced day
  (`no_price`). This settles the MT5 and CR-01 readings below.
- **D-06 (founder, "Book a follow-up phase (Recommended)"):** the Bridge matcher's
  `reconstruct_symbol_returns` (analytics-service/routers/match.py), which still builds
  per-holding returns as dollar ratios, is fixed in a follow-up phase booked by the orchestrator
  via `/gsd-phase --insert`. Tracked in TODOS.md as `167.1.1-BRIDGE-DOLLAR-RATIO-RETURNS`.

### Claude's Discretion
- (Orchestrator reading of D-02, NOT a founder decision.) "Disclosed like untrusted keys" applies
  to the money surfaces that carry the 167.1 disclosure: the composer's live-holdings summary
  (`summarizeLiveHoldings`), Holdings and Open Positions. The key-management flows in
  `ApiKeyManager.tsx` (rotate and retire success checks) and the `/strategies` key pill keep
  `isUntrustedKeySyncStatus` unchanged, because their question is the key's sync status, not
  whether its dollars need attention. The planner must name the predicate's new form and every
  call site it changes or deliberately leaves alone.
- (Orchestrator reading, 2026-10-15, review WR-03; ⭐ SETTLED by founder decision D-04.) D-02's "stay IN the total" is met as follows. On Holdings and Open Positions the
  soft-disconnected key's dollars are summed into the total and chipped. In the composer's
  book-mode AUM they are NOT summed: a soft-disconnected key is never in the contributing set
  (`disconnected_at` is non-null, so it is not eligible), so its dollars are excluded from the
  AUM and disclosed as "Excludes $Y from keys needing attention". They are in the composer total
  only in the degrade branch (empty contributing set), which production does not reach. This is
  the 167.1 D-20 narrowing keeping the AUM in agreement with the modelled book; no summed key set
  moved.
- The copy wording for a disconnected key's chip and clause follows DESIGN.md and the 167.1
  UI-SPEC curated copy.
- D-03's price source: per holding and per day, from the `allocator_holdings` rows'
  `mark_price` (one key per account per day, as for dollars). Where a day carries no price, it
  is a gap, never a zero. With too few priced days, the metrics are null and never invented.
  Annualization follows the repo rule: crypto 365.
- (Orchestrator reading, 2026-10-15, after RESEARCH.md; ⭐ SETTLED by founder decision D-05.) MT5 account rows under D-03. Their `symbol` is `ACCOUNT-<hex>`
  and their `mark_price` is 1.0 or a native close, so they carry no asset price. A price-based
  return there would print a confident 0%, which is an invented number. The repo rule says
  "never invent data: uncomputable metrics are null". So a spot holding on a venue that
  `isCryptoExchange` does not class as crypto gets no metrics: it shows the "not available"
  note, never numbers. Every crypto spot holding goes ready as D-03 says. If the founder wants
  non-crypto spot handled differently, that is a follow-up.
  ⛔ **CORRECTED 2026-10-15 (review CR-01, M-R2-01, M-R2-02; orchestrator; ⭐ SETTLED by
  founder decision D-05).** The sentence "Every crypto spot holding goes ready" is
  superseded, and so is "shows the 'not available' note". Review CR-01 found that the writer
  also stamps a constant mark of 1.0 on stablecoin symbols and on every sFOX row, so a price
  return there would be an invented 0% too. A spot holding goes ready only when its recorded
  mark is a market price. Otherwise the owned holding comes back as a WITHHELD item with a
  named reason, never as null (null keeps meaning unowned or nonexistent, D-15):
  - `fixed_price`: a stablecoin symbol or sFOX.
  - `account_balance`: a non-crypto venue such as MT5.
  - `no_price`: rows exist but no day carries a valid mark.
  The card names that reason. The text above is kept as lineage.
- (Orchestrator reading, from RESEARCH.md.) A failed ticker is written as `mark_price = 0.0`. A
  mark that is not finite and greater than 0 is a gap, never a price.
- (Orchestrator, from RESEARCH.md.) The test-only `historyState` seam on
  `fetchHoldingCompareItem` is removed together with the global `HOLDING_COMPARE_HISTORY_STATE`.
  The ready/withheld decision becomes per holding type (and per venue class, as above).
