## 3. Cycle 1b — a MEASUREMENT, not a required RED: clause 2 is subsumed by clause 4

While building the fixture rows it became visible that the `version_name` row the ledger really
stores (`version=<ts> name=<desc>`, the "old rows" shape at script :39-40) **also satisfies the
desc-only clause** (`m.name = substr(r.fname, strpos(r.fname,'_')+1)` — `m.name` *is* the
description). So for every underscore-free `version`, clause 2 implies clause 4, and removing
clause 2 cannot change the gate's verdict on any honest row. The script header's line :44-47
("BOTH clauses are required; neither is redundant, and dropping either reproduces a known-false
verdict") was written when only two clauses existed and is stale for clause 2 now that four do.

subsumption structure** (`EXPECTED_SPECIFICITY`) so it cannot rot silently, and this cycle
measures it.

direction only. sha256 under the neuter: `e50e1d50eeb5096094bbc57c3e0a292091f48e329653d5e75f139ef131d75db3`.
