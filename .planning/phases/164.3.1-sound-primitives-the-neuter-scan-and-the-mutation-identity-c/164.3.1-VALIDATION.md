## Anti-Vacuity Contract (this phase's own subject matter)

**Every gate this phase touches or ships must be observed RED under a neuter and GREEN under
restore.** This is not a style preference here — it is success criterion 1, and it is recursive:
a corpus entry that cannot fail is itself a Primitive-D instance.

Restores are verified by `shasum`, not by assumption. Byte backups go stale mid-edit — re-take
after every edit, and confirm the restore by grepping for the newest fix, not by hash alone.
Never use `git checkout --` in a neuter/restore harness: it restores to HEAD and silently
destroys uncommitted work.
