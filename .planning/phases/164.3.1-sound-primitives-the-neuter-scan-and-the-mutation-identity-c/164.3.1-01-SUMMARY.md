## Decisions Made

- **Descend into dollar-quoted bodies, expose `depth`.** All 104 corpus raises live inside a single `DO $$ … $$;` block spanning lines 207-2599. A tokenizer emitting only top-level statements would have made the neuter scan see the file as a handful of units with the RAISE buried inside one, i.e. completely broken. Emission is pre-order so siblings at any depth stay in source order and consumers walk them by index.
- **`isBranchHead` reads the tokenizer's `head` mark** rather than re-deciding. The classification lives in exactly one place; the predicate is a view of it.
- **A branch head must BEGIN its line.** This is the plan's stated rule ("including one sharing a line with the head, on either side of it") and it is what actually closes P3: the head `IF NOT ok THEN` is real, but `SET ROLE postgres;` shares its line before it, and the rewrite is line-oriented — it comments whole lines, splices `NULL;` at a line's indent, and addresses every diagnostic by line. Accepting a head whose line begins with something else means trusting a coincidence, and all four rounds of this defect were boundary disagreements read as agreement. Measured: 0 refusals added on the real corpus.
- **`failureBranches` requires a RAISE in the masking projection.** The reader it replaces walked back over the whole file for any `RAISE EXCEPTION` line, so a bare `TEST FAILED (` literal produced a branch anchored on an unrelated raise hundreds of lines earlier. A `TEST FAILED (` that is not raised is decidable at parse time (GRAMMAR rule 3a) and at runtime (the identity nonce, 3c) — the two places it can be decided.
- **Deleted rather than kept:** `IGNORABLE_LINE`, `neuterArm`'s local `isComment`, `statementEndLine`, both quote walkers, and the splice's own `^[ \t]*--` comment test. Each had become either unreachable or a *second* definition of what a comment is — and a second definition composing with the first is [VAC04-C1]'s exact shape.

**3. [Rule 1 — Bug, in the plan's own test] The R3 residual shapes needed splitting by what encloses them.**
- **Found during:** Task 2.
- **Issue:** the plan asks the three R3 residual multi-line shapes to "classify correctly", but the correct outcome differs by enclosure.
