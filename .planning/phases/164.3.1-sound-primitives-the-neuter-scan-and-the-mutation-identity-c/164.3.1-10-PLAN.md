<objective>
Extend the absurdity floor to the mutation runner (ROADMAP deliverable 9): the runner can today
report arm counts it did not execute — armsExecuted is a single tally incremented in the verdict
loop, and nothing compares it to what actually ran. The mirror of VAC-08's 253-of-262: a gate
holding every number needed to know its own verdict was absurd, and never comparing them.

The fix is an internal cross-check between two INDEPENDENT tallies: the verdict loop's
armsExecuted and a count of arm-scoped lane invocations recorded inside the lane runner itself,
plus the arithmetic invariant biting <= armsExecuted. Printed every run, asserted in-process as a
MEASURE_FAIL defect, and re-asserted by the sql-mutation CI job's count-recheck step (the
established anti-parse-only pattern: the step already reads the floors from run.mjs and fails
loud when a printed line is missing — this plan extends that step, never forks it).

Purpose: SC-8 (the floor exists on the mutation runner, proven fires AND silent), SC-7 (a firing
floor prints all its numbers), SC-9 (thresholds from plan 09's committed record, measurements
beside every rule).
Output: the cross-check in run.mjs, both-direction proofs, and the extended CI assertion.

⚠️ Anchor discipline: run.mjs was rewritten by plans 01/05 — locate by SYMBOL (runLane,
armsExecuted, the summary print block, the floor block). ci.yml's sql-mutation count-recheck step
is located by its step name ("Assert the run PRINTED its coverage and cleared both floors" at
pre-phase HEAD b4bdfc1e) — this plan is ci.yml's FIRST editor in the phase, so its current
anchors hold; re-grep regardless.

⛔ Safety: only the pg-lane throwaway cluster is written to. No supabase CLI commands.
</objective>
