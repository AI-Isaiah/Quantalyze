<objective>
Close `204-SENTRY-RAW-SUPABASE-ERRORS` at the capture boundary (ROADMAP SC1): `scrubCaptureInput` in `src/lib/sentry-capture.ts` renders every non-Error object, at the top level and anywhere in an Error's cause chain, through a new capture-mode renderer in `src/lib/seam-redaction.ts`. That renderer reads only `name`, `code`, `message`, `errno`, `syscall`, and renders an object with none of them as a fixed placeholder. The shared log renderer `scrubSeamError` stays as it is (D-03).

Purpose: a non-throwing PostgREST error is a plain object whose `details`/`hint` can carry a failing row's values (requester data). Today it goes to Sentry, a third party, through `scrubSeamError`, which removes known secrets but not row values. Fixing the chokepoint covers every caller of `captureToSentry` at once.

Output: `scrubSeamErrorForCapture` export plus two module-private constants in `seam-redaction.ts`; both render sites in `sentry-capture.ts` switched to it; a `[209 SC1]` describe in `sentry-capture.test.ts`; a direct capture-vs-log describe and the moved export-set pin in `seam-redaction.test.ts`. Each fix has a neuter-to-RED proof recorded in the SUMMARY (SC5).
</objective>
