    ⛔ **CORRECTED by D-21 B1 (2026-09-26); the sentence above is kept as lineage.** 20 + 5 + 1 is 26,

    `stdDev(diff, true) * Math.sqrt(periodsPerYear)`), so it moves to `sharpe(diff, { periodsPerYear,

### Plan-check round 4 (2026-09-26)

- **D-21 (2026-09-26, planner, autonomous, applying the round-4 checker findings; reversible per plan).**
  - **B1, the whole-tree matcher is widened and re-pinned from a MEASUREMENT (plan 07).** The checker
    re-ran plan 07's exact regexes at HEAD: 25 hits, 19 in-class plus 6 Sortino / Treynor, while the plan
    claimed "20 in-class + 5 + 1" (which sums to 26). The missing hit is `build-payload.ts`
    `pearsonCorr`, whose two-step form `const denom = Math.sqrt(va * vb); ... cov / denom` (the reference
    form `return-stats` `pearson` adopts) escaped the Pearson product regex (`var?\w*` needs operands
    starting `va`/`var`). Changes: (a) the product form is now `Math.sqrt(ID * ID)` with identifier-start
    operands, which catches the two-step `denom` and still leaves peer-cohort's `Math.sqrt(9 * d)`
    unmatched; (b) two unparenthesised Sharpe forms are added, `m / s * Math.sqrt(N)` and
    `Math.sqrt(N) * m / s`; (c) each new form has its own liveness fixture, plus negative fixtures for the
    numeric-operand root and `Math.sqrt(N) * sd / 100`; (d) the pin is re-measured with the revised
    regexes at `df4d9921d`: **26 hits = 20 in-class (SHARPE 10, PEARSON 7, BETA 3) + 5 Sortino + 1
    Treynor**, the two new Sharpe forms matching nothing and 0 false positives, broken down by file and
    form in plan 07's context. The count is of hits, not T-labels (T5, T10, T12, T18 have no ratio shape;
    one site can yield several hits). The executor re-measures at the merge-base (whose `src/` equals
    `df4d9921d`'s) and stops on any difference. The widened product form flags any future product of two
    identifiers under a root; a legitimate one gets a reasoned, count-pinned allowlist entry.
  - **W1, the SQN residual (plans 02 and 08).** Measured (see the D-16 addendum): no production path
    computes SQN since Phase 106, and both success writers NULL `trade_metrics`. Plan 02 Task 1 gains Q6,
    a founder read-only count, counts only, of rows with a stored |sqn| above 1e10 and of rows still
    carrying a legacy `trade_metrics` object, split published x in-recompute-set, with a CASE-guarded
    cast. Two consequences are now explicit: a plan 02 recompute NULLs the row's whole `trade_metrics`,
    which clears a stored residue SQN AND empties that row's trade panel (shown to the founder at the Task

    venue never recomputes on its own. Plan 08's CHANGELOG moves SQN out of "Changed" (no displayed SQN
    changes by itself) into a Notes bullet carrying the Q6 counts.
