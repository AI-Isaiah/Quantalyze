So the copy stays byte-verbatim and the guard is narrowed by a per-code, per-fragment exemption, `FORBIDDEN_FRAGMENT_SUBSTANTIATED_FOR = { KEY_MT5_SERVER_UNKNOWN: ["been notified"] }`, with its reason in a docblock. A new test pins the capture the claim rests on (the miss arm must still call `_capture_server_unknown_once(`) and pins the exemption map's keys, so deleting the capture reddens, rather than the copy lying quietly. Rewording to a synonym was rejected: it would keep the claim and dodge the guard.
