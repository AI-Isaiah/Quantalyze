## Sampling Rate

- **After every task commit:** the new test file, plus `tests/test_mt5_read.py`.
- **After every plan wave:** the full MT5 slice and the wizard vitest suites.
- **Before `/gsd-verify-work`:** the full slice, `make typecheck` (mypy) and the wizard suites must
  be green. CI is the final judge: bind green to the head SHA and the expected check count.
- **Max feedback latency:** ~180 s.
