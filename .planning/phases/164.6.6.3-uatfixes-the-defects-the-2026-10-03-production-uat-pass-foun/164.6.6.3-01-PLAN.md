<objective>
Item 0, derive half. A fresh MT5 login on the jobs terminal waits, bounded and failing loud, until
its deal history has settled, and only then hands it to the combine.

This phase is split A of the original 164.6.6.3 list (D-01: this phase delivers items 0 and 9 only;
D-02: it runs before .1, .2 and .3). Under D-12 it has no UI-SPEC and no AI-SPEC.

Purpose: SC1 and SC2. Today `read_mt5_deal_ledger` reads `history_deals_get` about one second after
a new account's authorization, before MT5 has downloaded the history. The derive then stamps a
funded account permanently with "<2 usable daily-return days" (`164.6.6-CONTEXT.md`
`## Live verification 2026-10-04`, Finding C: measured twice, with about 62k and 141k USD of
equity).

Decisions this plan implements:
- D-04: poll until the deal count is stable across consecutive reads; a cached account pays no wait.
- D-05: about 30 s of wait inside an outer bound derived from symbols, raised for this call only.
  It never reaches the 40 s bound's terminal-restart arm.
- D-06 and D-13: an expiry under material equity is `transient`, with its own message and its own
  ERROR line. It is never permanent and adds no new DB `error_kind`.
- D-15: freshness is decided per (terminal, key) by a settled record, before `login()`.
- D-16: this plan builds the helper both callers share and wires the derive caller. Plan 02 wires
  the backfill caller.

Output: the settle loop in `services/mt5_read.py`, the settled record in `services/mt5_client.py`,
the two constants in `services/mt5_concurrency.py`, the derive caller's wiring and its sixth
`except` arm in `services/job_worker.py`, and failing-first tests.

visible and recorded here. The edges come from RESEARCH section 0.3 and Pitfalls 1 to 3.
