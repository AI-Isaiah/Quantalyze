On every dedup hit, at both return sites (the optimistic look-up and the lost-race re-read) of both overloads of `_enqueue_compute_job_internal`, a new SECURITY DEFINER helper `public._record_enqueue_dedup`:
- records the caller's `p_metadata` in the new side table `public.compute_job_dedup_events` (D11-1);
