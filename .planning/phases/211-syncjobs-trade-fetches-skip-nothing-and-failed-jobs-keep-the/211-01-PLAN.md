<objective>
Close the user-triggered half of TODOS `167.1.2.2.2-SYNCTRADES-LASTSYNC-SEED` (ROADMAP SC-1). `POST /fetch-trades` (`fetch_trades` in `analytics-service/routers/exchange.py`) stops taking its resume point from `api_keys.last_sync_at`, which the daily holdings poll overwrites for every active key. It seeds from the strategy's stored high-water instead: the newest `trades` row with `is_fill = false`, floored to 00:00 UTC, or `None` (full reach) when there is none or the read failed. The rule has ONE definition: `_high_water_floor_iso` and `_load_strategy_high_waters` move out of `routers/cron.py` into a new leaf module `analytics-service/services/trade_resume.py`, and the cron imports the loader back under its old name.

Purpose: a fresh `last_sync_at` written by the poll today makes a user's fetch walk only `[last_sync_at, now]`, so a gap in stored days is never re-read. The cron was fixed in Phase 167.1.2.2.2; the route was left on the old seed.

Output: `services/trade_resume.py`; `cron.py` importing it; `fetch_trades` seeding from it; `tests/test_fetch_trades_resume_seed.py` with RED-first cases; neuter-to-RED proofs in the SUMMARY (SC-4). The queued `sync_trades` job is plan 04 (it edits `services/job_worker.py`, which plan 02 also edits).
</objective>
