<objective>
Bind the new SQL helper to the two runtimes that share its marker set, and bring every comment that describes the old dedup gap up to date (ROADMAP SC-2 and SC-4; CONTEXT D11-4, D11-7, D11-9, D11-12).

Purpose: three copies of the refresh-marker set (Python, TypeScript, SQL) must not drift. The repo already pins two SQL copies, in `retract_ledger_refresh_marker` and `sync_strategy_analytics_status`. The new helper `_record_enqueue_dedup` adds a third SQL list. Without a parity case, a marker added to one side alone leaves a Sync either unretracted or protected by a marker nothing else honours. The comments matter too: four files tell the next reader that `enqueue_compute_job` discards the caller's metadata and that D-02 is open. After plan 03 that is false for any caller that sends `correlation_id`.

Output:
- parity cases in `tests/test_compute_job_metadata_merge_parity.py` and `src/lib/ledger-refresh-marker.test.ts`, each shown RED by a byte-restored edit of the snapshot;
- dated comment updates in `long_fetch.py`, `job_worker.py`, `src/lib/ledger-refresh-marker.ts` and the `_Queue` double.
No production behaviour changes in this plan.
</objective>
