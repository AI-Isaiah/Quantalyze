site in analytics-service: the exact-zero guards S1-S7. The SQN block S8 (D-16) is plan `166.1-01b`

deviation of about 1e-16 when it is derived from a compounding NAV, never exactly 0. Every site that

portfolio optimizer, a "Daily Sharpe 2296215230173376.50" in a CSV upload error, a 48.6% / 51.4%
split of a zero risk. With plan 01b (the SQN block) this closes success criterion 3 for the variance
class.

</objective>
