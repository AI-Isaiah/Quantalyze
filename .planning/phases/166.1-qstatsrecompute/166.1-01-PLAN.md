<objective>
Carry Phase 166's relative dispersion floor to every remaining ratio-over-standard-deviation site in
analytics-service: the exact-zero variance guards (S1-S7) and the unguarded correlation sites (C1-C8).

Purpose: a series that does not vary (a stablecoin-lending yield, a paused strategy) has a standard
deviation of about 1e-16 when it is derived from a compounding NAV, never exactly 0. Every site that
tests `== 0` then divides by that residue and shows a fabricated number: a Sharpe of 1.28e13 in the
portfolio optimizer, a "Daily Sharpe 2296215230173376.50" in a CSV upload error, a 48.6% / 51.4%
split of a zero risk, a -0.036 correlation. This closes success criterion 3 and the class D-02 folds in.
Output: `services/dispersion.py`, the edited sites, two test files and a shared fixture module.
</objective>
