### WR-03 / SFH MEDIUM-1: two meanings of "average pairwise correlation"; the KPI does not say it covers a subset

**Commit:** `ae8619c8f`

**Rule chosen (recorded in CONTEXT):** skip the undefined pairs, average the defined ones, and state the count of pairs used.
- `dispersion.average_pairwise_correlation(frame)` returns `(mean, pairs_used, pairs_total)` and is the one implementation.
- `_avg_corr` reads its mean.
- `compute_avg_pairwise_correlation_with_pairs` applies the same rule to the C1 matrix. The old `compute_avg_pairwise_correlation` is kept as a wrapper.
- The router records `avg_pairwise_correlation_pairs_used` and `avg_pairwise_correlation_pairs_total` in the `data_quality` JSONB beside the KPI, which needs no migration.
- **The guard the rule needs:** with a flat leg skipped, a scorer whose added or swapped leg is flat would compare two averages over the same pairs and report 0.0. `_leg_is_flat` makes that delta None in `simulate_add_candidate`, `find_replacement_candidates` (incumbent or candidate) and `match_engine._compute_portfolio_fit_components`. Without the guard, match_engine would have regressed from None to 0.0.

**RED evidence:**
- The C4 invariant, re-pinned to the skip rule, failed 11 cases with the all-or-nothing gate restored.
- With the leg guard disabled, 7 cases failed: the match_engine case and the six simulator and bridge correlation cases.
- The router's pair count failed with every pair counted as used.
