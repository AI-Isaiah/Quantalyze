<objective>
DECISION B, the tracer: `sql-tests` moves off the shared advisory key, alone, onto the
ephemeral lane — and the one part of that job that is inherently ABOUT shared TEST moves into
its own job rather than being deleted or dragged along.

Purpose: CONTEXT Area B — `sql-tests` has the worst measured ratio (8.02 min of waiting to do
0.78 min of work on a typical 18.2-min run) and the most self-contained workload. ⛔ All-or-
nothing is explicitly rejected: with one job moved the mutex still exists and the remaining
holders still serialise, which is what makes the tracer's win MEASURABLE IN ISOLATION against
the same key — and that is what makes Area E falsifiable.

⚠️ THE PART RESEARCH DID NOT COVER, MEASURED WHILE PLANNING: `sql-tests` also carries the
**VAC-08** step (`bash scripts/test-ledger-drift-check.sh`), which compares this repo against
SHARED TEST's migration ledger and function bodies. It cannot follow the corpus onto an
ephemeral lane — an ephemeral database has no drift to measure — and CLAUDE.md records that
VAC-08 exits 1 when its credential is absent and never skips. So this plan SPLITS the job
rather than moving it: the corpus goes to the lane, the drift check stays on shared TEST in a
job of its own.

Output: two jobs where there was one, and one fewer holder of advisory key `61616158`.
</objective>
