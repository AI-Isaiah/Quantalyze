### Finding that contradicts a plan premise (fail loud)

The plan's backstop truth says the first post-deploy `match_batches` row shows personalized
`portfolio_fit` values that differ from pre-deploy. On current PROD data it cannot: all 8 ranked
personalized rows already read `portfolio_fit = 0`, and the 3 personalized allocators have 5 portfolios
with **0** portfolio strategies (0 across PROD), so there is no book to read through the boundary. The
stored zeros mean "no book", not "levels read as returns". The post-deploy reading is therefore expected
to show unchanged personalized and screening rows; that is a no-regression check, not evidence the fix
moved a stored score. The real before/after evidence is the replay table. The verifier should restate or
retire that backstop truth. The post-deploy section is PENDING as instructed.

Other measurements recorded: `_retention_sweep` keeps the newest 7 batches per allocator (no age-based
pruning); personalized batches are not written every hour (latest dated 10:00, 13:00, 14:00 UTC against
screening up to 19:00 UTC); 20 of 22 sample rows have no `cumulative_method` stamp (written before plan 02)
and none carries a `daily_returns` array or a `native_unit`.
