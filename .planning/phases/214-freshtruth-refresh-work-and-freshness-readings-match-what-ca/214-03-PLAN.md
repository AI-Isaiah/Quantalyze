<objective>
Cap, never exclude, a strategy whose completed refresh leaves `last_return_date` unmoved (ROADMAP SC-2, first half). The single-key fan-out's attempt cooldown goes from 20 hours to 7 days for it.

Decision this plan implements: CONTEXT "SC-2 — fan-out treatment of a strategy whose refresh cannot advance" (founder, Q1 = A):
- weekly cap, never exclude;
- trigger on the generic signal, not the dust flag: a completed derive + compute run happened after the strategy went stale, and `last_return_date` has stayed unmoved for 3 or more days;
- the throttle lives in the candidate cooldown, never in the staleness view.

The signal, from RESEARCH "SC-2 design": `lrs.stale_reason = 'series_behind'` AND `(CURRENT_DATE - lrs.last_return_date) >= 8` AND a `done` `compute_analytics_from_csv` job created on or after `last_return_date + 5`. The view reads stale from age 5, so age 8 is "unmoved 3 or more days after going stale". The chain tail is the completion marker because a derive can finish while its compute fails. It is OR-ed into the existing marks-paused `WHEN`, after the `EXISTS (...)` close paren (PATTERNS correction), so `THEN INTERVAL '7 days'` stays a single occurrence.

The skip counting half of SC-2 is plan 06. That keeps the cap and the counter in separate, reviewable commits on the same migration file.

Purpose: one idle MT5 account is re-derived every day for nothing. Each daily derive is a worker job and an analytics run that cannot change the answer.

Output: the re-based fan-out migration, its rollback, four new arms with twins, every existing twin re-pointed, the static-gate pointer move and the regenerated snapshot.
</objective>
