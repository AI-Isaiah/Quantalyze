# Phase 164.6.6 Plan 06: Park the validation terminal on the house account after every probe Summary

Both MT5 validate sites now log the validation terminal back into the house account after a probe. This happens inside the same lease and before the client is released. A default-deny gate stops the park whenever the probe thread may still be on the wire (W-1), the lease fence fired, the account was switched mid-probe, or less than `_MT5_PARK_MIN_BUDGET_S` of the deadline is left. Each such skip logs ERROR and alerts with a named cause. Per the founder's 2026-10-03 decision, a park that fails on a bridge glitch logs WARNING instead.

## Mode and precondition

- `164.6.6-CONTEXT.md` holds exactly one line `H3-MASTER-PASSWORD-MODE: park-after-login`, so the plan ran in that mode.
- S-01 records the validation terminal as authorized for the house account.
- ⚠️ **One part of the precondition is NOT met.** The parenthetical "both terminals on the house account at once" was never observed. The founder accepted it as an unmeasured premise (CONTEXT D-07 part 2). No test here depends on it: the fakes model one terminal only.
- **Open live check, `human_needed`, for plan 08 or verification:** after deploy, observe a park while the job terminal is on the house account. Confirm that both terminals stay authorized as house and that the park does not log the job terminal out.

## What changed

**`services/mt5_probe.py`**
- **`park_on_house_account(client, *, house, site) -> bool`** runs `client.login(*house)`. On success it restamps the holder `house` through `_note_terminal_holder`, whose epoch guard refuses the stamp from a thread that outlived its lease. It never raises `Exception`.
- **`report_park_skipped(cause, *, site)`** reports a skip.
- **One alert home.** Both functions alert through one private helper:
  - ERROR on every occurrence, naming the cause and the site;
  - at most one Sentry capture per cause per `_PARK_ALERT_WINDOW_S` (3600 s), tag `mt5_validation_park_failed`;
  - the Sentry calls are inside `try/except`.
- **Closed cause set `_PARK_ALERT_CAUSES`:**
  - park-attempt causes: `house_credentials_unset`, `login_refused`, `client_error`, `session_abandoned`, `ceiling`, `bridge_glitch`;
  - gate-skip causes: `probe_in_flight`, `probe_session_abandoned`, `probe_account_mismatch`, `budget_exhausted`;
  - fallback: `unrecognised_cause`. An unknown cause is never interpolated into alert text.
- **The leaf invariant holds.** The only in-tree imports are `services.mt5_client` and `services.mt5_validation`. The house triple and the site are parameters.

**`routers/exchange.py::_validate_mt5_key_probe`**
- Three gate facts sit in function scope beside `client`, and `_connect_and_probe` writes them via `nonlocal`:
  - `login_attempted`, set just before the probe's `to_thread`;
  - `probe_thread_done`, a `threading.Event` the probe thread sets in its own `finally`;
  - `probe_disqualified`, set on the `Mt5SessionAbandoned` and `Mt5AccountMismatchError` arms.
- The deadline start is stamped from `_park_clock` (a seam over `time.monotonic`).
- **`_MT5_PARK_MIN_BUDGET_S: Final[float] = _MT5_VALIDATE_STAGE_TIMEOUT_S`.** The comment above it gives the derivation, the cost at defaults (a connect plus probe longer than 15 s skips the park as `budget_exhausted`) and the two-round-trip residual.
- **Order of checks, inside the lease and before the release:**
  1. in flight;
  2. disqualified;
  3. budget below the floor;
  4. otherwise park via `to_thread`, under `wait_for(timeout=_MT5_PARK_MIN_BUDGET_S)`. A timeout reports `ceiling`.
- The whole park is wrapped in `except Exception`, so it can never replace the verdict on its way out.
- Nothing was added to the 105 s client-budget sum.

**`services/ingestion/mt5.py::Mt5Adapter.validate`**
- The same three facts, as plain locals.
- The park runs before the close and is bounded by `_MT5_PROBE_TIMEOUT_S`. The sequential worker has no client budget, so there is no floor here, and a comment says so.

**`tests/test_mt5_validate.py`**
- The D-15 bridge-blip test sets fabricated house credentials. That is the only edit this plan made to the file. The coordinator lifted the no-edit rule after plan 05 merged.

## Deviations from Plan

### Founder decision

**1. [Rule 4 - founder decision] A park that fails on a bridge glitch logs WARNING, and everything else pages**
- **Found during:** Task 1 verify.
- **Issue:** The plan requires ERROR on every park failure, including unset house credentials. But `test_d15_the_loud_check_never_false_alarms_on_a_bridge_blip` asserts nothing above WARNING on a bridge blip, and no house credentials are set in the test process. All 5 D-15 cases went red, and that file could not be edited in the wave.
- **Measured:**
  - Exporting house credentials made D-15 green, but 13 other `test_mt5_validate.py` tests went red. 11 of them read "login called once, called 2 times", so a `conftest.py` default was rejected.
  - In production, with credentials set, a park after a blip logs in over the same failing bridge, and a failure there would have paged.
- **The founder's decision, verbatim (AskUserQuestion, 2026-10-03):** "Warn on glitch, page else (Rec.)". What the founder was shown: "A park that fails for the same bridge-glitch reason the validation hit logs a WARNING only, which respects D-15. Every other skip or failure still pages: house credentials unset, the account refused, the stage or deadline exits, a fenced probe. This adds one small branch and its tests."
- **Fix:**
  - `_is_park_bridge_glitch` reuses the validate site's own WARNING chain and invents no class. On a match, the park logs WARNING with cause `bridge_glitch`.
  - **Sentry choice:** no capture on the glitch WARNING. The D-15 blip path logs WARNING and captures nothing, and so does the validate site's matching arm.
  - An IPC transport fault stays ERROR, as at the validate site.
  - The D-15 test sets house credentials (option a).
  - The coordinator records the decision in CONTEXT and the ROADMAP; this plan did not edit them.
- **Files modified:** `services/mt5_probe.py`, `tests/test_mt5_validation_park.py`, `tests/test_mt5_validate.py`.
- **Commit:** 4c1cfd071.

### Auto-fixed Issues

**2. [Rule 1 - Bug] Refusal is detected through `Mt5LoginRefusedError`, not a falsy return**
- The plan said "on a falsy result". `Mt5Client.login` returns None and raises on every failure, so reading a falsy return as refusal would have failed every park.

**3. [Rule 2 - Critical] Fallback cause `unrecognised_cause` added to the closed set**
- The plan requires a fixed fallback member, so nothing outside the set is ever interpolated into alert text.

**4. [Rule 3 - Blocking] `_park_clock` seam added in exchange.py**
- It lets the floor boundary be tested exactly. Patching the process-wide `time.monotonic` would break the event loop.

**5. [Rule 2 - Critical] Release and close moved into a `finally` under the park**
- A cancelled park must never skip the bounded release or close.
- The park is also wrapped in `except Exception`.

**6. Task 2's file list named `exchange.py`, but no edit was needed there**
- The gate was finished in Task 1.

**7. [Rule 3] One existing test folded into a new one**
- `test_a_raising_park_login_changes_nothing_the_caller_sees` expected `client_error` for a transport raise on the park login. Under the founder decision that case is the glitch class, so it now lives in the glitch test, with its credential-leak assertion kept.
