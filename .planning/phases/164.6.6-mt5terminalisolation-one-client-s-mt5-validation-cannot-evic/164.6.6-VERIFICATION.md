### What the code does at HEAD

**`services/mt5_probe.py::park_on_house_account`**
- The function now takes `validation_hit_glitch: bool`. It is keyword-only with no default, so mypy strict rejects any call site that does not decide it.
- In the `Mt5ClientError` arm:
  - `is_bridge_glitch(err) and validation_hit_glitch` leads to `_warn_park_bridge_glitch`: a WARNING with no capture.
  - `is_bridge_glitch(err)` without the flag leads to `_alert_park_failed("bridge_glitch")`: ERROR plus capture.
  - A refused sign-in or any other client error still pages.
- `house is None` and the fenced or abandoned arms are reached before the flag is ever read, so they page whatever its value.
- The flag can only lower the glitch class.

**Wizard site, `routers/exchange.py::_validate_mt5_key_probe`**
- The flag starts as `False` in function scope and is listed in `_connect_and_probe`'s `nonlocal` line.
- It is set by `is_bridge_glitch(e)` on the C5 arm (line ~925). That arm is reached only after the IPC-fault branch has already raised, and it sits inside `_connect_and_probe`, not inside the threaded `_probe`.
- It is set to `True` on the D-15 "capability undetermined, signal unavailable" arm (line ~1126).
- It is passed to the park (line ~1244).

**Worker site, `services/ingestion/mt5.py::Mt5Adapter.validate`**
- The flag is declared in the method scope that contains both assignments. Neither assignment is inside `_probe`, so no `nonlocal` is needed and the assignment cannot silently bind a local.
- On the transient arm it is assigned from `is_bridge_glitch(e)`, not from the whole arm, because that arm also re-raises IPC faults and login-stage refusals.
- It is set to `True` on the worker's D-15 arm.
- It is passed to the park.

**Set once per validation.** `_connect_and_probe()` is awaited exactly once (line ~1018), and the worker probe sits in no retry loop. So a glitch on one attempt cannot carry `True` into a later clean attempt. Neither site has a retry path.
