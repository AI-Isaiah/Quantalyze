<objective>
Route both validate sites to the validation terminal (D-02 = option (b)), so an onboarding
validation can no longer switch the terminal that serves live jobs. This is the part of criterion 1
that D-01 scopes in: validation switches.

Purpose: the lease registry is already keyed by `terminal_key`, so a second terminal is a second
key with its own lock, epoch and occupancy stamp (RESEARCH "What 137 and 164.6.5 already give each
option"). The code change is small; the gates that prove it are the work.

Output: a shared validation-endpoint reader, both validate sites routed through it with their own
misconfiguration dispositions, a routing pin, and the founder setting the two env vars before the
change deploys.

Spec-less probe fallback skipped: phase has no requirement IDs to probe (visible skip). The
`requirements:` IDs above are RESEARCH's proposed IDs.
</objective>

- Detector: `pluralization` on "another" and "second". This plan makes the second terminal live.
- Primary noun: `terminal_key` (promoted long ago; the lease registry keys on it). Decision:
  `add-alongside` for the endpoint configuration, as recorded in plan 02: a second env pair beside
  `MT5_GATEWAY_HOST` / `MT5_GATEWAY_PORT`, read by exactly two sites.
- Accepted debt: `read_env_gateway_endpoint` and its four readers (session monitor, boot heal,
  `ipc_fault` recycle, prod-prober arm) stay singular, per D-06. A third terminal, or D-01 widened to
  every switch, forces a `promote` to a role-to-endpoint roster.
- Invariant test (the checkpoint's suggested companion, adopted): the routing pin in Task 2 reds the
  moment a job site reads the validation endpoint or a validate site reads the job endpoint.
</assumption_delta_decision>
