### What closes T-164.6.6-07

Either one of these closes it. The second needs a founder answer.

1. **Code.** Pass the validation's own outcome into the park, for example a flag set by the
   transient-upstream arm, and downgrade to WARNING only when both the validation and the park hit
   the glitch class. At both sites, have the outer `except Exception` call
   `report_park_skipped("unrecognised_cause", site=...)`, and add an `except asyncio.CancelledError`
   arm around the park await that reports and re-raises. Each change needs a failing-first test.
2. **Founder decision.** Put the broader reading (every glitch-class park failure warns, whatever the
   validation hit) to the founder as an explicit amendment, then log it under Accepted Risks.

The auditor cannot accept this residual on the founder's behalf.
