<objective>
Stop showing a return for a period longer than the record (SC6, the math half) and make the freshness
chip's date agree with its own subject (SC5, the chip half).

`compute()` builds MTD/YTD/3M/6M/1Y with a cutoff that never returns null, so a record shorter than
the window shows its whole-record return under the window's label (RESEARCH root cause E). The chip
switches its subject to "Track record" when the series is the staler fact, but still prints the
compute date under it (root cause D). This plan fixes both at their one source, and adds the 3Y / 5Y
windows to `compute()` so plan 169-05 can delete MetricsColumn's index-clamped `periodReturn` instead of
rewriting it (D-11). `MetricsColumn.tsx` is NOT touched here: it is in flight in 167.1.2 (D-02).

Purpose: every window either covers its period or reads "—", and the chip states one fact per line.
Output: the coverage helper in `compute.ts`, widened types, moved snapshots explained, the chip fix.
</objective>
