<objective>
Stop showing a return for a period longer than the record (SC6, the math half) and make the freshness
chip's date agree with its own subject (SC5, the chip half).

`compute()` builds MTD / YTD / 3M / 6M / 1Y with a cutoff that never returns null, so a record shorter
than the window shows its whole-record return under the window's label (RESEARCH root cause E,
re-verified 2026-09-27). The chip switches its subject to "Track record" when the series is the staler
fact, but still prints the compute date under it (root cause D). This plan fixes both at their one
source, and adds the 3Y / 5Y windows to `compute()` so plan 169-05 can delete MetricsColumn's
index-clamped look-back instead of rewriting it (D-11, D-02). `MetricsColumn.tsx` is NOT touched here.

Purpose: every window either covers its period or is null (the em-dash for MTD / YTD / 3M; 169-05 omits a null 6 Month / 1 Year / 3 Year / 5 Year row, D-17 and D-57), and the chip states one fact per line.
Output: the coverage helper in `compute.ts`, widened types, moved snapshots explained, the chip fix.
</objective>
