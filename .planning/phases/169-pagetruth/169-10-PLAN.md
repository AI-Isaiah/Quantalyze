<objective>
`/allocations` Open Positions shows a $0.42 entry or mark price as "$0" and a +$0.37 P&L as "+$0", and a
tiny negative as a red "−$0" (R2, founder UAT 2026-09-27, D-50). Root cause (RESEARCH R2, replicated):
OpenPositionsTable formats prices through a private whole-dollar formatter (a byte-level duplicate of
`dollar-validation.ts` `formatUsd`), and both tables carry a private `formatPnl` that picks its sign before
rounding to whole dollars. HoldingsTable renders its entry price through the shared whole-dollar
`formatUsd` and has the same `formatPnl`.

Purpose: a sub-dollar price and a sub-dollar P&L render with their real precision, and a value that rounds
to zero never shows a sign or a colour.
Output: two formatters and one rounded-sign decision in the one money module, both tables on them, the
DESIGN.md currency row, and the moved test literals.
</objective>
