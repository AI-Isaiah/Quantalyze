## MEASURED AT HEAD BY THE PLANNER — read before writing code

**1. ⭐ THE EXACT PRECEDENT FOR THIS MOVE IS ALREADY IN `job_worker.py`.** A comment above the
concurrency imports records Phase 151 doing this same thing: *"moved to services/mt5_concurrency.py
(leaf) so allocator_positions.py can share the ONE terminal registry without an import cycle;
re-imported here so existing call sites are unchanged."* Follow that shape exactly — a leaf module
plus a re-import, so no existing call site or test changes.

**2. What the closure needs, and where each piece lives today.**
- `services.mt5_client`: `Mt5Session`, `Mt5ClientError`, `Mt5AccountMismatchError`, `Mt5SessionAbandoned`
- `services.mt5_concurrency`: `_Mt5PostReadVerificationError` (and, staying at the caller,
  `mt5_terminal_lease`, `_MT5_DERIVE_READ_TIMEOUT_S`, `_mt5_bounded_restart`)
- `services.job_worker`: `_MT5_MAX_SERVER_UTC_OFFSET_S`, `_MT5_DEAL_FETCH_MARGIN_S`, and the module-level
  `assert` tying them together.

**3. ⛔ THE CYCLE HAZARD, and it is real.** The two margin constants live in `job_worker.py`. If the
new module imported them from there, and `job_worker` imported the new module, that is a cycle. They
must MOVE into `services/mt5_read.py` with their `assert`, and `job_worker.py` must RE-IMPORT them —
because `analytics-service/tests/test_mt5_derive_branch.py` reads them as `jw._MT5_DEAL_FETCH_MARGIN_S`
and `jw._MT5_MAX_SERVER_UTC_OFFSET_S` through the module alias, and that test must not need editing.
Cycle-safety of the rest is MEASURED: `mt5_concurrency` imports only `mt5_client`; `mt5_probe` imports
only `mt5_client` and `mt5_validation`. Neither imports `job_worker`.

**4. ⭐ `assert_expected_login` ALREADY EXISTS AS A SHARED SYMBOL and the closure is a duplicate of
it.** `services/mt5_probe.py` exports `assert_expected_login(info, *, login)` with the identical
STRICT-equality, fail-loud-on-missing-field, raises-`Mt5AccountMismatchError` contract, and a docblock
explaining why it is deliberately not an `Mt5ClientError`. There are currently THREE hand-written
copies of this bracket: that shared one, `job_worker`'s closure, and a third in
`services/allocator_positions.py`. The new helper must CALL the shared one rather than carry a fourth.

**5. ⛔ OUT OF SCOPE, surfaced not smuggled: `services/allocator_positions.py`.** It has its own
`_mt5_read` closure, but it reads `account_info()` ONLY — no deals, no POST bracket. It is a DIFFERENT
read and is not this helper's caller. Its duplicated login bracket could adopt `assert_expected_login`
too, but CONTEXT.md scopes this extraction to "the backfill job and its sibling". Do not convert it;
name the residual in the SUMMARY.

**6. The DISPOSITIONS stay at the caller.** `job_worker`'s derive branch answers each of the five
raisable exceptions differently — timeout restarts the terminal, `Mt5SessionAbandoned` deliberately
does NOT restart, mismatch restarts and never stamps, the POST-read blip is transient with no stamp,
and `Mt5ClientError` goes through `classify_mt5_login_error`. Plan 06's backfill job will answer some
of them DIFFERENTLY. ⛔ The helper must not decide any of that: it performs the read and lets its
exceptions out unchanged.
