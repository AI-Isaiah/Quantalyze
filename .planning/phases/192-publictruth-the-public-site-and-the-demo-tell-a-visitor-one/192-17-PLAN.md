## Decision notes (recorded for the founder)

- **Its own plan, not folded into 192-13 or 192-15 (revision 1).** The checker offered either plan. Plan 192-13 already deletes fourteen files across three tasks, and 192-15 was the plan the checker found oversized. The seam censuses are a separate topic from the portfolio components, so they get their own plan. It runs in wave 5 because `vitest.node-env.ts` is also edited by 192-13 (wave 4), and same-wave plans may not share a file.
- **One commit for the deletion and every census, by necessity.** Measured during planning (2026-11-07): deleting the two files alone turned three cases red, "excluded path … still exists on disk" and "excluded path … does not enter the resilience core" in seam-budgets, and the eslint wiring case in contracts-registry. Removing the rows without moving the pins reds the set equality, the exactly-3 fence and the frozen-at-four pin. So Task 1 touches more than five files, because a split would leave CI red between commits. The working copy was restored from a byte backup (`cmp` silent).
- **The cron warmer stays.** `/api/cron/warm-analytics` is a generic daily `/health` ping with its own `vercel.json` schedule and critical-regressions pins. It is not demo wiring. Its docblock said the per-request warmer did "essentially all the work"; that warmer only fired on `/demo`, so the docblock now says the cron is the one warmer.
- **The A-12 reasoning still holds for the remaining warmer.** `WARMER_EXCLUSION_PATHS` keeps the cron route, so the guard that a `/health` warmer never enters the resilience core keeps its reach.
- **Lineage left in place (not edited):** `tools/eslint-plugin-quantalyze/tests/no-raw-analytics-fetch.test.ts`, whose case comment says where its synthetic pattern was copied from, and the ADRs under `docs/architecture/` that cite the module. Both are dated history.
